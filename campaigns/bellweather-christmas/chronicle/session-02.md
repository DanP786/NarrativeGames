# Session 02 — saved, spring 2027

## Christmas 2026
Harper and Eli spent Christmas together. Eli gave Harper a green leather artist's field folio and she gave him a framed portrait of him with Murphy. They attended Christmas lunch at Ruth Mercer's house, where Ruth displayed Harper's painting of the three Mercer brothers and privately apologized for listening to Hannah's accusations. Eli remained unaware that Harper had secretly visited Sebastian on Christmas Eve, heard his song about her, and received a Christmas message from him.

## Winter into spring 2027
Harper's trial work at Blackwood Events became a permanent full-time job. Working alongside Sebastian remains emotionally difficult for both: brief accidental hand contact and sometimes more deliberate, outwardly innocent touches sustain their attraction. Harper attends Sebastian's music gigs accompanied by Eli. Harper and Roxy have gone out together and grown closer. Eli now takes the lead in managing renovation of Harper's cottage, with Harper retaining final decision-making authority. The cottage heating is fixed, and the house and garden have been fully gutted/cleared; they await a roof grant before major roofing work. Harper continues living with Eli.
