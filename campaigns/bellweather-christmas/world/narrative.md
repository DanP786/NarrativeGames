Harper Devvene inherited Aunt May's badly deteriorated Victorian cottage at 18 Holly Lane in Bellweather, Vermont, and wants to establish an independent home and creative livelihood. Old letters tie May's love for Julian Blackwood to Arthur Ashford's disputed access across the conservatory garden and a hidden passage. Original documents remain hidden; Daniel Ashford has shown interest in the property.

Harper and local handyman Eli Mercer are exclusive, in love, and living together at his home during the renovation. Their relationship survived a split over jealousy and snooping, and Ruth Mercer's initial distrust. At Christmas 2026 Ruth apologized privately for listening to Hannah's accusations and began a tentative thaw. Harper gave Ruth a painting of the three Mercer brothers, and delighted the family children with drawings. Eli remains unaware that Harper visited Sebastian Blackwood privately on Christmas Eve to hear his song about loving her at the wrong time, and that they acknowledged powerful attraction without kissing.

Harper's childhood abuse and later abduction remain serious personal history; she has withheld parts of the latter from Eli. The complicated attraction to Sebastian persists despite her commitment to Eli. After quitting the bakery, Harper joined Blackwood Events; by spring 2027 her role became permanent full-time. Working closely with Sebastian is difficult for both, with fleeting touches stirring feelings neither has resolved. Harper attends his gigs with Eli, and has become closer friends with Roxy.

Through winter into spring 2027, Eli assumed practical control of the cottage renovation while Harper retained final authority on choices. The heating was repaired and the entire house and garden gutted and cleared. A roof grant application remains pending, with major roofing work awaiting the outcome. They still live together at Eli's house.
