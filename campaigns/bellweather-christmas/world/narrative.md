Harper Devvene inherited Aunt May's badly deteriorated Victorian cottage at 18 Holly Lane in snowy Bellweather, Vermont, and is determined to establish an independent home and creative livelihood. The cottage's costs threaten that dream, while old letters tie May's love for Julian Blackwood to Arthur Ashford's disputed access across the conservatory garden and a hidden passage. The original documents remain hidden; Daniel Ashford has shown interest in the property.

Harper fell hard for local handyman Eli Mercer, with an intense, sometimes jealous relationship. They became exclusive, split over Eli's snooping and accusations, and reconciled after Eli interrupted her date with Sebastian Blackwood. Both have said 'I love you,' but Eli's mother Ruth distrusts Harper, amplified by gossip from Sophie, Eli's ex and Harper's bakery boss. Harper disclosed a history of familial abuse, homelessness and being blamed or disbelieved. Ruth's surveillance and Eli's insecurity remain active pressures. Sebastian, an attractive goth musician and artist, still likes Harper and wrote an unfinished song about her; they are trying to be friends despite mutual attraction and his reported on-off history with Vivienne.

After severe hypothermia and hospitalization, Harper struggled to return to bakery shifts without medical clearance. She confronted Sophie for spreading personal gossip and quit on Dec 16. Money is scarce, with unpaid art commissions and costly cottage repairs. That same day she interviewed successfully at Bellweather Inn, though no offer has been made, and began an interview with Adrian Blackwood at Blackwood Events & Properties, facilitated by Sebastian. Adrian knows Harper from Caroline's party and knows about her inherited house. His opening question is whether Harper seeks specialist illustration commissions or a broader event/design role; compensation and employment remain undecided. Harper also completed an additional bookmark adaptation for Bellweather Books free in exchange for artist credit; the original commission remains unpaid.
