# Session 02 — Commitment strained; altered northern records
Early Frostwane 312, Blackthorn Bastion. Isolde continued restricted rehabilitation under Harris and Cassian Rook, whose return from his northern posting stirred old history. Garran Vale, still a corporal, received trial supervision of northern retaining-wall repairs and a written assessment for Calder; this is not a promotion. His shoulder restrictions remain. Elara Fen's alleged pregnancy and claim involving Garran remain unverified; Garran denies sleeping with her since last winter. Fen reportedly admitted to Harris that she falsely named Garran as a possible father for attention and denied intimacy since last winter; Isolde and Cassian do not know that private admission. No pregnancy or paternity is established.

Isolde and Garran discussed marriage painfully. Isolde told him she would marry him tomorrow; Garran said he was not ready, citing their repeated arguments and threats of leaving, and admitted he had sought impossible assurances. Isolde feared indefinite waiting and being deemed hard to love. She left his quarters tearful the previous night; he said, 'It's not all yours to carry.' They have not broken up or reconciled since. Isolde avoided him throughout the following day. Garran has not witnessed her private conversation with Cassian and does not know what she told him. Garran's proposal/breakup decisions remain autonomous, rooted in his own knowledge and character, not a scripted outcome.

Cassian showed Isolde suspicious changes to a northern charcoal-pit patrol report: 'three unwounded deer' replaced with 'several'; 'no scavengers' struck without initials; original field transcription marked received but missing from its expected place without an outgoing entry. Cassian pursued registers and authorization; Isolde checked other patrol reports without finding a confirmed wider pattern. Other separate observations include birds leaving western roosts and a figure at the eastern treeline; no causal link is established. Her injured hand ached after hours of writing.

Isolde confided her marriage troubles to Cassian. He acknowledged regretting that he had not married her years ago but did not claim their marriage would necessarily have succeeded; he still cares, has dated since their breakup, currently has no sweetheart at his northern posting, and is not positioning himself to replace Garran. Isolde said, 'You would have married all of me but didn’t. Garran doesn’t want to marry part of me. Maybe I shouldn’t get married at all.' She later apologized for confiding, and they regained a little of their old easy banter. Cassian promised to keep her confidence. They shared supper; Isolde playfully threw bread at him. Cassian teasingly described Isolde as having dark hair: a GM continuity mistake, NOT canon. Isolde's established appearance is blonde hair with dark roots, worn in fletter braids, grey eyes.

Elara Fen intruded at supper, assuming Isolde and Garran had broken up because they had not appeared together that day and Isolde was eating with Cassian. Elara invoked gossip about Cassian and Isolde's past relationship but could name no source. Isolde rebuked her: 'We’re not friends. Furthest from it. Why would I discuss my private life with you?' Elara left offended. Cassian offered to counter rumours, but Isolde declined, mentioning conflicting gossip about Elara and Garran. Isolde thanked Cassian, arranged to see him for training tomorrow, and went alone to her own quarters at night. She has not encountered Garran today. Scene rests in Isolde's room, with her hand stiff and the hearth low.
