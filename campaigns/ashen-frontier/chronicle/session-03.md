# Session 03 — ongoing
Isolde and Garran confronted the pain of his conditional demands after their marriage argument. They reconciled without a promise of marriage. A month later, in early Deepfrost, Isolde's physical strength and stamina had improved through training with Cassian, while she privately surrendered hope of marrying Garran. Garran noticed her increasing accommodation and acknowledged he had made disagreements feel dangerous. He brought her supper, and they spoke about his northern wall assessment, her eventual Severance retest, and solstice plans. When she declined to visit her own father, Garran invited her to meet his family if leave allowed. Isolde told him she no longer expected marriage because she could not meet his conditions, and later insisted she needed only their relationship. Garran was distressed by her withdrawal of hope but did not propose or pretend readiness. They shared a tender night; privately, Isolde grieved the imagined home, marriage and children she was giving up. Days later, Calder granted leave and the couple travelled together to the Vale family home for winter solstice, arriving late afternoon. Family introductions are just beginning. Military mysteries, duty clearance, and Elara's claim remain unresolved.
