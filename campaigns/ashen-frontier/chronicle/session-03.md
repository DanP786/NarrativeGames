# Session 03 — saved at T660

Isolde and Garran painfully reconciled after their unresolved marriage dispute. During early Deepfrost she had recovered strength through Cassian's training but surrendered hope of marrying Garran; she told him, “I don’t expect us to marry anymore Garran. You can stop worrying about that.” They visited the Vale family for solstice. A subsequent expedition to an abandoned emberstone station ended in Isolde's abduction and four deaths. She was taken to Eldoria, where King Alaric III declared she would marry his Lord Commander Evren Thorne, another Severance wielder. SECRET GM CANON: Caladorn High Command traded Isolde instead of two other Severance wielders; Isolde, Garran, Cassian and Edric do not know, and Calder's knowledge is unestablished.

Alaric compelled the marriage and sought children who might inherit Severance. Isolde lost her Warden life and freedom; she is legally Lady Isolde Thorne in Eldoria but remains Sergeant Varrick at Blackthorn. Over time Isolde and Evren built a genuine, consensual intimacy despite the coercive circumstances. Garran, devastated, received her farewell letter; Isolde secretly sent him the date and location of a northern inspection. Garran attempted to abduct her there, precipitating a Blight confrontation. Isolde's Severance returned, and she and Evren cut the bindings of a central Blight mass; she suffered headache and nosebleed. Garran was spared without pardon. Evren does not know about the secret inspection note, Garran's kiss, or their private pregnancy conversation. The Pell assassination inquiry, Caladorn dispatch investigation and Northern Crossing engineering repairs remain unresolved.

The king revoked Isolde's Eldorian adviser and military role, prohibited Warden insignia, demanded monthly reproductive examinations, prescribed a royal fertility tonic (whose contribution to conception is unknown), and tried to require a household witness for marital intimacy. Evren resisted the intrusion. In a royal audience, Isolde confirmed the marriage was consummated; Alaric threatened to offer Evren another woman or take an interest in Isolde himself. She argued this would violate their vows and undermine his own aims. Alaric suspended the witness for one night only. Isolde explained that her Severance acts only against Blight, and that at Northern Crossing the central mass seemed to command others. The king tentatively allowed a provisional Blight observation protocol, conditional on Anwen's clearance and a real field report, without restoring rank or authorizing a mission. Isolde said she would not withdraw if comrades were endangered; Evren proposed a narrowly defined emergency exception.

After that audience, Isolde admitted jealousy at the suggestion of another woman. Evren reassured her of his devotion. She chose to call herself “Isolde Thorne” and told him “I love you too”; their mutual love became explicit. Several weeks later, Mistress Anwen privately confirmed an early pregnancy from missed courses, morning sickness and examination; exact dating, sex, inheritance and outcome are unknown. Isolde told Evren “We are with child”; both were joyful, and Evren vowed their baby should be loved rather than treated as a royal experiment. Isolde said, “This is our child. We should choose when we want to share that news.” She suffered several days of significant nausea and fatigue, with Nell bringing food. A compulsory royal physician's exam found strong signs but no definitive confirmation. Isolde answered questions truthfully, too exhausted to lie. Evren stayed with her rather than rushing to announce the pregnancy. She suggested telling his parents privately; no notification has yet been established.

The physician's report of suspected pregnancy reached Alaric, who proclaimed an expected Thorne child to the court and staged a lavish celebration without consulting the parents. Isolde attended on Evren's arm, politely thanked the king for his generosity and publicly assented to his prohibition on frontier travel, Blight encounters and Severance exertion during pregnancy. The king ordered another physician's visit in a fortnight and insisted his physician report to him; Anwen may assist. Courtiers speculated about the child's sex and powers. Isolde answered, “A healthy baby is all I wish for.” T660 ends with Isolde and Evren sitting together on a sheltered palace gallery overlooking the gardens, away from the celebration. Evren offers to remain there or escort her back to their rooms. The king's celebration continues inside. Early spring 312 After Ashfall, exact date unknown.
