# Session 01 — The Night Before
Opening scene: Evening of 18 Emberwane, Year 312 After Ashfall. Blackthorn Bastion, Cinderhouse. Isolde Varrick is off duty on the night before a dawn expedition. Six weeks since Greywatch, with relationships strained and orders quietly changed. Scene begins before Isolde chooses an action.

## Later developments through 19 Frostwane 312
The expedition to Survey Station Seventeen ended with Isolde destroying a Blight heart using Severance, suffering catastrophic neurological injury, and being rescued from the collapse by Garran and fellow Wardens. Weeks of infirmary recovery followed; Garran and Isolde reconciled, grew close, and publicly acknowledged being together. Harris eventually cleared her for supervised physical and controlled sword rehabilitation, not field duty or Severance. On 18 Frostwane Isolde resumed supervised drills with Torren Kest and moved back to barracks. Garran's former partner Elara Fen made pointed public remarks at the homecoming gathering, reopening the wound of his jealousy-driven betrayal last winter; Isolde and Garran spoke privately and reaffirmed their relationship. He stayed overnight. On 19 Frostwane they told each other 'I love you' for the first time, shared playful morning banter, and Isolde made Garran promise to have Harris assess his lingering shoulder injury from her rescue. They left her quarters together for breakfast.

GM-established background, not yet revealed in play: Isolde's childhood friend and former long-term lover Sergeant Cassian Rook was her first profound love; they nearly married, but he could not reciprocate her commitment. Edric Varrick may recommend Cassian as an instructor for her continuing rehabilitation, unaware of their painful history. Garran does not know this backstory.
