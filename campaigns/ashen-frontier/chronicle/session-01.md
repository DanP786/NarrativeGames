# Session 01 — Blackthorn, Greywatch and recovery
Opening: 18 Emberwane 312, Blackthorn Bastion, Cinderhouse, before an expedition. Isolde Varrick is a Warden sergeant, a rare Severance wielder and survivor of the Greywatch disaster. The expedition to Survey Station Seventeen ended with Isolde destroying a Blight heart through Severance at catastrophic neurological cost. Garran Vale helped carry her from the collapse, injuring his shoulder. She endured months of recovery; Captain Calder removed field and supervisory duties following dangerous impulsive incidents, while physician Harris supervised gradual rehabilitation.

Isolde and Garran reconciled after his earlier jealousy-driven betrayal, made their relationship public, and exchanged sincere declarations of love. She told Garran that Cassian Rook, her childhood friend, was her first love and that they once planned a future together before Cassian chose his career. Garran knows this; older NPC and character records suggesting ignorance are stale. Their relationship developed strain over questions of whether Garran stayed out of love or responsibility, his need for independence and limits on being her sole emotional support, and contradictory invitations to share his professional life. One night Isolde asked, 'you definitely want to be together? I promise I won’t spiral or self destruct if you don’t.' Garran said he chose her but could not guarantee everything would be easy. Isolde privately began questioning whether making their relationship official had been a mistake.

Several weeks passed. Isolde advanced under Harris's supervision from gentle mobility to structured conditioning and sword training. She and Garran still share meals, affection, sex, public commitment and enjoyable companionship, and no longer argue, but Isolde has withdrawn emotionally and no longer shares vulnerabilities; Garran can notice distance but cannot know her private conclusions. Harris previously agreed to review less disruptive contraception than a prescribed internal sheath, but the outcome is not established. Isolde privately feels that emotional walls have dulled her old hurt over Cassian as well; she intends to marry neither man.

In early Frostwane 312, Sergeant Cassian Rook returned to Blackthorn from his northern station after waiting more than a month for Harris's clearance. Isolde had not known he'd left. Their first renewed training session began in the yard. Four years of separate experience have changed Isolde's technique; Cassian's expectations of her old leftward opening proved wrong. In three controlled exchanges Isolde scored two touches to Cassian's one, demonstrating sharp technique but reduced stamina. Cassian stopped the set and ordered water, asking how the exchanges felt. Harris's clearance covers structured swordsmanship and conditioning only, not unrestricted sparring, field duty or Severance. Current scene remains in progress at the training yard, morning.
