# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Isolde's quarters at Blackthorn Bastion, morning after the intimate supper
Previous day Fen admitted before Harris that she named Garran as possible father merely to get his attention. Fen affirmed no sexual encounter with Garran since last winter and denied any forced encounter; pregnancy and paternity have not been medically confirmed to Isolde. Isolde told Garran; they shared relief and affection. Harris cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Cassian showed Isolde an altered northern charcoal-pit patrol-office master report: original recorded three unwounded deer and absence of scavengers; uninitialled correction struck out scavenger detail and changed 'three' to 'several'. Isolde retrieved her own suspicious reports; changes' provenance uncertain. Cassian is investigating. He revealed Edric recommended him for Isolde's rehab and speculated Edric might question Garran's stalled rank; Cassian expressed concern about unverified Fen rumours, unaware Fen recanted. Isolde and Cassian discussed his past failure to commit. Isolde visited Garran in the engineers' workshop for consensual private intimacy (non-graphic), then overheard him defend her warmly to another soldier without his knowledge. They agreed to ask each other one honest question every night. At six Isolde wore a burgundy 'husband-catching' dress; Garran brought supper and wine. His question concerned her motive for seeking him; hers concerned Edric questioning his suitability. Garran admitted Edric thanked him for rescue but questioned his eight years as corporal and his discipline. Isolde reassured him rank doesn't matter to her; he confessed ambition to make sergeant and chief sapper for himself. They shared consensual intimacy off-page, supper and tart. Isolde discovered she strongly likes his affectionate praise 'good girl' and confirmed this. Later she willingly entrusted him with control during consensual intimacy off-page. Afterwards she cried a tear of relief and explained she chose vulnerability freely rather than from distress; Garran was moved and reassuring. They fell asleep together. Next morning Isolde woke from unusually deep restorative sleep; Garran greeted her affectionately and teasingly asked whether she slept well, 'good girl.' Isolde went visibly quiet and only nodded, affected by the familiar praise. Garran went to fetch breakfast and returned to find her dozing. After placing food down, he gently tilted her face toward him and asked, outside their agreed evening question ritual, whether Cassian knew about her liking to be called 'good girl.' Her breath caught and she shook her head no. Garran was relieved and pleased to discover this is new between them, though aware his comparison to Cassian reveals insecurity; he can respond with warmth rather than making her answer a competition. Isolde remains on restricted recovery status, not full duty. Both nightly questions from previous evening answered. Other unusual reports: birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; no confirmed connection.
