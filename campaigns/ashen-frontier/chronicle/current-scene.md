# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, late morning, northern Eldorian road. Isolde in Eldorian blue armour and twin blonde braids travels with Evren and Halden's strike force under limited royal attachment and Anwen's cautious clearance. Refugees from Briar Hollow report coordinated Blight attacks, a stationary bark-skinned large creature by the well, and villagers taken alive toward old charcoal road; eyewitness claims unverified. Isolde argued that the villages lack Fort Aster's defences and that failing to stop this Blight will doom more settlements. Evren agreed to investigate urgently but refused to commit the whole force blindly: he ordered Halden to dispatch an urgent warning to Aster, secure refugees and maintain a reserve, while scouts reconnoitre the charcoal road. Evren asks Isolde to help select the approach and immediate priority. No strike or rescue has occurred. Garran and Cassian have not been established to know of miscarriage.
