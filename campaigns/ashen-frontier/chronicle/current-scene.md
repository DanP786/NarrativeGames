# Current scene — S03
Early Deepfrost 312, late afternoon, covered barracks passage at Blackthorn Bastion. Garran had just told Isolde she need not earn his love by being easier to love and started to leave. Isolde held out her hands in disbelief and challenged, 'don't I?' Garran stopped and returned, acknowledging that his conduct has taught her otherwise and that declarations of unconditional love do not erase his repeated conditions and withdrawal. He admitted he had been walking away from the discomfort again and stayed to hear her, without proposing or declaring a breakup. Isolde privately believes she will never marry and has been accommodating Garran; he does not know that private conclusion. Recovery has improved with Cassian; unrestricted clearance not established. Northern records, retaining-wall assessment, Elara pregnancy/paternity remain unresolved.
