# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Isolde's quarters at Blackthorn Bastion, evening supper
Earlier today Fen admitted before Harris that she named Garran as possible father merely to get his attention. Fen affirmed no sexual encounter with Garran since last winter and denied any forced encounter; pregnancy and paternity have not been medically confirmed to Isolde. Isolde told Garran; they shared relief and affection. Harris has cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Cassian showed Isolde an altered northern charcoal-pit patrol-office master report: original recorded three unwounded deer and absence of scavengers; uninitialled correction struck out scavenger detail and changed 'three' to 'several'. Isolde retrieved her own suspicious reports; her summary records three deer but omits scavengers; provenance uncertain. Cassian accepted responsibility for investigating the altered report. Cassian revealed Edric recommended him for Isolde's rehab and speculated Edric might question Garran's stalled rank; Cassian expressed concern about unverified rumours Garran fathered another woman's child, unaware Fen recanted before Harris. Isolde and Cassian discussed his past failure to commit; Isolde teased him about an intimate trick he had lost and said she would try it with Garran. Isolde returned to Garran in the engineers' workshop, initiated consensual private intimacy in a cupboard (non-graphic), then rebuked an impatient private's tone. They agreed to ask each other one honest question every night. Isolde overheard Garran defend her warmly to a workshop soldier, without Garran knowing she heard. At six Isolde tidied her quarters, wore a striking burgundy dress once described as 'husband-catching', and unbraided her blonde hair with dark roots to her waist. Garran arrived with supper and wine, admired her. For his nightly question he asked whether she came to him from happiness or fear about Fen; she admitted she might have overcompensated to reassure him and distract him from Fen's game, but also wanted him. Garran appreciated her honesty. Isolde then asked her nightly question: while she was unconscious in the infirmary, had her father interrogated Garran's suitability for his daughter? Garran admitted Edric had thanked him for helping rescue Isolde but asked pointedly about eight years as corporal versus Isolde's rapid rise, his ambitions and his discipline. Garran said he had felt judged and inadequate while terrified for Isolde; he hadn't told her because he didn't want to burden her during recovery or seem to seek a fight with her father. He does not know how Isolde learned to ask this and awaits her reaction. Other unusual reports: birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; no confirmed connection.
