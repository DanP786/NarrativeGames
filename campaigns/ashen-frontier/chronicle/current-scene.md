# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, late afternoon, King Alaric III's private audience chamber, Eldorian royal palace. Isolde seeks military use of her Severance when necessary, on terms comparable to Evren, while saying she will continue trying to perform her duties as a wife. She argues Evren's strategic command is too important to risk on the Fort Aster strike. Alaric is prepared to grant a limited operational attachment for this urgent mission, contingent on Mistress Anwen's fitness assessment, under Evren's operational command, but not yet permanent rank or unrestricted deployment. He presses Isolde to accept those terms; she has not yet answered. Evren's strike force is preparing for dawn, not yet departed. Isolde still grieves miscarriage; Alaric has not received her socially before this audience. Garran and Cassian have not been established to know of miscarriage.
