# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, late afternoon, King Alaric III's private audience chamber, Eldorian royal palace. Isolde accepted the king's proposed terms with 'yes your majesty' and a curtsy. Alaric signed and sealed a limited authorization attaching her to Northern Command for the Fort Aster Severance strike, contingent on Mistress Anwen certifying her physically fit for combat. Evren retains overall command; no rank restored or permanent appointment yet, though Alaric will consider one if mission succeeds and her health permits. Isolde has been given the sealed authorization and is free to return to Evren or seek Anwen. Evren's force is preparing for dawn, not yet departed. Isolde still grieves miscarriage and wears fitted court dress rather than pregnancy gowns. Garran and Cassian have not been established to know of miscarriage.
