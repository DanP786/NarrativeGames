# Current scene — Blackthorn Bastion lower courtyard, dawn
Tier: charged. Warden Sergeant Isolde Varrick entered muster with sword and pack. She saw engineer Mara flirting conspicuously with Garran Vale among the expedition preparations and gave Mara a filthy look, then continued without confrontation to report for her assignment. Garran noticed the interaction but did not encourage Mara's advances; Mara saw Isolde's glare. Isolde has reached the senior personnel assignment point, where Captain Rhea Calder has the revised orders and is ready to address her. Dawn departure to abandoned Survey Station Seventeen is imminent. The Isolde–Garran post-expedition public dinner remains provisionally promised.
