# Current scene — Forward line overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, midday 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged reconnaissance. Isolde kept eyes on the faint, taut luminous Blight thread near the western perimeter wall and told Captain Rhea Calder that yesterday's Blight was stronger than any she had encountered, that Severance nearly knocked her unconscious on earlier cuts and did on the third, then pointed out the visible thread at Station Seventeen. Calder took the warning seriously, ordered scouts to hold outside the perimeter and avoid the western wall, and asked Isolde to describe the thread's route and whether it moves. Other Wardens cannot see the thread; its origin, purpose and relationship to yesterday's creature are unknown. Isolde remains injured, in uniform without armour, sword in hand, with painful cracked ribs and lingering dizziness; Harris has not cleared her. Await Isolde's answer and observation.
