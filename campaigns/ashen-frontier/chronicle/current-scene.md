Elara Fen claimed to Garran that she was pregnant with his child; paternity remains unverified. Garran maintains he has not slept with Elara since last winter and believes he cannot be the father; Isolde previously affirmed belief but now privately doubts after considering periods of absence. An OOC possibility of unremembered drugging is not canon and must not be presumed. Captain Calder and Physician Harris approved today's afternoon trip to the Copper Hart by cart, with minimal walking, warmth, prompt return if unwell and before evening gates close. Isolde has no rehabilitation with Cassian until tomorrow. Isolde grouped unusual patrol reports (unwounded dead deer at northern charcoal pits, birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline) for future comparison. Garran drove Isolde to the Copper Hart for roast beef. Elara arrived mid-meal complaining of abdominal pain and fearing for a pregnancy. Isolde called taking some of Garran's pudding compensation for their interrupted evening 'by a pregnant stalker'; Garran acknowledged the disruption, offered her a portion of his pudding, and stayed with her. Isolde's private doubts about possible gaps in Garran's fidelity are unknown to him. Harris's restrictions on Isolde's exertion remain in force.
