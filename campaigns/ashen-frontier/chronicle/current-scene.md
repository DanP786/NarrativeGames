Yesterday Isolde and Garran shared a rare Copper Hart dinner interrupted by Elara Fen's claim of abdominal pain and pregnancy by Garran. Midwife Bell found signs consistent with pregnancy but could not confirm pregnancy, gestational age or paternity; Edwin transported Elara to Physician Harris. He called a pause for water and assessment before any third exchange, mindful of Harris's restrictions. Cassian is Isolde's childhood friend, former serious partner and first heartbreak, assigned via Frontier Command and Edric Varrick's recommendation. Cassian does not automatically know about Garran or Elara.
