# Current scene — Beneath the western outbuilding, Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic revelation and imminent descent. Isolde exclaimed to the gaunt captive surveyor Mara Venn, “You've been here prisoner for years!” Mara replied that she does not know how many years have passed; she has counted seasons imperfectly in the dark, and sometimes the Blight seems to put her to sleep for long intervals. She claims the roots keep her alive but tethered, and other surveyors were taken below. She did not receive an answer to whether Isolde is an Ember-bearer. The root around Isolde's badly injured ribs continues drawing her toward the lower chamber despite her attempts to stay still; it is now pulling her along the floor toward a steep descending threshold. Mara's own wrist filament holds her back. Isolde remains conscious, unarmoured, swordless, in severe pain and able to take only shallow breaths. Aboveground Wardens search; no demolition ordered.
