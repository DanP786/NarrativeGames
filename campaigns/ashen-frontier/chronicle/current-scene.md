# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, outside Isolde's quarters, evening
Yesterday Isolde and Garran shared a rare Copper Hart dinner interrupted by Elara Fen's claim of abdominal pain and pregnancy by Garran. Midwife Bell found signs consistent with pregnancy but could not confirm pregnancy, gestational age or paternity; Edwin transported Elara to Physician Harris. Garran maintains he has not slept with Elara since last winter; Isolde privately doubts this due to past absences, unknown to him. This morning Harris cleared Isolde for supervised, restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Two days earlier Isolde won two of three training rounds against Cassian; her anger at his arrival weeks ago has considerably eased. Today she won first controlled exchange, Cassian won second, and he called a water break before the third; third outcome remains unestablished. At evening meal Garran saved her stew and bread, joked about Cassian and Elara, and arranged dinner tomorrow in her room; Isolde laughed aloud, startling Wardens, then silenced a staring private with an icy look. Garran accompanied her to her quarters hand in hand. Outside her door, Isolde wrapped her arms around his neck and proposed they each ask one question the other doesn't want to answer, leave the conversations outside, then spend an intimate evening together, asking him to check she hadn't overdone training. Garran accepted the exchange and invited her to ask her question first; neither question has yet been asked. No report on Elara's medical assessment or paternity is established. Earlier unusual patrol reports: unwounded dead deer at northern charcoal pits, birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; not confirmed connected. Harris's restrictions remain in force.
