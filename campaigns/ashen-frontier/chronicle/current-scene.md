# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Isolde's quarters at Blackthorn Bastion, evening supper
Earlier today Fen admitted before Harris that she named Garran as possible father merely to get his attention. Fen affirmed no sexual encounter with Garran since last winter and denied any forced encounter; pregnancy and paternity have not been medically confirmed to Isolde. Isolde told Garran; they shared relief and affection. Harris has cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Cassian showed Isolde an altered northern charcoal-pit patrol-office master report: original recorded three unwounded deer and absence of scavengers; uninitialled correction struck out scavenger detail and changed 'three' to 'several'. Isolde retrieved her own suspicious reports; her summary records three deer but omits scavengers; provenance uncertain. Cassian accepted responsibility for investigating the altered report. Cassian revealed Edric recommended him for Isolde's rehab and speculated Edric might question Garran's stalled rank; Cassian expressed concern about unverified rumours Garran fathered another woman's child, unaware Fen recanted before Harris. Isolde and Cassian discussed his past failure to commit; Isolde teased him about an intimate trick he had lost and said she would try it with Garran. Isolde returned to Garran in the engineers' workshop, initiated consensual private intimacy in a cupboard (non-graphic), then rebuked an impatient private's tone. She and Garran agreed to ask each other one honest question every night. Isolde overheard Garran defend her warmly to a workshop soldier, without Garran knowing she heard. At six Isolde tidied her quarters, wore a striking burgundy dress once described as 'husband-catching', and unbraided her blonde hair with dark roots to her waist. Garran arrived with supper and wine, admired her and jokingly doubted her mother had packed the bold dress. Isolde accepted his hand to sit at the table, brushed her hair back to reveal the neckline, and offered Garran first question in their nightly exchange. Garran was visibly distracted by her appearance but chose to ask a sincere question: whether her return to him earlier today came from happiness and wanting him, rather than obligation or worry about Fen. Awaiting Isolde's answer; she still has her own question to ask tonight. Other unusual reports: birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; no confirmed connection.
