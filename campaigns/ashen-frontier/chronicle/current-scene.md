# Current scene — Threshold of lower chamber beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic revelation. Isolde identified herself to captive surveyor Mara Venn as Isolde Varrick and revealed that she bears a Severance Ember. Mara reacted with terror and recognition, warning that the underground Blight mass might be seeking precisely that capacity and urging Isolde not to cut any glowing strand until she understands what it binds. The thick root dragging Isolde paused at the lip of the descending lower chamber; the pale strands in the masonry brightened and drew taut around a central unseen point. A low voice or vibration from below formed something like Isolde's name, ambiguous whether actual speech or a Blight echo. Mara remains tethered in the upper passage. Isolde remains unarmoured, swordless, severely rib-injured, conscious and able only to breathe shallowly; no Severance used. Wardens above search for her, demolition withheld. Await action.
