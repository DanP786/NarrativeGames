# Current scene — Lower-chamber slope beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic. Isolde carefully reached down with her free right hand and successfully grasped the leather-wrapped handle of the rusted surveyor's short blade she had hooked close with her boot. The blade is short, nicked and corroded but has a usable edge; she now holds it in her right hand. The movement aggravated her damaged ribs and drew a small reactive constriction from the thick root around her waist, but did not disarm or incapacitate her. The root still pins and slowly draws her down toward the pulsing lower-chamber mass. Isolde can see a luminous binding seam along the root, though Severance would require a deliberate strike and impose dangerous physiological cost; she has not used it. Mara Venn remains tethered behind and warns against cutting blindly. Aboveground Wardens search; no demolition authorized. Await action.
