# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Isolde's quarters at Blackthorn Bastion, evening supper
Earlier today Fen admitted before Harris that she named Garran as possible father merely to get his attention. Fen affirmed no sexual encounter with Garran since last winter and denied any forced encounter; pregnancy and paternity have not been medically confirmed to Isolde. Isolde told Garran; they shared relief and affection. Harris has cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Cassian showed Isolde an altered northern charcoal-pit patrol-office master report: original recorded three unwounded deer and absence of scavengers; uninitialled correction struck out scavenger detail and changed 'three' to 'several'. Isolde retrieved her own suspicious reports; her summary records three deer but omits scavengers; provenance uncertain. Cassian accepted responsibility for investigating the altered report. Cassian revealed Edric recommended him for Isolde's rehab and speculated Edric might question Garran's stalled rank; Cassian expressed concern about unverified rumours Garran fathered another woman's child, unaware Fen recanted before Harris. Isolde and Cassian discussed his past failure to commit; Isolde teased him about an intimate trick he had lost and said she would try it with Garran. Isolde returned to Garran in the engineers' workshop, initiated consensual private intimacy in a cupboard (non-graphic), then rebuked an impatient private's tone. They agreed to ask each other one honest question every night. Isolde overheard Garran defend her warmly to a workshop soldier, without Garran knowing she heard. At six Isolde tidied her quarters, wore a striking burgundy dress once described as 'husband-catching', and unbraided her blonde hair with dark roots to her waist. Garran arrived with supper and wine, admired her, and asked the first nightly question: whether her return to him earlier came from happiness and wanting him or fear about Fen. Isolde said she might have been overcompensating to reassure him and distract him from Fen's game. Garran said he appreciated it but didn't want her to feel obligated. Isolde then teasingly added that a phase of her menstrual cycle heightens her desire, sitting back, crossing her legs and tracing a finger along the neckline of her dress while holding his gaze. Garran was flustered and amused, acknowledging that she wanted him for her own sake, and gently teased her about trying to derail supper. Her own nightly question remains unasked. Other unusual reports: birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; no confirmed connection.
