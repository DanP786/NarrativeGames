# Current scene — Cinderhouse, evening
Tier: charged. Isolde entered Cinderhouse before dawn expedition. Garran made barbed greeting; Leif offered empty chair; Tamsin publicly accused Isolde of abandoning Greywatch dead. Isolde quietly noted Tamsin had been issued leave. Tamsin explained she returned early from compassionate leave after burying Oren, Calder knows and has her on tomorrow's roster. Isolde said “Be prepared for tomorrow” and left without argument. At Leif's table she asked “What's that?” about his map. Leif disclosed tomorrow's route has been altered from the standard northward sweep: a marked detour east toward an abandoned survey station; old scouting map doesn't match amended orders. He is wary of discussing classified detail in the crowded hall and awaits Isolde's response.
