# Current scene — Threshold of lower chamber beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic tense exchange. Isolde dryly told Mara Venn she has nothing to cut with, having lost her sword when dragged underground. Mara acknowledged this, then pointed out an old rusted surveyor's short blade embedded in a cracked timber support within arm's reach only if Isolde can free one hand and lean, a dangerous prospect given her ribs and the root's grip. Mara does not know whether it is useful for Severance. The thick root holds Isolde at the slope's edge, not presently dragging her; luminous strands remain taut and a pulsing mass is faintly visible in the lower chamber. Isolde remains unarmoured, swordless for now, in severe rib pain with shallow breathing. Mara is tethered by wrist filament. No Severance used; Wardens above searching, demolition withheld. Await Isolde's choice.
