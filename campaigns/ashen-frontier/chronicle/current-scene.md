# Current scene — Cinderhouse, evening
Tier: charged. Isolde entered Cinderhouse before dawn expedition. Garran made barbed greeting; Leif offered empty chair; Tamsin publicly accused Isolde of abandoning Greywatch dead. Isolde quietly noted Tamsin had been issued leave; Tamsin explained returning early from compassionate leave, Calder knows and rostered her. Isolde told her “Be prepared for tomorrow” and walked to Leif. Leif showed revised orders diverting tomorrow's expedition east to abandoned Survey Station Seventeen, near Greywatch. Isolde joked “Thought you liked intimidating women,” and said she goes where ordered. Leif challenged her unquestioning posture. Isolde leaned back with boot on support of his chair and said she is not paid to question orders, fifteen good Wardens died six weeks ago and nine more would have without her, she goes to prevent further deaths, and Calder is probably cautious. Leif acknowledged her rescue, but argued that careful questioning is part of keeping people alive, then softened rather than prolonging an unwinnable argument. Await Isolde's reply.
