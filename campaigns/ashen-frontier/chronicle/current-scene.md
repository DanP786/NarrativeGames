# Current scene — Beneath the western outbuilding, Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic abduction. A thigh-thick black Blight root seized Isolde around her waist and injured ribs while she knelt on the western line, then dragged her into the widening earth fissure before the shield line could stop it. Her sword was knocked from her grip and remains above ground near the fissure. The fall through dirt and shattered masonry left her battered and winded, with severely aggravated rib pain, but conscious. She landed in a cramped underground service void amid roots, old masonry and pale luminous Blight strands, with a deeper passage and a faint human-sounding knock somewhere beyond. A heavy root still grips her lower torso, pinning her against stone; it has not yet crushed her. Above ground Calder and the Wardens witnessed the abduction and are attempting to respond; Garran's charges are prepared but no detonation ordered. Isolde has no armour or sword and is explicitly forbidden Severance, though desperate circumstances now threaten her life. Her next action is awaited.
