# Current scene — Medical tent at expedition night camp, 18 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged aftermath. After Garran apologized for carelessly speaking about their private dinner in front of Medic Harris, Isolde gripped his hand and asked whether he had seen the extraordinary Blight creature. Garran explained he had not witnessed it alive (he was with the column) but saw its ruined remains when the recovery party passed: an immense mass of roots and pale tissue, unlike ordinary Blight growth, seemingly woven around a central knot. He mentioned soldiers were uneasy about what it was and that the remains were not brought to camp. He is still seated beside her cot holding her hand, and Harris is nearby. Isolde remains on cot, severely headache-stricken and dizzy, with painful ribs (three suspected cracked), bandaged temple, dressed palm, light sensitivity and weakness after three Severances and blast. Company camped at night en route to Survey Station Seventeen; Isolde has not yet reported to Captain Calder. Await her response.
