# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, late night, Fort Aster, northern Eldoria. After the woodland excavation battle, Isolde was transported on stretcher to Fort Aster and treated in a warm private bedroom: shoulder/back bruising from falling timber, painful breathing, severe headache, double vision and marked right-hand weakness/tremor after four recent Severance uses. Medic forbade further Severance and recommended observation. Isolde fell asleep after treatment. Evren remained away for hours overseeing battle aftermath, security, prisoners and casualty accounting; returned very late with his superficial brow cut dressed, quietly entering while Isolde slept. Seven captives were rescued alive; controller and red apparatus disabled, excavation collapsed. Full losses and underground purpose not yet established. One infantryman serious leg wound. Garran and Cassian not established to know miscarriage.
