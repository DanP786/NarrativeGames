# Current scene — S03
Late evening, three days after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312. Light snow as Isolde and Garran walk arm-in-arm from Fox and Lantern toward Vale family home. After Garran admitted jealousy over Adrian and teased whether she thought Adrian handsome, Isolde said 'You’re the one who said handsome. I’ve only got eyes for one man.' Garran received this warmly and was visibly pleased, flirted and kissed her, walking onward. Tavern friends Nell, Bram, Livia, Denny remain inside. Isolde drank whisky over evening, intoxication unspecified. Earlier Isolde said with strained smile 'I see no point in dreaming of a different life. With or without severance I’m a soldier'; Garran knows she once dreamed of a family with Cassian and later offered to marry him, cannot know present private grief. Her brothers: one tends horses at parents' barn, another bakes pastries king reportedly enjoyed. Livia probed intrusively about marriage; Isolde publicly said 'We’re happy as we are... Choosing someone every day isn’t determined by marriage' but privately disbelieves and mourns lost marriage/home/children/family hopes; Garran cannot know. Garran rebuked Livia and privately said he knows things aren't made right; Isolde said 'And I told you there’s nothing to make right,' kissed him, went to bar. At bar Garran acknowledged youthful reputation for flirting and careless promises; Isolde said 'No, just explains a lot' and walked back to table. Garran loves her but not ready to marry, knows she no longer expects it. His 'practically a married man' hinge joke privately stung her, unknown to him. She overheard Mara press Garran about settling down, neither knows she overheard. Isolde owns decorative Master Heddon hinge (3.5 silver), joking custody: she controls use and holds two days/week, Garran five, currently has it wrapped. Garran bought brass clockwork bird (four copper); Mara bought winter-branch brooch. Seven days approved leave, staying longer than three days at Vale home. Orchard wall rebuilding pending; Garran shoulder cold-stiff not painful. No unrestricted duty or Severance retest clearance; permanent Ember loss unconfirmed.
