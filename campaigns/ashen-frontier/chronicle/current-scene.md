# Current scene — Forward command position overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, early afternoon 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged reconnaissance. Isolde scowled and told Calder, “Something is moving under the outbuilding.” This confirms her concern but not a change in the luminous thread, which remains taut and motionless from her vantage. Calder signalled the four Warden scouts inside the eastern courtyard to withdraw rather than approach the scraping beneath the western outbuilding. As they began moving back, a second heavy scrape sounded and a dull knock followed beneath the outbuilding; no visible Blight emerged. One scout at the eastern breach briefly paused to look back toward the main building. Isolde remains seated, injured, medically unfit and barred from Severance. Await action.
