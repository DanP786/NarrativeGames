# Current scene — Beneath the western outbuilding, Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic revelation. Isolde, pinned and struggling to breathe, asked surveyor Mara Venn what is in the lower chamber. Mara said the surveyors found a buried emberstone seam threaded through an immense living Blight mass, and that the station's lower chamber contains a pulsing core-like structure that reacts to Ember-bearers; she claims people were brought there and did not return. This is Mara's testimony, not verified canon. Mara urgently asked Isolde whether she bears Ember. As they spoke, the thick root holding Isolde began to pull her a few inches toward the descending passage, despite her remaining still. She is conscious, swordless, unarmoured, severely rib-injured and breathing shallowly. Mara is tethered by a black filament at her wrist. Aboveground Wardens search for Isolde; demolition remains withheld. Await response.
