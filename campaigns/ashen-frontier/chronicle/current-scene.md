# Current scene — S04
Several days after the Eldorian court celebration, spring 312. At Blackthorn Bastion, now-Sergeant Garran Vale learned from Elara Fen (via family Eldorian merchant contacts) of King Alaric's public announcement of Isolde's pregnancy. After sending Fen away and grieving privately over Isolde's farewell letter, Garran went to Cassian Rook's workroom and told him the news. Cassian was shocked, noted the announcement was secondhand and could not establish the medical certainty, and asked Garran what precisely the source said. Their conversation remains open. Anwen's private confirmation is unknown to both. Isolde remains in Eldoria; her gallery scene with Evren has not been resolved.
