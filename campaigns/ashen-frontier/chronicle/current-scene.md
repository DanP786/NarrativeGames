# Current scene — Northern road, old survey milestone, midmorning
Tier: charged. Isolde kept her forward security squad marching north through misty pines for another stretch. Near a leaning old survey milestone, Nera Holt spotted peculiar impressions in the damp verge: several parallel narrow furrows crossing the road into the trees, with no obvious hoof or boot prints, and an unusually dark patch of soil. The marks look recent but their source is unknown. Hester Brann, Pell Rusk, and Davin Sorel are present and attentive; Captain Calder's main column follows at distance, Leif Ardent's scouts are farther ahead. Nera quietly pointed the marks out to Isolde, awaiting her decision whether to inspect, report, bypass, or act otherwise. No attack or confirmed Blight presence yet.
