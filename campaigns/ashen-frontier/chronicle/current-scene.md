# Current scene — Beneath the western outbuilding, Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic entrapment. Isolde struggled physically against the thigh-thick black root pinning her waist and injured ribs to the underground wall. She could not break free; her ribs were too painful to take a full breath, and the root responded by tightening just enough to stop her writhing. She remains conscious but increasingly short of breath, swordless and without armour. As she struggled, the deliberate knocking deeper in the passage ceased, replaced by a hoarse human voice whispering, “Don't fight it. It tightens when you fight.” The speaker is unseen and identity unknown. Pale Blight strands line the broken service passage, converging deeper underground. Above ground Wardens know Isolde was taken; no demolition authorized. Await Isolde's response.
