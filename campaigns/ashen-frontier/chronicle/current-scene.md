# Current scene — Firelit forward command overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, nightfall 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged tactical standoff. Isolde confirmed to Captain Calder that the glossy black scouting tendril emerging briefly from the western outbuilding's widening foundation crack resembled those deployed by yesterday's Blight creature. Calder treated this as confirmation of a similar threat, ordered soldiers to watch their footing and hold the illuminated defensive line, and directed a runner to warn sapper Garran Vale to keep demolition charges ready at a safe distance but not to detonate without command. The tendril withdrew and has not reappeared. The luminous thread visible to Isolde remains taut and still; its relationship to the tendril remains unknown. Isolde remains seated beneath the command tree under blankets, ribs painful, headache improved, no armour, sword at side, barred from entry and Severance. Await action.
