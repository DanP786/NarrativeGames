# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Blackthorn Bastion patrol records office, late morning
Earlier today Fen admitted before Harris that she named Garran as possible father merely to get his attention. Fen affirmed no sexual encounter with Garran since last winter and denied any forced encounter; pregnancy and paternity have not been medically confirmed to Isolde. Isolde told Garran; they shared relief and affection and plan dinner in her room tonight. Harris has cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty; latest training's third round outcome remains unestablished. Cassian showed Isolde an altered northern charcoal-pit patrol-office master report: original recorded three unwounded deer and absence of scavengers; uninitialled correction struck out scavenger detail and changed 'three' to 'several'. Isolde retrieved her own collected suspicious reports and returned to stand close beside Cassian. Her summary records three unwounded deer but omits scavenger observation; timing and provenance remain uncertain. Cassian explained that Edric Varrick, whose friendship with Cassian's father dates to service, had mentioned Isolde's promotion and recommended Cassian for rehabilitation without first asking Isolde. Isolde scowled, crossed her arms, turned to face taller Cassian and said she did not write to his father; asked why Edric, who knows her fighting style, had not volunteered himself. Cassian replied that Edric's retirement and lack of current instructional posting may have been factors but admitted he had not asked and should not presume Edric's motives. He acknowledged that Isolde should have been consulted and said he would not pretend the arrangement was fair merely because he could do the work. They remain close in the records office; awaiting Isolde's response.
