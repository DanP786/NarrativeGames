# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Blackthorn Bastion mess hall, dinner
Previous day Fen admitted before Harris that she falsely named Garran as possible father to get his attention, denied any encounter since last winter; pregnancy and paternity remain medically unconfirmed to Isolde. Harris cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Cassian showed Isolde an altered northern charcoal-pit patrol-office master report: three unwounded deer changed to several, absence of scavengers struck, no initials; provenance unknown, Cassian investigating. Other oddities: birds leaving western roosts and a figure at eastern treeline. Cassian said Edric recommended him as Isolde's trainer. She cautioned him to obey Harris's shoulder restrictions. Garran felt hopeful. Garran valued her advice and said he felt they were on the same side. She teased him for sentimentality and asked about his assignment; Garran described preliminary surveys and a mixed but manageable reception in the workshop. Their banter over the reserved chair and 'sweetheart' turned flirtatious; Garran reassured her he would never expose their private intimacy in public. Isolde sincerely told him she was proud he had approached Calder for himself and would be a great sergeant. Deeply touched, Garran kissed her knuckles, said he loved her, and accidentally called her a prospective 'sergeant's wife,' then embarrassedly asked to pretend he was talking about the weather. Isolde privately felt yearning rather than her expected panic at the marriage image; she raised an eyebrow and played along: 'I agree it has been threatening to rain all day.' Garran takes the offered retreat without assuming she has agreed to marriage, but the moment leaves him quietly hopeful. Isolde remains on restricted recovery status, not full duty. Both previous night's honest questions answered.
