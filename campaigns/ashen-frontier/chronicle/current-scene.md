Previous day Fen admitted before Harris that she falsely named Garran as possible father to get his attention, denied any encounter since last winter; pregnancy and paternity remain medically unconfirmed to Isolde. Harris cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Garran takes the distinction seriously, recognizing the value of listening before disciplining and using safety rather than pride as the foundation of authority. Isolde remains on restricted recovery status, not full duty. Both previous night's honest questions answered.
