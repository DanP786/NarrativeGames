# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Isolde's quarters, evening
Yesterday Isolde and Garran shared a rare Copper Hart dinner interrupted by Elara Fen's claim of abdominal pain and pregnancy by Garran. Midwife Bell found signs consistent with pregnancy but could not confirm pregnancy, gestational age or paternity; Edwin transported Elara to Physician Harris. Garran maintains he has not slept with Elara since last winter. Isolde earlier privately doubted due to past absences, but has now told him she believes him; do not assume more of her private thoughts. This morning Harris cleared Isolde for supervised, restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Two days earlier Isolde won two of three training rounds against Cassian; today she won first controlled exchange, Cassian won second, and he called a water break before the third; third outcome unestablished. At evening meal Garran saved her stew and bread, joked about Cassian and Elara, and arranged dinner tomorrow in her room; Isolde laughed aloud, startling Wardens, then silenced a staring private with an icy look. Garran accompanied her to her quarters hand in hand. Outside her door they agreed on one difficult question each, then leaving the subject outside. Isolde asked if Garran heard more of Fen's pregnancy; he said no, reiterated he had not slept with Fen since last winter, and asked if she still believed him. Isolde teased he was obsessed with her, mentioned Fen bringing food after Garran saw Cassian touch her cheek, and said she believed Garran's account that nothing happened. Garran expressed relief. Isolde then invited Garran in for a teasingly phrased 'thorough inspection' after training and pulled him backwards into her quarters. Garran entered, shut the door, and kissed her, affectionate and mindful of Harris's restrictions. No new medical report or confirmed paternity exists. Earlier unusual patrol reports: unwounded dead deer at northern charcoal pits, birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; not confirmed connected. Harris's restrictions remain in force.
