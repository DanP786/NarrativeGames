# Current scene — Forward command position overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, mid-afternoon 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged reconnaissance. Isolde continued waiting and resting seated against the tree while Calder sent a six-Warden supported entry party through the eastern breach to clear the main building's ground floor, with explicit orders not to descend underground. After a tense interval the team signalled the ground floor clear of visible hostiles but showed signs of recent human occupation: ash in a cold hearth and a dropped length of clean cord. They located an iron hatch leading below, secured from the upper side by a recently fitted chain and padlock; the scouts did not touch or open it. No humans were found upstairs. Calder has ordered them to hold and report, not force the hatch. The Blight thread remains stationary from Isolde's last view; occasional scraping beneath western outbuilding remains unexplained. Isolde remains injured, no armour, sword at side, barred from entry and Severance. Await action.
