# Current scene — Silent pine hollow, midmorning
Tier: charged. Isolde ordered Hester Brann, Nera Holt, and Davin Sorel to hold position, raised her shield and advanced alone toward the suspended deer, sword ready to sever its luminous Blight connection. The three Wardens held a defensive line, Nera with arrow drawn. At closer range Isolde sees the pale tether branching into several finer filaments inside the carcass, all leading beneath a fallen pine; it is taut and responsive, and a subtle tug travels toward her as she nears. The dead deer's head tracks her movement and its jaw opens without a sound. The link is within sword reach if she commits to a Severance strike; no strike has yet been made and the hidden source has not emerged. Pell Rusk is still away alerting Calder's column. Await Isolde's next action.
