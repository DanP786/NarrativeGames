# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, dusk, excavation battle outside Fort Aster. Isolde shouted 'Break!' and split away from Evren as controller lunged, drawing its attention from opposite sides. Despite pronounced tremor, double vision and severe headache from four recent Severance uses, Isolde struck controller's exposed rear leg/joint with ordinary steel, damaging its support but not severing Blight bindings. Evren exploited stagger to wound opposite flank; infantry pressed it away from lower passage. Controller remains alive, with one thick binding to red apparatus; its collapse threatens timber support and passage roof. Isolde near its damaged leg, in close danger, physically impaired. Brenn and two soldiers below searching for imprisoned captives behind iron doors; rescued woman on western platform. Archers and diversion troops engaged; one infantryman serious leg injury. Garran and Cassian not established to know miscarriage.
