# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, afternoon, Blackthorn Bastion command hall. Isolde argued that sixty troops at Station Seventeen would not have sufficed against the Blight threat that damaged her Severance; urged sappers, explosives, possible baited demolition trap and planning for multiple contingencies rather than understrength entry. Calder and Evren take the warning seriously: begin contingency planning for controlled demolition at a surveyed chokepoint, withdrawal routes and reserve support, but avoid assuming explosives can kill bound Blight or that the terrain is suitable until assessed. Calder intends to consult engineering sergeant Garran Vale professionally, despite his earlier disciplinary breach; this does not grant him private access to Isolde. Revised Frontier Command orders still require inspection of abandoned emberstone survey station near Greywatch; unknown enemy numbers and hidden routes make approach hazardous. Isolde fighting fit for sword combat, Severance medically restricted and resting; conditional operational attachment under Evren, no restored Warden rank. Garran knows pregnancy announcement but not miscarriage. Secret Caladorn High Command trade unknown to Isolde.
