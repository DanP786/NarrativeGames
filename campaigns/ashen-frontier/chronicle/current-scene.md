# Current scene — Cinderhouse, evening
Tier: charged. Isolde entered Cinderhouse before dawn expedition. Garran made barbed greeting; Leif offered empty chair; Tamsin publicly accused Isolde of abandoning Greywatch dead. Isolde quietly noted Tamsin had been issued leave; Tamsin explained returning early from compassionate leave, Calder knows and rostered her. Isolde told her “Be prepared for tomorrow” and walked to Leif. Leif showed revised orders diverting tomorrow's expedition east to abandoned Survey Station Seventeen, near Greywatch. Isolde joked about Leif liking intimidating women, said she goes where ordered; Leif disputed unquestioning trust. Isolde explained fifteen died at Greywatch, nine more would have without her, and she intends to prevent more losses. Leif acknowledged this and invited her to eat. Isolde declined, stood, told him to go. After he headed for kitchens she looked at Garran. Garran noticed her glance across the Cinderhouse, stopped his tankard demonstration, exchanged a pointed look and offered a dry invitation without approaching; waiting for Isolde's choice.
