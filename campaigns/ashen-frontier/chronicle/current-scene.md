# Current scene — S03
Winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, late afternoon, Vale family sitting room. After joking with Garran and his family about his failed stove repair, Isolde gives a strained smile at Mara Vale's warm suggestion she will fit into the family, and replies, 'Thank you Mrs Vale.' Garran and Isolde remain together, family visit is not a marriage promise. Harris has not cleared Severance retesting or unrestricted duty; northern wall assessment, altered charcoal-pit records and Elara pregnancy/paternity unresolved.
