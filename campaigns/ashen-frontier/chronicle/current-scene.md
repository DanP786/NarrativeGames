# Current scene — S03
Evening, day after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, Vale family orchard. Isolde and Garran spent the day working on the storm-damaged southern orchard retaining wall, with Tobin helping intermittently. They traced the altered runoff, cleared a blocked shallow ditch, marked a drainage route and sorted stable stones; the foundation still needs rebuilding with materials and thawed ground, so the wall is not fully repaired. Garran directed technical work while taking Isolde's observations seriously. Isolde's recovering endurance required occasional rests after her morning spar, but she enjoyed useful physical work. At dusk they are muddy, cold and tired, and the Vale house windows glow nearby; family supper awaits. Garran's previously injured shoulder is cold-stiff but not painful. Isolde has blonde dark-rooted hair in two braids. Garran loves her but has not proposed or resolved their marriage disagreement; her private grief over marriage/home/children remains unknown to him in full. No unrestricted duty or Severance retest clearance; permanent Ember loss unconfirmed. Northern wall assessment for Calder, altered charcoal-pit records and Elara pregnancy/paternity unresolved.
