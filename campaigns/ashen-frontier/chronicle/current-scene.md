# Current scene — Cinderhouse, evening
Tier: charged. Isolde entered the crowded common hall before dawn expedition. Garran made a barbed greeting; Leif silently offered an empty chair; Tamsin publicly accused Isolde of leaving people behind at Greywatch. Isolde walked straight to Tamsin and, without raising her voice or seeking to embarrass her, said: “You were issued leave 2 weeks ago Kest. Yet you sit around here drinking”. Tamsin remains seated and reacts with defensive anger; she has not yet answered Isolde's question about her leave in a way that establishes formal duty status. Await Isolde's response.
