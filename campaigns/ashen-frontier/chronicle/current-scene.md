# Current scene — At the central mass, lower chamber beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic. Isolde deliberately ceased bracing her boots against the bending iron bracket and allowed the thick root to carry her to the immense pulsing Blight/emberstone mass, conserving her remaining strength and Severance. The root hauled her across the chamber floor and lifted her upright against a fleshy, emberstone-studded surface, holding her badly injured ribs and waist painfully but easing enough to permit shallow breaths. At close range Isolde can see a dark vertical fissure in the mass and multiple luminous binding seams, including the seam of the root holding her; the mass pulses with neurological pain and seems to be positioning her beside the fissure, purpose unknown. She retains the rusted short knife in her right hand, unarmoured, conscious, without her own sword; no Severance used. Mara Venn fled toward the surface carrying Isolde's order for all Garran Vale's explosives; delivery not confirmed. Wardens above search, no detonation authorized. Await action.
