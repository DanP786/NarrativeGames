# Current scene — Cinderhouse, evening
Tier: charged. Isolde entered Cinderhouse before dawn expedition. Garran made barbed greeting; Leif offered empty chair; Tamsin publicly accused Isolde of abandoning Greywatch dead. Isolde quietly noted Tamsin had been issued leave. Tamsin explained she returned early from compassionate leave after burying Oren, Calder knows and has her on tomorrow's roster. Isolde said “Be prepared for tomorrow” and left without argument. At Leif's table she asked about his map. Leif disclosed tomorrow's route altered from northward sweep to eastern abandoned Survey Station Seventeen. Isolde joked without smiling, “Thought you liked intimidating women,” then sat and suggested intelligence, supplies, or Calder wanting Severance protection; “I go where I'm told.” Leif teased back, challenged the blind-duty logic without denying orders, and noted survey station lies uncomfortably near Greywatch's approaches. He suspects omissions in revised reports, not proven conspiracy. Await Isolde.
