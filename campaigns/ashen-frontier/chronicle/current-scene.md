# Current scene — S03
Near midday, two days after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, winter market around old clocktower. Isolde browsed the stalls after helping Mara select and purchase a silver winter-branch brooch with garnet berries. The intimate accessory she consensually tried has settled comfortably, though she remains privately aware of it. Garran is nearby browsing, Mara has brooch, Seren at books, Tobin near food.
