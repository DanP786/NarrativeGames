# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, morning, Cassian's temporary office
Isolde spent the previous late night with Garran Vale. After a warm intimate evening they discussed marriage; she said she would marry him tomorrow, but he was not ready to promise marriage immediately. She felt rejected and compared his indefinite readiness to Cassian Rook's old promises of a future without commitment. Garran acknowledged the hurt without making a guilt-driven proposal. Isolde left his quarters tearful, wearing his shirt and taking her other clothes; her last words apologized for having made him unsure about her. He said his uncertainty was not all hers to carry. Their marriage-readiness difference remains unresolved. Next morning Isolde rose early, took breakfast to eat on the go, and headed to her patrol-report office, passing Cassian's temporary office. Cassian noticed her and asked about her early arrival and breakfast. Isolde said her bread would probably last until supper, asked what he had found, and walked toward his office. Cassian presented a preliminary comparison of northern patrol-report copies: the altered charcoal-pit master has 'three unwounded deer' changed to 'several' and the absence of scavengers struck without initials; a register confirms an earlier field transcription was received but the transcription itself is not in its expected place. No culprit or explanation established. Isolde called Cassian a mother hen when he pressed her to eat, put down her remaining bread and tea, and examined the papers. The register shows receipt but no outgoing notation. Isolde suggested the report may simply have been updated and that recurrence matters; Cassian agreed to compare similar reports before assuming tampering. Isolde said she had found no corrections in the reports she reviewed, but objected that cutting her hours would reduce how many she could process. Cassian proposed dividing work and deferring medical limits to Harris. Isolde told Cassian that Garran's marriage conditions felt like impossible demands to never argue, similar to Cassian's former delays. She said she had told Garran no one can guarantee his conditions, feared waiting another ten years, and asked Cassian whether she was being unreasonable or was the problem. Cassian said wanting clarity and commitment was not unreasonable, reflected on his own failure to commit, and said it was tempting to think being easier to love would change things. Isolde directly asked, 'You really think I'm difficult to love?' Cassian immediately clarified that he did not mean that; his own fear and ambition had been the barrier, and he did love her, without claiming to know Garran's feelings or inviting renewed romance. Isolde remains on restricted supervised rehabilitation status under Harris, not full duty. Other unlinked oddities include birds leaving western roosts and a figure at the eastern treeline. Garran has trial supervision of northern retaining wall repairs and a written assessment for Calder, not an immediate promotion; shoulder restrictions apply. Fen previously admitted falsely naming Garran as possible father for attention, denied intimacy since last winter; pregnancy and paternity unconfirmed to Isolde.
