# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, outside Isolde's quarters, evening
Yesterday Isolde and Garran shared a rare Copper Hart dinner interrupted by Elara Fen's claim of abdominal pain and pregnancy by Garran. Midwife Bell found signs consistent with pregnancy but could not confirm pregnancy, gestational age or paternity; Edwin transported Elara to Physician Harris. Garran maintains he has not slept with Elara since last winter; Isolde privately doubts this due to past absences, unknown to him. This morning Harris cleared Isolde for supervised, restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Two days earlier Isolde won two of three training rounds against Cassian; her anger at his arrival weeks ago has considerably eased. Today she won first controlled exchange, Cassian won second, and he called a water break before the third; third outcome remains unestablished. At evening meal Garran saved her stew and bread, joked about Cassian and Elara, and arranged dinner tomorrow in her room; Isolde laughed aloud, startling Wardens, then silenced a staring private with an icy look. Garran accompanied her to her quarters hand in hand. Outside her door, Isolde wrapped her arms around his neck and proposed one difficult question each, to be left outside before an intimate evening. Isolde asked whether Garran had heard anything more about Fen's pregnancy. Garran said he had received no word from Fen or Harris, and still did not know whether she was pregnant or who the father was; he reiterated he had not slept with her since last winter. He then asked Isolde his reciprocal difficult question: whether she still believed him about that. Awaiting Isolde's answer. No new medical report or confirmed paternity exists. Earlier unusual patrol reports: unwounded dead deer at northern charcoal pits, birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; not confirmed connected. Harris's restrictions remain in force.
