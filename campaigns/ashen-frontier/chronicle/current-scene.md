# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, late afternoon, King Alaric III's private audience chamber in Eldorian royal palace. Isolde, wearing a fitted Alaric-commissioned court gown because pregnancy dresses are painful, chose to see the king for first time since early miscarriage. She reports coordinated Blight strikes on defensive infrastructure, Severance counterstrategy, and Evren preparing to ride to Fort Aster. She argues she is better suited to the strike: Evren's command position is more strategically important, while her rank was revoked. Alaric is challenged by her logic but is wary of restoring military autonomy; he asks whether she seeks only this single operation or a lasting return to military service, and what medical assurances she can offer. No permission yet granted. Evren's force prepares for dawn; no deployment yet. Garran and Cassian have not been established to know of miscarriage.
