# Current scene — Medical tent at expedition night camp, 18 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged aftermath. Isolde smiled and teased Garran that he must have thought she was trying to blow up a fortress when she sent Nera Holt to request his largest explosive. Garran recalled being alarmed by the demand and sending the charge immediately; he answered with affectionate, rueful humour, noting that the blast had nearly killed Isolde too. He remains seated at her cot holding her hand. Medic Harris is present, discouraging Isolde from straining or leaving. Isolde remains on cot with severe headache, dizziness, light sensitivity, bandaged temple, dressed palm, painful bound ribs (three suspected cracked) and weakness after three Severances and blast. Company camped at night en route to Survey Station Seventeen; Isolde has not yet reported to Captain Calder. Await response.
