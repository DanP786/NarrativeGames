# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, night, Isolde's quarters
Isolde Varrick and Garran Vale remain at odds after she said she would marry him tomorrow but he was not ready, citing their pattern of painful arguments and threats to leave. She fears indefinite waiting and feeling unworthy; she left his quarters tearful the previous night, and has avoided him today. In the morning Cassian Rook showed Isolde altered northern charcoal-pit paperwork: 'three unwounded deer' changed to 'several', absence of scavengers struck without initials, and original field transcription registered received but missing without outgoing notation. No culprit or explanation established. Cassian handles registers and authorization trail; Isolde reviews patrol reports for patterns. Other unlinked oddities: birds leaving western roosts, a figure at eastern treeline. Isolde confided her marriage troubles to Cassian, her former partner; he admitted regretting his failure to marry her but not seeking to replace Garran. She left upset, then spent all day checking patrol reports and skipped lunch to avoid Garran; no new confirmed alteration pattern found. Her injured hand is stiff and aching; Harris still limits her to restricted supervised rehabilitation. That evening Cassian delivered inconclusive register notes. They reconciled somewhat conversationally; Isolde apologized for confiding, Cassian said he still cares but has moved forward. He has dated since their breakup and has no sweetheart at his northern posting. Isolde teased him by proposing Elara Fen as a match, adding Fen supposedly has a baby on the way, 'A ready made family raring to go.' Cassian knows Isolde claims Fen is expecting, but has no independent confirmation and does not know Fen's admission to Harris that she falsely named Garran as possible father for attention and denied intimacy since last winter. Pregnancy and paternity unconfirmed to Isolde. Isolde and Cassian shared supper and playful banter. Elara Fen approached, incorrectly assuming Isolde and Garran had broken up from seeing Isolde dining with Cassian and apart from Garran for a day. Isolde rebuked her intrusive speculation and refused to discuss her private relationship; Elara withdrew offended. Cassian offered to counter gossip but Isolde declined, noting conflicting rumours about Elara and Garran. After supper Isolde thanked Cassian and wished him goodnight, saying she would see him at training tomorrow. She then walked alone to her own quarters and reached her door. No encounter with Garran has occurred today. Garran has trial supervision of northern retaining wall repairs and written assessment for Calder, not immediate promotion; shoulder restrictions apply.
