# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Isolde's quarters at Blackthorn Bastion, later evening
Earlier today Fen admitted before Harris that she named Garran as possible father merely to get his attention. Fen affirmed no sexual encounter with Garran since last winter and denied any forced encounter; pregnancy and paternity have not been medically confirmed to Isolde. Isolde told Garran; they shared relief and affection. Harris has cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Cassian showed Isolde an altered northern charcoal-pit patrol-office master report: original recorded three unwounded deer and absence of scavengers; uninitialled correction struck out scavenger detail and changed 'three' to 'several'. Isolde retrieved her own suspicious reports; uninitialled changes' provenance uncertain. Cassian accepted responsibility for investigating the altered report. Cassian revealed Edric recommended him for Isolde's rehab and speculated Edric might question Garran's stalled rank; Cassian expressed concern about unverified rumours Garran fathered another woman's child, unaware Fen recanted before Harris. Isolde and Cassian discussed his past failure to commit. Isolde returned to Garran in the engineers' workshop, initiated consensual private intimacy in a cupboard (non-graphic), then rebuked an impatient private's tone. They agreed to ask each other one honest question every night. Isolde overheard Garran defend her warmly to a workshop soldier, without Garran knowing she heard. At six Isolde wore a striking burgundy dress once described as 'husband-catching', unbraided blonde hair with dark roots to waist; Garran brought supper and wine. For his nightly question he asked whether she came to him from happiness or fear about Fen; she admitted some reassurance motive but also wanted him. For hers she asked if Edric had interrogated his suitability while she was unconscious. Garran admitted Edric thanked him for rescue but questioned eight years as corporal versus her fast rise, his ambitions and discipline. Isolde explained Cassian hinted at this based on letters with Edric. Isolde reassured Garran rank doesn't matter to her; she cares how he treats her. Garran confessed ambition to make sergeant and chief sapper for himself. Isolde kissed him, said she knows his worth, and promised to praise him. Isolde teasingly told Garran he was 'beneath' her; he carried her carefully to bed. They shared consensual lovemaking off-page; at Garran's playful request she kept the burgundy dress on. Afterwards Garran helped Isolde back to the table, seated her on his lap and put a fork in her hand to finish supper and apple tart. Garran praised her with 'good girl'; she audibly moaned. When he asked if she liked being called that, Isolde nodded and said 'uhuh', visibly eager. Now Isolde leaned against his shoulder, hand on his chest. Garran recognised with playful satisfaction that affectionate praise could leave his usually sharp-tongued lover flustered, and gently teased her with the phrase while continuing to hold her warmly; no coercion. They remain in his chair by the supper table, fire low, both nightly questions answered. Other unusual reports: birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; no confirmed connection.
