# Current scene — Entrance to lower chamber beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic. As Mara Venn attempted to hold Isolde back from the descending root, Isolde ordered her to reach the surface and tell the Wardens, “Varrick needs all the explosives Vale's got.” Mara reluctantly released Isolde, repeated the message, and fled uphill through the damaged service passage toward the surface. She is free of her wrist tether but has not yet reached the Wardens; delivery of the message is unconfirmed. Isolde remains alone at the lower-chamber entrance, trapped by the thick root around her injured ribs, barely breathing, conscious and gripping the rusted surveyor's knife. The root dragged her fully across the threshold, exposing an immense pulsating Blight/stone mass webbed in luminous bindings; no Severance used. Wardens above continue searching without knowing Mara's message yet, and demolition remains unauthorized. Await Isolde's next action.
