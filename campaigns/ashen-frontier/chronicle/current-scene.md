# Current scene — Lower chamber beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic. As the thick root hauled Isolde toward the immense pulsing Blight/emberstone mass, she scraped and dug her boots against the stone to slow its pull. Her heels found purchase in a broken iron survey-track bracket embedded in the floor; this halted her forward movement for several seconds, though the root tightened painfully against her damaged ribs and the bracket began to bend under strain. She remains conscious, barely breathing, unarmoured, holding the rusted surveyor's knife in her right hand, and has not used Severance. The root is still trying to draw her toward the central mass; the brace will not hold indefinitely. Mara Venn fled uphill with Isolde's order for all Garran Vale's explosives, but delivery is not yet confirmed. Aboveground Wardens are searching, no detonation authorized. Await action.
