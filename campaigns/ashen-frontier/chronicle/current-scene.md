# Current scene — Forward command position overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, dusk 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged quiet exchange. Isolde drank Harris's bitter tea and reported her headache almost gone but her ribs still intensely painful. She ruefully admitted she should have jumped for cover when Garran's explosive went off, and said she must be useful or might as well return to the bastion. Harris acknowledged her usefulness in spotting the thread and warning of scouting roots, but insisted usefulness does not require self-destruction; she cautioned that less headache does not mean concussion-like symptoms or cracked ribs are healed, and offered to arrange a watch relief if Isolde worsens. Isolde remains wrapped in blankets with food and tea, watching the luminous thread from the command tree. Firelit perimeter monitors cracks near western outbuilding; thread remains apparently still. No armour, sword at side, barred from entry and Severance. Await response.
