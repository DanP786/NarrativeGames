# Current scene — Medical tent at expedition night camp, 18 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged aftermath. Isolde told Garran that through her Ember the creature looked almost like ordinary Blight, but severing its bonds felt like cutting thick cord rather than the usual single hair. Garran, who saw the carcass but not the living creature, was disturbed by the distinction and suggested it might have been bound together deliberately; he did not assert this as fact. Medic Harris heard the exchange and reminded Isolde to rest, while advising that Calder should hear the exact comparison when she reports. Garran is seated beside Isolde holding her hand; Harris nearby. Isolde remains on cot, severely headache-stricken and dizzy, with painful ribs (three suspected cracked), bandaged temple, dressed palm, light sensitivity and weakness after three Severances and blast. Company camped at night en route to Survey Station Seventeen; Isolde has not yet reported to Captain Calder. Await her response.
