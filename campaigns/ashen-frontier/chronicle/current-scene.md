# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, afternoon, Blackthorn Bastion north courtyard. Isolde dismounted upon returning with Evren and Eldorian escort and adopted her familiar unreadable Warden-sergeant stoicism, a demeanour well known to Blackthorn soldiers but unfamiliar to Evren. He noticed the change and watched without challenging her in public. Nearby Wardens reacted with recognition and guarded curiosity; formal reception at command hall awaits. Isolde is fighting fit with ordinary swordsmanship after weeks of recovery, but has not used Severance since Fort Aster and remains medically restricted from it. Her conditional Eldorian operational attachment persists; former Caladorn rank not restored. Blight evidence linked Fort Aster apparatus to Blackthorn/Greywatch, prompting joint deployment. Garran and Cassian not established to know miscarriage. Secret Caladorn High Command trade unknown to Isolde.
