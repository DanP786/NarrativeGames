# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, morning, Cassian's temporary office
Isolde spent the previous late night with Garran Vale. After a warm intimate evening they discussed marriage; she said she would marry him tomorrow, but he was not ready to promise marriage immediately. She felt rejected and compared his indefinite readiness to Cassian Rook's old promises of a future without commitment. Garran acknowledged the hurt without making a guilt-driven proposal. Isolde left his quarters tearful, wearing his shirt and taking her other clothes; her last words apologized for having made him unsure about her. He said his uncertainty was not all hers to carry. Their marriage-readiness difference remains unresolved. Next morning Isolde rose early, took breakfast on the go, and went to the patrol office, passing Cassian's temporary office. Cassian invited her in and showed a northern charcoal-pit patrol-report master: 'three unwounded deer' changed to 'several', absence of scavengers struck without initials, and original field transcription registered as received but missing from expected place, with no outgoing notation. No culprit or explanation established. Isolde suggested routine updating might explain it and recurrence matters; she has found no corrections in her reviewed reports. Cassian will handle registers/transcriptions/authorisations, Isolde her assigned reports, subject to Harris's medical restrictions. When Cassian noticed her drive to stay busy, Isolde confided that marriage talk with Garran had gone as badly as with Cassian years before. Garran offered conditions that felt impossible, especially never arguing; she fears indefinite waiting despite Garran pursuing her for four years. Cassian acknowledged his old pattern of deferring commitment for career and his failure to choose a life with Isolde; he told her she was never difficult for him to love, but he was difficult to build a life with. Isolde asked directly, 'Do you regret not marrying me?' Cassian answered that he regrets failing to choose her when he had the chance and losing their possible life together, while acknowledging he cannot know whether marriage at that age would have worked and not treating regret as a present claim on her. Isolde remains on restricted supervised rehabilitation status under Harris, not full duty. Other unlinked oddities include birds leaving western roosts and a figure at the eastern treeline. Garran has trial supervision of northern retaining wall repairs and a written assessment for Calder, not an immediate promotion; shoulder restrictions apply. Fen previously admitted falsely naming Garran as possible father for attention, denied intimacy since last winter; pregnancy and paternity unconfirmed to Isolde.
