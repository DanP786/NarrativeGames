# Current scene — Forward line overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, midday 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged arrival. Despite medical orders, Isolde took her sword from the wagon, climbed down without armour and made her way toward the front of the halted expedition, intending to use her rare ability to perceive Blight binding threads even though she cannot safely fight. The descent and walk caused sharp rib pain and dizziness, forcing a slower pace, but she reached the forward line. From there the abandoned compound, damaged perimeter, leaning watch platform and dark service entrance are visible. On concentrating, Isolde perceives a faint, taut, unnatural luminous thread running near the base of the western perimeter wall and disappearing into the station grounds; its origin and purpose remain unknown. Captain Calder is overseeing scouts nearby, and Isolde has not yet given her report about yesterday's unusually resilient dormant Blight. Harris has not cleared Isolde for duty. Await Isolde's action.
