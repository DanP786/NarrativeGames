# Current scene — S03
Early Frostwane 312, night, Garran's quarters. After Elara Fen's unwelcome visit, Isolde and Garran argued about marriage, his prior withdrawals, and her offer to marry him tomorrow as proof of commitment. Garran recognized the offer and apologized for responding with conditions, but is still not ready to marry immediately. He did not propose or break up. Their relationship remains committed but seriously uncertain. Tomorrow restricted rehabilitation with Cassian; northern report alterations unresolved.
