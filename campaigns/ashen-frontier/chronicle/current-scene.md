# Current scene — S03
Evening, approximately one week after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, Captain Rhea Calder's office at Blackthorn Bastion. Isolde and Garran returned from seven days' leave. Calder informed Isolde she is assigned to depart at dawn on restricted expedition to inspect an abandoned emberstone survey station; Garran stays for northern retaining-wall assessment. Garran present and concerned. Isolde and Garran remain couple; Garran not ready to marry; Isolde privately believes he never will and accepts what he offers, unknown to him.
