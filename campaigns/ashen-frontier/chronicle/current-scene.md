Weeks after early miscarriage, Isolde and Evren grieve together; she seeks his comfort, accepts his fussing, refuses to see King Alaric. He intends to send to Eldorian northern posts and seek Caladorn warning channels, and asks Isolde to help frame the tactical instructions. Isolde retains no formal rank/field clearance. The precise coordination mechanism at Greywold remains unproven. Garran and Cassian have not been established to know of miscarriage.
