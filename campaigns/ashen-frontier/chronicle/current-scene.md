# Current scene — S03
Morning after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, behind Vale family stables on a snowy practice patch. Tobin asked whether Wardens ever stop feeling fear facing Blight. Isolde told him Blight are terrifying, that recruits often freeze or flee, but her Severance once gave her certainty she could kill them: she could see connecting threads between Blight and their source, and cutting a thread weakened them, often creating a momentary opening; the creatures are relentless and leave no time to be afraid. Tobin listens seriously, recognizing the difference between training and true combat and the loss implied by her past-tense description of Severance. He remains armed with an ashwood practice sword; spar has not begun. Garran is elsewhere inspecting the damaged orchard wall and has not heard this conversation. Isolde rested well, long blonde dark-rooted hair in two braids. Garran loves her but has not proposed or resolved marriage disagreement; her private grief over marriage/home/children remains unknown to him in full. No unrestricted duty or Severance retest clearance; permanent Ember loss unconfirmed. Northern wall assessment, altered charcoal-pit records and Elara pregnancy/paternity unresolved.
