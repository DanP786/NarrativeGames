# Current scene — S03
Late evening, three days after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, Fox and Lantern tavern. Isolde joined Garran's childhood friends dancing to lively music after drinking whisky over evening. Garran invited her to dance with exaggerated bow; she offered her hand with warm smile. He took opportunity to draw her close, hand at waist/back, affectionate and flirtatious while dancing; she did not object. Nell, Bram and other friends dance nearby, music lively. No forced degree of intoxication. Earlier Isolde said with strained smile 'I see no point in dreaming of a different life. With or without severance I’m a soldier'; Garran knows she once dreamed of a family with Cassian and later offered to marry him, and cannot know her current private grief. Her brothers: one tends horses at parents' barn, another bakes pastries king reportedly enjoyed. Livia Harrow probed intrusively about marriage; Isolde publicly said 'We’re happy as we are... Choosing someone every day isn’t determined by marriage' but privately disbelieves and mourns lost marriage/home/children/family hopes; Garran cannot know. Garran rebuked Livia and privately said he knows things aren't made right; Isolde said 'And I told you there’s nothing to make right,' kissed him, went to bar. At bar Garran acknowledged youthful reputation for flirting and careless promises; Isolde said 'No, just explains a lot' and walked back to table. Garran loves her but not ready to marry, knows she no longer expects it. His 'practically a married man' hinge joke privately stung her, unknown to him. She overheard Mara press Garran about settling down, neither knows she overheard. Isolde owns decorative Master Heddon hinge (3.5 silver), joking custody: she controls use and holds two days/week, Garran five, currently has it wrapped. Garran bought brass clockwork bird (four copper); Mara bought winter-branch brooch. Seven days approved leave, staying longer than three days at Vale home. Orchard wall rebuilding pending; Garran shoulder cold-stiff not painful. No unrestricted duty or Severance retest clearance; permanent Ember loss unconfirmed. Northern wall assessment for Calder, altered charcoal-pit records and Elara pregnancy/paternity unresolved.
