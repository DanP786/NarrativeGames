# Current scene — S03
Early Deepfrost 312, late afternoon, covered barracks passage at Blackthorn Bastion. One month after the unresolved marriage dispute, Isolde's recovery training with Cassian has improved her strength and stamina; unrestricted clearance remains unestablished. She privately believes she will never marry and has been accommodating Garran. After Garran acknowledged his moving demands and said she should not need to suppress herself to make him happy, Isolde offered a strained smile and said, 'I'll try harder.' Garran visibly recognized this as another concession, declined to accept a promise of further effort, apologized, and said he would show change through his conduct rather than ask her to trust another speech. He did not learn her private conclusion about marriage. They remain together; northern records, retaining-wall assessment and Elara pregnancy/paternity remain unresolved.
