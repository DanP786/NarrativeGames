# Current scene — S03
Evening, three days after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, Fox and Lantern tavern. Isolde stands by reunion table with whisky; Garran seated nearby; Nell, Bram, Livia, Denny present. Nell asked about Isolde's childhood and other dreams; Isolde answered 'Being a warden is my duty. There is nothing else,' smiling, and explained her brothers never took to fighting: one tends horses at their parents' barn, another is a baker whose pastries the king reportedly once tried and loved. Their names and precise locations not established. Nell and Bram interested, Garran listens, no assumption Isolde has no private dreams beyond what she said aloud. Earlier at bar Garran acknowledged youthful reputation for flirting and careless promises; Isolde said 'No, just explains a lot' and walked back to table. Garran somewhat unsettled but did not press. Livia asked intrusive marriage question; Isolde publicly answered 'We’re happy as we are... Choosing someone every day isn’t determined by marriage' but privately disbelieves and mourns marriage/home/children/family hopes; Garran cannot know. Garran rebuked Livia and privately said he knows things aren't made right; Isolde said 'And I told you there’s nothing to make right,' kissed him, went to bar. Garran loves her but not ready to marry, knows she no longer expects it. His 'practically a married man' hinge joke privately stung her, unknown to him. She overheard Mara press Garran about settling down, neither knows she overheard. Isolde owns decorative Master Heddon hinge (3.5 silver), joking custody: she controls use and holds two days/week, Garran five, currently has it wrapped. Garran bought brass clockwork bird (four copper); Mara bought winter-branch brooch. Seven days approved leave, staying longer than three days at Vale home. Orchard wall rebuilding pending; Garran shoulder cold-stiff not painful. No unrestricted duty or Severance retest clearance; permanent Ember loss unconfirmed.
