# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, morning on northern Eldorian road en route to Fort Aster. Isolde, wearing fine Eldorian blue field armour, twin blonde braids and superb sword, travels with Evren and Captain Halden's strike force under limited royal attachment and Anwen's cautious medical clearance. The column encountered southbound refugees from villages beyond fallen Greywold. Isolde agreed to speak and dismounted; Evren dismounted with her while Halden secured the road. A shaken refugee woman reports coordinated Blight attacks and people taken alive, not merely killed; she describes one larger stationary creature while smaller organisms gathered captives. These are eyewitness claims, not independently confirmed. Isolde is standing before the woman, who awaits questions. Fort Aster and controller strike remain urgent. Garran and Cassian have not been established to know of miscarriage.
