# Current scene — Forward line overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, midday 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged reconnaissance. Responding to Captain Calder, Isolde studied the faint luminous Blight thread along the western wall. She reported that it remains motionless and taut, disappearing through a break into the station, but its direction of flow cannot be determined visually from this vantage: it could lead inward or outward. Looking harder makes her lingering headache pulse, without revealing an origin. Calder treated the lack of movement as no guarantee of safety, maintained the hold on scouts, and asked whether Isolde can identify any other threads from their present position without approaching. Isolde remains wounded, without armour, sword in hand; Harris has not cleared her. Await action.
