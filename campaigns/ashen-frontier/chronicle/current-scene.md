# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, morning, corridor outside Cassian's temporary office
Isolde spent the previous late night with Garran Vale. After a warm intimate evening they discussed marriage; she said she would marry him tomorrow, but he was not ready to promise marriage immediately. She felt rejected and compared his indefinite readiness to Cassian Rook's old promises of a future without commitment. Garran acknowledged the hurt without making a guilt-driven proposal. Isolde left his quarters tearful, wearing his shirt and taking her other clothes; her last words apologized for having made him unsure about her. He said his uncertainty was not all hers to carry. Their marriage-readiness difference remains unresolved. Next morning Isolde rose early, took breakfast on the go, and went to the patrol office, passing Cassian's temporary office. Cassian invited her in and showed a northern charcoal-pit patrol-report master: 'three unwounded deer' changed to 'several', absence of scavengers struck without initials, and original field transcription registered as received but missing from expected place, with no outgoing notation. No culprit or explanation established. Isolde suggested routine updating might explain it and recurrence matters; she has found no corrections in her reviewed reports. Cassian will handle registers/transcriptions/authorisations, Isolde her assigned reports, subject to Harris's medical restrictions. When Cassian noticed her drive to stay busy, Isolde confided that marriage talk with Garran had gone as badly as with Cassian years before. Garran offered conditions that felt impossible, especially never arguing; she fears indefinite waiting despite Garran pursuing her for four years. Cassian acknowledged his old pattern of deferring commitment for career and his failure to choose a life with Isolde; he told her she was never difficult for him to love, but he was difficult to build a life with. Asked if he regretted not marrying her, Cassian admitted he did, while not claiming marriage would certainly have succeeded. Isolde said she had hoped he would say no so she could believe Garran's unreadiness and her unwillingness to wait were compatible with a right decision. Cassian said his regret does not mean Isolde was wrong to leave. Isolde says she doesn't want to leave Garran but cannot wait indefinitely or risk pressuring a fear-driven proposal. Cassian said she could state a limit without issuing an ultimatum. Isolde clarified that not threatening to leave during arguments is on Garran's list, that their difficult arguments are a pattern, and that she feels judged as not marriage-worthy; she fears Garran would be furious to know she confided in Cassian. Cassian admitted his conflicted position and promised not to repeat the conversation. After Cassian asked whether Garran literally said 'marriage worthy', Isolde concluded bitterly that she was hard to love and turned to leave. Cassian protested that she was not, said he regretted not marrying all of her, and apologized for interrogating her. Isolde answered, 'You would have married all of me but didn’t. Garran doesn’t want to marry part of me. Maybe I shouldn’t get married at all,' and left his office. Cassian did not follow or detain her. Isolde is now in the corridor, free to choose where to go. She remains on restricted supervised rehabilitation status under Harris, not full duty. Other unlinked oddities include birds leaving western roosts and a figure at the eastern treeline. Garran has trial supervision of northern retaining wall repairs and a written assessment for Calder, not an immediate promotion; shoulder restrictions apply. Fen previously admitted falsely naming Garran as possible father for attention, denied intimacy since last winter; pregnancy and paternity unconfirmed to Isolde.
