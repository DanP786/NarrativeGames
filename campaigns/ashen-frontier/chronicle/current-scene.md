# Current scene — Lower-chamber threshold beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic. Isolde answered Mara's question through gritted teeth: “312 After Ashfall.” Mara recalled entering the station in 282 After Ashfall, confirming thirty years elapsed by her account, though her own experienced time is uncertain. Shocked, she warned Isolde that the mass reacts when its pulses match a captive's breathing. The root around Isolde's badly injured ribs tightened and resumed drawing her toward the descending chamber, dragging her out of easy reach of the rusted short blade embedded in a timber support. Before the distance grew, Mara managed to kick the loose timber, dislodging the blade onto the sloped floor; it slid down beside Isolde's right boot, reachable only if she can move her hand down past the root. Mara remains tethered behind, conscious and terrified. Isolde is unarmoured, without her own sword, severely short of breath, and has not used Severance. Wardens above are searching, demolition withheld. Await action.
