# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Copper Hart tavern, early afternoon
Elara Fen claimed to Garran that she was pregnant with his child; paternity remains unverified. Garran maintains he has not slept with Elara since last winter and believes he cannot be the father; Isolde previously affirmed belief but now privately doubts after considering periods of absence. An OOC possibility of unremembered drugging is not canon and must not be presumed. Captain Calder and Physician Harris approved today's afternoon trip to the Copper Hart by cart, with minimal walking, warmth, prompt return if unwell and before gates close. Isolde has no rehabilitation with Cassian until tomorrow. Isolde grouped unusual patrol reports (unwounded dead deer at northern charcoal pits, birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline) for future comparison. Garran drove Isolde to the Copper Hart for roast beef. Elara arrived mid-meal complaining of abdominal pain and fearing for a pregnancy. Isolde summoned local midwife Mistress Bell, who examined Elara upstairs. Bell reported no immediate sign of catastrophe but urged further examination and observation by Harris; she found signs consistent with pregnancy but could not independently confirm pregnancy, gestational age or paternity. Elara wanted Garran to accompany her. Isolde suggested Garran take Elara back then return for pudding, but Marta arranged for brother Edwin to transport Elara to Harris, avoiding use of Garran and Isolde's cart. Garran went upstairs to tell Elara the plan. Isolde resumed dinner; when Garran passed through with Elara to see her into Edwin's cart, Isolde deliberately stole a potato from Garran's plate in his sight. Garran reacted with amused mock outrage and remained at the Hart with Isolde after Elara's departure. Isolde's private doubts are unknown to Garran.
