# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, dusk, excavation battle outside Fort Aster. Isolde switched to her left hand and struck the controller's already-damaged rear leg joint with ordinary steel. She crippled it, forcing the controller down, but its weight struck a timber brace and damaged the lower passage roof. Evren and infantry took advantage to pin the controller away from lower entrance; it remains alive, attached by one thick binding to red-lit apparatus. Isolde remains near fallen controller, neurologically impaired after four recent Severance uses, right hand weak and vision unstable; left hand holds sword. Brenn and two soldiers have located captives below, but damaged supports threaten extraction. Freed woman remains on western platform. Archers and diversion engaged; one infantryman serious leg injury. Garran and Cassian not established to know miscarriage.
