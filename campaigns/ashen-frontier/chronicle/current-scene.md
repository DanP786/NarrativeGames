# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Isolde's quarters at Blackthorn Bastion, early evening
Earlier today Fen admitted before Harris that she named Garran as possible father merely to get his attention. Fen affirmed no sexual encounter with Garran since last winter and denied any forced encounter; pregnancy and paternity have not been medically confirmed to Isolde. Isolde told Garran; they shared relief and affection. Harris has cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Cassian showed Isolde an altered northern charcoal-pit patrol-office master report: original recorded three unwounded deer and absence of scavengers; uninitialled correction struck out scavenger detail and changed 'three' to 'several'. Isolde retrieved her own suspicious reports; her summary records three deer but omits scavengers; provenance uncertain. Cassian accepted responsibility for investigating the altered report. Cassian revealed Edric recommended him for Isolde's rehab and speculated Edric might question Garran's stalled rank; Cassian expressed concern about unverified rumours Garran fathered another woman's child, unaware Fen recanted before Harris. Isolde and Cassian discussed his past failure to commit; Isolde teased him about an intimate trick he had lost and said she would try it with Garran. Isolde returned to Garran in the engineers' workshop, initiated consensual private intimacy in a cupboard (non-graphic), then rebuked an impatient private's tone. She and Garran agreed to ask each other one honest question every night. While Isolde lingered out of sight in the corridor, she overheard a soldier ask Garran what it felt like to be the only person Sergeant Varrick was nice to. Garran answered with a proud joke and sincere defense of Isolde, unaware she heard. At six Isolde tidied her quarters, put on the dress she had told Garran about months earlier, and unbraided her long hair to her waist. Garran arrived with supper and wine, visibly delighted by her appearance; Isolde mussed his carefully combed hair into the untidy style she prefers. While Garran set the table, Isolde bit her lip, rested a hand on the table and asked whether he got much done after she left the workshop. Garran admitted with teasing candour that he struggled to concentrate but finished the fitting and checked his work, and was clearly pleased she had sought him out. They stand close by the supper table; the one-question-each evening ritual has not begun yet. Other unusual reports: birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; no confirmed connection.
