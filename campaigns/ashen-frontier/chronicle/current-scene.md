# Current scene — S03
Early Deepfrost 312, late afternoon, covered barracks passage at Blackthorn Bastion. Garran had returned after Isolde challenged his claim that she need not earn his love, and asked whether she actually feels loved. Isolde answered, 'I know you love me.' Garran recognized the distinction between knowing he loves her and feeling secure or chosen by him. He did not treat her answer as reassurance or press for more; he acknowledged that he must demonstrate his love in how he behaves. They remain together with marriage unresolved. Isolde privately believes she will never marry and has been accommodating Garran; he does not know that conclusion. Recovery has improved with Cassian; unrestricted clearance not established. Northern records, retaining-wall assessment, Elara pregnancy/paternity remain unresolved.
