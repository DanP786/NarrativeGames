# Current scene — Medical tent at expedition camp, dawn 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged transition. Overnight passed without reported emergency. At dawn on 19 Emberwane 312 the company is breaking camp for the final approximately half-day march toward Survey Station Seventeen. Isolde wakes/rests on the cot in the medical tent. Her headache remains but is somewhat less savage after sleep; ribs remain acutely painful with movement and deep breaths; bandaged temple and dressed palm, dizziness and weakness persist. Medic Harris is preparing to transfer Isolde to a padded wagon and has forbidden combat and unnecessary exertion. A messenger outside the tent has brought word that Captain Calder expects Isolde's account when she is fit, but no immediate in-person summons. Isolde has not yet reported to Calder. Garran Vale is elsewhere in camp. Await Isolde's action at dawn.
