# Current scene — Inside fissure of central mass, lower chamber beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic irreversible change. Isolde chose to cut the luminous seam crossing the exposed underside of the suspended dark emberstone knot. She deliberately aligned the rusted surveyor's knife and invoked Severance for a brief strike; ordinary steel alone could not sever the luminous structural binding. The wrist tendril recoiled and the waist root slackened but has not released her. Other bindings remain taut, the central mass is still alive and convulsing, and something behind the opened knot is beginning to move; exact nature not yet revealed. Isolde retains the rusted knife, badly injured ribs, severe neurological strain, and precarious breathing. Mara Venn is en route to surface with explosive request, delivery unconfirmed.
