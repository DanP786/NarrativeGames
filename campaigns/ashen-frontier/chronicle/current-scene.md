# Current scene — Lower-chamber slope beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic. Isolde used the inside edge of her boot to catch the rusted surveyor's short blade by its handle and drag it uphill closer rather than bend abruptly against her injured ribs. The manoeuvre succeeded but scraped the blade against stone, drawing a reactive tightening from the root and a painful, brief loss of breath. She now has the blade's handle immediately beside her right thigh, within reach of her free right hand; she has not yet grasped it. The thick root still grips her waist and injured ribs, slowly drawing her down the slope toward the pulsing lower-chamber mass. Mara Venn remains tethered behind and witnessed the manoeuvre. Isolde is unarmoured, without her own sword, severely rib-injured and breathless; no Severance used. Wardens above search; no demolition authorized. Await action.
