# Current scene — S03
Early Deepfrost 312, late afternoon, covered barracks passage at Blackthorn Bastion. A month after their marriage dispute, Isolde has improved in Cassian-supervised training; formal unrestricted clearance remains unestablished. She privately believes she will never marry and has been accommodating Garran. Garran noticed she no longer challenges his complaints, acknowledged he had moved the target and asked what she wanted. Isolde replied that she wants to be herself, even if it causes arguments, but believes that is not what Garran wants and does not know how to make him happy. Garran said his happiness is not hers to manufacture by suppressing herself, accepted that disagreement is inevitable and that he must stop treating it as evidence she does not love him, while admitting he cannot promise never to be defensive again. He asked no new reassurance. Marriage remains unresolved; Garran does not know her private resignation. Northern records, retaining-wall assessment and Elara pregnancy/paternity remain open.
