# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, dusk, collapsing lower passage of Blight excavation outside Fort Aster. Isolde shouted for four freed captives to get out, reached through jammed iron doorway to help remaining young man and injured older woman. Brenn and two soldiers held twisted door and collapsing support; young man squeezed out first and with Isolde/Brenn pulled injured woman through. All six lower captives now free of cell, but Isolde, Brenn, two soldiers, and last two captives still in unstable underground passage, needing immediate escape. A roof beam fell as woman came through, striking Isolde across shoulder/back and knocking her down; pain and reduced mobility added to severe neurological symptoms (double vision, headache, weak right hand) from four recent Severance uses. Evren cut final binding above with Severance, extinguishing apparatus and controller; upper excavation unstable. One earlier cage captive on platform; four lower captives heading out; one infantryman serious leg wound. Garran and Cassian not established to know miscarriage.
