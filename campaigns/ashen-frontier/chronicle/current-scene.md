# Current scene — Beside medical wagon, expedition camp, dawn 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged recovery. Isolde walked out of the medical tent toward the waiting padded wagon and told Medic Harris, “I can march.” She can manage a short careful walk, but her cracked ribs hurt sharply with each step and lingering dizziness makes a sustained march unsafe. Harris firmly distinguished walking a few yards from marching half a day and refused medical clearance; she offered Isolde the choice to board under her own power or have assistance, without physically forcing her yet. Isolde wears clean Warden uniform without armour; sword and equipment are to be transported. Company preparing to depart toward Survey Station Seventeen, about half a day's march away. Calder expects Isolde's report when fit. Await response.
