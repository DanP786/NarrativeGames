# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Isolde's quarters at Blackthorn Bastion, evening supper
Earlier today Fen admitted before Harris that she named Garran as possible father merely to get his attention. Fen affirmed no sexual encounter with Garran since last winter and denied any forced encounter; pregnancy and paternity have not been medically confirmed to Isolde. Isolde told Garran; they shared relief and affection. Harris has cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Cassian showed Isolde an altered northern charcoal-pit patrol-office master report: original recorded three unwounded deer and absence of scavengers; uninitialled correction struck out scavenger detail and changed 'three' to 'several'. Isolde retrieved her own suspicious reports; her summary records three deer but omits scavengers; provenance uncertain. Cassian accepted responsibility for investigating the altered report. Cassian revealed Edric recommended him for Isolde's rehab and speculated Edric might question Garran's stalled rank; Cassian expressed concern about unverified rumours Garran fathered another woman's child, unaware Fen recanted before Harris. Isolde and Cassian discussed his past failure to commit; Isolde teased him about an intimate trick he had lost and said she would try it with Garran. Isolde returned to Garran in the engineers' workshop, initiated consensual private intimacy in a cupboard (non-graphic), then rebuked an impatient private's tone. They agreed to ask each other one honest question every night. Isolde overheard Garran defend her warmly to a workshop soldier, without Garran knowing she heard. At six Isolde tidied her quarters, wore a striking burgundy dress once described as 'husband-catching', and unbraided her blonde hair with dark roots to her waist. Garran arrived with supper and wine. For his nightly question he asked whether she came to him from happiness or fear about Fen; she admitted she might have overcompensated to reassure him but also wanted him. For hers she asked if Edric had interrogated his suitability while she was unconscious. Garran admitted Edric thanked him for rescuing her but questioned his eight years as corporal versus Isolde's fast rise, his ambitions and discipline; Garran felt inadequate. Isolde explained Cassian said he and Edric had exchanged letters throughout and had hinted at Edric's comments. She rose from supper and straddled Garran's lap, barefoot with her burgundy dress drawn to her thighs, arms around his neck, and reassured him she has never demanded promotion; she cares about his goals but not his rank, only how he treats her and makes her feel. Garran was deeply moved and reciprocated the embrace, admitted that he wants to advance for his own reasons too, and did not mistake her affection for an obligation. The couple remain close at the supper table, dinner partly uneaten; both nightly questions have been asked and answered. Other unusual reports: birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; no confirmed connection.
