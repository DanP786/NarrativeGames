# Current scene — Beneath the western outbuilding, Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic encounter. Isolde stopped fighting the constricting root and managed painful shallow breaths, then asked the unseen speaker who they were. A gaunt woman in a torn survey coat emerged partly into the faint Blight glow and identified herself as Mara Venn, a station surveyor. She appeared exhausted, with one wrist bound by a thin black filament; her claim is not independently verified. She said the roots respond to struggling and urged Isolde not to let them pull her farther into the passage. The heavy root around Isolde's injured ribs eased marginally once she ceased fighting, though she remains pinned, swordless, in severe pain and short of breath. Blight strands converge farther underground; aboveground Wardens are searching, with demolition still withheld. Await Isolde's response.
