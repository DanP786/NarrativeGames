# Current scene — S03
Evening, three days after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, at Fox and Lantern tavern near Vale family home. Isolde and Garran have arrived for Livia Harrow's reunion of Garran's childhood acquaintances. Isolde wears fitted outfit, waist-length blonde hair with dark roots loose except front pieces tied behind head. Garran wears clean rustic shirt, waistcoat and trousers, arm around her waist. Bram Fenwick, broad red-bearded childhood friend, greeted them; another man at the table crudely joked about Wardens keeping a 'harem' and guessed Isolde was a kitchen cook. Livia initially looked annoyed, then laughed at cook comment. Garran corrected the man firmly: Isolde is Sergeant Varrick, an experienced Warden who has saved lives fighting Blight, not a cook, without forcing Isolde to speak for herself. The table has heard his correction; Isolde can react. Livia Harrow, pretty auburn-haired childhood acquaintance, kissed Garran's cheek uninvited at market and initially ignored Isolde; romantic history with Garran unestablished. Prior night Isolde said 'You don’t need to make anything right. We’re fine as we are. Let’s just leave it alone' after Garran acknowledged painful unresolved matters; he respected request, privately concerned silence may not equal peace. His 'practically a married man' hinge joke privately stung Isolde, who suspects marriage jokes might test her; he cannot know. Garran loves her, not ready to marry, knows she says she no longer expects it. Isolde privately grieves marriage/home/children/family future she believes she cannot have; Garran does not know. She overheard Mara press Garran privately about settling down; neither knows she overheard. Garran bought brass clockwork bird (four copper); Mara bought winter-branch brooch. Seven days approved leave, staying longer than three days at Vale home.
