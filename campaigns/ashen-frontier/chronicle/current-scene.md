# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Blackthorn Bastion training yard, morning
Cassian Rook returned from his northern station to conduct Isolde's first resumed structured sword rehabilitation after Harris's clearance. In three controlled exchanges Isolde scored two touches to his one, showing improved technique but limited endurance. She said it was good to swing a sword again and drank water; she remains visibly slimmer after illness. Cassian observed lost strength and asked whether she still hurt or was mainly weak. Isolde replied she was weak and had not tried Severance. Cassian approved not testing Severance and asked whether uncertainty about its future bothered her. Isolde raised an eyebrow and challenged him: 'What's with the 20 questions Cassian?' Harris's restrictions still apply: no unrestricted sparring, field duty or Severance. Privately Isolde has emotionally withdrawn from Garran despite their pleasant, argument-free public relationship; old pain over Cassian has faded and she intends to marry neither man. Cassian cannot know her thoughts. Garran already knows Cassian was her first love and they once planned a future.
