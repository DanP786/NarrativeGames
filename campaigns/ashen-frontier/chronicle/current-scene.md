# Current scene — Medical tent at expedition camp, dawn 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: routine recovery transition. Isolde drank the entire cup of bitter willowbark tea indignantly in one go. Medic Harris was amused, took the empty cup, and prepared to help Isolde walk slowly to the padded medical wagon without straining her injured ribs. Isolde wears a clean Warden uniform without armour, with her sword and equipment to be transported separately. She has painful suspected cracked ribs, bandaged temple, dressed palm, lingering headache, weakness and occasional dizziness. The company is nearly ready to break camp for the half-day march to Survey Station Seventeen. Captain Calder expects a report when Isolde is fit, not immediately. Await action.
