# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, dawn into morning. Isolde, in Eldorian blue fitted armour, trousers and boots, blonde hair in signature two braids, smiled at Evren in the palace mustering courtyard; he tipped her chin up and kissed her passionately before they mounted and departed together with Captain Halden's strike force for Fort Aster.
