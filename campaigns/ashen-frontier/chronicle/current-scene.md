# Current scene — Approach to Survey Station Seventeen, midday 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged arrival. Isolde endured the approximately half-day wagon ride lying among blankets, restless, bored within the first hour and deeply frustrated that she could not protect her people and instead needed protection herself. The jolts worsened her painful cracked ribs; headache, light sensitivity and dizziness linger. Around midday the expedition reached the approach to abandoned emberstone Survey Station Seventeen. The column slowed at the forest edge. Through wagon opening Isolde can see a low, weathered compound of stone and timber, a leaning watch platform, damaged perimeter fencing, and the dark mouth of a service entrance; no clear signs of current occupation from this vantage. Captain Calder has not yet received Isolde's account of the unusual Blight encounter. Medic Harris has not cleared Isolde for duty. Isolde wears Warden uniform without armour; sword and gear secured in wagon. Await Isolde's action as expedition scouts assess the station.
