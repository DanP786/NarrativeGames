# Current scene — S03
Evening, approximately one week after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, Captain Rhea Calder's office at Blackthorn Bastion. Isolde and Garran returned from seven days' leave. Calder informed Isolde she is assigned to depart at dawn on restricted expedition to inspect an abandoned emberstone survey station; Garran stays for northern retaining-wall assessment. No unrestricted duty or Severance-use clearance. Isolde and Garran requested shared quarters; Calder noted request and told Garran to submit written requisition through quartermaster, with approval and room availability pending. Isolde asked 'How big is the party?' Calder answered twelve Wardens plus two survey specialists (fourteen total), Calder personally commanding, with Isolde assigned as specialist escort/assessment rather than unrestricted front-line fighter. Route details restricted until dawn briefing. Garran present and concerned. Isolde and Garran remain couple; Garran not ready to marry; Isolde privately believes he never will and accepts what he offers, unknown to him. Garran holds wrapped decorative hinge (Isolde owns it) and brass clockwork bird. Garran's wall assessment, altered charcoal-pit records and Elara pregnancy/paternity unresolved.
