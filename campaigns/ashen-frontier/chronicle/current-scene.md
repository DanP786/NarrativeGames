# Current scene — S03
Early Deepfrost 312, late afternoon, Blackthorn Bastion. One month has passed since Isolde and Garran's painful marriage argument and embrace. Isolde has trained regularly with Cassian, with substantially improved strength and stamina and no recent injury symptoms; formal unrestricted clearance is not established. She stays with Garran, giving him the reassurance and accommodation she believes he wants, but privately has resigned herself to never marrying and to believing she will never be loved wholly enough for that commitment. Garran does not know her private conclusion; the change in her usual manner is observable. Scene opens after training as Garran encounters Isolde in a bastion passage and notices her uncharacteristic lack of argument. Northern records mystery, retaining-wall assessment and Elara's unverified pregnancy remain open.
