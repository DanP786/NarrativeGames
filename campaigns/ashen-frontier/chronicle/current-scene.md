# Current scene — Forward command position overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, early afternoon 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged reconnaissance. Isolde remained seated on a fallen log at Calder's forward command, resting as ordered and watching the station rather than trying to enter. A small Warden reconnaissance party crossed the eastern breach under observation-only orders, following signs of at least four or five human boot tracks bearing loads inward; no return tracks identified. After several tense minutes the scouts signalled from cover: eastern courtyard clear, recent human disturbance at main building entrance, and an unnatural scraping sound from beneath the western outbuilding. The luminous Blight thread remains motionless as far as Isolde can see from her position. Calder has not yet ordered the scouts deeper and is awaiting further information. Isolde's ribs remain acutely painful, headache and dizziness persist, no armour, sword at side, barred from combat and Severance. Await Isolde's action.
