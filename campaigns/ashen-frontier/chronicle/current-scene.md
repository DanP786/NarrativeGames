# Current scene — S04
Spring 312 After Ashfall, dusk, battle at Blight excavation outside Fort Aster. After freeing woman from suspended cage, Isolde ordered Corporal Brenn and two soldiers to descend beneath the red-lit machinery to rescue additional captives reported by woman. Brenn obeyed, leaving freed woman sheltered on western platform; he and two soldiers entered lower access passage, where voices and metallic scraping can be heard. Isolde rose unsteadily, retrieved sword with severely trembling right hand and staggered toward controller despite acute neurological injury after fourth recent Severance use. Evren and infantry continue fighting controller on excavation floor; controller has remaining binding strands connected to red apparatus. Isolde now on descending walkway/steps toward fight, exposed and physically impaired. Archers and diversion engaged; one infantryman has serious leg wound. Garran and Cassian have not been established to know of miscarriage.
