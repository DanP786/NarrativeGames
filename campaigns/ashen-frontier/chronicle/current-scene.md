# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Blackthorn Bastion dining hall, evening
Earlier today Isolde resumed structured sword rehabilitation with Cassian Rook, scoring two touches to one over three exchanges but tiring quickly. After washing and changing into clean uniform she entered the dining hall for dinner. She spotted Corporal Elara Fen, whose open jacket and rounded abdomen made Isolde suspect pregnancy; this remains unconfirmed and paternity unknown. Elara greeted Isolde with barbed comments about her recovery and asked whether Isolde wanted to ask something. Isolde replied, 'Looks like one of us put on weight,' and walked toward dinner service. Elara insinuated there might be a reason for her weight gain, without confirming pregnancy. Isolde sat at her usual table. Garran arrived from sapper work, joined her, teased her about landing two touches on Cassian, and asked whether she was sore. Isolde replied, 'Nothing I can't handle.' Garran suggested an afternoon date in the nearby settlement tomorrow. Elara called to Garran from another table, said she had an important personal matter to discuss privately later, and left him visibly unsettled after he noticed her abdomen. Garran asked Isolde what that was about; Isolde shrugged and said, 'I believe you were asking me on a date.' Elara's pregnancy and any paternity remain unconfirmed. Garran and Isolde remain publicly together, but Isolde has privately withdrawn emotionally; Garran cannot know her unspoken thoughts. Harris's restrictions still apply: structured conditioning/swordsmanship only; no unrestricted sparring, field duty or Severance.
