# Current scene — Inside fissure of central mass, lower chamber beneath Station Seventeen, night 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: climactic. Isolde chose not to resist as the thick root positioned her nearer what might be the core, hoping proximity would give her a decisive target. The root drew her sideways through the narrow fleshy fissure, scraping her shoulders and badly injured ribs; she remains conscious, breathless, and gripping the rusted short knife in her right hand, with Severance still unused. Inside, she sees an emberstone-dark central knot suspended in layered pale tissue, its luminous bindings thick and complex; several taut strands run outward into the chamber, and one narrow seam appears to pass across the knot's exposed lower surface. A faint mechanical ticking from an old survey instrument embedded in the wall is audible between painful pulses. The root has stopped her just short of the knot, within possible knife reach if she extends her arm; its purpose remains unknown. Mara Venn is still en route to the surface with Isolde's request for all Garran Vale's explosives, delivery unconfirmed. Wardens above continue searching, no demolition authorized. Await action.
