# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Blackthorn Bastion corridor towards Isolde's quarters, evening
Yesterday Isolde and Garran shared a rare Copper Hart dinner interrupted by Elara Fen's claim of abdominal pain and pregnancy by Garran. Midwife Bell found signs consistent with pregnancy but could not confirm pregnancy, gestational age or paternity; Edwin transported Elara to Physician Harris. Garran maintains he has not slept with Elara since last winter; Isolde privately doubts this due to past absences, unknown to him. This morning Harris cleared Isolde for supervised, restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Two days earlier Isolde won two of three training rounds against Cassian; her anger at his arrival weeks ago has considerably eased. Today she won first controlled exchange, Cassian won second, and he called a water break before the third; third outcome remains unestablished. In the evening Garran saved Isolde stew and bread and teased about Cassian and Elara; she asked him not to mention either and agreed to dinner tomorrow in her room after training, with Garran bringing food. Garran joked he had the conversational instincts of a concussed goat; Isolde laughed aloud, drawing startled looks from Wardens. After her flat farewell and icy look at a staring private, she left the dining hall. Garran caught up and asked if she was heading to her room; Isolde said 'So long as you're coming' and took his hand. Garran warmly agreed and accompanied her towards her quarters, mindful of her medical recovery. No report on Elara's medical assessment or paternity is established. Earlier unusual patrol reports: unwounded dead deer at northern charcoal pits, birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; not confirmed connected.
