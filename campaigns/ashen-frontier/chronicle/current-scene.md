# Current scene — Forward command position overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, midday 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged reconnaissance. A scout reported recent human boot tracks on the eastern approach heading into abandoned Station Seventeen. Isolde exclaimed, “What human lives out here?!” Captain Calder said no sanctioned inhabitants are expected this far beyond the defended frontier, but tracks do not establish who made them or whether anyone remains alive. She ordered the scout to check number, age and direction of tracks without crossing the perimeter, and to watch for signs of survivors, scavengers or hostile human activity. The luminous Blight thread still leads beneath the western outbuilding, stationary as last seen; relationship to tracks unknown. Isolde is wounded and barred from entry or Severance use. Await Isolde's action.
