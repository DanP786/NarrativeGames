# Current scene — S03
Evening, three days after winter solstice, Deepfrost 312, at the Fox and Lantern tavern near Vale family home. Isolde and Garran have arrived together for Livia Harrow's reunion of Garran's childhood acquaintances. Isolde wears a fitted outfit, waist-length blonde hair with dark roots loose except front pieces tied behind her head. Garran wears clean rustic shirt, waistcoat and trousers, arm possessively around her waist. Bram Fenwick, broad red-bearded childhood friend, exclaimed at Isolde's beauty, crudely joked about Wardens keeping a 'harem' and guessed Isolde was a kitchen cook. Garran corrected him firmly that Isolde is Sergeant Varrick, an experienced Warden who has saved lives fighting Blight, not a cook, and kept the exchange social rather than making Isolde speak for herself. Livia Harrow, pretty auburn-haired childhood acquaintance, is present, had kissed Garran's cheek uninvited at market and initially ignored Isolde; romantic history with Garran unestablished. Prior night Isolde said 'You don’t need to make anything right. We’re fine as we are. Let’s just leave it alone' after Garran acknowledged painful unresolved matters; he respected request, privately concerned silence may not equal peace. His 'practically a married man' hinge joke privately stung Isolde, who suspects marriage jokes might test her; he cannot know. Garran loves her, not ready to marry, knows she says she no longer expects it. Isolde privately grieves marriage/home/children/family future she believes she cannot have; Garran does not know. She overheard Mara press Garran privately about settling down; neither knows she overheard. Isolde owns decorative Master Heddon hinge (3.5 silver), joking custody: she controls use and holds two days/week, Garran five, currently has it wrapped. Garran bought brass clockwork bird (four copper); Mara bought winter-branch brooch. Seven days approved leave, staying longer than three days at Vale home. Orchard wall rebuilding pending; Garran shoulder cold-stiff not painful. No unrestricted duty or Severance retest clearance; permanent Ember loss unconfirmed. Northern wall assessment for Calder, altered charcoal-pit records and Elara pregnancy/paternity unresolved.
