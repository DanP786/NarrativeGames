# Current scene — Forward command position overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, early afternoon 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: routine pause. Isolde answered Calder, “Aye, Captain,” and rested her head back against the tree while seated on the log. She remains in pain from suspected cracked ribs, with lingering headache and fatigue; resting relieves some strain. Calder left her undisturbed while ordering the four returned scouts debriefed and sending for survey records, maintaining observation of the western outbuilding and eastern breach. After a brief interval a scout reported that the boot tracks enter the main building, while a runner brought an old, incomplete station plan showing a service passage that may link the main structure and western outbuilding underground. This link is unconfirmed. Blight thread remains motionless as last observed; no entry team has yet been sent. Isolde remains outside perimeter, sword at side, no armour, medically unfit, barred from Severance. Await action.
