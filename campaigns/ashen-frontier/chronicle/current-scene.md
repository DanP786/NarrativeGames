# Current scene — Medical wagon, expedition camp, morning 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: routine recovery. Isolde glared daggers at Medic Harris but reluctantly climbed aboard the padded medical wagon under her own power. Climbing jarred her cracked ribs and caused a brief dizzy spell; Harris steadied the wagon and made sure she could settle against blankets without further strain. Harris accepted the glare with dry amusement and reaffirmed that Isolde is not cleared to march or fight. Isolde wears clean Warden uniform without armour; her sword and gear are being secured for transport. Company is about to depart for Survey Station Seventeen, approximately half a day's march away. Calder wants Isolde's report when fit. Await Isolde's action.
