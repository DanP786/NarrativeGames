# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, Blackthorn Bastion training hall, morning
Yesterday Isolde and Garran shared a rare Copper Hart dinner interrupted by Elara Fen's claim of abdominal pain and pregnancy by Garran. Midwife Bell found signs consistent with pregnancy but could not confirm pregnancy, gestational age or paternity; Edwin transported Elara to Physician Harris. Garran maintains he has not slept with Elara since last winter; Isolde privately doubts this due to past absences, unknown to him. This morning Harris cleared Isolde for supervised, restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Two days earlier Isolde won two of three training rounds against Cassian; her anger at his arrival weeks ago has eased considerably. Cassian teased her for gloating about victories as a child, recalling her nickname for him after midsummer trials. Isolde answered 'Gladly… second place' and took guard with a wooden practice sword. In the first of three controlled technical exchanges, she deliberately avoided her favourite demanding moves because she knows she lacks the strength, relying instead on proven basics. She read Cassian's probing feint, kept her centre guarded and landed a clean controlled touch to his forearm. Cassian acknowledged the point and praised her restraint, then reset for the second exchange. Harris's limits remain in force. Cassian is Isolde's childhood friend, former serious partner and first heartbreak, assigned via Frontier Command and Edric Varrick's recommendation. Cassian does not automatically know about Garran or Elara. Earlier unusual patrol reports: unwounded dead deer at northern charcoal pits, birds abandoning western roosts, unidentified figure along eastern treeline; not confirmed connected.
