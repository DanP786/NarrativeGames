Earlier today Fen admitted before Harris that she named Garran as possible father merely to get his attention. Fen affirmed no sexual encounter with Garran since last winter and denied any forced encounter; pregnancy and paternity have not been medically confirmed to Isolde. Isolde told Garran; they shared relief and affection. Harris cleared Isolde for supervised restricted training with Cassian Rook, not full duty. Isolde retrieved her own suspicious reports; changes' provenance uncertain. Cassian is investigating. Garran admitted Edric thanked him for rescue but questioned his eight years as corporal and his discipline. Garran helped her eat supper and tart in his lap. Isolde discovered she strongly likes his affectionate praise 'good girl' and confirmed this; Garran delighted in gently teasing her with it. She urgently requested more intimacy. Garran responded tenderly, attentive to her healing and setting a slower pace, and she willingly entrusted him with control rather than their usual playful pretense. Their consensual intimacy occurred off-page. Afterwards Isolde curled close to him in bed, deeply relaxed and feeling an unfamiliar relief at relinquishing responsibility. A tear slipped down her cheek; Garran worried he'd hurt her. Isolde explained she had been vulnerable with him before from distress, but tonight was different because she chose vulnerability freely; she smiled and admitted she never knew how much she liked being called 'good girl.' Garran was touched and pleased, assured her she can choose to let him look after her without losing strength or agency, and affectionately teased her. Both nightly questions answered.
