# Current scene — Forward command position overlooking Survey Station Seventeen, mid-afternoon 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged investigation. When Calder asked whether the recently chained upper-side hatch was meant to keep something out or in, Isolde smiled and suggested, “Maybe both?” Calder accepted this as possible, though unproven, and ordered the entry party to inspect the hatch, chain, lock and nearby signs without touching the seal. A subsequent scout report indicated scrape marks on the underside-facing lip of the hatch frame and fresh scuffing around the padlock; no determination of cause yet. The main building ground floor is clear of visible hostiles but recently occupied, and the luminous Blight thread vanishes beneath the western outbuilding. Isolde remains seated and resting at forward command with cracked ribs, lingering headache, no armour, sword at side, prohibited from entry and Severance. Await response.
