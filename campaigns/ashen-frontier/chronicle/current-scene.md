# Current scene — IN PROGRESS, early Frostwane 312, evening, dining hall
Isolde Varrick and Garran Vale remain at odds after she said she would marry him tomorrow but he was not ready, citing their pattern of painful arguments and threats to leave. She fears indefinite waiting and feeling unworthy; she left his quarters tearful the previous night, and has avoided him today. In the morning Cassian Rook showed Isolde altered northern charcoal-pit paperwork: 'three unwounded deer' changed to 'several', absence of scavengers struck without initials, and original field transcription registered received but missing without outgoing notation. No culprit or explanation established. Cassian handles registers and authorization trail; Isolde reviews patrol reports for patterns. Other unlinked oddities: birds leaving western roosts, a figure at eastern treeline. Isolde confided her marriage troubles to Cassian, her former partner; he admitted regretting his failure to marry her but not seeking to replace Garran. She left upset, then spent all day checking patrol reports and skipped lunch to avoid Garran; no new confirmed alteration pattern found. Her injured hand is stiff and aching; Harris still limits her to restricted supervised rehabilitation. That evening Cassian delivered inconclusive register notes. They reconciled somewhat conversationally; Isolde apologized for confiding, Cassian said he still cares but has moved forward. He has dated since their breakup and has no sweetheart at his northern posting. Isolde teased him by proposing Elara Fen as a match, adding Fen supposedly has a baby on the way, 'A ready made family raring to go.' Cassian knows Isolde claims Fen is expecting, but has no independent confirmation and does not know Fen's admission to Harris that she falsely named Garran as possible father for attention and denied intimacy since last winter. Pregnancy and paternity unconfirmed to Isolde. Isolde walked with Cassian to the dining hall, teasing that he could get any woman he wanted. She rolled her eyes, collected supper, and sat down without looking for Garran. Cassian joined her and joked Harris would scold her for overworking her injured hand, demanding bread as a bribe for silence. Isolde smilingly threw her bread at him; Cassian caught it against his chest and joked about assaulting a superior officer, continuing their easy banter. Isolde ate her stew and quipped, 'Now I know where I got my mean streak from.' Cassian playfully claimed partial credit. Elara Fen approached, incorrectly assuming Isolde and Garran have broken up; Isolde scowled and demanded who told her they fell out. Elara admitted no one told her outright; she inferred it from Isolde's absence and supper with Cassian. Isolde sharply challenged the notion that one day apart justified breakup rumours and accused Elara of making something out of nothing. Elara then invoked supposedly common knowledge that Cassian and Isolde were former sweethearts. Isolde demanded how Elara knew, since neither told her, and accused her of spreading it. Elara claimed she heard old talk around the garrison, but provided no named source; she did not confess to spreading it. Isolde explicitly told Elara they are not friends and she has no reason to discuss her private relationship with her. Elara took offense and withdrew. Cassian offered to intervene if Elara spread stories, but Isolde declined, saying there was enough conflicting gossip about Elara and Garran already. Cassian agreed not to interfere and did not press for details. Garran's presence in the hall is not established. Garran has trial supervision of northern retaining wall repairs and written assessment for Calder, not immediate promotion; shoulder restrictions apply.
