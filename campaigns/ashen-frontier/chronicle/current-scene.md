# Current scene — S03
Early Deepfrost 312, evening, Isolde's quarters at Blackthorn Bastion. After she washed up and lay unmoving on her bed, Garran came to her door after supper with a plate of food, noting she missed the meal. Isolde rose and opened the door wearing only one of Garran's shirts. He noticed with affection but also saw her fatigue; he offered the food and asked whether she wanted company, without entering uninvited. They remain together; marriage unresolved. Isolde privately doubts Garran's promises and has resigned herself to never marrying, unknown to him. Recovery has improved with Cassian's training; unrestricted clearance unestablished. Northern records, retaining-wall assessment and Elara pregnancy/paternity remain open.
