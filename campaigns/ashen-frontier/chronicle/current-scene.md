# Current scene — Pine hollow overlook, midmorning
Tier: climactic. Isolde stood despite severe post-Severance headache and nosebleed, quipped “Thing's about to give birth,” and ordered Hester Brann and Davin Sorel farther back while retaining line of sight. The three withdrew roughly twenty paces up the slope to a rocky overlook with clear view of the hollow. Davin's recovering knee slowed him slightly but he stayed mobile. Beneath the fallen pine, the soil ruptured and a bulky mass of black rootlike fibres and pale exposed tissue began to emerge, lifting the trunk and exposing a central pulsing knot; it has not yet fully emerged or reached the squad. Through Ember sight Isolde sees luminous threads concentrated in that central knot. Nera Holt is still en route to Garran Vale to request a large explosive and send Pell Rusk to the squad; neither has returned. Await Isolde's response from the overlook.
