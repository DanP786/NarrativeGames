# Current scene — Eastern ridge approach above Survey Station Seventeen, midday 19 Emberwane 312
Tier: charged reconnaissance. Isolde tightened her jaw but obeyed Captain Calder's refusal of solo entry, answering, “Yes, Captain.” Calder acknowledged her discipline and assigned a guarded reconnaissance to the eastern ridge for a better view before anyone enters the station. Isolde begins the slow ascent under escort, still without armour, sword at side, with severe rib pain, lingering headache and dizziness; she has been ordered not to use Severance. From the ridge approach she can still glimpse the western wall's motionless luminous Blight thread between trees, but the source remains hidden. Scouts hold outside the station perimeter pending further assessment. Await Isolde's action or observations.
