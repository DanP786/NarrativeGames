# Leif Ardent
- Description/voice: 26, lean tall reconnaissance corporal, dark hair worn loose, pale green eyes, crooked nose. Speaks carefully in low, precise sentences; when irritated, becomes icily formal. Habitually peels labels from bottles.
- Traits: exacting, ambitious, privately jealous, slow to admit error, protective of his own reputation.
- Short-term goals: check expedition route assignments; keep an ongoing disagreement with Garran from spilling into a public scene; determine whether Isolde has seen the amended route.
- Long-term goals: earn a command posting away from Blackthorn; prove himself without favours; discover why scouting reports around Greywatch are being amended.
- Methods & lines: Will challenge an order through proper channels, withhold personal information, compete ruthlessly. Won't falsify scouting observations. Last resort: defy orders to prevent a needless slaughter.
- Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers): Trust 55, Affection 49, Respect 78, Loyalty 32, Suspicion 30, Desire 47.
- Relationship to Isolde: Neutral. Long acquaintance through overlapping patrols, training sparring, and sharp arguments about risk. Isolde has an unspoken attraction to him per player-established seed; Leif's feelings are uncertain, complicated by career ambitions and his own commitments. Do not assume he knows her attraction. Her plain acknowledgement of the nine she saved and her purpose of preventing further deaths increased his respect a little, though he disputes her approach to orders.
- History with player: trained and served alongside Isolde intermittently for years; not part of Greywatch split, knows official report. S01 Cinderhouse: Isolde came to his map table and asked what he was examining; he disclosed troubling eastward route amendment. They argued quietly over duty and scrutiny of orders; she stated fifteen good soldiers died and nine more would have without her.
- Relationships with other NPCs: considers Garran careless but talented; owes Captain Calder his promotion; distrusts alterations to scouting reports.
- Knowledge: knows revised expedition route differs from original, toward an abandoned eastern survey station; does not know why. Knows no private PC thoughts.
