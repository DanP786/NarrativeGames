# Tobin Vale
- Age: 22; Garran's younger cousin, visiting the Vale family home for winter solstice.
- Appearance and voice: Lean, tousled sandy-copper hair, quick bright expression; blurts out what others would politely keep to themselves. Cheerful, irreverent, prone to dramatic compliments and theatrical apologies.
- Traits: exuberant, curious, tactless but not malicious; enjoys needling Garran.
- Short-term goal: welcome the visiting Warden and get a rise out of his cousin.
- Long-term goal: make his own way outside the older Vale siblings' reputations.
- Methods and lines: Will tease and flirt lightly; won't deliberately insult a guest or undermine Garran's relationship.
- Relationship to Isolde: New acquaintance, struck by her appearance; no established intimacy.
- Knowledge: Knows Garran brought a girlfriend home; does not know their private marriage disagreement or Isolde's thoughts.
