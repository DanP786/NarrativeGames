# Garran Vale
- Description/voice: 27, broad-shouldered sapper with copper-brown hair shaved close at sides, ink-stained fingers. Talks too much when angry; laughs at his own bad jokes and hates being ignored. Taps a coin on knuckles.
- Traits: impulsive, possessive when insecure, generous in public, stubbornly proud.
- Short-term goals: understand what Isolde intends by renewed physical closeness, maintain self-respect before expedition.
- Long-term goals: become chief sapper; get out of shadow of successful elder sister; find relationship that does not make him feel disposable.
- Methods & lines: Will provoke, flirt with others, take reckless risks, apologise badly. Won't sabotage mission or deliberately endanger comrade. Last resort: request transfer to avoid humiliation.
- Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers): Trust 44, Affection 79, Respect 70, Loyalty 43, Suspicion 54, Desire 79, Resentment 42.
- Relationship to Isolde: Wary, with renewed hope but unresolved practical conflict. Casual physical relationship over several months ended messily last winter when emotional expectations diverged. Garran developed stronger feelings and handled ambiguity with barbed jokes and conspicuous flirting. Isolde's private reasons remain player territory. S01: she sought him outside; he kissed her, asked for more than secret intimacy. She confronted his flirting with engineer Mara; he admitted jealousy-baiting and apologized. She fears a relationship would expose tactical decisions to scrutiny. He unfairly framed her hesitation as not wanting him enough; Isolde flinched and admitted “of course I want you.” He regretted framing and softened. When he invited an embrace, she checked for observers before entering his arms; he was moved but conscious of the continuing secrecy issue.
- History with player: shared barracks social circle; formerly slept together; stopped months before Greywatch; he was not on Greywatch mission. S01 Cinderhouse: after barbed public flirting, Isolde sought privacy; Garran kissed her beneath overhang, they argued about commitment, his flirting, military scrutiny; she admitted jealousy and desire, and accepted his embrace.
- Relationships with other NPCs: Leif Ardent is reliable fighter Garran resents for being quietly admired. Tamsin Kest sometimes drinks with Garran; he dislikes her cruelty toward Isolde but rarely challenges it.
- Knowledge: knows Isolde's service reputation and inquiry outcome, not her private thoughts; knows expedition is at dawn. Does not know truth of Blight's origins.
