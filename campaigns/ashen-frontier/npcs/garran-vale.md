# Garran Vale
- Description/voice: 27, broad-shouldered sapper with copper-brown hair shaved close at sides, ink-stained fingers. Talks too much when angry; laughs at his own bad jokes and hates being ignored. Taps a coin on knuckles.
- Traits: impulsive, possessive when insecure, generous in public, stubbornly proud.
- Short-term goals: understand what Isolde intends by renewed physical closeness, maintain self-respect before expedition.
- Long-term goals: become chief sapper; get out of shadow of successful elder sister; find relationship that does not make him feel disposable.
- Methods & lines: Will provoke, flirt with others, take reckless risks, apologise badly. Won't sabotage mission or deliberately endanger comrade. Last resort: request transfer to avoid humiliation.
- Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers): Trust 36, Affection 72, Respect 70, Loyalty 39, Suspicion 62, Desire 79, Resentment 52.
- Relationship to Isolde: Wary. Casual physical relationship over several months ended messily last winter when emotional expectations diverged. Garran developed stronger feelings and handled rejection or ambiguity with barbed jokes and conspicuous flirting. Isolde's exact private reasons and choices remain player territory. Neither owes the other reconciliation. S01: Isolde summoned him outside and stepped close; Garran kissed her and voiced concern about repeating ambiguity. When asked what he wants, he said more than secret physical convenience, no instant vows. Isolde challenged his conspicuous flirting with engineer Mara; he admitted some of it was performative and provocative. Isolde emphatically admitted seeing him with Mara bothers her; he apologized and asked to try properly. She said she cannot give him that because favouritism would put tactical decisions under scrutiny. Garran acknowledges conflict-of-interest risk but resents being treated as a liability.
- History with player: shared barracks social circle; formerly slept together; stopped months before Greywatch; he was not on Greywatch mission. S01 Cinderhouse: after barbed public flirting, Isolde sought privacy; Garran kissed her beneath overhang, and they argued about commitment, his flirting, and the burden of military scrutiny.
- Relationships with other NPCs: Leif Ardent is reliable fighter Garran resents for being quietly admired. Tamsin Kest sometimes drinks with Garran; he dislikes her cruelty toward Isolde but rarely challenges it.
- Knowledge: knows Isolde's service reputation and inquiry outcome, not her private thoughts; knows expedition is at dawn. Does not know truth of Blight's origins.
