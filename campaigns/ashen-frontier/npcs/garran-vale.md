# Garran Vale
- Age/rank/service: 27, Warden Corporal; eight years in the Wardens, six posted at Blackthorn Bastion. Experienced combat sapper, specializing in demolitions, fortifications, field engineering and repairs. He was already at Blackthorn when Isolde arrived four years ago. Isolde is a sergeant and outranks him despite having half his service years.
- Description/voice: Broad-shouldered sapper with copper-brown hair shaved close at sides, ink-stained fingers. Talks too much when angry; laughs at his own bad jokes and hates being ignored. Taps a coin on knuckles.
- Traits: impulsive, possessive when insecure, generous in public, stubbornly proud. Highly capable at sapper work; impulsiveness, occasional insubordination and disputes with superiors have slowed advancement.
- Short-term goals: support Isolde's recovery and make their newly public, mutually loving relationship work; recover from his own lingering shoulder injury.
- Long-term goals: become chief sapper; get out of shadow of successful elder sister; find relationship that does not make him feel disposable.
- Methods & lines: Will provoke, flirt with others, take reckless risks, apologise badly. Won't sabotage mission or deliberately endanger comrade. Last resort: request transfer to avoid humiliation.
- Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers): Trust 56, Affection 89, Respect 74, Loyalty 49, Suspicion 42, Desire 84, Resentment 31.
- Relationship to Isolde: Last winter he deliberately threatened to sleep with another woman to provoke jealousy; Isolde said 'Fine,' and he impulsively followed through once with Corporal Elara Fen. Their relationship ended. During S01 they reconciled, publicly acknowledged being together, and exchanged sincere mutual declarations of love on 19 Frostwane 312. Garran regrets his jealousy games and knows Elara has been provoking them. Isolde outranks him and rose unusually fast owing to her combat leadership and rare Severance ability; this may sometimes touch his professional insecurity without diminishing his genuine respect for her. Garran does not know about Isolde's past relationship with Cassian Rook.
- History with player: Shared Blackthorn barracks social circle; Garran was already posted here two years before Isolde arrived. Casual intimacy ended last winter after Garran's jealousy ploy; reconciliation during S01, including shared nights, open commitment and mutual 'I love you.' Garran helped rescue Isolde from the collapse of Survey Station Seventeen, injuring his shoulder; later Harris advised against heavy lifting and overhead work pending improvement. Elara Fen has since provoked the couple; Isolde privately threatened to break her arm, and Garran worries about disciplinary repercussions.
- Relationships with other NPCs: Leif Ardent is reliable fighter Garran resents for being quietly admired. Tamsin Kest sometimes drinks with Garran; he dislikes her cruelty toward Isolde but rarely challenges it. Elara Fen is the woman he slept with once last winter; he does not reciprocate her present flirtation.
- Knowledge: Knows Isolde's service reputation and Greywatch inquiry outcome, not her private thoughts; does not know truth of Blight's origins or Isolde's history with Cassian.
