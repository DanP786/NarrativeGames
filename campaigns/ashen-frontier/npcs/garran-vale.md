# Garran Vale
- Description/voice: 27, broad-shouldered sapper with copper-brown hair shaved close at sides, ink-stained fingers. Talks too much when angry; laughs at his own bad jokes and hates being ignored. Taps a coin on knuckles.
- Traits: impulsive, possessive when insecure, generous in public, stubbornly proud.
- Short-term goals: survive the expedition and follow through on Isolde's conditional offer of dinner; learn whether she will acknowledge their connection beyond private quarters.
- Long-term goals: become chief sapper; get out of shadow of successful elder sister; find relationship that does not make him feel disposable.
- Methods & lines: Will provoke, flirt with others, take reckless risks, apologise badly. Won't sabotage mission or deliberately endanger comrade. Last resort: request transfer to avoid humiliation.
- Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers): Trust 56, Affection 89, Respect 74, Loyalty 49, Suspicion 42, Desire 84, Resentment 31.
- Relationship to Isolde: Wary but hopeful, with unresolved practical conflict. Casual physical relationship ended messily last winter when expectations diverged. Garran developed stronger feelings and handled ambiguity with barbed jokes and conspicuous flirting. Last winter he deliberately threatened to sleep with another woman to provoke jealousy; Isolde said, “Fine.” Too proud to back down, he impulsively followed through. Isolde gave him none of the jealous confrontation he expected, and their arrangement ended. He regrets trying to force a reaction and is aware, or strongly believes, that she has not slept with anyone else in the months since. This confuses him: he cannot reconcile her insistence on a casual arrangement with her apparent lack of other partners, and he does not know her private reasons or feelings. S01: Isolde sought him outside, they kissed and discussed more than secret intimacy. He admitted flirting with engineer Mara to provoke jealousy and apologized; whether Mara was the woman involved in last winter's incident is not established. Isolde worries a public relationship would invite scrutiny of her tactical choices. She admitted wanting him and entered his embrace after checking for observers. She invited him to stay with her tonight; he accepted, meaning to stay the whole night rather than slip away. She took his hand and teased “If I come first you can stay the night”; he flirted back. In her quarters they exchanged vulnerable admissions and spent the night together in mutually welcomed intimacy. He stayed through the night as promised. In the predawn, when he asked to see her again, she offered to let him take her to dinner if he survived the expedition. No agreed relationship label yet.
- History with player: shared barracks social circle; formerly slept together; stopped months before Greywatch; he was not on Greywatch mission. Their breakup last winter followed Garran's calculated jealousy threat, Isolde's “Fine,” and his impulsive decision to sleep with another woman; he did not get the reaction he wanted. S01 Cinderhouse: barbed public flirting, private kiss beneath overhang, frank argument about relationship, then embrace and invitation to stay overnight. Later that night they renewed their physical relationship in her quarters; she offered a conditional post-expedition dinner.
- Relationships with other NPCs: Leif Ardent is reliable fighter Garran resents for being quietly admired. Tamsin Kest sometimes drinks with Garran; he dislikes her cruelty toward Isolde but rarely challenges it.
- Knowledge: knows Isolde's service reputation and inquiry outcome, not her private thoughts; knows expedition is at dawn. Does not know truth of Blight's origins. Believes Isolde has had no other sexual partners since their breakup, but has no certain proof and cannot know why.
