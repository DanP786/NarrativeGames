# Garran Vale
- Description/voice: 27, broad-shouldered sapper with copper-brown hair shaved close at sides, ink-stained fingers. Talks too much when angry; laughs at his own bad jokes and hates being ignored. Taps a coin on knuckles.
- Traits: impulsive, possessive when insecure, generous in public, stubbornly proud.
- Short-term goals: understand what Isolde intends by renewed physical closeness, maintain self-respect before expedition.
- Long-term goals: become chief sapper; get out of shadow of successful elder sister; find relationship that does not make him feel disposable.
- Methods & lines: Will provoke, flirt with others, take reckless risks, apologise badly. Won't sabotage mission or deliberately endanger comrade. Last resort: request transfer to avoid humiliation.
- Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers): Trust 48, Affection 82, Respect 71, Loyalty 45, Suspicion 49, Desire 81, Resentment 38.
- Relationship to Isolde: Wary but hopeful, with unresolved practical conflict. Casual physical relationship ended messily last winter when expectations diverged. Garran developed stronger feelings and handled ambiguity with barbed jokes and conspicuous flirting. S01: Isolde sought him outside, they kissed and discussed more than secret intimacy. He admitted flirting with engineer Mara to provoke jealousy and apologized. Isolde worries a public relationship would invite scrutiny of her tactical choices. She admitted wanting him and entered his embrace after checking for observers. She then invited him to stay with her tonight; he accepted, meaning to stay the whole night rather than slip away. No agreed relationship label yet.
- History with player: shared barracks social circle; formerly slept together; stopped months before Greywatch; he was not on Greywatch mission. S01 Cinderhouse: barbed public flirting, private kiss beneath overhang, frank argument about relationship, then embrace and invitation to stay overnight.
- Relationships with other NPCs: Leif Ardent is reliable fighter Garran resents for being quietly admired. Tamsin Kest sometimes drinks with Garran; he dislikes her cruelty toward Isolde but rarely challenges it.
- Knowledge: knows Isolde's service reputation and inquiry outcome, not her private thoughts; knows expedition is at dawn. Does not know truth of Blight's origins.
