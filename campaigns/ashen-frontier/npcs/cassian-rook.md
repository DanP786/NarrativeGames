# Cassian Rook
- Age/rank: 27, Warden Sergeant; posted to a northern Warden garrison, temporarily at Blackthorn to help Isolde's rehabilitation.
- Description/voice: Controlled, exacting, socially assured without Garran's exuberance. Notices mechanical errors in movement almost reflexively; speaks with measured clarity that can feel painfully intimate when he recalls old habits. Physical appearance to emerge in play.
- Traits: ambitious, disciplined, emotionally avoidant under pressure, proud of competence, capable of genuine care without knowing how to prioritize it.
- Short-term goals: train Isolde within Harris's restrictions; check register/authorization trail for altered northern charcoal-pit paperwork; preserve Isolde's confidence about her marriage dispute.
- Long-term goals: advance Warden career; reconcile what he once wanted with what he was unwilling to promise Isolde. Not scheming to replace Garran.
- Methods & lines: Will challenge Isolde professionally, use shared training knowledge, withdraw from uncomfortable personal discussions. Won't intentionally jeopardize neurological recovery or use Command authority to force intimacy. Last resort: request reassignment if professional conflict becomes untenable.
- Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers): Trust 64, Affection 69, Respect 83, Loyalty 36, Suspicion 18, Regret 63, Desire 43. Provisional; routine supper banter alone does not shift these.
- Relationship to Isolde: Childhood friend and former serious partner; their fathers served together. Trained with wooden swords as children, competed as adolescents, became romantically involved in late teens. Cassian was Isolde's first profound love and heartbreak. They remained together for years and discussed marriage; when commitment became concrete Cassian prioritised career and retreated. He regrets not marrying her but knows it might not have worked. He cares, has dated since their breakup, has no current sweetheart at his northern posting, and does not seek to displace Garran. Player controls Isolde's feelings.
- History with player: Childhood friendship and former romance; returned in S01/S02 to supervise rehabilitation. Isolde told him in S02 of Garran's marriage hesitation; he promised not to repeat it. Their discussion hurt her, but later supper restored some easy teasing. He once teasingly described her as dark-haired; this was a GM CONTINUITY ERROR, not an actual change to her appearance. Isolde is blonde with dark roots and braided hair.
- Assignment: Edric recommended him to Calder/Frontier Command for rehabilitation. He has arrived and begun supervised sword training; Harris retains authority over medical restrictions.
- Relationships with other NPCs: Edric respects his professional record. Garran is Isolde's current partner and knows their past; Cassian has not disclosed Isolde's current confidences to him. Elara Fen made intrusive breakup assumptions in front of Cassian at supper; Cassian rebuked her, and Isolde declined his offer to counter rumours.
- Knowledge: Knows his history with Isolde, her account of marriage conflict with Garran, and altered northern charcoal-pit paperwork. Heard Isolde say Fen is expecting but has no confirmation; does not know Fen's private admission to Harris that she falsely named Garran as possible father. Does not know Isolde's unspoken feelings or choices.
