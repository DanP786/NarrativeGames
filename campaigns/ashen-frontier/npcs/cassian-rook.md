# Cassian Rook
- Age/rank: 27, Warden Sergeant; currently posted to a northern Warden garrison. Experienced advanced swordsmanship and conditioning instructor.
- Description/voice: Controlled, exacting, socially assured without Garran's exuberance. Notices mechanical errors in movement almost reflexively; speaks with a measured clarity that can feel painfully intimate when he recalls old habits. Physical appearance to emerge in play.
- Traits: ambitious, disciplined, emotionally avoidant under pressure, proud of competence, capable of genuine care without knowing how to prioritize it.
- Short-term goals: fulfil an anticipated Frontier Command assignment to help rehabilitate Isolde's swordsmanship and conditioning while respecting Harris's restrictions; preserve professional composure on seeing her again.
- Long-term goals: advance his Warden career; reconcile what he once wanted with what he was unwilling to promise Isolde. Whether he wants a renewed romance is undecided and must emerge organically.
- Methods & lines: Will challenge Isolde professionally, use shared training knowledge, withdraw from uncomfortable personal discussions. Won't intentionally jeopardize her neurological recovery or use Command authority to force intimacy. Last resort: request reassignment if professional conflict becomes untenable.
- Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers): Trust 64, Affection 69, Respect 83, Loyalty 36, Suspicion 18, Regret 63, Desire 43. These are provisional and can evolve through play.
- Relationship to Isolde: Childhood friend and former serious partner; their fathers served together. They trained with wooden swords as children, competed as adolescents, and became romantically involved in their late teens. Cassian was Isolde's first profound love and first serious heartbreak. They remained together for years and discussed a shared future, possibly marriage; Isolde hoped to build a life with him. When commitment became concrete, Cassian retreated, prioritizing career and unable or unwilling to choose the same future. The relationship ended painfully. He cared deeply but did not match her commitment. Do not presume Isolde's current private feelings; player controls them.
- History with player: Childhood friends; trained together; first serious romance from late teens into early adulthood; near-engagement discussions ended when Cassian would not commit. Have been apart for years. Neither Garran nor Edric is established to know the true depth or cause of their breakup.
- Relationships with other NPCs: Edric Varrick knew Cassian as a boy, respected his ability, and may recommend him to Frontier Command without knowing the romantic history. Garran Vale is Isolde's current partner; Cassian's knowledge of this and attitude are not established.
- Knowledge: Knows Isolde's old fighting habits, their shared history and his own failure to commit. Knows nothing of her private present feelings. Frontier Command appointment has not yet occurred on-screen; introduce after Isolde and Garran have had some time together. Training remains under Harris's medical restrictions; no Severance use.
- GM pacing: Do not introduce immediately in the current morning scene. Let the couple experience their new relationship before Cassian arrives. He is neither automatically villain nor predetermined romantic rival; friction should arise from real history and choices.
