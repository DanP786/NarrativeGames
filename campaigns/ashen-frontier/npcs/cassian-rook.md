# Cassian Rook
- Age/rank: 27, Warden Sergeant; currently posted to a northern Warden garrison. Experienced advanced swordsmanship and conditioning instructor.
- Description/voice: Controlled, exacting, socially assured without Garran's exuberance. Notices mechanical errors in movement almost reflexively; speaks with measured clarity that can feel painfully intimate when he recalls old habits. Physical appearance to emerge in play.
- Traits: ambitious, disciplined, emotionally avoidant under pressure, proud of competence, capable of genuine care without knowing how to prioritize it.
- Short-term goals: fulfil a forthcoming Frontier Command assignment to help rehabilitate Isolde's swordsmanship and conditioning while respecting Harris's restrictions; preserve professional composure on seeing her again.
- Long-term goals: advance Warden career; reconcile what he once wanted with what he was unwilling to promise Isolde. Whether he wants renewed romance is undecided and must emerge organically.
- Methods & lines: Will challenge Isolde professionally, use shared training knowledge, withdraw from uncomfortable personal discussions. Won't intentionally jeopardize neurological recovery or use Command authority to force intimacy. Last resort: request reassignment if professional conflict becomes untenable.
- Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers): Trust 64, Affection 69, Respect 83, Loyalty 36, Suspicion 18, Regret 63, Desire 43. Provisional and evolves through play.
- Relationship to Isolde: Childhood friend and former serious partner; their fathers served together. Trained with wooden swords as children, competed as adolescents, became romantically involved in late teens. Cassian was Isolde's first profound love and heartbreak. They remained together for years and discussed a shared future, possibly marriage; Isolde hoped to build a life with him. When commitment became concrete, Cassian retreated, prioritizing career and unable or unwilling to choose the same future. Relationship ended painfully. He cared deeply but did not match her commitment. Player controls Isolde's current private feelings.
- History with player: Childhood friends; trained together; first serious romance from late teens into early adulthood; near-engagement discussions ended when Cassian would not commit. Apart for years. Neither Garran nor Edric knows the true depth or cause of the breakup.
- Assignment: Edric Varrick, who has long respected Cassian's discipline and swordsmanship, has recommended him to Captain Calder/Frontier Command as an ideal specialist to help Isolde regain technique and conditioning. Edric genuinely believes he is helping his daughter and does not know how deeply Cassian hurt her. The recommendation is established off-screen; formal orders and Cassian's arrival should emerge later in play, not be treated as already delivered. Harris's restrictions still govern all training, with Torren Kest presently overseeing rehabilitation.
- Relationships with other NPCs: Edric Varrick knew Cassian as a boy and respects his professional record. Garran Vale is Isolde's current partner, a same-age corporal with eight years' service and possible promotion ahead; Edric recently questioned Garran's stalled rank. Cassian's knowledge of Garran and their relationship, and his attitude, are not established.
- Knowledge: Knows Isolde's old fighting habits, shared history and his own failure to commit. Knows nothing of her private present feelings. Introduce after Isolde and Garran have enjoyed some time together. No automatic antagonism or romance; friction should arise from authentic choices and history.
- GM pacing: Formal assignment and arrival forthcoming, not yet on-screen. Let their current relationship breathe before Cassian arrives. He is neither automatic villain nor predetermined romantic rival.
