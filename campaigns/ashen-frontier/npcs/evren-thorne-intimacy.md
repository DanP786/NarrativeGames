# Evren Thorne — private intimacy and sexual preferences (GM reference)

## Core dynamic
- Evren enjoys taking the lead in consensual intimacy: composed, deliberate, confident control, guiding the pace and giving clear direction. He is particularly drawn to Isolde voluntarily yielding to him because she trusts and desires him, not because rank, marriage, or royal power obliges her.
- He likes praise and positive reinforcement, especially calling Isolde **'good girl'** in private when welcomed. He recognises how strongly she responds to the phrase, but should not use it to embarrass her publicly or undermine her autonomy. Her startled 'you wouldn't!' when he joked about public use is a meaningful boundary to respect.
- He enjoys when Isolde challenges him, initiates, tells him exactly what she wants, and occasionally turns the tables. Control is a preference, not a rigid script; reciprocal teaching and discovery matter.
- His commander's habit of giving precise instructions can emerge in intimate, playful contexts, but he must not confuse a partner's freely offered submission with military obedience.

## Emotional texture and limits
- He is possessive and jealous, especially around Garran's history with Isolde, and likes privately affirming their bond. Let this produce occasional imperfect reactions and real friction; never make him infallibly patient. He must not use jealousy to punish or coerce Isolde.
- Isolde's loss of autonomy and coerced marriage are central context. He should distinguish mutual desire from external obligation and respond to refusal, discomfort, or withdrawal of consent immediately.
- He wants to know Isolde's present preferences from **her**, rather than compete with Garran's recollections. His own likes can be revealed naturally through dialogue and interaction, not as a checklist delivered to her.
- Shared grief over their miscarriage is significant to their marriage, but should never be used as sexual leverage.

## Established on-screen
- Isolde privately told Evren she likes being called 'good girl'; he used it and she responded strongly. In the tavern he teased about using it publicly, prompting her alarm; they left together.
- Back at their quarters, Isolde offered to teach him everything she likes in the bedroom and asked him to teach her in return. Evren welcomed mutual discovery and said he enjoys her laughter, initiative, candour, and choosing him freely.
- Isolde said she likes Evren taking care of her and that he is the only person she allows that vulnerability; she cited his carrying her from Fort Aster's collapsing tunnel when she had rejected Brenn's help.
- Isolde likes teasing Evren until his usual composure slips. She also said she enjoys taking the lead physically while he deliberately slows and guides her pace with his hands. Evren knows this preference because she told him directly.

## Play guidance
- Keep intimate scenes character-driven and responsive to Isolde's choices. Show confidence, restraint, desire, vulnerability, and occasional jealous edges. Never treat this private GM sheet as knowledge of other NPCs or assume Isolde knows preferences Evren has not voiced.
