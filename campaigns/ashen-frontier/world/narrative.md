The night before a new Warden expedition, Isolde Varrick returned to Blackthorn Bastion's off-duty life six weeks after the Greywatch disaster, while coordinated Blight activity gathered beyond the walls.

At Survey Station Seventeen, Isolde destroyed a Blight heart through Severance at devastating neurological cost, and Garran Vale rescued her. Rehabilitation and command restrictions followed. Garran and Isolde reconciled after a jealousy-driven betrayal involving Elara Fen, but his refusal to marry when she offered strained them. Cassian Rook, Isolde's childhood friend and first love, returned to help with her training and quietly investigated altered northern patrol records: a precise deer count obscured, absence of scavengers removed and the original transcription missing. The mystery remained open.

During S03 Isolde relinquished hope of marriage to Garran, though they reconciled enough to visit his family for solstice. A later expedition ended in Isolde's abduction and transfer to Eldoria, where King Alaric III compelled her marriage to Lord Commander Evren Thorne, another Severance wielder. GM-ONLY SECRET: Caladorn High Command traded Isolde instead of two other Severance wielders; Isolde, Garran, Cassian and Edric do not know, Calder's knowledge is unestablished. The king sought children who might inherit Severance, restricted her Warden identity and freedom, and subjected the marriage to coercive medical and household scrutiny. Isolde's relationship with Evren developed into mutual, freely expressed love despite its origin. She chose the Thorne name in Eldoria without renouncing her Varrick history.

Garran secretly received Isolde's northern inspection date and location and attempted to abduct her; a Blight attack followed. Isolde's Severance returned, and she and Evren severed the bindings of a central Blight mass apparently directing lesser creatures. The gift remains neurologically dangerous. Garran was spared without pardon. Isolde lost her Eldorian adviser and military role, and a king-approved provisional Blight observation protocol remained contingent on medical clearance and actual sightings. The Pell assassination inquiry and the Caladorn dispatch investigation are unresolved.

Several weeks after Isolde and Evren confessed mutual love, Anwen privately confirmed early pregnancy. The couple rejoiced but wished to decide when to tell others. A compulsory royal physician suspected pregnancy without certifying it; Alaric nevertheless announced an expected child to the entire court and held a lavish celebration. Isolde publicly accepted restrictions on frontier travel and Severance during pregnancy, while Evren resisted the crown's claims over her body and their child. The couple withdrew from the crowded celebration to a sheltered gallery. The baby's sex, inheritance, exact gestation and outcome remain unknown; the king's dynastic ambitions now directly threaten the family's privacy and autonomy.
