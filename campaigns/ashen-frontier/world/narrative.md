The night before a new Warden expedition, Isolde Varrick returns to Blackthorn Bastion's off-duty life six weeks after the Greywatch disaster, while coordinated Blight activity gathers beyond the walls.

The Survey Station Seventeen expedition brought Isolde into contact with a Blight heart, which she destroyed through Severance at devastating neurological cost. Garran Vale rescued her from the collapse, suffering a shoulder injury. Months of rehabilitation followed, with command restricting her duties and Harris overseeing her recovery. The cost of being Blackthorn's rare Severance wielder remains central: her combat skill endures, but physical recovery and her authority to return to the field cannot be rushed.

Isolde and Garran reconciled and publicly committed to one another, yet their relationship grew strained by doubts about duty, emotional responsibility and independence. In the weeks after a difficult night of questioning whether they should remain together, they continued to enjoy companionship without arguing, while Isolde privately withdrew from emotional intimacy. Her first love, Cassian Rook, returned from his northern posting as her rehabilitation sword instructor. Isolde's old hurt over Cassian has faded; she privately intends to marry neither man. At their first resumed training session in early Frostwane 312, her evolved technique surprised Cassian, though her endurance still lagged. Their first controlled three-exchange set ended two touches to one in Isolde's favour; Cassian has asked how it felt.
