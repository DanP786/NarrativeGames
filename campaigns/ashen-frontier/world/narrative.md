The night before a new Warden expedition, Isolde Varrick returns to Blackthorn Bastion's off-duty life six weeks after the Greywatch disaster, while coordinated Blight activity gathers beyond the walls.

The Survey Station Seventeen expedition brought Isolde into contact with a Blight heart, which she destroyed through Severance at devastating neurological cost. Garran Vale rescued her from the collapse, suffering a shoulder injury. Months of rehabilitation followed, with command restricting her duties and Harris overseeing her recovery. The cost of being Blackthorn's rare Severance wielder remains central: her combat skill endures, but physical recovery and her authority to return to the field cannot be rushed.

Isolde and Garran reconciled and publicly committed to one another after his earlier jealousy-driven betrayal involving Elara Fen. Their relationship remains loving but strained by his insecurity and repeated conflicts over commitment. During S02 Isolde told Garran she would marry him tomorrow, but he admitted he was not ready, fearing their recurring arguments and threats to leave. She left his quarters in tears and avoided him the following day; neither has ended the relationship. Garran has trial oversight of northern retaining-wall repairs and a written assessment for Calder, not an awarded promotion. Fen's pregnancy and paternity claims remain unverified.

Cassian Rook, Isolde's childhood friend and former serious partner, returned from his northern posting to supervise restricted sword rehabilitation. Their old hurt resurfaced when Isolde confided her marriage troubles and Cassian acknowledged regret for not marrying her, without seeking to displace Garran. They regained some conversational ease over supper; Cassian is keeping her confidence. Isolde has agency over her feelings and future, and neither man can know her unspoken thoughts.

A northern charcoal-pit patrol report has been altered: a precise deer count was blurred, a note about absence of scavengers removed without initials, and its original transcription is missing despite being registered received. Cassian is checking the paper trail; Isolde found no further confirmed pattern among patrol reports. Birds departing western roosts and an unidentified figure at the eastern treeline are separate unexplained observations, not proven connected. S02 closes at night with Isolde alone in her quarters.
