# Isolde Varrick
- Age: 24; woman; 5'8, athletic, strong arms and legs, fair Nordic features, piercing grey eyes, straight dark eyebrows with a scar through one; blonde hair with dark roots in fletter braids.
- Origin: military family. Father Edric Varrick, respected retired Warden officer injured after 25 years; mother Signe Varrick from outside the military. Isolde has multiple younger brothers, including Edric Jr., whom she describes as timid and burdened by sharing their father's name. Other brothers' names/ages unspecified. Isolde trained from childhood but did not plan to join the Wardens; rare Severance Ember manifested and made service unavoidable. She accepts it as duty. She rode horses and play-jousted as a child; her brothers were too small to participate with her.
- Station: Blackthorn Bastion; rank: Warden Sergeant; four years of service. Swordfighter, rare Severance bearer. As sergeant she can command a small field squad under officers such as Captain Rhea Calder.
- Defining history: Greywatch disaster; chose which trapped group to save; nine survived directly, fifteen of thirty-two lost. Official inquiry cleared her. Some peers disagree.
- Personality seed: guarded, distrustful, may retreat from emotional vulnerability; player chooses every present action, word and thought.
- Romantic history: Cassian Rook, a childhood friend and fellow Warden now 27, was Isolde's first serious love. They trained together as children and were a couple for years beginning in their late teens. They discussed marriage/a future together and came close to engagement, but Cassian drew back when lasting commitment became real; his ambition and inability to reciprocate fully ended the relationship. Isolde loved him deeply and this heartbreak is a possible major reason she subsequently guarded herself against commitment; player retains authority over her present thoughts and emotional interpretation. Edric Varrick does not know the painful truth. Later Isolde had a casual relationship with Garran Vale; last winter Garran tried to provoke jealousy by threatening to sleep with another woman, Isolde answered 'Fine,' he followed through, and they split. They reconciled during S01, have publicly acknowledged being together, and on 19 Frostwane 312 exchanged their first mutual declarations of love. Garran does not automatically know the Cassian history.
- Current condition (19 Frostwane 312): recovering from severe neurological backlash, concussion and physical injuries sustained destroying the Blight heart at Survey Station Seventeen. Ribs and basic mobility largely recovered; strength and endurance diminished, occasional fatigue-related tremor/headache. Harris permits supervised controlled combat rehabilitation with Torren Kest, not unrestricted sparring, duty, deployment or Severance.
- Current location: Blackthorn Bastion barracks common room, evening, sore after supervised training, speaking with Garran about possible summer jousting.
