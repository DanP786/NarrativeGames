# Significant deeds
- Six weeks before S01: during Greywatch disaster, Isolde chose to sever the Blight bonds around one trapped group, directly saving nine; seventeen of thirty-two returned. Formal inquiry found her decision tactically defensible.
- S01: At Survey Station Seventeen, Isolde destroyed a Blight heart through Severance despite catastrophic neurological backlash; Garran Vale and fellow Wardens rescued her from the collapse.
- S03: Survived abduction and forced diplomatic transfer from Caladorn to Eldoria; entered compelled marriage to Evren Thorne without relinquishing her Caladorn history.
- S03: At Northern Crossing, used restored Severance alongside Evren to destroy a central Blight binding coordinating other creatures, at neurological cost; Garran's abduction attempt ended with him spared but not pardoned.
- S03: Supplied king with tactical testimony about the Blight's apparent central commands; negotiated provisional observation discussion, though no mission or rank restored.
- S03: Chose the name Isolde Thorne in Eldoria, declared mutual love with Evren, and privately confirmed pregnancy with Anwen. King later publicly announced suspected pregnancy without her consent and imposed additional restrictions; she complied before court.
