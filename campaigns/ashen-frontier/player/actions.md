# Significant deeds
- Six weeks before S01: during Greywatch disaster, Isolde chose to sever the Blight bonds around one trapped group, directly saving nine; seventeen of thirty-two returned. Formal inquiry found her decision tactically defensible.
- S01: At Survey Station Seventeen, Isolde destroyed a Blight heart through Severance despite catastrophic neurological backlash; Garran Vale and fellow Wardens rescued her from the collapse. Her survival and prolonged rehabilitation have shaped Blackthorn's duty restrictions and command's view of rare Ember costs.
