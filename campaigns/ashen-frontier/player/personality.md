# Reputation and presentation
- Guarded and distrustful, especially in intimate relationships; player decides how this appears in any scene.
- Blackthorn Wardens recognise Isolde's swordsmanship and rare Severance. Some respect her; others resent command's special attention.
- Greywatch inquiry formally cleared her; unofficial blame and gossip persist.
- Among family, Varrick surname carries both respect and pressure.
