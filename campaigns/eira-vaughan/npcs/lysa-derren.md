# Lysa Derren

- Status: alive
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; Eira Vaughan's dorm roommate
- Assignment: not yet established
- Current location: shared first-year dorm room with Eira
- Public disposition toward Eira: Cautiously receptive
- Eira knowledge: Lysa shared Eira's dorm from the beginning of first year and sat beside her at breakfast on the morning after Conscription Day. She learned through Mara's teasing and the surrounding exchange that Soren Mair, the marked cadet Eira spent Conscription Night with, is one of the marked ones. She then witnessed Brinna Korr single Eira out over perceived loyalty to marked cadets and saw the ensuing fight. Since the second day, particularly after Brinna's death became known, Lysa has been noticeably distant from Eira rather than openly hostile. When Eira directly asks what is bothering her, Lysa admits she is frightened by what she witnessed: not Eira defending herself, but Eira continuing the choke after gaining control until Brinna went limp, followed by the news that Brinna died. Eira then explains that she did not know Brinna would stop if released because Brinna had been choking her hard enough that she could not breathe. Eira also candidly admits that she wanted to deter future attacks: because of her size, she feared mercy would be read as weakness and make her a target. Lysa now understands that the decision combined immediate survival fear with deliberate reputation management rather than indiscriminate violence. This honesty makes her more receptive to Eira, though she remains unsettled by the fatal consequence.
- Presentation: Cautious and conflict-averse rather than confrontational. When directly invited to speak, she tries to distinguish fear from accusation and acknowledges facts that complicate her discomfort, including that Brinna attacked Eira first. She responds positively to candid explanations even when the answer itself is morally uncomfortable.
- POV boundary: Knows only what she personally observes, is told, or could plausibly learn around the Riders Quadrant. She cannot know Eira's private motives unless Eira tells her.
