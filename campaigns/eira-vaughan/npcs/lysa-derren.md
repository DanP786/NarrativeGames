# Lysa Derren

- Status: alive
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; Eira Vaughan's dorm roommate
- Assignment: not yet established
- Current location: shared first-year dorm room with Eira
- Public disposition toward Eira: Cautiously receptive
- Eira knowledge: Lysa shared Eira's dorm from the beginning of first year and sat beside her at breakfast on the morning after Conscription Day. She learned through Mara's teasing and the surrounding exchange that Soren Mair, the marked cadet Eira spent Conscription Night with, is one of the marked ones. She then witnessed Brinna Korr single Eira out over perceived loyalty to marked cadets and saw the ensuing fight. Since the second day, particularly after Brinna's death became known, Lysa has been noticeably distant from Eira rather than openly hostile. When Eira directly asks what is bothering her, Lysa admits she is frightened by what she witnessed: not Eira defending herself, but Eira continuing the choke after gaining control until Brinna went limp, followed by the news that Brinna died. Eira then explains that she did not know Brinna would stop if released because Brinna had been choking her hard enough that she could not breathe. Eira also candidly admits that she wanted to deter future attacks: because of her size, she feared mercy would be read as weakness and make her a target. Lysa now understands that the decision combined immediate survival fear with deliberate reputation management rather than indiscriminate violence. This honesty makes her more receptive to Eira, though she remains unsettled by the fatal consequence. Lysa subsequently asks whether Eira's rapid involvement with Soren was also partly strategic, explaining that cadets commonly form romantic, sexual and social attachments that provide protection, training, information or useful connections. She cites Jessa Vale/Orrin Cade, Pell Rusk/Nadiya Sorn, and Catrin Bell/Harl Vey as examples based on what she has observed or heard around the quadrant. Eira tells Lysa that Soren was not originally a calculated survival arrangement: she is surprised things became physical so quickly, and Brinna's death has since sharpened her awareness that any of them might die soon. Eira nevertheless acknowledges with blunt humour that if sleeping with Soren also helps keep her alive to Threshing, she sees no reason to reject the incidental advantage.
- Presentation: Cautious and conflict-averse rather than confrontational. When directly invited to speak, she tries to distinguish fear from accusation and acknowledges facts that complicate her discomfort. She is more socially observant than her reserve initially suggests and pays attention to the informal alliances forming among cadets. She responds positively to candid explanations even when the answer itself is morally uncomfortable.
- POV boundary: Knows only what she personally observes, is told, or could plausibly learn around the Riders Quadrant. Her claims about other cadets' relationship motives are social observations and gossip, not privileged knowledge of their private thoughts. She cannot know Eira's private motives unless Eira tells her.
