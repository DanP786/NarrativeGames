# Lysa Derren

- Status: alive
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; Eira Vaughan's dorm roommate
- Assignment: not yet established
- Current location: Riders Quadrant; regularly around Eira through shared quarters
- Public disposition toward Eira: Cautiously receptive
- Eira knowledge: Lysa shared Eira's dorm from the beginning of first year and sat beside her at breakfast on the morning after Conscription Day. She learned through Mara's teasing and the surrounding exchange that Soren Mair, the marked cadet Eira spent Conscription Night with, is one of the marked ones. She then witnessed Brinna Korr single Eira out over perceived loyalty to marked cadets and saw the ensuing fight. Since the second day, particularly after Brinna's death became known, Lysa was noticeably distant from Eira rather than openly hostile. When Eira directly asked what was bothering her, Lysa admitted she was frightened by what she witnessed: not Eira defending herself, but Eira continuing the choke after gaining control until Brinna went limp, followed by the news that Brinna died. Eira explained that she did not know Brinna would stop if released because Brinna had been choking her hard enough that she could not breathe. Eira also candidly admitted that she wanted to deter future attacks: because of her size, she feared mercy would be read as weakness and make her a target. Lysa came to understand that the decision combined immediate survival fear with deliberate reputation management rather than indiscriminate violence. This honesty made her more receptive to Eira, though she remained unsettled by the fatal consequence. Lysa subsequently asked whether Eira's rapid involvement with Soren was also partly strategic, explaining that cadets commonly form romantic, sexual and social attachments that provide protection, training, information or useful connections. Eira told Lysa that Soren was not originally a calculated survival arrangement, while acknowledging the incidental survival advantages. By the second month, Eira's continuing training with small groups of marked cadets has become widely noticed and many other first-years socially avoid Eira. Lysa notices the contraction of Eira's social options but does not join it; she continues talking to Eira and sharing ordinary life with her. Her caution remains, but she sees no practical sense in refusing communication with someone she lives and trains alongside merely to follow the wider cohort's social pressure.
- Presentation: Cautious and conflict-averse rather than confrontational. When directly invited to speak, she tries to distinguish fear from accusation and acknowledges facts that complicate her discomfort. She is more socially observant than her reserve initially suggests and pays attention to the informal alliances forming among cadets. She responds positively to candid explanations even when the answer itself is morally uncomfortable.
- POV boundary: Knows only what she personally observes, is told, or could plausibly learn around the Riders Quadrant. Her claims about other cadets' relationship motives are social observations and gossip, not privileged knowledge of their private thoughts. She cannot know Eira's private motives unless Eira tells her.
