# Garrick Tavis

- Status: alive
- Role: senior Riders Quadrant cadet; marked one; canon character
- Current location: Basgiath War College, Riders Quadrant training area
- Public disposition toward Eira: Neutral
- Eira knowledge: Garrick has observed that Soren Mair is spending substantial extra time training Eira Vaughan, an unmarked first-year he has known only for a short time. He knows enough from observation to recognize Soren's unusual investment in her but has no access to Eira's private thoughts or to interactions he did not witness or learn about plausibly. After Soren loses a formal matchup at the beginning of the second month, Garrick immediately confronts him and attributes the loss at least partly to Soren being distracted by his involvement with Eira. Within Eira's hearing he tells Soren to get his head out of the clouds, says he is thinking with his cock, warns him to start thinking of his own survival first, and says, "She will get hurt, she will lose, she will die." Eira leaves for the healers before hearing the rest of the exchange.
- Presentation: Experienced, physically capable and protective of the marked cadets' survival interests. He tries to discourage Soren from over-investing time and attention in Eira, framing it as a survival concern rather than personal hostility toward her. His delivery can be harsh and unsentimental when he believes another marked cadet is risking survival.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he witnesses, is told, or could plausibly learn. Canon knowledge remains limited to what Garrick knows at this point in the Fourth Wing timeline.
