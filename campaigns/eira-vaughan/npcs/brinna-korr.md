# Brinna Korr

- Status: dead
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year
- Assignment: not established
- Final location: Basgiath War College
- Public disposition toward Eira before death: Hostile
- Eira knowledge: Brinna was the physically imposing first-year who confronted Eira at breakfast over perceived loyalty to marked cadets, mocked Eira's small size, dragged her from her seat and initiated a fight by choking her. Eira reversed the attack using lower-body leverage and maintained a neck hold until Brinna went limp. Brinna never regained consciousness and was confirmed dead by the next morning's roll call. Eira did not know her name during the fight; Mara learned it afterward and identified her as Brinna Korr.
- Presentation: Very large, physically strong female first-year; openly prejudiced against marked cadets and willing to use violence against Eira over perceived association with them.
- Death: Died after being rendered unconscious by Eira Vaughan's sustained choke during the breakfast fight on the morning after Conscription Day.
