# Soren Mair

- Status: alive
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; marked one
- Assignment: not Flame Section, First Squad; exact squad assignment not yet established on-screen
- Current location: his Riders Quadrant dorm room with Eira
- Public disposition: Interested
- Eira knowledge: Saw Eira after her successful crossing and found her physically attractive. Eira chose to stay with him at the gathering, explicitly encouraged their first kiss, and plainly told him she wanted to go with him. They spent Conscription Night together. During the night he learned that Eira had no previous sexual experience; this increased his tenderness and personal interest rather than creating any assumed entitlement. As they settled to sleep, he whispered, "You're mine," reflecting his own emerging possessiveness/attachment rather than any agreed relationship status. The next morning he kissed Eira before she left. At breakfast, he witnessed Eira being attacked by a much larger female first-year after the woman challenged Eira over perceived loyalty to marked cadets. Soren moved close enough toward the fight that Coren physically held him back; he saw Eira reverse the attack and choke the woman unconscious. After breakfast he pulled Eira aside, checked her visible injuries and asked whether she was hurt. Later that day, Soren deliberately volunteered for the same evening job duty Eira had been assigned, despite being in another squad. After seeing the aftermath of Eira's difficult first formal sparring lesson, he offered himself as a sparring partner who can teach her. During their evening stores duty, Eira prioritizes actually performing the assigned work, but once circumstances allow they find a secluded corner and share another consensual intimate encounter. Over the following days Soren makes good on his training offer and regularly works with Eira on hand-to-hand fundamentals. Garrick Tavis notices the amount of time Soren is investing in Eira and tries to discourage him from becoming so involved with an unmarked first-year he barely knows. Later that evening, Eira herself suggests Soren stop training her because she takes seriously the concern that he needs to protect his own survival and preparation; she does not frame it as rejection or lack of interest in him. Soren refuses to treat his voluntarily given time as something Eira is taking from him, while agreeing they can change the arrangement if his own preparation begins to suffer. Later in the first month, Soren's unnamed roommate dies, leaving Soren temporarily alone in their dorm room. Eira becomes comfortable spending private time there, including studying in his bed and borrowing one of his shirts. Their physical relationship and recurring training continue. When Eira eventually asks why he chose her, Soren tells her initial physical attraction was only the beginning. He was struck by her ordinary treatment of his rebellion relic, her candid admission that she entered Riders simply to discover whether she could do it, her refusal to perform fearlessness, her stubbornness and her habit of adapting after failure. Watching Brinna attack Eira made him realize that he wanted Eira to survive rather than merely wanting her physically, which became a major reason for his investment in training her. When Eira presses him on why he cares whether she survives, Soren admits that he has become attached to the ordinary details of her presence and now expects and wants her to remain part of his days. He cannot offer a grand explanation for such a rapid attachment, but the prospect of Eira disappearing from roll has become personally painful to contemplate. At the beginning of the second month, Soren loses a formal challenge matchup legitimately after being distracted by seeing Eira hurt; he treats the lapse as his own failure of focus and intends to correct it without withdrawing his attachment. Later in the second month he brings Eira into one of the marked cadets' small training rotations, sponsoring her presence for training without demanding the others trust her with private matters. During a water break Eira remarks, "Are all marked ones attractive?" Soren reacts with obvious jealousy. While she continues teasing him, he tips her face up by the chin and quietly tells her, "Mine." Eira later asks whether he is trying to make them official; Soren admits he wants exclusivity but leaves the choice with her. Eira accepts with teasing, and they are now mutually exclusive in practice. Later, after Eira aggravates a knee during hard training, Soren carefully wraps the sore/swollen joint in his room. Knowing her pain tolerance is abnormally high, he does not accept "it doesn't hurt much" as useful evidence and instead watches the injury's physical signs and function.
- Presentation: Tall, broad and strongly built; self-possessed, openly interested in Eira, and amused rather than discouraged by her scowls. His interest has a possessive edge, now grounded in mutually agreed exclusivity rather than an assumed claim. Jealous when Eira openly notices other attractive men, but aware that jealousy does not give him authority over her choices. Physically formidable but not invulnerable or unbeatable.
- Appearance continuity: Broad like a wardrobe. Dark rebellion relic on his right arm swirls like smoke over his skin, beginning on his hand and extending to his collarbone. Wears the black leather Riders uniform in the current period.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he personally observes or is told. He may interpret Eira's observable choices but cannot know her private beliefs unless she states them.
