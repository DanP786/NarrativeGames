# Soren Mair

- Status: alive; completed Gauntlet and Presentation; survived Threshing and returned to Basgiath bonded to a blue dragon
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; marked one
- Assignment: not Flame Section, First Squad; exact squad assignment not yet established on-screen
- Current location: Basgiath post-Threshing return field, newly landed on his bonded blue dragon
- Dragon: blue; name, sex, tail type, age, lineage and deeper personality/history not yet established
- Public disposition: Reconciled and mutually exclusive with Eira; intensely attached, jealous and possessive while recognizing those feelings do not grant authority
- Eira knowledge: Saw Eira after her successful Parapet crossing and was initially physically attracted to her; their relationship developed through consensual intimacy, recurring training, ordinary private time and growing attachment. Soren values Eira's unremarkable treatment of his rebellion relic, refusal to perform fearlessness, stubborn adaptability and ordinary presence in his days. He became protective after witnessing Brinna Korr attack her. Their relationship became mutually exclusive after Soren admitted jealousy and asked for exclusivity, which Eira accepted. A major rupture occurred over marked-cadet secrecy and misleading redirects; they reconciled under a clearer honesty boundary that permits Soren to refuse secrets plainly but not manufacture safer explanations. Their mutual exclusivity resumed.

Soren completed the official Gauntlet and Presentation alive. After Presentation, Bram Auln kissed Eira without permission; Eira immediately rejected the kiss and told Soren. Soren initially intended to punch Bram, but Eira stopped him by saying, 'I love you.' Eira repeated it shyly when Soren asked; Soren answered, 'I love you too.' Later Soren and Bram spoke without violence. Soren knows Bram's kiss was unsolicited and that Eira did not reciprocate. Eira and Soren publicly identify as girlfriend/boyfriend and are mutually exclusive.

During the post-Presentation celebration, Soren and Eira spent the night together cuddling/sleeping without sex. Soren told Eira he realized he needed her during their two-week rupture and knew/admitted to himself that he loved her at reconciliation. Eira told him she realized she loved him during Presentation and wants him around tomorrow and later. Neither promised survival. On Threshing morning they shared private intimacy before separating, later met fully equipped and again said they loved one another. Eira's last words to him before Threshing were, 'Don't die.' Soren answered, 'You either, Vaughan.'

Soren has now survived Threshing. He returns to Basgiath riding a blue dragon. Eira, who has been waiting anxiously after her own return, sees him land. His exact injuries and the blue dragon's details beyond color remain unestablished until shown.
- Presentation: Tall, broad and strongly built; self-possessed, openly interested in Eira, and usually amused rather than discouraged by her scowls. His interest has a pronounced possessive edge, particularly when jealous, but he understands it does not give him authority over her choices. Physically formidable but not invulnerable or unbeatable. When loyalty boundaries involving the marked ones are pressed, he becomes controlled and immovable. Eira is one of the few people/places around whom he can temporarily set down some of the constant pressure surrounding the marked ones.
- Appearance continuity: Broad like a wardrobe. Dark rebellion relic on his right arm swirls like smoke over his skin, beginning on his hand and extending to his collarbone. Wears the black leather Riders uniform in the current period.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he personally observes or is told. He may interpret Eira's observable choices but cannot know her private beliefs unless she states them.
