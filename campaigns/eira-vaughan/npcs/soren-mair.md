# Soren Mair

- Status: alive; officially completed the Gauntlet and Presentation
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; marked one
- Assignment: not Flame Section, First Squad; exact squad assignment not yet established on-screen
- Current location: walking away from the Presentation survivor gathering with Eira
- Public disposition: Reconciled and mutually exclusive with Eira; intensely attached, jealous and possessive while recognizing those feelings do not grant authority
- Eira knowledge: Saw Eira after her successful Parapet crossing and was initially physically attracted to her; their relationship developed through consensual intimacy, recurring training, ordinary private time and growing attachment. Soren values Eira's unremarkable treatment of his rebellion relic, refusal to perform fearlessness, stubborn adaptability and ordinary presence in his days. He became protective after witnessing Brinna Korr attack her. Their relationship became mutually exclusive after Soren admitted jealousy and asked for exclusivity, which Eira accepted. Soren's possessive language reflects chosen relationship dynamics and his own attachment, not authority over Eira's decisions.

A major rupture occurred when Eira challenged the asymmetry created by Soren protecting marked-cadet secrets while sometimes redirecting, minimizing or allowing misleading explanations to stand. For roughly two weeks Eira withdrew from Soren and the marked training rotations. During that separation Soren saw Bram Auln sitting pressed close to Eira at dinner and reacted with visible jealousy, briefly starting to rise before stopping himself because he recognized he had no right to intervene. Eira and Soren later reconciled after establishing a clearer honesty boundary: Soren may refuse information that is not his to disclose, but he will say so plainly rather than manufacture a safer explanation, and he will answer honestly about implications for his own danger as far as he can. Eira explicitly told him, "I'm yours," and their mutual exclusivity resumed. Soren answered with fierce affection while continuing to recognize that possessiveness does not grant authority.

On the morning of the official Gauntlet, Soren and Eira wake together and deliberately avoid discussing the danger until they have to rise. Before Eira reaches staging, Soren steals one last private moment and kisses her without adding further coaching. Eira completes the Gauntlet first. Hours later Soren completes his own official run alive. He accepts his squad's immediate celebration before looking for Eira. He finds her slung over Bram Auln's shoulder while Bram carries her around amid First Squad horseplay. Bram has previously triggered Soren's jealousy, and this sight provokes an immediate strong possessive/angry reaction layered over profound relief that Eira survived. After Bram puts Eira down, Soren approaches without disguising his jealousy. He does not threaten Bram or claim authority over Eira; instead he closes the distance to Eira, pulls her into a tight reunion embrace and kisses her with intense relief and possessive affection, making their relationship publicly obvious while repeatedly cutting a hard look toward Bram. The tension is visible, but no fight or explicit confrontation is established. Soren later survives Presentation as well. Afterward, dusty but alive, he walks toward the healers' tent and encounters Eira as she is being released after treatment for dragonfire burns. Eira grins on seeing him, then pulls him into a kiss with tears in her eyes from happiness and relief that they have both survived this far. Soren returns the kiss and holds her close. Immediately afterward Eira tells him plainly, "Bram kissed me." This is the first established moment Soren learns of Bram's unsolicited kiss. He does not have access to Eira's simultaneous private realization that she has fallen in love with him. Soren responds by pulling away and walking rapidly toward the First Squad survivors where Bram remains. His pace is angry and purposeful enough that shorter Eira has to jog intermittently to keep up. Eira tries to catch his arm before he reaches Bram, but Soren continues forward. On reaching Bram, Soren punches him in front of First Squad. Eira then orders Bram to keep his lips to himself and Soren to keep his fists to himself, and begins physically pushing Soren away. Soren allows Eira to move him rather than resisting her with his greater size. While she is doing so, Bram taunts from behind her, "She liked it you know." Eira responds by calmly walking back to Bram and slapping him once, then returns to Soren, takes his arm and leads him away. Soren goes with her rather than renewing the fight. He has now directly witnessed Eira reject Bram's provocative claim, although Eira has not necessarily given him the complete verbal account of the original kiss.
- Presentation: Tall, broad and strongly built; self-possessed, openly interested in Eira, and usually amused rather than discouraged by her scowls. His interest has a pronounced possessive edge, particularly when jealous, but he understands it does not give him authority over her choices. Physically formidable but not invulnerable or unbeatable. When loyalty boundaries involving the marked ones are pressed, he becomes controlled and immovable. Eira is one of the few people/places around whom he can temporarily set down some of the constant pressure surrounding the marked ones; after their rupture he understands that this refuge cannot depend on misleading her about risks that materially affect him. He remains intensely protective and jealous while actively distinguishing those feelings from authority over Eira.
- Appearance continuity: Broad like a wardrobe. Dark rebellion relic on his right arm swirls like smoke over his skin, beginning on his hand and extending to his collarbone. Wears the black leather Riders uniform in the current period.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he personally observes or is told. He may interpret Eira's observable choices but cannot know her private beliefs unless she states them.
