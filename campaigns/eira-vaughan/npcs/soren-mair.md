- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; marked one.
- Assignment: not Flame Section, First Squad; exact squad assignment not yet established on-screen.

- Public disposition: Reconciled and mutually exclusive with Eira; intensely attached, jealous and possessive while recognizing those feelings do not grant authority.

## GM-only signet continuity — not Eira's knowledge

**Secret second signet: Truthsense.** This manifests FIRST, soon after Threshing, before the publicly acknowledged signet. Soren senses when a speaker knowingly asserts something they themselves believe false. It does NOT reveal the true answer, read thoughts, expose withheld information, detect mere omissions, or identify honest errors, mistaken beliefs, ambiguity, or unspoken intent. Evasive statements that are literally believed true need not register. Initially involuntary/inconsistent, later controllable, with strain/fatigue and possible overload amid conflicting speech. His possession of this ability must remain secret from unmarked people including Eira; even his private understanding of it should develop in fiction, not be assumed mastered immediately. It does not authorize violating Eira's privacy; using it on her creates real ethical tension. Do not retroactively treat previous conversations as signet-detected.

**Public first signet: Kinetic Redirection.** Manifests SECOND, later, and becomes the one he acknowledges openly. Can change direction/distribution of existing force/impacts/momentum, not create force from nothing; early effects small and reflexive, precise control and larger forces require training. Significant redirection taxes body and can injure him; not invulnerability, flight, telekinesis, or automatic defense. Synergy with exacting blue Swordtail Rhovan. Neither signet has manifested as of Threshing night; future emergence should be paced in fiction.

**Marked-one lore:** In this campaign, all marked riders have a second signet associated with the rebellion relic; it is a dangerous collective secret, not known to Eira. The wider revelation belongs much later in the Empyrean timeline (user references end of *Onyx Storm*); do not reveal early. Their second signets need not be identical, and not all marked cadets have necessarily manifested both yet.

- Eira knowledge: Saw Eira after her successful Parapet crossing and was initially physically attracted to her; their relationship developed through consensual intimacy, recurring training, ordinary private time and growing attachment. Their mutual exclusivity resumed.
