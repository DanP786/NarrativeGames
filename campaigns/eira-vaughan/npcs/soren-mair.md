# Soren Mair

- Status: alive; officially completed the Gauntlet
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; marked one
- Assignment: not Flame Section, First Squad; exact squad assignment not yet established on-screen
- Current location: official Gauntlet finish area
- Public disposition: Interested; reconciled and mutually exclusive with Eira after a roughly two-week rupture
- Eira knowledge: Saw Eira after her successful crossing and found her physically attractive. Eira chose to stay with him at the gathering, explicitly encouraged their first kiss, and plainly told him she wanted to go with him. They spent Conscription Night together. During the night he learned that Eira had no previous sexual experience; this increased his tenderness and personal interest rather than creating any assumed entitlement. As they settled to sleep, he whispered, "You're mine," reflecting his own emerging possessiveness/attachment rather than any agreed relationship status. The next morning he kissed Eira before she left. At breakfast, he witnessed Eira being attacked by a much larger female first-year after the woman challenged Eira over perceived loyalty to marked cadets. Soren moved close enough toward the fight that Coren physically held him back; he saw Eira reverse the attack and choke the woman unconscious. After breakfast he pulled Eira aside, checked her visible injuries and asked whether she was hurt. Later that day, Soren deliberately volunteered for the same evening job duty Eira had been assigned, despite being in another squad. After seeing the aftermath of Eira's difficult first formal sparring lesson, he offered himself as a sparring partner who can teach her. During their evening stores duty, Eira prioritizes actually performing the assigned work, but once circumstances allow they find a secluded corner and share another consensual intimate encounter. Over the following days Soren makes good on his training offer and regularly works with Eira on hand-to-hand fundamentals. Garrick Tavis notices the amount of time Soren is investing in Eira and tries to discourage him from becoming so involved with an unmarked first-year he barely knows. Later that evening, Eira herself suggests Soren stop training her because she takes seriously the concern that he needs to protect his own survival and preparation; she does not frame it as rejection or lack of interest in him. Soren refuses to treat his voluntarily given time as something Eira is taking from him, while agreeing they can change the arrangement if his own preparation begins to suffer. Later in the first month, Soren's unnamed roommate dies, leaving Soren temporarily alone in their dorm room. Eira becomes comfortable spending private time there, including studying in his bed and borrowing one of his shirts. Their physical relationship and recurring training continue. When Eira eventually asks why he chose her, Soren tells her initial physical attraction was only the beginning. He was struck by her ordinary treatment of his rebellion relic, her candid admission that she entered Riders simply to discover whether she could do it, her refusal to perform fearlessness, her stubbornness and her habit of adapting after failure. Watching Brinna attack Eira made him realize that he wanted Eira to survive rather than merely wanting her physically, which became a major reason for his investment in training her. When Eira presses him on why he cares whether she survives, Soren admits that he has become attached to the ordinary details of her presence and now expects and wants her to remain part of his days. At the beginning of the second month, Soren loses a formal challenge matchup legitimately after being distracted by seeing Eira hurt; he treats the lapse as his own failure of focus. Later in the second month he brings Eira into one of the marked cadets' small training rotations, sponsoring her presence without demanding the others trust her with private matters. During a water break Eira remarks, "Are all marked ones attractive?" Soren reacts with jealousy and later admits he wants exclusivity; Eira accepts, making them mutually exclusive in practice. During Gauntlet preparation Eira increasingly uses his room as a familiar private space and steals his shirts; Soren admits he finds her scent lingering on them intensely distracting and privately likes the evidence of her presence.

A major argument develops when Eira presses him about his family, Garrick, the marked cadets' organization and why conversations close around her. Soren distinguishes personal information he can share from secrets involving other marked cadets and their families that are not his to disclose. Eira clarifies that she does not demand those secrets; her objection is being lied to or deliberately misled while being asked to treat the relationship as deeply trusting. Soren acknowledges that he has sometimes redirected, minimized or allowed incomplete explanations to stand in order to protect information he cannot disclose. Eira interprets the resulting asymmetry as being permitted into the part of his life where he can escape pressure while consequential parts remain behind a wall, and she storms out.

For roughly two weeks afterward Eira stops attending the marked-cadet training rotations, does not return to Soren's room, has no sexual/romantic contact with him, and keeps shared stores duties sparse and practical. Soren sees new minor training injuries accumulate and becomes increasingly angry and worried while deliberately respecting that he has no authority to inspect, instruct, or stop her. At dinner he sees Bram Auln sitting pressed close to Eira and becomes visibly jealous: he stares, shoves/scrapes his bench back and briefly starts to rise before stopping himself because he recognizes he has no right to intervene. He does not confront or threaten Bram.

During a later shared stores duty Soren and Eira finally confront the rupture directly. Soren states that a sex-only arrangement is not enough for him and that he wants Eira's ordinary presence, arguments, training, room-sharing habits and deeper relationship. Eira explains that her real fear around his secrecy is not entitlement to marked-cadet information but being unable to tell whether Soren is in serious danger, including the possibility of arrest, interrogation or torture. Soren admits he misunderstood and that misleading redirects were unfair. They establish a new boundary: secrets belonging to others remain protected, but Soren will plainly say when he cannot answer rather than manufacture a safer explanation; if Eira asks whether a hidden matter creates danger for him, he will answer its implications honestly as far as he can without betraying protected details. He cannot promise that marked status and perceived treason could never expose him to arrest or brutal interrogation, but no imminent known arrest is established.

Their affection resumes. Eira explicitly makes clear that she considers them back together and, when Soren asks whether she is his again, answers, "I'm yours." Their prior mutual exclusivity is renewed. Soren answers the declaration by kissing her fiercely enough to surprise her, then checks his momentum rather than treating reconciliation as unlimited consent. His possessive language is mutually chosen relationship language, not ownership or authority over Eira's choices.

On the morning of the official Gauntlet, Soren and Eira wake together and deliberately avoid discussing the danger until they have to rise. Before Eira reaches staging, Soren steals one last private moment and kisses her without adding further coaching. Eira completes the Gauntlet first. Hours later Soren completes his own official run alive. He accepts his squad's immediate celebration at the top before looking for Eira. When he finds her, she is visibly slung over Bram Auln's shoulder and protesting while Bram carries her around the First Squad group; Soren has previously been jealous of Bram's physical closeness with Eira and knows Eira remains mutually exclusive with him, but his response to this new sight is not yet established.
- Presentation: Tall, broad and strongly built; self-possessed, openly interested in Eira, and usually amused rather than discouraged by her scowls. His interest has a possessive edge but he understands it does not give him authority over her choices. Physically formidable but not invulnerable or unbeatable. When loyalty boundaries involving the marked ones are pressed, he becomes controlled and immovable. Eira is one of the few people/places around whom he can temporarily set down some of the constant pressure surrounding the marked ones; after their rupture he understands that this refuge cannot depend on misleading her about risks that materially affect him. He remains intensely protective and jealous but is actively distinguishing those feelings from authority over Eira.
- Appearance continuity: Broad like a wardrobe. Dark rebellion relic on his right arm swirls like smoke over his skin, beginning on his hand and extending to his collarbone. Wears the black leather Riders uniform in the current period.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he personally observes or is told. He may interpret Eira's observable choices but cannot know her private beliefs unless she states them.
