# Soren Mair

- Status: alive
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; marked one
- Assignment: not Flame Section, First Squad; exact squad assignment not yet established on-screen
- Current location: his first-year dorm room with Eira
- Public disposition: Interested
- Eira knowledge: Saw Eira after her successful crossing and found her physically attractive. Eira chose to stay with him at the gathering, explicitly encouraged their first kiss, and plainly told him she wanted to go with him. They spent Conscription Night together. During the night he learned that Eira had no previous sexual experience; this increased his tenderness and personal interest rather than creating any assumed entitlement. As they settled to sleep, he whispered, "You're mine," reflecting his own emerging possessiveness/attachment rather than any agreed relationship status. The next morning he kissed Eira before she left. At breakfast, he witnessed Eira being attacked by a much larger female first-year after the woman challenged Eira over perceived loyalty to marked cadets. Soren moved close enough toward the fight that Coren physically held him back; he saw Eira reverse the attack and choke the woman unconscious. After breakfast he pulled Eira aside, checked her visible injuries and asked whether she was hurt. Later that day, Soren deliberately volunteered for the same evening job duty Eira had been assigned, despite being in another squad. After seeing the aftermath of Eira's difficult first formal sparring lesson, he offered himself as a sparring partner who can teach her. During their evening stores duty, Eira prioritizes actually performing the assigned work, but once circumstances allow they find a secluded corner and share another consensual intimate encounter. Over the following days Soren makes good on his training offer and regularly works with Eira on hand-to-hand fundamentals. Garrick Tavis notices the amount of time Soren is investing in Eira and tries to discourage him from becoming so involved with an unmarked first-year he barely knows. Later that evening, Eira herself suggests Soren stop training her because she takes seriously the concern that he needs to protect his own survival and preparation; she does not frame it as rejection or lack of interest in him. Soren refuses to treat his voluntarily given time as something Eira is taking from him, while agreeing they can change the arrangement if his own preparation begins to suffer. Later in the first month, Soren's unnamed roommate dies, leaving Soren temporarily alone in their dorm room. Eira becomes comfortable spending private time there, including studying in his bed and borrowing one of his shirts. Their physical relationship and recurring training continue, but they have not established exclusivity or a formal relationship status.
- Presentation: Tall, broad and strongly built; self-possessed, openly interested in Eira, and amused rather than discouraged by her scowls. His interest developed a possessive edge after their first night together, though he has no basis to assume Eira shares that framing unless she says so.
- Appearance continuity: Broad like a wardrobe. Dark rebellion relic on his right arm swirls like smoke over his skin, beginning on his hand and extending to his collarbone. Wears the black leather Riders uniform in the current period.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he personally observes or is told. He may interpret Eira's observable choices but cannot know her private beliefs unless she states them.
