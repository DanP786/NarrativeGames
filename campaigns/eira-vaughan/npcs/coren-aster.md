# Coren Aster

- Status: alive
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; marked one
- Current location: secluded Riders Quadrant training space with Soren and Eira
- Public disposition: Neutral
- Eira knowledge: Previously noticed Soren staring at Eira and teased him about it. At breakfast the morning after Conscription Day, Coren witnessed a much larger female cadet attack Eira over her perceived association with marked cadets. He physically held Soren back as Eira reversed the fight and choked the attacker unconscious. Having inferred that Soren and Eira spent the previous night together, he loudly needled Soren, "You had your head between those thighs?" Several days later he happened upon Eira alone during an early-morning gymnastics/flexibility practice and, impressed by what he saw and characteristically irreverent about Soren's attraction to her, remarked that he could see what Soren sees in her. Later in the second month, Soren brings Eira to a small marked-cadet training rotation in which Coren participates. Coren is willing to train with her and already has more social familiarity with her than many marked cadets do, but he does not treat Soren's trust as transferable proof that Eira can be trusted with marked-cadet confidences.
- Presentation: Socially alert and quick to needle a companion when he catches something amusing. Comfortable using provocative humour, especially around Soren and Eira's obvious attraction. Beneath the humour he understands the marked cadets' need for caution and does not casually expose private information to an outsider.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he personally observes or is told.
