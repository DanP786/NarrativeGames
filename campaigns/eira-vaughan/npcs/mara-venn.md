# Mara Venn

- Status: alive; officially completed the Gauntlet
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year
- Assignment: Flame Section, First Squad
- Current location: official Gauntlet finish area
- Public disposition: Warm
- Eira knowledge: Met Eira immediately after the Parapet and discovered her reserved manner hides dry humour. Sought Eira out again in the dormitory, pulled her to the informal first-night gathering, and teased her about Soren Mair's obvious physical interest. Left Eira later that night to pursue her own romantic/sexual interest. The following morning, Mara learned she had been moved into Flame Section, First Squad with Eira and sought Eira out at breakfast. She witnessed Brinna Korr, a much larger female first-year, attack Eira over perceived loyalty to marked cadets and saw Eira reverse the fight and choke Korr unconscious. The exchange around the fight, including Coren's teasing of Soren, gave Mara strong observable reason to conclude Eira spent the previous night with Soren. After Eira's food was scattered during the fight, Mara silently shared her own breakfast. On the way to first instruction, Mara saw Soren pull Eira aside by the arm; after recognizing him, she left them to it with an amused smile. After the fight Mara made a point of learning the attacker's name. At roll call the next morning, she quietly told Eira the dead cadet was Brinna Korr when it became clear Korr never regained consciousness. By the second month, Eira's continuing training with small groups of marked cadets has become widely noticed and many other first-years socially avoid Eira. Mara does not follow that retreat. She remains personally warm and continues talking to Eira both because they are squadmates who need to function together and because her friendship predates the broader social reaction. During First Squad's scored casualty exercise she sees Eira's bad knee and works with her normally while Vale assigns around the injury. After Eira visits a healer and improves substantially, Mara welcomes her naturally when Eira chooses to sit with First Squad at lunch. On the morning of the official Gauntlet, Mara braids Eira's hair tightly out of her eyes while Eira eats breakfast. Mara later completes the official Gauntlet alive after Eira finishes first; at the top Eira hugs her tightly in open relief and Mara returns it. Two unnamed First Squad first-years die during the squad's Gauntlet attempts.
- Presentation: Bright, energetic and socially forward; approaches strangers readily and has rapidly taken a liking to Eira. She is practical enough not to sabotage squad cohesion over broader cohort prejudice and comfortable enough with Eira to tease her without withdrawing support.
- POV boundary: Knows only what she personally observes or is told.
