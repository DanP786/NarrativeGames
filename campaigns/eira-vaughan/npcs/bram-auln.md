# Bram Auln

- Status: alive; officially completed the Gauntlet and Presentation
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; Flame Section, First Squad
- Current location: Presentation survivor gathering with First Squad after Eira and Soren walk away
- Public disposition toward Eira: Respectful as a squadmate but openly romantically drawn to her; has crossed a stated relationship boundary and been rejected
- Eira knowledge: Bram knows Eira as a squadmate who killed Brinna Korr after being attacked, survived hard training encounters and became conspicuously associated with marked cadets. He does not like or understand the marked-cadet association, but considers refusing to work with a squadmate idiotic when survival depends on cooperation. During the scored casualty exercise his size and raw strength make him a major load-bearer while Eira contributes through sequencing and observation because of her injured knee; he sees her planning with Nessa work and gains practical respect for her. Later he becomes casually physically comfortable with Eira at a squad dinner, sitting pressed close enough to provoke a visible jealous reaction from Soren Mair, though no kiss, romance or sexual relationship occurs. During Eira and Soren's roughly two-week rupture, Bram notices their distance. Immediately before the official Gauntlet he realizes Eira and Soren have reconciled and openly tells Eira he thinks returning to Soren is stupid because Soren is marked and the association brings trouble; this is disapproval of the marked association rather than a claim of romantic entitlement. Vale shuts down the discussion so the squad can focus. Bram later completes the official Gauntlet alive. At the finish he pulls Eira into a large bear hug and holds it noticeably longer than necessary; Eira allows the hug rather than pushing him away. Two unnamed First Squad first-years die during the squad's official Gauntlet attempts. While the surviving squad later sits together laughing and decompressing, Bram participates in playful physical banter by showing off his very large biceps beside Eira's much smaller but visibly strong arms. When another squadmate revives the joke, Bram says his arms have already been established as being for carrying Eira, then physically demonstrates by lifting Eira over his shoulder and carrying her casually around for longer than the joke strictly requires. Eira allows the horseplay. When Bram finally puts her down, he gives her a complicated lingering look that visibly suggests his feelings may extend beyond ordinary squad camaraderie. Later, after Eira publicly jokes that she is taken, Bram remarks that Mair made sure of that, showing clear awareness of Eira and Soren's relationship. Bram subsequently survives Presentation. During the surviving squad's celebration on the safe side, he approaches Eira and kisses her without first asking. Eira immediately pushes him away with both hands on his chest, exclaims, "Bram!" and warns, "You better hope Soren doesn't find out." The surrounding squad reacts with shock. Eira's response is a rejection of the kiss; no romantic reciprocation is established. Eira then jokingly warns the squad not to tell Mair if they value their lives and want to bond dragons the next day, slaps Bram once on the chest, rolls her eyes at him, and leaves to have her dragonfire burn treated. Bram is left with the squad after the rejection. Later Soren approaches after learning that Bram kissed Eira and punches Bram. As Eira orders both men to stop and begins pushing Soren away, Bram deliberately tells Soren, "She liked it you know." Bram knows this is not an accurate description of Eira's response because he personally experienced her immediate rejection; the statement is a deliberate provocation after being punched, not evidence of reciprocity. Eira responds by calmly walking back to Bram and slapping him once, then immediately returns to Soren and leads him away by the arm. Bram does not receive or deliver any further established blows.
- Presentation: Large, physically powerful and straightforward. Bram tends to evaluate immediate practical usefulness before personality. His attraction to Eira became overt enough that he acted impulsively across a boundary he knew existed. Eira rejected that advance. After Soren punched him, Bram knowingly misrepresented Eira's response in order to provoke Soren and Eira slapped him for it. He has no authority over Eira's relationships, and his feelings do not establish reciprocity or entitlement.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he personally observes, is told, or could plausibly hear within the squad/quadrant.
