# Bram Auln

- Status: alive; officially completed the Gauntlet
- Role: Riders Quadrant first-year; Flame Section, First Squad
- Current location: official Gauntlet finish area with surviving First Squad cadets
- Public disposition toward Eira: Respectful and increasingly personally drawn to her as a squadmate; still wary/disapproving of her marked associations
- Eira knowledge: Bram knows Eira as a squadmate who killed Brinna Korr after being attacked, survived hard training encounters and became conspicuously associated with marked cadets. He does not like or understand the marked-cadet association, but considers refusing to work with a squadmate idiotic when survival depends on cooperation. During the scored casualty exercise his size and raw strength make him a major load-bearer while Eira contributes through sequencing and observation because of her injured knee; he sees her planning with Nessa work and gains practical respect for her. Later he becomes casually physically comfortable with Eira at a squad dinner, sitting pressed close enough to provoke a visible jealous reaction from Soren Mair, though no kiss, romance or sexual relationship occurs. During Eira and Soren's roughly two-week rupture, Bram notices their distance. Immediately before the official Gauntlet he realizes Eira and Soren have reconciled and openly tells Eira he thinks returning to Soren is stupid because Soren is marked and the association brings trouble; this is disapproval of the marked association rather than a claim of romantic entitlement. Vale shuts down the discussion so the squad can focus. Bram later completes the official Gauntlet alive. At the finish he pulls Eira into a large bear hug and holds it noticeably longer than necessary; Eira allows the hug rather than pushing him away. Two unnamed First Squad first-years die during the squad's official Gauntlet attempts. While the surviving squad later sits together laughing and decompressing, Bram participates in playful physical banter by showing off his very large biceps beside Eira's much smaller but visibly strong arms. When another squadmate revives the joke, Bram says his arms have already been established as being for carrying Eira, then physically demonstrates by lifting Eira over his shoulder and carrying her casually around for longer than the joke strictly requires. Eira allows the horseplay. When Bram finally puts her down, he gives her a complicated lingering look that visibly suggests his feelings may extend beyond ordinary squad camaraderie, though he does not confess, make a demand, or challenge her exclusivity with Soren. Eira has not yet responded to or interpreted that look.
- Presentation: Large, physically powerful and straightforward. Bram tends to evaluate immediate practical usefulness before personality. He has grown markedly more physically/socially comfortable with Eira and appears increasingly personally drawn to her, while remaining wary of her marked associations. He can be blunt enough to overstep socially but does not have authority over Eira's relationships.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he personally observes, is told, or could plausibly hear within the squad/quadrant.
