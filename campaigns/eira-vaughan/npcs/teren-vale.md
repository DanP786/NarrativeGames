# Teren Vale

- Status: alive
- Role: Riders Quadrant second-year; Flame Section, First Squad leader
- Current location: Riders Quadrant during Gauntlet practice period
- Public disposition toward Eira: Neutral-to-respectful; attentive to competence and recurring injury risk
- Eira knowledge: Teren knows Eira as one of his first-years: small, physically capable in unusual ways, associated increasingly with marked cadets, responsible for Brinna Korr's death during an attack, and injured often enough that her willingness to push through damage is a recurring concern. During a scored casualty exercise he immediately noticed that Eira's knee altered her gait and refused to use her for heavy loading or twisting work merely because she reported for duty. Eira and Nessa Tor developed a controlled staged-transfer solution for a broken-wall obstacle, and Eira contributed through observation, sequencing and practical problem-solving rather than load-bearing. Teren noted positively that she obeyed the physical limitation but warned that repeatedly arriving injured reduces the options she gives her squad.

Later, after Eira directly asks Teren to train her because she is still losing most formal matches, he agrees on the practical basis that improving a first-year's survival improves his squad. He diagnoses a central flaw in her developing combat style: Eira often solves a problem only after it has already become a bad exchange, and her high pain tolerance encourages her to treat absorbing damage as an acceptable cost of gathering information. Teren trains her to recognize a losing exchange earlier, abandon it before the clean hit lands, and measure improvement by avoided damage rather than by how much punishment she can function through. During the later Gauntlet period Eira continues training with Teren separately while also resuming work with Soren Mair. Teren treats this as useful cross-training rather than a personal rivalry and remains focused on making Eira a less costly, more reliable member of First Squad.
- Presentation: Controlled, concise and unsentimental. Teren treats squad leadership as resource management under lethal conditions: he notices what people can actually do, dislikes wasted motion and performative toughness, and is willing to give abrupt orders without making them personal. He does not cultivate intimacy with first-years but pays attention to competence and recurring liabilities. He is distinct from Dain Aetos, who leads Second Squad.
- POV boundary: Knows only what he personally observes, is officially told, or could plausibly learn as First Squad's second-year leader.
