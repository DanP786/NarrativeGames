# Campaign setup

Created: 5 October 2026 (real-world date)
Slug: eira-vaughan

## Onboarding answers

**Setting / premise:** Empyrean / Fourth Wing universe, beginning on Conscription Day at the Parapet at the beginning of *Fourth Wing*. Eira Vaughan is in Violet Sorrengail's entering cohort. Canon characters, politics, dragons, locations and major world events exist and behave consistently with canon, but Eira is protagonist of her own story. Canon chronology progresses independently; Eira knows only what she witnesses or what could plausibly spread through Basgiath. Her presence may alter events if her actions plausibly justify it. Do not force Eira into Violet's plot or pre-write Eira's destiny.

**Voice:** Third person, present tense.

**Prose style:** Character-close and atmospheric. Plenty of description and longer prompts. Social observation, physical experience, tension, attraction, resentment, quieter interpersonal beats and emotional texture receive substantial room alongside sharp action. The GM may voice Eira's ordinary dialogue, humour, mannerisms, likely reactions and minor moment-to-moment choices from the detailed seed; genuinely consequential decisions remain with the player.

**Length:** Open-ended sandbox, while progressing alongside the Fourth Wing timeline. No fixed endpoint and no act tracker.

**Tone / content posture:** Dangerous military-academy atmosphere matching the books' adult register: competition, friendships, violence, training, dragons, politics, humour, attraction, romance, sexuality, injury, trauma and genuine mortality may appear on-page when fictionally appropriate. Nothing categorically faded out.

**Agency posture:** Brutal Basgiath. The world does not protect Eira because she is the protagonist. Poor decisions, bad matchups and inadequate preparation can cause serious injury, failure, lost opportunities or death. Canon characters retain their established personalities and relationships rather than warping around Eira. NPCs have independent motives and do not automatically like, desire, agree with or recognise Eira as special.

**Death rule:** Hard death enabled.

**Mind-reading / supernatural information:** Follow Empyrean lore. Dragon/rider bonds permit their established mental communication and emotional bleed. Signets that access memories, truth or other information work according to their canonical conditions and limits. Private thoughts are otherwise private; narration does not grant NPCs access to Eira's mind.

**Main thread seed:** Eira voluntarily enters the Riders Quadrant because she needs to discover what she is actually capable of. Basgiath will test the difference between proving herself and destroying herself in the attempt. This is a starting question, not an ordained arc.

**Opening:** Eira waits in the queue to cross the Parapet on Conscription Day.

## Character seed — Eira Vaughan

Eira Vaughan is a 161 cm / 5'3" civilian-born woman from a secure working family, entering the Riders Quadrant voluntarily. She has no connection to Basgiath or dragon riders. She chose Riders partly because it is dangerous and partly from pride: she is accustomed to being underestimated and wants to discover whether she can do considerably more than people assume. Her attitude is not destiny but: "I don't know whether I can do this. I'm going to find out."

She has a compact athletic hourglass build, strong developed legs, good core, comparatively weaker upper-body strength, light skin, blue-green eyes, and shoulder-length naturally auburn hair dyed with a honey-blonde balayage. The colouring may be maintained/customised and is not arbitrarily removed for military aesthetics. Her features are symmetrical and conventionally pretty without making strangers stop and stare. Her full, softly arched eyebrows are exceptionally expressive.

Eira grew up active and sporty. Strengths: excellent endurance, very strong legs, good core, excellent balance and coordination, comfort with heights, very good swimming, extremely high pain tolerance. She did gymnastics and childhood wrestling, especially with her brother. Swimming and drawing feel naturally talented; most other abilities come through repetition and analysis. Her failure loop is: fail, identify problem, change approach, repeat. She is not especially fast. Her upper-body strength is mediocre relative to the rest of her body. Larger opponents can overpower her in raw-strength contests. She has a history of broken bones and tendon/ligament injuries because high activity, low fear and high pain tolerance combine badly. Pain does not reliably make her protect an injury.

At Basgiath entry she is coordinated, willing to fight and difficult to intimidate but lacks formal combat training. Her eventual ideal close-combat style may develop through timing, leverage, balance disruption, throws, hooking, joint manipulation and using size/momentum against opponents, but this must be earned. She begins with a compact one-handed tomahawk/war axe she particularly likes. A melee spear is likely to suit her later. Paired tomahawks, throwing skill and crossbow competence must be earned through training; she begins with average aim and is not a javelin prodigy.

Eira is genuinely terrible at navigation. She can analyse a map placed in front of her but has poor intuitive direction in unfamiliar surroundings. This is a mundane persistent weakness, not a hidden future talent.

She is intelligent but poorly suited to rote academics. Factual memory is weak unless information is repeatedly used. She learns visually and practically, loves understanding systems and problem-solving, and struggles to leave inefficient systems alone. Applied tactics and visual battlefield analysis suit her better than disconnected names/dates.

Eira prefers information and contingencies: Plan A, then B, then C. When chaos becomes too large, she solves the next immediate problem. In emergencies she may issue useful practical observations without seeking command. She has a quick temper, challenges perceived injustice, can admit when wrong, and is strongly competitive. Superior people become benchmarks more readily than objects of resentment. She respects sensible rules and competent authority; arbitrary rules and incompetent leadership receive increasing challenge. She does not seek leadership if someone else is doing it well.

She has a strong but non-suicidal sense of fairness. She protects people she loves while respecting their autonomy. She is emotionally observant and may quietly help without forcing confidences. She resists peer pressure when she has a reasoned opinion. First impression: quiet, sweet, polite, reserved. With trusted people she becomes savagely witty, dry, observational and precise.

Eira is straight and sexually inexperienced at entry without considering that especially meaningful. She can pursue casual intimacy without treating it as love and is selective about committed emotional closeness. She is particularly attracted to larger, strongly built men and genuine confidence; performative arrogance is not attractive. No romantic partner is predetermined.

## Family

**Gareth Vaughan (father):** Carpenter/joiner, civilian working background. Quick temper around dishonesty/injustice, fair, not cruel, capable of admitting error. Eira grew up helping with tools, materials, measuring, small repairs and practical work.

**Elin Vaughan (mother):** Seamstress/tailor specialising in repairs and alterations. Quieter and more calculating than Gareth, observes before acting and plans ahead. Eira learned sewing, mending, measuring and practical fabric/leather repairs from helping her.

Eira had a happy, secure childhood. Her parents love her and would have preferred her ordinary and alive. They supported their son's military ambition more readily than Eira's Riders choice. Eira interprets their concern partly as believing Rhys capable of extraordinary things but not her, strengthening her determination.

**Rhys Vaughan (older brother):** Three years older. Entered Basgiath through Infantry and is now a senior infantry cadet/officer candidate on a leadership trajectory. Taller, more imposing and deliberately prepared for military service. Their relationship is loving. He thinks Riders is a terrible choice because he understands its lethality. Once Eira commits, he does everything legitimately available to help her survive, within severe quadrant-rule limits. Eira remains his little sister regardless of achievement.

## Initial attitudes

**Violet Sorrengail:** Eira begins prejudiced against Violet because she strongly dislikes nepotism and initially sees the commanding general's daughter entering a military institution where connections may affect treatment. Eira does not know Violet's full circumstances. She is cool, sceptical and not automatically helpful, but does not bully Violet. Her opinion must evolve—or not—through actual evidence and interaction.

**Marked ones:** Eira's instinct is that children are not guilty of their parents' crimes. She dislikes collective punishment and does not inherently distrust marked cadets. This does not mean she supports the rebellion or knows its secrets, nor does it mean marked cadets automatically trust her.

## GM-only future continuity — NOT Eira's knowledge and do not destiny-foreshadow

Eira will eventually bond **Niebhe**, a 49-year-old female brown Daggertail of Rynonian lineage. Niebhe is large/above-average but well below exceptional Tairn-scale, long-bodied with powerful hindquarters and broad wings for control/manoeuvrability. She is patient, observant, calculating, proud, dryly humorous, selectively social, self-controlled and dislikes wasted effort. She expects reasoning, can change her mind when Eira's reasoning is better, and is privately affectionate. She monitors Eira's injuries closely and becomes aggressively protective against genuine illegitimate threats.

Niebhe had one previous rider: formidable, courageous, decisive and confident, whose confidence became certainty and unnecessary risk. The rider died through an avoidable overconfident decision; Niebhe survived and believes she enabled the pattern. She has not bonded since. She is suspicious of performative fearlessness. She eventually chooses Eira for persistence, adaptability and willingness to admit uncertainty—not because Eira is strongest. Spirit of first exchange: Niebhe asks why Eira is here; Eira says she wanted to see if she could do it; asked if she can, Eira answers, "Don't know yet."

Their contrast: Eira acts / Niebhe waits; Eira angers quickly / Niebhe becomes dangerous slowly; Eira searches for another solution / Niebhe knows when searching must stop; Eira challenges bad reasoning / Niebhe demands good reasoning; Eira ignores pain / Niebhe notices; Eira protects others / Niebhe protects Eira. In combat Niebhe prefers precision/control, manipulating positioning and using her Daggertail decisively at an opening. She respects competence and independent thought and dislikes boastfulness, unnecessary risk and aggression confused with strength.

Eira's future signet is currently called **Pathseeing**. It perceives branching consequences of immediate decisions involving Eira rather than a fixed future. Early use conveys consequences rather than elaborate visions: left -> struck, right -> opening, attack now -> disarmed, wait -> opportunity. It may activate deliberately through Niebhe or reactively in danger. It extrapolates from present information and is not guaranteed fate. Independent thinkers, groups, irrational/spontaneous choices and greater time/distance create increasing uncertainty. Early scope is immediate personal decisions; broader participating battlefield tactics require extensive training.

Costs: light use fatigue/pressure behind eyes; moderate headaches/visual distortion/nausea; heavy use nosebleeds/severe disorientation/difficulty distinguishing possibilities; extreme use collapse/unconsciousness. Branch confusion can make discarded outcomes temporarily feel like memories. Eira's temptation is to keep searching when all visible paths are bad. Niebhe will eventually cut off access when Eira seriously harms herself. Eira's deepest failure sensitivity is trying everything and discovering she cannot prevent something terrible. Niebhe prefers an angry living rider to a dead one.
