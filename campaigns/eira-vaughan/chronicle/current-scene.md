# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant challenge mat
Time: Later in the second month after Conscription Day
Default significance: charged

Time advances through a run of official challenge matches. Despite sustained extra training with Soren and the marked-cadet rotations, Eira loses most of her formal matches. Her balance, adaptability, pain tolerance and emerging grappling make her difficult to finish cleanly, but she remains a small beginner fighting cadets with more formal combat experience, greater reach, more upper-body strength or simply better-developed technique. Several losses are severe enough that walking away under her own power is an achievement; her pain tolerance repeatedly helps her stay functional while also exposing her to additional damage. The losses become an important corrective to her early narrow victory: training is improving Eira, but it is not making her exceptional overnight.

One official pairing puts Eira against Soren. Neither refuses or treats the match as play. Their familiarity makes the fight unusually technical in one sense—each knows many of the other's habits—but the physical and experience mismatch remains decisive. Eira adapts, forces Soren to work, and attempts to exploit balance/leverage rather than contest his strength, but Soren knows her methods well enough to deny the openings he has helped her practice. He defeats her legitimately and decisively without humiliating her or inflicting unnecessary injury once control is established. Eira loses. The result reinforces that their relationship and his training do not grant her parity with him, while also giving both of them a clear measure of how far her formal fighting still has to develop.
