# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Soren Mair's dorm room
Time: Evening during Gauntlet practice period
Default significance: charged

Eira pulls Soren's shirt off her face after their teasing. The mood shifts toward a genuine relationship fault line rather than manufactured jealousy: Eira's natural pattern-recognition and need to understand systems have led her to accumulate questions about Soren's family, the marked ones, their unusually organized training/support network, what Garrick is protecting, and why conversations sometimes stop around her. Soren can answer ordinary personal questions about himself, but there are subjects involving marked cadets, their families and shared secrets that he cannot and will not disclose simply because Eira is his partner. His secrecy is not lack of attachment; some information is not solely his to give and disclosure could endanger other people. Eira has legitimate grounds to feel the asymmetry because Soren knows increasingly ordinary and vulnerable details about her life while whole regions of his remain deliberately closed.

The immediate argument is primed by Eira's physical exhaustion from pushing herself on the Gauntlet. Soren's concern about her habit of driving her body beyond sensible limits can collide with her frustration that he expects her to accept his warnings and trust his judgment while asking her not to press for explanations in areas he keeps closed. The core conflict is therefore trust versus access: Eira may reasonably want to understand the man she has chosen and the people with whom she is increasingly socially associated; Soren may reasonably insist that intimacy does not entitle her to other people's secrets. Neither side should be reduced to obviously right or wrong. No hidden rebellion information has been disclosed to Eira.
