# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Eira's shared first-year dorm room
Time: Several days after Brinna Korr's death, evening
Default significance: charged

Eira directly asks Lysa Derren what has been bothering her. Lysa admits that her distance is primarily fear and uncertainty after witnessing Eira continue choking Brinna Korr after gaining control of the fight, then learning Korr died. Lysa does not claim Brinna was innocent or that Eira should have allowed herself to be hurt; what unsettles her is that she cannot tell where Eira's stopping point is once Eira decides someone is a threat. Lysa also admits the marked-cadet association makes her nervous because she does not understand the politics or dangers surrounding them, but she distinguishes that anxiety from hatred of them. She asks Eira why she did not let Brinna go once she had won.
