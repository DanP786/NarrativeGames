# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Soren Mair's first-year dorm room
Time: Later in the first month after Conscription Day, evening
Default significance: charged

After their politically sensitive discussion about omissions in Navarre's official history, Eira shifts closer to Soren and asks why he chose her. Soren answers personally rather than politically: initial physical attraction drew his attention, but her ordinary treatment of his rebellion relic, her candid reason for entering Riders, her refusal to perform fearlessness, her stubbornness and the way she keeps adapting after failure made his interest deepen. He tells her that after Brinna's attack he realized he did not merely want her physically; he wanted her to survive, which is why he began investing time in training her. He does not claim that this gives him ownership or establishes a relationship Eira has not agreed to.
