# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant dining hall
Time: Beginning of the second month after Conscription Day, evening
Default significance: charged

Eira asks Soren directly why he lost his matchup. Soren admits that he was off his game because he had seen Eira get hurt in her own fight: the damage to her face and hand remained in his head when his own match began, and part of his attention stayed on whether she was all right rather than entirely on his opponent. He does not blame Eira for this; he identifies the lapse as his own failure of focus and acknowledges that Garrick was right about that part of the criticism. This confirms for Eira that Soren's growing attachment to her materially affected his performance and makes her attempt to end their extra training more than hypothetical concern.
