# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant dining hall
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, lunch
Default significance: routine

Eira is still expected to avoid unnecessary hard loading for the immediate period, but she walks much more normally and is no longer compensating heavily. At lunch she chooses to sit with Flame Section, First Squad rather than defaulting toward Soren or the marked cadets. Mara welcomes her naturally, Nessa makes room without ceremony, and Bram remains straightforward but less socially wary after seeing Eira contribute usefully during the exercise. Other unnamed squadmates are present and interact normally enough that Eira experiences the squad as a real social group rather than background faces. The broader cohort's discomfort with her marked-cadet association remains, but within First Squad the morning's shared exercise has strengthened practical familiarity and begun turning enforced cooperation into genuine social possibility.
