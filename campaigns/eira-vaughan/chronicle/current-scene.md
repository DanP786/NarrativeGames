# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — hall outside the Riders Quadrant sparring gym
Time: Day after Conscription Day, late afternoon before evening duty
Default significance: charged

Tavis offers to become Eira's additional sparring partner and teach her. Eira asks, without dismissing the offer, "Shouldn't you be focusing on your own survival?" Her question makes clear she recognizes that training her would cost Tavis time and effort in a quadrant where he also has to survive. Tavis understands it as a practical concern rather than rejection; Eira has not yet accepted or declined his offer.
