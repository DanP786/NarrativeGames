# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant, Gauntlet training grounds
Time: Later in the first-year training cycle, Gauntlet stage
Default significance: charged

Time advances to the Gauntlet phase. By this point Basgiath's attrition has become impossible to treat as isolated tragedy: hundreds of cadets across the entering Riders cohort have died since Conscription Day through the Parapet, challenges, training accidents, deliberate violence and other lethal failures. Empty beds, missing names and compressed squads are ordinary. Eira has survived without becoming dominant; she still loses many official fights and has repeatedly been injured, while her practical adaptation, balance and persistence keep her progressing.

Flame Section, First Squad has suffered losses among its broader unnamed membership, preserving the sense that the squad began as a sizable body rather than only its named characters. Teren Vale, Mara Venn, Nessa Tor and Bram Auln remain alive at the Gauntlet stage unless later play establishes otherwise. First Squad's shared survival has strengthened its internal familiarity even as attrition changes its shape. Across the quadrant, Dain Aetos's Second Squad—the squad containing Violet Sorrengail—has suffered the least attrition and retains the strongest numbers entering the Gauntlet stage. Eira notices the disparity as a practical fact rather than being pulled into Violet's plot. The Gauntlet now becomes the immediate training pressure: balance, climbing, upper-body endurance, timing, route-solving and repeated attempts under real consequences.
