# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Eira's shared first-year dorm room
Time: Several days after Brinna Korr's death, evening
Default significance: charged

Eira tells Lysa she is surprised her involvement with Soren became physical so quickly, but says Brinna's death has sharpened the reality that any of them might die at Basgiath without much warning. She jokes with characteristic bluntness that if sleeping with Soren also helps keep her alive long enough to reach Threshing, she is not going to object to the advantage. The remark makes clear that Eira did not originally choose Soren as a calculated protector, but she is pragmatic enough to recognize the protective benefit now that Lysa has pointed it out. Lysa receives the answer as candid pragmatism rather than proof that Eira's attraction to Soren is fabricated.
