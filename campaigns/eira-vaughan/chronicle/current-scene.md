# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant dining hall
Time: Beginning of the second month after Conscription Day, evening
Default significance: charged

At dinner after their respective first formal matchups, Eira tells Soren, "I don't need your help training anymore." Because she previously suggested ending their extra training out of concern for Soren's own preparation, Soren immediately recognizes that the statement may be connected to his loss rather than Eira suddenly believing she has nothing left to learn. He does not simply override her decision or insist on continuing. Instead he challenges the wording: her narrow, injury-heavy victory does not mean she no longer needs training, so if she wants him specifically to stop helping, he asks her to say that plainly. Eira has not yet told him that she overheard Garrick blaming Soren's loss on his investment in her.
