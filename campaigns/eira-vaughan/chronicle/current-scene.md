# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Soren Mair's first-year dorm room
Time: Later in the first month after Conscription Day, evening
Default significance: charged

After Soren says he kept choosing Eira because he began caring whether she survives, Eira leans closer and directly asks why he cares. Soren does not reduce the answer to physical attraction. He admits that in Basgiath's compressed, lethal environment he has become attached to the ordinary details of Eira's presence: her arguments, persistence, humour, frustration, curiosity and the expectation that he will see her again. He tells her he cannot give the attachment a grand explanation after less than a month, but he knows the possibility of Eira disappearing from roll now feels personal to him. He explicitly frames this as his feeling rather than a claim on Eira or a demand that she reciprocate.
