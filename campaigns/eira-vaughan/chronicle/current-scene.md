# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant obstacle grounds
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, morning
Default significance: charged

The morning after Soren wrapped Eira's aggravated knee, he insists she take it easy before she leaves his bed. The knee is visibly swollen, stiff, and unstable enough to alter her gait; Eira's pain tolerance makes her subjective pain report unreliable. She nevertheless reports for a scored Flame Section, First Squad exercise under its actual squad leader, second-year Teren Vale. Dain Aetos leads Second Squad and is not Eira's squad leader. First Squad contains a substantial number of cadets, most still background to Eira; only those currently notable are named. Mara Venn remains Eira's warm friend. Bram Auln is a large, physically powerful first-year who has become useful whenever the squad needs weight moved and is wary of Eira's marked-cadet associations without refusing to work with her. Nessa Tor is a compact, sharp-eyed first-year with a practical streak who notices weaknesses in plans and people quickly and treats squad function as more important than social factions. The exercise requires First Squad to move a weighted casualty litter plus supplies through rough obstacles while maintaining cohesion. Vale immediately notices Eira's altered gait and assigns around the injury rather than allowing her to load or twist the knee. The first obstacle is a broken-wall crossing where the litter must be transferred over a high timber-and-stone barrier without dropping the casualty weight or abandoning supplies. Eira cannot safely provide her usual leg drive, creating a real squad problem and an opportunity for her to contribute through observation, sequencing, balance knowledge and practical problem-solving rather than brute force.
