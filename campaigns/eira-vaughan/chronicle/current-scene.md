# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — corridor outside Soren Mair's dorm room

Default significance: charged

The argument reaches a deeper rupture. Eira clarifies that confidentiality itself is not the problem: she does not need marked-cadet or family secrets, but she cannot treat their relationship as emotionally serious if Soren lies to her rather than simply refusing to answer. The distinction between being loved and being used as refuge becomes emotionally intolerable in the moment.
