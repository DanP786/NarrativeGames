# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant storeroom
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, evening
Default significance: routine

During evening storeroom duty, Eira continues reorganizing boxes before Soren arrives, protecting her recovering knee but increasingly relying on her comparatively weaker upper body. Soren enters just as she begins visibly struggling with a particularly awkward heavy crate. He immediately reads the situation: the healer has improved her knee, but Eira is compensating by trying to muscle work through her arms rather than resting. He takes the crate from her before she can turn the struggle into another injury and reacts with exasperated amusement rather than treating her as helpless. Eira's knee remains substantially better than that morning and is not newly worsened in this moment. Soren now knows she did see the healer and that she has nevertheless interpreted 'avoid unnecessary hard loading' creatively.
