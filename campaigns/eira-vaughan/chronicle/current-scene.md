# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — secluded Riders Quadrant training space
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, later afternoon
Default significance: charged

During the water break, after Eira needles Soren about the attractiveness of the other marked cadets and his jealousy becomes obvious, Soren steps close and tips Eira's face up by her chin. Very quietly, meant for her rather than the training group, he says, "Mine." Unlike his half-asleep use of the word on Conscription Night, this time Eira is awake, looking directly at him, and the deliberate possessiveness stops her teasing completely; she goes still. Soren notices the abrupt change and does not mistake stillness for agreement or push the claim further. The moment leaves the question of what Eira actually wants from their increasingly attached, still-undefined relationship unresolved.
