# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — secluded Riders Quadrant training space
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, later afternoon
Default significance: charged

After Eira's hand has recovered enough for structured practice, Soren eventually brings her into one of the marked cadets' small informal training rotations. They obey the rule limiting marked cadets to groups of no more than three, so the session never has more than three marked ones together at once; Eira, who is unmarked, is an outsider brought by Soren rather than part of their network. The marked cadets have developed a deliberate system: rotating partners, sharing corrections and making efficient use of limited safe opportunities to train together without gathering illegally. Coren Aster is among the marked cadets present in this first session with Eira; the other marked participant(s) in later rotations are not yet established by name. They do not trust Eira simply because Soren does. They are willing to train with her under his sponsorship and observe how she behaves, but sensitive information, private marked-cadet concerns and rebellion-related matters remain outside her access. Eira is expected to earn any deeper trust through conduct over time rather than receive it through Soren.
