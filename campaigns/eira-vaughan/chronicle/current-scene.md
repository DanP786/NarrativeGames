# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant obstacle grounds, finish of First Squad course
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, late morning
Default significance: charged

Flame Section, First Squad completes the scored casualty-and-supplies course with the weighted litter and issued equipment intact. The first wall transfer succeeds through the staged plan Eira and Nessa developed, though execution is imperfect and requires corrections. Across later obstacles the squad gradually becomes more coordinated: Bram Auln proves dependable under heavy load, Mara Venn is quick and communicative, Nessa Tor continues spotting structural weaknesses and sequencing problems, and Eira contributes primarily as an observer/planner because Teren Vale keeps her visibly unstable knee out of load-bearing and twisting roles. Eira obeys the limitation despite frustration, preventing the injury from becoming a second casualty problem. The squad is not polished and loses time to early argument, awkward handoffs and fatigue, but it finishes together rather than through a single standout performance. Vale's debrief emphasizes that their improvement came when they stopped treating every obstacle as an individual contest. He notes Eira's useful problem-solving but also warns her that repeatedly arriving injured reduces the options available to the squad. First Squad has begun to acquire a functional identity and several cadets who were previously background faces now recognize Eira as useful beyond her reputation for violence or marked-cadet association.
