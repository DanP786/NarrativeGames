# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Eira's shared first-year dorm room
Time: Several days after Brinna Korr's death, evening
Default significance: charged

Eira answers Lysa honestly about why she did not release Brinna earlier. She says she did not know Brinna would stop if released: Brinna had been choking her hard enough that Eira could not breathe, so Eira did not experience the attack as a mere beating or warning. Eira also admits a second motive: she feared that showing mercy once she gained control would make other cadets read her small size as weakness and turn her into a target. She says she generally does not care what people think of her except when their perception affects whether they believe they can safely prey on her. Lysa understands that Eira's choice came from both immediate survival fear and deliberate deterrence; the honesty reduces some uncertainty without erasing Lysa's discomfort over Brinna's death.
