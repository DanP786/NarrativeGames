# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant obstacle grounds
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, morning
Default significance: charged

At the first broken-wall obstacle of First Squad's scored casualty exercise, Eira and Nessa Tor compare observations rather than joining the louder argument about brute-force lifting. Eira identifies the central problem as maintaining control and keeping the weighted litter level while its load transfers from cadets below the wall to cadets above and then down the far side. Nessa agrees and adds that the uneven top edge can serve as a temporary support point if protected from snagging the canvas. Together they sketch a staged transfer: send agile cadets and supplies across first, place strong cadets such as Bram at the main lift points, use spare jackets/straps over the rough edge as a sliding buffer, and rotate control of the litter rather than trying to throw or muscle it across in one motion. The plan still needs the squad to execute it and Vale has not endorsed or rejected it; Eira's bad knee keeps her in a sequencing/spotting role rather than taking the load herself. Nessa's opinion of Eira shifts slightly toward practical respect because Eira contributes without pretending the injured leg is usable.
