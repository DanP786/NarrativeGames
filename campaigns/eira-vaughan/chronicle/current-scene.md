# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Soren Mair's dorm room
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, night
Default significance: charged

After another stretch of hard second-month training, Eira spends the night in Soren's room with a newly aggravated knee from training. The exact mechanism and diagnosis are not yet established, but the joint is sore/swollen enough to merit support. Soren has Eira sit while he wraps the knee carefully and firmly. Because Eira's unusually high pain tolerance makes her a poor judge of when an injury deserves rest, he pays more attention to swelling, stability and function than to her claims about how much it hurts. The scene is domestic and intimate rather than sexual: their newly exclusive relationship is beginning to include mundane care alongside training, banter and physical attraction.
