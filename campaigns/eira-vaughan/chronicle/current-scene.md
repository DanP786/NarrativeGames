# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Eira's shared first-year dorm room
Time: Several days after Brinna Korr's death, evening
Default significance: charged

Eira tells Lysa that she was not consciously treating Soren as a survival advantage until Lysa raised the possibility. She says the only thing she currently knows is that she and Soren want each other, that she is deliberately not thinking beyond that, and that any incidental advantages are welcome. Lysa challenges the simplicity of that framing: Soren is spending scarce time training Eira, which in Lysa's view is evidence of investment beyond merely hoping Eira survives long enough for continued physical intimacy. Lysa does not claim to know Soren's feelings, but pushes Eira to recognize that his observable behaviour carries more commitment than a purely casual arrangement would require.
