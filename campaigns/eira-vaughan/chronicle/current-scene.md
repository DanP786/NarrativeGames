# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Eira's shared first-year dorm room
Time: Several days after Brinna Korr's death, evening
Default significance: charged

Eira asks whether cadets really use romantic and sexual relationships as survival calculations at Basgiath. Lysa says they do, and points to several first-year examples she has observed or heard openly discussed: Jessa Vale has attached herself to a physically formidable second-year named Orrin Cade and now rarely gets challenged alone; Pell Rusk has been pursuing squadmate Nadiya Sorn partly because her older sister is a respected third-year; and Catrin Bell openly treats her involvement with a larger first-year, Harl Vey, as a mutual protection arrangement rather than romance. Lysa emphasizes that attraction and calculation can coexist at Basgiath, and that cadets trade companionship, training, information, reputation and protection in overlapping ways. These examples are Lysa's social observations rather than omniscient proof of anyone's private motives. Eira has not yet answered whether protection played any role in her own involvement with Soren.
