# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant storeroom
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, evening
Default significance: routine

That evening Eira has another storeroom duty and arrives before Soren. Despite the healer having substantially improved and supported her aggravated knee earlier in the day, she has been told to avoid unnecessary hard loading for the immediate period. She nevertheless begins reorganizing and lifting supply boxes before Soren arrives. Eira works intelligently rather than recklessly—keeping loads close, using shelves and surfaces to reduce awkward carries, avoiding twisting through the recovering knee—but she is still testing the boundary of the healer's instruction because she dislikes standing idle and sees manageable work that needs doing. Her comparatively weaker upper body makes some boxes genuinely awkward without her usual leg drive. Soren has not yet arrived, so he has not witnessed the lifting or reacted to it.
