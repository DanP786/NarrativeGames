# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant training area
Time: Beginning of the second month after Conscription Day, morning
Default significance: charged

Eira wins her first formal matchup, but only narrowly and without looking polished. Her opponent is the cleaner formal fighter and repeatedly exploits weaknesses in Eira's guard; Eira takes a hard strike around one eye that will develop into a black eye and suffers broken fingers during the fight. Her unusually high pain tolerance lets her continue but also makes the hand injury easy for her to underweight. She survives the matchup by adapting under pressure rather than outclassing her opponent: abandoning exchanges she is losing, using balance, lower-body strength, awkward angles and grappling instincts to disrupt the opponent and grind out a decisive controlling finish. The victory demonstrates real improvement from her extra practice without making her suddenly expert; she leaves the mat visibly battered and with a hand that needs medical attention.
