# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Soren Mair's first-year dorm room
Time: Later in the first month after Conscription Day, evening
Default significance: charged

After Soren admits he does not want Eira to die and be replaced in the ordinary machinery of Basgiath, Eira breaks the emotional intensity by teasing that she initially interpreted his remark about not wanting someone else sleeping in her bed as sexual jealousy rather than fear of her death. Soren recognizes the double meaning and admits, with possessive humour, that he would also dislike the sexual interpretation, while carefully distinguishing jealousy from any right to dictate whom Eira sleeps with. The exchange leaves the deeper admission intact while restoring some of their familiar flirtation.
