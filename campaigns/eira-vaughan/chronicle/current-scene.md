# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant training area
Time: Beginning of the second month after Conscription Day, morning
Default significance: charged

After narrowly winning her first formal matchup, Eira cradles her injured hand against her chest and starts toward the healers with broken fingers and a developing black eye. Before she leaves, she catches sight of Soren in his own formal matchup. Soren loses. His opponent defeats him legitimately rather than through outside interference, demonstrating that Soren is capable and physically formidable but not invulnerable or automatically superior to every first-year opponent. The loss lands sharply for Eira because Soren has spent weeks functioning as her more experienced trainer and because their recent conversation established how personally each has begun to care about the other's survival. Eira remains at the edge of the training area long enough to see that Soren is alive and conscious, but the extent of his injuries and his reaction to the loss have not yet been established.
