# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — secluded Riders Quadrant training space
Time: Second month after Conscription Day, later afternoon
Default significance: charged

During a water break in her first small marked-cadet training rotation, Eira looks over the marked cadets and asks Soren, "Are all marked ones attractive?" The question is delivered more as an amused observation than a serious request for information. Soren immediately understands that Eira has been noticing the other marked cadets physically and reacts with visible jealousy. He does not claim authority over whom she can look at, but his possessive streak surfaces in dry, territorial banter; Eira's attraction to others clearly bothers him even though they have never agreed to exclusivity. The exchange adds a new edge to Eira's outsider position in the training group without changing the marked cadets' underlying caution toward her.
