# Current scene

Location: Basgiath War College — Riders Quadrant dining hall
Time: Beginning of the second month after Conscription Day, evening
Default significance: charged

Eira challenges Soren's concern directly: "Why does it matter Soren? You can't protect me, especially not from a dragon." Soren agrees with the premise rather than claiming he can keep her safe. He tells her that caring whether she survives is not the same as believing he can control whether she survives, and that training her was never a promise of protection but one practical way to improve her chances where he actually could help. He acknowledges that Threshing, dragons, other cadets and Basgiath contain dangers he cannot stand between her and. His failure in the matchup came from confusing caring about her with mentally trying to monitor an outcome he could not control; he accepts that this lapse is his to correct. He does not withdraw his attachment simply because it creates vulnerability.
