# Session 01 — Parapet through first post-assessment flight

Eira Vaughan, civilian-born Flame Section First Squad cadet, survives Parapet, combat challenges and Gauntlet by balance, endurance, stubborn adaptation and hard-earned grappling. She kills Brinna Korr in self-defence. She becomes close to Mara Venn and Bram Auln, and falls deeply in love with marked cadet Soren Mair of Claw Section Third Squad, whose blue Swordtail is Rhovan. Their relationship includes jealousy, an earlier rupture over marked-rider secrecy, reconciliation, exclusivity and intimacy. Bram once kissed Eira without permission; she rejected it, and Soren ultimately respected her handling of it. Eira's squad includes Mara, Bram, Nessa, Kellan and Joryn, led by second-year Teren Vale. Eira's family are Gareth, Elin and older Infantry brother Rhys.

At Threshing, three men corner Eira and attempt to rape her; **no rape or sexual contact is completed**. Eira escapes and bonds Niebhe, a large 49-year-old female brown Daggertail, patient, calculating and protective. Niebhe burns two attackers; third, stocky Hadrik Voss, survives with burns, unknown to Eira initially. Soren knows the attempted rape occurred, but Mara/Bram know only that men tried to kill Eira. Xaden, Garrick and Dax later learn of the attempted rape via plausible channels; do not leak it elsewhere. Eira and Niebhe's bond is intimate and dryly funny. Soren bonds Rhovan, large male blue Swordtail. Mara bonds male orange Solas; Bram bonds female forest-green Veyrath; Lysa Derren bonds a female **orange** dragon (earlier black/grey descriptions retconned).

Voss later resurfaces, threatens Eira in a corridor, and challenges her to a sanctioned death match. Soren intervenes violently in the corridor, drawing disciplinary restrictions; Xaden insists Eira face the sanctioned match herself. Eira fights Voss in the arena, sustains severe injuries including a deep dagger wound to **left forearm**, pulls the dagger out with her teeth, and kills Voss with her axe to his neck. **Voss is permanently dead.** Fourteen stitches and repair to left forearm, concussion/bruising/right wrist strain; rehab and graduated combat restrictions. Soren and Eira remain close. Rhys visits while Eira is asleep in infirmary. Garrick and Xaden remain guarded allies/mentors.

During later flight-formation observation under Captain Halden, Eira's signet manifests: **Pathseeing**, military label **Conditional Foresight**. It reveals branching possible consequences of Eira's *own immediate actions and decisions*, including waiting, but not minds, other people's independent intentions/decisions, or fixed fate. Longer chains and unpredictable actions grow fuzzy. Use causes headaches, nausea, nosebleeds, disorientation, collapse; Niebhe may cut access to protect her. Eira averts a collision by warning Niebhe telepathically to alert dragons. Soren notices evasive/knowingly false spoken claims because his own FIRST, SECRET signet **Truthsense** has manifested: detects knowingly false SPOKEN assertions, not thoughts, truth itself, honest mistakes, omissions or intentions. He tells Eira only that he can recognise certain things people say and cannot read thoughts, refuses precise explanation because marked riders' collective secret. Eira sees hypocrisy in his withholding while pressing her, and the relationship ruptures. She stays with Lysa, cries; next day they partially reconcile, still love each other, but agree **no sex until they resolve the dispute**.

Eira voluntarily reports possible signet to Lt Serah Veyne for fear she may be classified an illegal inntinnsic and executed. Immediately detained and assessed for **24 days**, never physically abused, but deeply afraid they won't believe her. Tests by Major Ilyra Senn, Veyne and others establish she cannot read minds, memories, hidden numbers or independent intent, only branches of her own actions. Overuse causes nosebleed and collapse; Niebhe fiercely protects her. Restricted lessons include tactics, dragon anatomy, history, and controlled sparring with Instructor Pellan. Eira excels at applied analysis and diagrams but struggles rote names/navigation. Final obstacle course passed by judicious signet use and willingness to accept uncertainty. Board (Colonel Ardent, Senn, Veyne, specialist Caelis) rules **not inntinnsic**, lawful but restricted intelligence-sensitive Conditional Foresight; she receives silver closed-eye badge beneath squad insignia, weekly medical monitoring, twice-weekly signet control, supervised combat reintegration, restricted disclosure of exact mechanics. Lesser magic fundamentals due next week. She told command Soren had heard something she didn't understand, without precise details; Commander Thorne may arrange Soren nondisclosure briefing. Eira's single letter to Soren: 'Soren, I’m safe. For now. I could be here for a long time, so you’ll have to be patient. Don’t get yourself in any more trouble than you already have for my sake. I love you. Eira.' Soren wrote repeatedly, worried and patient; Garrick confirmed only that she was alive and medically stable.

Eira is released, rejoins Mara/Bram and squad at Battle Brief, and reunites passionately with Soren. They spend a night cuddling but honour no-sex agreement, discussing the asymmetry of secrets. Soren promises to consult Garrick about what he can safely disclose. Eira admits she cannot end the relationship but is hurting. Next morning she teases him mercilessly; at breakfast squad gossip includes Violet Sorrengail/Xaden Riorson, Tairn/Sgaeyl mating, and Mara's speculation about Eira's classified signet. Dax Aster (formerly Coren Aster; **Dax is authoritative retcon**) jokes Eira's signet may involve ducks. Soren and Dax belong to **Claw Section, Third Squad**, not Eira's Flame First Squad.

Eira attends theory and sparring: grapples Bram successfully; narrowly loses an even technical bout to **Violet Sorrengail, who is SHORTER than Eira**, partly due to hesitating over healing left forearm. Ridoc Gamlyn flirts with Eira; she says she has a boyfriend. Eira and Mara lose paired bout to Ridoc and Rhiannon. At dinner Soren asks Eira to his room. He reports Garrick advised him against partial disclosure. Soren hints too much about dangerous secrets and a 'cause', alarming Eira about what interrogation could force her to reveal; she insists he stop telling her fragments, and he does. He applies salve to her sore arm; she tells him about Ridoc and her reduced enjoyment of being desired by anyone but Soren since Threshing. Eira declares she will deliberately withhold her signet details from Soren to make things even; he accepts. They affirm their love: Eira says 'You're mine, Soren Mair.' They cuddle, she enthusiastically recounts her Violet sparring bout, and confides her fear of execution and obedient confinement during assessment. Niebhe was her company throughout.

That evening Eira and Soren mutually choose to resume sexual intimacy despite their earlier no-sex-until-resolved boundary; they agree they must still discuss unresolved trust issues, but choose each other. They spend the night and morning together, reconnecting after nearly a month apart. Soren arrives at class utterly exhausted and happy, teased by Dax; Eira arrives in excellent spirits at **her first flight manoeuvres session in over a month**. Captain Halden permits a supervised reintroduction, no aggressive dives/high-load turns, immediate landing if signet symptoms. Niebhe awaits her; Eira is at the flight field ready to mount for an eastern practice circuit. **This is the current in-fiction point.**
