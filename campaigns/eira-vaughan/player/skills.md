# Skills

## Master
_None._

## Adept
- Balance & Coordination (4 ticks) — gymnastics background, strong core, excellent proprioception and broad physical activity; defining starting competence. During her first serious Basgiath fight, Eira converted an awkward high leg hook while being choked into a two-leg neck clamp as the larger opponent changed grips, maintaining control through the subsequent fall. In her first formal matchup she again relies on balance and awkward angle changes to survive a cleaner fighter and create the opening that lets her grind out a narrow win.

## Novice
- Endurance (0 ticks) — excellent civilian conditioning, not yet Rider/military conditioning.
- Practical Craft (0 ticks) — tools, measurement, knots, basic repairs, sewing/mending, fabric and leather repair from helping both parents.
- Grappling (4 ticks) — childhood wrestling and physical instinct; no formal martial training. Against a substantially larger opponent, Eira abandoned a losing strength contest, used unconventional lower-body leverage, reversed an active choke into a controlling neck hold, and maintained it under resistance until the attacker lost consciousness. In her first formal matchup she uses grappling as an escape from losing striking exchanges, disrupting the opponent's base and securing a controlling finish despite broken fingers and accumulating facial damage.

## Notable Untrained
- Formal hand-to-hand combat (several weeks of training plus first formal matchup victory) — after being decisively beaten in her first formal spar, Eira begins regular extra practice with Soren Mair. She remains a beginner rather than a polished fighter, but her guard, footwork and recognition of trained responses have improved enough to keep her functional under pressure. Her first formal matchup is a narrow, ugly win: the cleaner fighter repeatedly gets through her defence, while Eira adapts and ultimately wins through balance, lower-body leverage and grappling rather than superior formal technique.
- Spear fighting (first formal exposure) — an instructor recommends the weapon because reach, distance control, footwork and leverage may compensate for Eira's size while making use of her balance, coordination and strong lower body. She has begun basic grip, stance and thrust mechanics but remains fully untrained.
- Tomahawk / war axe combat — owns and likes a compact one-handed axe but lacks formal combat mastery.
- Thrown weapons — average aim; no prodigy advantage.
- Crossbow — average beginner potential; requires practice.
- Navigation — genuinely poor intuitive sense of direction in unfamiliar surroundings; persistent mundane weakness.

## Traits not treated as skills
- Extremely high pain tolerance — advantage and liability; can conceal injury severity from Eira herself. Her first formal matchup reinforces the liability when she continues effectively despite broken fingers.
- Strong legs / good core.
- Very good swimming ability.
- Visual/practical learning and iterative improvement.
