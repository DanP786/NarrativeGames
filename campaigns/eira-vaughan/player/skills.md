# Skills

## Master
_None._

## Adept
- Balance & Coordination (4 ticks) — gymnastics background, strong core, excellent proprioception and broad physical activity; defining starting competence. During her first serious Basgiath fight, Eira converted an awkward high leg hook while being choked into a two-leg neck clamp as the larger opponent changed grips, maintaining control through the subsequent fall. Her official challenge record repeatedly shows this remains one of her best survival tools: even in losses she uses balance and awkward angle changes to avoid cleaner finishes and create brief openings. During early Gauntlet work this background translates strongly to narrow traverses, body positioning and transitions.

## Novice
- Endurance (0 ticks) — excellent civilian conditioning, now reinforced by months of Riders training though not yet tracked as a formal advancement.
- Practical Craft (0 ticks) — tools, measurement, knots, basic repairs, sewing/mending, fabric and leather repair from helping both parents.
- Grappling (4 ticks) — childhood wrestling and physical instinct; no formal martial training. Against a substantially larger opponent, Eira abandoned a losing strength contest, used unconventional lower-body leverage, reversed an active choke into a controlling neck hold, and maintained it under resistance until the attacker lost consciousness. In formal challenges she increasingly tries to use grappling as an escape from losing striking exchanges, but trained opponents often deny her entry, overpower her grips or recognize the leverage before she can complete it.

## Notable Untrained
- Formal hand-to-hand combat (ongoing training and multiple official matches) — Eira trains regularly with Soren Mair and in small marked-cadet rotations, but remains a developing fighter rather than a polished one. Her first formal matchup was a narrow, ugly victory through adaptation, balance and grappling. Subsequent official matches correct any impression that this made her unusually dominant: she loses most of them, sometimes badly. Cleaner fighters exploit her guard and reach; larger fighters can still overwhelm her; experienced opponents increasingly recognize her attempts to change levels, disrupt balance or force grappling. She is becoming harder to surprise and better at identifying why she lost. In one official match Soren defeats her decisively and legitimately.
- Upper-body strength / grip development — Eira entered Basgiath with comparatively mediocre upper-body strength. Months of grappling, challenges, marked-cadet training, weapons work, carries, climbing and general Riders conditioning have produced substantial unnoticed improvement in her shoulders, back, arms and grip. During early Gauntlet attempts she discovers she can pull, hang and transition through obstacles she had expected to exceed her strength. She remains short, lacks exceptional raw upper-body power, and can still be overpowered by larger strong cadets; this is earned physical development rather than a new exceptional talent.
- Spear fighting (early training) — an instructor recommends the weapon because reach, distance control, footwork and leverage may compensate for Eira's size while making use of her balance, coordination and strong lower body. She has begun basic grip, stance and thrust mechanics but remains untrained.
- Tomahawk / war axe combat — owns and likes a compact one-handed axe but lacks formal combat mastery.
- Thrown weapons — average aim; no prodigy advantage.
- Crossbow — average beginner potential; requires practice.
- Navigation — genuinely poor intuitive sense of direction in unfamiliar surroundings; persistent mundane weakness.

## Traits not treated as skills
- Extremely high pain tolerance — advantage and liability. Across repeated formal challenge losses, it lets Eira remain functional through punishment that would stop some cadets, but it also makes her more likely to continue after meaningful injury and absorb damage she should not.
- Strong legs / good core.
- Very good swimming ability.
- Visual/practical learning and iterative improvement.
