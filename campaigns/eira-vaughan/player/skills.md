Against a substantially larger opponent, Eira abandoned a losing strength contest, used unconventional lower-body leverage, reversed an active choke into a controlling neck hold, and maintained it under resistance until the attacker lost consciousness.

## Notable Untrained

She is still an untrained beginner overall, but is working specifically on guard, footwork, telegraphing less and recognizing trained responses through repetition.

She has begun basic grip, stance and thrust mechanics but remains fully untrained.

- Crossbow — average beginner potential; requires practice.
- Navigation — genuinely poor intuitive sense of direction in unfamiliar surroundings; persistent mundane weakness.
