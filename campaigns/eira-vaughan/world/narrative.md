# Narrative

Eira Vaughan, a civilian carpenter's and tailor's daughter, voluntarily entered Basgiath's Riders Quadrant to discover her limits. She survived Parapet, Gauntlet and lethal challenges through persistence and adaptability, fell in love with marked rider Soren Mair, and bonded Niebhe, a formidable brown Daggertail. Threshing brought an attempted sexual assault by three cadets; Niebhe killed two, and Eira later killed surviving attacker Hadrik Voss in a sanctioned death match, at serious cost to her left forearm and health. Her hard-won First Squad friendships with Mara and Bram remain central.

Eira's signet, Pathseeing (military Conditional Foresight), perceives possible consequences branching from her own decisions, not minds or others' independent intentions. Its manifestation brought fear of inntinnsic execution, voluntary disclosure and twenty-four days of confinement and testing. The board classified it lawful but intelligence-restricted; Eira returned to Basgiath with a silver closed-eye badge, monitoring requirements and controlled reintegration. Her growth lies in recognising when to act without exhausting every possible branch.

Soren's hidden first signet Truthsense (detecting knowingly false spoken assertions only) strained their relationship when he could detect Eira's lies but would not disclose his own dangerous marked-rider secret. After painful conflict and partial reconciliation, Eira chose to withhold her signet mechanics in return; they nevertheless reaffirmed love, resumed intimacy, and acknowledged unresolved trust questions. Soren's future public Kinetic Redirection manifestation, marked riders' secrets, competitions, War Games and Resson remain ahead. Eira currently prepares for her first flight with Niebhe in over a month, under Captain Halden's restrictions.
