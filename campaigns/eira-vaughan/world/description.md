# World description

The campaign uses the Empyrean / Fourth Wing setting and lore as its world framework: Navarre, Basgiath War College, its military quadrants, dragonkind, rider bonds, signets, wards, politics and the war exist consistently with established canon at the beginning of *Fourth Wing*.

Eira's story begins in Violet Sorrengail's entering year. Canon chronology continues even when Eira is elsewhere. Public events can become known through witnesses, gossip, announcements and ordinary institutional channels; private events remain private unless information plausibly travels. Eira's actions may alter local and eventually larger outcomes where causally justified, but canon characters do not orbit her by default.
