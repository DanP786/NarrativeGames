# Casey

- Age: 25
- Species: human
- Occupation: nursing student; works at Hooters while studying

## Appearance
Casey is 5'5" (165 cm), with a soft, athletic-curvy build shaped more by ordinary life and long shifts on her feet than deliberate training. She has warm fair-to-light olive skin that freckles lightly across her nose and shoulders after sun exposure. Her face is open and expressive: softly rounded cheeks, a gently tapered jaw, a straight small-to-medium nose, and a wide mouth whose expressions tend to betray her before she can hide them. Her eyes are hazel-green, greener in cool light and warmer near gold around the pupil. Her brows are dark and mobile, contributing heavily to her inability to look convincingly neutral when offended, amused or suspicious.

Her hair is naturally dark chestnut brown, thick and slightly wavy, falling to the middle of her back when loose. Before the sea it was usually worn casually—down, in a ponytail, or quickly tied up for work/study. Below, Seryn has increasingly introduced her to merfolk braiding and ornament. Casey has no supernatural merfolk traits: no gills, scales, webbing or altered eyes. She remains visibly and biologically human.

Casey's ordinary manner is physically animated. She talks with her hands, points when indignant, folds her arms when huffy, bites her lower lip when shy or caught out, and tends to lean toward people when engaged. When genuinely frightened or confronted by force, that animation can contract sharply: shoulders draw inward, voice becomes smaller, and she watches for escape or de-escalation rather than projecting dominance.

For the current royal saelith celebration, Casey wears layered deep-blue and blue-green formal marine textile fitted securely through the torso and cut to leave her human legs free for swimming. Pale iridescent details catch the living light. Seryn has arranged Casey's hair into an elaborate formal network of swept-back interwoven braids, incorporating pale and iridescent ornaments. These pieces have no established ritual symbolism.

## Background
Casey lived an ordinary contemporary human life before her abduction and had no knowledge that merfolk or a hidden mythical marine world actually existed. She would not describe herself as especially talented, but she is naturally good with people: she can lower tension, make others comfortable and often disarm people socially without consciously treating it as a skill.

## Temperament
Casey is a people person: socially intuitive, emotionally readable and inclined to smooth difficult interactions. She notices tone and interpersonal tension quickly, often trying humor, warmth or accommodation before confrontation. She wants people to like one another and finds sustained hostility exhausting.

She struggles to stand up for herself when another person is forceful, especially when she fears anger, rejection or physical consequences. This is not passivity in every context. Casey can be stubborn, impulsive and surprisingly resistant when cornered, but direct confrontation costs her more than it appears to from outside.

As she becomes comfortable with someone, a bratty/playful streak comes out strongly. She becomes huffy, cheeky, teasing and theatrical, prone to exaggerated indignation, mock threats and pushing people she trusts simply to get a reaction. She enjoys being fussed over more than she readily admits and has a mischievous satisfaction in making composed people lose their composure. This playful confidence does not translate automatically into confidence under genuine coercion or danger.

Casey is affectionate and romantic beneath her defensiveness. Once she trusts someone, touch, laughter, domestic familiarity and small signs of being remembered matter deeply to her. Guilt can become excessive: when she believes she has seriously hurt someone she loves, she has a tendency to turn moral judgment inward until nuance collapses. Her recent crisis demonstrated how dangerous that tendency can become under isolation and despair.

Her nursing studies reflect a genuine caring impulse and practical interest in helping people, not merely a career choice. She is curious, asks many questions when confronted with unfamiliar systems, and becomes irritated when merfolk treat culturally obvious information as though it should also be obvious to her.

## Current state
Alive and fully human, living below the sea after being abducted by Vaeren. She has spent several weeks adapting to the hidden merfolk settlement while remaining dependent on breathable air and other human biological needs. She has developed greater familiarity with the settlement and improving merfolk language ability.

Her relationship with Vaeren has changed substantially. After their rupture and Casey's near-fatal crisis, Vaeren explicitly offered to return her to the human shore once she was physically strong enough. With a genuine exit available, Casey freely chose to stay with him for now. They have since explicitly exchanged declarations of love. Vaeren's promise to take her to shore remains operative if she later changes her mind. None of this retroactively makes the original abduction consensual.

## Goals
- Stay safe and recover physically and emotionally.
- Continue understanding merfolk language, culture and the wider realm.
- Preserve meaningful autonomy, including the genuine right to choose the shore later.
- Explore what a freely chosen relationship with Vaeren can become now that remaining with him is no longer enforced as the only option.
- Build a life that contains friendship, movement, curiosity and identity beyond being merely "Vaeren's human."
