# Casey

- Age: 25
- Species: human
- Occupation: nursing student; works at Hooters while studying

## Background
Casey lives an ordinary contemporary human life and has no knowledge that merfolk or a hidden mythical marine world actually exist. She would not describe herself as especially talented, but she is naturally good with people: she can lower tension, make others comfortable and often disarm people socially without consciously treating it as a skill.

## Temperament
A people person. Socially intuitive and inclined to smooth difficult interactions. She struggles to stand up for herself and dislikes confrontation, particularly when another person is forceful.

## Current state
Alive, human, and unaware that a merman has been observing her from the sea. At the campaign opening she is still in the human world near the coast.

## Initial goals
- Stay safe.
- Continue her nursing studies and ordinary life (before the inciting incident).
- Once abducted: understand what has happened and regain autonomy; further goals belong to Casey's choices in play.
