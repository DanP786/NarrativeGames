# Casey

- Age: 25
- Species: human
- Occupation: nursing student; works at Hooters while studying

## Background
Casey lived an ordinary contemporary human life before her abduction and had no knowledge that merfolk or a hidden mythical marine world actually existed. She would not describe herself as especially talented, but she is naturally good with people: she can lower tension, make others comfortable and often disarm people socially without consciously treating it as a skill.

## Temperament
A people person. Socially intuitive and inclined to smooth difficult interactions. She struggles to stand up for herself and dislikes serious confrontation, particularly when another person is forceful.

As Casey becomes comfortable with someone, a bratty/playful streak comes out strongly: huffy, cheeky, teasing, expressive, prone to exaggerated indignation and willing to push at people she trusts for a reaction. This is not the same as effortless assertiveness under genuine threat; fear, coercion and forceful confrontation can still make her retreat, seek safety or struggle to advocate for herself.

## Current state
Alive and human, living below the sea after being abducted by Vaeren. She has spent several weeks adapting to the hidden merfolk settlement while remaining dependent on air and other human biological needs. She has developed greater familiarity with the settlement, improving merfolk language ability, and a complicated but increasingly intimate relationship with Vaeren. None of this retroactively consents to the original abduction or resolves her lack of freedom to return to the human shore.

## Goals
- Stay safe.
- Understand merfolk language, culture and the settlement well enough to navigate her circumstances.
- Gain meaningful autonomy and influence over her life.
- Her private long-term strategy remains to build enough understanding, trust and leverage that she may eventually persuade Vaeren to return her to the surface, unless her choices later change that goal.
