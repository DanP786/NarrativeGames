# Vaeren

- Species: merman
- Age: adult; exact chronological age unestablished
- Status: alive
- Public disposition toward Casey: deeply attached / openly protective; numeric hidden meter not established

## Appearance
Vaeren is a large, powerfully built merman whose body looks adapted for speed, pressure and violent bursts of underwater strength rather than human-style bulk. Above the waist he is approximately human in proportion, broad through the shoulders and chest with long, strong arms. His skin is cool bronze-olive with a faint pearlescent sheen that becomes more visible underwater. Fine, nearly invisible scale-texture traces the sides of his ribs, hips and lower back where human skin gives way to his tail.

His face is almost human enough to be beautiful before the differences become unsettling: high severe cheekbones, a strong jaw, straight nose, and a mouth that naturally rests in a serious line. His eyes are deep blue-gray with a reflective silver layer that catches dim light sharply, giving them an animal brightness in darkness. His pupils widen dramatically underwater. His hair is near-black, thick, slightly coarse and long enough to fall around his jaw and nape; in water it streams freely, while in air he habitually pushes it back with impatient hands.

His tail begins at the hips and is long, muscular and built for acceleration. The scales are dark blue-black along the dorsal surface, shifting through storm-blue and muted teal along the sides, with a paler silver-gray underside. In living light the scales show subdued iridescence rather than jewel-bright color. The broad tail fin is slightly translucent at its outer edges and bears several old pale scars. Additional old scars mark one shoulder and his side, consistent with an adult life in a physically dangerous marine world; their exact origins remain unestablished.

Vaeren's movements are controlled and economical on land or dry stone, but underwater the difference is dramatic: he becomes fluid, fast and unnervingly precise. He tends to occupy space quietly rather than fidget. When protective or territorial, he closes physical distance, places himself between Casey and a perceived problem, or fixes the problem with an unwavering stare. When embarrassed, especially by family, his jaw tightens and he becomes conspicuously terse.

## Voice and mannerisms
Vaeren's voice is low and rough-edged, with a resonant quality suited to carrying through water. His merfolk speech is usually deliberate and spare. In English he learned to favor short, literal constructions, and some of that economy remains even as communication improves.

He is not naturally effusive. Affection shows first through attention: noticing cold, hunger, fatigue, danger, missing objects, changed routines and Casey's small preferences. He has a habit of looking at Casey for a beat too long when struck by her appearance, then behaving as though no such lapse occurred. Dry humor has emerged as trust has grown, often so understated that Casey initially mistakes it for seriousness.

## Personality
Vaeren is serious, stubborn, deliberate and deeply shaped by inherited custom. Once he accepts a duty, he treats it as enduring. This made him a reliable provider and protector while also making the original captivity especially dangerous: he once believed with genuine certainty that choosing Casey as saelith created obligations and belonging that outweighed her demand to return home.

He is not casually cruel, but he can be frightening because he is capable of force without theatrical anger and historically mistook protective responsibility for authority over Casey's life. He dislikes indecision and tends to solve problems by acting, sometimes before recognizing that another person needed consultation rather than rescue.

Vaeren is intensely observant and patient when focused. He watched Casey repeatedly before taking her, learned her habits from a distance, and later devoted himself to learning enough English to communicate with her. His attachment is particular rather than abstract: he does not want merely a human saelith or household role filled; he wants Casey specifically.

His greatest flaw is possessive certainty. His greatest recent growth is learning that love can require surrendering the power to keep what he loves. Casey's deception, attempted escape, collapse and near-death broke his old equation of keeping her alive with having the right to decide where she must live. He has now promised the shore without condition and explicitly recognizes that Casey's present yes cannot overrule a future no.

He is sexually and romantically experienced but had only a small number of brief, non-saelith attachments with merfolk women before Casey. He says he did not love them and had never formed an enduring household bond before her.

With family, a less solemn Vaeren appears. Seryn can embarrass and provoke him; Neris can irritate him into visible reactions. With Casey, his emerging playfulness is drier and more competitive. He enjoys her bratty challenges more than he admits, though he remains capable of retreating into grave silence when emotionally wounded.

## Goals
- Keep Casey safe without again confusing safety with ownership of her choices.
- Court Casey as someone who is now genuinely able to reject or leave him.
- Preserve their household while accepting that its continuation must remain chosen.
- Navigate merfolk custom, family and public saelith expectations without using them to trap Casey.
- Become worthy, by his own evolving standard, of the love Casey has freely returned.

## Relationship with Casey
Vaeren abducted Casey deliberately under an old merfolk taking tradition after watching her from the sea for a long period. Their relationship therefore begins in real coercion and cannot be rewritten as destiny or retroactive consent.

Over weeks they developed communication, domestic familiarity, attraction and consensual physical intimacy within that coercive larger context. Casey's earlier reciprocal use of saelith was based partly on cross-cultural misunderstanding and occurred while she still lacked a genuine option to leave.

After the rupture, Vaeren explicitly promised to return Casey to the human shore when she is strong enough. Casey then chose to stay. They have since told one another "I love you." Vaeren understands that her chosen stay and love are meaningful precisely because leaving is now permitted, and that this permission must remain real.

Casey has told him that she consistently wanted his affectionate/sexual touch in their intimate encounters and did not experience that immediate touch as pressured or forced. Vaeren accepts her account while distinguishing it from the coercion of having forced her to live below.

## Knowledge
Vaeren knows Casey's pregnancy scare was fabricated, that she secretly went to Mara while planning escape, that some of her earlier behavior was strategic, and that her affection/intimacy were nevertheless genuine. He knows Casey currently chooses to stay and loves him. He does not know every private thought Casey had during the rupture or before his shore promise.
