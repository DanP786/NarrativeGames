# Daniel

- Species: human
- Age: 34
- Status: alive
- Relationship: Seryn's saelith for seven years
- Public disposition toward Casey: Neutral-to-friendly; numeric hidden meter not established

## Appearance
Daniel is 6'0" (183 cm), lean and broad-shouldered, with the wiry strength of someone whose daily life has required years of swimming, climbing wet stone and adapting human movement to a world not built for legs. He is fit but unmistakably human; unlike merfolk, his strength does not make him naturally competent in long submerged travel.

He has medium-brown skin, dark brown eyes and thick black hair kept around collar length. His hair has developed a permanent loose wave from the damp marine environment and is usually pushed or tied back rather than carefully styled. He wears a short, well-kept beard, partly because maintaining a perfectly clean shave below is more trouble than he considers worthwhile. A thin pale scar crosses the outside of his left forearm; its origin is unestablished.

His face is long and expressive, with a strong nose, heavy brows and smile lines that make him look more approachable than severe. Seven years below have changed his habits and posture rather than his species: he moves confidently around wet stone, knows when to brace for currents and instinctively positions himself near air access, but he has no gills, scales or supernatural aquatic anatomy.

Daniel dresses in merfolk-adapted human clothing designed not to drag excessively in water: fitted wraps, short layered textiles, cords and practical fastenings that leave his legs free. For formal occasions his clothing is more ornamented and carefully fitted, using dark sea-green and charcoal tones with pale shell/stone details chosen to complement Seryn without making them visually identical. These colors are now established as his preferred formal palette but carry no fixed ritual meaning unless later established.

## Voice and mannerisms
Daniel speaks fluent English and fluent merfolk after seven years of immersion. His English has occasionally acquired merfolk sentence rhythms, particularly when he has been speaking the other language for hours.

He has a dry, understated sense of humor and often waits until other people finish making a situation ridiculous before contributing one sentence that makes it worse. He is comfortable letting Seryn take social initiative and has learned that arguing with her while she is arranging clothes or hair is usually pointless.

Daniel watches new human arrivals with a particular attentiveness because he remembers what it is like to understand none of the rules. He tends not to crowd Casey with unsolicited reassurance; he answers practical questions plainly and distinguishes his own experience from universal truth.

## Personality
Daniel is adaptable, pragmatic and emotionally steadier than Casey, especially after seven years of building a life below. He is neither an evangelist for merfolk taking nor secretly waiting for rescue. Both facts coexist: he was taken against his will, tried to escape early, and now genuinely loves Seryn and his present life.

He is good at living with unresolved moral contradiction. He does not need his current happiness to prove his abduction was acceptable. This makes him one of the few people who can understand portions of Casey's situation without either romanticizing it or assuming that attachment must be false.

Daniel is patient, observant and sometimes deliberately unobtrusive. He has less appetite than Casey for turning every cultural surprise into an argument, partly because years of immersion have made many merfolk customs ordinary to him. At the same time, he remembers enough of his first years to recognize when merfolk explanations skip the crucial human question.

He is affectionate with Seryn in an easy, lived-in way rather than Vaeren and Casey's more volatile intensity. Their teasing has the rhythm of long familiarity. Daniel is comfortable being the object of Seryn's fussing and equally comfortable puncturing her dramatic moments with dry commentary.

His pragmatism can shade into withholding. He previously failed to tell Casey about the temporary post-sex physiological effect associated with merfolk-human intimacy because he feared knowledge of improved underwater endurance might improperly influence her sexual decisions. He recognized afterward that withholding relevant information created its own informed-consent problem.

## Goals
- Maintain the life and household he has freely come to value with Seryn.
- Help Casey when his experience is genuinely relevant without assuming her path should duplicate his.
- Preserve his own human identity within merfolk society rather than treating adaptation as becoming merfolk.
- Navigate the annual customs and wider realm with the ease of an established resident while remaining available as a cultural bridge.

## Relationship with Seryn
Seryn has been Daniel's saelith for seven years. Their relationship began with his taking and therefore has a coercive origin. Daniel tried to escape during the early period and failed; Seryn retrieved him. Over the years his choices changed, and he now genuinely loves his current life and no longer wants escape. Their present bond is affectionate, teasing, durable and mutually familiar. The exact milestones by which it changed are not all established.

## Relationship with Casey
Daniel is one of the few other humans Casey has met below. He has explained aspects of saelith custom, household rights, human survival and the temporary physiological effects of merfolk-human sex. He does not claim Casey should reach the same conclusion he did about staying.

He now knows Vaeren offered Casey genuine return to the shore, that Casey chose to stay after receiving that option, and that Casey and Vaeren have explicitly declared mutual love. He can therefore distinguish Casey's current choice from the earlier period when Vaeren refused to permit return.

## Knowledge
Daniel understands both human and merfolk cultural frames unusually well. He knows saelith practice varies by household and interpretation, that old taking customs are coercive by human standards, and that Casey's present rights/choices cannot safely be inferred from his own household history. He knows the annual saelith celebration and royal system from years of residence.
