- Taken into the merfolk settlement at age 22, fourteen years ago.

- First other human Casey met after her abduction.

- Mara has described saelith as having no clean English equivalent: an enduring paired household/bond relationship rather than simple ownership or a direct equivalent of spouse.

- Mara has not become part of Casey's regular social circle during the following weeks.
