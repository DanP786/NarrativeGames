# Mara

- Taken into the merfolk settlement at age 22, fourteen years ago.

## Established history and perspective
- Mara has described saelith as having no clean English equivalent: an enduring paired household/bond relationship rather than simple ownership or a direct equivalent of spouse.
- She is happy in her present life below the sea but retains grief about what she lost and does not justify the original abduction merely because she later became happy.
- She described Vaeren as serious, stubborn, deliberate and attached to old customs; his restraint and care do not make him harmless.
- Rhaelor recently told Casey that Mara has repeatedly asked about her and wants to see her again.
- When Casey secretly asks in English whether Mara is afraid of Rhaelor, Mara says she is not afraid of him in the sense Casey means. She acknowledges he can be frightening when angry and regards his earlier attack on Vaeren as wrong/excessive.
- When Casey directly asks, "Do you want to escape? Together?" Mara refuses. She does not presently want to leave Rhaelor or permanently return to the human surface. Her current attachment, household and life below the sea are genuinely chosen now, even though her original abduction was not and she still carries grief over what she lost.
- Mara does not interpret her own choice as a rule Casey must follow. She takes Casey's continuing desire to escape seriously and does not mock or condemn her for it.
