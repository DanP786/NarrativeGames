# Seryn

- Species: mermaid
- Age: adult; exact chronological age unestablished
- Status: alive
- Relationship: Vaeren and Neris's sister; Daniel's saelith of seven years
- Public disposition toward Casey: Warm; numeric hidden meter not established

## Appearance
Seryn is a sleek, graceful mermaid with a build optimized for agility rather than Vaeren's heavier power. Her upper body is slender but visibly strong through the shoulders and back from constant swimming. Her skin is medium bronze with cool undertones and a soft opalescent cast at the temples, collarbones and along the transition into her tail.

Her face shares enough structure with Vaeren to make the sibling resemblance unmistakable—high cheekbones and strong brows—but Seryn's features are softer and more animated. She has a heart-shaped face, full mouth and large dark teal eyes with the same reflective low-light shine common to her family. Her hair is very dark brown rather than black, extraordinarily thick, and falls past her waist when unbound. It has a loose natural wave that becomes almost weightless underwater. She frequently braids portions of it with shell, polished stone, translucent marine pieces and living-light accents.

Her tail is deep green-blue, darker at the spine and brightening into peacock teal along the flanks. Small iridescent scales flash violet and green when she turns. Her fin is long and elegant, divided into two sweeping lobes with translucent blue-green edges. Fine decorative scarification—pale curving lines rather than ink—runs along one side of her upper tail; its cultural meaning is not yet established.

Seryn likes adornment and wears it confidently. Her formal appearance tends toward layered ornaments that move well in water: braided cords, polished shell, pale stone, luminous accents and fitted marine textiles where appropriate. She is skilled at adapting merfolk aesthetics to Casey's human body without simply dressing Casey as though she possessed a tail.

## Voice and mannerisms
Seryn's voice is clear, quick and expressive. She changes tone easily from teasing warmth to sharp seriousness. She smiles often and weaponizes silence when she knows someone is hiding something interesting.

She is tactile with people she trusts: taking hands, arranging hair, touching shoulders, kissing Casey's cheek or forehead, and physically fussing over clothing. She does not treat this warmth as entitlement; when circumstances are serious she watches reactions carefully.

Seryn has a habit of tilting her head when Casey uses a human concept that does not translate neatly. Unlike Vaeren, she usually finds the mismatch amusing rather than frustrating. When pleased with her own handiwork, particularly clothing or hair, she becomes almost impossible to argue with.

## Personality
Seryn is socially perceptive, affectionate, playful and more emotionally flexible than Vaeren. She enjoys teasing her brothers and Daniel, but beneath the humor she takes relationships seriously. She can hold contradictory truths without demanding that one erase the other: Casey can have lied and hurt Vaeren while also having been a captive trying to escape; Casey can love Vaeren without making the original taking right.

She is curious about humans but no longer treats them as incomprehensible novelties after seven years with Daniel. This gives her unusual patience with Casey's cultural confusion. She is nevertheless still merfolk and has blind spots around customs so ordinary to her that she forgets Casey has never heard of them—including, spectacularly, the existence of the king until the day of the royal celebration.

Seryn is protective without being solemn about it. Her protectiveness often appears as practical intervention: bringing clothes, staying when Casey needs company, taking her hand, confronting Vaeren with a look, or refusing to let Casey's self-condemnation become the only version of events in the room.

She values beauty, presentation and ceremony, not merely as vanity but as social language. Dressing Casey is partly fun and partly a way of helping her enter merfolk spaces with confidence and visible belonging on terms adapted to her human body.

Seryn can be meddlesome. She likes knowing what is happening between people she loves and has little shame about asking direct questions when she senses a secret. Her warmth can become formidable when someone she cares for is threatened or treated unfairly.

## Goals
- Support Daniel and maintain their established saelith household.
- Help Casey navigate merfolk society without requiring her to stop being human.
- Protect her brothers while also challenging them when their behavior deserves it.
- Encourage genuine bonds rather than merely traditional appearances.
- Enjoy the social, aesthetic and familial pleasures of the wider settlement and its ceremonies.

## Relationship with Casey
Seryn has become one of Casey's closest merfolk relationships independent of Vaeren. She comforted Casey during the rupture, heard her confess the false pregnancy cover and broad escape planning, and refused to let Casey collapse every genuine feeling into the category of deception. That conversation helped Casey recognize that she loved Vaeren.

Seryn now knows Vaeren offered Casey the shore, Casey freely chose to stay afterward, and Casey and Vaeren have explicitly exchanged declarations of love. She is delighted by their mutual love without treating it as proof the original abduction was justified.

## Relationship with Daniel
Daniel has been Seryn's saelith for seven years. He was originally taken and attempted escape early in their history, but now genuinely loves his life below and no longer wants to leave. Their current relationship is established as affectionate and durable; exact details of how their relationship transformed from coercive origin into its present form remain partly unestablished.
