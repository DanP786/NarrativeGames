# Main thread

- Status: established and hardened
- Central question: Can anything genuine grow between Casey and Vaeren when their relationship begins in abduction, captivity and profound unequal power—and what happens if Casey's growing attachment never becomes consent to the life he chose for her?
- PC want: Casey wants safety, autonomy, understanding, meaningful freedom of movement, and ultimately a way home or out of captivity. Her private strategy has been to learn the language and settlement, gain trust and influence, and use that leverage in hopes of eventually persuading Vaeren to return her or creating an escape opportunity.
- Antagonist / obstacle: Vaeren himself, the ancient merfolk assumptions that make his taking culturally meaningful, Casey's human biological vulnerability in an underwater world, and the disputed status of a human's continuing refusal under saelith custom.
- Established direction: Vaeren deliberately chose Casey after watching her from the sea and forcibly brought her alive into the merfolk settlement under the old taking tradition. "Saelith" is an enduring paired household/bond status rather than simple ownership or a clean equivalent of human spouse/mate. It imposes duties of air, food, warmth, refuge, protection and social recognition on the merfolk partner, while older interpretations treat the initial taking as sufficient to create an enduring bond even without human consent. Less traditional interpretations give greater weight to continuing refusal and may recognize dissolution/return in disputed cases.
- Relationship state: Casey and Vaeren developed real trust, attraction and voluntary physical intimacy without erasing the coercive foundation. Casey explicitly called Vaeren her saelith in return and has genuinely reciprocated much of his affection/intimacy, but she also continued privately wanting the surface and hoped his attachment would eventually give her leverage or an escape opportunity. Reciprocal saelith status has never been blanket sexual consent.
- Current pressure: After a clandestine solo trip to Mara in which Casey unsuccessfully proposed escaping together, Casey covered the trip with a false pregnancy scare. Vaeren believed her and responded with concern, tenderness and an offer of greater privacy. Overwhelmed by guilt and by how impossible the later stages of her escape strategy feel now that much of their relationship is genuine, Casey sobbed that she still wants her human home. When Vaeren confronted the contradiction with her reciprocal saelith claim, Casey admitted she had been lying. Asked what she lied about, she answered, "Everything…"
- Critical distinction: Casey's "everything" is an emotionally sweeping confession, not a canon retcon that every affectionate act or intimate choice was fabricated. Her attraction, affection, enjoyment, home-making and attachment have become real. What has also been real is her continuing wish to return and her strategy of cultivating trust/influence partly as leverage. Vaeren does not yet understand this distinction and currently has reason to fear that even the parts he treasured were performance.
- Sharp unresolved issue: Vaeren's belief that the imposed bond became mutually chosen has ruptured. He now needs to learn what "everything" actually means: whether the pregnancy was false, whether Casey truly regards him as saelith, whether her affection/desire were genuine, where she really went, and whether she has been planning escape. Casey must decide how much truth to tell, while her genuine attachment and genuine desire for the surface collide openly rather than remaining compartmentalized.
- Outcome remains open: romance, escape, negotiated autonomy, dissolution, return to the surface, reconciliation, tragedy or some other resolution are not predetermined.
