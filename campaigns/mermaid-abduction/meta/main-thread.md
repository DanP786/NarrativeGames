# Main thread

- Status: sketch
- Central question: Can anything genuine grow between Casey and the merman who takes her when their relationship begins in captivity and profound unequal power?
- PC want: Initially, Casey wants safety, autonomy, understanding, and—once the truth becomes clear—a way out of captivity.
- Antagonist / obstacle: Her merman captor and the ancient merfolk assumptions that make his taking of her meaningful in his culture; additionally, Casey's human vulnerability in a world not built for her.
- Establishing direction: Bring the captor and the folklore behind his choice on-screen; make Casey's response to being taken concrete; allow the relationship's actual shape to emerge rather than predetermining romance, escape, or reconciliation.
