# Main thread

- Status: established
- Central question: Can anything genuine grow between Casey and the merman who takes her when their relationship begins in captivity and profound unequal power?
- PC want: Casey wants safety, autonomy, understanding, and a way home or out of captivity.
- Antagonist / obstacle: Her merman captor and the ancient merfolk assumptions that make his taking of her meaningful in his culture; additionally, Casey's human vulnerability in a world not built for her.
- Established direction: The captor deliberately chose Casey after watching her from the sea and brought her alive into a merfolk settlement according to an old taking tradition. He considers the human limitations he anticipated—air, cold, underwater travel—problems to accommodate rather than reasons the taking cannot occur. When Casey asks what he wants, he indicates that he wants Casey herself in a culturally meaningful role expressed by an untranslated merfolk term. The exact nature, obligations and contested meaning of that role remain to emerge through play; romance, escape and reconciliation remain undetermined.
