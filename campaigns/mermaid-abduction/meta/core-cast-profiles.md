# Core cast profiles — Casey, Vaeren, Seryn, Daniel

These profiles consolidate established characterization and visual canon as of the party's arrival at the annual saelith celebration. Details not established in play remain explicitly open rather than being invented.

## Casey

### Appearance
- Human woman, age 25.
- Her exact height, build, ethnicity, skin tone, eye color, natural hair color and ordinary surface wardrobe have not been fixed in repository canon and remain open unless established later.
- She is unmistakably human among merfolk: legs rather than a tail, ordinary human respiratory limits, and no gills. Several weeks below have made her more practiced in underwater travel, but she remains biologically human and dependent on breathable air.
- For tonight's annual saelith celebration, Seryn has dressed her in formal clothing made for movement in both air and water: layered deep-blue and blue-green material, fitted securely through the torso, with pale iridescent detailing that catches the living light. Her legs remain free for swimming rather than being enclosed in a human-style gown.
- Seryn has arranged Casey's hair in an elaborate formal style of multiple swept-back, interwoven braids. Casey's existing ornaments and additional pale/iridescent pieces are worked through the braids. The style is intended to survive submerged travel. No specific ritual meaning has been established for the ornaments.
- In manner, Casey is expressive. Her face, posture and gestures readily carry amusement, indignation, embarrassment, fear and curiosity. When comfortable she can become theatrically huffy or visibly mischievous; when genuinely pressured or frightened she is more likely to retreat, appease or struggle to assert herself.

### Personality
- Fundamentally a people person: warm, approachable, socially intuitive and naturally inclined to make other people comfortable.
- Her strongest social instinct is de-escalation rather than dominance. She reads atmosphere reasonably well, softens tense exchanges and often disarms people without consciously treating this as a technique.
- Serious confrontation is difficult for her, especially against forceful personalities. Her ability to tease Vaeren or argue when safe should not be mistaken for effortless assertiveness under coercion or threat.
- Once she trusts someone, a pronounced bratty/playful streak appears. She teases, exaggerates indignation, pushes for reactions, makes mock threats with cushions, invents things like Vaeren's "courtship score," and enjoys getting the better of people she loves.
- She is emotionally demonstrative and capable of impulsive affection. At the same time, guilt can become severe and self-punishing; after the rupture with Vaeren, her shame and despair contributed to dangerous withdrawal, food refusal and a suicidal crisis. Current happiness does not erase that recent fragility.
- Casey can hold contradictory truths rather than reducing them to one clean answer. She recognizes that Vaeren abducted and kept her against her will, while also recognizing that her attraction, desired intimacy, affection and eventual love for him were genuine. She has explicitly said that his affectionate/sexual touch was wanted in those immediate contexts, without treating that as consent to captivity.
- Her relationship to Vaeren has changed fundamentally because he finally restored a real alternative: he promised to take her to the human shore once strong enough. Only after that promise did Casey explicitly choose to remain below with him. She has since told him she loves him. Her stay is a present choice, not an irrevocable surrender of the right to leave.
- She speaks merfolk in ordinary life now at a developing level; dialogue is generally rendered in English for readability. Rapid, unfamiliar or culturally dense language can still challenge her.

## Vaeren

### Appearance
- Adult merman; exact age has not been established.
- Almost-human face with severe cheekbones and reflective, distinctly nonhuman eyes. His skin carries a faint marine sheen.
- Dark hair, usually affected by the water around him rather than styled according to human expectations.
- Broad shoulders and a powerful upper body, continuing into a long, muscular scaled tail built for speed and force underwater.
- His physical presence is imposing even when he is still. Underwater he is extraordinarily more capable than Casey: fast, strong and completely at home in an environment that remains dangerous to her.
- His exact scale colors, eye color, height-equivalent, scars and detailed formal adornment have not been fixed in repository canon and remain open.
- At tonight's celebration he is formally dressed/adorned according to merfolk custom, but the exact pieces and their symbolism have not yet been established.
- His body language tends toward controlled stillness rather than restless movement. When territorial or protective, he closes physical space beside Casey, watches other people carefully and places himself where he can intervene, but his post-rupture development requires that this not become obstruction of Casey's choices.

### Personality
- Serious, stubborn, deliberate and deeply shaped by old merfolk custom. He is not casually cruel, but his original worldview allowed him to commit profound cruelty while understanding himself as responsible and caring.
- Once Vaeren decides something matters, he commits intensely. This quality drove the original taking: he watched Casey repeatedly from offshore, became fixated on her, deliberately chose her and enacted an old human-taking tradition despite her resistance.
- His care is practical and observant. He notices cold, hunger, breath, fatigue and physical danger. He provides food, warmth, breathable refuge and protection, and he tends to express attachment through doing and providing as much as through speech.
- Historically, his greatest moral blind spot was confusing responsibility with authority: because he had taken Casey and believed himself obligated to keep her alive and provide for her, he believed he also had the right to decide that she must remain below. The rupture forced him to confront that keeping someone alive is not the same as having the right to decide where that life belongs.
- He is possessive and territorial by temperament, especially about saelith attachment, but is learning the difference between wanting Casey and having a right to keep her. His current possessiveness can appear as courtship, jealousy, proximity and protective attention; it cannot legitimately override a refusal or his promise of the shore.
- He is capable of considerable restraint. Even during captivity he did not treat saelith status as blanket sexual consent, and Casey has since clarified that the affectionate/sexual contact they did share was wanted by her in those immediate moments.
- Vaeren is not naturally verbally expansive. Important admissions tend to cost him something. His eventual declaration of love came only after he had learned to distinguish love from possession: he explicitly recognized that he would rather give Casey up than keep her wrongly.
- He has a dry, increasingly playful side that Casey draws out. He can tease, be smug, retreat into the pool when threatened with a cushion, and participate in domestic absurdity despite his generally severe demeanor.
- Before Casey, he had a small number of brief romantic/physical attachments with merfolk women and prior sexual experience, but no previous saelith or enduring household bond. He says he did not love those women. Casey is the first person for whom his attachment has forced a fundamental reordering of his beliefs.
- He loves Casey and knows she loves him. Crucially, he has promised that if she later asks for the human shore, he will take her there; her present choice to stay does not cancel that promise.

## Seryn

### Appearance
- Adult mermaid; Vaeren and Neris's sister and Daniel's saelith.
- Her exact age, facial features, skin tone, hair color, eye color, scale coloration, build and tail details have NOT yet been established in repository canon. Generated imagery outside the repo is not automatically canon.
- She is currently formally dressed/adorned for the annual saelith celebration, but exact clothing, jewelry and symbolic details remain open.
- Her presentation is established more strongly through behavior than fixed physical traits: she is comfortable with ornament, clothing and hair arrangement and has repeatedly taken pleasure in dressing Casey and working decorative pieces into Casey's appearance.
- Her movements and expressions tend to be socially readable and animated: delighted looks, stern protective looks, teasing scrutiny and affectionate physical gestures such as holding Casey's hand, hugging her and kissing her cheek/forehead have all appeared in play.

### Personality
- Warm, playful, socially confident and affectionate, with a strong streak of practical emotional intelligence.
- Seryn teases easily, particularly within family, but she is not merely comic relief. When Casey was in serious distress, Seryn could become quiet, protective and direct without demanding that Casey perform happiness for her.
- She is capable of holding moral contradictions. When Casey confessed lying about pregnancy and secretly planning to leave, Seryn did not declare the deception harmless; she called the lie wrong and hurtful while also recognizing that a captive trying to escape the man who took her may resort to deception.
- She was instrumental in breaking Casey's guilt-driven belief that everything between Casey and Vaeren had been false. Rather than telling Casey what she felt, Seryn asked whether specific moments of care, pleasure and attachment had also been lies. That helped Casey realize her genuine feelings belonged to her even if other parts of the relationship were coercive.
- Seryn is protective of both Casey and Vaeren without collapsing their interests into one. She can love her brother and still give him a stern look, criticize him, or recognize that he has done wrong.
- She enjoys family chaos. She readily joined Casey's cushion-side against Vaeren, jokingly banished him from his own chamber, and was delighted by Casey's invented "courtship score."
- She has a strong aesthetic/ceremonial interest and appears comfortable navigating merfolk social customs that remain unfamiliar to Casey. She brought Casey a formal outfit and created an elaborate hairstyle for the annual saelith celebration.
- Seryn is Daniel's saelith of seven years. Their current relationship is loving and settled, but Daniel's original taking/coercion is not erased by that outcome.
- She now knows Vaeren offered Casey the shore, Casey freely chose to stay afterward, and Casey and Vaeren have explicitly declared mutual love. She reacted with delight while still treating Vaeren's restoration of Casey's choice as something he finally did correctly—not as proof that the original taking was justified.

## Daniel

### Appearance
- Adult human man; Seryn's saelith for seven years.
- His exact age, height, build, ethnicity, skin tone, hair color/style, eye color, scars and other physical specifics have NOT yet been established in repository canon and remain open.
- He remains biologically human and does not have gills. He has lived below for seven years and is highly adapted in knowledge and routine, but his humanity and respiratory needs remain relevant.
- He is formally dressed for tonight's annual saelith celebration. Exact garments/adornments and their symbolism have not yet been established.
- He carries himself with the ease of someone deeply accustomed to this world and its customs. Compared with Casey, he reads as an experienced resident rather than a bewildered newcomer, even though both are human.

### Personality
- Grounded, dryly humorous and pragmatic. Daniel often serves as a human cultural bridge for Casey because he understands both surface assumptions and merfolk household/customary logic.
- He is fluent in English and merfolk and has enough years below to explain customs without treating every merfolk assumption as self-evident.
- His humor is understated. He tends to recognize when silence is safer during Seryn/Vaeren family teasing, and he can puncture tension with a dry observation rather than competing for attention.
- Daniel's history prevents a simplistic "happy captive" reading. He was taken by Seryn, struggled with that reality and tried to escape early. He failed because the underwater routes were beyond his human capabilities and Seryn retrieved him. He no longer wants to escape and genuinely loves his current life with Seryn, but the initial coercion remains part of the truth.
- His inward relational struggle was not identical to Casey's. He has jokingly characterized human men as "simple" in that context, but this is teasing rather than objective lore or a universal claim about gender.
- He is capable of caution around consent and information, though not infallibly. He previously withheld information about the temporary post-sex merfolk biological effect because he worried that knowing the mobility benefit might influence Casey's sexual choices; he later acknowledged the informed-consent problem with withholding it.
- Daniel understands that saelith is not straightforward ownership and that household practice varies. He has explained that established saelith can have friendships, visits and practical freedom, while old taking customs remain coercive by human standards and questions of dissolution/return are culturally disputed.
- He now knows Vaeren offered Casey a genuine return to shore and that Casey chose to stay afterward. He also knows Casey and Vaeren have explicitly declared mutual love. His response is warm and amused rather than triumphalist; he does not treat Casey's love as retroactive justification for her abduction.

## Group dynamic at the celebration
- Casey and Vaeren are newly and explicitly in love, with Casey's current stay now grounded in a genuine option to leave. Their dynamic mixes intense attachment, rebuilding trust, cultural negotiation and increasingly playful courtship.
- Seryn is Casey's closest established merfolk female friend and also Vaeren's sister, placing her in a natural but complicated bridge role. She tends to protect Casey without ceasing to care about Vaeren.
- Daniel is the most experienced human bridge in the immediate group. His seven years below give him perspective Casey lacks, while his own history means he can recognize both genuine attachment and coercive origins.
- Seryn and Daniel form the established long-term saelith pair against which Casey and Vaeren's much newer, more volatile bond is often implicitly contrasted.
- The four currently travel/arrive together at the annual saelith celebration in the Tide Hall. Casey is likely to draw attention as an uncommon recently taken human appearing formally beside Vaeren. Vaeren is attentive and territorially protective, but Casey remains free to speak, interact and choose.
