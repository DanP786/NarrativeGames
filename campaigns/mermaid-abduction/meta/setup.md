# Campaign setup

Created: 31 August 2026 (real-world date)
Slug: mermaid-abduction

## Onboarding answers

**Genre / premise:** Dark maritime folklore and mythic romance. A merman abducts a human woman. The taking of human women is deeply rooted in merfolk folklore and tradition rather than an aberration, though what the tradition truly means and how it functions will emerge through play.

**Voice:** Third person, present tense.

**Prose style:** Blend of dark maritime folklore and mythic romance. Lush, atmospheric, ominous sea imagery and old-story weight, with lyrical but accessible emotional focus. Fear, fascination and intimacy receive room to breathe. Merfolk should feel genuinely alien rather than like humans with aquatic decoration.

**Length:** Open-ended sandbox. No fixed endpoint and no act tracker.

**Tone / content posture:** Dark. Nothing categorically off the table. Captivity, violence, horror, sexuality, moral darkness and other difficult material may appear when fictionally appropriate.

**Stakes / scope:** Intimate captivity, while exploring a large hidden mythical underwater world. The camera remains with Casey and the merfolk world. No human rescue/investigation plot: humans believe Casey drowned and her body was never recovered. Humanity considers mermaids and merfolk fantasy and does not know they are real.

**Agency posture:** Gritty survival. Failure is real and consequential. Casey has ordinary human physical limits in a world not designed for her, and the world does not bend to keep her safe.

**Death rule:** Hard death enabled. Casey can die in genuinely lethal circumstances.

**Mind-reading rule:** None. No one can read minds or directly perceive inner thoughts. Characters infer only from speech, actions and visible behaviour.

**Main thread:** Captor and captive. The emotional heart is Casey's relationship with the merman who abducts her: fear, hostility, dependence, attempts at understanding, unequal power, attraction if it develops, and whether anything genuine can grow from an abduction. No romantic outcome is predetermined, and the abduction is not retroactively made harmless by later feelings.

**Character seed:** Casey, age 25. Human woman. She works at Hooters while studying to become a nurse. She would not describe herself as particularly good at anything, but she is a people person who is good at disarming people and putting them at ease. She is poor at standing up for herself and confrontation.

**Opening situation:** Chosen from the shore. The merman has seen Casey before, perhaps many times. Casey has no idea merfolk exist or that she has been watched. An old merfolk tradition or piece of folklore finally moves him from watching to taking her.

**Confirmed starting skills:** Adept — Rapport & De-escalation; Novice — Practical First Aid; Novice — Reading People. Notable Untrained gap — Assertiveness/Intimidation.
