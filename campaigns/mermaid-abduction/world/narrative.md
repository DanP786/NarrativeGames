# Narrative

Casey, a 25-year-old nursing student, was unknowingly watched from the sea by a merman shaped by ancient folklore concerning the taking of human women. On a late-summer night he revealed himself near her familiar shore after luring her concern by appearing like a swimmer in the dark. When Casey realized he had been watching her and tried to retreat, he seized her by the ankle, dragged her beyond standing depth, took hold of her, and pulled her beneath the sea. The ancient taking has begun in earnest.
