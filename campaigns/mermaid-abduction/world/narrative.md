# Narrative

Casey, a 25-year-old nursing student, was unknowingly watched from the sea by a merman shaped by ancient folklore concerning the taking of human women. On a late-summer night he revealed himself near her familiar shore after luring her concern by appearing like a swimmer in the dark. When Casey realized he had been watching her and tried to retreat, he seized her and dragged her beneath the sea.

Casey fought repeatedly for the surface, but her ordinary human strength was useless against him underwater. When she reached the edge of drowning, the merman supplied air directly from his mouth, then continued carrying her downward. Casey eventually abandoned both physical resistance and attempts to gesture for the surface after it became clear he understood her distress but would not turn back. He now carries her through deep water while repeatedly controlling the breaths that keep her alive.
