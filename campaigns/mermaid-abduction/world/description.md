# World description

The human world is contemporary and mundane. Merfolk are folklore to humanity: stories, art, tourist kitsch, and fantasy. No recognised human institution knows they are real.

Beyond the human shore lies a hidden mythical marine world. Merfolk possess their own communities, customs, taboos, stories and ways of understanding the sea. Their biology and culture are not merely human society transplanted underwater. Other mythical marine beings, places and dangers may exist and will be established through exploration rather than catalogued in advance.

Among at least part of merfolk culture, stories of mermen taking human women from the shore are ancient and culturally significant. The exact origins, rules, disagreements, ritual meanings and consequences of this folklore remain to be discovered in play. The practice is not assumed to be morally benign merely because it is traditional.

Humans believe Casey drowned after her disappearance. Her body is never recovered. No human rescue or investigation thread intrudes into the campaign; the narrative follows Casey into the hidden world.
