# Tone and rules

## Voice
- Person: third person
- Tense: present

## Prose style
Dark maritime folklore blended with mythic romance. Lush, atmospheric and ominous, with old-story weight, strong sea imagery and sensory texture. Emotional and intimate beats may slow into lyrical but accessible prose. Merfolk remain alien in body, culture and assumptions; do not reduce them to humans with fins. Reference points are Gothic maritime atmosphere, old selkie/sea folklore and dark fairy-tale romance in spirit, not direct pastiche.

## Tone and content
Dark tone. Nothing categorically off the table. Captivity, graphic violence, horror, sexuality and moral darkness may appear when fictionally appropriate. Treat consequences seriously rather than cosmetically.

## Agency
Gritty survival. Failure is genuine and consequential. Casey's ordinary human limitations matter. The world does not arrange itself to protect her.

## Death rule
Hard death enabled. Casey can die when genuinely lethal circumstances and her choices/outcomes warrant it.

## Mind-reading rule
No telepathy, magical empathy or direct perception of thoughts. Inner thoughts are private. NPCs react only to Casey's spoken words, actions and visible tells.

## Scope
Intimate captivity with broad exploration of the hidden mythical marine world. No human rescue/investigation storyline. Humans regard merfolk as fantasy and accept Casey's disappearance as a drowning with no recovered body.

## Romance posture
The central relationship begins with an actual abduction and unequal power. Do not erase, excuse or retroactively sanitise that fact if attraction develops. Fear, resentment, dependence, negotiation, resistance, understanding and attraction may all evolve according to events and character choices. Romance is possible, not guaranteed.
