# Current scene

- Scene significance: charged
- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber within the unnamed deep merfolk settlement
- Time: after Casey has slept for several hours; exact surface time unknown
- Casey remains captive after being forcibly taken from the shore by Vaeren, who refuses to return her human home and calls her "saelith."
- Casey privately concludes that an immediate escape attempt is unlikely to succeed: she cannot navigate or swim to the surface unaided and expects Vaeren could catch her quickly. She forms a tentative strategy of learning the merfolk language and more about the settlement before trying to persuade Vaeren to return her home.
- Casey removes and wrings out her soaked shirt, wrings and deliberately arranges her hair, and wraps a woven covering around her shoulders. Conscious that human men have often treated her as physically attractive, she deliberately presents herself appealingly as part of an attempt to engage Vaeren and gain leverage/understanding rather than from established trust or romantic commitment.
- Casey sits at the water's edge with her legs in the pool and Vaeren positioned in the water between them. With the covering not fully concealing her upper chest, she taps herself and says, "Casey. Saelith," testing the word and inviting Vaeren to respond/teach.
- Vaeren visibly recognizes both the deliberate closeness and the use of "saelith." He does not sexualize the moment into immediate physical escalation; instead he answers by identifying Casey as saelith, then himself as Vaeren, and begins using simple gestures and repeated words to distinguish names, person, bond and home, taking her attempt as an opening for communication.
- Casey's longer-term intentions remain private and unknown to Vaeren.
