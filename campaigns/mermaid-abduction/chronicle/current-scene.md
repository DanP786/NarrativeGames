# Current scene

- Scene significance: charged
- Location: submerged route returning toward Casey and Vaeren's air chamber
- Time: several weeks after Daniel and Seryn's housewarming visit; returning home from the hidden air garden; exact surface date/time remains difficult for Casey to verify below the sea
- Casey and Vaeren are returning from the hidden air garden, carrying a small transplant of living blue-green growth intended for the submerged margin of their household pool. Earlier on the route they unexpectedly encountered Rhaelor, Mara's saelith.
- Rhaelor asked whether Casey intends to see Mara again and reported that Mara has repeatedly asked about Casey and wants to see her. Casey refused twice, hid behind Vaeren and wrapped her arms around him. When Casey needed air but would not leave that sheltering position, Vaeren immediately ended the exchange and took her to the nearest familiar breathable refuge. Rhaelor did not follow or obstruct them.
- In the refuge, Casey ranted in merfolk that she likes Mara but refuses to visit under Rhaelor's household conditions after Rhaelor frightened her and violently attacked Vaeren. During the rant she accidentally invented an absurd phrase roughly meaning Rhaelor may "go court his own tail," which Vaeren teased her about.
- Their teasing escalated into Casey insisting she can swim and threatening, "I can make it home without you." Vaeren refused to let her make the remaining submerged journey truly alone but offered to follow while letting her swim under her own power.
- Casey takes a deliberate breath and begins navigating the familiar homeward route herself. Weeks of guided exploration show in her movement: she is more practiced and less panicked underwater than when first taken, though still human and air-limited.
- Casey improvises a human-specific way to gain speed: wherever the familiar passage gives her a safe hard surface at a useful angle, she plants her feet against stone and forcefully pushes off, gliding faster before resuming her stroke. She uses walls, ledges and sloping rock opportunistically rather than trying to match Vaeren's tail-driven swimming directly. The technique works well in confined stretches and briefly gives her satisfying bursts of speed, though repeated hard pushes also spend oxygen and leg strength faster than relaxed swimming.
- Vaeren follows without towing or carrying her, keeping enough distance that the effort and navigation are genuinely Casey's while remaining close enough to intervene if air, direction or strength fails. He recognizes the springboard tactic as clever adaptation rather than dismissing it simply because it is unlike merfolk swimming.
- Casey's ability to complete the entire remaining route without physical help remains unresolved and should be determined in play by her human limits, route familiarity, exertion and use of breathable refuges rather than assumed either way.
- The small living-light transplant from the hidden garden remains with them for installation at the wet/submerged margin of their home pool.
- The surface remains a genuinely unresolved subject despite the playful exchange: Vaeren still refuses to return Casey there, and Casey's private long-term strategy still includes hoping eventually to persuade him to do so.
- Casey's bratty/playful streak is established: huffy, cheeky, teasing, expressive, prone to exaggerated indignation and pushing at people she trusts for a reaction. This complements rather than erases her people-person instincts, dislike of serious confrontation, difficulty asserting herself under forceful pressure, fear responses, or genuine survival concerns.
- Casey ordinarily speaks merfolk now; dialogue is rendered in English for readability. Her ability remains Novice: ordinary conversation is workable, but fast emotional/culturally nuanced speech can produce mistakes.
- Casey remains human and cannot breathe water. Any temporary post-intimacy warmth/oxygen-conservation effect may extend her breath-holding margin but does not remove her need for air.
- Casey's private long-term strategy of learning the language and settlement, gaining trust/influence and hoping eventually to persuade Vaeren to return her remains unspoken to Vaeren unless later changed by player action.
