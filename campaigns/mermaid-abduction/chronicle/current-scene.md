# Current scene

- Scene significance: charged
- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber
- Time: some days into Casey's physical recovery after the drowning attempt; exact surface date/time unverifiable
- Casey survived a serious drowning/aspiration event after prolonged food refusal and an active suicidal crisis. She has resumed eating and sleeping, and her physical strength is gradually returning, but her mood remains withdrawn, defeated and emotionally low.
- One day Casey deliberately chooses one of her subdued, non-colourful outfits rather than the bright clothing and ornamentation she had increasingly worn during the weeks when she was becoming involved in settlement life.
- Casey then unbraids her hair and removes the ornaments Seryn had put into it. She sets the ornaments down rather than discarding them. The act is an observable withdrawal from colourful presentation and adornment, not yet proof of rejection of Seryn or every genuine memory below.
- Vaeren notices the change but initially says nothing. Casey notices his attention and asks in merfolk, rendered as English, "Do you not have anything better to do?"

He answers plainly that he does have other things to do and gives her the option of greater solitude, while making clear that his continued presence since the drowning has been motivated by concern rather than an assumption that the rupture is repaired.

- Casey's physical stabilization must not be narrated as emotional recovery, forgiveness, acceptance of captivity or renewed desire for the relationship. Her recent suicidal crisis remains significant even though she is eating and has not repeated the attempt in this period.
- The question of returning Casey to the human shore remains unresolved. Vaeren previously broke from his automatic refusal but has neither promised nor refused the return since the drowning.
- Neris's earlier intention to alert someone about Casey's deterioration and whether it led Vaeren to the rescue remain unestablished.
- Vaeren's exact whereabouts during his earlier absence remain unestablished.
- Casey's relationship with Vaeren remains acutely ruptured. Some affection/intimacy/home-making were genuinely desired; her reciprocal saelith wording arose from a cross-cultural misunderstanding; she still wants freedom/the surface despite her defeated statements.
- Casey ordinarily speaks merfolk now; dialogue is rendered in English for readability.
