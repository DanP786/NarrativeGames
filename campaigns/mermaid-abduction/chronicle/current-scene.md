# Current scene

- Scene significance: charged
- Location: Seryn's air chamber within the unnamed deep merfolk settlement
- Time: after Casey has slept for several hours; exact surface time unknown
- Casey remains captive after being forcibly taken from the shore by Vaeren, who refuses to return her human home and calls her "saelith."
- Casey sits on the dry ledge in the colorful two-piece outfit Seryn selected while Seryn decorates her wet hair with beautiful marine-made ornaments.
- After learning that Daniel attempted escape early in his captivity, Casey asks what the rules are below the sea.
- Daniel explains that there is no single simple rulebook. For a human recognized as a merfolk person's saelith, practical life is governed by a mixture of settlement custom, household expectations, safety limits and the standing of the merfolk partner.
- Daniel identifies several broad norms he has learned: a saelith is publicly attached to a particular household/bond rather than treated as an unattached visitor; other merfolk are generally expected to respect that bond and not simply claim or interfere with another's saelith; the merfolk partner is expected to provide breathable refuge, food, warmth and safe passage through environments a human cannot navigate alone; and humans normally rely on merfolk assistance for travel through submerged areas.
- Daniel stresses that individual households vary considerably. Seryn gives him broad freedom within what he can physically reach safely, while Vaeren may set different boundaries for Casey. Daniel does not present every preference of a saelith partner as universal law.
- Daniel also explains that serious disputes, taboos, punishment, formal dissolution of a saelith bond and any recognized right of return are more complicated than the everyday customs he is describing and should not be assumed from his summary.
- Daniel translates the substance for Vaeren and Seryn at Vaeren's request. Vaeren therefore knows Casey is actively learning the local rules, but still does not know her private strategy.
- Seryn resumes working on Casey's hair while listening to Daniel's translation.
