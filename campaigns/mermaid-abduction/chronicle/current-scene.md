# Current scene

- Scene significance: climactic
- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber, on dry stone near the pool after Casey's drowning
- Time: shortly after Casey lost consciousness underwater following prolonged food refusal; exact elapsed time and surface date/time unverifiable
- Casey left the home chamber severely weakened after days of refusing food, swam destinationlessly into the near-home submerged passages, deliberately/passively failed to return for air, inhaled water and lost consciousness underwater.
- Survival required external intervention because Casey was unconscious and incapable of self-rescue. Casey has been returned to breathable air in the home chamber and is lying on dry stone near the pool.
- Casey regained consciousness coughing/gasping and opened her eyes to find Vaeren kneeling beside her, visibly wet. Vaeren has said that he withdrew because he believed his presence/touch had become unwanted and false, while admitting that leaving provisions and leaving Casey alone were not the same thing. He also established that he sent Neris with at least some provisions during his absence.
- Casey tried to sit up, but severe weakness, dizziness and coughing prevented normal movement. Vaeren offered physical help only with her permission.
- Casey answers, "You can help me by taking me home," using "home" in the established sense of her human home/the surface rather than the shared air chamber.
- Vaeren understands what Casey means. The request directly confronts his longstanding refusal to return her to the shore and the central contradiction of his saelith claim: he insists on keeping her alive while continuing to deny the freedom she has repeatedly asked for.
- In the immediate aftermath of Casey nearly dying, Vaeren does not answer with his old automatic refusal. He is visibly shaken and cannot plausibly treat the request as unchanged after seeing the consequences of isolation/captivity. However, he has not yet agreed to return her to the surface in this beat; his response is suspended at the decision point.
- Vaeren does not claim to know Casey's private suicidal thoughts. He can infer from her condition and circumstances that the drowning was not an ordinary accident, but the exact private path to it remains Casey's knowledge unless disclosed.
- Casey is breathing spontaneously but remains in severe immediate aftermath of drowning/aspiration. She is weak, coughing, exhausted and at risk of delayed complications. Survival does not mean she is medically unharmed.
- Casey's prior contemplation of death through starvation, desire for suffering to stop, destinationless departure, refusal to move toward air and continuation through aspiration establish an active suicidal crisis/attempt in-fiction.
- Neris's earlier intention to alert someone about Casey's deterioration remains only partly resolved: Vaeren has established that he sent Neris with provisions, but whether Neris later alerted Vaeren to Casey's worsening condition and whether that led to the rescue remain unestablished.
- Vaeren's exact whereabouts during his absence remain unestablished. His current presence does not itself resolve the rupture, restore trust, grant surface freedom or erase the coercive context.
