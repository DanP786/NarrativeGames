# Current scene

- Scene significance: climactic
- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber, on dry stone near the pool after Casey's drowning
- Time: shortly after Casey lost consciousness underwater following prolonged food refusal; exact elapsed time and surface date/time unverifiable
- Casey left the home chamber severely weakened after days of refusing food, swam destinationlessly into the near-home submerged passages, deliberately/passively failed to return for air, inhaled water and lost consciousness underwater. She survived through external intervention and has been returned to the home chamber.
- Casey regained consciousness coughing/gasping and found Vaeren kneeling beside her, visibly wet. Vaeren has said that he withdrew because he believed his presence/touch had become unwanted and false, while admitting that leaving provisions and leaving Casey alone were not the same thing. He also established that he sent Neris with at least some provisions during his absence.
- Casey asked Vaeren to take her "home," explicitly meaning her human home/the surface. Vaeren understood and, for the first time, did not answer with his longstanding automatic refusal; he has not yet agreed either.
- Casey forced herself into an unstable sitting position despite severe weakness, dizziness and coughing. She remains physically unable to stand or travel under her own power.
- Casey expressed intense guilt and said she might deserve death for what she did. Vaeren rejected death as punishment while acknowledging that her deception hurt him and that the captivity he imposed shaped her choices.
- Casey recognized what she sees as another attempt to manipulate Vaeren toward taking her home and demanded that he stop and listen. Vaeren complied and waited without completing her thought.
- Casey now says, "Leave me. I'll be a good pet and eat your food. I'll stay here… forever." The line combines self-degradation, bitterness/resignation about captivity, a request for Vaeren to leave, a promise to resume eating and an expressed surrender of hope that she will ever be allowed to leave.
- Vaeren does not accept Casey's statement as meaningful reciprocal saelith consent or a freely chosen commitment to remain forever. In the immediate context—after a suicide attempt, severe guilt, physical collapse and repeated request for the surface—he recognizes it as capitulation rather than the mutual choosing he once believed he had.
- Vaeren does not accept the label "pet" as an accurate or acceptable description of Casey. He does not use her promise to eat/stay as proof the crisis is resolved, nor does he demand that she repeat or formalize it.
- Because Casey has just survived a suicide attempt, is profoundly weak and is medically unstable after aspiration, Vaeren will not simply leave her entirely unattended at this moment. He can respect her request for distance by moving away, not touching and not pressing the relationship conversation while remaining able to respond if her breathing or consciousness worsens.
- The unresolved question of returning Casey to the surface remains separate and unanswered. Casey's declaration that she will stay forever does not settle it on Vaeren's behalf or erase his earlier hesitation.
- Casey is breathing spontaneously but remains in severe immediate aftermath of drowning/aspiration. Speech and emotion aggravate coughing/breathlessness. She is weak from prolonged inadequate food intake, exhausted and at risk of delayed complications.
- Casey's prior contemplation of death through starvation, destinationless departure, refusal to move toward air and continuation through aspiration establish an active suicidal crisis/attempt in-fiction. Her promise to eat/stay is meaningful dialogue but does not by itself erase immediate risk.
- Neris's earlier intention to alert someone about Casey's deterioration remains only partly resolved: Vaeren established that he sent Neris with provisions, but whether Neris later alerted Vaeren to Casey's worsening condition and whether that led to the rescue remain unestablished.
- Vaeren's exact whereabouts during his absence remain unestablished. His current presence does not itself resolve the rupture, restore trust, grant surface freedom or erase the coercive context.
- Casey's relationship with Vaeren remains acutely ruptured. Some affection/intimacy/home-making were genuinely desired; her reciprocal saelith wording arose from a cross-cultural misunderstanding; she still wants freedom/the surface despite her present defeated declaration.
- Casey ordinarily speaks merfolk now; dialogue is rendered in English for readability.
