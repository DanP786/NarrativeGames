# Current scene

- Scene significance: climactic
- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber
- Time: later in Casey's gradual physical recovery after the drowning attempt; exact surface date/time unverifiable
- Casey survived a serious drowning/aspiration event after prolonged food refusal and an active suicidal crisis. She has resumed eating and sleeping, and her physical strength is gradually returning, but her mood remains fragile and the larger crisis unresolved.
- Earlier in this scene Seryn comforted Casey, heard her confess the false-pregnancy cover and admit genuine care for Vaeren, made her laugh for the first time in weeks, and arranged her hair with the ornaments Casey had previously removed. Seryn then left Casey and Vaeren alone.
- Casey privately recognizes that she loves Vaeren, but has NOT yet said this aloud; neither Vaeren nor Seryn knows it.
- Vaeren explicitly promised: "When you are strong enough to survive the journey, I will take you back to the shore." Return became something Vaeren agreed to enable rather than prevent, restoring a genuine choice Casey had not previously possessed.
- Vaeren made clear that he still wants Casey, their household and the relationship/life they were building, but distinguishes wanting her to stay from making her stay. He recognizes that if he withholds the shore, neither of them can know whether Casey ever truly chooses him.
- Casey feared returning might mean never seeing Vaeren again and showed genuine hurt/jealousy at the idea he might take another saelith. Vaeren said he does not want a replacement or "a" saelith; he wants Casey, while reiterating that wanting her does not grant him the right to keep her.
- Casey asked why Vaeren wants her. Vaeren answered personally: her laughter/social warmth, resistance, arguments, curiosity, care for others/his family, humor, household-making and the ways she changed him. He admitted physical desire but said Casey is not interchangeable: "I cannot find another you."
- Casey then challenged Vaeren to court/woo her. She deliberately drew him close and asked what he would do if she stayed. Vaeren said he would remember she could have left, stop hiding ways out or making friendships depend on permission, learn the difference between protection and possession, and try to make her want to choose him while preserving the shore if he failed.
- Casey answered, "I'd like to see you try." Their flirtation became consensual physical intimacy, kept off-page/non-graphic. In the affectionate aftermath Casey teased him about beginning his courtship that way; Vaeren answered playfully and gently.
- Casey now looks toward the ceiling rather than directly at Vaeren and says aloud, "I want to stay." This is a major voluntary choice and the first explicit statement after Vaeren restored her right to return that Casey presently wants to remain below with him.
- Casey's statement is meaningful precisely because Vaeren has already promised the shore without conditions. It must NOT be reframed as proof that the original abduction was justified or that captivity was secretly wanted. Her present choice cannot retroactively consent to what Vaeren did.
- Vaeren should not immediately cancel, destroy or deny Casey's future access to the shore. Given the recent crisis and the magnitude of the choice, he can take her seriously while making clear that staying is not a new cage: if she later asks for the shore, the promise remains. This preserves the distinction between a freely chosen life and permanent possession.
- Casey has chosen to stay for now, but she has NOT explicitly said she loves Vaeren, has NOT necessarily settled every meaning of reciprocal saelith, and has NOT automatically forgiven every past wrong. Those remain distinct questions.
- Casey's new choice shifts the central relationship: for the first time, Vaeren has an explicit statement that Casey wants to remain when leaving is genuinely available. Future conflict can concern what freely chosen saelith/partnership looks like, trust rebuilding, autonomy, family/custom reactions, surface grief and whether Casey ever wants to visit/return to the human world.
- Casey's recent laughter/smiles and present emotional shift do NOT establish that her depression, guilt or suicidal vulnerability is fully resolved.
- Casey ordinarily speaks merfolk now; dialogue is rendered in English for readability.
