# Current scene

- Scene significance: charged
- Location: occupied air chamber within the unnamed deep merfolk settlement
- Time: after Casey has slept for several hours; exact surface time unknown
- Casey remains captive after being forcibly taken from the shore by Vaeren, who refuses to return her human home and calls her "saelith."
- Casey asks Mara what customs govern a traditional saelith taking and bond.
- Mara explains the broad customs she knows: the taker must deliberately choose rather than seize a random human; must personally bring the chosen human alive across the boundary into merfolk territory; and after the taking assumes enduring duties to provide breathable refuge, food, warmth, safety and access through an underwater world the human cannot navigate alone. Traditionally the human is incorporated into the taker's household and publicly recognized as their saelith rather than treated as ordinary property or a temporary captive.
- Mara says traditional custom also expects exclusivity/loyalty within the paired bond and treats abandonment of a saelith after taking as a serious disgrace. Intimacy and children may belong to the expected long-term shape of some bonds but are not the definition of the role; customs vary and are disputed, and Mara warns Casey not to assume every traditional expectation is an enforceable rule or that every merfolk interprets it identically.
- Mara stresses that these duties do not erase the foundational lack of human consent. She has not yet explained whether a saelith can formally refuse, dissolve the bond, return to the surface, inherit status, or what sanctions exist for violating custom.
- Vaeren remains in the pool at the ledge, observing without understanding the full English exchange.
