# Current scene

- Scene significance: charged
- Location: air chamber within the unnamed deep merfolk settlement
- Time: late-summer night
- Casey remains captive in the breathable chamber after being forcibly taken from the shore by the merman who had watched her there.
- After learning that the chamber's air is naturally replenished, Casey asks him directly, "What do you want with me?"
- He struggles with the language but understands the question's intent. He indicates Casey herself rather than an object or service, then uses a merfolk word associated with the old taking tradition and gestures between himself, Casey, and the settlement.
- His response establishes that the abduction is personal and culturally meaningful to him, not ransom or predation for food, but Casey still does not know the exact meaning of the merfolk term or what role he expects her to occupy.
- Casey remains exhausted, cold and isolated; the only usable exit is underwater.
