# Current scene

- Scene significance: climactic
- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber, on dry stone near the pool after Casey's drowning
- Time: shortly after Casey lost consciousness underwater following prolonged food refusal; exact elapsed time and surface date/time unverifiable
- Casey left the home chamber severely weakened after days of refusing food, swam destinationlessly into the near-home submerged passages, deliberately/passively failed to return for air, inhaled water and lost consciousness underwater. She survived through external intervention and has been returned to the home chamber.
- Casey regained consciousness coughing/gasping and found Vaeren kneeling beside her, visibly wet. Vaeren has said that he withdrew because he believed his presence/touch had become unwanted and false, while admitting that leaving provisions and leaving Casey alone were not the same thing. He also established that he sent Neris with at least some provisions during his absence.
- Casey asked Vaeren to take her "home," explicitly meaning her human home/the surface. Vaeren understood and, for the first time, did not answer with his longstanding automatic refusal; he has not yet agreed either.
- Casey forced herself into an unstable sitting position despite severe weakness, dizziness and coughing. Vaeren observed that she cannot cross the chamber alone in her present state and that she is nevertheless asking him to carry her all the way to the shore.
- Casey closes her eyes against the spinning chamber and answers, "I don't care." In context, she is dismissing the physical difficulty/danger and reiterating that she wants to be taken to the surface despite her condition. Do not automatically interpret the line as a new private statement about whether she cares if she lives; her earlier suicidal behavior remains separately established.
- Vaeren hears the exhaustion and determination but cannot know Casey's unspoken thoughts. Her insistence forces him to confront that keeping her alive in the settlement without resolving the question of her freedom may simply recreate the crisis.
- Vaeren has not yet answered the surface request. He remains within reach, ready to prevent an immediate physical fall, but does not treat Casey's weakness as permission for affectionate touch or as a reason to dismiss what she is asking.
- Casey is breathing spontaneously but remains in severe immediate aftermath of drowning/aspiration. She is weak from prolonged inadequate food intake, coughing, exhausted and at risk of delayed complications. Survival does not mean she is medically unharmed.
- Casey's prior contemplation of death through starvation, desire for suffering to stop, destinationless departure, refusal to move toward air and continuation through aspiration establish an active suicidal crisis/attempt in-fiction.
- Neris's earlier intention to alert someone about Casey's deterioration remains only partly resolved: Vaeren established that he sent Neris with provisions, but whether Neris later alerted Vaeren to Casey's worsening condition and whether that led to the rescue remain unestablished.
- Vaeren's exact whereabouts during his absence remain unestablished. His current presence does not itself resolve the rupture, restore trust, grant surface freedom or erase the coercive context.
- Casey's relationship with Vaeren remains acutely ruptured. Some affection/intimacy/home-making were genuinely desired; her reciprocal saelith wording arose from a cross-cultural misunderstanding; she still wants freedom/the surface.
- Casey ordinarily speaks merfolk now; dialogue is rendered in English for readability.
