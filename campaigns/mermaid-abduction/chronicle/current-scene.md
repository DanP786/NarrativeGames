# Current scene

- Scene significance: climactic
- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber
- Time: the day after Casey freely said she wants to stay; exact surface date/time unverifiable
- Casey survived a serious drowning/aspiration event after prolonged food refusal and an active suicidal crisis. She has resumed eating and sleeping and is gradually recovering physically; her recent happiness does NOT by itself establish that the preceding emotional crisis is fully resolved.
- The previous day Vaeren explicitly promised to return Casey to the shore once strong enough. With that genuine alternative available, Casey said, "I want to stay," then reinforced the sincerity of that choice by telling Vaeren, "I'm not lying about this, Vaeren." Vaeren accepted her statement while preserving her right to ask for the shore later.
- The next day Casey asks what changed Vaeren's mind after she admitted lying. Vaeren explains that the lie eventually forced him to understand why a captive who expected honesty to be answered with prevention might lie, and Casey's later deterioration/crisis forced him to confront that keeping her alive was not the same as having the right to decide where her life must be. Casey says she is glad he offered the shore because now he can believe her when she says she wants to stay; Vaeren agrees that a real alternative makes her choice credible.
- Casey asks whether Vaeren had "girlfriends" before taking her. Established: Vaeren had prior sexual experience and a small number of brief/non-saelith romantic or physical attachments with merfolk women, but never previously had/took a saelith or formed an enduring household bond comparable to what he sought with Casey. He says he did not love those prior women. Exact names, number and timing remain unestablished.
- Casey challenges how Vaeren can know he did not love them if he has never been in love. Vaeren contrasts those prior attachments with Casey: losing prior women did not rearrange his world, while Casey led him to learn another language, question lifelong beliefs and ultimately value her freedom enough to risk losing her.
- Vaeren explicitly tells Casey for the FIRST time, "I love you." Casey immediately answers, "I love you too!" This is Casey's FIRST explicit declaration of love to Vaeren. Their mutual declarations are voluntary and do not erase the coercive history or weaken Casey's right to leave later.
- Casey explains Seryn helped her recognize her love by challenging Casey's guilt-driven claim that she had lied about "everything" and asking whether specific genuine parts of her relationship with Vaeren had also been lies. Casey realized they were not.
- Casey now tells Vaeren directly: "I want you to know. I never once didn't want you to touch me. You never made me feel pressured or forced." This is Casey's retrospective account of her own experience of Vaeren's affectionate/sexual touch during their relationship: she says she consistently wanted that touch and did not subjectively experience Vaeren as pressuring or forcing her in those immediate intimate contexts.
- Casey's statement does NOT erase the broader coercive context of abduction/captivity, does NOT mean she consented to being taken or kept below, and does NOT establish blanket or irrevocable consent for future touch. It clarifies the distinction Casey herself has been making: she could genuinely desire Vaeren's affection/intimacy while simultaneously opposing captivity and wanting the option to return home.
- Vaeren can accept Casey's testimony about her own desires without using it to absolve himself of the taking. Given his rupture-era fear that Casey had been pretending physical affection, this reassurance can carry substantial emotional weight. He should not respond by claiming that therefore he did nothing wrong; the appropriate distinction is that the touch can have been wanted even while the captivity was not.
- Casey and Vaeren now both explicitly know the other loves them. Their freely chosen relationship is becoming more clearly separable from its coercive origin, but trust rebuilding, courtship, autonomy and the meaning of saelith remain ongoing.
- Vaeren's shore promise remains operative if Casey later changes her mind.
- Casey ordinarily speaks merfolk now; dialogue is rendered in English for readability.
