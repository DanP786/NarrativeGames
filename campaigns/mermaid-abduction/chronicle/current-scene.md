# Current scene

- Scene significance: charged
- Location: air chamber within the unnamed deep merfolk settlement
- Time: late-summer night
- Casey remains captive in the breathable chamber after being forcibly taken from the shore by Vaeren, who refuses to return her home and calls her "saelith."
- Casey has removed her soaked clothes beneath the dry woven covering and wrapped herself securely in it for warmth. She wrings seawater from her hair and twists it into a bun, explaining that the wet clothes will make her colder.
- Vaeren understands the connection between wetness and Casey's cold from her gestures and condition. He leaves the clothes on the dry stone rather than returning them to her or putting them back in the water.
- Casey's shivering is beginning to ease, though she remains exhausted and isolated; the only usable exit is underwater.
