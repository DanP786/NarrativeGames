# Current scene

- Scene significance: charged
- Location: occupied air chamber within the unnamed deep merfolk settlement
- Time: after Casey has slept for several hours; exact surface time unknown
- Casey remains captive after being forcibly taken from the shore by Vaeren, who refuses to return her human home and calls her "saelith."
- Casey asks Mara why her own obligations under the saelith custom are complicated.
- Mara explains that traditional merfolk generally regard the successful taking itself as creating the bond and therefore expect the human saelith to enter the taker's household, recognize the pairing publicly, keep faith with that household, and eventually participate in its shared life. More conservative interpretations assume eventual emotional/physical partnership and possibly children, while less traditional interpretations treat intimacy as something that must develop separately rather than something automatically owed.
- Mara emphasizes an important distinction between social expectation and what any particular merfolk will personally force. The culture's old framework presumes belonging after the taking, but merfolk disagree over whether a human's continuing refusal can invalidate, limit or dissolve the bond. Casey has not yet learned Vaeren's position on those disputed questions.
- Mara says she will not tell Casey that custom obligates her to sex, affection or pregnancy simply because Vaeren took her; those are precisely the areas where human consent and old merfolk assumptions collide most dangerously.
- Vaeren remains in the pool at the ledge, observing without understanding the full English exchange.
