# Current scene

- Scene significance: charged
- Location: occupied air chamber within the unnamed deep merfolk settlement
- Time: after Casey has slept for several hours; exact surface time unknown
- Casey remains captive after being forcibly taken from the shore by Vaeren, who refuses to return her human home and calls her "saelith."
- Casey asks Mara to question Vaeren directly because Casey does not know the merfolk language.
- Mara asks Vaeren which interpretation of the saelith bond he intends: whether he believes Casey's taking entitles him to physical intimacy or children regardless of Casey's wishes, and what he believes Casey's refusal means.
- Vaeren answers at length in merfolk language. Mara translates that Vaeren regards the taking as establishing Casey as his saelith and household bond and does not intend to return her to the human shore. However, he rejects the idea that the taking grants him a right to force physical intimacy or pregnancy. He expects the bond to become a genuine paired life over time rather than treating sex or children as immediately owed acts.
- Vaeren's answer does not resolve the central coercion: he still believes Casey belongs in the shared life created by the taking and does not presently accept her demand to go home. His position therefore limits some forms of coercion while preserving the foundational captivity and unequal power.
- Mara translates his answer for Casey rather than presenting it as moral exoneration.
