# Current scene

- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber

If she ultimately stays, she intends that Vaeren must earn that freely chosen decision. She has NOT told him she is considering staying and has NOT withdrawn her right to return.

It is stronger than mere politeness or appeasement, but Vaeren still cannot know Casey's private realization that she loves him or conclude that she has chosen to stay.

He does not treat Casey's enchanted look as automatic consent to touch/kiss/sex. The charged attraction is now openly mutual, while any physical escalation still requires a clear voluntary choice in the immediate context.
- Casey's love for Vaeren, jealousy, attraction, desire for the surface, new sense of agency and the reality of his abduction can all coexist. The promise of return does not erase the original captivity or predetermine whether she ultimately leaves or stays.
- Casey's recent laughter/smiles and present emotional shift do NOT establish that her depression, guilt or suicidal vulnerability is fully resolved.
