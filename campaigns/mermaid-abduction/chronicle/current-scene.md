# Current scene

- Scene significance: climactic
- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber
- Time: later in Casey's gradual physical recovery after the drowning attempt; exact surface date/time unverifiable
- Casey survived a serious drowning/aspiration event after prolonged food refusal and an active suicidal crisis. She has resumed eating and sleeping, and her physical strength is gradually returning, but her mood remains fragile and the larger crisis unresolved.
- Earlier in this scene Seryn comforted Casey, heard her confess the false-pregnancy cover and admit genuine care for Vaeren, made her laugh for the first time in weeks, and arranged her hair with the ornaments Casey had previously removed. Seryn then left Casey and Vaeren alone.
- Casey privately recognizes that she loves Vaeren, but has NOT said this aloud; neither Vaeren nor Seryn knows it.
- Vaeren explicitly promised: "When you are strong enough to survive the journey, I will take you back to the shore." Return is now something Vaeren has agreed to enable rather than prevent. Exact timing/route/preparations and what follows remain open.
- Vaeren has made clear that he still wants Casey, their household and the relationship/life they were building, but distinguishes wanting her to stay from making her stay. He recognizes that if he withholds the shore, neither of them can know whether Casey ever truly chooses him.
- Casey fears returning may mean never seeing Vaeren again and shows genuine hurt/jealousy at the idea he might take another saelith. Vaeren says he does not want a replacement or "a" saelith; he wants Casey, while reiterating that wanting her does not grant him the right to keep her.

- Casey's internal posture has changed now that return is genuinely available: she is no longer preparing to surrender simply to secure Vaeren's forgiveness. If she ultimately stays, she intends that Vaeren must earn that freely chosen decision rather than receive it through captivity, guilt or capitulation.
- Casey bites her lip and looks at Vaeren thoroughly enchanted. She then deliberately hooks her feet around him in the pool and uses them to draw him closer to the edge where she sits. This is a voluntary initiation of physical closeness and flirtation by Casey, but it is not blanket consent to further sexual contact.
- With Vaeren drawn close, Casey asks, "What will you do if I stayed?" This is the first time she directly raises staying as a possible choice after Vaeren has restored the option of return. The conditional wording matters: Casey has NOT yet chosen to stay, withdrawn her right to return, or told Vaeren she loves him.
- Vaeren can recognize the enormous significance of Casey asking what a freely chosen stay would look like. He must not seize the hypothetical as a commitment. His answer should focus on what he would do differently if she chose him freely: honor the promised choice, continue expanding her autonomy/movement/relationships, listen when she says no, stop treating saelith custom as authority over her wishes, and court/earn rather than assume her affection. He may also admit personal hopes for shared household, affection and intimacy, but not as obligations.
- Casey's deliberate closeness and flirtation make their mutual attraction unmistakable, but any kiss/touch beyond what Casey initiated still requires a clear immediate choice; Vaeren's restraint remains part of his effort to make the relationship something she can actually choose.
- The promise of return does not erase the original captivity. If Casey stays, it will matter precisely because leaving has become a real available option; she can still decide against staying.
- Casey's recent laughter/smiles and present emotional shift do NOT establish that her depression, guilt or suicidal vulnerability is fully resolved.
- Casey ordinarily speaks merfolk now; dialogue is rendered in English for readability.
