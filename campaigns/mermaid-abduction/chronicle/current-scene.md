# Current scene

- Scene significance: climactic
- Location: Casey and Vaeren's air chamber
- Time: the day after Casey freely said she wants to stay; exact surface date/time unverifiable
- Casey survived a serious drowning/aspiration event after prolonged food refusal and an active suicidal crisis. She has resumed eating and sleeping and is gradually recovering physically; her recent happiness does NOT by itself establish that the preceding emotional crisis is fully resolved.
- The previous day Vaeren explicitly promised to return Casey to the shore once strong enough. With that genuine alternative available, Casey said, "I want to stay," then reinforced the sincerity of that choice by telling Vaeren, "I'm not lying about this, Vaeren." Vaeren accepted her statement while preserving her right to ask for the shore later.
- The next day Casey asks what changed Vaeren's mind after she admitted lying. Vaeren explains that the lie eventually forced him to understand why a captive who expected honesty to be answered with prevention might lie, and Casey's later deterioration/crisis forced him to confront that keeping her alive was not the same as having the right to decide where her life must be. Casey says she is glad he offered the shore because now he can believe her when she says she wants to stay; Vaeren agrees that a real alternative makes her choice credible.
- Casey asks whether Vaeren had "girlfriends" before taking her. Established: Vaeren had prior sexual experience and a small number of brief/non-saelith romantic or physical attachments with merfolk women, but never previously had/took a saelith or formed an enduring household bond comparable to what he sought with Casey. He says he did not love those prior women. Exact names, number and timing remain unestablished.
- Casey challenges how Vaeren can know he did not love them if he has never been in love. Vaeren contrasts those prior attachments with Casey: losing prior women did not rearrange his world, while Casey led him to learn another language, question lifelong beliefs and ultimately value her freedom enough to risk losing her.
- Vaeren explicitly tells Casey for the FIRST time, "I love you." Casey immediately answers, "I love you too!" This is Casey's FIRST explicit declaration of love to Vaeren. She launches herself at him and embraces him; he catches and holds her. Their mutual declarations are voluntary and do not erase the coercive history or weaken Casey's right to leave later.
- While still close with Vaeren, Casey explains how she recognized her own feelings: "It was Seryn who helped me realise. I told her I lied to you about everything. But then she listed things asking if they were lies and they weren't."
- This disclosure tells Vaeren that Seryn played a key role in Casey separating the real parts of their relationship from the deception. It also clarifies Casey's earlier sweeping claim that she had lied "about everything" was itself an overstatement born of guilt: decorating/making the chamber a home, caring about Vaeren and his family, enjoying at least some affection/touch/intimacy, and other genuine attachments were not fabricated simply because Casey also wanted escape and lied about the pregnancy cover.
- Vaeren already knows from the rupture that Casey later clarified "not all of it was a lie" and that genuine household/family/physical-affection elements existed. He does NOT know every detail of Casey and Seryn's private conversation unless Casey tells him. He can now infer only what Casey explicitly says: Seryn questioned her blanket claim and helped her recognize genuine feelings.
- Vaeren may be touched, rueful or unsurprised that Seryn challenged Casey's self-condemnation, but should not claim Seryn told him anything about the conversation; there is no established disclosure from Seryn to Vaeren.
- Casey and Vaeren now both explicitly know the other loves them. This love is not proof the taking was justified; it is part of a relationship that became freely chosen only after Casey received a genuine option to leave.
- Vaeren's shore promise remains operative if Casey later changes her mind. Trust rebuilding, courtship and autonomy remain ongoing rather than completed.
