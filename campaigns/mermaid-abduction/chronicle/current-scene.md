Nothing in canon establishes that returning/dissolving a saelith bond automatically requires or predicts taking another human. Vaeren should answer from his own feelings rather than inventing a universal remarriage/re-taking rule.

Given his established deliberate fixation and the gravity with which he treats saelith, he is unlikely to regard Casey as interchangeable.
- Casey's private willingness to concede before Vaeren's promise remains unspoken and is not a commitment. Her love for Vaeren, jealousy, guilt, attraction, desire for the surface and the reality of his abduction can all coexist.
- The promise of return does not erase the original captivity or automatically resolve whether Casey will ultimately leave, stay, forgive him, maintain contact if possible, or understand herself as his saelith.
- Casey's recent laugh/smile and present emotional shock do NOT establish that her depression, guilt or suicidal vulnerability is fully resolved.
