# Varr family — established OOC canon (session 01, after T357)

The player explicitly approved the following proposed family history and ages as canon. These are GM truths, not automatically known to Poppy or other NPCs.

- **Rhydian Varr**, age **38**: Alpha of Blackthorn, Poppy's mate. Experienced warlord. Father of **Elowen**, his biological daughter from a relationship before Poppy; he has never publicly acknowledged her. Rowan's biological paternity remains uncertain (Rhydian or Daine), despite Rhydian sometimes calling him his son.
- **Poppy Cooper**, age **22**: Rhydian's mate and Blackthorn Luna; gave birth to Rowan three weeks before the current scene.
- **Lady Seraphine Varr**, age **34**: Rhydian's younger sister. Beautiful, sharp-tongued, politically astute, unimpressed by Rhydian's authority. Married into another powerful pack; periodically returns to Blackthorn. Loves her brother despite a difficult relationship. Her potential alliance with Poppy is a possibility, not a predetermined outcome.
- **Lucan Varr**, age **29**: Rhydian's youngest brother. Charming, reckless, less disciplined than Rhydian. Left Blackthorn five years ago after a bitter disagreement with Rhydian; Rhydian rarely speaks of him.
- **Elowen Varr**, age **8**: Rhydian's biological daughter with a noblewoman he had an affair with before meeting Poppy. Rhydian was approximately 30 when she was born. He knows of her and quietly provides for her but has never publicly acknowledged her. She lives with her mother in another territory. Her existence and Rhydian's connection to her are not automatically known to Poppy.
- **Rowan**, age **three weeks** at T357: Poppy's newborn son with thick black hair; biological father unresolved between Rhydian and Daine. Eye color not established. Night nurse helps between feeds; Poppy nurses him herself.

Do not casually reveal Rhydian's hidden daughter to Poppy through omniscient narration or NPC knowledge. Introduce any revelations through plausible in-world discovery.
