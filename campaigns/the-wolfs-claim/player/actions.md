# Significant deeds
- Resisted coercive public claiming by Blackthorn Alpha Rhydian Varr after village raid; later formed a complex mate bond and became Luna and werewolf.
- Protected eight children during an attack on Blackthorn's schoolroom; helped uncover clues concerning southern passage and the broken-crown conspiracy.
- Investigated Daine Gregory's possible link to the conspiracy and secured protective consideration and review of his detention, without obtaining a verdict.
- Exercised Luna's authority over winter relief: secured twenty-three cottages for displaced families, twelve grain wagons, medical reserves, prevention and case tracking; Millford Crossing transport issue remains pending.
- Survived a dangerous solitary wolf-form flight into mountains and Ashfell territory, accepted aid from Captain Hadrik, and returned to Blackthorn.
- Following the paternity revelation, returned to Rowan's nursery and held him; subsequently carried him to Daine's guarded detention door without established authorisation.
