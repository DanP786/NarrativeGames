# World
Medieval feudal territories dominated by werewolf packs, fortified castles, ancient forests and brutal territorial wars. Alphas rule like warlords; humans are vulnerable to raiding and enslavement. Some packs regard mate claims as sacred and unquestionable; laws and traditions vary. Wolf senses and strength exceed human limits. No automatic mind-reading or telepathy.
Werewolves can be born or made by a werewolf bite; ordinary bite transformation is established canon (T93 correction). Transformation may be painful and carry risks. A human can be a wolf's fated mate without becoming a werewolf.
Blackthorn Keep houses the Alpha's family, nursery, administrative offices, stables and west guest chambers. Village school serves children including many who lost parents. Details beyond established play remain to be discovered.
