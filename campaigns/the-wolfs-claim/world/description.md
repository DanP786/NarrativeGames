# World
Medieval feudal territories dominated by werewolf packs, fortified castles, ancient forests and brutal territorial wars. Alphas rule like warlords; humans are vulnerable to raiding and enslavement. Some packs regard mate claims as sacred and unquestionable; laws and traditions vary. Wolf senses and strength exceed human limits. No automatic mind-reading or telepathy.
Werewolves are born, not made through an ordinary bite. Rumours persist of ancient, dangerous rites that might alter a human, but their reality and effects are unconfirmed. A human can be a wolf's fated mate without becoming a werewolf.
Details beyond the fortress remain to be discovered through play.
