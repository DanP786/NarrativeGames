# Tone and rules
- Voice: first person, present tense (Poppy's perspective). Do not dictate Poppy's consequential choices or private thoughts.
- Style: raw, brutal, emotionally intense; naturalistic dialogue, direct visceral physical and social observation, little purple prose.
- Content: uncompromising adult dark fantasy, coercive control, captivity, violence and torture possible; do not depict sexual violence graphically. Coercion never equals consent.
- Agency: preserve meaningful choices and consequences even under captivity. No guaranteed romance or redemption.
- Death: hard death enabled.
- Mind-reading: none, including through the mate bond. NPCs only know witnessed or plausibly learned facts.
- Bond: unequal; Alpha feels overwhelming recognition, Poppy weaker sensation; refusal remains possible and is her decision, even if Alpha refuses to honour it.
- Length: open-ended, no act tracker.
