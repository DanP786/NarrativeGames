# Narrative — Blackthorn's Wolf Claim
Poppy Cooper was captured in a raid and claimed as fated mate by Rhydian Varr, Blackthorn's brutal Alpha. Their relationship has moved through coercion, resistance, difficult attachment and shared authority, without erasing its wounds. Poppy became a small black she-wolf and Luna. An older political conspiracy involving Vale, Halden, the broken-crown mark, southern crossings and the Keeper of the Third Bell remains incompletely resolved. Rhydian's estranged brother Lucan opposed his secret concession of Greyfen Valley to Ashfell; Rhydian banished him five years ago, later apologized, and has invited Lucan's family home without an answer yet. Rhydian also has an unacknowledged daughter, Elowen.

Poppy bore Rowan after months fearing Rhydian would not love a child who might be another man's. Rhydian grew attached to Rowan and publicly recognized him as son and heir, then insisted on a bloodline rite. The private, reliable result established Rowan is Daine Gregory's biological son, not Rhydian's. Only Poppy, Rhydian, Elara and Daine know. Rhydian maintained his pledge to recognize and protect Rowan; actual succession to Alpha remains contested in principle because a leader must be a capable wolf accepted by the pack. The revelation shattered Poppy's sense of family. She fled as a wolf into wilderness and Ashfell territory, was sheltered by Captain Hadrik, and returned exhausted. She avoided Rowan, took refuge in Luna's administrative work and privately confessed to healer Elara her inability to face her baby. Rhydian is now aware she associates Rowan with his hurtful earlier words and the rite, but not of every private fear she disclosed to Elara.

After a breakdown in Rhydian's arms and a night beside him, Poppy expressed numbness and despair, denied intent to kill herself and said Rowan needed his mother. Rhydian remains alarmed and wants Elara's help. Poppy suggested freeing Daine only if he renounces Rowan, then suggested execution; Rhydian has agreed to neither. Poppy went to the nursery, held Rowan for the first time since her flight, and then took him to the guarded eastern detention wing where Daine is held. Guards, unaware of the secret paternity, have not admitted her. Daine hears her voice and a baby from behind the locked door. The current dramatic question is what Poppy intends by bringing Rowan to his biological father and whether she can proceed without exposing the secret or provoking Rhydian.
