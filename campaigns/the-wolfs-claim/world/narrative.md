# Narrative — Blackthorn's Wolf Claim
Poppy Cooper was captured in a raid and claimed as fated mate by Rhydian Varr, Blackthorn's brutal Alpha. Their relationship has moved through coercion, resistance, difficult attachment and shared authority, without erasing its wounds. Poppy became a small black she-wolf and Luna. An older political conspiracy involving Vale, Halden, the broken-crown mark, southern crossings and Keeper of the Third Bell remains incompletely resolved. Rhydian's estranged brother Lucan opposed his secret concession of Greyfen Valley to Ashfell; Rhydian banished him five years ago, later apologized and invited Lucan's family home, permanent return unresolved. Rhydian also has an unacknowledged daughter, Elowen.

Poppy bore Rowan after months fearing Rhydian would not love a child who might be another man's. Rhydian grew attached to Rowan and publicly recognized him as son and heir, then insisted on a bloodline rite. The reliable private result established Rowan is Daine Gregory's biological son. Only Poppy, Rhydian, Elara and Daine know. Rhydian maintained his pledge; succession remains contingent upon capability and acceptance by pack. Poppy fled as wolf into wilderness and Ashfell territory, received shelter from Captain Hadrik and returned exhausted. She avoided Rowan, took refuge in administrative duties and confided distress to Elara; later held Rowan again and resumed looking after him. Earlier despair and coercive relationship history remain consequential.

Daine was tried and released with imprisonment counted as sentence. Poppy privately planned a second pregnancy, confirmed by Elara; Rhydian is delighted but unaware of her motivation and continuing private low mood. She reconnected privately with Daine, who knows about pregnancy, then secured paid probationary stable work for him under Rhydian's authority and provided anonymous necessities. Daine has never held Rowan; potential future contact depends on Rhydian's permission and boundaries. He has requested a private conversation with Poppy. The competing claims of blood, love and authority remain unresolved.

Elowen and Lady Vivienne Ashcombe arrived at Blackthorn, initially mistaking Poppy for a servant. Poppy established her Luna authority while offering Elowen a place in family conditional on respectful conduct for residence. Elowen apologized, bonded with Rowan and Poppy, and wishes to learn among village children. Vivienne remains class-conscious and resistant but reluctantly permitted school attendance. Poppy continues using her teaching experience and administrative authority for vulnerable children and winter relief. At end S01, family supper underway with Elowen beside Poppy; school visit tomorrow. No formal acknowledgment of Elowen yet.
