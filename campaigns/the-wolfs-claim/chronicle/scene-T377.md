T377: Poppy commands Rhydian to apologize to estranged brother Lucan, offer Lucan and his family a home again at Blackthorn, accept accountability, and end his tyrannical rule. Rhydian initially bristles at being called tyrant but recognizes the secret Greyfen bargain and banishment of Lucan as abuses of his authority. He agrees to apologize and offer Lucan's family a place without coercing them, and to bring the Ashfell agreement before council rather than hiding it; he insists accountability is not simply obeying Poppy. He asks Poppy to come when he speaks to Lucan. No image.
