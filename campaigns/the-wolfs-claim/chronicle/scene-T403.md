T403: Poppy wakes in the mountain hollow and continues travelling in black wolf form, attempting to hunt to ease her hunger. She tracks and catches a mountain hare after a difficult pursuit, gaining a small meal despite her fatigue and sore paws. Unbeknownst to her, her route down a wooded slope crosses the unmarked border into Ashfell pack territory. She notices unfamiliar wolf scent and old territorial marks but cannot identify the pack. Two Ashfell border wolves detect the trace of an unknown she-wolf on their land and begin investigating from a distance; they have not yet confronted her. Poppy remains lost and unaware she has crossed the political boundary. No image.
