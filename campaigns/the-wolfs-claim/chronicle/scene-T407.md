T407: At the Ashfell border garrison gate, Poppy remains in black wolf form and deliberately keeps her posture low, ears back, movements still and nonthreatening. The tawny patrol wolf stays beside her. Captain Hadrik, a weathered Ashfell officer, arrives and notes her exhaustion, recent motherhood, unusual silver-blue eyes, and foreign pack scent; he orders the guards not to harm or crowd her and has water and food brought. He addresses her aloud as a shifter and asks whether she can shift to speak, but does not force her. Poppy has not yet answered, shifted, or learned what the Ashfell officers intend. No wolf speech. No image.
