# Session 01 — Blackthorn, Poppy's claim and Rowan's paternity

## T001–T243
Poppy Cooper, captured in a Blackthorn raid, refused to submit to Alpha Rhydian Varr, who claimed her as his mate. Their difficult, coercive relationship evolved into an acknowledged mate bond; Poppy became a small black werewolf with silver-blue eyes. The Vale/Halden/broken-crown conspiracy, southern crossings and Keeper of the Third Bell remain unresolved; exact guilt of prisoners unproven. Daine Gregory, Poppy's former lover, supplied testimony implicating Halden and toll clerk Tomas Rell and was detained pending judgment.

## T244–T471
Poppy became Luna and bore Rowan after a pregnancy shadowed by Rhydian's declaration that he might not love another man's child. Rhydian acknowledged Rowan publicly as son and heir. Rhydian's estranged brother Lucan was banished five years ago after opposing Rhydian's secret promise of Greyfen Valley to Ashfell; Rhydian apologized and invited Lucan, Mara and Nessa to live at Blackthorn; acceptance not established. Rhydian disclosed his unacknowledged biological daughter Elowen, whose paternity he verified years ago. Poppy administered winter welfare: twenty-three cottages allocated (two need chimney repair), twelve grain wagons authorized, infirmary prevention and tracking requested. Millford Crossing bridge damage remains unresolved.

A reliable private bloodline rite established Rowan is Daine Gregory's biological son, not Rhydian's. Only Poppy, Rhydian, Elara and Daine know. Rhydian pledged to remain Rowan's father; actual succession remains uncertain. Poppy removed her collar, fled in wolf form through mountains into Ashfell territory, received shelter and discreet escort from Captain Hadrik and returned. She avoided Rowan, immersed herself in Luna duties and privately confessed to Elara her inability to face him. After breaking down in Rhydian's arms, she denied suicidal intent: 'I won’t kill myself. Rowan needs a mother.' She suggested release conditional on Daine renouncing Rowan, then execution; neither was agreed at that time. She returned to nursery, held Rowan and carried him to guarded detention wing, where guards blocked access; Daine heard her and baby. Earlier detailed scene ledgers T244–471 remain historical reference.

## T472–T581 — New pregnancy and Daine's return
Daine was tried and released, prior imprisonment counted as full sentence (player-established canon). Rowan is now six months old, can sit with support, has dark curls, babbles Mama and loves wooden wolf toy. Poppy cares for him again, though private low mood persists. Elara confirmed new pregnancy, deliberately planned by Poppy to stabilize marriage; Rhydian is delighted but unaware of her motivation. Poppy met Daine privately T520–526; he had struggled to find employment. They recalled their intended ordinary life together, cottage with crooked apple tree, and Poppy told him of pregnancy. Rhydian does not know Daine knows. Daine retains Rowan's worn knitted bootie secretly given by Poppy; has never held Rowan. Poppy persuaded Rhydian to offer Daine probationary paid stable work after interview with Corwin and stablemaster T549–550. No oath or permission for Rowan contact yet; Rhydian wants boundaries respected. Poppy anonymously provided clothing, boots, food and three library books (The Wanderer's Hearth, northern folktales, The King's Road); Daine received them and inferred donor. He lives in a room above far end of stables. Rhydian unaware of gifts. After affectionate evening with Rhydian, Poppy visited stables in all-black winter riding gear; Daine discreetly advised against riding on icy trails given pregnancy. Poppy agreed and fed chestnut mare. Daine requested private talk later; not arranged yet.

## T582–T618 — Elowen and Vivienne
Lady Vivienne Ashcombe and daughter Elowen arrived unexpectedly, mistook uncollared Poppy for servant or groom's sweetheart; Elowen joked she was 'Father’s little household favourites.' Poppy arranged genuine hospitality in distant west guest chambers without prioritizing guests over staff's other duties. Rhydian was amused by her approach and angered by insult. At luncheon they formally presented Poppy as Luna and wife in striking black gown and silver necklace with ice-blue gemstone. Vivienne and Elowen were shocked. Rhydian insisted rank does not determine courtesy. Poppy set boundary: 'You will always have a place in this family Elowen. But not every family member has a place at Blackthorn.' Elowen asked Poppy to teach her, began practising polite requests and thanks and met Rowan in nursery, held and played wolves with him. Rhydian thanked Poppy for giving daughter chance. Former pupil Hari arrived with message; Poppy taught him since age four and is fond of pupils, most of whose parents have died. She plans surprise school visit tomorrow, bringing Elowen to learn among peers. Elowen voluntarily embraced Poppy, apologized for morning insult and expressed desire to have her as stepmother. Poppy embraced her as her own and taught kindness and boundaries without unkindness. Corwin's message raised grain collection challenges for isolated households; Poppy proposed larger allocations where storage permits, deliveries elsewhere, elders identifying needs. Rhydian conveyed instructions to Corwin that afternoon. Couple spent afternoon together affectionately, including private consensual intimacy. At supper Vivienne objected to village school and Elowen sitting by Poppy; Poppy invited Vivienne to join next time, Rhydian defended children under his protection, Vivienne reluctantly yielded on school and seating.
