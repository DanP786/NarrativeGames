# Session 01 — Blackthorn, Poppy's claim and Rowan's paternity

## Earlier continuity (T001–T243)
Poppy Cooper, captured in a Blackthorn raid, refused to submit to Alpha Rhydian Varr, who claimed her as his mate. Their difficult, coercive relationship evolved into an acknowledged mate bond; Poppy became a small black werewolf with silver-blue eyes. The campaign's unresolved political threads include the Vale/Halden/broken-crown conspiracy, southern crossings and the Keeper of the Third Bell; the exact guilt of prisoners remains unproven. Daine Gregory, Poppy's former lover, supplied testimony implicating Halden and toll clerk Tomas Rell but remains in guarded detention pending judgment. See `chronicle/current-scene.md` for the detailed T001–T243 ledger, retained as historical reference only.

## Later developments (T244–T471)
Poppy became Luna, and Rhydian and Poppy had a son, Rowan, after a pregnancy shadowed by Rhydian's declaration that he might not love another man's child. Rhydian acknowledged Rowan publicly as son and heir. Family relationships developed with Rhydian's sister Seraphine and estranged brother Lucan, who was banished five years ago after opposing Rhydian's secret promise of Greyfen Valley to Ashfell. Rhydian apologized and invited Lucan, Mara and Nessa to live at Blackthorn; they have not accepted yet. Rhydian also disclosed his unacknowledged biological daughter Elowen, whose paternity he had verified years ago. Poppy administered winter welfare measures: twenty-three cottages allocated (two need chimney repair), twelve grain wagons authorized, infirmary prevention and tracking requested. Millford Crossing bridge damage leaves grain convoy route/repair decision pending.

After Rhydian raised Rowan's paternity, Poppy was deeply hurt; she removed the silver Luna collar and fled in wolf form. Elara performed a reliable private bloodline rite with Daine's participation: Rowan is **Daine Gregory's biological son, not Rhydian's**. Only Poppy, Rhydian, Elara and Daine know. Rhydian promised nonetheless to remain Rowan's father and protect him; succession to Alpha remains uncertain, as Rowan may not be a wolf or able to hold the mantle. Poppy wandered Blackthorn forests and mountain ridges, crossed into Ashfell territory, received shelter and a discreet escort from Captain Hadrik and Ashfell wolves, and returned to Blackthorn. She avoided Rowan and withdrew into Luna's duties. She privately confessed to Elara that she could not face the baby and feared her love had faltered; Elara offered support and confidentiality.

Rhydian and Poppy reconciled only enough for physical comfort after she broke down sobbing; he carried her to bed, removed her silver collar, and stayed beside her. The following morning they discussed Rowan's uncertain succession, Lucan's exile, Daine's imprisonment and the emotional cost of the bloodline rite. Poppy told Rhydian she could not face Rowan, that thinking of him recalled Rhydian's devastated face and earlier rejection, and said she did not know whether she could live with the knowledge. When directly asked about suicide she said, 'I won’t kill myself. Rowan needs a mother.' Rhydian remains concerned and wants Elara to see her. Poppy proposed Daine's release if he renounces any claim to Rowan, then suggested execution when Rhydian resisted; **neither release nor execution was ordered**. Rhydian does not know the full extent of Poppy's prior private conversation with Elara.

Poppy returned to Rowan's nursery for the first time since fleeing, asked to hold him and held him while he slept. She now perceives Daine's resemblance in Rowan. After the nurse left, she carried Rowan to the guarded eastern detention wing to see Daine, without first obtaining Rhydian's authorization. The guards, unaware of the paternity secret, block access and ask whether Rhydian approved. Daine hears Poppy and the baby's sound from beyond the inner door and asks whether she is there. **Current scene: Poppy stands holding Rowan at the guarded threshold; no meeting with Daine has occurred yet.**
