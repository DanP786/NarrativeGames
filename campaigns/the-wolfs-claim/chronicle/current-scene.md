# Current scene — Fortress infirmary, morning
- Location: infirmary adjoining lower fortress holding cells, morning after autumn raid.
- Scene significance: charged.
- T01–T19: Poppy captured after village raid; Rhydian Varr, Alpha of Blackthorn, recognizes her as fated mate, takes her to his chambers. She eats, bathes, and learns about his mate claim and werewolves.
- T20: Poppy enters his bed; night passes with sexual intimacy, details and her internal response not established.
- T21–T23: Morning, Garrick reports seven village survivors held prisoner, three badly wounded; Rhydian orders immediate healing and asks if Poppy wants to see them.
- T24–T26: Poppy visits infirmary with Rhydian. Older woman Martha recognizes her; a wounded male prisoner sneers. Rhydian declares Poppy his mate, promises medical treatment, and asks her who deserves mercy or harmed her.
- T27: Poppy says 'The women were kind. The men were not.' Rhydian orders the three women protected, fed, treated; four men held for investigation and treatment.
- T28: Poppy asks 'Can you free them?' Rhydian grants the three women formal freedom and safe passage after recovery, instructing Garrick to document release and provide supplies. Four men remain prisoners pending inquiry. Rhydian makes clear this concession does not extend to Poppy's own freedom; her reaction pending.
