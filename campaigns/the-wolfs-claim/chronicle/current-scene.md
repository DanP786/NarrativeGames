# Current scene — Bathing room
- Location: private bathing room adjoining Alpha's chambers, upper fortress, night after autumn raid.
- Scene significance: charged.
- T01–T09: Poppy refuses to kneel; Alpha recognizes her as mate, claims her publicly, takes her to chambers. She refuses ownership, demands food, reveals a month without a proper meal. Alpha orders broth and bread, physically seats her and watches her eat.
- T10: Poppy says she put a fork in the eye of the last man who proposed to her, continues eating, and says the Alpha killed everyone in her village anyway; she does not feel especially sorry about those deaths. Alpha admits ordering raid, asks who she was glad to see dead.
- T11: Poppy says she has no family and village men took what and whom they wanted; Alpha responds with possessive anger and asks whether any survived.
- T12: Poppy asks for a bath; Alpha orders hot water, clean clothing and an inquiry into village survivors.
- T13: Once servants leave after filling tub, Poppy steps backward, says 'thank you' and drops her dress, revealing she has no underclothes. Alpha is taken aback, turns to practical matter of bathing and privacy, but stays controlling and tells her to call if needed; Poppy has not yet entered bath. No sexual contact occurs.
