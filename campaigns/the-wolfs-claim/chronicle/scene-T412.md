T412: In human form wrapped in a blanket at Ashfell's border garrison, Poppy tells Captain Hadrik she became a wolf only about a year ago, got lost in unfamiliar land, and would be grateful for an escort to the border. Hadrik hears the explicit request and agrees in principle to arrange a safe escort to the Blackthorn boundary, while cautioning that the crossing is not nearby and she is exhausted, barefoot, and improperly clothed. He offers clothes, boots, food, and a short rest, and says he must inform his superior of the presence of Blackthorn's Luna before moving her through Ashfell patrol lines. He does not demand details of her dispute with Rhydian or learn Rowan's paternity. The escort has not yet departed; Poppy may choose how to respond. No image.
