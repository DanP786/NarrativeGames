S01 T01–T13, evening ninth day after raid: Rovan interrogated Veyra in dungeon, recognised mate by scent and skin contact; she insulted him, refused name, denied village guilt and requested release. Three missing grey-cloaked scouts expected through Alderwick six days before raid, two mounted and third leading lame chestnut mare with white hind markings and left forehoof injury. One scout horse found throat cut in ravine two miles north, blood and boot prints but no bodies. Veyra privately recalls matching mare and three strangers at forge; has not disclosed this. Rovan reports two prisoners died of wounds, two hanged after admissions and fifth questioned elsewhere. Five others remain, including Oswin who pleaded not to forget them. Rovan ordered healer Maret, who cleaned and bandaged infected ankle shackle wound. Rovan denied freedom and ordered guarded transfer to old steward's room; departed for council. Maret suspects unusual circumstance but no bond confirmation; Veyra refused name and announced intent not to stay. No meaningful relationship shift beyond initial wary disposition.
S01 T14–T16: transfer to former steward room, inner keep, barred window/outside lock, bed, hearth, water, linen shift. Kitchen worker whose brother wounded in raid asks why special; Veyra silent, privately fears exploitation. Restricted entry to healer/Alpha-authorised people. Eats stew, refuses wash to remain repulsive, sleeps undisturbed. Day 10, Maret changes dressing, redness not spread. Lady Sereva challenges guard about room use.
S01 T17–T20: Veyra provokes Sereva with claim Rovan wants sex and babies; Sereva confronts Rovan. He confronts Veyra, reveals Sereva shared his bed six years, manages household feeding nearly 200. Veyra admits provocation, calls wolves barbaric for possessive language. Rovan explains reciprocal custom, Sereva has own property/authority. Oswin lives beside northern road, asks after Veyra and resists questioning. Rovan offers visit. Morning duties include sinking gate, grazing dispute, patrols forty miles.
S01 T21–T23: Veyra suggests scouts killed lame horse and explains intermittent lameness. Rovan says ordinary practice board valuable horse at farm; blood elsewhere in ravine, rain ruined tracks. Mare sound leaving fortress, last scout report mentions left foreleg lameness and intention seek treatment. Scouts carried silver, weapons, correspondence; one two daughters, another due to marry. Asks about forge accounts. Veyra confronts him over raid and father, refuses cooperation. He acknowledges ordering raid on reports of sheltered armed men, scouts sighted and withheld information, no apology. Calls her a complication, orders Oswin brought after questioning and fed. Leaves. No disclosure of forge memory.
S01 T24–T27: Veyra eats breakfast, refuses washing. Oswin visits quarter hour, bruised and weak. Reports Tomas not eating, Anwen crying. Veyra asks truth. Oswin tells wolves four or five armed strangers asked about charcoal burners' track, collapsed bridge and upstream crossing; thought toll avoidance. Privately reveals one returned next evening and spoke to Edric at eastern gate, conversation unknown. Veyra says Edric told her nothing, always bitter about village life. Oswin promises silence unless asked; wolves not established to know. Discuss possible executions and release, Oswin offers to care for others. Visit ends; guards announce assembly and separate orders for Veyra.
S01 T28–T29: Veyra paces, ankle hurts. Maret assesses and dresses, redness not spreading. Told to assess other prisoners for travel. Five assembled downstairs. Veyra remains locked in room.
S01 T30: Veyra rests until evening, pain subsides. Firewood, candle, vegetable soup and brown bread delivered. Rovan reports five downstairs prisoners released at dawn, escorted beyond southern boundary with provisions; Tomas may remain until fit. Insufficient evidence and cost, not gift to Veyra. She excluded, not due injury.
S01 T31–T33: Veyra denies involvement, knows nothing of wolf pair customs. Rovan explicitly reveals mate recognition by scent and touch, rare and involuntary, distinct from chosen partners and love. Has not told Sereva, intends to. Veyra calls him insane, reduces detention to sexual desire; he rejects interpretation, owns choice to detain, refuses release, says cannot indefinitely keep her in room but won't trust her outside gates. No disclosure of Edric or forge memory.
S01 T34–T36: Veyra asks mate duties. Rovan explains customary household authority, formal appearances, heirs, but no inherent duties or vows; separate rooms for now. Asks name, not given. Veyra argues Sereva already fulfils needs and requests release; refused. Says she would live and can look after herself. Rovan proposes useful work after healer clearance, offers guarded farewell at dawn. Leaves for dinner with Sereva. No contact or meaningful disposition shift.
S01 T37: Veyra eats supper, washes body and hair keeping dressing dry, changes into supplied linen shift, sleeps undisturbed. Day 11 first light guard knocks, announces departure preparations and offers wool dress and cloak for outdoor farewell. Four villagers preparing to leave, Tomas remains for treatment.
S01 T38: Veyra dresses in borrowed brown wool dress, charcoal cloak, slippers. Guard escorts downstairs to gate courtyard; four villagers awaiting mounted escort, provisions ready. Oswin approaches and asks whether she is coming; departure not yet occurred. Guard within hearing. No new disclosures.
S01 T39: Veyra abruptly spooks first escort horse, loosens second girth, rider falls and saddle slips. Using adept horse handling and mounting block, mounts second horse bareback, reins in hand; ankle pain aggravated. Guard shouts alarm, gate still open but men moving to close it, escort guard rushing towards horse. Villagers not involved.
S01 T40: Veyra rides through narrowing gate successfully, guard misses bridle, cloak fastening tears and cloak lost at gate. Alarm horn sounds. Gallops downhill bareback on southern road, reaches woodland bend with branching narrow track; pursuit not yet visible, distance from fortress a few hundred yards. No supplies, weapon or saddle. Villagers' fate unknown to Veyra.
