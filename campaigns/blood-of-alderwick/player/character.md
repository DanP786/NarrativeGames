Veyra Fenwick, human woman, 22. 5'5, lean wiry, dark auburn hair, pale green eyes, freckles across shoulders, slightly crooked nose from riding accident. Farrier's daughter of Alderwick. Mother died of fever when Veyra was fourteen. Father killed in raid nine days ago; older brother Edric missing, closest friend. Practical, stubborn, protective, dry sometimes vicious humour, dislikes pity and distrusts authority, remembers insults. Temper exceeds fighting ability. Currently captive, hungry, bruised and exhausted. Wants freedom and answers about Edric.
