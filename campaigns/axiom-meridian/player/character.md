# Mara Vey

- Age: 23
- Current state: Healthy; beginning her first day as a junior employee at Axiom Meridian.
- Background: Raised in a respectable but financially precarious household in an outer metropolitan zone. Her parents broadly trust managed society. Mara mostly does too.
- Personality: Observant, conscientious, quietly ambitious, patient. Good with people without being naturally charismatic. Authority makes her nervous rather than angry. Under fear, she tends to comply long enough to understand the situation.
- Primary goal: Build a secure adult life through her position at Axiom Meridian.
- Weaknesses: Not technically gifted, physically formidable or naturally rebellious.
