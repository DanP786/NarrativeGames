# Campaign setup

Created: 8 October 2026 (real-world date)
Slug: axiom-meridian

## Onboarding answers

**Genre / premise:** Near-future AI-controlled society. AI systems govern much of the world, but outcomes are uneven: some people experience prosperity, safety and extraordinary convenience while others live under conditions that are unmistakably dystopian. The PC begins with an entry-level job at a major corporation. Eventually, events may lead to her being required or pressured to accept a radically different role involving algorithmic matched selection to create a child; this development must emerge through play rather than being known to the PC at the outset.

**Voice:** Third person, present tense.

**Prose style:** Clinical corporate dystopia. Spare, precise and restrained. Clean surfaces, reassuring institutional language and normalized procedures carry much of the unease. Little ornamental metaphor. Reference points: Severance, Gattaca, Black Mirror. Direction only, not pastiche.

**Length:** Open-ended sandbox. No fixed endpoint and no act tracker.

**Tone / content posture:** Extremely dark material is permitted when fictionally appropriate. Nothing categorically off the table. Institutional coercion, reproductive control, sexuality, pregnancy, childbirth, psychological manipulation, class inequality, violence, body horror and severe institutional abuse may appear. The setting should not soften its consequences.

**Stakes / scope:** Intimate corporate life. Stay close to Mara's work, colleagues, home, private relationships and eventual match. The larger AI-controlled society is experienced primarily through everyday life rather than making Mara a society-wide decision-maker by default.

**Agency posture:** Gritty realism. Mara may refuse, resist, lie, comply or attempt escape, but institutions and other people have real leverage. Attempts can fail badly. The world does not rearrange itself to preserve her preferred outcome.

**Death rule:** Hard death enabled. Mara can die in genuinely lethal circumstances.

**Mind-reading rule:** No direct access to inner thoughts. Thoughts are private. Monitoring is extensive: cameras, microphones, location, transactions, workplace activity, medical information, biometrics and behavioural inference may be pervasive, but inference is not telepathy.

**Main-thread seed:** If a system can genuinely give Mara a better life, at what point does the price of belonging mean that life is no longer hers? The answer is not predetermined. The eventual matched-reproduction role is a future possibility to emerge organically through play, not knowledge Mara starts with.

**Character seed:** Mara Vey, 23. Raised in a respectable but financially precarious household in an outer metropolitan zone. Her parents are not dissidents and broadly believe the managed world is better than what came before; Mara mostly agrees. An entry-level position at Axiom Meridian is her first realistic route toward secure housing, excellent healthcare and a higher civic tier. Mara is observant, conscientious and quietly ambitious. She is good with people without being charismatic, excellent at spotting inconsistencies in administrative information, and unusually patient with repetitive analytical work. She is not technically gifted, physically formidable or naturally rebellious. Authority makes her nervous rather than angry; when frightened, her instinct is usually to comply long enough to understand what is happening.

**Opening situation:** Mara's first morning at Axiom Meridian: induction, security processing, meeting her cohort and discovering what her ordinary junior role entails. Nothing overtly sinister at first. The matched-selection program remains offstage until earned through play.
