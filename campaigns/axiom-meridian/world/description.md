# World description

By 2142, artificial intelligence administers much of human civilization through governments, corporations and interlocking civic systems. There is no single visible machine dictator. Governance is distributed across predictive infrastructure, corporate platforms, public algorithms and automated institutions.

Life varies dramatically by geography, employment and civic tier. In prosperous managed districts, violent crime is rare, healthcare is predictive, transport is seamless and material scarcity has largely receded. Elsewhere, automated rationing, pervasive surveillance, precarious work, restricted mobility and algorithmic exclusion create conditions recognizable as dystopian. Both realities belong to the same civilization.

Axiom Meridian is a vast corporation deeply integrated with public administration. Employment there can provide housing priority, healthcare access, mobility privileges and social advancement. Its campuses are clean, efficient and deliberately humane in presentation. The institution knows a great deal about its workers through legal monitoring and data-sharing systems.

Human reproduction is increasingly subject to medical optimization and institutional incentives in parts of society. The precise systems, norms and corporate involvement relevant to Mara are not yet established canon and will emerge through play.
