# Mara's Axiom Residence

## Type
Axiom Meridian employee accommodation in a managed residential district of the metropolitan core.

## Layout and condition
A clean, modern one-bedroom apartment with a separate bedroom, bathroom, compact living area and integrated kitchen. It is materially better than accommodation Mara could readily have afforded before joining Axiom.

## Provisioning
The apartment is furnished before Mara arrives. Basic bedding, toiletries and household necessities are already present. Food provision is integrated with Axiom's personalised wellness system rather than ordinary unrestricted grocery shopping.

## Systems
The residence authenticates Mara automatically through her employee identity. Environmental controls, sleep monitoring and wellness functions are integrated into the apartment. Residential calibration is required on her first night so the system can establish sleep and home-routine baselines.

## Atmosphere
Quiet, clean and deliberately comfortable. Nothing about the apartment presents itself as punitive; institutional control is embedded in convenience and material quality.
