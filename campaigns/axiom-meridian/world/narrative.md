# Narrative

Mara Vey, 23, has won an entry-level position at Axiom Meridian, giving her a rare route out of financial precarity and into the comfortable tier of AI-managed society.

By May 2143, seven months after joining, Mara has found genuine stability. Her health is excellent, her work in Operations Integrity is rewarding, and she has begun forming friendships. The corporation's comprehensive support has earned considerable practical trust. Her favourable six-month development review now brings a confidential Personal Futures consultation, where the institution may begin to make more intimate claims upon her future.
