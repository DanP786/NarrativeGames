# Tone and rules

- Voice: third person, present tense.
- Prose style: clinical corporate dystopia; spare, precise, restrained, clean and socially observant. Reassuring institutional language should often carry the unease. Avoid ornamental metaphor and melodramatic signalling.
- Length: open-ended sandbox.
- Tone: may become extremely dark. Do not soften institutional power or consequences.
- Content posture: nothing categorically off the table when fictionally appropriate, including institutional coercion, reproductive control, sexuality, pregnancy, childbirth, psychological manipulation, class inequality, violence, body horror and severe institutional abuse.
- Scope: intimate. Camera stays close to Mara's work, home, colleagues, private relationships and personal consequences.
- Agency: gritty realism. Choices are real; power is unequal; failure is real and can be severe.
- Death rule: hard death enabled.
- Mind-reading: impossible. No AI, implant or person can directly access Mara's inner thoughts. Pervasive monitoring, biometrics and behavioural prediction exist and may be highly accurate, but remain inference.
