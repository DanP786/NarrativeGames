# Current scene

8 May 2143, late morning, Central Campus Development Suite 14. Mara Vey attends a confidential Personal Futures consultation after her excellent six-month review. Evelyn Cross explains Axiom's AI-governed population-stabilisation policy and Mara's priority eligibility for a Managed Family Formation Pathway. Mara cautiously says she might want children someday, then is stunned by the prospect of an algorithmically selected father. Cross details proposed matching, partner integration, possible shared housing, preference for natural conception, scheduled fertility monitoring, an initial pregnancy commitment, enhanced benefits and institutional oversight. No partner has yet been assigned, but preliminary matching is authorised. Declining triggers a benefits and classification review. Mara asks whether she can change her mind after agreeing. Cross distinguishes preliminary interest (withdrawable before contract), formal enrolment (withdrawal petition with benefit/classification consequences), and bodily consent (intercourse cannot be compelled). After enrolment, withdrawal is not guaranteed as a simple unilateral cancellation, and refusal to participate can lead to compliance review, though no forced sexual act is authorised. Mara has not agreed to anything.
