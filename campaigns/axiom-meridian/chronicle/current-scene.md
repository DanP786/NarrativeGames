# Current scene

8 November 2142, morning. After a month of consistent compliance with Axiom Meridian's work and wellness programme, Mara Vey reports to the Employee Health clinic at Central Campus for her first-month physical. Her work and residential routines have become familiar, her exercise tolerance has improved, and she continues to receive folate under preventative reproductive-health support. In a private examination room, Mara changes into the provided gown and sits on the examination couch. A human clinician, Dr Iona Senn, enters, introduces herself and begins by explaining the review and asking Mara whether she has experienced symptoms or concerns during the month.
