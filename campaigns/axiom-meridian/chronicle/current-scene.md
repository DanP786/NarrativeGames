# Current scene

8 November 2142, morning. After a month of consistent compliance with Axiom Meridian's work and wellness programme, Mara Vey reports to the Employee Health clinic at Central Campus for her first-month physical. Her work and residential routines have become familiar, her exercise tolerance has improved, and she continues to receive folate under preventative reproductive-health support. In a private examination room, Mara changes into the provided gown and sits on the examination couch. Dr Iona Senn begins the consultation. Mara reports no unusual symptoms and says she is sleeping better than she has in some time. Senn notes the improvement, confirms that the residential sleep data supports it, and asks permission to begin routine vital signs and examination before discussing the endocrine and reproductive-health review.
