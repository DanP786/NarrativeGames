# Current scene

8 May 2143, late morning, Central Campus Development Suite 14. Mara Vey attends a confidential Personal Futures consultation with Evelyn Cross after her excellent six-month review. Cross explains Axiom's demographic-stabilisation programme, including algorithmic partner selection, preferred natural conception, monitored reproductive commitment, benefits and withdrawal consequences. Mara asks whether she can change her mind; Cross explains that preliminary interest is revocable, while formal enrolment entails a petition process and potential consequences; sexual consent cannot be overridden. Mara cautiously says 'Ok' to viewing a preliminary match, without enrolling. Cross authorises a nonbinding compatibility preview. The system returns a leading candidate, Adrian Kest, 29, an Axiom infrastructure systems specialist, with favourable genetic and behavioural compatibility indicators. Cross shows Mara his basic profile and notes that the man has not been informed of Mara's identity and that neither is yet assigned. The consultation remains in progress.
