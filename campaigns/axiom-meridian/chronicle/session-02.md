# Session 02

On 8 November 2142, Mara completes her first-month physical with Dr Iona Senn. Her tests show no obvious abnormalities; Senn explains reproductive monitoring and preventive folate supplementation, and sends Mara voluntary social-programme information without enrolling her.

Six months pass. By 8 May 2143, Mara is thriving: strong workplace performance in Operations Integrity, excellent health under company-managed routines, and a modest social circle including Nessa Vale and another colleague, Talia Renn. Axiom's six-month development review has resulted in a confidential Personal Futures consultation, which Mara has not yet attended. The institution is beginning to broach longer-term family and reproductive planning, without having disclosed any specific selection programme to Mara.
