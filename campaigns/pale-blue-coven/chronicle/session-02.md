# Session 02

## Summary
Seraphina's playful campaign to recover her betrothal ring collapsed into a painful reckoning with Lucien over what trust, intimacy and chosen loyalty actually meant. Lucien came to understand that Seraphina's temptation and private deference had been her way of saying, "I still choose you," but his undefined demands that she earn trust and his mishandled attempt to return the ring had already damaged its meaning. Seraphina told him she did not know whether she wanted the ring anymore and that his asking what more she would do after she willingly gave herself to him made her feel personally insufficient, worse in that moment than those who had previously used her. Their conversation ended without reconciliation.

Before leaving, Seraphina spoke with the recovering Frederick, apologized for her role in the Bellwater separation and learned that he considers Lucien's near-fatal punishment excessive but accepts responsibility for choosing to obey Seraphina over Lucien's protective assignment. Frederick said he will not obey her next time if doing so makes him fail Lucien's duty. Seraphina told him Garran had kissed her and corrected the idea that she nearly died in Bellwater, saying she had been in control and retreated when outnumbered. She also took interest in the household garden, praised an unnamed gardener and identified lavender as her favorite.

After the final private reckoning with Lucien, Seraphina dressed for travel. She deliberately chose her original fighting daggers rather than Lucien's ivory-handled pair, left his red armour behind and walked out of Veyne Hall alone without announcing a destination. She wandered through the night and entered dense woodland near dawn, where she found a deliberately hung cloth-and-twig human effigy with an iron nail at its mouth and animal hair tied to its chest. Following the sound of chopping led her to the isolated hut of Edric, a weathered human woodsman in his early forties.

Edric offered Seraphina shelter from the approaching daylight without knowing she was a vampire. Seraphina gave him her name and voluntarily surrendered her original daggers into his keeping while she slept in his bed through the day. She told him that one man might be looking for her and would not hurt him. Before leaving at dusk, she instructed Edric to tell any searchers everything rather than put himself at risk, warning that unless the seeker went by Sir Vale he should not trust his safety. Edric returned her weapons. Seraphina secretly left her coin purse behind as payment for his kindness.

At nightfall Seraphina resumed travelling. Rather than head east toward the village Edric described, she took the westward woodland track toward larger roads. She has now reached a country road with no declared final destination. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not formally ended, but Lucien still possesses the ring, Seraphina does not know whether she wants it, and whether she will return to him and House Veyne remains unresolved.

## Unresolved threads
- Seraphina's destination and whether she will return to Veyne Hall.
- Lucien's response to discovering that Seraphina has left; no search or discovery has yet been established in play.
- The damaged meaning of the betrothal ring and whether the engagement survives on new terms.
- Lady Yseld and the Pale Court remain uninformed of Seraphina's defection as far as established; their current knowledge and plans are unknown.
- Garran remains in the Bellwater region and does not know Seraphina told Lucien she no longer loves him.
- The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside storehouse and Master Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
- The meaning and maker of the woodland effigy remain unknown.
- Edric now knows Seraphina's name and that Sir Vale is a name she associates with relative safety, but he does not know she is a vampire or the identities of her other possible searchers.
- Christian's stewardship test, Frederick's recovery and Elianor Thorne's planned turning remain unresolved at House Veyne.

## End state
Seraphina is alone at night on a larger country road west of Edric's woodland hut, dressed in her civilian black travelling clothes and ordinary black cloak and carrying her original fighting daggers. She has no coin purse, having left it with Edric. Her proper sunlight cloak remains behind with the Bellwater operation cart. She has not chosen a destination. The engagement with Lucien remains formally intact but deeply uncertain; the ring is still in Lucien's possession.
