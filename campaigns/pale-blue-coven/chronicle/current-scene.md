# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Sir Garran Vale is gone and Seraphina remains voluntarily held against Lucien Veyne. After telling Lucien that what she truly wants is to be human, Seraphina explains the sharper wound beneath that longing: "I hate that I spent 16 years creating myself a code I could live with for it to be dismissed by Garran. I hate that it bothers me." This establishes that Seraphina has consciously spent sixteen years developing a personal code governing how she lives as a vampire, and that Garran's moral scrutiny wounds her partly because she values his judgment despite resenting its power over her. Lucien hears this directly; Garran does not. Garran knows Seraphina and Lucien are lovers and that Lucien intends to build some larger enterprise with her, but he still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, the blood-oath, the prospective-queen title, or the exact nature and scale of the new coven.
