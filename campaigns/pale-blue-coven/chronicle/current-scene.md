# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane, on the dark road away from the Bellwater corpse-processing site. Seraphina has left Garran behind after he decided neither to kill nor imprison her tonight. While walking alone without looking back, she hears the same smooth, familiar address used earlier by the unidentified rival vampire: "Hello, little bunny." Before she can effectively respond, sharp pain strikes her neck and arms close around her. Seraphina loses consciousness. The attacker is strongly identifiable to Seraphina by voice and wording as the rival vampire who approached her in the Bellwater yard, offered his blood and a change of allegiance, called her "little bunny," and withdrew rather than meet Garran. His exact method of rendering her unconscious, his identity, coven, rank, destination, and ultimate purpose remain unresolved. Seraphina's two fighting daggers were sheathed on her person when she was seized; whether they remain with her after the abduction is not yet established. Her crossbow wound is cleaned, substantially healed, and bound with a clean cloth. Garran remains behind at the Bellwater site and does not witness the seizure.
