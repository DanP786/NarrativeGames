# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the burial passages beneath the corpse-processing site beyond Bellwater. Garran is occupied with the captured crossbow defender while Seraphina waits close behind him with both daggers sheathed. After Garran notices her discomfort and asks whether she needs to sit, Seraphina opens her eyes, silently shakes her head, and becomes almost deliberately still and stoic so he can continue his duty without attending to her. Garran can observe her refusal of rest and the effort to suppress visible discomfort, but he does not know the private memories stirred by the attempted sexual assault or her history of being ordered by her maker to sexually please powerful visitors. Seraphina remains because she will not leave Garran in danger, a motive he also cannot directly know. Her full feeding has restored enough strength for steady movement and accelerated the crossbow wound's healing, though accumulated injuries remain painful. Garran knows the swordsman attempted sexual violence but did not rape her. He still does not know Seraphina drank her fill from the dying swordsman, briefly fed from the bound cellar worker, or encountered an unidentified rival vampire. The wounded worker remains alive and bleeding. The bound cellar worker remains alive. The captured archer is alive, hurt, disarmed, and bound. The rival vampire has departed the immediate yard.
