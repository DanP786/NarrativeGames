# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in an old burial chamber beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina forces herself to her feet despite lingering blurred vision, pounding head pain, and the effects of strangling. She leans heavily against the stone wall for balance and takes one cautious step into the deeper passage with her second dagger in hand. The movement immediately makes her balance falter, confirming she is not fully recovered. Garran sees this and orders her to stop rather than advance alone while he remains occupied with the wounded prisoner. The low wooden obstruction remains partly across the passage around the left bend, with at least one human defender concealed near it. The escaped worker remains alive nearby with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee. The fallen lantern still burns. The tunnels remain alerted.
