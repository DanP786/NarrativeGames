# Current scene

Several nights after Seraphina's return to Bellwater, she and Lucien Veyne are using Veyne Hall as a base while searching for useful, trusted recruits for their intended new coven. Tonight Lucien has dressed Seraphina in expensive but restrained feminine clothing and jewelry for an approach to Mistress Elianor Thorne, a thirty-seven-year-old widow whom they have watched for two nights. Lucien has identified Thorne as the effective owner of three warehouses, with money, clerks, carters, keys, a strong memory for obligations, and a precarious position among Bellwater's respectable men. His intended role for Seraphina is not primarily sexual seduction but to embody a woman visibly free of mortal male ownership and thereby make freedom itself attractive to Thorne. Lucien arranges a carriage from Veyne Hall to carry himself and Seraphina into Bellwater for the meeting, allowing them to arrive as people of means rather than on foot. The exact venue and terms of their first direct approach to Thorne remain unresolved. The Pale Court has not yet been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance, and Garran does not know the circumstances of her disappearance or her allegiance to Lucien.
