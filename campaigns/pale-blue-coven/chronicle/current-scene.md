# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the burial tunnels beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. During Garran's interrogation, Seraphina's much larger captive suddenly uses his size and strength to slam her away. She is thrown across the cellar and strikes the back/side of her head hard against the stone wall. The worker bolts into the dark passage. Seraphina gets up despite immediate pounding pain and a brief stagger, then pursues him into the burial tunnels. Her vampiric speed allows her to regain ground, but the head impact disrupts her balance enough that she does not instantly catch him. Ahead, the fleeing worker shouts a warning into the tunnels, alerting whoever may be deeper below. Garran remains in the cellar with the second prisoner at the moment Seraphina enters the passage. The tunnels are old, narrow burial works with branching recesses and little light; one additional man was already known to be somewhere deeper below.
