# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane beyond the denser Bellwater streets. Seraphina moves ahead at Garran's signal and successfully reacquires the heavily laden covered cart after it passes behind neglected trees and an old boundary wall. She shadows it from cover while Garran remains farther back. The cart turns off the rough road through a narrow opening into a neglected walled yard containing a long, low stone building with no visible signboard and only a faint shielded light at the rear. The driver gives a short two-knock signal on the cart's wooden side; after a delay, a rear door opens from within. At least one person is inside the compound, but Seraphina cannot yet establish numbers or identities. The cart is now apparently at a destination or transfer point. Neither Seraphina nor Garran has been detected.
