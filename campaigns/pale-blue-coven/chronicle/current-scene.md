# Current scene

Nightfall on the 20th of Harvestwane in a narrow Bellwater lane. Seraphina, wearing fitted red leather armour and carrying ivory-handled daggers, has chosen as prey the broad drunken man who had cornered a frightened serving girl. She deliberately allows him to seize and press her against the wall so that his aggression brings his neck within easy reach. As he begins attempting to force the encounter further, Seraphina loops her arms around his neck, pulls him close, bites hard into his neck, and begins feeding. The serving girl has had an opportunity to escape the immediate confrontation. Lucien Veyne remains nearby, watching Seraphina conduct the hunt herself. Seraphina has begun drinking but has not yet established how deeply she will feed or whether she intends to leave the man alive. Garran still does not know Seraphina has returned to Bellwater.
