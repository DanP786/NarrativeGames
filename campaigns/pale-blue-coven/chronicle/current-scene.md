# Current scene

Daylight on the 20th of Harvestwane at Veyne Hall, Lucien Veyne's old family seat in wooded hills north of Bellwater. The wealthy old stone residence is heavily shuttered against daylight and serves as the beginning of Lucien's intended new coven rather than an already established Court. Lucien says nearby mortal villages believe the Veyne line dwindled years ago and that the house has belonged to him longer than living tenants remember. He acknowledges that the Pale Court could eventually threaten the holding if Lady Yseld learns its precise location and decides Seraphina is worth recovering. Seraphina is unrestrained, cleaned and dressed in a loose white linen dress; her own clothing, daggers, cloak, and possessions remain elsewhere and their exact location is unknown. Lucien has offered her a place as queen beside him, and Seraphina has deliberately shifted her allegiance toward him. After Lucien asks what their prospective coven needs first, Seraphina looks around the largely empty hall and answers, "You need trusted right hands," advising that dependable senior lieutenants should come before simply gathering numbers. Lucien's prior allegiance, full resources, actual source of his earlier information, and the identities of any prospective followers remain unresolved. Garran did not witness Seraphina's seizure and does not know where she is.
