# Current scene

In the yard of the isolated carter's holding, Seraphina stands holding up the concealed wooden token she found in the split-bell satchel and asks the traveler directly, "What is this?" The token bears the split-bell mark and three short vertical cuts. The traveler initially hesitates, but under Garran's controlled questioning admits that it is a route tally used to identify an authorized courier or load at a collection point without requiring names. He says the three cuts mean "third route" rather than a quantity of cargo. According to his account, the tally is shown at a collection point and exchanged or recognized there so the next handler knows the bearer belongs to the network. He claims he was supposed to carry the satchel and tally onward today but has not yet named the collection point. Garran treats this as a testable claim rather than established truth and immediately presses for the location. The traveler has still not identified his clerk contact, employer, Hadrik, author of the order concerning Seraphina, or the destination/purpose of living captives. The carter remains under control nearby, the frightened adult woman remains inside the closed dwelling, and Frederick stays with Seraphina under Lucien's field conditions. No violence has begun. Seraphina remains in fitted red leather armour with matched ivory-handled fighting daggers, black hooded cloak protecting her from daylight, braided/plaited ponytail, and her betrothal ring hidden against her chest.
