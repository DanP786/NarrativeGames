# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina remains inside Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard under the false story that her mistress is seeking a missing woman named Raquelle. In the cool stone holding room, she steps close enough to make a convincing inspection of the unidentified brown-haired woman's face, then says the corpse is not Raquelle. She thanks the lay burial keeper for letting her look and remarks that Saint Orra's must do good work taking in the unknown. The keeper accepts her conclusion and responds with weary institutional pride rather than obvious guilt: Saint Orra's receives people the city would otherwise leave unclaimed, and the work is unpleasant but necessary. He does not volunteer the covert rear-yard operation. During the exchange, Seraphina remains near the internal service door leading toward the rear work-yard side of the complex, but she has not opened it or learned whether the keeper himself participates in, knows of, or is deceived about the processed remains and split-bell-marked crate she found earlier. Sir Garran Vale remains elsewhere in Bellwater.
