# Current scene

In the field around the ruined croft north of the old gallows road, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale are finishing the last disordered risen dead after killing the necromancer and breaking his ritual. Asked why she was on the old gallows road, Seraphina says, with visible amusement, that perhaps she has a morbid fascination with necromancy and came to look for herself; glancing down at her ruined finery, she adds that she likes to look her best. She walks toward the last remaining zombie and puts it down with her dagger. Garran does not find her explanation convincing as a whole and continues to question her rather than accusing her outright. The necromancer's ledger remains their lead toward the unidentified 'tithe pit.'
