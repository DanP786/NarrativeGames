# Current scene

Nightfall on the 20th of Harvestwane in a narrow Bellwater lane. Seraphina, wearing fitted red leather armour and carrying ivory-handled daggers, feeds from the broad drunken man who had cornered a frightened serving girl. She drinks deeply enough that his strength gives way and he sags against her, but she stops before deliberately killing him. Seraphina licks the bite wound to encourage it to close and clot, pushes herself free of his failing weight, and leaves him alive but severely weakened against the wall. The serving girl has escaped the immediate confrontation. Seraphina then walks back toward Lucien Veyne, wiping the remaining blood from her mouth. Lucien has watched her conduct the hunt without intervening. Garran still does not know Seraphina has returned to Bellwater.
