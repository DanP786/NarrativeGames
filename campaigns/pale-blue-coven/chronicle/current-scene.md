# Current scene

After being forcibly recovered by the Pale Court near the hill-road priory, Seraphina is taken back to the Pale Court stronghold. Lady Yseld Veyr has removed her from the Hadrik investigation and assigned another Pale Court operative to continue it. Back at the Pale Court, the household absorbs Seraphina's return into routine, restores her black-and-pale-blue formal appearance, keeps her original daggers, and treats her as though she has resumed her old place despite her explicit rejection of its claim on her loyalty.

At the Pale Court ball, Yseld assigns Seraphina to Lord Othmar Rell, a wealthy mortal magnate whose river toll rights, grain warehouses, and wagon contracts make him commercially useful. Seraphina resumes the pleasant, gracious social persona expected of her and spends the opening of the evening accompanying him.

Lucien Veyne appears in a quieter gallery and calls Seraphina "little bunny." When Othmar asks whether they are acquainted, Lucien answers publicly, "She is my betrothed." Seraphina immediately realizes the danger of the disclosure, dismisses Othmar, and urgently tells Lucien, "Take me away from here. Now." Warning bells sound as they attempt to leave.

Seraphina and Lucien flee through Pale Court passages. Sven, a familiar Pale Court coven-mate, blocks them with another vampire. After Seraphina insists she wants to leave and pleads, "You know what she makes me do," Sven understands the reference to Yseld's treatment and gives them a narrow opening, restraining his companion rather than forcing Seraphina to remain. Yseld's voice is heard behind them calling Seraphina's name as the escape reaches the exterior.

Lucien lifts Seraphina onto one of two prepared horses and mounts the same animal behind her rather than lose time taking the second. They flee together. Seraphina directs Lucien onto a darker road through country she knows, then upstream from a broken bridge to a shallower river crossing. They successfully force the horse across and continue on the opposite bank while pursuit reaches the river behind them.

Mounted closely together after the crossing, Lucien admits that he had believed Seraphina left him and willingly returned to the Pale Court. Seraphina answers plainly, "My home is with you. I know that now." Lucien receives it seriously, tells her he is taking her home, and does not use the declaration to force resolution of the ring or their remaining trust wounds.

Seraphina deliberately leans fully back against Lucien, rests her head against him, reaches one hand behind his neck, closes her eyes, and says, "I missed you." Lucien says he missed her too and admits that when he could not find her he feared her disappearance was the answer she had chosen to give him. Seraphina then asks, "Would you believe me if I told you I got lost? I didn't know how to return." Lucien believes the admission in the context of her conduct: she had left Veyne Hall without a destination, was later forcibly recovered by the Pale Court, urgently chose to flee with him, and has now explicitly said her home is with him. He distinguishes being unable to find the road back from deliberately choosing not to return.

They have not yet safely broken pursuit, though distance and the river have bought time. Pale Court riders remain somewhere behind them. Seraphina is unarmed; her original daggers remain in Pale Court possession. Lucien's exact original entry method and any additional preparations or companions remain unestablished. One prepared horse was left behind and taken by the pursuers.

Yseld has been close enough to the escape route for Seraphina and Lucien to hear her call Seraphina's name, but it is still not established that Yseld personally heard Lucien's betrothal declaration, received Othmar's report, or clearly identified Lucien as the unknown vampire Seraphina protected during interrogation. Lord Othmar knows Lucien directly claims Seraphina as his betrothed. Lucien knows Seraphina's former maker used her body for political and social sexual service but does not yet have established knowledge that Yseld assigned Seraphina to Othmar for the evening.

The unresolved rupture remains: Seraphina has explicitly told Yseld that her loyalty ended when Yseld began giving her sexually to useful men, while Yseld continues to regard those assignments as legitimate coven duty. Seraphina has now explicitly said her home is with Lucien, that she missed him, and that she became lost and did not know how to return after leaving Veyne Hall. The engagement's symbolic status remains unresolved: Lucien still possesses the ring and Seraphina previously said she did not know whether she wanted it returned. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead, and Hadrik network remain unresolved.
