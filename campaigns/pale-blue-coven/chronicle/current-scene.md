# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has identified Seraphina as a vampire and holds his sword lowered but still in hand while she remains backed against the wall. He knows she followed him for roughly a year before their direct meeting, says she wanted only to know he was safe, and says she feeds mostly on drunks and men who are rough with women. When Garran asks whether the people she feeds upon live, Seraphina answers barely above a whisper, 'Yes.' Garran now knows she claims to deliberately leave her feeding victims alive. This claim fits her demonstrated restraint at the tithe pit but remains unverified beyond her word. He still does not know that she fed from a warehouse laborer earlier today and left him alive but profoundly weakened. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
