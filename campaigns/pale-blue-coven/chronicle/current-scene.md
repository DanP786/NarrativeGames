# Current scene

Late in the working day on the 19th of Harvestwane, Sir Garran Vale finds Seraphina at a Bellwater jeweller's stall after several hours of rest. She sets down the pearlescent jewellery box and walks far enough from the merchant before giving him her report: nothing suspicious occurred during his sleep, but she travelled every route she could find to Saint Orra's while circulating through the district. Her repeated approaches from different directions have given her a practical map of the chapel's surrounding streets, the rear service lane, changing pedestrian traffic, and several useful after-dark observation positions without establishing an obvious fixed surveillance pattern. Garran accepts the work as useful preparation for the night watch. The legitimate working day is now winding down, and they are ready to move toward one of the concealed sightlines Seraphina identified before the district empties completely. Garran does not know Seraphina fed on a warehouse laborer while he slept.
