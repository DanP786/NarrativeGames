# Current scene

Seraphina has escaped a forced return to the Pale Court with Lucien Veyne. At the Pale Court ball, Lucien publicly called her his betrothed; Seraphina asked him to take her away immediately. Sven let them pass after Seraphina pleaded that he knew what Yseld made her do. During the escape Seraphina told Lucien, "My home is with you. I know that now," explained Yseld's coercive assignment of her to Lord Othmar Rell, and explicitly asked Lucien to return her betrothal ring when they reached home. Lucien promised to do so without another test. They later sheltered through daylight at a roadside inn, reconciled physically by mutual choice, and resumed the road after sunset.

Seraphina and Lucien reach Veyne Hall without another established Pale Court interception. Seraphina is unarmed and still wearing her rumpled, travel-stained black-and-pale-blue Pale Court ball gown.

In the entrance hall they find Sir Garran Vale dozing on a bench after an evidently prolonged wait. Garran has knowingly crossed Lucien's explicit prohibition against entering Veyne property or seeking Seraphina. When Lucien demands an explanation, Garran says he came to make certain Seraphina was safe and acknowledges that he remembers Lucien's threat but came anyway. He has not yet explained what specifically caused him to fear for her safety, how he knew she was absent, or how long he has waited.

Seraphina initially steps close to Lucien and places a hand on his arm while visually making room for Garran to leave. An unfamiliar beautiful woman with long, straight black hair then enters. The woman, Mara Voss, is a new addition to Veyne Hall whom Seraphina has never met. Mara has a low, sultry voice, carries herself with household authority, places a hand familiarly on Lucien's other arm, and firmly tells Garran that Lucien already barred him from the house and that he must leave.

Seraphina removes her hand from Lucien's arm noticeably quickly after Mara takes the other. She gives Garran a small smile, then quietly tells Lucien, "I'm going to change." Before Lucien can answer fully, Mara speaks more loudly and announces that she has already had a bath drawn for Seraphina so she can fix her hair and has laid out clothes for her. Mara gives Seraphina a visibly judgmental appraisal while saying it, directing particular attention toward Seraphina's travel-disordered hair and rumpled Pale Court gown. The intervention lands as presumptuous rather than merely helpful: Mara is arranging Seraphina's private rooms and appearance despite this being their first meeting.

Lucien notices Seraphina's quick withdrawal and Mara's tone. He has not yet explained Mara's role or relationship to the household, and Mara's exact nature, rank, origin, duties, history with Lucien, and circumstances of joining Veyne Hall remain unestablished to Seraphina. Mara clearly believes she has standing to organize household matters and speak with authority, but the source and limits of that authority have not yet been revealed.

Garran does not know Seraphina's recent forced return to the Pale Court, escape with Lucien, renewed engagement choice, or declaration that Veyne Hall is home unless told now.
