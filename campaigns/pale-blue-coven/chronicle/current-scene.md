# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has drawn his sword after concluding Seraphina is a vampire. With fear and revulsion overtaking the warmth that had developed between them, he touches/checks the side of his own neck and reinterprets Seraphina's repeated concern that he eat, clean himself, and sleep as possible preparation of prey: keeping him healthy, rested, and fit to drink from. He accuses her of preparing to feed on him. Seraphina shakes her head and says, 'No,' without reaching for her daggers or breaking eye contact. The accusation strikes directly at the secret reason she has cared for him: she has loved him from afar and feared that discovery of her nature would make him see her only as a monster. Garran cannot know her private heartbreak or love unless she tells him; outwardly he sees only her denial, retreat, and refusal to attack. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
