# Current scene

Inside the ruined croft north of the old gallows road, the necromancer is mortally wounded but still alive after Seraphina drove Sir Garran Vale's boot dagger beneath his ribs. The raising ritual has faltered and the remaining dead outside have lost much of their coordination, though danger has not conclusively passed. Garran demands Seraphina's identity; she deflects, telling him he may wish to question the necromancer before the man dies, then moves the wounded necromancer's hair aside and forces his attention up toward Garran. Garran accepts the immediate priority and begins questioning the necromancer, but remains suspicious of Seraphina and intends to return to her identity and inexplicable knowledge of his concealed weapon.
