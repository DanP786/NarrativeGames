# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, while Sir Garran Vale sleeps elsewhere in Bellwater before the expected evening/night surveillance of Saint Orra's rear gate, Seraphina feeds in a narrow working-quarter alley. She launches onto the slight sober warehouse laborer she selected, bites his neck, and his resistance collapses almost immediately. Seraphina drinks deeply but deliberately stops short of killing him, taking as much blood as she judges she can without crossing that line. The man survives but is left unconscious or nearly so and profoundly weakened, likely to be ill and unsteady when he recovers. Seraphina's hunger is substantially relieved for the coming night. Garran does not know she has fed or attacked the laborer.
