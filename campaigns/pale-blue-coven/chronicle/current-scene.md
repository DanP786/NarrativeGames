# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina sits with Sir Garran Vale outside a modest Bellwater cookshop near the east-bank market lanes and gives him a full report of her independent investigation. She tells him Saint Orra's is a legitimate burial chapel and paupers' yard for unclaimed dead; a beggar described a covert signal in which a handbell rings three times after public burial work, followed by covered carts using the rear lane. The last such signal was the previous night, when a heavily laden cart arrived and departed riding higher. Seraphina explains that she entered Saint Orra's under the pretext of searching for a missing servant, secretly inspected the rear service outbuilding while the burial keeper checked records, and found processed human remains, bones and dismembered parts with deliberate tool marks, plus a transport crate bearing the same vertically split-bell emblem as the tannery tally slip. She also reports that the public side of Saint Orra's appears genuinely functional and that the keeper showed her an intact unidentified woman from the legitimate intake records, so she cannot yet say whether ordinary staff are complicit or whether the covert operation is concealed inside legitimate burial work. Garran now knows all of this. He treats the matching split-bell mark and previous night's delivery as strong evidence of a direct connection, but recognizes that moving openly against Saint Orra's now could warn whoever operates the covert traffic and endanger innocent burial staff. The immediate investigative choice is whether to watch the rear gate for the next third-bell movement, investigate the marked crate/transport chain more deeply, or seek records/authority identifying who has access to Saint Orra's rear operation.
