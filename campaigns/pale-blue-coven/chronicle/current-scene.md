# Current scene

Seraphina returns to the Pale Court in deep night after another long absence spent secretly following the mortal knight. Her maker, Lady Yseld Veyr, waits in Seraphina's chamber and confronts her about the repeated excursions. Seraphina truthfully explains that a human is attempting to reanimate a horde of zombies and that she has found it amusing to watch, while concealing that the knight she loves is hunting the necromancer. Yseld accepts the factual core but suspects Seraphina has omitted something and asks whether the necromancer knows she is watching him.
