# Current scene

Seraphina has escaped a forced return to the Pale Court with Lucien Veyne. At the Pale Court ball, Lucien publicly called her his betrothed; Seraphina asked him to take her away immediately. During the escape Seraphina told Lucien, "My home is with you. I know that now," explained Yseld's coercive assignment of her to Lord Othmar Rell, and explicitly asked Lucien to return her betrothal ring when they reached home. Lucien promised to do so without another test. They later sheltered through daylight at a roadside inn, reconciled physically by mutual choice, and resumed the road after sunset.

Seraphina and Lucien have returned to Veyne Hall. Garran Vale was waiting despite Lucien's prohibition, saying he came to ensure Seraphina was safe. Seraphina also met Mara Voss, a striking black-haired mortal household addition who displayed confidence, household authority and physical familiarity with Lucien. Mara ordered Garran out and presumptuously arranged Seraphina's bath and clothing. Seraphina rejected Mara's chosen garments, bathed, dressed in her own white gown, repaired her hair and deliberately applied makeup because Mara made her feel threatened and competitive.

Seraphina questioned Christian about what changed during her absence. He confirmed Lucien remained preoccupied with searching for her, Mara arrived during that absence and quickly began organizing household routines, and Mara has entered Seraphina's rooms without living in them.

Seraphina waits for Lucien in his office, blocks his path to his desk, and demands her ring. Lucien honors his promise without testing her and restores the gem-encrusted betrothal ring to her finger.

Seraphina interrogates Lucien about Mara. Lucien states Mara has not shared his bed, he has not taken her as a lover, and nobody warmed his bed while Seraphina was gone. Asked why Mara touched him, Lucien explains that Mara is confident, useful and presumptuous, and that he allowed more familiarity than usual while his attention was consumed by finding Seraphina. He acknowledges that he should have considered how Mara's behavior would look when Seraphina returned. When Lucien calls Mara beautiful, Seraphina catches the adjective; Lucien distinguishes noticing beauty from desire and reminds Seraphina that she is the woman he searched for, entered the Pale Court for and intends to marry.

Seraphina pouts and demands Mara be gone. Lucien asks her first to hear why Mara is present. Seraphina agrees reluctantly. Lucien explains that Mara Voss is a mortal gentlewoman from a minor but well-connected merchant-gentry family whom he knew socially before Seraphina joined him, though never as a lover. Mara has experience managing a substantial household. During Seraphina's disappearance, Veyne Hall became increasingly disordered because Lucien repeatedly left to search and Christian was not ready for full stewardship, so Lucien called Mara in temporarily to manage servants, stores, suppliers, correspondence, schedules and guests. Mara's intended authority never included Seraphina's private chambers, wardrobe or standing.

Seraphina immediately objects: "Those are tasks for the lady of the house." The objection reframes her jealousy as a claim of rank and responsibility within the household she chose as home. Lucien agrees with the principle rather than treating Seraphina as ornamental. He tells her Mara was filling an absence, not replacing her, and that if Seraphina intends to take the household duties herself now that she has returned, Lucien will recognize them as hers. He cautions that the work is substantial and expects Seraphina to exercise actual authority rather than merely hold a title. Mara can either work beneath boundaries Seraphina sets during a transition or leave if Seraphina truly has no use for her.

Mara's nature is established as mortal. Her current role is temporary household administrator/organizer rather than lover, rival consort or superior to Seraphina. She and Lucien had a pre-existing social acquaintance but no sexual relationship. Mara's private ambitions, feelings toward Lucien, and whether she intends her role to remain temporary remain unestablished.

What occurred with Mara and Garran after Seraphina left the entrance hall remains unestablished. Garran's deeper reason for concern, how he knew Seraphina was absent, and whether he has now left Veyne Hall remain unestablished.

Seraphina's original daggers remain confiscated by the Pale Court. Her gifted ivory-handled daggers and fitted red leather armour remain available at Veyne Hall. Her betrothal ring is restored to her hand.
