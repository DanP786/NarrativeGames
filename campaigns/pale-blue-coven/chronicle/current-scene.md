# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne face Sir Garran Vale. Lucien holds Seraphina against his chest while Garran, now aware Lucien is another vampire, remains armed but has not drawn. Garran says he searched for Seraphina because she disappeared after he let her leave, he did not know whether she reached safety, their matter was unfinished, and he did not want to find her dead. Seraphina scowls, unable to reconcile those statements with Garran's oath and their last encounter, and presses him to finish the matter now: "You do not want me dead… So what do you want?" Garran is forced to confront the unresolved substance of his judgment rather than merely explaining his search. His established concerns remain whether Seraphina's restraint around feeding can be relied upon, what happened when she fed from the dead swordsman, and ultimately what his oath requires regarding her. Garran knows Lucien's name and vampire nature but still does not know Lucien abducted Seraphina, that she later willingly chose him and completed a blood-oath, or that they are building a prospective coven.
