# Current scene

Several days pass at Veyne Hall after Seraphina's playful withholding campaign turns sour in Lucien's office. She abandons the striking dresses for simple linen dresses or practical trousers and tunics and does not speak to Lucien. The household has spent enough time around Seraphina to recognize that her clothing often reflects her mood, so the abrupt change is noticeable even though retainers do not openly comment on their lord and lady's private dispute. From Seraphina's perspective, Lucien rejected the only way she knows how to make amends: chosen intimacy, playful deference and deliberate effort to please him. The ring dispute therefore no longer feels like a game to her.

Lucien does not force conversation or interfere with Seraphina's movement during these days. The engagement remains intact and he still possesses the gem-encrusted betrothal ring. Frederick remains at Veyne Hall recovering from his beating; Seraphina has not yet spoken to him about whether he believes she broke his trust. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
