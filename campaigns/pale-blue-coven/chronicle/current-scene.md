# Current scene

After being forcibly recovered by the Pale Court near the hill-road priory, Seraphina is taken back to the Pale Court stronghold. Lady Yseld Veyr has removed her from the Hadrik investigation and assigned another Pale Court operative to continue it. Yseld still does not know the identity of the unknown vampire Seraphina stayed with, nor that he is Lucien Veyne, that Seraphina swore allegiance to him, or that they became engaged.

Back at the Pale Court, the machinery of Seraphina's old life closes around her with almost no visible disruption. Her prolonged absence is absorbed into routine rather than treated publicly as a dramatic return. She is again housed, watched, fed, cleaned, and handled according to Pale Court expectations. Her original daggers remain in Pale Court possession rather than being returned to her. Whatever private rupture exists between Seraphina and Yseld, the household proceeds as though Seraphina has simply resumed her place.

As Yseld promised, Seraphina is prepared for a Pale Court ball. Attendants bathe away the road grime and old blood, arrange her honey-blonde hair, restore the cosmetics she traditionally uses to imitate living warmth, and dress her for formal display in Pale Court black and pale blue. At the summons to descend as the first guests arrive, Seraphina gives herself one final look in the mirror and deliberately resumes the social persona the Pale Court expects: a pleasant, gracious little lady, composed and easy to underestimate. She enters the ball without outwardly displaying the rupture beneath the performance.

During the opening reception, Yseld summons Seraphina across the hall and introduces her to Lord Othmar Rell, a wealthy mortal magnate in his early fifties whose river toll rights, grain warehouses, and wagon contracts make him commercially useful to the Pale Court. Yseld presents Seraphina as his companion for the evening and privately instructs her to keep him pleased, listen carefully, and prevent him from feeling neglected. Yseld does not explicitly order sexual contact in the ballroom, but the assignment deliberately recreates the same social machinery Seraphina has just condemned.

Seraphina responds by maintaining the pleasant social mask expected of her. She smiles and takes Othmar's offered arm without protest. Othmar leads her into the gathering and initially behaves as a confident, socially practiced guest rather than immediately testing sexual boundaries. He begins talking about the river trade and the unusually early autumn grain movement, clearly pleased to have an attentive companion and inclined to display his expertise. Seraphina remains publicly positioned as his companion for the evening.

The unresolved rupture remains beneath the restored surface: Seraphina has explicitly told Yseld that her loyalty ended when Yseld began giving her sexually to useful men, while Yseld continues to regard those assignments as legitimate coven duty. Seraphina is physically back within the Pale Court but has not retracted her rejection of its claim on her loyalty.

Far away, Veyne Hall remains unaware on-screen of Seraphina's present captivity unless information reaches it through events not yet established. Her engagement with Lucien has not formally ended; Lucien still possesses the ring, and Seraphina does not know whether she wants it returned. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead, and Hadrik network remain unresolved.
