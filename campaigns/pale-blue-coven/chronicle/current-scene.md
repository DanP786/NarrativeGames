# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has privately warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Their discussion has shifted to Seraphina's former coven role and how she came to join Lucien. Seraphina has told Garran that her former master assigned her to please whoever the master wanted, that she originally followed and fell in love with Garran because she believed he could save her, and that Lucien knows this history but does not assign her sexually to others; she says she gives herself to Lucien willingly. Seraphina has also revealed that Lucien first offered her a way out while she was injured in the Bellwater bone yard, she refused, and he later took/abducted her while she was returning home before offering again, at which point she accepted. Seraphina has stepped deliberately into Garran's personal space and challenged that he asks questions as though he cares for her. After Garran acknowledges that he does care for her but does not know what that permits him to do about it, Seraphina remains close, looks up at him, and breathes, "What is that you want to do about it?" Garran must answer from his own conflicted oath, concern, attraction and knowledge; he cannot know Seraphina's private uncertainty about consent or that she recently declared to Lucien that she no longer loves Garran. Garran still does not know Seraphina and Lucien are engaged, blood-bound, or planning a new coven with Seraphina as prospective queen. Lucien remains at the goldsmith's shop and has not been established as seeing Seraphina leave. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
