# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne unexpectedly encounter Sir Garran Vale. Garran says he had been looking for Seraphina because she disappeared after he let her leave and he wanted to know whether she reached home alive. Seraphina still fears he may ultimately kill her. Lucien identifies himself as Lucien Veyne and refuses Garran's demand that he stop standing between them, stating that Garran is armed, hunts monsters, and Seraphina believes he may kill her. When Lucien challenges Garran to explain why he searched for a woman he sent away, Seraphina clasps the back of Lucien's travel clothes and tugs at them while trying to step sideways into Garran's view. Lucien feels the movement but keeps a firm hand on her hip and shifts enough to continue concealing her behind his body, still treating Garran as an immediate potential threat rather than allowing Seraphina to expose herself to him. Garran can see Seraphina attempting to move and Lucien preventing that movement, but he does not yet know whether this is an agreed protective habit or coercion. Garran knows Lucien's name but still does not know Lucien is a vampire, that Lucien abducted Seraphina after Garran let her leave, that Seraphina subsequently chose Lucien and completed a blood-oath with him, or that they are building a prospective coven.
