# Current scene

Inside a private chamber of the Pale Court, Seraphina is reporting to Lady Yseld Veyr as ordered. Seraphina says that while out feeding she overheard a knight was hunting the necromancer; because Yseld had ordered her to investigate, she returned to the area. She reports that the necromancer had actually succeeded in animating corpses and that both the necromancer and the raised dead are now destroyed. Yseld now knows another hunter was involved but does not yet know he is Sir Garran Vale, how the necromancer and corpses died, how closely Seraphina cooperated with him, the intended number of roughly two dozen corpses, the tithe-pit corpse source, or the Bellwater tannery lead. Seraphina's ruined, blood-and-mud-stained evening clothes remain visible evidence that the investigation became physical.
