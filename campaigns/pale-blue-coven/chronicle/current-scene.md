# Current scene

Several nights after Seraphina's return to Bellwater, she and Lucien Veyne arrive by an unmarked but expensive carriage for their first direct meeting with prospective recruit Mistress Elianor Thorne. Seraphina wears expensive but restrained feminine clothing, jewelry, and a convincing living complexion rather than visible fighting gear, and she willingly follows Lucien's lead in the approach. Lucien has identified Thorne as a thirty-seven-year-old widow who effectively owns three warehouses and commands money, clerks, carters, keys, and useful knowledge of Bellwater's obligations. His intended role for Seraphina is not primarily sexual seduction but to embody a woman visibly free of mortal male ownership and let Thorne become curious about the source of that freedom. Lucien introduces himself as Lord Veyne and Seraphina as his companion, initially framing the meeting as ordinary business. Thorne receives them in a private office associated with her warehouse interests and is alert, controlled, and skeptical rather than immediately impressed. No supernatural truth or recruitment offer has yet been disclosed. The Pale Court has not yet been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance, and Garran does not know the circumstances of her disappearance or her allegiance to Lucien.
