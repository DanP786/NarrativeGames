# Current scene

After being forcibly recovered by the Pale Court near the hill-road priory, Seraphina is taken back to the Pale Court stronghold. Lady Yseld Veyr has removed her from the Hadrik investigation and assigned another Pale Court operative to continue it. Back at the Pale Court, the household absorbs Seraphina's return into routine, restores her black-and-pale-blue formal appearance, keeps her original daggers, and treats her as though she has resumed her old place despite her explicit rejection of its claim on her loyalty.

At the Pale Court ball, Yseld assigns Seraphina to Lord Othmar Rell, a wealthy mortal magnate whose river toll rights, grain warehouses, and wagon contracts make him commercially useful. Seraphina resumes the pleasant, gracious social persona expected of her and spends the opening of the evening accompanying him.

Lucien Veyne appears in a quieter gallery and calls Seraphina "little bunny." When Othmar asks whether they are acquainted, Lucien answers publicly, "She is my betrothed." Seraphina immediately realizes the danger of the disclosure, dismisses Othmar, and urgently tells Lucien, "Take me away from here. Now." Warning bells sound as they attempt to leave.

Seraphina and Lucien flee through Pale Court passages. Sven, a familiar Pale Court coven-mate, blocks them with another vampire. After Seraphina insists she wants to leave and pleads, "You know what she makes me do," Sven understands the reference to Yseld's treatment and gives them a narrow opening, restraining his companion rather than forcing Seraphina to remain. Yseld's voice is heard behind them calling Seraphina's name as the escape reaches the exterior.

Seraphina makes a desperate run across the service yard toward the horses Lucien prepared outside the Pale Court. Lucien lifts her into the saddle and, rather than lose time mounting the second horse, jumps onto the same horse behind her. They flee mounted together, abandoning the second prepared horse. Pursuit begins behind them after Pale Court personnel seize the abandoned mount.

At the first road fork Lucien asks Seraphina which way will make pursuit hardest. Seraphina directs him onto a darker, narrower road through country she knows. The route buys them time but Pale Court riders eventually find the turn. The road descends to a river where a nearby wooden bridge is partially collapsed. Asked about the water, Seraphina points upstream and says, "Go up river, it's not as deep." She removes her hand from the pommel only to point, keeping her other hand firmly around Lucien's arm and deliberately maintaining close physical contact with him. Lucien follows her direction immediately, keeping her secured against him as he takes the horse upstream toward the shallower crossing.

The escape has succeeded in getting them beyond the Pale Court walls but they have not safely broken pursuit. Pale Court riders are behind them on the same general route. Seraphina is unarmed; her original daggers remain in Pale Court possession. Lucien's exact original entry method and any additional preparations or companions remain unestablished. One of his prepared horses was left behind and has been taken by the pursuers.

Yseld has been close enough to the escape route for Seraphina and Lucien to hear her call Seraphina's name, but it is still not established that Yseld personally heard Lucien's betrothal declaration, received Othmar's report, or clearly identified Lucien as the unknown vampire Seraphina protected during interrogation. Lord Othmar knows Lucien directly claims Seraphina as his betrothed. Lucien knows Seraphina's former maker used her body for political and social sexual service but does not yet have established knowledge that Yseld assigned Seraphina to Othmar for the evening.

The unresolved rupture remains: Seraphina has explicitly told Yseld that her loyalty ended when Yseld began giving her sexually to useful men, while Yseld continues to regard those assignments as legitimate coven duty. Her engagement with Lucien has not formally ended; Lucien still possesses the ring, and Seraphina does not know whether she wants it returned. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead, and Hadrik network remain unresolved.
