# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina enters the dark interior of the old tannery on Bellwater's east bank with the conspicuous confidence of a predator rather than creeping cautiously. She wears her usual feminine, practical black leather armor beneath a hooded cloak used to keep direct sunlight off her skin and carries both fighting daggers. Sir Garran Vale has already delivered the wounded tithe-pit prisoners to lawful custody and is now established as being inside the tannery ahead of her, investigating the collection point. Seraphina's confident arrival fulfills his earlier teasing prediction that she might appear at his next destination, though her manner also reinforces his unresolved suspicion about her unusual abilities and nature. What Garran has found inside the tannery and what caused the earlier wooden crack remain to be established.
