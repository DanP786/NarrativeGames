# Current scene

Night has fallen on the day after Seraphina left Veyne Hall without announcing a destination. After wandering through the previous night and sheltering through the daylight in Edric's isolated woodland hut, she has resumed travelling in her civilian black ensemble, boots, leather harnesses and black cloak, armed with her original fighting daggers. She deliberately left her coin purse behind as payment for Edric's shelter.

Rather than taking the old road east toward the village Edric mentioned, Seraphina chooses west, following the woodland track toward the larger roads. She still has no declared final destination. The forest path gradually broadens and bears signs of more regular traffic as it approaches the road network beyond the deep woods.

Seraphina reaches the larger road and deliberately follows the busier route, accepting greater exposure in exchange for the traffic and settlements it is likely to reach. After roughly two hours she crests a low hill overlooking a walled river market town and encounters an ongoing roadside attack around a stopped coach. A wounded mortal woman carrying a leather document case breaks from the coach and runs toward Seraphina with an armed raider pursuing her.

Seraphina neither answers the woman's plea nor changes her westward course. She walks steadily toward the approaching pair with both hands resting on the hilts of her concealed daggers beneath her cloak. When the armed pursuer orders her aside, Seraphina stops directly before him and calmly says, "You are in my way." She waits for him to commit to the attack, having watched his stance and armour for openings. When he swings, she slips inside the falchion's useful arc and drives a dagger through the vulnerable gap beneath his raised arm.

The wounded raider's fresh blood sharply aggravates Seraphina's hunger. She visibly has to master the urge to feed, then grips his hair and cuts his throat instead, killing him without drinking. With her back deliberately turned toward the remaining raiders, she looks at the wounded woman and the leather document case she carries and asks, "Shouldn't you be running to town?" The woman flees toward the town.

Two surviving raiders approach cautiously from downhill, one identified by his companion as Hobb and armed with a spear. When Hobb orders Seraphina to turn around, she does so while deliberately licking Rusk's fresh blood from her hand. The small taste sharply intensifies rather than satisfies her hunger. Hobb recognizes her as a vampire; his companion Jerrick panics and runs. With town alarm bells ringing and Hobb retreating behind his spear, Seraphina advances on him until Hobb breaks and flees toward the fields.

Six mounted town guards are now approaching from the gate after speaking with the escaped woman. Unable to feed openly with them coming, Seraphina goes instead to the wounded swordsman beside the coach. He asks whether Mara escaped with the case. Seraphina tells him the case is still present but Mara took a ledger, then uses a cushion from the wrecked coach to apply hard pressure to his bleeding side. Her pressure is harsher than ideal because resisting the surrounding blood and the wounded man's pulse is consuming most of her concentration, but it helps slow the external bleeding while causing him considerable pain. The town riders arrive at the wreck with Seraphina still crouched over him.

Veyne Hall remains far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
