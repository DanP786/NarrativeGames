# Current scene

Late in the working day on the 19th of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale maintain their concealed watch over Saint Orra's rear service approach. With the legitimate working day nearly over and no covert three-bell signal or covered cart yet appearing, Garran uses the quiet to turn his attention toward Seraphina. His curiosity is sharpened by several unresolved facts: she appeared at the ruined croft specifically to help a knight she claims merely to have heard was alone, has since repeatedly protected his back and wellbeing, possesses unusual covert and combat competence, and has still said almost nothing concrete about where she comes from or why his safety mattered to her. Without claiming knowledge he does not possess, Garran asks Seraphina where she comes from and why she cared enough about the safety of a knight she supposedly did not know to enter a fight with risen corpses. Garran does not know she is a vampire, has followed him since before their first meeting, loves him, or fed on a warehouse laborer while he slept.
