# Current scene

Late at night after a painful conversation with Lucien ends without reconciliation, Seraphina dresses for travel in her civilian black ensemble, takes her original fighting daggers rather than the ivory-handled pair Lucien gave her, adds a black cloak, and leaves Veyne Hall. She does not stop to speak to anyone in the household. Beyond the estate she keeps walking along the dark country roads without choosing or paying attention to a destination, taking turns as they come rather than navigating toward any established place. Veyne Hall is now well behind her, and she is alone on unfamiliar or unregarded roads in the early-autumn night.

The engagement has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring and Lucien can no longer assume the intended marriage will proceed unchanged. Lucien still possesses the gem-encrusted betrothal ring. The fitted red leather armour and ivory-handled daggers he provided remain behind at Veyne Hall. Frederick remains injured but recovering. Christian has an expected channel for garden requests backed directly by Seraphina. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
