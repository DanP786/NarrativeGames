# Current scene

Near dawn after leaving Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, Seraphina has wandered for hours and entered dense old woodland. She found a deliberately hung cloth-and-twig human figure beside the road, its mouth marked by an iron nail and animal hair tied to its chest with red thread, then followed a repeated chopping sound away from the road to a small clearing and a rough timber hut.

The hut's occupant is a broad-shouldered human man in his early forties, weathered and plainly dressed for woodland work. Seraphina deliberately announced her approach and accepted his cautious offer of shelter as daylight approached. Inside the shuttered single-room hut, when he asks her name, she gives it simply as "Seraphina." She then unsheathes both of her original fighting daggers and offers them to him handle-first, voluntarily placing her weapons in the host's keeping while she shelters under his roof. The man's name and history remain unestablished, and he does not yet know Seraphina is a vampire.

The hut contains a hearth, table, stool, narrow bed and practical woodland tools and stores. Its closed shutters exclude the immediate dawn sufficiently for shelter. Seraphina wears her civilian black travelling ensemble and black cloak. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
