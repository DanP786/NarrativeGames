# Current scene

Several days after returning from Bellwater, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne are together privately at Veyne Hall after Seraphina explicitly accepts his marriage proposal. They are engaged to marry, while the form, timing, witnesses, rites, legal standing, and any public announcement remain undecided. Their conversation has turned to Lady Yseld Veyr and the Pale Court. Seraphina has received no direct contact from Yseld since leaving and has not informed the Pale Court of her defection, blood-oath, prospective queenship, or engagement. Asked what Yseld would do if she learned the full truth, Seraphina answers, "She would destroy us. But use others to do so." This is Seraphina's informed assessment of her maker's likely strategy, not confirmation that Yseld has already acted. Lucien now knows Seraphina expects an indirect campaign rather than an open Pale Court assault if Yseld chooses destruction.
