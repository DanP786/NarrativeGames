# Current scene

Dusk has nearly fallen after Seraphina left Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, wandered through the previous night and sheltered through the daylight in Edric's rough timber hut deep in old woodland. Edric is a broad-shouldered weathered human man in his early forties who gave Seraphina his bed and kept the shutters closed. He does not know she is a vampire.

Seraphina has dressed again in her civilian black travelling ensemble, boots, leather harnesses and black cloak, with her original fighting daggers back in her possession. She has told Edric to reveal everything if anyone comes looking for her rather than risk himself, and warned him that unless a seeker goes by "Sir Vale" he should not trust his safety. When Edric questions the wisdom of her continuing alone under those circumstances, Seraphina smiles, rests her hands on her daggers and tells him, "No one is dangerous to me." Edric has no established knowledge of what Seraphina truly is.

The last daylight is failing outside the shuttered hut and Seraphina is preparing to resume her wandering. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
