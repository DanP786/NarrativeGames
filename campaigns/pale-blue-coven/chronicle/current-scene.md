# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane inside the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. Seraphina and Garran are concealed against the rear wall of the low stone building beside the cellar-level loading hatch. Seraphina silently draws one dagger and holds it close with the blade running along her forearm, then nods to Garran that she is ready. Garran acknowledges her and begins testing the weather-darkened hatch carefully rather than forcing it immediately. The hatch is secured from within but has enough play in its old frame to reveal a narrow seam. Through it come the dry smell of bone, damp stone, and a sharper organic smell consistent with recent human remains. Muted movement continues below, while the two known workers remain somewhere inside the building. The seventh load described as fresh and whole when loaded remains unlocated. Neither Seraphina nor Garran has been detected.
