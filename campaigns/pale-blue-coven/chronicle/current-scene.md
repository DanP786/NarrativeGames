# Current scene

Several days after returning from Bellwater, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne are together in his bed at Veyne Hall after Lucien asks Seraphina to become his wife. Seraphina has challenged whether his proposal is partly driven by feeling threatened by her continuing love for Garran. Remaining physically close to Lucien, she tells him, "You know he has never had me, kissed me, the night in the alley was the first time he touched me." Lucien now knows directly from Seraphina that despite her love for Garran, there has never been a sexual or romantic physical relationship between them: Garran has never had or kissed her, and his touch during their recent Bellwater encounter was the first time he touched her. Seraphina still has not answered Lucien's marriage proposal.
