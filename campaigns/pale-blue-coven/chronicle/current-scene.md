# Current scene

At the Lantern Rooms, Seraphina hunts discreetly among the late-night revelers after Yseld orders her to investigate the necromancer. Dressed in a flowing black gown with pale-blue accents and gold makeup, she selects a man who has been drinking but is not yet drunk enough for the alcohol in his blood to badly affect her. After he buys her wine, she leads him into a dark side passage and feeds from him. Seraphina deliberately stops short of killing him, leaving him alive but deeply weakened and likely to wake the following day sick, exhausted, and uncertain about the night's events. Her immediate hunger is satisfied.
