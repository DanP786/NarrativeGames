# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has drawn Seraphina away from the goldsmith's shop where Lucien Veyne remains occupied. Garran has shown Seraphina a message that gave him enough information about a vampire matching her description to find her, making him suspect someone is deliberately trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Seraphina hands the paper back and asks, "And what have you been offered in exchange?" As Garran considers her question, his gaze passes over Seraphina and a brief flash of physical desire is visible before his discipline reasserts itself. Seraphina can observe the look but cannot know more than it reveals. Garran has not yet answered what, if anything, was offered in exchange for acting on the information. He still does not know Seraphina and Lucien are engaged, about their blood-oath or prospective queenship, or that Seraphina recently declared she no longer loves Garran. Lucien has not been established as seeing Seraphina leave with Garran. The watchers' allegiance remains unresolved.
