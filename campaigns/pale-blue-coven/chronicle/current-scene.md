# Current scene

After the failed attempt to resolve the ring dispute with Lucien, Seraphina spends the remainder of the day on duties around Veyne Hall and encounters Frederick in a quiet service passage. Still visibly recovering from Lucien's near-fatal beating, Frederick tells Seraphina that he chose to obey her order over Lucien's protective assignment, believes Lucien punished him too severely, and does not hold Seraphina responsible for Lucien's violence. He nevertheless asks that if he is assigned to protect her again she not use her authority to make him fail that duty, warning that next time he would refuse such an order. Seraphina tells Frederick that Garran kissed her and says she believes Lucien was angrier about the kiss than about either of their disobedience. When Frederick characterizes Bellwater as Seraphina having nearly died, she firmly corrects him: she says she was in complete control and retreated when outnumbered. She describes her current dispute with Lucien only as something they are "working out."

The engagement between Seraphina and Lucien remains intact. Lucien still possesses the gem-encrusted betrothal ring because Seraphina did not accept his attempted return of it. Seraphina remains free to move within Veyne Hall. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
