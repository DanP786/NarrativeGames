# Current scene

Near dawn after leaving Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, Seraphina has wandered for hours and entered dense old woodland. She found a deliberately hung cloth-and-twig human figure beside the road, its mouth marked by an iron nail and animal hair tied to its chest with red thread, then followed a repeated chopping sound away from the road to a small clearing.

A rough timber hut stands at the far side of the clearing with a lantern burning beside its closed door. Its occupant is a broad-shouldered human man in his early forties, weathered and plainly dressed for woodland work, who has been splitting kindling with a small axe. Seraphina deliberately announced her approach rather than sneaking closer. As dawn begins to pale the eastern sky, she pulls back her hood, revealing her honey-blonde hair and youthful appearance, and answers his question about whether she is lost: "I can't be lost if I have no destination." The man does not yet know what Seraphina is, and his name and history remain unestablished.

Seraphina wears her civilian black travelling ensemble and black cloak and carries her original fighting daggers. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
