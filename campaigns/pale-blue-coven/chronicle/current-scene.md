# Current scene

After a day spent on household duties, confronting Frederick about Bellwater and encouraging the planting of Veyne Hall's neglected garden, Seraphina retires early to her private rooms. After her bath, she sits at the armoire in a linen nightgown with her hair arranged into soft curls. Lucien knocks and enters only after she invites him in. He acknowledges that his attempted return of the ring earlier became another test and begins asking what Seraphina had wanted from him at each stage of their dispute. Seraphina says that after Bellwater she simply did not want him to end their engagement, which he did not. She explains that her later campaign of dresses, teasing and chosen private deference was meant to show that she still chose him. Seraphina tells Lucien that although she understands trust takes time to earn, he never told her how she could earn his trust back, so she did what she knew how to do.

Seraphina names the deeper wound: giving herself to Lucien was something she enjoyed, but when he responded by asking what more she would do, she heard that chosen gift as being judged insufficient and herself as not good enough, worse than the people who had used her before. With tears in her eyes and her arms wrapped around herself, she identifies the contradiction in Lucien's attempted return of the ring. He offered it even while saying his trust was not fully restored; because he had originally made the ring conditional on earning that trust, returning it without that condition being met makes his earlier decision and the ring's meaning feel hollow to her. After Lucien says he will not offer it again until they know what it means and that any future proposal must be a genuine question rather than another test, Seraphina turns her back on him without answering.

The engagement has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring and Lucien can no longer assume the intended marriage will proceed unchanged. Lucien still possesses the gem-encrusted betrothal ring. Seraphina remains free to move within Veyne Hall. Frederick remains injured but recovering, and he and Seraphina have now confronted the question of responsibility for Bellwater and his punishment. Christian now has an expected channel for garden requests backed directly by Seraphina. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
