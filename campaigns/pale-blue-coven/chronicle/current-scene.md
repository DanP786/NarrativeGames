# Current scene

Outside Veyne Hall, with Frederick still watching from the entrance, Garran has warned Seraphina that the Bellwater corpse operation is consolidating larger shipments, at least one involving living captives, and that a seized notation describes Seraphina and orders that she be watched rather than taken. Garran asks for her help. Seraphina agrees, saying, "I will help. Even just to find out what they want with me. But how I help… that is up to Lucien." Seraphina therefore commits herself in principle to helping investigate and, if possible, aid the living captives, while explicitly making the form of her participation subject to Lucien's decision. Garran now knows both that Seraphina wants to help and that she treats Lucien as having authority over how she may do so. He also knows Seraphina believes Lucien would kill Garran if he saw them together and would not permit her simply to leave Veyne Hall with Garran, but he does not know the full wording or circumstances of Lucien's earlier threat. Frederick is a direct witness to these statements and has not intervened or received an order. Garran has not established who wrote the notation, why the operation is interested in Seraphina, whether Master Hadrik personally issued it, the final destination of the shipments, or the intended purpose of the living captives. The exact route by which Garran located Veyne Hall and who admitted him remain unestablished. Seraphina remains Lucien's betrothed and intended queen. Christian remains under consideration for the inner circle while handling household provisions. Mistress Elianor Thorne remains mortal while arranging her affairs before Seraphina turns her. The earlier anonymous-message and Bellwater-follower questions remain unresolved and may or may not connect to this new evidence.
