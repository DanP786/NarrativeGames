# Current scene

Seraphina has escaped a forced return to the Pale Court with Lucien Veyne. At the Pale Court ball, Lucien publicly called her his betrothed; Seraphina asked him to take her away immediately. During the escape Seraphina told Lucien, "My home is with you. I know that now," explained Yseld's coercive assignment of her to Lord Othmar Rell, and explicitly asked Lucien to return her betrothal ring when they reached home. Lucien promised to do so without another test. They later sheltered through daylight at a roadside inn, reconciled physically by mutual choice, and resumed the road after sunset.

Seraphina and Lucien have returned to Veyne Hall. Garran Vale was waiting despite Lucien's prohibition, saying he came to ensure Seraphina was safe. Seraphina also met Mara Voss, a striking black-haired new household addition who displayed confidence, household authority and physical familiarity with Lucien. Mara ordered Garran out and presumptuously arranged Seraphina's bath and clothing. Seraphina rejected Mara's chosen garments, bathed, dressed in her own white gown, repaired her hair and deliberately applied makeup because Mara made her feel threatened and competitive.

Seraphina questioned Christian about what changed during her absence. He confirmed Lucien remained preoccupied with searching for her, Mara arrived during that absence and quickly began organizing household routines, and Mara has entered Seraphina's rooms without living in them.

Seraphina waits for Lucien in his office, blocks his path to his desk, and demands her ring. Lucien honors his promise without testing her and restores the gem-encrusted betrothal ring to her finger.

Seraphina interrogates Lucien about Mara. Lucien states Mara has not shared his bed, he has not taken her as a lover, and nobody warmed his bed while Seraphina was gone. Asked why Mara touched him, Lucien explains that Mara is confident, useful and presumptuous, and that he allowed more familiarity than usual while his attention was consumed by finding Seraphina. He acknowledges that he should have considered how Mara's behavior would look when Seraphina returned. When Lucien calls Mara beautiful, Seraphina catches the adjective; Lucien distinguishes noticing beauty from desire and reminds Seraphina that she is the woman he searched for, entered the Pale Court for and intends to marry.

Seraphina pouts and demands Mara be gone. Lucien asks her first to hear why Mara is present. Seraphina finally scowls, crosses her arms and says, "Fine."

Lucien explains that Mara Voss is a mortal gentlewoman from a minor but well-connected merchant-gentry family whom he knew before Seraphina joined him, though never as a lover. Mara has practical experience running a large household and managing correspondence, suppliers, servants and the social presentation expected of a lord's seat. During Seraphina's disappearance, Veyne Hall was increasingly disordered because Lucien repeatedly left to search for her and Christian was not yet ready to bear full stewardship. Lucien therefore called Mara to Veyne Hall as a temporary household administrator and organizer. Mara's authority was meant to cover servants, stores, schedules, guests and ordinary domestic administration while Lucien searched—not Seraphina's personal chambers, wardrobe or standing as Lucien's betrothed and cofounder of House Veyne. Mara overstepped by extending that practical authority into Seraphina's private rooms and presentation without invitation. Lucien acknowledges he failed to define that boundary tightly enough before Seraphina returned.

Mara's nature is now established as mortal. Her current role is temporary household administrator/organizer rather than lover, rival consort or superior to Seraphina. She and Lucien had a pre-existing social acquaintance before Seraphina joined him, but no sexual relationship. Mara's own private ambitions, feelings toward Lucien, and whether she intends her role to remain temporary remain unestablished.

What occurred with Mara and Garran after Seraphina left the entrance hall remains unestablished. Garran's deeper reason for concern, how he knew Seraphina was absent, and whether he has now left Veyne Hall remain unestablished.

Seraphina's original daggers remain confiscated by the Pale Court. Her gifted ivory-handled daggers and fitted red leather armour remain available at Veyne Hall. Her betrothal ring is restored to her hand.

The Pale Court rupture, Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead, Hadrik network, Christian's prospective stewardship and Elianor Thorne's planned turning remain unresolved.
