# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina has reached the jail while dressed in her usual feminine, practical black leather armor, carrying her pair of fighting daggers, and wearing a hooded cloak to keep direct sunlight off her skin. At the entrance, a mortal guard asks her business. Seraphina gives him a sweet smile and says, 'Sir Vale wishes me to accompany him. Do you know where I can find him?' She has therefore openly used Garran's name at the jail while seeking him. Whether Garran has already delivered the tithe-pit prisoners here, whether he remains inside, and what the guard knows have not yet been established.
