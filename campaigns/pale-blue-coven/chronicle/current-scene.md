# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina stands apart from Lucien Veyne and speaks directly with Sir Garran Vale. Garran's unresolved oath and Seraphina's assumption that her vampiric nature makes them enemies have come into direct conflict. After Seraphina says her nature cannot be reconciled in one conversation, Garran challenges her assumption that she is necessarily his enemy and asks whether she actually is. Seraphina answers quietly, "I don't mean to be." This is the clearest statement yet that despite leaving her former coven and voluntarily joining Lucien, Seraphina does not intend hostility toward Garran. Garran knows she loves him because she previously told him so, has seen her repeatedly protect him and preserve human lives, and now has her direct denial of hostile intent; he still has not resolved what his oath requires regarding her. Garran knows Seraphina voluntarily joined Lucien but still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, that she later completed a blood-oath with him, that Lucien calls her his prospective queen, or that they are building a new coven.
