# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina finishes questioning the one-legged beggar in Bellwater. She releases the first offered coin, stands, and flips him a second coin as she leaves; these ordinary small payments are treated as abstracted personal effects rather than tracked inventory. She proceeds alone toward Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard, keeping her hood arranged against direct sunlight. From a discreet initial approach she confirms a small, old burial chapel farther inland from the river, enclosed by a worn stone wall. The public entrance faces the street; a narrower lane runs along the rear wall, consistent with the beggar's account of covert cart traffic. Recent wheel ruts are visible in damp ground at the rear approach, and one set runs through a broad service gate into the yard. Seraphina has not yet entered the chapel grounds or established what was unloaded there the previous night. Sir Garran Vale remains elsewhere in Bellwater obtaining food and does not witness this scouting.
