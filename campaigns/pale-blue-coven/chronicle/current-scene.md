# Current scene

After paying the butcher and collecting the six newly filled animal-blood bottles, Seraphina drives the limeworks cart to the rendezvous point she, Garran and Frederick agreed upon when they separated on entering Bellwater. The meeting place is now established as a quiet practical roadside/yard position in Bellwater where a cart and horse can wait without blocking the main street; no named landmark or proprietor is established. Seraphina parks the cart there and waits for the others to finish their errands and return.

The cart now carries twelve filled bottles total: six original sealed bottles of collected human blood and six newly filled bottles of same-day animal blood. The animal-blood bottles still need to be stoppered/sealed and packed to resemble the original consignment. The matching stoppers, available limeworks sealing/packing materials and two straw-lined cases are present. Whether the substitution can pass inspection remains unknown.

Garran is separately completing the Saint Orra handoff of the maimed commander, two wounded surviving guards and four dead hostile men. Frederick is separately completing the medical-care delivery of the twelve freed captives and still has the captured split-bell tally, delivery papers, correspondence and remaining operational purse funds unless deliberately transferred. Both intend to return to Seraphina at this rendezvous. Hadrik remains absent/unidentified. The intended next lead remains the riverside storehouse east/downstream of Bellwater with the whitewashed gable and broken waterwheel beside the old millrace. It is fully night.
