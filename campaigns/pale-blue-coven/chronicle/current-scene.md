# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Sir Garran Vale is resting elsewhere in Bellwater before the expected evening/night surveillance of Saint Orra's rear gate. Seraphina has moved away from Saint Orra's immediate district to feed first, keeping her hood positioned against direct sunlight. In a working quarter she selects a sober adult man of small build who is alone and appears unlikely to overpower her: a slight warehouse laborer carrying no obvious weapon as he takes a narrow alley between buildings. The alley is confined enough to limit witnesses and escape routes, though Seraphina has not yet attacked, lured, restrained, or fed from him. Garran does not know she has gone hunting.
