# Current scene

In the yard of the isolated carter's holding, after Garran asks how much the traveler would pay a carter for moving living cargo, Seraphina says nothing and fixes the traveler with a sustained stare while holding the repacked split-bell satchel and its operational coin purse. Her silent pressure contributes to the traveler breaking the stalemate without Seraphina resorting to torture or overt threats. The traveler has not identified his clerk contact, employer, Hadrik, author of the order concerning Seraphina, the exact number or identity of any captives expected today, or their ultimate destination/purpose.
