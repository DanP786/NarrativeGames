# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in an old burial chamber beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Behind stone cover near the mouth of the deeper passage, Seraphina lies facedown after tearing the crossbow bolt from her side. Tears stream down her face and her whole body shakes with pain, but she forces herself to remain quiet, pressing and packing her cloak against the bleeding wound while still gripping her second dagger. Her earlier head symptoms continue to improve; the side wound remains the dominant injury, and her accelerated vampiric healing has begun but is neither instant nor sufficient to make her combat-ready. Garran hears the abrupt silence after her scream and remains acutely aware of her condition while facing the active crossbow threat. The defender remains behind the low wooden obstruction around the left bend. The escaped worker remains alive nearby with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee; the other wounded armed man remains nearby and unsecured enough to require attention. The fallen lantern still burns. The tunnels remain alerted.
