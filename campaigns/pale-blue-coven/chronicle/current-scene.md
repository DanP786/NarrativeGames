# Current scene

At the Lantern Rooms, Seraphina hunts discreetly among the late-night revelers after Yseld orders her to investigate the necromancer. Dressed in a flowing black gown with pale-blue accents and gold makeup, she draws attention. A drinking man approaches and nearly spills his ale over her, but Seraphina's practiced agility lets her slip aside before a drop touches the gown. Seraphina dislikes feeding from heavily intoxicated humans because alcohol in their blood affects her and her small body becomes intoxicated quickly. The man is drinking but not yet slurring, making him an acceptable prospect. After he offers her a fresh drink, Seraphina smiles and says she is parched. Taking the remark innocently, he offers to buy her wine and gestures toward the bar, remaining an available feeding prospect.
