# Current scene

Several nights after Seraphina's return to Bellwater, she and Lucien Veyne are meeting prospective recruit Mistress Elianor Thorne in Thorne's private warehouse office. Seraphina wears expensive but restrained feminine clothing, jewelry, and a convincing living complexion and is willingly participating in Lucien's recruitment strategy. She has truthfully but broadly described herself and Lucien as building a new household and network, acquiring property, arranging money and supplies, and choosing useful, trusted people, while withholding the supernatural truth of the intended coven. When Elianor directly asks whether that search for people is why Seraphina and Lucien are sitting in her office, Seraphina answers simply, "Yes," refusing to evade the recruitment purpose. Lucien does not contradict or speak over her. Elianor now knows plainly that she is being evaluated and approached as a potential member of whatever new house or organization they are building, though she still does not know they are vampires or that immortality may ultimately be part of the offer. The Pale Court has not yet been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance, and Garran does not know the circumstances of her disappearance or her allegiance to Lucien.
