# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina leaves the Pale Court dressed in her usual feminine, practical black leather armor with her pair of fighting daggers. She adds a hooded cloak to keep direct sunlight off her skin and sets out to find Sir Garran Vale at the jail where she expects the prisoners from the tithe pit to have been delivered. Lady Yseld has ordered her to accept the unnamed knight's invitation and remain close enough to gather intelligence while concealing the Pale Court and her vampiric nature. Seraphina knows the knight is Garran but has concealed his identity from Yseld. Whether Garran is still at the jail when Seraphina arrives has not yet been established.
