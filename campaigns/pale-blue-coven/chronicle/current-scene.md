# Current scene

Several days after returning from Bellwater, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne are together privately in his bed at Veyne Hall after Seraphina explicitly accepts his marriage proposal. They are engaged to marry, while the form, timing, witnesses, rites, legal standing, and any public announcement remain undecided. Their conversation has turned to Lady Yseld Veyr and the Pale Court. Seraphina has received no direct contact from Yseld since leaving and has not informed the Pale Court of her defection, blood-oath, prospective queenship, or engagement. After asking whether Lucien has heard anything from Yseld, Seraphina now asks him directly, "Are you prepared for a full coven to be at war with us?" The question forces Lucien to address the practical danger of the Pale Court treating Seraphina's defection and alliance with him as grounds for war; the Pale Court's actual intentions remain unknown.
