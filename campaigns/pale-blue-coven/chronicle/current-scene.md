# Current scene

Several nights have passed since Seraphina returned to Bellwater beside Lucien Veyne. Seraphina and Lucien have begun deliberately searching Bellwater and its surrounding roads, taverns, households, and night traffic for people who might be useful recruits for the new coven they intend to build. Lucien varies Seraphina's clothing and outward role according to each evening's purpose rather than always presenting her in the red fighting armour. Seraphina willingly plays along and discovers that she enjoys the changing costumes and social identities: appearing as the kind of woman most useful for the person or place they intend to approach while remaining herself beneath the performance. These roles are part of their recruitment and reconnaissance rather than proof that any target has yet joined them. Their desired recruits, selection criteria beyond usefulness and trust, and first successful recruit remain unresolved. The Pale Court has not yet been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance. Garran still does not know the circumstances of her disappearance or her allegiance to Lucien.
