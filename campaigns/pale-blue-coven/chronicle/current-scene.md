# Current scene

From concealment near the rear of the abandoned lime kiln, Seraphina tosses several small pebbles so they strike and scatter near the underground entrance, then immediately shifts closer under cover rather than remaining where the sound originated. Her novice Stealth succeeds. The concealed human guard below reacts to the irregular noise and comes up far enough to inspect the entrance, leaving his deeper position beyond the bend and exposing himself nearer the mouth of the passage. He does not visibly detect Seraphina. She uses his movement to close the distance and reaches a tighter concealed position near the entrance, where broken kiln stone and weeds screen her from the aboveground handoff area. The guard is now only a short distance away and alert, listening and looking for the source of the disturbance. The five bound adult human captives have already been taken farther below, and voices deeper underground continue faintly beyond the guarded bend. Frederick remains above maintaining his courier role with the seized split-bell satchel and tally; Garran remains concealed separately nearby and has not initiated an attack. No general alarm has been raised. Seraphina is now positioned close enough to attempt to slip past, ambush or distract the exposed guard, but she has not yet acted against him. She remains in fitted red leather armour with matched ivory-handled fighting daggers, black hooded cloak protecting her from daylight, braided/plaited ponytail, and her betrothal ring hidden against her chest.
