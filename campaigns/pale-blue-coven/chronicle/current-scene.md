# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina remains inside Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard under the false story that her mistress is seeking a missing woman named Raquelle. The lay burial keeper checks the recent intake ledger and finds an unidentified woman brought in two nights earlier whose rough description could match Seraphina's invented Raquelle: brown hair, bad teeth, and around Seraphina's height or slightly taller. When Seraphina asks whether the woman has already been buried, the keeper says no. According to the public ledger, the unidentified woman's burial is still pending because Saint Orra's holds unknown bodies briefly in case someone identifies or claims them. He says the body should still be in Saint Orra's keeping and offers to let Seraphina view her for identification. This creates a discrepancy worth testing against Seraphina's secret discovery of processed human remains in the rear service outbuilding, but it is not yet established whether this particular woman's intact body is actually present or connected to the covert operation. The keeper remains unaware that Seraphina entered the service outbuilding. Sir Garran Vale remains elsewhere in Bellwater.
