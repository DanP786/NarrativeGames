# Current scene

Several days after returning from Bellwater, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne are together privately at Veyne Hall after Seraphina explicitly accepts his marriage proposal. They are engaged to marry, while the form, timing, witnesses, rites, legal standing, and any public announcement remain undecided. Their conversation has turned to Lady Yseld Veyr and the Pale Court. Seraphina says Yseld relied mostly on her and that she was Yseld's favourite persuader. When Lucien suspiciously asks how she persuaded people, Seraphina answers solemnly, "My body." Lucien realizes Seraphina was effectively used as the Pale Court household's concubine: Yseld repeatedly treated her body as political and social utility and ordered her to sexually please visiting men of power, both human and vampire. This history helps Lucien understand the practiced agreeableness and deference to authority he has observed in Seraphina, but he cannot assume every instance of her willing deference to him is merely conditioning; her present choices remain her own unless established otherwise. Seraphina has received no direct contact from Yseld since leaving, and Yseld's actual current plans remain unknown.
