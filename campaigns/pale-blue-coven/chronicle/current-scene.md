# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane inside the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. Seraphina follows Garran's lead from the cellar hatch to the low stone building's rear door, keeping one dagger drawn tight along her forearm. They reach the door without detection. At close range they can hear the two known workers below rather than immediately behind the door; one complains about the weight while the other orders him to keep moving. The rear door itself is closed but not locked, and opens inward into a dim service room with crates, tools, a shielded lamp, and a stone stair descending to the cellar. No one is presently visible in the service room. The sharper smell of recent human remains rises from below. Garran is poised to enter first with Seraphina following. The seventh load described as fresh and whole when loaded remains below or otherwise unlocated.
