# Current scene

Late at night after a painful conversation with Lucien ends without reconciliation, Seraphina dresses for travel in her civilian black ensemble, takes her original fighting daggers rather than the ivory-handled pair Lucien gave her, adds a black cloak, and leaves Veyne Hall. She does not stop to speak to anyone in the household. Beyond the estate she keeps walking along dark country roads without choosing or paying attention to a destination. After hours of wandering, a road divides: one branch descends toward running water and a distant orange light, while the other continues beneath dense trees. Seraphina chooses the wooded road and continues into the forest alone.

The road beneath the trees is narrow, muddy and increasingly enclosed by old growth and tangled underbrush. No destination or settlement ahead has been established. Veyne Hall is now far behind her.

The engagement has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring and Lucien can no longer assume the intended marriage will proceed unchanged. Lucien still possesses the gem-encrusted betrothal ring. The fitted red leather armour and ivory-handled daggers he provided remain behind at Veyne Hall. Frederick remains injured but recovering. Christian has an expected channel for garden requests backed directly by Seraphina. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
