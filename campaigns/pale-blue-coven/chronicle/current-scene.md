# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne unexpectedly encounter Sir Garran Vale. Both vampires are in ordinary travel clothes. Garran says he has been looking for Seraphina; she answers, "Why? So you can finally kill me?" Lucien keeps himself physically between them, treating Garran as a potential lethal threat. Garran states that he meant his earlier decision not to kill Seraphina and says he wanted to know whether she reached home alive after she disappeared. He identifies Seraphina as being with him and makes clear that Garran will not approach her through him without her permission, but does not volunteer the circumstances of the abduction, blood-oath, or new coven. Garran now knows Lucien's name and can observe that Lucien is protective and possessive toward Seraphina, but he still does not know Lucien is a vampire, that Lucien abducted Seraphina after Garran let her leave, that Seraphina subsequently chose Lucien and completed a blood-oath with him, or that they are building a prospective coven. Seraphina has not yet explained her relationship with Lucien.
