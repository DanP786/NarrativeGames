# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina stands between Sir Garran Vale and Lucien Veyne after Lucien's possessiveness erupts into an open claim. Lucien has told Garran that Seraphina chose him, shares his bed, and will stand beside him in the larger future he intends to build. Garran answers by observing that Lucien seems unusually concerned to convince him of Seraphina's choice. Seraphina swallows in the resulting silence. Privately, her position is now explicit: she still loves Garran, but believes Garran would never choose her, and that belief is a central reason she has accepted a life with Lucien instead. Neither Garran nor Lucien can know this private reasoning unless Seraphina says it aloud. Garran knows Seraphina and Lucien are lovers and that Lucien intends to build some larger enterprise with her, but he still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, the blood-oath, the prospective-queen title, or the exact nature and scale of the new coven.
