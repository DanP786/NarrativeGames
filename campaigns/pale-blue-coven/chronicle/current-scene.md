# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane at the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. Concealed beside Garran, Seraphina gives him a sideways look after his decision that he will not send her below alone, then returns her attention to the yard without asking what he meant. Garran notices the glance but does not interpret or answer the unspoken thought. They continue watching the hooded driver and two workers finish unloading the Saint Orra cart. The crates are being carried through the rear door of the low stone building; at least one contained cleaned human bones. A cellar-level opening or loading hatch remains visible near the rear wall. Neither Seraphina nor Garran has been detected. They are waiting to see what the driver does once the cart is empty before deciding whether and how to investigate below.
