# Current scene

Seraphina approves Christian's proposed handling of the household solstice celebration and gives him a larger test: "Excellent. See to this, Christian, and you will be steward of the house." The position is therefore offered conditionally rather than immediately granted. Christian must successfully oversee the household-welfare inquiry and preparations for the mortals' winter-solstice celebration before Seraphina will make him steward of Veyne Hall.

Christian takes the offer seriously. What began as a provisions assignment has become a path to formal household authority and responsibility. His active duties now include continuing sensible provisioning, discreetly assessing the happiness and reasonable needs of mortal retainers and willing blood donors, and arranging a private household solstice celebration in which the humans are guests rather than servants or feeding stock. Exact feast, gifts, entertainment and logistics remain his to develop and report back on.

Christian remains at his provisions worktable with Seraphina beside him. Seraphina is wearing the fitted red silk halter dress with its long side slit, soft honey-blonde waves and red lipstick. She is moving freely within Veyne Hall. Lucien retains her gem-encrusted betrothal ring; the engagement has not explicitly ended and the larger trust issue remains unresolved.

Seraphina's fitted red leather armour and matched ivory-handled daggers remain set aside in the private rooms. The leather straps are off her body and remain among their private effects. Her black sunlight cloak remains behind at the Bellwater operation cart. The Bellwater substitution plan, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved. Frederick's return to Veyne Hall has not yet been established.
