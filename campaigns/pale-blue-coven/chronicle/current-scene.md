# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne face Sir Garran Vale. Garran has recognized Lucien as a vampire and asked whether he is Seraphina's master. Lucien laughed at the assumption; his established relationship with Seraphina is possessive and dominant in some private contexts but not a maker-fledgling bond, and he has chosen her as his prospective queen whose judgment and choices matter. Lucien then asks Seraphina whether she wants him to release his protective hold and let her step toward Garran. Seraphina does not answer that question. Remaining held with her back against Lucien's chest, she instead looks to Garran and asks, "Why were you looking for me?" Garran had already said he wanted to know whether she reached home alive after disappearing, but Seraphina is now pressing him for the deeper reason behind his sustained search. Garran knows Lucien's name and vampire nature but still does not know Lucien abducted Seraphina after their last encounter, that Seraphina later willingly chose Lucien and completed a blood-oath with him, or that they are building a prospective coven.
