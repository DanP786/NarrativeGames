# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has privately warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Their discussion has shifted to why Seraphina left her former coven. After explaining that coven members fill roles including patrol, assassination, recruitment, household/business work and concubinage, Seraphina bitterly tells Garran, "it is obvious now, I followed you, fell in love with you. All Because I thought you could save me." Garran now knows from Seraphina herself that her long period of following him and her former love for him were bound up with a hope that he could save her from the life/role she had in her former coven. Her earlier discomfort around the word concubine strongly suggests personal relevance, but she has still not explicitly described Yseld's orders to sexually please powerful visitors. Garran does not know that Seraphina later declared she no longer loves him, nor that she and Lucien are engaged, blood-bound, or planning a new coven with Seraphina as prospective queen. Lucien remains at the goldsmith's shop and has not been established as seeing Seraphina leave. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
