# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Sir Garran Vale has departed after Seraphina publicly confirmed her present choice of Lucien Veyne and the life he offers. Seraphina turned voluntarily into Lucien's chest for comfort. When Lucien asked whether he is the life she wants to build or merely the life she chose because she believes another life would never have her, Seraphina opens her eyes, looks up at him, sighs, and says, "What I want is to be human, Lucien." Lucien now knows that the impossible desire behind Seraphina's earlier statement is, at least as she expresses it here, a desire to be human rather than simply a declaration that she wishes Garran had chosen her. Garran is gone and does not hear this. Garran knows Seraphina and Lucien are lovers and that Lucien intends to build some larger enterprise with her, but he still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, the blood-oath, the prospective-queen title, or the exact nature and scale of the new coven.
