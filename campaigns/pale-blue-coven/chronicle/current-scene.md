# Current scene

At the tithe pit north beyond the old gallows road, Garran and Seraphina have subdued the bone-harvesting crew. The armed overseer has revealed that the necromancer killed at the croft was merely a customer of a larger operation. Seraphina has told Garran she came because she heard a knight was alone and wanted to help, and that she watches him so he can investigate without watching his own back. Garran now interrogates the captured overseer, using controlled questions and the evidence already found rather than immediate torture. Seraphina is privately excited by Garran's earlier scrutiny and continues watching him with even greater intensity; Garran cannot know that private reaction, but her conspicuous stare is visible to him.
