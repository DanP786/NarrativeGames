# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina stands with Sir Garran Vale while Lucien Veyne remains nearby. Garran has explained that he cannot cleanly equate vampirism with monstrosity after witnessing Seraphina's choices: her predatory feeding remains morally serious to him, but so do her repeated acts of protection, restraint, loyalty, and refusal to attack him. He therefore offers a narrow accord in which he judges what she actually does rather than declaring her an enemy by nature alone. Seraphina steps back slightly, withdrawing her hand from his, and answers, "I will keep out of trouble." The response accepts the practical boundary without resolving her resignation or the deeper emotional conflict between them. Garran knows Seraphina voluntarily joined Lucien and does not intend to be Garran's enemy, but still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, that she later completed a blood-oath with him, that Lucien calls her his prospective queen, or that they are building a new coven.
