# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has his sword drawn on Seraphina after concluding she is a vampire and accusing her of preparing him as prey. When he demands why she has cared for him if not to feed on him, Seraphina answers simply, 'Because I love you.' Garran does not believe the declaration. Shocked and repulsed, he shakes his head and interprets her long attention as surveillance instead, accusing her of having been told to monitor him. He presses for actionable information: where her coven is and how many vampires belong to it. Seraphina has not answered those questions. Garran now knows she claims to love him but does not accept the claim as true. His sword remains drawn; Seraphina has not reached for her daggers or attacked. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
