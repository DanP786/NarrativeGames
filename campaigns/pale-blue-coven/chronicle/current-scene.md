# Current scene

Several nights have passed since Seraphina returned to Bellwater beside Lucien Veyne. They are using Veyne Hall as a base while searching Bellwater and its surroundings for people who might become useful, trusted recruits for their intended new coven. Lucien varies Seraphina's clothing and social role according to each evening's purpose, and Seraphina willingly participates because she enjoys the costumes and performances. Tonight at Veyne Hall, Lucien has dressed her particularly carefully in expensive but restrained feminine clothing and fastened a necklace at her throat. A sealed scrap bearing the name of a person they have watched for two nights lies nearby. Lucien tells Seraphina that tonight he needs her to be "irresistible" because he thinks they have finally found someone worth asking. Seraphina raises an eyebrow and asks, "Irresistible how?" The prospective recruit's identity, nature, vulnerabilities, and what precise role Lucien wants Seraphina to play have not yet been established. The Pale Court has not yet been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance, and Garran does not know the circumstances of her disappearance or her allegiance to Lucien.
