# Current scene

Inside a private chamber of the Pale Court, Seraphina is reporting to Lady Yseld Veyr. She says she initially intended only to observe the necromancer and the knight, but intervened when there was a threat. She reports that the necromancer recognized what she was, so she killed him, and admits that she is now personally known to the knight. Seraphina then lies by omission/fabrication that she did not catch the knight's name; in fact she knows him as Sir Garran Vale. Yseld has no direct evidence proving this false, but the claim is suspicious because Seraphina admits substantial direct contact with a mortal hunter after a pattern of unexplained excursions. Yseld still does not know Garran's identity, Seraphina's feelings for him, the full extent of their cooperation, the roughly two-dozen intended raising, the tithe-pit corpse source, or the Bellwater tannery lead.
