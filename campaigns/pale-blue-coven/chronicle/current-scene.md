# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has Seraphina pinned against the wall with his forearm across her throat after stopping her attempted escape. Her hands pull at his restraining arm without reaching for her daggers or attacking him. When Garran demands to know why she ran, Seraphina answers, 'You kill monsters like me, of course I ran.' Garran now has a straightforward explanation for her flight: she believes discovery of her vampirism puts her in mortal danger from him. Her explanation fits both his known oath and her continued refusal to fight him, weakening the idea that she fled in order to attack, warn someone, or conceal an immediate plot. He still does not know why she selected the sober laborer she fed upon or precisely what condition she left him in. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
