# Current scene

Late in the working day on the 19th of Harvestwane, Seraphina leads Sir Garran Vale from Bellwater's market district to the best observation position she identified during her daylight reconnaissance of Saint Orra's approaches. The position gives them a useful concealed view toward the rear service lane and gate without requiring them to sit directly opposite it or stand conspicuously in the open. As they settle into the approach to the watch, Seraphina tells Garran, 'You look better.' He understands the comment in light of her earlier concern about his sleepless condition and the fact that she insisted he eat and rest. Garran is now substantially better rested and mostly cleaned of the previous night's grime, while Seraphina is fed and prepared for a long night though he does not know the source of that readiness. The legitimate working day is ending; no covert three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet occurred.
