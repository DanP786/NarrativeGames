# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has privately warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with three drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Seraphina tells Garran that she and Lucien have taken willing thralls under the feeding agreement she and Garran previously discussed: the thralls live in luxury and their sole role is to provide blood. She says she dislikes that arrangement personally and prefers feeding from a foolish drunk who might learn a lesson from the encounter. This gives Garran new information that Seraphina and Lucien now maintain willing blood donors, while preserving Seraphina's stated preference for opportunistic nonlethal feeding. Garran still does not know Seraphina and Lucien are engaged, about their blood-oath or prospective queenship, or that Seraphina recently declared she no longer loves Garran. Lucien remains at the goldsmith's shop and has not been established as seeing Seraphina leave. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
