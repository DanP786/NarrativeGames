# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina finishes her covert investigation of Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard. She thanks the lay burial keeper and leaves through the public entrance at an unhurried pace, preserving her cover as a servant searching for the fictitious missing woman Raquelle. She now knows Saint Orra's legitimate public work coexists with a covert rear-yard operation: the previous night's heavy cart delivered processed human remains to a service outbuilding containing a crate marked with the same vertically split-bell emblem as the tannery tally slip. She has not established who controls the operation, whether the burial keeper knows of it, or where the processed remains ultimately go. Seraphina leaves Saint Orra's intending to find Sir Garran Vale and report what she learned. Garran is still in Bellwater after separating from her to obtain food; his exact current establishment has not yet been fixed.
