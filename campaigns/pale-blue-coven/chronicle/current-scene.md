# Current scene

Daylight on the 20th of Harvestwane at Veyne Hall, Lucien Veyne's old family seat in wooded hills north of Bellwater. In the armoury, after Seraphina's willing intimate blood-oath to Lucien, she puts on the new leather armour he prepared for her. It is red rather than Pale Court black, fitted precisely to her small frame, unmistakably feminine and designed to make her strikingly attractive without ceasing to function as fighting armour. Lucien steps back and openly admires the result, treating her changed appearance as part of the identity he is creating for his intended queen. Seraphina's original black leather armour, daggers, cloak, pale-blue items, and personal effects remain elsewhere at Veyne Hall. Her declared allegiance is now to Lucien and his prospective coven, though the Pale Court has not yet been informed. Lucien's prior allegiance, full resources, actual source of his earlier information, and prospective followers remain unresolved. Garran does not know where Seraphina is.
