# Current scene

Seraphina leaves Sir Garran Vale at the tithe pit after the night's fight and interrogation. When Garran dryly suggests she may mysteriously lurk near his next destination, she replies in mock offense, 'A lady does not lurk,' and saunters away into the darkness. She does not follow him to Bellwater. Instead she returns to the Pale Court in the late deep night, her black-and-pale-blue gown badly muddied and bloodstained from the croft and tithe pit. She now possesses the substance of the information Lady Yseld ordered her to gather: the necromancer is dead; his intended raising involved roughly two dozen corpses; the tithe pit supplied human remains; a larger trade in bones exists with an old tannery on Bellwater's east bank as a collection point; and another hunter, Sir Garran Vale, was pursuing the necromancer. Yseld does not yet know any of these results, Garran's identity, Seraphina's cooperation with him, or Seraphina's feelings for him. Seraphina has returned to the coven and still owes Yseld a direct report.
