# Current scene

Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale travel together away from the ruined croft north beyond the old gallows road, pursuing the necromancer's ledger lead toward the unidentified 'tithe pit.' Their conversation has become increasingly teasing despite Garran's continuing wariness. After Garran suggests Seraphina would probably ignore an order to fetch soldiers and follow him instead, she pouts and replies, 'Who's to say I would do any of that. Maybe I'd run back and say nothing at all.' Garran continues to accept her as a useful companion for the investigation while remaining unconvinced by her explanations about who she is and why she came to the croft.
