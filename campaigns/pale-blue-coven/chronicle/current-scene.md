# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the neglected yard outside the corpse-processing site beyond Bellwater. Angry after the confrontation over her feeding, Seraphina waits outside while Garran remains below securing the surviving prisoners. At the water trough she has already washed blood from her hands, cleaned the substantially healed but still painful crossbow wound in her side, and rebound it with a clean cloth. She now washes the blood and grime from her face and tries to put her disordered appearance back into some kind of order. Instead of leaving her honey-blonde hair in its usual loose waves, she gathers it and braids it down her back. Her face and hands are cleaner and her hair is controlled, though her black leather and blood-soaked cloak remain battered evidence of the night's violence. Both fighting daggers are in her possession and sheathed. Garran knows Seraphina fed from both the bound cellar worker and the swordsman in the yard, but he does not know she drank her fill from the swordsman, whether feeding materially contributed to his death, or that an unidentified rival vampire approached her before his arrival. The wounded worker remains alive and bleeding, the bound cellar worker remains alive, and the captured archer remains alive, hurt, disarmed, and bound. The swordsman in the yard is dead. The rival vampire has departed the immediate yard.
