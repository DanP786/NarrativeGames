# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in an old burial chamber beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Garran has the wounded armed man controlled after pulling him off Seraphina. Seraphina, still coughing and suffering blurred vision and pounding head pain, tries to sit up rather than remain down. A heavy scraping sound comes from farther along the burial tunnels. Seraphina turns toward it and, despite her impaired vision, identifies movement beyond the lantern's strongest light: something large is being dragged or shifted across stone around the next bend, followed by a brief human movement withdrawing from view. Garran notices Seraphina tracking the sound and immediately shifts his attention toward the deeper passage while keeping his prisoner controlled. The escaped worker remains alive nearby with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee. The fallen lantern still burns; Seraphina retains her second dagger. The tunnels remain alerted.
