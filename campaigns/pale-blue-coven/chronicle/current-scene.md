# Current scene

Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale overlook the tithe pit north beyond the old gallows road, an enormous old mass-burial trench containing hundreds of exposed bones. They have observed at least three lantern-bearing workers opening old burial layers and loading bones, while a fourth unseen voice ordered them to finish before dawn. Their exact allegiance and degree of involvement in the necromancer's work remain unconfirmed. Seraphina stays directly behind Garran with her dagger ready and whispers, 'This is your operation. I'm waiting for you to do… whatever you're going to do.' Garran is taking tactical lead and intends to distinguish hired laborers from willing necromantic accomplices before committing to lethal force.
