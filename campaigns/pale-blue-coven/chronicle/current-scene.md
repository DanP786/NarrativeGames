# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran remains close to Seraphina at the wall after stopping her attempted escape, though the pressure at her throat has eased. Her breathing is ragged, tears remain on her face, and both hands are now flat against the wall at her sides rather than near her daggers. When Garran asks whether she still believes he is going to kill her, Seraphina looks from his sword to his eyes and answers, 'Yes.' Garran now knows that even after he has hesitated, questioned her, and refrained from striking, Seraphina still expects death from him because of what she is. Her posture and continued refusal to arm herself reinforce that she is not presently preparing to attack him. He still does not know why she selected the sober laborer she fed upon or precisely what condition she left him in. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
