# Current scene

Several days after Seraphina's playful withholding campaign turned sour, Lucien approaches the silence in Veyne Hall's lower hall. After clearing nearby servants away, he tells Seraphina he misunderstood her attempt to make amends: he had thought she was merely trying to distract him from Bellwater, but now understands that chosen intimacy, playful deference and deliberate effort to please him were the language in which she was trying to come back to him. He apologizes without qualification. Seraphina turns her head away and tries to hide tears in her eyes. Lucien notices the visible distress but does not assume permission to touch her or compel her attention.

The engagement remains intact and Lucien still possesses the gem-encrusted betrothal ring. Seraphina remains free to move within Veyne Hall. Frederick remains at Veyne Hall recovering from his beating; Seraphina has not yet spoken to him about whether he believes she broke his trust. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
