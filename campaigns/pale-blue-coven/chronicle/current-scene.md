# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in an old burial chamber beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina remains seated against the wall with her second dagger ready, keeping her eyes fixed on the deeper passage while waiting for the effects of the head impact and strangling to ease. Her vision remains impaired but begins to settle gradually rather than worsening; the doubling recedes intermittently, though her head still pounds and she is not yet fully recovered. Garran keeps the wounded armed man controlled and questions him about another route past the prepared position. The prisoner says there is no second tunnel from this chamber, but claims the passage beyond the bend opens into older burial works with side recesses; whether he is telling the whole truth is not established. The low wooden obstruction remains partly across the passage, with at least one human defender concealed near it. The escaped worker remains alive nearby with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee. The fallen lantern still burns. The tunnels remain alerted.
