# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has identified Seraphina as a vampire and now holds his sword lowered but still in hand while she remains backed against the wall. He knows she followed him for roughly a year before their direct meeting and before her coven knew about him, and she says she wanted nothing except to know he was safe. When pressed about whom she feeds upon, Seraphina looks at him with visible shame and answers, 'Drunks mostly, men who are rough with women.' Garran now knows the broad category of prey she says she chooses, but not how often she feeds, whether she kills them, or that she fed from a warehouse laborer earlier today. Her admission confirms that she does attack humans for blood even while claiming to choose particular targets. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
