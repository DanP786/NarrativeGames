# Current scene

Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale have reached a weathered stone marker beside the road north beyond the old gallows road. The marker bears the same crown-over-line-over-crosses symbol found beside the necromancer's repeated 'tithe pit' entries. Beyond it, a narrow woodland path descends into darkness, making it their strongest lead toward the unidentified pit. When Garran remarks that Seraphina's claimed morbid fascination is about to be rewarded, she tells him, 'You're making a lot of assumptions about a lady you just met,' and casually tosses her dagger into a reverse grip so the blade lies along her forearm. Garran remains wary but increasingly accustomed to her teasing and obvious knife skill.
