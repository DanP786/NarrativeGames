# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina leaves Saint Orra's intending to find Sir Garran Vale and report what she learned. She locates him from a distance outside a modest Bellwater cookshop near the east-bank market lanes and watches before approaching, deliberately assessing his wellbeing. Garran has obeyed her instruction to eat: he has finished a substantial simple meal and has water with him. He remains visibly short of sleep after the previous night's fighting and investigation, but food and a brief period seated out of immediate danger have improved him; his movements are steadier and less depleted than at the tannery, with no newly apparent wound or acute physical distress. He has not yet noticed or acknowledged Seraphina watching him. Seraphina still carries the important Saint Orra intelligence: covert three-ring handbell signals, late rear-gate cart traffic, a heavy delivery the previous night, processed human remains in the rear service outbuilding, and a crate bearing the same split-bell emblem as the tannery tally slip. She has not established who controls the operation, whether Saint Orra's ordinary burial staff know of it, or where the processed remains ultimately go.
