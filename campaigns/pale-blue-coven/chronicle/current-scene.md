# Current scene

The next day at Veyne Hall, Seraphina walks the halls in close-fitted trousers, boots, tunic and waistcoat with numerous leather straps, her honey-blonde hair in soft bouncing curls. After firmly telling Christian that House Veyne will have no concubines and that any pleasure he seeks must involve a willing participant, Seraphina enters the entrance hall and unexpectedly finds Sir Garran Vale standing there. Seraphina freezes, eyes widening in fear for Garran because Lucien previously threatened to kill Garran and make Seraphina watch if he found her with him again. She then crosses the space quickly toward Garran and demands, "What are you doing here?!" Seraphina remains Lucien's betrothed and intended queen and continues to wear his enormous gem-encrusted betrothal ring. Christian remains under consideration for the inner circle while handling household provisions; Frederick is Lucien's muscle and is elsewhere or not yet established as acting in this entrance-hall moment.
