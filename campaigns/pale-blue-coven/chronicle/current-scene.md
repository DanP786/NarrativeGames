# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina works alone in the poorer lanes of Bellwater after sending Sir Garran Vale to find food. She deliberately selects a one-legged elderly beggar who did not see her with Garran, crouches beside him without smiling, assesses him, and asks directly what he knows of Saint Orra. The beggar recognizes the name locally: Saint Orra's is a small burial chapel and paupers' yard farther inland from the river, used for unclaimed bodies and poor burials. He also recognizes 'third bell' as language he has heard attached to activity around the place, but he is cautious and has not yet volunteered what he knows about it. Seraphina has not shown him the tally slip or mentioned the split-bell seal.
