# Current scene

Daylight on the 20th of Harvestwane in an unfamiliar lavishly furnished bedroom at an unknown location. Seraphina is cleaned and dressed in a white linen dress, her honey-blonde hair loose in light curls, and her wrists and ankles restrained with soft leather. Her crossbow wound no longer hurts; her own clothing, daggers, cloak, and possessions remain absent from her person. The unidentified rival vampire who abducted her has declared that he intends to take her from the Pale Court, make her his "in every way," found a new coven, and make Seraphina his queen. Seraphina seriously considers the offer rather than rejecting it. She weighs her weak attachment to the Pale Court, her longstanding tendency toward strong masters, her maker's history of using her body for coven advantage, her violent and unhappy mortal marriage, her physical disadvantage despite loving to fight, and the appeal of having someone strong beside her. She also weighs Garran's revulsion and oath against vampires, interpreting his use of her help during the Bellwater investigation and subsequent release as a form of being discarded once his immediate duty was done, and doubts she would survive another confrontation with him. Against that, the rival vampire wants her openly and has, since abducting her, cleaned, sheltered and tended her rather than immediately harming her. Seraphina looks at him and gives a deliberate nod, accepting his offered place beside him at least in principle. Her acceptance does not establish unquestioning loyalty, love, or consent to every future act. The rival vampire's identity, current coven or prior allegiance, resources, exact location, source of his prior information, and the practical terms of this new bond remain unresolved. Garran did not witness the seizure and does not know where Seraphina is.
