# Current scene

At the service junction beneath the abandoned limeworks, with the search for Renn's killer active, Seraphina removes her black hooded sunlight cloak for freer movement, throws it partway up the steep stone stairs toward the daylight-edged hatch as a deliberate false trail, and slips into the unlit lateral/deeper branch to lie in ambush. Her novice Stealth succeeds. She is concealed in deep darkness where a lantern-carrying human entering from the lit service route will have difficulty seeing her before reaching close range. The cloak is now lying visibly partway up the stair rather than concealed with Seraphina; she is not wearing it and therefore lacks its protection if she later emerges into direct daylight without retrieving it.

The first adult human searcher reaches the service junction carrying a hooded lantern and a short weapon. He has not detected Seraphina. He notices the discarded cloak on the upward stair and treats it as evidence that Renn's killer may have gone toward the hatch. Rather than immediately entering the dark lateral branch, he pauses at the junction and calls back to the other searchers that he has found something. Additional men remain audible farther down the service route, so Seraphina's bait has drawn the first searcher's attention but also risks bringing another hostile man toward the junction before he commits to the stairs. Five bound adult human captives remain alive in the blood chamber as far as Seraphina last established. Hadrik remains unidentified/unconfirmed. Garran and Frederick remain outside without knowing Seraphina's exact position or the developing underground alarm. Seraphina remains in fitted red leather armour with matched ivory-handled fighting daggers, one dagger bloodied from killing Renn, braided/plaited ponytail, and her betrothal ring hidden against her chest.
