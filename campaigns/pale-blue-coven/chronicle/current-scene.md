# Current scene

In the yard of the isolated carter's holding, Seraphina repacks the split-bell satchel and holds up the small coin purse from inside it, asking the traveler, "What is this for?" The traveler says the purse is operational money rather than simply his personal payment: some is intended to pay carters and incidental road costs, while his own courier fee is included in the total. He says handlers are expected to make necessary small payments without producing detailed accounts, but claims he does not know who ultimately funds the network. Garran treats the explanation as provisional and presses him on how much is normally paid, who receives it, and whether the amount in this purse corresponds to a particular load. The purse's exact coin count and value have not yet been established. The traveler has now identified the old abandoned limeworks south of Bellwater, with a track behind the kilns, as the place where the third route is due to meet before dusk; the concealed split-bell tally with three cuts is said to authorize or identify a courier/load on that route. These claims remain to be tested. The traveler still has not identified his clerk contact, employer, Hadrik, author of the order concerning Seraphina, or the destination/purpose of living captives. The carter remains under control nearby, the frightened adult woman remains inside the closed dwelling, and Frederick stays with Seraphina under Lucien's field conditions. No violence has begun. Seraphina remains in fitted red leather armour with matched ivory-handled fighting daggers, black hooded cloak protecting her from daylight, braided/plaited ponytail, and her betrothal ring hidden against her chest.
