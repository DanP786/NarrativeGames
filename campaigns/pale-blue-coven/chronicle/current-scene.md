# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at Saint Orra's rear service approach. After the confrontation between Garran and Seraphina eases, three slow strokes of a handbell sound from within Saint Orra's yard, matching the beggar's account of the covert after-hours signal. Garran turns back to the surveillance objective and postpones judgment between himself and Seraphina. Seraphina swallows, hugs her recovered cloak tightly to herself, and falls silently into step with him rather than fleeing. They are again operating together, but under a newly exposed and deeply uncertain relationship: Garran knows she is a vampire and Seraphina still fears he may ultimately kill her. They move to observe the rear service approach for the covered-cart activity associated with the three-bell signal. No covered cart has yet been sighted.
