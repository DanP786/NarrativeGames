# Current scene

Late in the working day on the 19th of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale maintain their concealed watch over Saint Orra's rear service approach. Asked why his safety mattered enough for her to enter the fight at the ruined croft, Seraphina looks at Garran for a beat and answers, 'Because the work you do is important. People are safer with you around.' Garran accepts the compliment but points out that, from what he has personally witnessed, people can also be safer with Seraphina around: she fought the risen dead, protected his back, and disabled fleeing suspects without killing them. He therefore asks whether she too is sworn to fight monsters. The question is sincere but also probes the unexplained source of her combat competence and motives. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared. Garran still does not know Seraphina is a vampire, has followed him since before their first meeting, loves him, or fed on a warehouse laborer while he slept.
