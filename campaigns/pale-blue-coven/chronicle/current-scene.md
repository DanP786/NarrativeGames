# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina rejoins Sir Garran Vale outside a modest Bellwater cookshop near the east-bank market lanes. After first watching him from a distance and satisfying herself that he has eaten and is physically steadier, she approaches at the same unhurried pace she used leaving Saint Orra's. Garran notices her approach before she reaches his table and recognizes that she has returned as promised. He remains visibly short of sleep but has eaten a substantial simple meal, has water, and shows no newly apparent wound or acute distress. He gives Seraphina his attention and asks what she found, ready to hear the results of her independent inquiry. Seraphina has not yet told him about Saint Orra's covert three-ring handbell signal, late rear-gate carts, the previous night's heavy delivery, the processed human remains in the rear service outbuilding, or the crate bearing the same split-bell emblem as the tannery tally slip.
