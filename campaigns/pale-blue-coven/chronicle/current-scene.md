# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has privately warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Their discussion shifted through Seraphina's former coven role, her recruitment by Lucien, and Garran's feelings toward her. Seraphina revealed that her former master assigned her to please whoever the master wanted; that she originally followed and fell in love with Garran because she believed he could save her; that Lucien knows this history and does not assign her sexually to others; and that she says she gives herself to Lucien willingly. She also told Garran that Lucien first offered her a way out while she was injured in the Bellwater bone yard, she refused, and he later took/abducted her while she was returning home before offering again, at which point she accepted. The exchange became physically intimate: Seraphina stepped into Garran's space, later placed her hands on his chest and leaned against him, while Garran admitted he cares for her and wants to touch and kiss her but insisted she tell him what she wants rather than acting on her former declaration of love. Before Seraphina answers that question, Lucien arrives at the alley entrance and says, "There you are, little bunny." Seraphina turns her head toward him and does not step back from Garran immediately, but then does. She tells Lucien, "Garren was telling me he had been sent information on a target. Me." Lucien therefore now finds Seraphina and Garran alone together immediately after visible physical closeness, although what exact degree of contact he personally saw before speaking is not yet established beyond Seraphina initially remaining close before stepping back. Lucien now knows from Seraphina that Garran received information identifying her as a target; he does not yet know the message's full contents unless she or Garran explains them. Garran still does not know Seraphina and Lucien are engaged, blood-bound, or planning a new coven with Seraphina as prospective queen, nor that Seraphina recently declared to Lucien that she no longer loves Garran. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
