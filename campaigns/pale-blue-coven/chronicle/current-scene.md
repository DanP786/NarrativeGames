# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina has stepped away from Lucien Veyne's protective hold and is speaking directly with Sir Garran Vale. Garran has acknowledged that genuinely willing blood would trouble him less than predation without consent, while refusing to pretend Seraphina's need for blood has an easy moral solution. Seraphina says building enough trust to find a willing donor would take time because the only people she knew were her coven, then abruptly stops after saying, "and now I'm not…" The unfinished admission strongly signals to Garran that Seraphina is no longer with the coven she previously refused to betray, though she has not yet explained whether she left willingly, was cast out, or what Lucien's role is. Garran knows Lucien's name and vampire nature and has seen Seraphina voluntarily pull free of Lucien's arm and approach him, but still does not know Lucien abducted Seraphina after their last encounter, that she later willingly chose Lucien and completed a blood-oath, or that they are building a prospective new coven.
