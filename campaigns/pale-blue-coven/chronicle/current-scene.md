# Current scene

Several days after returning from Bellwater, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne are together intimately in his bed at Veyne Hall after Lucien asks Seraphina to become his wife. Seraphina has challenged whether his proposal is partly driven by feeling threatened by her continuing love for Garran and has told Lucien that Garran has never had or kissed her, and that their recent Bellwater encounter was the first time Garran touched her. Lucien asks whether, if Garran had never entered her life at all, Seraphina would want to be Lucien's wife. While Lucien caresses her during their consensual intimacy, Seraphina moans and answers, "Yes." She has therefore answered Lucien's hypothetical affirmatively, but has not yet explicitly answered the actual marriage proposal without the hypothetical condition. Lucien hears the distinction and may seek confirmation rather than assuming more than she said.
