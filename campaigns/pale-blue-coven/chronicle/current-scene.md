# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne face Sir Garran Vale. Garran had been looking for Seraphina after her disappearance because he wanted to know whether she reached home alive. Lucien has identified himself but remains physically between Garran and Seraphina. When Garran tells Seraphina to say so if she wants Lucien to move, Seraphina tugs Lucien's coat again and says, "Lucien?" Rather than releasing her into Garran's line, Lucien slips an arm around her waist and keeps her back flush to his chest, continuing his protective restraint while leaving her able to speak. In the close confrontation Garran recognizes Lucien for what he is: another vampire. This changes Garran's assessment of the encounter from an unknown protective man to a second supernatural threat, though Garran does not immediately draw his weapon. Seeing Lucien hold Seraphina close and recalling Seraphina's deferential behavior around authority without knowing its history, Garran asks Lucien directly, "Are you her master?" Garran still does not know that Lucien abducted Seraphina after Garran let her leave, that Seraphina later willingly chose Lucien, completed a blood-oath with him, and accepted the proposed place as queen of a new coven. Lucien has not yet answered Garran's question.
