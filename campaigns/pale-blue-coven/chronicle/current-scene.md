# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Their private discussion became physically intimate before Lucien arrived: Seraphina stepped into Garran's space, placed her hands on his chest and leaned against him, while Garran admitted he cares for her and wants to touch and kiss her but insisted she tell him what she wants. Lucien arrived before she answered, revealed their engagement to Garran, and later watched Garran leave after Seraphina firmly confirmed, "I am," when asked whether she would marry Lucien. Once Garran was gone, Lucien confronted Seraphina over finding her physically close to Garran. He caught her by the hair in a recognized vampiric demand for deference, escalated the grip almost to pain, and pinned her against the wall when she initially said she could not explain. Seraphina yielded rather than fighting and admitted she did not know whether she wanted Garran to kiss her; she explained that she had wanted Garran to rescue her from her former coven, Garran rejected her, and Lucien offered what she wanted, so she chose Lucien. When Lucien presses the deeper question—whether she would still choose him if Garran now offered everything she once wanted—Seraphina answers unequivocally: "Yes. Every time. You know what you offer me. I would be a fool to turn it down for a fantasy a desperate woman conjured." Lucien therefore has a renewed, explicit declaration that Seraphina chooses him over the imagined life she once projected onto Garran, even while her physical attraction or unresolved responsiveness to Garran may still exist. Her answer frames the old rescue-dream as a fantasy born from desperation rather than something she would now exchange Lucien for. Her immediate yielding to Lucien remains deliberate but does not establish blanket future consent. Garran has left and is not established as witnessing this. Seraphina still has the anonymous message unless she chooses otherwise. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
