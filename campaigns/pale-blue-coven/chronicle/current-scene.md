# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina works alone in the poorer lanes of Bellwater after sending Sir Garran Vale to find food. Crouched beside a one-legged elderly beggar, she keeps one finger on the coin offered in his cup and asks when he last heard Saint Orra's covert three-ring handbell signal. The beggar says the most recent ringing was the previous night, late after the district had quieted. He saw a single covered cart take the rear lane afterward; it arrived loaded heavily enough to ride low and departed riding higher, indicating that something substantial was unloaded at Saint Orra's. He did not see what was under the covering or identify the men. This timing means activity at Saint Orra's occurred during the same night Seraphina and Garran were disrupting the necromancer and tithe-pit operation, making the chapel an immediate lead rather than merely an old connection. Seraphina has still not shown the tally slip or mentioned the split-bell seal, and she has not yet released the offered coin.
