# Current scene

At the tithe pit north beyond the old gallows road, Garran is interrogating the captured armed overseer while Seraphina patrols the prisoners and perimeter. The overseer has given a lead toward Bellwater, specifically an old tannery on the east bank, though the larger operation remains unidentified. Seraphina notices one wounded laborer covertly reaching beneath a cart. She steps onto his forearm with her muddy pump; her light weight does not seriously hurt him, but it stops the reach. Crouching to inspect beneath the axle, she finds what he was reaching for: a narrow utility knife with a worn wooden handle, hidden in the mud beneath the cart and positioned within his reach. His intended use is not yet established.
