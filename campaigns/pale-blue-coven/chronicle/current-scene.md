# Current scene

Late in the working day on the 19th of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale occupy the concealed observation position she identified overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Seraphina tells Garran, 'You strike me as someone who puts their duty above all else. If you are not at your best, then you miss things. And suddenly you have a little bossy lady telling you what to do.' Garran hears this as another openly personal but practical expression of her concern for his condition, delivered with teasing self-awareness. The legitimate working day is ending and the district continues to thin around Saint Orra's; no covert three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet occurred. Garran does not know Seraphina fed on a warehouse laborer while he slept.
