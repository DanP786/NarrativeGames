# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has identified Seraphina as a vampire and holds his sword lowered but still in hand while she remains backed against the wall. Seraphina has admitted feeding on a sober laborer while Garran slept. Asked what condition she left him in, she says, 'He was alive and unconscious. Hidden behind a barrel.' Garran now knows the laborer survived the feeding but was left unconscious and concealed rather than given aid. He still does not know why Seraphina selected this particular man or exactly how much blood she took, though her description confirms substantial harm despite her stopping short of death. Her continued candor gives Garran specific incriminating facts he could not otherwise easily verify. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
