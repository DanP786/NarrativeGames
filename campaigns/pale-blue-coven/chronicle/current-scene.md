# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has privately warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Seraphina has explained that she and Lucien maintain willing thralls whose feeding role is known only within their household as far as she knows. When Garran considers Lucien among the possible beneficiaries of Seraphina's death, Seraphina answers, "You know almost nothing about me. If Lucien wanted me dead he could do it himself. He gains nothing if I die." She rejects Lucien as a plausible architect of the attempt to turn Garran against her and points out that Garran's knowledge of her is itself limited. Garran still does not know Seraphina and Lucien are engaged, about their blood-oath or prospective queenship, or that Seraphina recently declared she no longer loves Garran. Lucien remains at the goldsmith's shop and has not been established as seeing Seraphina leave. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
