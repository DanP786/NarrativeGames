# Current scene

Late at night after leaving Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, Seraphina wanders for hours along country roads and eventually chooses a narrow road leading into dense old woodland. Far inside the trees she finds a deliberately placed object hanging from a low oak branch beside the road and approaches to inspect it.

The object is a small crude human-shaped figure made from pale cloth wrapped around bundled twigs and tied with coarse cord. Its head is featureless except for a dark iron nail driven through the cloth where the mouth would be. A small tuft of animal hair is bound at its chest with red thread. The cloth is weather-stained rather than fresh, and the cord and surrounding branch show it was deliberately tied there rather than caught by accident. No established supernatural effect is apparent from simple inspection, and its exact maker, purpose and meaning remain unknown. The road continues deeper into the forest beyond it.

Seraphina wears her civilian black travelling ensemble and black cloak and carries her original fighting daggers. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
