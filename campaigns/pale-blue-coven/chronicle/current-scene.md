# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane at the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. After confirming that the Saint Orra cart is unloading crates of cleaned human bones into the low stone building, Seraphina eases back from her close position until she can be seen by Garran's approach without exposing herself to the yard. She does not turn to look for him; instead she keeps her eyes and ears fixed on the three visible humans and the unloading. Garran spots her and closes the distance carefully, reaching her concealed position. Seraphina remains focused on the yard while he joins her. The hooded driver and two workers continue carrying crates through the rear door; one worker has a shielded lantern. A cellar-level opening or loading hatch remains visible near the building's rear wall. Neither Seraphina nor Garran has been detected.
