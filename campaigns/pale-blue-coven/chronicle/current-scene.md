# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Sir Garran Vale leaves after Seraphina publicly confirms her present choice of Lucien Veyne and the life he offers, while admitting that what she truly wants does not exist. Garran has accepted her choice without demanding she betray Lucien and has left her knowing she can find him if she chooses continued contact or involvement in the unresolved Hadrik investigation. Seraphina watches Garran depart, then turns into Lucien's chest and closes her eyes. The gesture is voluntary and seeks Lucien's closeness immediately after the painful encounter. Lucien heard Seraphina distinguish between what she wants and what she chooses, but cannot know her private reasoning unless she tells him. Garran now knows Seraphina and Lucien are lovers and that Lucien intends to build some larger enterprise with her, but he still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, the blood-oath, the prospective-queen title, or the exact nature and scale of the new coven.
