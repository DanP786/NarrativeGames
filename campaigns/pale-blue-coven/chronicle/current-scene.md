# Current scene

Late afternoon has come after Seraphina left Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, wandered through the previous night and took shelter at dawn in a rough timber hut deep in old woodland. Its human occupant, Edric, is a broad-shouldered weathered man in his early forties. He gave Seraphina the hut's narrow bed and kept the shutters closed through the day. He does not know she is a vampire.

Seraphina gave Edric her name and voluntarily surrendered her original fighting daggers into his keeping while she sheltered under his roof. Waking as the short early-autumn daylight begins to fail, she tells Edric she is ready to keep moving. Edric returns her original daggers handle-first. As Seraphina dresses again in her travelling clothes, boots, leather harnesses and black cloak, she refuses his offer of food and instructs him that if anyone comes looking for her he should tell them everything rather than risk himself for her sake. She adds that unless the seeker goes by "Sir Vale," Edric should not trust his own safety. This is the first time Edric has heard that name from Seraphina; she gives him no further explanation before preparing to leave.

The last daylight is failing outside the shuttered hut. Seraphina has her original daggers back in her possession and is dressing to resume her wandering. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
