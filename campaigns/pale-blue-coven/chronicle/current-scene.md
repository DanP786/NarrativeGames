# Current scene

Late in the working day on the 19th of Harvestwane, Seraphina is browsing a Bellwater jeweller's wares while continuing her intermittent surveillance of the approaches to Saint Orra's. She is inspecting a pearlescent jewellery box when Sir Garran Vale finds her after several hours of rest. He is visibly better rested than before and has cleaned away most of the grime from the previous night's fighting and investigation, though he remains equipped for work. Seraphina can report that her daylight checks showed only ordinary public activity: no three-bell signal, covered rear-lane cart, or clearly suspicious transfer. Her circulating watch has identified several useful after-dark observation positions around the rear approach without establishing an obvious surveillance pattern. Garran does not know that Seraphina fed on a warehouse laborer while he slept. The legitimate working day is now winding down, bringing them closer to the period when the covert Saint Orra traffic may begin.
