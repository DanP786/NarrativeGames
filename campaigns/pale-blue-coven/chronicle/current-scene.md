# Current scene

Nightfall on the 20th of Harvestwane in Bellwater. After feeding deeply but nonlethally from the drunken man who had cornered a serving girl, Seraphina asks to watch Lucien Veyne feed and willingly follows at a slow pace while he hunts. Lucien has socially lured a prosperous, wine-flushed man in a fur-edged coat away from the busier street and into a narrow passage between a shuttered chandlery and warehouse. Seraphina trails them closely enough to observe without interrupting. Lucien now has the mortal isolated from ordinary street traffic; his actual feeding method, depth of feeding, and whether he leaves this prey alive remain to be established. Garran still does not know Seraphina has returned to Bellwater.
