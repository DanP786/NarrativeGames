# Current scene

At the tithe pit north beyond the old gallows road, Garran and Seraphina have subdued the immediate bone-harvesting crew. The armed overseer has revealed that the necromancer killed at the croft was only a customer rather than the master of the larger operation. Garran notices Seraphina is watching him instead of showing the morbid fascination with necromancy she previously claimed and asks why. Seraphina sighs and admits a simpler motive: she heard a knight was alone out here and wanted to help. She says she watches Garran so he can conduct his investigation without needing to watch his back. The explanation fits her actions better than her earlier story, but leaves Garran with unanswered questions about how she heard of him and why she chose to risk herself for a stranger.
