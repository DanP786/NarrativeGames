# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane at the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. Concealed beside Garran, Seraphina keeps her eyes on the yard and quietly tells him that she saw inside only one crate and it contained bones. Garran accepts the limited report without pressing her to speculate. The hooded driver and two workers continue unloading rough crates from the Saint Orra cart and carrying them through the low stone building's rear door. The cellar-level opening or loading hatch remains visible near the rear wall. Neither Seraphina nor Garran has been detected, and they still do not know the occupants' identities, the full contents of the cart, or what lies below the building.
