# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina is inside Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard, investigating alone under the false story that her mistress is seeking a missing woman named Raquelle. While the lay burial keeper is inside the chapel consulting the intake ledger, Seraphina immediately crosses to the rear stone-and-timber service outbuilding and slips inside without being seen. The building is a body-handling room: stone floor, drainage channel, trestle tables, burial cloth, lime, hooks and washing buckets. The previous night's cart delivery is partly concealed beneath coarse sacking at the rear. Seraphina discovers bundled human remains there, including bones and dismembered body parts rather than intact pauper corpses; several pieces bear fresh cut or tool marks consistent with deliberate processing. She also sees a wooden transport crate stamped with the same vertically split-bell emblem as the tally slip found in the Bellwater tannery. This establishes a direct material connection between Saint Orra's covert rear-yard activity and the tannery clue, but not who controls the operation or the final destination/use of the remains. The keeper is still inside the chapel and may return soon. Sir Garran Vale remains elsewhere in Bellwater.
