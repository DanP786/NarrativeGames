# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale are together inside the old tannery on Bellwater's east bank. After finding a tally slip bearing a split-bell seal and the notation 'Saint Orra — third bell,' Seraphina stands and says she is not well versed in saints. Garran identifies Saint Orra only from ordinary regional knowledge as a saint associated with burial and care of the unclaimed dead; he does not yet know whether the notation refers to a church, shrine, institution, meeting place, or code. The 'third bell' likewise remains ambiguous and requires further investigation. The recently cleared tannery, bone dust, and tally slip remain the strongest evidence toward the organization buying harvested remains.
