# Current scene

Seraphina has returned to the Pale Court in the late deep night after leaving Sir Garran Vale at the tithe pit. Lady Yseld Veyr intercepts her and orders her into a private room to report. Seraphina obeys, closes the door, and shows no concern for the state of her badly muddied and bloodstained black-and-pale-blue gown or muddy pumps. She possesses the substance of the information Yseld ordered her to gather: the necromancer is dead; his intended raising involved roughly two dozen corpses; the tithe pit supplied human remains; a larger trade in bones exists with an old tannery on Bellwater's east bank as a collection point; and another hunter, Sir Garran Vale, was pursuing the necromancer. Yseld does not yet know any of these results, Garran's identity, Seraphina's cooperation with him, or Seraphina's feelings for him. Yseld is now beginning the private debrief.
