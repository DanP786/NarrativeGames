# Current scene

At the tithe pit north beyond the old gallows road, Garran and Seraphina have broken the immediate resistance of a nighttime crew harvesting bones from the ancient mass grave. Seraphina disabled fleeing workers with precise nonlethal dagger cuts as Garran requested. The armed overseer is subdued and has revealed that the necromancer killed at the croft was only a customer rather than the master of the larger operation. Seraphina shows little interest in solving that mystery; instead, while Garran secures the prisoners and begins pursuing answers, she watches him. Garran notices that her attention does not match the morbid fascination with necromancy she claimed as her reason for being here, though he does not know why she is focused on him.
