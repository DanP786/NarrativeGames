# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. Seraphina catches the fleeing wounded swordsman just outside the rear door. She uses her second dagger to slash through a tendon in his leg, then drives bodily into him. Both collapse hard onto the yard ground. Seraphina rolls away from the impact, but the man's remaining good arm catches and locks around her dagger wrist before she can disengage. His cut sword arm is already badly impaired and the newly severed leg tendon prevents effective flight, but he remains substantially larger and stronger than Seraphina and can still contest her dagger arm with his good hand. Seraphina's crossbow wound is healing faster after her brief feed but remains painful and incompletely closed; the collision jars it. Her fangs remain exposed and blood from the bound prisoner is still on her mouth. The bound cellar worker remains alive below after the brief feeding. Garran remains farther down in the burial works dealing with the crossbow position and has not witnessed this fight or the feeding. The other wounded worker remains alive below with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee.
