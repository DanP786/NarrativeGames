# Current scene

After a day spent on household duties, confronting Frederick about Bellwater and encouraging the planting of Veyne Hall's neglected garden, Seraphina retires early to her private rooms. After her bath, she sits at the armoire in a linen nightgown with her hair arranged into soft curls. Lucien knocks and enters only after she invites him in. He acknowledges that his attempted return of the ring earlier became another test and begins asking what Seraphina had wanted from him at each stage of their dispute. Seraphina says that after Bellwater she simply did not want him to end their engagement, which he did not. She explains that her later campaign of dresses, teasing and chosen private deference was meant to show that she still chose him. Seraphina tells Lucien that although she understands trust takes time to earn, he never told her how she could earn his trust back, so she did what she knew how to do. She then tells him she no longer knows whether she wants the ring: he hurt her, giving herself to him was something she enjoyed, and his asking what more she intended to do after she offered that chosen intimacy was an insult.

The engagement between Seraphina and Lucien remains intact, but Seraphina has now explicitly said she is uncertain whether she wants the betrothal ring back. Lucien still possesses the gem-encrusted ring. Seraphina remains free to move within Veyne Hall. Frederick remains injured but recovering, and he and Seraphina have now confronted the question of responsibility for Bellwater and his punishment. Christian now has an expected channel for garden requests backed directly by Seraphina. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
