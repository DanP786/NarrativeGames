# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane. Seraphina obeys Garran's direction and shadows the heavily laden covered cart that departed Saint Orra after the three-bell signal. Using the dark edges of the road and maintaining distance, she keeps the cart in sight without approaching closely enough to expose herself; Garran follows farther back, relying on her to hold the trail. The cart leaves the denser Bellwater streets and continues along a rougher road past scattered sheds and fenced plots. Seraphina notices that the driver does not use a lantern despite the darkness and that the cart avoids the more travelled road where possible. No obvious escort has revealed itself. The cart has not yet reached its destination. Garran and Seraphina remain operationally together, their personal conflict unresolved.
