# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has Seraphina backed against a wall with his sword drawn after identifying her as a vampire. He has learned that her coven knows she has followed him, but that Seraphina began doing so before the coven knew about him. When Garran asks how long she had been watching him before their direct meeting, Seraphina swallows hard and whispers, 'A year.' Garran now knows her attention to him predates their meeting by roughly a year and began independently of any coven assignment. The duration makes her claim of love harder for him to dismiss as an improvised cover, but it is also deeply unsettling: an unknown vampire secretly tracked him for a year. Seraphina remains backed against the wall with her hands visible and away from her daggers. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
