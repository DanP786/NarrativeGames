# Current scene

At the ruined croft north of the old gallows road, Seraphina remains concealed and watches Sir Garran Vale fight the necromancer's risen dead. Her observation shows the danger worsening: Garran is capable and has destroyed several corpses, but more are emerging from both the croft and disturbed ground around him, beginning to form a loose encirclement. A newly risen corpse approaches his rear while he is occupied with threats ahead. Seraphina also identifies the necromancer's likely position inside or immediately behind the croft, where the reddish ritual light and movement are concentrated. Garran remains unaware of Seraphina's presence.
