# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale remain outside a modest Bellwater cookshop after agreeing that Saint Orra's rear gate should be watched rather than confronted openly. Seeing that Garran is still carrying the effects of a sleepless night despite having eaten, Seraphina suggests that he sleep for a while and let her watch the surrounding streets until the working day ends. Garran recognizes the practical merit: the covert third-bell traffic is associated with the period after Saint Orra's legitimate public work, and an exhausted knight is a liability during a potentially long night surveillance and pursuit. He accepts a limited period of rest rather than insisting on remaining awake, while making clear that Seraphina is to observe only and not confront or follow any unexpected movement alone before he returns. Seraphina is positioned to spend the remaining working hours discreetly watching the approaches around Saint Orra's and identifying useful sightlines while Garran rests nearby in Bellwater.
