# Current scene

At the tithe pit north beyond the old gallows road, Garran has finished interrogating the captured bone-harvesting crew and has a lead toward an old tannery on Bellwater's east bank. The prisoners remain alive and Garran intends to turn them over to lawful custody. After he thanks Seraphina for saving him, preserving his prisoners, and watching his back, Garran offers his hand. Seraphina privately knows that touching him risks revealing the unnatural coldness of her skin, so she does not take it. Looking at him from beneath heavy eyelashes, she says, 'You're welcome, Sir Vale,' then begins walking away with, 'Good luck with the rest of your investigation.' Garran does not know why she refused the handshake.
