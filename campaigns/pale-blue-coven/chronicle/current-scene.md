# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has identified Seraphina as a vampire and holds his sword lowered but still in hand while she remains backed against the wall. He knows she followed him for roughly a year, says she wanted only to know he was safe, and claims she deliberately leaves feeding victims alive. When Garran asks when she last fed, Seraphina scowls as if the answer hurts, tears welling in her eyes, and admits, 'While you slept, a labourer.' Garran now knows she fed on a laborer during the period when he was resting and she was ostensibly handling the watch. He does not yet know the victim was a slight sober warehouse laborer, that Seraphina attacked and drank deeply, or that she deliberately stopped short of killing him and left him alive but profoundly weakened. The admission is particularly charged because Garran had entrusted himself and the watch to Seraphina during that rest. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
