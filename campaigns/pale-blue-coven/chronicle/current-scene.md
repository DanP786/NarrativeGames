# Current scene

At the tithe pit north beyond the old gallows road, Garran has challenged a nighttime crew harvesting bones from the ancient mass grave. At least five men were present. Their armed overseer recognized Garran's name, ordered the others to run, drew steel, and rushed him. Seraphina obeys Garran's instruction to stop fleeing suspects without killing them: she pursues the runners with predatory speed and uses precise dagger cuts to injure legs and otherwise prevent escape rather than opening throats. Multiple fleeing workers are now wounded and unable to continue running. Garran remains engaged with the armed overseer in the pit; the confrontation is active, and Seraphina's extraordinary speed and precision are visible enough to deepen Garran's questions about her.
