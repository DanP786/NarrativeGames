# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has Seraphina backed against a wall with his sword drawn after identifying her as a vampire. After learning that her coven knows she has been following him, he asks whether she was following him before the coven knew about him. Seraphina swallows and answers, 'Before.' Garran now knows her original interest in him predated the coven's awareness and therefore was not initially an assignment. This weakens his theory that her entire approach was ordered surveillance, though it does not tell him why the coven later permitted or directed continued contact. It also forces him to reconsider her declaration of love without yet requiring him to believe it. Seraphina remains backed against the wall, hands visible and away from her daggers. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
