# Current scene

Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale overlook the tithe pit north beyond the old gallows road: an enormous old mass-burial trench with hundreds of bones exposed by erosion. A crown-marked stone matches the necromancer's ledger symbol. After hearing metal scrape against stone and seeing a lantern on the opposite ridge, Seraphina steps directly behind Garran with her dagger ready and peers around him. From cover they can now distinguish more than one human figure below: at least three lantern-bearing workers moving among the exposed bones, using tools to open or disturb the old burial layers. Their allegiance and exact purpose are not yet established. Garran is holding position rather than charging in, trying to determine what they are facing.
