# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane at the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. Seraphina creeps closer while Garran remains farther back and successfully reaches a concealed position near a damaged section of boundary wall without being detected. From there she sees the Saint Orra cart being uncovered and unloaded. The load consists of several rough wooden crates and bundled material; one opened crate visibly contains cleaned human bones packed tightly together. Three human figures are presently visible inside the yard: the hooded cart driver and two workers from the low stone building. One worker carries a shielded lantern. The crates are being taken through the building's rear door. Seraphina also notices a narrow cellar-level opening or loading hatch near the rear wall of the building, suggesting material may be moved below ground. Garran has not yet reached Seraphina's close observation position. No one in the yard appears aware of her.
