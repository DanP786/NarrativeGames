# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina remains inside Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard under the false story that her mistress is seeking a missing woman named Raquelle. She follows the lay burial keeper to a cool stone holding room adjoining the chapel where recent unclaimed dead are kept before burial. The unidentified brown-haired woman entered in the public ledger two nights earlier is physically present and intact enough for ordinary identification; she has several missing teeth and is around Seraphina's height or slightly taller, accidentally matching Seraphina's invented description closely. The body therefore does not itself prove falsification of Saint Orra's public intake record. However, while the keeper exposes the woman's face for identification, Seraphina can see other shrouded bodies in the holding room and a separate internal service door leading toward the rear work-yard side of the complex. The covert processed remains and split-bell-marked crate Seraphina discovered in the rear service outbuilding remain unexplained. The keeper waits for Seraphina to say whether the dead woman is the supposed Raquelle. Sir Garran Vale remains elsewhere in Bellwater.
