# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the burial tunnels beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina, still suffering pounding pain and impaired balance from striking her head against the cellar wall, pursues the escaped worker. She throws her first dagger and strikes the back of his knee, dropping him alive in the passage. Without stopping, she uses his collapsing body as a springboard, draws her second dagger in the leap, and attacks the lantern-bearing man who had emerged ahead with a short heavy blade. Her speed and agility carry her inside his preferred striking distance before he can set himself. He swings anyway; Seraphina avoids the full blow but the heavy blade catches and cuts across the outer leather at her left upper arm rather than landing cleanly. She collides with him and drives him backward into the burial-chamber wall, knocking the lantern from his hand. The lantern remains lit on the stone floor. Seraphina is now at close dagger range with the armed man, while the escaped worker lies behind her with a dagger lodged behind his knee. Garran remains farther back with the second prisoner and has not yet reached this chamber.
