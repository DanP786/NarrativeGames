# Current scene

Late afternoon has come after Seraphina left Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, wandered through the previous night and took shelter at dawn in a rough timber hut deep in old woodland. Its human occupant, Edric, is a broad-shouldered weathered man in his early forties. He gave Seraphina the hut's narrow bed and kept the shutters closed through the day. He does not know she is a vampire.

Seraphina gave Edric her name and voluntarily surrendered her original fighting daggers into his keeping while she shelters under his roof; they remain on the table unless otherwise established. She removed her boots, black cloak and leather travelling harnesses, leaving herself in comfortable cloth layers. After telling Edric that only one man might be looking for her and that he would not hurt Edric, Seraphina finally slept. She remains asleep until late afternoon, when the short early-autumn daylight is beginning to fail.

The hut's shutters have kept the day's direct light out. Seraphina's removed travelling gear remains inside the hut. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
