# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale sit outside a modest Bellwater cookshop after she reports her full Saint Orra investigation. Asked whether to pursue records or watch the covert traffic directly, Seraphina says she would watch Saint Orra's rear gate. Garran agrees that surveillance offers the best chance to identify the next link without alerting whoever controls the body trade. Because the beggar described the covert three-ring handbell and cart traffic as occurring after Saint Orra's public burial work is finished and late enough that respectable streets are quiet, Garran does not propose visibly occupying the rear lane all day. His plan is to inspect the surrounding approaches separately while there is daylight, choose concealed observation positions that do not expose a conspicuous knight beside Seraphina, then return for the evening/night watch. Until then they need to avoid warning Saint Orra's or revealing that its rear operation has been discovered. Garran remains tired from the sleepless previous night despite having eaten, and the coming surveillance may require hours of waiting.
