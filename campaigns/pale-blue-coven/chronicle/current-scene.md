# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane at the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. Concealed beside Garran, Seraphina's jaw tightens as they continue watching in silence. The Saint Orra cart is now empty and sits higher on its axle. The hooded driver and the lantern-bearing worker confer over a small tally board or ledger. Seraphina and Garran overhear that seven loads were delivered: six described as bone and one as fresh; the driver states the fresh one was whole when loaded. Garran decides they will let the driver leave and then find the seventh load rather than expose themselves prematurely. Seraphina says nothing and continues watching. The driver is preparing to depart while the two workers remain associated with the low stone building. Neither Seraphina nor Garran has been detected.
