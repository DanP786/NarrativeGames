# Current scene

Late in the working day on the 19th of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale maintain their concealed watch over Saint Orra's rear service approach. When Garran asks whether she too is sworn to fight monsters, Seraphina studies him, caught by a conversation she had not prepared for, and answers only, 'I fight monsters, yes.' The statement is literally true but deliberately avoids answering whether she is sworn to do so: Seraphina is sometimes pitted against members of the Pale Court to test her fighting skills and has fought vampires of rival covens because vampire covens are highly territorial. Garran does not know this context and can hear only the careful distinction in her answer. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared. Garran still does not know Seraphina is a vampire, belongs to the Pale Court, has followed him since before their first meeting, loves him, or fed on a warehouse laborer while he slept.
