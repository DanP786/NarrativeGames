# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has privately warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Their discussion has shifted to why Seraphina left her former coven. Seraphina has said she followed Garran and fell in love with him because she believed he could save her. I pleased whoever my master wanted me to please."
