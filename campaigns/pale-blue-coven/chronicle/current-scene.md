# Current scene

After completing her report to Lady Yseld Veyr, Seraphina takes her time washing away the grave mud and blood from the night's fighting. She sleeps for several hours at the Pale Court, then rises on the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane. She dresses in her usual feminine but practical black leather armor, returning to the style she normally favors after the previous night's ruined gown. Yseld has ordered her to accept the unnamed knight's invitation and remain close enough to gather intelligence while concealing the Pale Court and her vampiric nature. Seraphina knows the knight is Sir Garran Vale but has concealed that fact from Yseld. Garran's established actionable lead remains the old tannery on Bellwater's east bank; Seraphina had referred to the next destination as 'Blackwell' in her report, but Blackwell has not otherwise been established in canon.
