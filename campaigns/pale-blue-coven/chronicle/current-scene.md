# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in an old burial chamber beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina's collision with the lantern-bearing armed man carries both of them down onto the stone floor, with the larger man landing on top of her. She slashes his sword arm with her remaining dagger, damaging his ability to use the heavy blade, but he clamps his free forearm and hand around her throat and uses his weight to pin her. Seraphina chokes and claws at his face with her free hand while kicking beneath him, unable to simply overpower his much greater mass. Her earlier head injury continues to impair her. The escaped worker remains alive behind them with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee. The fallen lantern still burns nearby. Garran reaches the chamber during the struggle and sees Seraphina pinned and choking beneath the armed man; he is moving to intervene.
