# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has privately warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Their discussion has shifted to Seraphina's former coven role and how she came to join Lucien. Seraphina has told Garran that her former master assigned her to please whoever the master wanted, that she originally followed and fell in love with Garran because she believed he could save her, and that Lucien knows this history but does not assign her sexually to others; she says she gives herself to Lucien willingly. Seraphina now tells Garran that Lucien first found her injured in the Bellwater bone yard and offered her a way out, which she refused. She says that while she was later returning home Lucien "took" her, offered again, and she accepted. Garran therefore now knows Lucien's recruitment involved an initial refused offer followed by Lucien forcibly taking or abducting Seraphina before making the second offer she accepted; Seraphina has not yet explained the exact conditions of that captivity or how free she was to refuse the second time. Garran does not know Seraphina and Lucien are engaged, blood-bound, or planning a new coven with Seraphina as prospective queen, nor that Seraphina recently declared she no longer loves Garran. Lucien remains at the goldsmith's shop and has not been established as seeing Seraphina leave. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
