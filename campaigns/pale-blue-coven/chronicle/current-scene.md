# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the neglected yard outside the corpse-processing site beyond Bellwater. Seraphina has washed her hands and face, cleaned and rebound the substantially healed crossbow wound in her side, and braided her honey-blonde hair down her back. Garran has now come outside after finishing below. Seraphina rises to face him and waits silently for his verdict, eyes lowered and expression solemn. Both fighting daggers remain sheathed. Her posture gives Garran no immediate resistance or attempt to flee, echoing the deference he has repeatedly observed without knowing its origin. Garran knows Seraphina fed from both the bound cellar worker and the swordsman in the yard, but he does not know she drank her fill from the swordsman, whether feeding materially contributed to his death, or that an unidentified rival vampire approached her before his arrival. The wounded worker remains alive, the bound cellar worker remains alive, and the captured archer remains alive, hurt, disarmed, and bound below. The swordsman in the yard is dead. The rival vampire has departed.
