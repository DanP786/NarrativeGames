# Current scene

In Bellwater's crowded night market, Seraphina waits outside a goldsmith's shop while Lucien Veyne conducts business inside. Two coordinated watchers have been suspected but remain unidentified. Sir Garran Vale appears and confirms he too has been watching the grey-coated man. Seraphina scowls and tells Garran that Lucien is inside the shop. Garran replies, "Good, come with me. You're impossible to get alone," takes Seraphina by the wrist, and leads her away from the market crowd toward somewhere quieter. Because of the old softness and habitual responsiveness Seraphina still has toward Garran despite having recently declared to Lucien that she no longer loves Garran, she does not resist and allows Garran to pull her away. Lucien remains inside the goldsmith's shop and has not been established as seeing them leave. Garran knows Seraphina voluntarily joined Lucien, that they are lovers, and that Lucien intends to build something larger with her, but he does not know Seraphina and Lucien are now engaged, about their blood-oath or prospective queenship, or the full new-coven plan. The watchers' allegiance and Garran's reason for urgently seeking privacy with Seraphina remain unresolved.
