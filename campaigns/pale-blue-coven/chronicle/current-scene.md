# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne face Sir Garran Vale. Garran has said that before deciding what his oath requires he needs truth about Seraphina's feeding restraint and what happened to the swordsman who attempted to sexually assault her. Seraphina pulls Lucien's arm away and deliberately steps out from his protective hold toward Garran. She admits the swordsman was alive when she began feeding from him but states that the injuries she inflicted while escaping his assault were already going to kill him. She then challenges Garran on what viable feeding conduct he expects from her, saying she does not kill when she feeds yet he takes issue with the circumstances around it, and asks whether he wants her to take a willing thrall instead. Seraphina glances back at Lucien while advancing but makes the choice to approach Garran herself. Garran now knows Seraphina admits feeding from the still-living but already mortally wounded swordsman. He must distinguish the necessity of her feeding from the questions of consent, predation, and lethal responsibility rather than assuming the feeding itself caused the man's death. Garran knows Lucien's name and vampire nature but still does not know Lucien abducted Seraphina, that she later willingly chose him and completed a blood-oath, or that they are building a prospective coven.
