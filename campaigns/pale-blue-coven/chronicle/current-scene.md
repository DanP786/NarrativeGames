# Current scene

Nightfall on the 20th of Harvestwane in Bellwater. Seraphina watches from the shadows as Lucien Veyne feeds on the prosperous, wine-flushed man he socially lured into the narrow passage between a shuttered chandlery and warehouse. Lucien controls the mortal quickly and efficiently, bites into his neck, and drinks while keeping him upright and quiet. He takes a substantial feed but stops before killing the man, leaving him alive, weakened, dazed, and slumped safely out of the main street. Seraphina has now seen that Lucien's hunting style relies first on social confidence and manipulation, then sudden physical control, and that on this occasion he chooses not to kill his prey. Garran still does not know Seraphina has returned to Bellwater.
