# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina works alone in the poorer lanes of Bellwater after sending Sir Garran Vale to find food. Crouched beside a one-legged elderly beggar who did not see her with Garran, she puts her coin purse into his begging cup but keeps hold of it and asks, 'The third bell?' The beggar explains that 'third bell' is not simply the chapel's ordinary service time: on certain nights, after Saint Orra's public burial work is finished, a handbell is rung three times from inside the paupers' yard. Carts have sometimes arrived by the rear lane shortly afterward, covered and late, and men unload or remove things without using the chapel's public gate. The beggar has seen the activity from the street but does not claim to know what is carried or who ultimately controls it. Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard farther inland is now a concrete lead. Seraphina has still not shown him the tally slip or mentioned the split-bell seal. Her exact payment has not yet been established because she has not released the purse.
