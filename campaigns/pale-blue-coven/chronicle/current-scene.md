# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. After the unidentified rival vampire withdraws, Seraphina crawls to the severely wounded swordsman who attempted to sexually assault her. He is already dying from the wounds she inflicted. Seraphina bites him and drinks her fill rather than stopping short as she usually does. His remaining blood substantially feeds her and accelerates the healing of her crossbow wound and other injuries, though healing is not instantaneous. Whether the feeding itself or his existing wounds are the final cause of death need not be distinguished: after Seraphina finishes, the swordsman is dead. Seraphina remains armed with her bloodied second dagger. The rival vampire has departed the immediate yard after learning Seraphina belongs to the Pale Court, is acting independently at Bellwater, and is cooperating with a mortal monster-hunter. The bound cellar worker remains alive below after Seraphina's earlier brief feeding. Garran remains somewhere in the burial works and has not witnessed either feeding, the yard struggle, or the rival-vampire encounter. The other wounded worker remains alive below with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee.
