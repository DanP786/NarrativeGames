# Current scene

Inside the ruined croft north of the old gallows road, Garran questions the mortally wounded necromancer while Seraphina moves to inspect the source of the ritual's reddish light. She finds a crude working circle of grave soil, bones, black candles, blood and an iron brazier whose coals had produced the red glow; the ritual is faltering rather than actively building. Nearby she discovers a dirt- and blood-marked ledger or working book containing notes on corpse collection and the raising. The visible entries indicate the disturbed public graveyards were not the necromancer's only source: some bodies are marked as coming from an additional source described in shorthand as 'the tithe pit.' The meaning and location of the tithe pit are not yet established. Garran remains occupied with the necromancer behind her; remaining risen corpses outside are disordered but not all conclusively destroyed.
