# Current scene

Several days after returning from Bellwater, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne are together intimately in his bed at Veyne Hall after Lucien asks Seraphina to become his wife. Seraphina has challenged whether his proposal is partly driven by feeling threatened by her continuing love for Garran and has told Lucien that Garran has never had or kissed her, and that their recent Bellwater encounter was the first time Garran touched her. After first answering a hypothetical version of Lucien's question affirmatively, Seraphina is asked directly whether the woman she is now—who still loves Garran and nevertheless chose Lucien—will marry him. Seraphina answers, "Yes." Seraphina has now explicitly accepted Lucien Veyne's marriage proposal with both of them acknowledging that her continuing love for Garran has not vanished. They are engaged to marry; the form, timing, witnesses, rites, legal standing, and any public announcement remain undecided.
