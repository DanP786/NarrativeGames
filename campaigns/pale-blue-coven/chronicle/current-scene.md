# Current scene

Late at night after leaving Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, Seraphina wanders for hours along country roads and eventually enters dense old woodland. She finds a deliberately hung cloth-and-twig human figure beside the road, its mouth marked by an iron nail and animal hair tied to its chest with red thread. While inspecting it, she hears a repeated wooden knocking deeper in the forest and chooses to follow the sound.

The knocking leads her away from the road along a faint path through wet undergrowth. Deeper among the trees she reaches sight of a small clearing containing a rough timber hut or woodland shelter. A lantern burns dimly beside its closed door. The repeated sound comes from a person-shaped figure standing at a chopping block near the hut, rhythmically bringing a small axe or hatchet down onto something on the block. At Seraphina's present distance and in the poor light, the figure's identity and what is being chopped are not yet established.

Seraphina wears her civilian black travelling ensemble and black cloak and carries her original fighting daggers. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
