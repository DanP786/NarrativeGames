# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne unexpectedly encounter Sir Garran Vale. Both vampires are dressed in ordinary travel clothes, and Lucien had an arm around Seraphina's waist before Garran called, "Sera!" Lucien instinctively pulled Seraphina behind himself and interposed his body between her and the knight. Garran says he has been looking for her. Seraphina steps partly to one side and answers, "Why? So you can finally kill me?" exposing that she still interprets their last unresolved encounter through the expectation that Garran may ultimately decide his oath requires her death. Lucien immediately shifts with her and conceals her behind himself again, treating Garran as a potential lethal threat. Garran has not drawn his weapon and knows only that Seraphina is unexpectedly alive, in Bellwater, and accompanied by a protective unknown man; he does not know Lucien is a vampire, that Lucien abducted Seraphina after Garran let her leave, that Seraphina subsequently chose Lucien and completed a blood-oath with him, or that they are building a prospective coven. Garran's response to Seraphina's accusation remains unresolved.
