# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. After Seraphina answers Garran's question about her experience with monsters by saying only, 'I fight monsters, yes,' her eyes snap to him as he begins putting together the anomalies he has personally observed. Garran draws his sword and confronts her with the accumulating signs: her impossible pursuit speed at the tithe pit, fighting skill and precision, her refusal of his handshake, her apparent indifference to cold while lightly dressed during their first night's work, her deliberate use of a hooded cloak through the daylight, and now the distinctive silvery reflection in her eyes as the light fails, consistent with unnatural night sight. Seraphina steps back without breaking eye contact and listens rather than attacking or fleeing. Garran now strongly concludes that Seraphina is a vampire, though he does not know her coven, her history, her feelings for him, her prior following of him, or that she fed on a warehouse laborer while he slept. His sword is drawn and the confrontation is immediately dangerous. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
