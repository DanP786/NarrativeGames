# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne face Sir Garran Vale. Lucien holds Seraphina with her back against his chest after repeatedly interposing himself when she tries to step into Garran's view. Garran has recognized Lucien as another vampire and, seeing Lucien's possessive protection alongside Seraphina's familiar deference to authority, asks, "Are you her master?" Lucien laughs at the question rather than reacting with offense or immediately giving Garran a simple yes or no. His amusement reflects the mismatch between Garran's assumption and the relationship Lucien and Seraphina have established: Lucien enjoys Seraphina's willing private deference and is possessive of her, but he has chosen her as his prospective queen and expects her judgment and choices to matter. Garran does not know that history and therefore cannot yet tell whether Lucien's laughter means contempt, confirmation, or something more complicated. Garran still does not know Lucien abducted Seraphina after their last encounter, that Seraphina later willingly chose Lucien and completed a blood-oath with him, or that they are building a prospective coven.
