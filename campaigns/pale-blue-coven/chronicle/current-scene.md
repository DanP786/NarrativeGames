# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina stands between Sir Garran Vale and Lucien Veyne after Lucien openly asserts that she chose him, shares his bed, and will stand beside him in the future he intends to build. Garran asks Seraphina directly whether that life is what she wants. Seraphina answers, "What I want doesn't exist. But I choose this." Her answer publicly confirms her present choice of Lucien and the life he offers while also revealing that it is not identical to her deepest desire. Garran knows Seraphina previously declared that she loves him, so he can recognize the possible implication but cannot know with certainty what unattainable thing she means unless she says it. Lucien likewise hears the distinction between desire and choice but cannot read her private reasoning. Garran knows Seraphina and Lucien are lovers and that Lucien intends to build some larger enterprise with her, but he still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, the blood-oath, the prospective-queen title, or the exact nature and scale of the new coven.
