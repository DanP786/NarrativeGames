# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the cellar beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina follows closely behind Garran down the stone stair, dagger held tight along her forearm and attention fixed ahead. They descend without being detected and gain a view into the cellar. The six bone crates are stacked along one wall. The two known workers are at a heavy wooden table farther in. On the table lies the seventh load: the intact body of a recently dead young human man, stripped to the waist, with fresh postmortem cutting begun at the torso. Nearby are knives, a small saw, bowls, cloths, and marked containers consistent with deliberate processing rather than ordinary burial preparation. A second passage or doorway leads deeper beyond the cellar, its interior dark. The workers have not yet noticed Seraphina or Garran.
