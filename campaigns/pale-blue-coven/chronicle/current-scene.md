# Current scene

Several days have passed since Seraphina and Lucien Veyne returned from Bellwater to Veyne Hall. During those days Seraphina remains with Lucien and their shared plans continue, while Sir Garran Vale remains outside their household and the Hadrik investigation is unresolved. In Lucien's bed during consensual intimacy, Seraphina kisses down his stomach. Lucien abruptly interrupts the moment with a direct proposal: "Be my wife." The proposal is distinct from his earlier claim that Seraphina will be queen beside him; he is now asking for marriage as an additional chosen bond. Seraphina has not yet answered.
