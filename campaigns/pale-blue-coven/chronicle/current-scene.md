# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Their private discussion moved through Seraphina's former coven role, her recruitment by Lucien, and Garran's feelings toward her, becoming physically intimate: Seraphina stepped into Garran's space, placed her hands on his chest and leaned against him, while Garran admitted he cares for her and wants to touch and kiss her but insisted she tell him what she wants. Before she answered, Lucien arrived and found Seraphina and Garran together immediately after that closeness. Lucien revealed their engagement to Garran; when Garran asked Seraphina directly whether she would marry Lucien, she answered with certainty, "I am," looked up at Lucien, and smiled. Garran gave Seraphina the anonymous message as evidence and then leaves the passage. Seraphina takes the message. Lucien watches Garran leave, then turns on Seraphina angry at having found her with her arms around Garran. He catches Seraphina by the hair and pulls it back to expose her neck to him, using a recognized vampiric gesture meant to demand deference. The gesture is possessive and disciplinary rather than an attack established as intended to injure her. Seraphina's response to Lucien's anger and demanded deference remains entirely hers to choose. Garran now knows Seraphina and Lucien are betrothed but still does not know they are blood-bound or planning a new coven with Seraphina as prospective queen, nor that Seraphina recently declared to Lucien that she no longer loves Garran. Lucien has the immediate fact that Garran was sent information targeting Seraphina; the exact contents of the message are now physically in Seraphina's possession. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
