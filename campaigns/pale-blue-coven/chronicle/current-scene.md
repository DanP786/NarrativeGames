# Current scene

Several days later at Veyne Hall, Seraphina and Lucien share a private bath before the fire. In the privacy of their rooms, it has become usual for Seraphina to wear nothing but her betrothal ring. Lucien has revealed that before their Bellwater encounters his old master once took him to the Pale Court, where Lucien saw Seraphina and thought her beautiful. Lucien later killed that master after rejecting the belief that making Lucien meant owning him, and he chose not to inherit the old coven. During the Pale Court visit Lady Yseld offered Seraphina's company to Lucien's master as part of the Court's hospitality. Lucien says that when younger he did not fully understand the coercive structure behind what he saw, envied his master, and only later understood it differently. Lucien also reports that afterward his master spoke of Seraphina as a possession or amenity, praising her beauty, training, eagerness to please and ability to understand expectations, and congratulated Yseld on her. Seraphina now asks Lucien, "Did that make you want to be a master? So you were offered people like me?" Whether that experience contributed to Lucien's desire for authority, whether he ever wanted the exploitative privileges his master possessed, and how he distinguishes his current wish to found and lead a coven from his old master's model remain to be established by Lucien's answer.
