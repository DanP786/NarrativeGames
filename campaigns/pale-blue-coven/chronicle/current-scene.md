# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. Seraphina lies on her back beside the dead escaped swordsman while the unusually large feeding continues accelerating her healing. Garran kneels near her after returning from the burial works. Asked what happened, Seraphina gives only the immediate outline: "He ran… so I chased. The archer?" She redirects attention to the deeper threat before explaining the yard struggle, the attempted sexual assault, either feeding, or the rival-vampire encounter. Garran has not yet learned those details and can only infer from the disturbed yard, the corpse, Seraphina's condition, and blood around her mouth. Seraphina's second dagger remains with her; her first remains lodged behind the knee of the other wounded worker below. The bound cellar worker remains alive after Seraphina's earlier brief feeding. The unidentified rival vampire has departed the immediate yard after learning Seraphina belongs to the Pale Court, is acting independently at Bellwater, and is cooperating with Garran.
