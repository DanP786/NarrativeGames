# Current scene

The group spends the remainder of the daylight securing the old limeworks and preparing to leave. Seraphina, Garran and Frederick retrieve the three horses they rode to the site; the horses travel with the convoy rather than being abandoned. Serviceable limeworks carts and harness are pressed into use. By the time the work is finished and the convoy starts for Bellwater, night has fallen, removing the immediate direct-sunlight concern for Seraphina.

Frederick drives the covered wagon carrying the twelve freed adult captives. The weakened prisoners are given the easiest positions available, including the three repeatedly bled captives and the man with the badly swollen ankle. They remain free passengers under protection, not prisoners. Their immediate destination is Bellwater, with medical attention/infirmary intended before people disperse or decide whether to accept Seraphina's voluntary Veyne Hall employment offer.

Garran takes charge of a separate limeworks cart carrying the hostile casualties/prisoners from the underground operation. The better-dressed commander is alive but badly maimed and secured for transport. The two final guards Seraphina incapacitated are established during loading as alive but wounded; they are secured as prisoners. The four hostile men Seraphina definitively killed underground—Renn, Derrick, the unnamed lantern-bearing searcher, and the captive-side guard—are also loaded as dead evidence/casualties. Garran intends this cart for Saint Orra in Bellwater, where the two healthy surviving guards from the earlier operation can receive the living prisoners and the dead can be dealt with appropriately. No additional limeworks hostile survivor is established by this loading.

Inventory during preparation establishes the expected order is exactly half complete: six sealed bottles of collected human blood are already filled and usable, leaving six bottles still required to complete the two-case/twelve-bottle order. The existing straw-lined cases, empty matching bottles, stoppers and available sealing/packing materials are taken with her cart. The precise bottle volume and any special authentication beyond the observed physical packing/sealing remain unestablished. Seraphina's plan is to obtain enough fresh animal blood in Bellwater to fill the remaining six bottles rather than bleed any rescued captive further, then attempt to pass the completed shipment through the third-route network to the riverside storehouse with the whitewashed gable and broken waterwheel.

The captured split-bell tally, delivery papers, correspondence and remaining operational purse funds remain with Frederick unless deliberately transferred later. Hadrik remains absent and unidentified in person; his exact role and the ultimate purpose/recipient of the blood remain unresolved. Night has now fallen as the three-vehicle convoy sets out toward Bellwater.
