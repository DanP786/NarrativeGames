# Current scene

Below the abandoned lime kiln, Seraphina follows the captive party through the narrow worked passage and closes as far as she dares while remaining hidden. Her novice Stealth succeeds, bringing her within close observation distance of the inner checkpoint without alerting the escort, the man operating the checkpoint, or whoever is beyond it. Five bound adult human captives are present. The underground route is blocked by a heavy timber door reinforced with iron and operated from the far side; the outside man gives the arrival as 'Third route. Five alive,' after which the door is unbarred from within. When it opens, Seraphina sees that the route continues into a larger underground working beyond rather than ending at a holding cellar. At least two additional adult human men are visible on the far side, meaning the captive party has more support beyond the door than the single escort behind the prisoners. Lantern light and worked stone continue deeper. The captives begin passing through the doorway. Seraphina remains concealed on the near side, close enough that the door's closing will soon force a choice between slipping through with the movement, remaining behind and losing direct sight of the captives, or acting. The entrance guard above still has not raised an alarm as far as Seraphina can hear. Garran and Frederick remain outside and do not know Seraphina's exact underground position. Seraphina remains in fitted red leather armour with matched ivory-handled fighting daggers, black hooded cloak, braided/plaited ponytail, and her betrothal ring hidden against her chest.
