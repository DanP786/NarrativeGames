# Current scene

The next morning at Veyne Hall, Seraphina prepares for the Bellwater investigation. Lucien has her dressed in the fitted red leather armour he originally had made for her before she agreed to join him. As Seraphina finishes her hair, Lucien personally slides her matched ivory-handled fighting daggers into their sheaths. Seraphina wears her honey-blonde hair braided and plaited back into a ponytail while retaining visible soft waves. She is therefore equipped in her standard red martial armour with both commissioned daggers for the coming field work. The field conditions from the previous evening remain in force: Frederick accompanies Seraphina; she is not to disappear alone with Garran; she may heed Garran's immediate tactical commands in a fight when necessary to preserve life without granting him broader authority; she is expected to exercise her own field judgment; she is not to pursue alone if separated or deliberately lured away; and if the plan collapses, survival and returning to Veyne Hall take priority. Garran's evidence remains active: the Bellwater operation is consolidating larger shipments, at least one included living captives, and someone has ordered Seraphina watched rather than taken. The exact purpose of the shipments, identity of the person who ordered Seraphina watched, Hadrik's direct involvement, and destination of the living captives remain unknown.
