# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. After Seraphina's failed attempt to flee, her hands close around Garran's wrist where he has caught her by the hair, but she still does not reach for her daggers or attack. Garran forces the smaller vampire back against the wall and pins her there with his forearm across her throat, maintaining close physical control while his sword remains available. Tears stream down Seraphina's cheeks as she asks, 'Would that make it easier to kill me? If I fought you?' Her question puts words to the contradiction Garran is confronting: resistance would fit his expectations of a monster and make lethal action easier to justify, while her refusal to fight despite pain and fear denies him that clarity. Garran still does not know why Seraphina selected the sober laborer she fed upon or precisely what condition she left him in. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
