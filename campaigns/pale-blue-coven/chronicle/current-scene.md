# Current scene

The next day at Veyne Hall, Seraphina walks the halls in a deliberately close-fitted outfit: trousers, boots, a tunic and waistcoat, with numerous leather straps as part of the ensemble. Her honey-blonde hair is arranged in soft curls that bounce as she walks. She remains Lucien's betrothed and intended queen and continues to wear his enormous gem-encrusted betrothal ring. Veyne Hall is increasingly active as the new household takes shape around Seraphina and Lucien. Christian remains under consideration for the inner circle while handling the household's provisions; Frederick has become Lucien's muscle. Mistress Elianor Thorne has accepted Seraphina's offer in principle and chosen Seraphina to make her a vampire, but remains mortal while arranging her affairs before a carriage is sent for her. The new coven has no institutional concubine role or assigned sexual service. The anonymous-message investigation and Bellwater followers remain unresolved.
