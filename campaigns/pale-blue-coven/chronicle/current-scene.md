# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane inside the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. After the Saint Orra cart departs, Seraphina follows Garran toward the breach in the boundary wall with such quiet footing that he briefly checks over his shoulder to confirm she is still behind him. They pause together at the breach, listening and watching, then enter the yard without being detected. The rear door of the low stone building is shut; thin yellow light shows around its edges and shutter seams. The two known workers are somewhere inside. Seraphina and Garran cross toward the rear of the building, where the cellar-level loading hatch sits low in the stonework. At close range they can hear muted movement below ground as well as faint movement within the building. The seventh load described as fresh and whole when loaded remains unlocated. Neither infiltrator has been detected.
