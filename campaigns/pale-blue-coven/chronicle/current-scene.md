# Current scene

Inside a private chamber of the Pale Court, Lady Yseld Veyr has converted Seraphina's unexpected contact with the unnamed mortal knight into an intelligence assignment. Yseld orders Seraphina to accept the knight's invitation to continue helping him, learn who he is and what he hunts or knows about creatures like the Pale Court, keep the coven and her vampiric nature concealed, never feed from him, and report any threat to the Pale Court before acting independently. Seraphina answers, 'Yes ma'am,' and accepts the order without argument. Yseld still believes Seraphina does not know the knight's name and does not know he is Sir Garran Vale; she also remains unaware of Seraphina's feelings for him and of Garran having witnessed Seraphina's alarming speed. Seraphina has therefore gained explicit coven permission—indeed an order—to remain close to Garran, though for reasons very different from her private ones.
