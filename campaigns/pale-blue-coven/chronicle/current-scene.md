# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina stands apart from Lucien Veyne and speaks directly with Sir Garran Vale. She has admitted voluntarily leaving her former coven and joining Lucien. When Garran asks why she made that choice, Seraphina challenges the premise of his concern instead: "Why do you care Garran? I am your enemy. My nature cannot be reconciled in one conversation." Her challenge forces Garran to distinguish his sworn opposition to monsters from his demonstrated concern for Seraphina personally and from the unresolved question of whether he truly regards her as an enemy. Garran knows Seraphina loves him because she told him so, knows she repeatedly protected him and preserved lives when asked, and has chosen not to kill or imprison her, but he has not resolved what his oath ultimately requires. He knows Seraphina voluntarily joined Lucien but still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, that she subsequently completed a blood-oath with him, that Lucien calls her his prospective queen, or that they are building a new coven.
