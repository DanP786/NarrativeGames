# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has his sword drawn after identifying Seraphina as a vampire, rejecting her declaration that she loves him, and demanding the location and strength of her coven. Seraphina retreats until her back meets the wall, keeping both hands visibly away from her daggers. She shakes her head and answers, 'I can't tell you that.' Garran reads the refusal as confirmation that a coven exists and that Seraphina is protecting it, but her continued refusal to attack or reach for a weapon complicates his view of her as an immediate predator. He still does not know the Pale Court's name, location, strength, Seraphina's maker, her prior following of him, or that her declaration of love was sincere. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
