# Current scene

North of town beyond the old gallows road, Seraphina closes stealthily on the ruined croft where the necromancer is operating. She remains in her flowing black-and-pale-blue gown with gold makeup and a concealed dagger. From cover she sees Sir Garran Vale alive and fighting several risen corpses near the croft. More undead are emerging from the ruin and surrounding ground than are immediately engaged with him; the necromancer's intended force is roughly two dozen, though not all are necessarily raised or active yet. Garran has not yet been established as aware of Seraphina's presence. Seraphina is now close enough to observe the fight and potentially intervene.
