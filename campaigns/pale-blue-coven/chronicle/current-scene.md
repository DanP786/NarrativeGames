# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Sir Garran Vale has gone to secure several hours of sleep nearby in Bellwater before the expected evening/night surveillance of Saint Orra's rear gate. Seraphina intends to spend the working day watching the approaches and identifying concealed sightlines, but first decides she should feed so hunger will not become a liability during a potentially long night beside Garran. Keeping her hood positioned against direct sunlight, she moves away from the streets immediately around Saint Orra's rather than risk linking herself to the target. Bellwater's working districts provide several plausible adult feeding prospects without requiring her to prey on someone visibly drunk: laborers taking breaks in quieter lanes, solitary travelers moving between market yards, and other adults briefly isolated from the busiest streets. Seraphina has not yet selected or fed from a victim. Garran is resting elsewhere and does not know she has gone hunting.
