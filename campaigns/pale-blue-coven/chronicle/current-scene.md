# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the neglected walled yard beyond Bellwater. After drinking her fill from the mortally wounded swordsman, Seraphina lies flat on her back beside his corpse while the unusually large feeding restores strength and accelerates her healing. Hearing someone approach from inside, she turns only her head toward the rear doorway and otherwise remains motionless. Garran reaches the doorway from the burial works and sees Seraphina lying bloodied beside the dead swordsman. From the visible scene he can establish that she survived and that the escaped swordsman is dead, but he does not yet know the details of the attempted sexual assault, Seraphina's feeding from the dead man, her earlier feeding from the bound worker, or the rival-vampire encounter. Seraphina has not yet explained any of it. Her second dagger remains with her; her first remains lodged behind the knee of the other wounded worker below. The bound cellar worker remains alive after Seraphina's earlier brief feeding. The unidentified rival vampire has departed the immediate yard after learning Seraphina belongs to the Pale Court, is acting independently at Bellwater, and is cooperating with Garran.
