# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina stands apart from Lucien Veyne and speaks directly with Sir Garran Vale. Garran has offered a narrow path forward: rather than automatically treating Seraphina's vampiric nature as sufficient reason to make her an enemy, he will judge her by what she actually does, while retaining his sworn opposition to murder and harmful predation. Seraphina accepted his offered hand with visible resignation, reversing her earlier refusal to touch him when she still concealed her nature. She now questions the entire premise of the accord, asking Garran, "I don't understand. Why make a deal with a vampire?" Garran must now explain why his oath and experience with Seraphina permit any continuing accord at all rather than simply reiterating behavioral conditions. He knows Seraphina loves him because she told him so, that she repeatedly protected him and preserved lives, that she does not intend to be his enemy, and that she voluntarily joined Lucien. He still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, that she later completed a blood-oath with him, that Lucien calls her his prospective queen, or that they are building a new coven.
