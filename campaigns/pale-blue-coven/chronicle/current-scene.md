# Current scene

Several days after Seraphina's playful withholding campaign turned sour, Lucien approaches the silence in Veyne Hall's lower hall. Seraphina is dressed plainly in practical clothes, as she has been throughout the estrangement. When Lucien says that he thinks he misunderstood what she was trying to give him, Seraphina stops and first looks around to see who might overhear. She says nothing. She does not lower herself into deference, but neither does she meet his eyes; instead she looks into the distance. Lucien recognizes both the privacy concern and the deliberate absence of their usual chosen deference, and does not force the conversation in public.

The engagement remains intact and Lucien still possesses the gem-encrusted betrothal ring. Seraphina remains free to move within Veyne Hall. Frederick remains at Veyne Hall recovering from his beating; Seraphina has not yet spoken to him about whether he believes she broke his trust. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
