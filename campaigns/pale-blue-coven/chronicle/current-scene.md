# Current scene

Morning has come after Seraphina left Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, wandered for hours and took shelter in a rough timber hut deep in old woodland. Its human occupant, Edric, is a broad-shouldered weathered man in his early forties who had been splitting kindling outside. He has given Seraphina the hut's narrow bed and agreed to keep the shutters closed. He does not know she is a vampire.

Seraphina has given Edric her name and voluntarily surrendered her original fighting daggers into his keeping while she shelters under his roof; he left them untouched on the table. She has removed her boots, black cloak and leather travelling harnesses, leaving herself in comfortable cloth layers. After Edric observes that she appears to be running from someone or somewhere and raises the practical concern that whoever it is might come looking, Seraphina tells him, "There's just one man. He won't hurt you." She then settles back into the bed, watches Edric tend to his ordinary work for a while, and finally drifts into sleep.

The hut's closed shutters exclude the daylight sufficiently for shelter. Seraphina's removed travelling gear remains inside the hut, and her daggers remain on the table in Edric's keeping. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
