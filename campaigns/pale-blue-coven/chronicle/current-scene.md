# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Seraphina stands apart from Lucien Veyne and speaks directly with Sir Garran Vale. After Seraphina says she does not mean to be Garran's enemy, Garran offers a narrow path forward: he will judge Seraphina by what she actually does rather than automatically treating her vampiric nature as sufficient reason to make her an enemy, while warning that murder, careless predation, or knowingly standing beside such conduct would still bring them into conflict. He extends his bare hand and asks whether she can live with that. Seraphina takes Garran's hand with resignation visible on her face. The gesture reverses her earlier refusal to shake his hand when she feared her unnatural cold would expose her nature; now both know what she is and there is nothing left to conceal in the contact. Garran knows Seraphina voluntarily joined Lucien and does not intend to be Garran's enemy, but still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, that she later completed a blood-oath with him, that Lucien calls her his prospective queen, or that they are building a new coven.
