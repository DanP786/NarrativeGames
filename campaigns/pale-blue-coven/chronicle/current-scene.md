# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in an old burial chamber beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Garran commits forward into the deeper passage to deal with the crossbow defender, leaving Seraphina behind stone cover in the chamber. Seraphina remains badly wounded from the bolt she tore from her side, her cloak packed against the injury and her second dagger still in hand; her earlier head symptoms continue improving, but her body still shakes from pain. Once Garran has moved away, the wounded armed prisoner whose sword arm Seraphina cut begins cautiously shifting from where Garran had left him, testing whether the crisis has given him an opening. Seraphina immediately notices the movement and snaps her head up toward him. The prisoner's intention beyond exploiting the lapse in control is not yet established, and neither he nor Seraphina has yet committed to another attack. The escaped worker remains alive nearby with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee. Garran is now farther into the passage confronting the prepared position. The fallen lantern still burns. The tunnels remain alerted.
