# Current scene

Several nights after Seraphina's return to Bellwater, she and Lucien Veyne conclude their first direct recruitment approach to Mistress Elianor Thorne in Thorne's private warehouse office. Seraphina has truthfully but broadly described herself and Lucien as building a new household and network, openly admitted that they are evaluating Elianor as someone they want, and reframed the offer around freedom, protection, respected judgment, and extraordinary time while stopping short of explicitly naming vampirism or immortality. When Elianor asks exactly what they are offering, Seraphina stands and says, "Something you cannot take back. If it is something you truly desire, send word. Sleep on it." She deliberately refuses to push Elianor into an irreversible decision in the moment and leaves the initiative for any next meeting with Elianor. Lucien allows Seraphina to close the approach on those terms. Elianor has not accepted, rejected, or been told the full supernatural truth; whether she sends word remains unresolved. The Pale Court has not yet been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance, and Garran does not know the circumstances of her disappearance or her allegiance to Lucien.
