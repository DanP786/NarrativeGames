# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has privately warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Seraphina has explained that she and Lucien maintain willing thralls who live in luxury and provide blood, though she personally dislikes that arrangement and prefers opportunistic nonlethal feeding from troublesome drunks. When Garran asks who knows about the thralls, Seraphina answers irritably, "Only our household." This narrows direct knowledge of the household feeding arrangement to people within Seraphina and Lucien's household as far as Seraphina knows, but does not establish that the false information sent to Garran came from an insider or that the sender knows about the thralls at all. Garran still does not know Seraphina and Lucien are engaged, about their blood-oath or prospective queenship, or that Seraphina recently declared she no longer loves Garran. Lucien remains at the goldsmith's shop and has not been established as seeing Seraphina leave. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
