# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina reaches what she reasonably believes is the old tannery on Bellwater's east bank: a weather-blackened, apparently disused tannery yard that nearby workers repeatedly glanced toward after a knight had been asking questions there. Wearing black leather armor, carrying both fighting daggers, and keeping her hooded cloak up against direct sunlight, she approaches the crooked gate and enters the yard. The ground shows recent disturbance and the place smells of old tanning waste, damp timber, river mud, and something recently moved. A wooden crack had sounded from within shortly before her approach. Garran's exact position and the cause of the sound remain unestablished.
