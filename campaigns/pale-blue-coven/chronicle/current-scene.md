# Current scene

After being forcibly recovered by the Pale Court near the hill-road priory, Seraphina is taken back to the Pale Court stronghold. Lady Yseld Veyr has removed her from the Hadrik investigation and assigned another Pale Court operative to continue it. Back at the Pale Court, the household absorbs Seraphina's return into routine, restores her black-and-pale-blue formal appearance, keeps her original daggers, and treats her as though she has resumed her old place despite her explicit rejection of its claim on her loyalty.

At the Pale Court ball, Yseld assigns Seraphina to Lord Othmar Rell, a wealthy mortal magnate whose river toll rights, grain warehouses, and wagon contracts make him commercially useful. Seraphina resumes the pleasant, gracious social persona expected of her, dances with Othmar, and gives him the familiar cultivated story she has historically used with politically useful men: sheltered, modest, flattered by worldly attention, shaping the interaction so that any later intimacy he seeks feels to him mutually anticipated rather than imposed duty. Othmar accepts the performance and grows increasingly comfortable and personally interested.

In a quieter gallery, Lucien Veyne appears and calls Seraphina "little bunny." When Othmar asks whether they are acquainted, Lucien answers publicly, "She is my betrothed." The declaration exposes a major secret Seraphina had withheld from Yseld. Seraphina immediately checks who might have heard. The gallery is not sealed from the ballroom; a servant has already passed back inside, and the risk of the information spreading is immediate.

Seraphina dismisses Othmar with an apology, saying their evening has concluded. Othmar withdraws with controlled displeasure and heads across the ballroom toward Lady Yseld. Watching him approach her, Seraphina tells Lucien, "Take me away from here. Now." Lucien accepts without demanding an explanation first and starts moving her toward an exit route. Warning bells sound and Pale Court movement begins closing around the passage.

When Lucien asks whether they are leaving politely or simply leaving, Seraphina urgently answers, "Leaving! What idiot enters another vampire's home?!" Lucien commits to immediate flight. Seraphina uses her agility to keep pace through the confined passages in formal ball clothing while Lucien stays with her. Lucien says he brought horses. They reach a corridor with an exterior door roughly thirty yards ahead, but two Pale Court vampires block the route, one armed with a sword. The familiar one addresses Seraphina by name and tells her not to do this. Seraphina identifies him as Sven and urgently says, "Let me go, Sven!"

Sven is now established as a Pale Court vampire personally familiar enough with Seraphina for her to appeal to him by name. His deeper history, disposition, duties, and exact relationship to Seraphina are not yet established. Faced with her direct plea, he hesitates rather than immediately attacking, but he has not yet stepped aside. His armed companion remains ready and the stronghold continues closing around the escape.

Othmar has not yet been established as reaching Yseld or speaking to her. Yseld has not yet been established as hearing Lucien's betrothal claim or identifying him as the unknown vampire Seraphina protected during interrogation, but the warning bells mean Pale Court personnel are reacting to the attempted departure. Lucien's exact entry method, preparations, companions, and external escape arrangements remain unestablished. Seraphina is unarmed; her original daggers remain in Pale Court possession.
