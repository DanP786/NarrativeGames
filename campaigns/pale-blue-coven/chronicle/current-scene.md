# Current scene

At the ruined croft north of the old gallows road, Seraphina breaks concealment when a risen corpse closes on Sir Garran Vale from behind. She crosses the distance quickly and kills the corpse with her dagger before it can seize him, revealing herself to Garran for the first time. Garran now knows an unknown, finely dressed and highly capable woman is present and has just protected his blind side, but he does not know her name, vampiric nature, history of following him, or feelings. The fight continues: more risen dead are converging, and Seraphina has identified the necromancer's likely position inside or behind the reddish-lit croft.
