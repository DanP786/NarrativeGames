# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the cellar beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina holds the taller worker at dagger point, staring up at him in silence while Garran interrogates the other prisoner. Under controlled questioning, Garran establishes that the workers are paid processors rather than the operation's masters. They say prepared bones and selected fresh remains are passed onward through the dark passage to a buyer or intermediary they know only as Master Hadrik. Hadrik does not usually come to the workroom; collections or instructions come from deeper below. The workers claim they do not know what Hadrik ultimately does with the remains. Garran presses on who else is presently below and learns that one man went through the deeper passage earlier and has not returned. Both prisoners remain alive and controlled. The recently dead young man's body and six bone crates remain in the cellar.
