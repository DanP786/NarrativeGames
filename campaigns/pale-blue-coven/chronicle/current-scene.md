# Current scene

Several nights after Seraphina's return to Bellwater, she and Lucien Veyne are meeting prospective recruit Mistress Elianor Thorne in Thorne's private warehouse office. Seraphina has truthfully but broadly described herself and Lucien as building a new household and network while withholding the supernatural truth of the intended coven, and has directly admitted that they came because they are considering Elianor as someone they want. When Elianor asks what she possesses that they need, Seraphina deliberately reframes the negotiation around what Seraphina and Lucien possess that Elianor needs. She points to freedom from dependence on a husband, male partner, or eventual male heir; durable protection for what Elianor has built; access to a household in which her judgment would be valued rather than merely tolerated; and the possibility of time sufficient to build something that cannot simply be inherited away from her. Seraphina stops short of explicitly naming vampirism or immortality, making the reference to extraordinary time an intentional opening rather than a full supernatural disclosure. Lucien allows Seraphina to lead. Elianor now has reason to suspect that the offer is far stranger and larger than ordinary commercial partnership. The Pale Court has not yet been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance, and Garran does not know the circumstances of her disappearance or her allegiance to Lucien.
