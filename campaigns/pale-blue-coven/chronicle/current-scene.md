# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the cellar beneath the corpse-processing site beyond Bellwater. Garran asks whether the fresh blood on Seraphina's mouth in the yard belonged to the swordsman. Seraphina irritably admits that it did. She argues that she cannot change her nature and challenges Garran over whether he would rather have had her remain dying on the ground or too weak to stop the swordsman from sexually assaulting her. Disgusted with the exchange, she says, "I will wait outside," turns, and begins leaving the cellar. Garran now knows Seraphina fed from both the bound cellar worker and the swordsman in the yard, and that she frames both feedings in terms of survival, healing, and resisting the man who attacked her. He does not yet know she drank her fill from the swordsman, whether her feeding contributed materially to his death, or that an unidentified rival vampire approached her between the fight and Garran's arrival. Seraphina has both daggers sheathed. The wounded worker remains alive and bleeding, the bound cellar worker remains alive, and the captured archer remains alive, hurt, disarmed, and bound. The swordsman in the yard is dead. The rival vampire has departed the immediate yard.
