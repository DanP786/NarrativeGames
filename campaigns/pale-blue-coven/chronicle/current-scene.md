# Current scene

At the Pike and Crown near the military quarter, Seraphina discreetly listens to soldiers discussing missing corpses and the hunt for the necromancer. She inches nearer while pretending to watch the tavern's distant performance. The soldiers identify the hunter as Sir Garran Vale and reveal that he went north beyond the old gallows road after the necromancer, apparently leaving without waiting for ordinary soldiers to accompany him. Seraphina now has both useful intelligence for Yseld and a lead on Garran's current trail.
