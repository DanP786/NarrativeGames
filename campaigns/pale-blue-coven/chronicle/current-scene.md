# Current scene

Inside the ruined croft north of the old gallows road, the necromancer is dead and the raising ritual has failed. Seraphina finally gives Sir Garran Vale her name: 'Seraphina. You can call me Sera.' She pulls Garran's boot dagger from the necromancer's side; when the blade gives way she nearly loses her balance, then wipes it clean on the dead man's cloak and offers it back to Garran hilt-first. Garran now knows her name and accepts the returned weapon, but remains wary because her presence at the remote croft, combat ability and precise knowledge of his concealed dagger are unexplained. The necromancer's ledger has revealed an additional corpse source called the 'tithe pit,' associated with a crown/line/crosses symbol. Remaining risen dead outside are disordered and still require attention before the site is secure.
