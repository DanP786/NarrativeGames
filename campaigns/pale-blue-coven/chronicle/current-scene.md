# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale are together inside the old tannery on Bellwater's east bank. Their strongest lead is a tally slip bearing a split-bell seal and the notation 'Saint Orra — third bell.' Garran knows Saint Orra is associated with burial and care of the unclaimed dead, but the notation's exact referent remains unknown. When Garran suggests finding Saint Orra without openly announcing their investigation, Seraphina proposes using beggars and prostitutes as sources of local gossip. Garran accepts the practical value of asking people who spend their lives watching streets and customers, while recognizing that his own conspicuous appearance as a sworn knight may make him poorly suited to discreet questioning. No specific informant or Saint Orra location has yet been established.
