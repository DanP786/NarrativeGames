# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the burial passages beneath the corpse-processing site beyond Bellwater. Garran is occupied with the captured crossbow defender while Seraphina waits close behind him with both daggers sheathed. She shifts uncomfortably as the lingering pain of the night's injuries persists despite accelerated vampiric healing, and briefly closes her eyes when the attempted sexual assault in the yard returns to mind. The experience touches an older pattern in Seraphina's life under her maker: she is accustomed to her body being treated as something her maker may command her to offer, having often been ordered to sexually please visiting men of power, both human and vampires from other clans. This history helps explain the practiced, deferential way she falls in behind authority, but Garran does not know these memories or this history; he can observe only her discomfort, closed eyes, and unusually deferential posture. Seraphina remains because she will not leave Garran in danger, a motive he also cannot directly know. Her full feeding has restored enough strength for steady movement and accelerated the crossbow wound's healing, though accumulated injuries remain painful. Garran knows the swordsman attempted sexual violence but did not rape her. He still does not know Seraphina drank her fill from the dying swordsman, briefly fed from the bound cellar worker, or encountered an unidentified rival vampire. The wounded worker remains alive and bleeding. The bound cellar worker remains alive. The captured archer is alive, hurt, disarmed, and bound. The rival vampire has departed the immediate yard.
