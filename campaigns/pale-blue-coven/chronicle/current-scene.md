# Current scene

Late at night in Seraphina's private rooms at Veyne Hall, a painful conversation with Lucien ends without reconciliation. Seraphina has told him that her campaign of chosen intimacy and deference was meant to show that she still chose him, that his demand for more made her feel personally insufficient and worse than those who had used her before, and that returning the ring while his trust remained damaged made its meaning hollow. Lucien apologizes, says he will not ask her to prove her choice through intimacy again, and leaves when Seraphina does not stop him.

Alone after his departure, Seraphina opens her wardrobe and changes out of her linen nightgown into her civilian black travelling ensemble: high-waisted trousers, thigh-high boots and the feminine black overcoat cinched at the waist. Rather than taking the matched ivory-handled daggers Lucien provided, she retrieves her original fighting daggers and arms herself with those. She has not yet packed, chosen a destination, or explicitly decided to leave, but she is dressed and armed for movement beyond her rooms.

The engagement has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring and Lucien can no longer assume the intended marriage will proceed unchanged. Lucien still possesses the gem-encrusted betrothal ring. Seraphina remains free to move within Veyne Hall. Frederick remains injured but recovering. Christian has an expected channel for garden requests backed directly by Seraphina. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
