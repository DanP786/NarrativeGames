# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has identified Seraphina as a vampire and has been questioning her about her feeding. Seraphina admits her most recent victim, a sober laborer attacked while Garran slept, was alive afterward. When Garran glances toward Saint Orra's rear lane, Seraphina suddenly bolts. Her vampiric speed gives her an explosive first movement, but she is small and her cloak gives Garran something to catch: he snatches the cloak before she can clear him, abruptly arresting her escape and pulling the fabric taut between them. Garran now knows that despite her previous nonaggression and candor, Seraphina will flee when the confrontation becomes unbearable. His sword remains in his other hand. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
