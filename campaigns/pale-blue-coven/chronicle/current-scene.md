# Current scene

Inside the ruined croft north of the old gallows road, Seraphina brings the necromancer's dirt- and blood-marked ledger to Sir Garran Vale while the necromancer lies mortally wounded. Garran reads enough to learn the intended raising involved roughly two dozen corpses and that the disturbed public graveyards were not the only source. Repeated entries identify an additional source as 'the tithe pit,' accompanied by a crude symbol of a crown over a vertical line and a heap of crosses. Its location and exact meaning remain unknown, but the necromancer's fear when Garran asks about the pit confirms it matters. Garran remains wary of Seraphina but now recognizes that she is actively supplying useful evidence. The ritual is faltering and the remaining risen dead outside are disordered, though not all have been conclusively destroyed.
