# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane. In the neglected yard outside the Bellwater corpse-processing site, Garran has made his immediate decision: he will neither kill Seraphina nor take her prisoner tonight, and she is free to go home, though he considers the matter between them unresolved. Seraphina receives that verdict with her jaw set. She gives no reply, turns away, and walks out of the yard without looking back. Both fighting daggers remain in her possession and sheathed; her crossbow wound is cleaned, substantially healed, and bound with a clean cloth, while her leather and cloak remain battered and bloodstained. Her honey-blonde hair is braided down her back. Garran remains behind at the Bellwater site with the surviving prisoners and the unresolved Hadrik investigation. He knows Seraphina fed from both the bound cellar worker and the dead swordsman but does not know she drank her fill from the swordsman, whether feeding materially contributed to his death, or that an unidentified rival vampire approached her before his arrival. Seraphina and Garran are now physically separating for the first time since the night's confrontation over her nature.
