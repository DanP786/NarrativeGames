# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina has joined Sir Garran Vale inside the dark old tannery on Bellwater's east bank. She wears her feminine, practical black leather armor beneath a hooded cloak used to keep direct sunlight off her skin and carries both fighting daggers. Garran has already delivered the tithe-pit prisoners to lawful custody and begun investigating the tannery. After entering with conspicuous predator-like confidence, Seraphina ignores whatever Garran had been examining, walks directly up to him, and tells him, 'You look awful.' Their interaction is becoming more personally familiar despite Garran's continuing suspicion of her unexplained abilities and motives. What Garran has found in the tannery remains to be established.
