# Current scene

At night outside the underground Bellwater gladiator ring, Sir Garran Vale is gone and Seraphina remains close to Lucien Veyne after confessing how deeply Garran's judgment of her sixteen-year personal code has affected her. Looking up at Lucien from beneath heavy eyelashes, Seraphina says, "Take me home, Lucien." Lucien understands the request as a voluntary desire to leave Bellwater with him and return to Veyne Hall. Garran does not hear this conversation. Garran knows Seraphina and Lucien are lovers and that Lucien intends to build some larger enterprise with her, but he still does not know Lucien originally abducted her, the blood-oath, the prospective-queen title, or the exact nature and scale of the new coven.
