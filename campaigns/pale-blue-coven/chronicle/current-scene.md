# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale are together inside the old tannery on Bellwater's east bank. Seraphina cheerfully confirms that she slept after commenting on Garran's exhausted appearance, then steps around him to survey the building and asks whether there is any sign of the second buyer. Garran has found no buyer waiting here. The tannery instead appears to have been recently cleared: fresh scrape marks remain where heavy containers or bundles were moved, fine bone dust persists in seams of the floor, and investigation has exposed a concealed floor compartment beneath an old worktable. Seraphina's active survey succeeds as a nontrivial use of Observation, advancing the skill from 8 to 9 ticks. The compartment's contents have not yet been established.
