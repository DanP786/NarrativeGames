# Current scene

Near the abandoned limeworks south of Bellwater, Seraphina removes the seized split-bell courier satchel and asks Garran and Frederick, "Which one of you goes?" The group has identified fresh wheel marks on the concealed cart track and at least one man already present near one of the ruined kilns. Garran argues that Frederick is the better choice to carry the satchel and present the third-route tally because Garran is a sworn knight and monster-hunter whose bearing and possible recognition make him a poor imitation of an anonymous network courier. Frederick accepts the role. The working plan is for Frederick to approach the collection point with the satchel and tally while Garran remains concealed close enough to observe and intervene. Seraphina has not yet stated where she will position herself or whether she will accompany Frederick, remain with Garran, or take another concealed approach. The expected shipment has not yet arrived as far as the group can establish. The old limeworks remain potentially hostile and the traveler's information remains unverified. Seraphina remains in fitted red leather armour with matched ivory-handled fighting daggers, black hooded cloak protecting her from daylight, braided/plaited ponytail, and her betrothal ring hidden against her chest. Frederick remains subject to Lucien's instruction to accompany Seraphina and stay close enough to reach her.
