# Current scene

At the ruined croft north of the old gallows road, the necromancer is dead and his raising ritual has failed. Seraphina recovers her thrown fighting dagger from the necromancer's limp fingers, then walks out into the field and calmly destroys a disordered risen corpse that is kneeling and clawing uselessly at the mud.
