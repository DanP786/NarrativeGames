# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has Seraphina backed against a wall after identifying her as a vampire. He now knows she followed him for roughly a year before their direct meeting and before her coven knew about him. Asked what she wanted from that long secret attention, Seraphina answers, 'I don't want anything. I just wanted to know you were safe.' The answer directly contradicts Garran's theory that her original interest was predatory or coven-directed and fits her later observable behavior of protecting him, watching his back, making him eat and rest, and taking the daylight watch while he slept. It does not erase the danger or deception of a vampire secretly following him for a year. Garran's sword remains in hand, though Seraphina has still made no move for her daggers or against him. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
