# Current scene

After being forcibly recovered by the Pale Court near the hill-road priory, Seraphina is taken back to the Pale Court stronghold. Lady Yseld Veyr has removed her from the Hadrik investigation and assigned another Pale Court operative to continue it. Yseld still does not know the identity of the unknown vampire Seraphina stayed with, nor that he is Lucien Veyne, that Seraphina swore allegiance to him, or that they became engaged.

Back at the Pale Court, the machinery of Seraphina's old life closes around her with almost no visible disruption. Her prolonged absence is absorbed into routine rather than treated publicly as a dramatic return. She is again housed, watched, fed, cleaned, and handled according to Pale Court expectations. Her original daggers remain in Pale Court possession rather than being returned to her. Whatever private rupture exists between Seraphina and Yseld, the household proceeds as though Seraphina has simply resumed her place.

At the Pale Court ball, Seraphina resumes the pleasant, gracious social persona expected of her. Yseld assigns her to Lord Othmar Rell, a wealthy mortal magnate whose river toll rights, grain warehouses, and wagon contracts make him commercially useful. Seraphina smiles, takes his arm, dances with him, and gives him the familiar cultivated story she has historically used with politically useful men: sheltered, modest, flattered by worldly attention, shaping the interaction so that any later intimacy he seeks feels to him mutually anticipated rather than imposed duty. Othmar accepts the performance and grows increasingly comfortable and personally interested.

Othmar leads Seraphina to a quieter gallery and lightly challenges her cultivated innocence, amused by the sense that she is pretending. Seraphina laughs politely. Before she can answer, a familiar male voice behind her says, "There you are, little bunny." Seraphina immediately goes stiff. The speaker is Lucien Veyne, whose use of "little bunny" is an established private familiarity between them. His presence at the Pale Court has just been revealed; how he learned Seraphina was here, why he has come, how he entered the ball, and whether the Pale Court knows his connection to Seraphina are not yet established.

Lord Othmar does not know Lucien, Seraphina's allegiance to him, their engagement, or their private history. Yseld still does not know that Lucien is the unknown vampire Seraphina stayed with, that Seraphina swore allegiance to him, or that they became engaged. Lucien knows Seraphina's former maker used her body for political and social sexual service and has explicitly promised never to require her to give sexual access to another for political utility. He does not yet have established knowledge on-screen that Yseld has assigned Seraphina to Othmar tonight.

The unresolved rupture remains beneath the restored surface: Seraphina has explicitly told Yseld that her loyalty ended when Yseld began giving her sexually to useful men, while Yseld continues to regard those assignments as legitimate coven duty. Seraphina is physically back within the Pale Court but has not retracted her rejection of its claim on her loyalty. Her engagement with Lucien has not formally ended; Lucien still possesses the ring, and Seraphina does not know whether she wants it returned. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead, and Hadrik network remain unresolved.
