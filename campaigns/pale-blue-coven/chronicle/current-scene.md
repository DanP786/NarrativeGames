# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in an old burial chamber beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina has stepped into the deeper passage while still unsteady from a hard head impact and strangling. The concealed human defender behind the low wooden obstruction fires a crossbow at her. The bolt strikes Seraphina in the side and remains embedded. She goes down with a single gasp, one hand pressing around the shaft while the other keeps hold of her second dagger. The exact depth and internal severity of the wound are not yet established, but it is a serious penetrating injury and she is immediately unable to continue standing. Garran witnesses the shot and shifts from prisoner control toward protecting Seraphina and responding to the crossbow threat. The defender remains behind the obstruction around the left bend; whether he has another loaded weapon or assistance is unknown. The escaped worker remains alive nearby with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee. The fallen lantern still burns. The tunnels remain alerted.
