# Current scene

Late afternoon has come after Seraphina left Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, wandered through the previous night and took shelter at dawn in a rough timber hut deep in old woodland. Its human occupant, Edric, is a broad-shouldered weathered man in his early forties. He gave Seraphina the hut's narrow bed and kept the shutters closed through the day. He does not know she is a vampire.

Seraphina gave Edric her name and voluntarily surrendered her original fighting daggers into his keeping while she sheltered under his roof. Waking as the short early-autumn daylight begins to fail, Seraphina tells Edric that she is no more certain where she is going but is ready to keep moving. Edric returns her original daggers handle-first. Seraphina smiles as she takes them and tells him, "You're very generous of a stranger." Her boots, black cloak and leather travelling harnesses remain inside the hut and have not yet been put back on.

The hut's shutters have kept the day's direct light out. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
