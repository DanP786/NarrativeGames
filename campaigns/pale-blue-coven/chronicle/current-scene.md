# Current scene

Seraphina has escaped a forced return to the Pale Court with Lucien Veyne. At the Pale Court ball, Lucien publicly called her his betrothed; Seraphina asked him to take her away immediately. During the escape Seraphina told Lucien, "My home is with you. I know that now," explained Yseld's coercive assignment of her to Lord Othmar Rell, and explicitly asked Lucien to return her betrothal ring when they reached home. They later sheltered through daylight at a roadside inn, reconciled physically by mutual choice, and returned to Veyne Hall.

Garran Vale was waiting at Veyne Hall despite Lucien's former prohibition, saying he came to ensure Seraphina was safe. Mara Voss, a striking black-haired mortal household administrator brought in during Seraphina's absence, also appeared and immediately created tension through household authority, intrusion into Seraphina's rooms and physical familiarity with Lucien. Seraphina later learned from Christian that Lucien had remained preoccupied with searching for her and that Mara had assumed considerable organizational authority during his absences.

In Lucien's office, Seraphina demanded her ring and Lucien restored the gem-encrusted betrothal ring immediately, without another test. She interrogated him about Mara; Lucien stated Mara has never been his lover. Seraphina then challenged whether House Veyne was truly different from the Pale Court if she was reduced to decoration and Lucien's exclusive concubine. Lucien accepted the criticism and committed to Seraphina possessing real power in the house and emerging coven, with her chosen private submission explicitly separate from her public judgment and office.

The Pale Court has now sent a formal message demanding a truce meeting at an old roadside chapel at midnight three nights hence. Two Pale Court representatives will attend; Lucien may bring Seraphina and one other companion. Refusal will be treated as confirmation that House Veyne chooses open hostility. The writer is unnamed and the letter pointedly calls Seraphina "the fledgling Seraphina." Whether Yseld will attend and whether the truce is trustworthy remain unknown.

Seraphina judges the meeting reasonable and Lucien agrees they should attend while preparing for possible treachery. Seraphina wants the permitted third attendee to be someone who can fight. Frederick is the preferred choice if sufficiently recovered. Garran remains a possible but politically dangerous alternative.

Lucien has explained that his former death threat against Garran is no longer absolute. During Seraphina's disappearance Lucien went to Garran himself for help searching. Garran was openly pleased to hear Seraphina had apparently left Lucien, but nevertheless helped rather than exploiting her disappearance. Lucien also admits Garran was one of the first places he checked because he considered the possibility that Seraphina had gone to him. Garran's genuine surprise and pleasure at learning she had left Lucien showed she was not there. Lucien checked the fear rather than treating jealousy as proof.

Seraphina settles onto Lucien's desk and lifts the skirts of her white gown to her thighs, leaning back on her hands in an unmistakably suggestive invitation while returning to practical business. She decides, "We bring Frederick if he is well enough," then observes that Lucien must have done considerable damage for Frederick to be healing so slowly despite being a vampire. Lucien acknowledges that he beat Frederick severely after the Bellwater failure and agrees Frederick must be assessed before they rely on him at a potentially violent parley.

Seraphina then nips at Lucien's lip, widens her legs and tells him, "I am finished for now." Lucien takes the invitation for what it is. He closes the remaining distance, kisses her with deliberate possessive warmth and draws the private moment away from politics. Their encounter remains consensual and chosen; the scene fades from explicit detail with the Pale Court letter lying displaced on the desk beside them.

Whether Garran is still physically present at Veyne Hall after Mara ordered him out remains unestablished. Mara remains a temporary household administrator pending Seraphina's larger decision about whether she stays, hands over authority, or leaves. Mara's private ambitions and feelings toward Lucien remain unestablished.

Seraphina's original daggers remain confiscated by the Pale Court. Her gifted ivory-handled daggers and fitted red leather armour remain available at Veyne Hall. Her betrothal ring is restored to her hand.
