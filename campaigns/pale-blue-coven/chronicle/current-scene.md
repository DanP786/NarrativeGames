# Current scene

Late in the working day on the 19th of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale occupy the concealed observation position she identified overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. After commenting that Garran looks better following his rest, Seraphina tells him that he strikes her as someone who puts his needs above all else: if he is not at his best he will miss things, and then he finds himself with 'a little bossy lady telling you what to do.' Garran hears this as another openly personal but practical expression of her concern for his condition, delivered with teasing self-awareness. The legitimate working day is ending and the district continues to thin around Saint Orra's; no covert three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet occurred. Garran does not know Seraphina fed on a warehouse laborer while he slept.
