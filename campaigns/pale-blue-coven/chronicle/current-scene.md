# Current scene

The following evening, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne leave an underground gladiator ring after scouting its fighters and patrons for possible recruits to their intended new coven. Both are dressed in ordinary travel clothes rather than courtly dress or Seraphina's red fighting armour, and Lucien has an arm around Seraphina's waist as they emerge. A familiar voice suddenly calls, "Sera!" Sir Garran Vale has seen her. Lucien reacts instinctively to the perceived threat: he pulls Seraphina behind himself and presses her back against a nearby wall while interposing his own body between her and Garran. Garran's last direct encounter with Seraphina ended when he chose neither to kill nor imprison her and permitted her to go home while saying their matter was unfinished; he does not know that Lucien abducted her afterward, that she willingly accepted Lucien's offer and blood-oath, or that she is now helping him build a prospective coven. Seraphina has not yet answered Garran. Garran has not yet been given any reason to know Lucien's name or their exact relationship, though he can plainly see Lucien's protective physical reaction and Seraphina's presence with him.
