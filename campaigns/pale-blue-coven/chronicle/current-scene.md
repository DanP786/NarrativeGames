# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has identified Seraphina as a vampire and has been questioning her about her feeding. After admitting that the sober laborer she fed upon while Garran slept was alive afterward, Seraphina tries to flee. Garran first catches her cloak; when the fastening chokes her, Seraphina releases it and runs on. Garran discards the cloak and catches her before she gets three steps farther, seizing her by the hair and hauling her back. Seraphina cries out from the painful restraint but does not reach for either dagger or otherwise attack him. Garran now has immediate physical control of the smaller vampire at close range, while her refusal to use her weapons despite pain remains conspicuous. His sword is still in his other hand. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
