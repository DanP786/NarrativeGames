# Current scene

At the rear entrance beneath the abandoned lime kiln, Seraphina gathers several more pieces of loose limestone and throws them into the scrub in the direction the exposed human guard is already facing. When the impacts draw his head and attention outward, she uses the opening to slip behind him and descend into the underground entrance without being detected. Her novice Stealth succeeds under favorable distraction, and no alarm is raised. She can hear them but has not yet caught up or been detected.
