# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has Seraphina backed against a wall with his sword drawn after identifying her as a vampire. He asks whether her coven knows she has been following a monster-hunter. Seraphina gives him a pained look and answers, 'Yes.' Garran now knows that Seraphina's coven is aware of her attention to him, but he does not know the crucial distinction that Seraphina began following him by her own choice and that Lady Yseld only later ordered her to remain close after learning of the unnamed knight. He still does not know the coven is the Pale Court, its location or strength, or that Seraphina's declaration of love is sincere. Seraphina remains unarmed with her hands visible. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
