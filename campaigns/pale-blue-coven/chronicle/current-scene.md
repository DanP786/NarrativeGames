# Current scene

At the tithe pit north beyond the old gallows road, Garran has finished interrogating the captured bone-harvesting crew. The strongest actionable lead is Bellwater: an old tannery on the east bank is used as a collection point for harvested human remains. The pit crew dealt through intermediaries and cannot reliably identify the ultimate patron controlling the trade. The prisoners remain alive, several wounded by Seraphina's deliberately nonlethal cuts, and Garran intends to turn them over to lawful custody rather than abandon them. Seraphina has spent the questioning watching the perimeter and Garran, and caught one wounded laborer reaching for a concealed utility knife. The immediate fight and interrogation are over; Garran must now decide how to handle the prisoners and pursue the Bellwater lead.
