# Current scene

After feeding without killing at the Lantern Rooms, Seraphina crosses the large town to the Pike and Crown, a tavern near the military quarter where soldiers and guards drink after duty. Still dressed in her flowing black-and-pale-blue gown with gold makeup, she settles where she can listen without appearing to do so. Her observation pays off: nearby soldiers discuss a string of disturbed graves outside town, missing bodies, and orders connected to the problem. One mentions that a knight has already gone after the suspected corpse-thief, giving Seraphina potentially useful information for Yseld while risking further exposure of the knight she has concealed.
