# Current scene

In a cramped passage off Bellwater's crowded night market, Sir Garran Vale has privately warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Their discussion shifted through Seraphina's former coven role, her recruitment by Lucien, and Garran's feelings toward her. Seraphina revealed that her former master assigned her to please whoever the master wanted; that she originally followed and fell in love with Garran because she believed he could save her; that Lucien knows this history and does not assign her sexually to others; and that she says she gives herself to Lucien willingly. She also told Garran that Lucien first offered her a way out while she was injured in the Bellwater bone yard, she refused, and he later took/abducted her while she was returning home before offering again, at which point she accepted. The exchange became physically intimate: Seraphina stepped into Garran's space, later placed her hands on his chest and leaned against him, while Garran admitted he cares for her and wants to touch and kiss her but insisted she tell him what she wants rather than acting on her former declaration of love. Before Seraphina answered, Lucien arrived and found them alone together immediately after visible closeness. Seraphina stepped back and told Lucien Garran had received information making her a target. Lucien pulled Seraphina to his side and thanked Garran for warning his "betrothed," revealing the engagement to Garran for the first time. When Garran asks Seraphina directly whether she is going to marry Lucien, Seraphina answers with certainty, "I am," then looks up at Lucien and smiles. Garran therefore now knows from Seraphina herself that the engagement is real and that she presently affirms it willingly and confidently. He still does not know Seraphina and Lucien are blood-bound or planning a new coven with Seraphina as prospective queen, nor that Seraphina recently declared to Lucien that she no longer loves Garran. Lucien knows Garran received information targeting Seraphina but not necessarily the message's full contents unless Garran or Seraphina explains them. The two suspected watchers remain unidentified.
