# Current scene

Inside a private chamber of the Pale Court, Seraphina continues reporting to Lady Yseld Veyr. She tells Yseld that the mortal knight knows her only as 'Sera,' does not know where she lives, does not know she is a vampire, and knows she can fight with a dagger. This deliberately understates Garran's exposure: he also witnessed Seraphina pursue fleeing humans with alarming speed and disable them with precise nonlethal cuts, and he remains suspicious of her unexplained abilities and motives. Yseld cannot prove Seraphina's account false, but already suspects the report is incomplete. Yseld still does not know the knight is Sir Garran Vale, Seraphina's feelings for him, the full extent of their cooperation, the intended raising of roughly two dozen corpses, the tithe-pit corpse source, or the Bellwater tannery lead.
