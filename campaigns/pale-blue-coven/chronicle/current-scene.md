# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale leave the investigative pause inside the old tannery on Bellwater's east bank with their strongest lead still the tally slip marked by a split-bell seal and 'Saint Orra — third bell.' After Garran again jokes about lurking, Seraphina tuts, says, 'The lurking again?' and walks out to begin pursuing her own suggestion of gathering discreet street gossip from beggars and prostitutes. Before crossing from the tannery's dark interior into direct morning sunlight, she deliberately pulls her hood down over her head to keep the light off her skin. Garran follows her out, remaining conspicuous enough that Seraphina may need to choose whether to keep him close or have him hang back while she approaches potential informants. No specific informant or Saint Orra location has yet been established.
