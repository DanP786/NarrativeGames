# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina is inside Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard, investigating alone under a mortal pretext. She tells the lay burial keeper that the missing woman her mistress seeks is named Raquelle, with mouse-brown hair, several missing teeth, and a little taller than Seraphina. The keeper accepts the description without obvious suspicion and goes to consult Saint Orra's recent burial/intake ledger, leaving Seraphina waiting in the public portion of the yard. Before going, he says they have received several unidentified women recently and that he will need to check rather than dismiss the inquiry outright. The fresh wheel ruts from the previous night's heavily laden cart still lead from the rear service gate to the shut stone-and-timber outbuilding, not to the public chapel. Seraphina has not entered that outbuilding or established what was unloaded there. Sir Garran Vale remains elsewhere in Bellwater and does not witness the deception.
