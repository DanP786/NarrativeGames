# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in an old burial chamber beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina lies wounded near the mouth of the deeper passage with a crossbow bolt embedded in her side, one hand pressing around the shaft and the other still gripping her second dagger. Through gritted teeth she tells Garran, "Take them prisoner. Leave me." Garran refuses to leave her exposed in the crossbowman's line of fire. He drags Seraphina back toward the stone cover at the chamber mouth without removing the bolt, then turns his attention toward suppressing and capturing the defender while keeping himself between Seraphina and the bend. The defender remains behind the low wooden obstruction around the left bend and is believed to be reloading. The escaped worker remains alive nearby with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee; the previously controlled armed man remains wounded and nearby, but the crisis has disrupted Garran's direct hold on him and his exact ability to exploit that is unresolved. The fallen lantern still burns. The tunnels remain alerted.
