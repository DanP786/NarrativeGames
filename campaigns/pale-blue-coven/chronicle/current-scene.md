# Current scene

Daylight on the 20th of Harvestwane at Veyne Hall, Lucien Veyne's old family seat in wooded hills north of Bellwater. The wealthy old stone residence is heavily shuttered against daylight and serves as the beginning of Lucien's intended new coven rather than an already established Court. Lucien says nearby mortal villages believe the Veyne line dwindled years ago and that the house has belonged to him longer than living tenants remember. He acknowledges that the Pale Court could eventually threaten the holding if Lady Yseld learns its precise location and decides Seraphina is worth recovering. Lucien has offered her a place as queen beside him, and Seraphina has deliberately shifted her allegiance toward him. After Lucien says he needs more than walls and values Seraphina's knowledge of how a real coven functions, she looks to him and says, "Whatever you desire," openly offering her cooperation. Lucien's prior allegiance, full resources, actual source of his earlier information, and the practical structure of the intended new coven remain unresolved.
