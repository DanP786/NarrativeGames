# Current scene

Dusk has fallen after Seraphina left Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, wandered through the previous night and sheltered through the daylight in Edric's rough timber hut deep in old woodland. Edric is a broad-shouldered weathered human man in his early forties who gave Seraphina his bed and kept the shutters closed. He does not know she is a vampire.

Seraphina has dressed again in her civilian black travelling ensemble, boots, leather harnesses and black cloak, with her original fighting daggers back in her possession. She told Edric to reveal everything if anyone comes looking for her rather than risk himself, warned him that unless a seeker goes by "Sir Vale" he should not trust his safety, and confidently told him that no one is dangerous to her. At the threshold she thanks Edric by name and leaves the hut as darkness settles over the woods. Without announcing it to him, she deliberately leaves her coin purse behind on the side as payment for his shelter and kindness.

Seraphina is once again outside in the woodland at dusk with no declared destination. Edric has told her that the old road east leads to a village and west eventually returns toward larger roads; she remains free to choose either direction or continue wandering. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
