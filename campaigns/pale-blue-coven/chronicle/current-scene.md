# Current scene

At dawn after leaving Veyne Hall without announcing a destination, Seraphina has wandered for hours and taken shelter in a rough timber hut deep in old woodland. Its human occupant, Edric, is a broad-shouldered weathered man in his early forties who had been splitting kindling outside. He has given Seraphina the hut's narrow bed and agreed to keep the shutters closed. He does not know she is a vampire.

Seraphina has given Edric her name and voluntarily surrendered her original fighting daggers into his keeping while she shelters under his roof; he left them untouched on the table before going outside. Once alone, Seraphina removes her boots and black cloak and strips away the leather harnesses and other stiff travelling pieces, leaving herself in the comfortable cloth layers of her clothing. She climbs into Edric's narrow bed to rest through the daylight. Her removed travelling gear remains inside the hut unless otherwise established.

The hut's closed shutters exclude the immediate daylight sufficiently for shelter. Veyne Hall is far behind her. The engagement with Lucien has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring; Lucien still possesses it. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
