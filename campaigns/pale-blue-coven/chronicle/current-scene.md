# Current scene

After being forcibly recovered by the Pale Court near the hill-road priory, Seraphina is taken back to the Pale Court stronghold. Lady Yseld Veyr removes her from the Hadrik investigation and assigns another Pale Court operative to continue it. At a Pale Court ball, Yseld assigns Seraphina to Lord Othmar Rell, a wealthy mortal magnate, expecting Seraphina to keep him pleased and submit to whatever he wants while maintaining a pleasant outward performance.

Lucien Veyne appears at the ball. When Othmar asks whether they are acquainted, Lucien states publicly, "She is my betrothed." Seraphina dismisses Othmar and urgently tells Lucien, "Take me away from here. Now." They flee. Sven, a familiar Pale Court coven-mate, briefly blocks them, but after Seraphina insists she wants to leave and pleads, "You know what she makes me do," he restrains his companion and lets her pass. Yseld's voice is heard calling Seraphina's name behind them.

Lucien gets Seraphina onto a prepared horse and mounts behind her. Seraphina guides them through country around the Pale Court and across a shallower upstream river crossing while pursuit reaches the river behind them. During the escape Seraphina tells Lucien, "My home is with you. I know that now," says she missed him, and explains that after leaving Veyne Hall she became lost rather than deliberately choosing not to return. She tells him exactly what Yseld expected of her with Othmar. Lucien explains he came without a war-band because he did not know whether she wanted extraction and did not want to trigger open war or have her confined before he could reach her.

Seraphina and Lucien acknowledge that his public claim and their escape mean secrecy around House Veyne is effectively ending. Seraphina explicitly asks for her betrothal ring back when they reach home. Lucien promises to return it without another test. Seraphina later teases that he should marry his bride to stop her running away; Lucien says he wants to marry her because she chooses to stay, not as a restraint. She also reminds him of the revealing dresses she commissioned at Veyne Hall.

Without sunlight cloaks and with dawn approaching, they take emergency shelter in a small roadside inn. Lucien secures a shuttered upstairs room. Seraphina asks for a bath; afterward their reconciliation becomes physically intimate by mutual choice, with details remaining private. They sleep through the daylight tangled together beneath a sheet.

Near dusk they wake and linger together before preparing for travel. Lucien dresses and goes downstairs to inspect the horse while Seraphina repairs her honey-blonde hair as best she can in a cracked mirror. Her black-and-pale-blue Pale Court ball gown is rumpled and stained at the hem but structurally intact and wearable.

Seraphina finishes dressing and turns ready to leave as Lucien returns. He reports that the horse has recovered enough for careful travel but should not be driven hard again. With night finally safe outside, they leave the room and descend through the inn. Seraphina remains conspicuous in her formal ball gown and unarmed. Lucien settles the remaining account and brings her toward the stable yard to resume the road to Veyne Hall.

No Pale Court riders have been seen since before reaching the inn; pursuit is not confirmed ended. It is still not established that Yseld personally heard Lucien's betrothal declaration, received Othmar's report, or clearly identified Lucien as the unknown vampire Seraphina protected during interrogation, though Seraphina and Lucien reasonably expect information to reach her.

The unresolved rupture with the Pale Court remains. Seraphina has explicitly chosen Veyne Hall as home, chosen Lucien again, asked for the betrothal ring back, and raised proceeding to marriage. Their prior trust injuries are not erased. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead, and Hadrik network remain unresolved.
