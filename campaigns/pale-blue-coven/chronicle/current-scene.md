# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. After Seraphina admits she still expects Garran to kill her, Garran chooses not to strike and the immediate physical confrontation eases. Seraphina sags back against the wall, breathing raggedly, and lowers her hands to rest behind her at the base of the wall, looking at Garran with worry and sadness rather than reaching for her weapons or attempting another escape. Garran remains close and watchful. He still has not resolved what his oath requires of a vampire who feeds on humans but has repeatedly protected human life and refused to harm him. He also still does not know why Seraphina selected the sober laborer she fed upon or precisely what condition she left him in. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
