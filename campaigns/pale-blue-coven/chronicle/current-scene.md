# Current scene

Seraphina returns to the Pale Court in deep night after another long absence spent secretly following the mortal knight. Her maker, Lady Yseld Veyr, waits in Seraphina's chamber and confronts her about the repeated excursions. Seraphina truthfully explains that a human is attempting to reanimate a horde of zombies and that she has found it amusing to watch, while concealing that the knight she loves is hunting the necromancer. Seraphina confirms the necromancer does not know he is being watched. Yseld decides that if Seraphina insists on observing the affair, she will do so usefully: she orders Seraphina to learn the necromancer's numbers, source of corpses, and whether anyone else is hunting him, then report directly to Yseld. Seraphina answers, “Yes ma’am,” accepting the order without argument; Yseld leaves her chamber. Seraphina changes for a night out into a flowing black gown with pale-blue accents, conceals a dagger beneath it, and paints warm color into her face with gold worked around her blue eyes. Hungry, she heads for the Lantern Rooms, an entertainment house where drunken revelers and private chambers make discreet feeding easy and where she knows how to take heavily from a victim without killing them.
