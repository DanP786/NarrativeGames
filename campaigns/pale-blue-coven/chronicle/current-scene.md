# Current scene

Seraphina and Lucien leave the cramped passage and return to Bellwater's crowded night market after Sir Garran Vale has departed. Garran warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with three drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Before Lucien arrived, Seraphina's private exchange with Garran became physically intimate: she stepped into his space, put her hands on his chest and leaned against him while he admitted wanting to touch and kiss her. Lucien later confronted Seraphina over finding them together, pulled her hair in a recognized vampiric demand for deference, escalated almost to pain and pinned her to the wall. Seraphina yielded and admitted she did not know whether she wanted Garran to kiss her, but unequivocally reaffirmed that she would choose Lucien every time and called her old rescue-dream with Garran "a fantasy a desperate woman conjured." Lucien warned, "If I find you with him again I will kill him and make you watch." He then gave Seraphina an enormous gem-encrusted betrothal ring, resized to fit her small left hand; she put it on and continues wearing it. Seraphina now follows Lucien obediently back into the market, consciously remembering how angry he was with her. This remembered anger influences her present deference but does not establish that Lucien knows her private thoughts or that she has surrendered future agency. Lucien has the anonymous message Garran gave Seraphina. The exact wording of that message remains unestablished beyond Garran's report that it contained enough description to identify Seraphina and falsely linked her to three drained deaths. Their immediate purpose is to determine whether either of the two suspected watchers remains nearby and whether the surveillance is connected to whoever targeted Seraphina. The watchers' identities, allegiance, and motive remain unknown.
