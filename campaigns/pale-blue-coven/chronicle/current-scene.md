# Current scene

Several nights after Seraphina's return to Bellwater, she and Lucien Veyne are meeting prospective recruit Mistress Elianor Thorne in Thorne's private warehouse office. Seraphina wears expensive but restrained feminine clothing, jewelry, and a convincing living complexion and is willingly participating in Lucien's recruitment strategy. After Elianor describes how men tried to take control of her business immediately after her husband's death, she asks what sort of business Seraphina conducts that requires a lord's signature while allowing Seraphina to direct him. Seraphina decides that trust cannot be built on a direct lie. She therefore answers truthfully but broadly: she and Lucien are establishing a new household and network of their own, acquiring property, arranging money and supplies, choosing the people they want around them, and building something intended to endure; Lucien can provide the male name and signature mortal institutions expect, while Seraphina has a genuine voice in deciding what they build and whom they trust. She does not yet disclose vampirism, feeding, immortality, or the intended supernatural coven. Lucien does not contradict her or speak over her. No supernatural recruitment offer has yet been made. The Pale Court has not yet been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance, and Garran does not know the circumstances of her disappearance or her allegiance to Lucien.
