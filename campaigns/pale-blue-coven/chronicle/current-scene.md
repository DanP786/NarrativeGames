# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in an old burial chamber beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina pulls herself fully upright into a seated position with her back against the stone wall, still coughing intermittently and suffering blurred vision and pounding head pain. She keeps her attention fixed on the dark passage where movement was seen rather than trying to stand or pursue. Garran keeps the wounded armed man controlled and watches the same passage, approving Seraphina's decision to remain down for the moment. The darkness beyond the bend stays quiet after the earlier scraping and glimpse of a withdrawing human figure. The escaped worker remains alive nearby with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee. The fallen lantern still burns; Seraphina retains her second dagger. The tunnels remain alerted, and an unknown person or persons may be preparing something beyond the bend.
