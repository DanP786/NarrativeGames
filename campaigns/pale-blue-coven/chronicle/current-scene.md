# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane beneath the neglected Bellwater building. The wounded swordsman reaches the cellar ahead of Seraphina and continues fleeing toward the service room, rear door, and yard beyond. In the cellar, the original worker who had remained behind during the tunnel pursuit is alive and tied to the processing table beside the recently dead young man's corpse. Seraphina reaches the cellar still badly wounded, hesitates, then deliberately feeds briefly from the bound living prisoner so fresh blood can accelerate her vampiric healing enough to continue the chase. She stops the feeding after a short drink and leaves him alive. The blood immediately gives her wounded body more to work with: the torn side begins healing more rapidly and her strength steadies, though the injury is not instantly erased and movement still hurts. This feeding is a tactical act under severe injury rather than a prolonged feed. The bound prisoner has directly witnessed Seraphina feeding from him and now knows she is a vampire or comparable blood-drinking creature. Seraphina intends to resume pursuit of the wounded swordsman before he escapes the building. Garran remains farther below confronting the crossbow position and does not witness the feeding. The worker with Seraphina's first dagger lodged behind his knee remains alive in the burial works below.
