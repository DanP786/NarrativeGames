# Current scene

Several days after returning from Bellwater, Seraphina and Lucien Veyne are together in his bed at Veyne Hall during consensual intimacy when Lucien asks Seraphina to become his wife. He frames marriage as an additional chosen bond beyond blood-oath, shared bed, prospective queenship, household, name, fortune, and future coven. Seraphina does not immediately accept. She studies him and asks, "You feel threatened I love Garran and want me to make a lasting declaration that I choose you?" Her question directly confronts Lucien with the fact that she still loves Garran and challenges whether jealousy and insecurity are driving the marriage proposal. Lucien must answer for his own motive rather than treating marriage as already agreed.
