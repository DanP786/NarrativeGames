# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina enters Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard alone through the public entrance, using a plausible mortal pretext rather than sneaking in. She presents herself as a servant sent by her mistress to locate a missing woman, with the uncomfortable possibility that the woman has died and been brought to Saint Orra's among the unclaimed dead. A lay burial keeper receives her without immediate alarm and accepts the premise provisionally, asking Seraphina for the missing woman's name and description before he will check the burial records or recent bodies. From inside the grounds Seraphina can see the ordinary burial yard and a low service outbuilding nearer the rear cart gate; the fresh wheel ruts from the previous night's heavily laden cart continue toward that outbuilding rather than toward the public chapel door. Seraphina has not yet supplied an identity for the supposedly missing woman, entered the service outbuilding, or established what was unloaded there. Sir Garran Vale remains elsewhere in Bellwater and does not witness the deception.
