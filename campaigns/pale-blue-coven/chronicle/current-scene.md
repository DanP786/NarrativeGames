# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina and Sir Garran Vale are together inside the old tannery on Bellwater's east bank. Seraphina crouches beside the concealed floor compartment Garran has pried open and tells him that saying he looked awful was not criticism but concern for his wellbeing because he has not slept. Inside the shallow compartment they find a small packet wrapped in waxed cloth, apparently overlooked when the tannery was recently cleared. The packet contains a narrow tally slip bearing a simple seal shaped like a bell split down the middle and the notation 'Saint Orra — third bell.' The precise meaning of the seal, Saint Orra, and 'third bell' has not yet been established. Garran remains wary of Seraphina's unexplained abilities and motives, but her explicit personal concern registers with him.
