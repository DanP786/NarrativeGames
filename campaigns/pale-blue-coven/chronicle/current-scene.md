# Current scene

Dusk on the 19th of Harvestwane at the concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service approach. Garran has identified Seraphina as a vampire and holds his sword lowered but still in hand while she remains backed against the wall. Seraphina has admitted feeding on a laborer while Garran slept. When pressed whether the man was drunk, Seraphina closes her eyes, tears falling, and answers, 'No.' Garran now knows this most recent victim did not fit the 'drunks mostly' category Seraphina had described, sharpening the question of how and why she chooses victims. He still does not know that the man was a slight warehouse laborer, why Seraphina selected him, how deeply she fed, or that she deliberately stopped short of killing him and left him alive but profoundly weakened. Seraphina continues to answer incriminating questions rather than lie or attack. No three-bell signal or covered rear-lane cart has yet appeared.
