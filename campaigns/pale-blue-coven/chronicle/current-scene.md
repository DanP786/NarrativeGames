# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina has reached the jail in her usual feminine, practical black leather armor, carrying her pair of fighting daggers and wearing a hooded cloak against direct sunlight. After asking for Sir Garran Vale, she learns from the jail guards that Garran brought in the wounded tithe-pit grave robbers earlier that morning, remained long enough to ensure they entered lawful custody, and then left. One guard believes Garran headed east. Seraphina replies, 'Worry not, I know where. Good day, gentlemen,' and leaves on foot, intending to follow Garran toward the old tannery on Bellwater's east bank. Garran has a head start; his exact present location and the current state of the tannery have not yet been established.
