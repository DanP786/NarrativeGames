# Current scene

On the morning of the 19th day of Harvestwane, Seraphina is inside Saint Orra's burial chapel and paupers' yard, investigating alone under the false story that her mistress is seeking a missing woman named Raquelle. After secretly inspecting the rear service outbuilding and discovering processed human remains plus a transport crate bearing the same vertically split-bell emblem as the tannery tally slip, Seraphina hears the lay burial keeper returning from the chapel. She immediately slips out of the outbuilding, restores the door to its prior closed appearance, and returns toward the public portion of the burial yard without being caught inside or seen leaving it. The keeper emerges carrying the recent intake ledger and has no immediate indication that Seraphina entered the service building. He is returning to answer her inquiry about the fictitious Raquelle. The covert remains operation is still not publicly confronted, and Seraphina has not established who controls it or where the processed remains ultimately go. Sir Garran Vale remains elsewhere in Bellwater.
