# Current scene

Seraphina and Lucien have returned to Bellwater's crowded night market after Sir Garran Vale departed. Garran warned Seraphina that someone supplied him with a description of her and falsely associated her with three drained deaths, apparently trying to turn him into a weapon against her. Lucien has the anonymous message Garran gave Seraphina; its exact written wording remains unestablished beyond Garran's report. After confronting Seraphina over her physical closeness with Garran, threatening Garran's life if he finds them together again, and giving Seraphina an enormous gem-encrusted betrothal ring that she now wears, Lucien leads her back into the market to test whether the suspected surveillance remains. Seraphina follows obediently, consciously remembering Lucien's anger. Lucien takes her ringed hand and tells her not to stare for watchers directly but to notice who behaves differently from the surrounding crowd. Seraphina identifies a man near a brewer's awning who appears to be performing drunkenness: he has a cup but scarcely drinks, shifts whenever the crowd would block his view of Seraphina and Lucien, and maintains controlled balance despite exaggerated swaying. Seraphina quietly tells Lucien what she has noticed, including the man's location and the behavioral details that make him suspicious. Lucien therefore now knows which individual Seraphina has marked without needing to turn and stare at him. The man's identity, allegiance, motive, and whether he is one of the previously suspected watchers remain unknown.
