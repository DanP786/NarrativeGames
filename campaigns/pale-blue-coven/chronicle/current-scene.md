# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the cellar beneath the neglected stone building beyond Bellwater. Seraphina silently rises from her crouch behind Garran, one dagger drawn, and waits for his move. Garran initiates the ambush on the nearer worker, closing rapidly and controlling him before he can shout. Seraphina simultaneously reaches the farther worker with predatory speed and precision, seizing him and placing her dagger at his throat without killing him. Both workers are now controlled alive and have not raised an effective alarm. The recently dead young man's partly processed body remains on the heavy table, six bone crates stand against the wall, and the dark deeper passage remains unexplored. Garran has just ordered both prisoners not to make a sound.
