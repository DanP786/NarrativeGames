# Current scene

Night on the 19th of Harvestwane in the neglected yard outside the corpse-processing site beyond Bellwater. Angry after the confrontation over her feeding, Seraphina waits outside while Garran remains below securing the surviving prisoners. Cold night air fills her lungs. She finds a water trough and plunges her hands into it, washing away blood, then peels the leather back from the healing wound in her side and cleans the wound with the cold water. She takes an ordinary clean cloth from her personal effects and binds it around her middle over the injury, replacing the blood-soaked cloak as the immediate dressing. The wound remains incompletely healed but has closed substantially after her full feeding from the dying swordsman. Her hands and side are cleaner, though her clothing and cloak remain bloodstained and battered. Both fighting daggers are in her possession and sheathed. Garran now knows Seraphina fed from both the bound cellar worker and the swordsman in the yard, but he does not know she drank her fill from the swordsman, whether feeding materially contributed to his death, or that an unidentified rival vampire approached her before his arrival. The wounded worker remains alive, the bound cellar worker remains alive, and the captured archer remains alive, hurt, disarmed, and bound. The swordsman in the yard is dead. The rival vampire has departed the immediate yard.
