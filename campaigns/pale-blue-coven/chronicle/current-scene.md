# Current scene

Concealed behind old timber at the near edge of the underground blood-collection chamber, Seraphina studies the hostile personnel specifically for opportunities to remove them one at a time rather than starting an open fight against superior numbers. Her Observation succeeds. Four adult human men are directly involved in the chamber: two remain occupied controlling the first captive at the restraint frame, one works at the blood-collection table, and the original escort moves between the chamber mouth and the others. The two checkpoint guards remain farther back beyond the passage, separate from the chamber group. This puts him near concealment and away from the men restraining the captive, although killing or disabling him without noise would still require Seraphina to cross part of the exposed interval and act quickly. The escort is a poorer first target because his movement keeps him within sight of multiple men. The two men at the frame are presently too close together to remove separately. No attack has yet been made and no one has detected Seraphina.

Five bound adult human captives remain alive; the first is being fixed to a frame. The narrow service passage beyond the worktable still carries cooler air and remains a possible alternate route whose destination/security are unknown. The barred inner gate remains the known route back toward Garran and Frederick, who remain outside without knowing Seraphina's exact position. Hadrik remains unidentified/unconfirmed.
