# Current scene

After a day spent on household duties, confronting Frederick about Bellwater and encouraging the planting of Veyne Hall's neglected garden, Seraphina retires early to her private rooms. After her bath, she sits at the armoire in a linen nightgown with her hair arranged into soft curls. Lucien knocks and enters only after she invites him in. He acknowledges that his attempted return of the ring earlier became another test and asks what she wanted him to do, beginning with the night after Bellwater when he removed the ring and said she would earn it back. Seraphina turns from the mirror to face him and gives a spare answer: she did not want him to end their engagement, and he did not. She offers nothing further about that first moment.

The engagement between Seraphina and Lucien remains intact. Lucien still possesses the gem-encrusted betrothal ring because Seraphina did not accept his attempted return of it. Seraphina remains free to move within Veyne Hall. Frederick remains injured but recovering, and he and Seraphina have now confronted the question of responsibility for Bellwater and his punishment. Christian now has an expected channel for garden requests backed directly by Seraphina. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
