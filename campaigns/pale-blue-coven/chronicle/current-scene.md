# Current scene

While Seraphina waits alone at the Bellwater rendezvous, seated on the parked cart with her black hood down now that the sun is gone, three adult human men notice her. From a distance they read her small stature, solitary position and apparent lack of companions as vulnerability. Their interest is predatory and sexual rather than ordinary flirtation: they approach together expecting that numbers and physical size will let them intimidate or corner her. They do not yet know she is a vampire or that she is wearing fitted red leather armour beneath the cloak and carries two sheathed ivory-handled fighting daggers.

Seraphina notices the three men's approach before they are within immediate grabbing distance. Her novice Observation is sufficient because they are not attempting stealth and their attention is plainly fixed on her.
