# Seraphina

## Identity
- Apparent age: 24
- Actual age: 42
- Nature: vampire
- Current allegiance: Lucien Veyne and the emerging House Veyne by declared choice, formerly the Pale Court; currently travelling away from Veyne Hall while deciding what that allegiance and relationship mean in practice
- Current role: Lucien's betrothed and partner in founding House Veyne, though the intended marriage is no longer secure; prospective maker of Elianor Thorne

## Appearance
Seraphina has honey-blonde hair, blue eyes and a small build suited to speed and agility rather than strength. She enjoys makeup, especially using it to imitate the warmth and colour of living skin, and has learned to use clothing as both self-expression and a social weapon. She owns fitted red leather armour and matched ivory-handled daggers provided by Lucien, but deliberately left both behind when she walked away from Veyne Hall. She is currently travelling in a black civilian ensemble and black cloak with her original fighting daggers.

## Temperament
Seraphina is quiet and observant by nature. She tends to watch before speaking and has spent much of her vampiric life surviving through usefulness, restraint and careful obedience rather than physical dominance. Since joining Lucien she has become increasingly willing to exercise authority and test the difference between obedience she chooses and obedience imposed upon her.

She dislikes needless cruelty and torture. Her instinct is usually to leave feeding victims alive, and she has repeatedly shown concern for mortals who exist within vampire power structures. At House Veyne she pushed for willing sexual relationships rather than concubinage, fair treatment of retainers, reasonable material comfort for the humans of the household and a winter-solstice celebration specifically for them.

## History
Seraphina was born mortal and married off to a lazy farmer who drank too much; the marriage was unhappy and violent. Her village was later attacked by vampires. She was taken by the Pale Court after most of the village was killed and was turned by Lady Yseld Veyr for practical rather than affectionate reasons.

As Yseld's fledgling/spawn, Seraphina learned practiced deference to authority: keeping close behind, yielding space, obeying promptly and making herself useful. Yseld also treated Seraphina's body as available for political and social utility, ordering her to sexually please visiting men of power, both human and vampire. Seraphina later described her Pale Court role as pleasing whoever her master wanted her to please. Privately, she understands those historical encounters as choices she made as duty to the coven while also recognizing that years of role-based sexual compliance blurred her sense of consent.

Seraphina nevertheless became a proven member of the Pale Court. She patrolled, fought, recruited and performed other coven duties, though her restrained feeding and unexplained excursions kept her outside its innermost circle. She has fought both fellow Pale Court vampires in tests and rival-coven vampires in territorial violence.

## Feeding and vampirism
Seraphina dislikes torturing prey and generally prefers feeding without killing. She commonly selects people she can rationalize as deserving some consequence, particularly drunks or men behaving badly toward women, but she has also fed tactically when survival required it.

During the Bellwater investigation she fed briefly from a bound worker to accelerate healing after severe injuries and deliberately left him alive. Later, after a mortally wounded swordsman attempted to sexually assault her, Seraphina drank completely freely from him. It was the first time since her turning that she had allowed herself that degree of unrestricted feeding; he died afterward.

Her skin is unnaturally cold enough that she has avoided casual physical contact when she believed it might expose her nature. Direct sunlight is dangerous enough that she uses a hooded cloak to protect herself, but exact damage thresholds have not yet been established. Her proper hooded sunlight cloak was left behind on the Bellwater operation cart; the ordinary black cloak she currently wears has no established special protection.

## Garran Vale
Seraphina followed Sir Garran Vale secretly for roughly a year before their first direct relationship, drawn to his authority, seriousness, strength, confidence and devotion to duty. During the Bellwater investigation she revealed that she was a vampire and told him, "Because I love you."

Their relationship became increasingly complicated by Garran's oath to kill monsters, Seraphina's feeding, his inability to reconcile her conduct with his doctrine and her eventual allegiance to Lucien. Garran later kissed Seraphina while knowing she was engaged to Lucien. Seraphina did not kiss him back, though she also did not pull away. She subsequently told Lucien plainly, "I do not love Garran anymore. I have chosen you because I want to." That remains Seraphina's declared position. Garran does not know that she said this to Lucien.

When leaving Edric's woodland hut, Seraphina warned the mortal woodsman that if anyone came looking for her he should tell them everything rather than risk himself. She specifically said that unless the seeker went by Sir Vale, Edric should not trust his safety. This does not establish that Garran is actually searching for her.

## Lucien Veyne
Lucien first approached Seraphina as a rival vampire during the Bellwater investigation and offered her another allegiance. She initially refused. After later being taken away from Garran's orbit and offered the choice again, Seraphina eventually accepted Lucien's offer, disclosed relevant Pale Court secrets and entered a consensual blood-bound allegiance with him.

Seraphina accepted Lucien's marriage proposal and regarded herself as choosing him freely. Lucien promised that he would never use her body politically as Yseld had done, would never ask her to give herself sexually to another and would not build House Veyne around institutional concubinage.

Seraphina and Lucien maintain a consensual private dominant/submissive relationship. Seraphina often enjoys deliberately yielding physical control and deference to him in private, while Lucien expects her to exercise independent judgment and authority publicly. This is not blanket consent and does not transform coercion into consensual play merely because the relationship contains chosen submission.

That distinction became central after Bellwater. Seraphina violated Lucien's field rules by separating from Frederick and entering danger alone; Garran then revealed that he had kissed her. Lucien removed Seraphina's gem-encrusted betrothal ring and coercively confined her under lock and key while demanding that she rebuild his trust. Seraphina challenged the premise directly. Lucien acknowledged that locked-door obedience could not constitute genuine trust and restored her freedom of movement within Veyne Hall. Their later consensual intimacy remained distinct from the confinement.

Seraphina then tried to repair the rupture through a playful campaign of chosen intimacy, temptation and private deference intended to communicate, "I still choose you." Lucien initially misunderstood the campaign and continued demanding that trust be earned without giving her a clear path by which to earn it. His attempt to return the ring became another test when he told her to come take it, and she refused. Seraphina later told him she did not know whether she wanted the ring anymore. She explained that giving herself to him had been something she enjoyed and chose, but his asking what more she would do made that gift feel insufficient and made her feel not good enough, worse in that moment than the people who had used her before.

Lucien acknowledged the harm and said he would not ask her to prove her choice through intimacy again. The engagement has not been formally ended, but restoring the ring before trust is genuinely repaired would now hollow out its meaning. After their conversation ended without reconciliation, Seraphina deliberately dressed for travel, chose her old fighting daggers rather than Lucien's gifted pair, left his red armour behind and walked out of Veyne Hall without announcing a destination. She does not currently know whether she wants the ring or whether she will return.

## House Veyne
Seraphina helped shape the emerging coven rather than merely joining Lucien's household. She selected Christian for closer consideration, tested him by placing provisioning in his hands and conditionally offered him stewardship if he proved capable of overseeing retainer welfare and arranging a household winter-solstice celebration. She explicitly told him that House Veyne would not have concubines and that sexual companionship must involve willing participants, whether won by charm or paid fairly.

Mistress Elianor Thorne accepted vampirism in principle after Seraphina explained that turning means actual death followed by awakening, permanent blood hunger, dangerous daylight, difficult early adjustment and an irreversible choice. Elianor chose Seraphina as the vampire she wants to make her. Elianor remains mortal while arranging her affairs.

Frederick serves as Lucien's muscle and was assigned to remain close enough to protect Seraphina in Bellwater. Seraphina deliberately separated from him. Lucien later beat Frederick nearly to death for failing that duty. Seraphina apologized to Frederick for her role; he told her he chose which master to obey when he followed her order to leave, considers Lucien's punishment excessive, and will not obey her next time if doing so would make him fail Lucien's protective duty. Seraphina also told Frederick that Garran kissed her and corrected the idea that she nearly died in Bellwater, saying she had been in control and retreated when outnumbered.

## Bellwater and unresolved threats
Seraphina helped expose an organized network collecting blood from living captives. Twelve captives were freed. The chain leads upward toward Master Hadrik, but Hadrik's exact role and the final recipient or use of the blood remain unknown.

A riverside storehouse east/downstream of Bellwater, identified by a whitewashed gable, broken waterwheel and old millrace, remains a lead. A deception operation was prepared around an expected consignment of twelve sealed bottles: six original human-blood bottles and six animal-blood substitutes. Seraphina left Bellwater with Lucien before the operation was completed. Her proper sunlight cloak remains behind with the operation cart.

Lady Yseld and the Pale Court have not been informed of Seraphina's defection as far as established. Yseld's actual plans and knowledge remain unknown.

## Current state
Seraphina is travelling alone at night on a larger country road west of an isolated woodland hut belonging to a mortal man named Edric. She has no declared final destination. She wears her black civilian travelling ensemble and ordinary black cloak and carries her original fighting daggers. Her coin purse was deliberately left with Edric as payment for shelter.

She remains formally engaged to Lucien, but he still possesses the ring and she does not know whether she wants it. She has physically removed herself from Veyne Hall after their trust rupture rather than continuing to perform or prove her choice. Whether she returns to Lucien and House Veyne, and on what terms, is unresolved.
