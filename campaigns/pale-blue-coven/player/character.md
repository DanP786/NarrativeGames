# Seraphina

- Apparent age: 24
- Actual age: 42
- Nature: vampire
- Coven standing: useful outsider; one of the youngest members, but proven
- Appearance: honey-blonde hair, blue eyes, small build suited to agility rather than strength. She favors black leather with pale-blue fabrics, the colors of her coven. She loves experimenting with makeup and uses it to lend her skin a more living flush.
- Temperament: quiet and observant.
- Feeding: dislikes torturing prey and usually leaves those she feeds from alive. She has not allowed herself to drink completely freely since she was first turned until the full feeding from the mortally wounded swordsman at Bellwater.

## History

Seraphina was taken by her present coven after its vampires killed most of her village. She does not carry simple resentment over being removed from her mortal life: she had been married off to a lazy farmer who drank too much. Her maker turned her for practical reasons rather than affection and expects competence and obedience.

As her maker's fledgling/spawn, Seraphina learned a practiced deference to authority: keeping close behind, yielding space, obeying promptly, and making herself useful. Her maker has also often treated Seraphina's body as something that could be used for political or social utility, ordering her to sexually please visiting men of power, including both humans and vampires from other clans. Seraphina is therefore accustomed to commanded sexual access even though the attempted rape by the wounded swordsman in the Bellwater yard was a distinct act of hostile violence rather than an order from her maker.

## Current state

Seraphina has revealed herself as a vampire to Sir Garran Vale during their Bellwater investigation and confessed that she loves him. She was badly wounded when a crossbow bolt struck her side in the burial tunnels; she tore the bolt free and initially packed the wound with her cloak. During pursuit of an escaping wounded prisoner, she briefly fed from a living worker tied to the cellar processing table, deliberately stopping after a short drink and leaving him alive. Later, after that escaping swordsman attempted to sexually assault her and was mortally wounded in their struggle, Seraphina crawled to him and drank her fill, the first time she has permitted herself to drink that freely since she was first turned. He is dead afterward. The unusually full feeding substantially strengthened Seraphina and accelerated her vampiric healing, though her injuries did not vanish instantaneously and lingering pain remains.

Garran has since learned from the surviving cellar worker that Seraphina fed from him, and Seraphina admitted she did so to heal enough to catch the escaping swordsman. She also admitted to Garran that the fresh blood on her mouth in the yard belonged to the swordsman, but Garran does not know she drank her fill from him or the exact relation between that feeding and his death.

After the confrontation, Seraphina goes outside angry, washes blood from her hands at a trough, cleans the healing wound in her side with the cold water, and binds it with a clean ordinary cloth from her personal effects. The wound has closed substantially but remains incompletely healed and painful. Her blood-soaked cloak is no longer being used as the immediate wound dressing.

After Garran told Seraphina she was not his prisoner and physically left her a route out, she chose not to leave because she would not abandon him in danger. She has unconsciously reproduced some of the deferential habits learned as her maker's spawn while working beside him. The attempted sexual assault has also stirred memories of being ordered by her maker to please powerful visitors. Garran does not know this history or Seraphina's private associations.
