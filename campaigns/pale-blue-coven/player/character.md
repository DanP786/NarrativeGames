# Seraphina

## Identity
- Apparent age: 24
- Actual age: 42
- Nature: vampire
- Current allegiance: Lucien Veyne and the emerging House Veyne; formerly the Pale Court
- Current role: Lucien's betrothed and partner in founding House Veyne; prospective maker of Elianor Thorne

## Appearance
Seraphina has honey-blonde hair, blue eyes and a small build suited to speed and agility rather than strength. She enjoys makeup, especially using it to imitate the warmth and colour of living skin, and increasingly treats clothing as both self-expression and a social weapon. Her martial appearance centers on fitted red leather armour and matched ivory-handled daggers. She also enjoys deliberately striking feminine clothes; her current campaign to recover her betrothal ring has prompted her to commission additional dresses designed to be particularly difficult for Lucien to ignore.

## Temperament
Seraphina is quiet and observant by nature. She tends to watch before speaking and has spent much of her vampiric life surviving through usefulness, restraint and careful obedience rather than physical dominance. Since joining Lucien she has become increasingly willing to exercise authority, tease him openly and test the difference between obedience she chooses and obedience imposed upon her.

She dislikes needless cruelty and torture. Her instinct is usually to leave feeding victims alive, and she has repeatedly shown concern for mortals who exist within vampire power structures. At House Veyne she has pushed for willing sexual relationships rather than concubinage, fair treatment of retainers, reasonable material comfort for the humans of the household and a winter-solstice celebration specifically for them.

## History
Seraphina was born mortal and married off to a lazy farmer who drank too much; the marriage was unhappy and violent. Her village was later attacked by vampires. She was taken by the Pale Court after most of the village was killed and was turned by Lady Yseld Veyr for practical rather than affectionate reasons.

As Yseld's fledgling/spawn, Seraphina learned practiced deference to authority: keeping close behind, yielding space, obeying promptly and making herself useful. Yseld also treated Seraphina's body as available for political and social utility, ordering her to sexually please visiting men of power, both human and vampire. Seraphina later described her Pale Court role as pleasing whoever her master wanted her to please. Privately, she understands those historical encounters as choices she made as duty to the coven while also recognizing that years of role-based sexual compliance blurred her sense of consent.

Seraphina nevertheless became a proven member of the Pale Court. She patrolled, fought, recruited and performed other coven duties, though her restrained feeding and unexplained excursions kept her outside its innermost circle. She has fought both fellow Pale Court vampires in tests and rival-coven vampires in territorial violence.

## Feeding and vampirism
Seraphina dislikes torturing prey and generally prefers feeding without killing. She commonly selects people she can rationalize as deserving some consequence, particularly drunks or men behaving badly toward women, but she has also fed tactically when survival required it.

During the Bellwater investigation she fed briefly from a bound worker to accelerate healing after severe injuries and deliberately left him alive. Later, after a mortally wounded swordsman attempted to sexually assault her, Seraphina drank completely freely from him. It was the first time since her turning that she had allowed herself that degree of unrestricted feeding; he died afterward.

Her skin is unnaturally cold enough that she has avoided casual physical contact when she believed it might expose her nature. Direct sunlight is dangerous enough that she uses a hooded cloak to protect herself, but exact damage thresholds have not yet been established.

## Garran Vale
Seraphina followed Sir Garran Vale secretly for roughly a year before their first direct relationship, drawn to his authority, seriousness, strength, confidence and devotion to duty. During the Bellwater investigation she revealed that she was a vampire and told him, "Because I love you."

Their relationship became increasingly complicated by Garran's oath to kill monsters, Seraphina's feeding, his inability to reconcile her conduct with his doctrine and her eventual allegiance to Lucien. Garran later kissed Seraphina while knowing she was engaged to Lucien. Seraphina did not kiss him back, though she also did not pull away. She subsequently told Lucien plainly, "I do not love Garran anymore. I have chosen you because I want to." That is Seraphina's current declared position.

Old habits and emotional softness around Garran have not vanished automatically, but they do not override that declaration. Garran does not know that Seraphina has told Lucien she no longer loves him.

## Lucien Veyne
Lucien first approached Seraphina as a rival vampire during the Bellwater investigation and offered her another allegiance. She initially refused. After later being taken away from Garran's orbit and offered the choice again, Seraphina eventually accepted Lucien's offer, disclosed relevant Pale Court secrets and entered a consensual blood-bound allegiance with him.

Seraphina accepted Lucien's marriage proposal and regards herself as choosing him freely. Lucien promised that he would never use her body politically as Yseld had done, would never ask her to give herself sexually to another and would not build House Veyne around institutional concubinage.

Seraphina and Lucien maintain a consensual private dominant/submissive relationship. Seraphina often enjoys deliberately yielding physical control and deference to him in private, while Lucien expects her to exercise independent judgment and authority publicly. This is not blanket consent and does not transform coercion into consensual play merely because the relationship contains chosen submission.

That distinction became central after Bellwater. Seraphina violated Lucien's field rules by separating from Frederick and entering danger alone; Garran then revealed that he had kissed her. Lucien removed Seraphina's gem-encrusted betrothal ring and coercively confined her under lock and key while demanding that she rebuild his trust. Seraphina eventually challenged the premise directly. Lucien acknowledged that locked-door obedience could not constitute genuine trust and restored her freedom of movement within Veyne Hall. Their later consensual intimacy is distinct from the confinement.

The engagement remains intact, but Lucien still possesses the ring. Seraphina has turned its recovery into a playful contest: after deliberately tempting Lucien into conceding that he finds her extremely difficult to resist, she announced that the campaign would continue until her ring is returned and has begun commissioning additional striking dresses for that purpose.

## House Veyne
Seraphina is actively shaping the emerging coven rather than merely joining Lucien's household. She selected Christian for closer consideration, tested him by placing provisioning in his hands and has conditionally offered him stewardship if he proves capable of overseeing retainer welfare and arranging a household winter-solstice celebration. She has explicitly told him that House Veyne will not have concubines and that sexual companionship must involve willing participants, whether won by charm or paid fairly.

Mistress Elianor Thorne has accepted vampirism in principle after Seraphina explained that turning means actual death followed by awakening, permanent blood hunger, dangerous daylight, difficult early adjustment and an irreversible choice. Elianor chose Seraphina as the vampire she wants to make her. Elianor remains mortal while arranging her affairs.

Frederick serves as Lucien's muscle and was assigned to remain close enough to protect Seraphina in Bellwater. Seraphina deliberately separated from him. Lucien later beat Frederick nearly to death for failing that duty, though Lucien said Seraphina's own role in causing the separation was why Frederick survived. Seraphina has recognized that she broke more than one person's trust but has not yet spoken to Frederick about his own view; his feelings remain unknown.

## Bellwater and unresolved threats
Seraphina helped expose an organized network collecting blood from living captives. Twelve captives were freed. The chain leads upward toward Master Hadrik, but Hadrik's exact role and the final recipient or use of the blood remain unknown.

A riverside storehouse east/downstream of Bellwater, identified by a whitewashed gable, broken waterwheel and old millrace, remains a lead. A deception operation was prepared around an expected consignment of twelve sealed bottles: six original human-blood bottles and six animal-blood substitutes. Seraphina left Bellwater with Lucien before the operation was completed. Her black sunlight cloak remains behind with the operation cart.

Lady Yseld and the Pale Court have not been informed of Seraphina's defection as far as established. Seraphina expects Yseld to respond indirectly and dangerously rather than simply march openly against House Veyne, but Yseld's actual plans remain unknown.

## Current state
Seraphina is at Veyne Hall and is free to move within it. She is engaged to Lucien, though he still retains her ring while their trust is repaired. She is helping build the new household and has recently begun commissioning additional striking dresses as part of her playful effort to make Lucien surrender the ring.

Her immediate unresolved personal thread is Frederick: he is badly beaten but healing somewhere in Veyne Hall, and Seraphina has not yet asked him whether he believes she broke his trust.
