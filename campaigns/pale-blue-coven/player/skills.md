# Skills

## Master
- None

## Adept
- Agility & dagger fighting (3 ticks) — Seraphina's defining competence; fast and precise at close range, but not built to overpower stronger opponents.

## Novice
- Stealth (1 tick) — practiced through hunting and secretly following the knight.
- Observation (3 ticks) — quiet, watchful, attentive to details and behavior.
- Mortal disguise (0 ticks) — makeup, clothing and practiced behavior used to conceal signs of undeath and pass among humans.

## Notable Untrained
- Raw strength — physically weak by vampiric standards.
- Torture / coercive interrogation — neither practiced nor inclined toward deliberately breaking victims.
