# Personality and reputation

## Perceived character
- Quiet and observant.
- Proven enough to retain a place in the coven despite being among its youngest.
- Somewhat apart from the inner circle.

## Coven reputation
- Useful outsider.
- Known for restrained feeding habits and for generally leaving prey alive.
- Her unexplained long excursions are becoming a liability if noticed.

Other regional and faction reputations are not yet established.
