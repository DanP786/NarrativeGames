# Personality and reputation

## Perceived character
- Quiet, observant and usually controlled.
- Small and physically unimposing by vampiric standards, but a proven close-range fighter whose speed and daggers make underestimating her dangerous.
- More comfortable yielding to chosen authority than commanding by reflex, but increasingly willing to exercise household authority in her own name.
- Shows unusual concern, by vampire standards, for whether mortals survive feeding and whether humans under vampire authority are willing, materially cared for and treated as people rather than consumable property.
- Capable of playful provocation and deliberate sensual confidence with Lucien in private, a marked contrast with her older habit of restrained usefulness.

## Pale Court reputation
- Formerly a useful outsider: proven and accepted, but never fully inside the inner circle.
- Known for restrained feeding habits and for generally leaving prey alive.
- Her repeated unexplained excursions toward Garran had become a liability under Yseld's scrutiny.
- Seraphina has now defected to Lucien Veyne, but the Pale Court's confirmed knowledge of that defection has not yet been established.

## House Veyne reputation
- Lucien's betrothed and partner in founding the emerging coven.
- Exercises real influence over household values and recruitment rather than functioning merely as Lucien's consort.
- Has explicitly rejected institutional concubinage and requires sexual companionship within the household to be voluntary.
- Has taken an active interest in mortal-retainer welfare, provisioning and household morale.
- Selected Christian for greater responsibility and conditionally offered him stewardship.
- Has been chosen by Elianor Thorne as her prospective maker.
- Her Bellwater decision to separate from Frederick and enter danger alone caused a serious trust rupture with Lucien and contributed to Frederick's later punishment.

## Bellwater / Garran circle
- Sir Garran Vale knows Seraphina is a vampire, has fought beside her and has witnessed both her feeding and her repeated protection of human lives.
- Garran's view of her is morally conflicted rather than reducible to his original monster-hunter doctrine.
- Garran kissed Seraphina despite knowing she was Lucien's betrothed; Seraphina did not reciprocate the kiss, though she did not pull away.
- Lucien has since barred Garran from Veyne property and threatened him if he crosses the boundary around Seraphina again.

Other regional and faction reputations remain unestablished unless recorded through play.
