# Lord Othmar Rell

- Status: alive
- Nature: mortal human
- Location: The Pale Court
- Relationship to Seraphina: disappointed former companion for the evening

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Othmar is a wealthy mortal magnate in his early fifties, heavyset and carefully groomed, with grey beginning at his temples. He dresses in costly dark wool and fur and wears several rings that advertise wealth without noble delicacy. His manner is confident and proprietary rather than openly crude; he is accustomed to households making him comfortable because his business is valuable.

## Position and interests
- Controls or holds substantial interests in river toll rights, grain warehouses, and wagon contracts.
- His commercial reach makes him useful to the Pale Court for discreet movement of goods, access to transport, and influence over regional trade.
- Attends the Pale Court ball as an important mortal guest whose goodwill Yseld wants maintained.

## Relationship to Seraphina
- Lady Yseld personally assigned Seraphina to accompany and entertain him through the evening.
- Seraphina was warm, attentive, danced with him, and cultivated the impression that his growing personal interest was welcome.
- Lucien Veyne then interrupted them in a quieter gallery and directly stated, "She is my betrothed."
- Seraphina immediately ended her evening with Othmar, apologizing and telling him that it appeared their evening had concluded.
- Othmar withdraws with controlled dignity but is displeased and confused by the apparent contradiction between Yseld assigning Seraphina to him and Lucien publicly claiming her as his betrothed.

## Knowledge
- Knows Seraphina as a Pale Court woman presented to him personally by Yseld.
- Knows an unfamiliar vampire/man, Lucien Veyne, has directly claimed Seraphina as his betrothed in Seraphina's presence.
- Knows Seraphina did not deny the claim and instead immediately ended her assigned evening with him.
- Does not know Seraphina has recently been forcibly recovered after leaving the Pale Court.
- Does not know about Seraphina's blood oath/allegiance to Lucien, House Veyne, Garran, or the Hadrik investigation.

## GM notes
Othmar has a legitimate political grievance from his own perspective: Yseld assigned him a companion under circumstances he now believes may have concealed an existing betrothal. He is unlikely to create a vulgar public scene, but he may seek a private explanation from Yseld, cool commercial cooperation, or use the embarrassment as leverage. His boundaries and appetites should continue to emerge through play rather than being retroactively assumed.
