# Lord Othmar Rell

- Status: alive
- Nature: mortal human
- Location: The Pale Court
- Relationship to Seraphina: newly introduced guest

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Othmar is a wealthy mortal magnate in his early fifties, heavyset and carefully groomed, with grey beginning at his temples. He dresses in costly dark wool and fur and wears several rings that advertise wealth without noble delicacy. His manner is confident and proprietary rather than openly crude; he is accustomed to households making him comfortable because his business is valuable.

## Position and interests
- Controls or holds substantial interests in river toll rights, grain warehouses, and wagon contracts.
- His commercial reach makes him useful to the Pale Court for discreet movement of goods, access to transport, and influence over regional trade.
- Attends the Pale Court ball as an important mortal guest whose goodwill Yseld wants maintained.

## Relationship to Seraphina
- Has just been introduced to Seraphina by Lady Yseld Veyr.
- Yseld has assigned Seraphina to accompany and entertain him through the evening.
- He does not yet know Seraphina's private history with Yseld or the significance this assignment carries for her.

## Knowledge
- Knows Seraphina as a Pale Court woman presented to him personally by Yseld.
- Does not know Seraphina has recently been forcibly recovered after leaving the Pale Court.
- Does not know about Lucien Veyne, House Veyne, Seraphina's engagement, Garran, or the Hadrik investigation unless told in play.

## GM notes
Othmar's boundaries, appetites, and behavior should emerge through play rather than be assumed from the assignment. Yseld's history makes the situation threatening to Seraphina regardless of whether Othmar himself immediately behaves badly. He is politically useful, not automatically omnipotent, and should have concrete commercial motives beyond sexual access.
