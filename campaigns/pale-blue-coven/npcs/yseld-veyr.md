# Lady Yseld Veyr

- Status: alive
- Nature: vampire
- Location: The Pale Court
- Relationship to Seraphina: Wary; maker and fledgling

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Yseld appears to be a woman in her late thirties, broad-shouldered and severe, with iron-grey hair worn in a single heavy braid. She dresses plainly by the standards of the Pale Court: black wool, pale-blue sash, little jewelry. She speaks in complete, deliberate sentences and dislikes rhetorical games. When displeased, she becomes quieter rather than louder. She habitually cleans or repairs useful objects while speaking, as if conversation is not sufficient reason for idle hands.

## Traits
- Practical to the point of cruelty
- Patient
- Values usefulness over affection
- Dislikes waste and public disorder

## Short-term goals
- Determine the identity, allegiance, competence, and current knowledge of the unnamed knight Seraphina says was hunting the necromancer.
- Establish exactly what the knight saw of Seraphina's abilities and what Seraphina told him about herself.
- Learn the corpse source, intended numbers, and whether the necromancer was part of a larger operation.
- Determine what Seraphina is still omitting about her repeated interest in the affair.

## Long-term goals
- Preserve and strengthen the Pale Court's position against mortal hunters and rival covens.
- Ensure that vampires she has personally sponsored or made justify the resources invested in them.

## Methods & lines
- Will: question directly, surveil, impose duties, threaten consequences, use violence when efficient.
- Won't: indulge cruelty merely for pleasure; publicly humiliate one of her own without strategic reason.
- Last resort: destroy a fledgling she believes has become an uncontrollable existential threat to the coven.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 35 / 100 — Seraphina gives useful operational facts but claims she did not catch the knight's name despite direct contact; Yseld cannot prove the claim false, but finds the omission troubling.
- Affection: 20 / 100 — unchanged.
- Respect: 58 / 100 — Seraphina reports eliminating an immediate threat and the necromancer after he recognized her nature, which is tactically defensible.
- Loyalty: 25 / 100 — useful reporting is offset by concern that Seraphina may be withholding information about the mortal hunter.
- Fear: 5 / 100 — unchanged.
- Suspicion: 72 / 100 — Seraphina admits she is now personally known to the knight, says the necromancer recognized what she was, and nevertheless claims not to know the knight's name. Combined with the prior unexplained excursions, Yseld sees a potentially serious gap in the story.

## Relationship to player
**Wary.** Seraphina reports that she initially intended only to observe, intervened when a threat emerged, killed the necromancer because he recognized what she was, and is now personally known to the knight. She claims she did not catch the knight's name. Yseld has no direct evidence that this last statement is false, but direct contact with an unidentified mortal hunter creates a security concern and sharpens her suspicion that Seraphina is withholding something.

## History with player
- Yseld participated in the Pale Court's destruction of Seraphina's village eighteen years ago.
- She chose to turn Seraphina for practical reasons and trained her without offering much affection.
- Seraphina has since proven herself useful enough to retain standing despite remaining outside the coven's inner circle.
- S01: Seraphina initially explained her repeated excursions by revealing a human necromancer attempting to reanimate a horde of zombies.
- S01: Yseld ordered Seraphina to investigate the necromancer's numbers, corpse source, and whether anyone else was hunting him, then report directly.
- S01: Seraphina returns and reports that she overheard a knight was hunting the necromancer, that the necromancer successfully animated corpses, and that both he and the raised dead are now destroyed.
- S01: Seraphina says she intended only to observe but intervened against a threat, killed the necromancer after he recognized her nature, admits the knight now knows her personally, and claims she did not catch his name.

## Knowledge
- Knows Seraphina has been making increasingly long excursions away from the Pale Court.
- Knows a human necromancer in that region succeeded in animating corpses and is now dead.
- Knows Seraphina overheard while feeding that a knight was hunting the necromancer and says this prompted her to return under Yseld's investigation order.
- Knows Seraphina says she initially intended only to observe but intervened when there was a threat.
- Knows Seraphina says she killed the necromancer because he recognized what she was.
- Knows the knight has now met Seraphina personally and has seen at least some of her involvement in the violence.
- Believes, based on Seraphina's report, that the knight's name is unknown to Seraphina; Yseld has no proof otherwise.
- Does not know the knight is Sir Garran Vale.
- Does not yet know precisely what supernatural abilities the knight witnessed or what Seraphina told him about herself.
- Does not yet know the intended raising involved roughly two dozen corpses, the tithe pit supplied remains, or that a larger bone trade leads toward an old tannery in Bellwater.
- Does not know the full nature of Seraphina's feelings for the knight.
- Still suspects Seraphina's stated reasons may not fully explain the intensity of her repeated journeys.
- Is hiding how she learned the pattern of Seraphina's excursions and whether anyone else in the coven knows.
