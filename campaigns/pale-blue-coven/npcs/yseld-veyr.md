# Lady Yseld Veyr

- Status: alive
- Nature: vampire
- Location: The Pale Court
- Relationship to Seraphina: Wary; maker and fledgling

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Yseld appears to be a woman in her late thirties, broad-shouldered and severe, with iron-grey hair worn in a single heavy braid. She dresses plainly by the standards of the Pale Court: black wool, pale-blue sash, little jewelry. She speaks in complete, deliberate sentences and dislikes rhetorical games. When displeased, she becomes quieter rather than louder. She habitually cleans or repairs useful objects while speaking, as if conversation is not sufficient reason for idle hands.

## Traits
- Practical to the point of cruelty
- Patient
- Values usefulness over affection
- Dislikes waste and public disorder

## Short-term goals
- Learn where Seraphina has been going and why her excursions have become so frequent.
- Determine whether Seraphina has created a threat to the Pale Court.

## Long-term goals
- Preserve and strengthen the Pale Court's position against mortal hunters and rival covens.
- Ensure that vampires she has personally sponsored or made justify the resources invested in them.

## Methods & lines
- Will: question directly, surveil, impose duties, threaten consequences, use violence when efficient.
- Won't: indulge cruelty merely for pleasure; publicly humiliate one of her own without strategic reason.
- Last resort: destroy a fledgling she believes has become an uncontrollable existential threat to the coven.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 35 / 100 — Seraphina is competent, but unexplained absences have made her unreliable in Yseld's eyes.
- Affection: 20 / 100 — little maternal attachment; Seraphina is primarily a choice Yseld expects to prove worthwhile.
- Respect: 55 / 100 — Seraphina has proven useful despite her youth and physical limitations.
- Loyalty: 25 / 100 — Yseld will protect her creation while that protection serves coven order, not from unconditional devotion.
- Fear: 5 / 100 — Yseld does not regard Seraphina as physically threatening to her.
- Suspicion: 65 / 100 — the repeated excursions have become a pattern and Yseld assumes Seraphina is concealing something consequential.

## Relationship to player
**Wary.** Yseld regards Seraphina as a successful but increasingly troublesome investment. She does not need obedience for its own sake; she needs Seraphina's choices not to endanger the Pale Court. The fact that Seraphina has begun keeping a pattern of behavior from her irritates Yseld more than youthful independence would.

## History with player
- Yseld participated in the Pale Court's destruction of Seraphina's village eighteen years ago.
- She chose to turn Seraphina for practical reasons and trained her without offering much affection.
- Seraphina has since proven herself useful enough to retain standing despite remaining outside the coven's inner circle.

## Knowledge
- Knows Seraphina has been making increasingly long excursions away from the Pale Court.
- Has enough evidence to know the journeys are deliberate and repetitive rather than ordinary hunting.
- Does not yet know the full nature of Seraphina's feelings for the knight.
- Suspects the repeated journeys concern a specific person, place, or hidden obligation.
- Is hiding how she learned the pattern and whether anyone else in the coven knows.
