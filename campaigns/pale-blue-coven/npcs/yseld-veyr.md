# Lady Yseld Veyr

- Status: alive
- Nature: vampire
- Location: The Pale Court
- Relationship to Seraphina: Wary; maker and fledgling

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Yseld appears to be a woman in her late thirties, broad-shouldered and severe, with iron-grey hair worn in a single heavy braid. She dresses plainly by the standards of the Pale Court: black wool, pale-blue sash, little jewelry. She speaks in complete, deliberate sentences and dislikes rhetorical games. When displeased, she becomes quieter rather than louder. She habitually cleans or repairs useful objects while speaking, as if conversation is not sufficient reason for idle hands.

## Traits
- Practical to the point of cruelty
- Patient
- Values usefulness over affection
- Dislikes waste and public disorder

## Short-term goals
- Determine the identity, allegiance, competence, and current knowledge of the knight Seraphina says was hunting the necromancer.
- Establish exactly how the necromancer and animated corpses were destroyed and what Seraphina did during the confrontation.
- Learn the corpse source, intended numbers, and whether the necromancer was part of a larger operation.
- Determine what Seraphina is still omitting about her repeated interest in the affair.

## Long-term goals
- Preserve and strengthen the Pale Court's position against mortal hunters and rival covens.
- Ensure that vampires she has personally sponsored or made justify the resources invested in them.

## Methods & lines
- Will: question directly, surveil, impose duties, threaten consequences, use violence when efficient.
- Won't: indulge cruelty merely for pleasure; publicly humiliate one of her own without strategic reason.
- Last resort: destroy a fledgling she believes has become an uncontrollable existential threat to the coven.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 38 / 100 — Seraphina promptly reports the existence of another hunter and confirms the necromancer successfully animated corpses, materially answering part of Yseld's order.
- Affection: 20 / 100 — unchanged.
- Respect: 57 / 100 — Seraphina investigated as ordered and returned with useful confirmation that the threat was real and is now destroyed.
- Loyalty: 26 / 100 — direct reporting of the knight is a small sign of compliance with coven interests.
- Fear: 5 / 100 — unchanged.
- Suspicion: 66 / 100 — the newly disclosed knight gives Yseld a concrete missing element behind the affair and immediately raises questions about Seraphina's contact with him and the circumstances of the deaths.

## Relationship to player
**Wary.** Seraphina has returned from the investigation and begun reporting directly as ordered. She says she overheard while feeding that a knight was hunting the necromancer, went back out because Yseld had ordered an investigation, found that the necromancer had actually animated corpses, and reports that both necromancer and raised dead are now destroyed. Yseld regards this as useful but incomplete: she now needs the knight's identity and the exact circumstances of the destruction.

## History with player
- Yseld participated in the Pale Court's destruction of Seraphina's village eighteen years ago.
- She chose to turn Seraphina for practical reasons and trained her without offering much affection.
- Seraphina has since proven herself useful enough to retain standing despite remaining outside the coven's inner circle.
- S01: Seraphina initially explained her repeated excursions by revealing a human necromancer attempting to reanimate a horde of zombies.
- S01: Yseld ordered Seraphina to investigate the necromancer's numbers, corpse source, and whether anyone else was hunting him, then report directly.
- S01: Seraphina returns and reports that she overheard a knight was hunting the necromancer, that the necromancer successfully animated corpses, and that both he and the raised dead are now destroyed.

- Knows a human necromancer in that region succeeded in animating corpses.
