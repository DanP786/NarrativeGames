# Sir Garran Vale

- Status: alive
- Nature: human
- Role: sworn knight and monster-hunter
- Location: concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service lane; sword in hand facing Seraphina, who is backed against a wall
- Relationship to Seraphina: Hostile uncertainty; fear and revulsion remain, but her explanation increasingly fits her observable conduct

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Seraphina knows him as a man of authority, seriousness, physical strength and confidence. He carries himself with the discipline of someone who treats duty as obligation rather than performance. In immediate danger he gives short, practical commands and prioritizes the threat at hand over argument. He does not readily accept explanations merely because they are possible; when he thinks someone is evading him, he tests the story against concrete evidence. His humor, when it surfaces, is dry and understated. In questioning, he prefers specific facts, controlled silence, and evidence rather than theatrical threats.

## Traits
- Serious
- Confident
- Dutiful
- Physically formidable by ordinary human standards
- Committed to hunting monsters

## Short-term goals
- Determine whether Seraphina presents an immediate threat.
- Test Seraphina's claim that she followed him only because she wanted him safe against the evidence of the past year and their recent cooperation.
- Learn what changed once her coven discovered her interest and whether they later ordered her to remain close.
- Decide what his oath requires when confronted with a vampire whose observable conduct toward him and other humans does not fit his simplest assumptions.

## Long-term goals
- Fulfil his oath to hunt and destroy monsters.

## Methods & lines
- In immediate danger, gives short practical commands and prioritizes the active threat.
- When an explanation seems incomplete, tests it with concrete follow-up questions rather than immediately accepting or rejecting it without evidence.
- In interrogation, uses specific questions, known evidence, and uncomfortable silence before escalating force.
- Existing oath and conduct establish that he is willing to pursue dangerous supernatural threats personally.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 14 / 100 — her explanation aligns with multiple concrete choices he personally witnessed, though the year of concealment remains severe.
- Affection: 30 / 100 — the claim that she wanted nothing except his safety resonates painfully with the concern she repeatedly showed him.
- Respect: 55 / 100 — her continued refusal to attack under threat and her consistent protection of others remain difficult to dismiss.
- Loyalty: 4 / 100 — slight movement because her actions toward him have repeatedly been protective, but coven allegiance remains unresolved.
- Fear: 59 / 100 — still high, though her continued nonviolence makes immediate attack seem less certain.
- Suspicion: 89 / 100 — still profound, but the predatory explanation no longer accounts cleanly for her conduct.

## Relationship to player
**Hostile uncertainty.** Seraphina says she wanted nothing from Garran and followed him only to know he was safe. Garran cannot simply accept that from a vampire who concealed herself for a year, but the claim matches too many things he personally witnessed to dismiss outright. His oath, fear, and disgust remain in conflict with accumulating evidence that her concern for him may have been genuine.

- S01: Seraphina follows Garran to the necromancer's ruined croft and intervenes when the risen dead begin surrounding him.
- S01: Seraphina and Garran kill the necromancer and break his raising, then discover his ledger and the tithe-pit lead.

- S01: Seraphina independently exposes Saint Orra's covert body-processing operation and returns to Garran as promised.

- S01: Garran identifies Seraphina as a vampire and draws his sword.
- S01: Garran accuses Seraphina of preparing him as prey. She denies it without drawing a weapon.
- S01: Seraphina says, 'Because I love you.' Garran initially refuses to believe her.
