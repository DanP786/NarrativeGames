# Sir Garran Vale

- Status: alive
- Nature: human
- Role: sworn knight and monster-hunter
- Location: concealed observation position overlooking Saint Orra's rear service lane, sword drawn on Seraphina
- Relationship to Seraphina: Hostile; revulsion and fear now dominate unresolved personal attachment

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Seraphina knows him as a man of authority, seriousness, physical strength and confidence. He carries himself with the discipline of someone who treats duty as obligation rather than performance. In immediate danger he gives short, practical commands and prioritizes the threat at hand over argument. He does not readily accept explanations merely because they are possible; when he thinks someone is evading him, he tests the story against concrete evidence. His humor, when it surfaces, is dry and understated. In questioning, he prefers specific facts, controlled silence, and evidence rather than theatrical threats.

## Traits
- Serious
- Confident
- Dutiful
- Physically formidable by ordinary human standards
- Committed to hunting monsters

## Short-term goals
- Determine whether Seraphina intended to feed on him and whether she presents an immediate threat.
- Force an explanation for her repeated proximity, personal concern, and deception about her nature.
- Reconcile, if possible, her probable vampirism with her repeated actions protecting him and preserving human lives.

## Long-term goals
- Fulfil his oath to hunt and destroy monsters.

## Methods & lines
- In immediate danger, gives short practical commands and prioritizes the active threat.
- When an explanation seems incomplete, tests it with concrete follow-up questions rather than immediately accusing without evidence.
- In interrogation, uses specific questions, known evidence, and uncomfortable silence before escalating force.
- Existing oath and conduct establish that he is willing to pursue dangerous supernatural threats personally.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 10 / 100 — Garran now reinterprets much of Seraphina's unexplained intimacy and concern through the possibility that he was being cultivated as prey.
- Affection: 24 / 100 — attachment remains underneath the shock but is badly wounded by fear and revulsion.
- Respect: 52 / 100 — her prior competence and restraint remain facts, though Garran now questions their motives.
- Loyalty: 4 / 100 — nearly severed by the revelation and perceived deception.
- Fear: 52 / 100 — Garran believes a vampire of demonstrated unnatural speed has deliberately remained close to him for an extended period.
- Suspicion: 97 / 100 — he is convinced she is a vampire and now suspects predatory intent behind her personal attention.

## Relationship to player
**Hostile.** Garran is disgusted and frightened by the realization that Seraphina is probably a vampire. He has begun reinterpreting her care as possible predatory preparation, including her insistence that he eat and rest. Yet her history of saving his life, preserving human prisoners, and now refusing to attack despite his drawn sword remains contradictory evidence he cannot erase.

## History with player
- Before S01: Seraphina repeatedly followed Garran from afar, sometimes travelling far from the Pale Court to do so; Garran does not know this.
- S01: Seraphina follows Garran to the necromancer's ruined croft and intervenes when the risen dead begin surrounding him.
- S01: Seraphina and Garran kill the necromancer and break his raising, then discover his ledger and the tithe-pit lead.
- S01: Their cooperation continues through the tithe-pit investigation, where Seraphina obeys Garran's demand for restraint and protects him and the prisoners.
- S01: Seraphina repeatedly expresses concern for Garran's wellbeing, tells him to eat and sleep, and takes over the daylight watch while he rests.
- S01: Seraphina independently exposes Saint Orra's covert body-processing operation and returns to Garran as promised.
- S01: At their concealed watch position, Seraphina tells Garran his work is important and people are safer with him around.
- S01: When Garran asks whether she is sworn to fight monsters, Seraphina says only, 'I fight monsters, yes.'
- S01: Garran connects her extraordinary speed, combat ability, avoided handshake, unusual tolerance of cold, daylight hooding, and silvery eye reflection and draws his sword, concluding she is a vampire.
- S01: Garran checks his neck and accuses Seraphina of having kept him clean, fed, and rested in preparation to drink his blood. Seraphina shakes her head and says, 'No,' without drawing a weapon or attacking.

## Knowledge
- Knows Seraphina's name and that she offered 'Sera' as the name he may call her.
- Knows Seraphina repeatedly protected him, preserved human lives when asked, exposed the corpse trade, and returned as promised.
- Knows Seraphina says his work is important and people are safer with him around.
- Knows Seraphina says, 'I fight monsters, yes,' but avoided saying whether she is sworn to do so.
- Has personally witnessed Seraphina's extraordinary pursuit speed and precise combat ability.
- Knows Seraphina declined his offered handshake and now suspects she did so to conceal unnaturally cold skin.
- Has observed her hooding against daylight, apparent indifference to cold, and silvery eye reflection in failing light.
- Strongly concludes Seraphina is a vampire.
- Suspects her insistence that he eat, rest, and maintain himself may have been preparation to feed on him; this is Garran's inference, not established fact.
- Has seen Seraphina deny that accusation by shaking her head and saying, 'No,' while remaining unarmed under his drawn sword.
- Does not know Seraphina belongs to the Pale Court.
- Does not know Seraphina's maker or history.
- Does not know Seraphina fed on a warehouse laborer while he slept.
- Does not know Seraphina has followed him since before their first direct meeting.
- Does not know Seraphina loves him or that his disgust confirms a fear she has carried while loving him from afar.
