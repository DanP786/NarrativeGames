# Sir Garran Vale

- Status: alive
- Nature: human
- Role: sworn knight and monster-hunter
- Location: inside the ruined croft north beyond the old gallows road
- Relationship to Seraphina: Wary; unknown woman who intervened in his fight

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Not yet fully established in play. Seraphina knows him from prolonged observation as a man of authority, seriousness, physical strength and confidence. He carries himself with the discipline of someone who treats duty as obligation rather than performance. In immediate danger he gives short, practical commands and prioritizes the threat at hand over argument.

## Traits
- Serious
- Confident
- Dutiful
- Physically formidable by ordinary human standards
- Committed to hunting monsters

## Short-term goals
- Determine the location and nature of the 'tithe pit' referenced by both the necromancer and his ledger.
- Ensure the disrupted raising cannot resume and deal with any remaining active dead.
- Determine who the unknown woman with the dagger is and why she is here.

## Long-term goals
- Fulfil his oath to hunt and destroy monsters.

## Methods & lines
- In immediate danger, gives short practical commands and prioritizes the active threat.
- Existing oath and conduct establish that he is willing to pursue dangerous supernatural threats personally.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 8 / 100 — Seraphina voluntarily brings him material evidence rather than concealing it, though her identity remains unexplained.
- Affection: 0 / 100 — no personal relationship yet.
- Respect: 20 / 100 — her intervention and search of the ritual site continue to produce useful results.
- Loyalty: 0 / 100 — no established bond.
- Fear: 5 / 100 — her speed and inexplicable familiarity with his concealed weapon remain concerning.
- Suspicion: 43 / 100 — she still has knowledge of him that a stranger should not obviously possess and has not identified herself.

## Relationship to player
**Wary.** Garran has now seen Seraphina directly after she emerged from concealment and killed a risen corpse approaching his back. She then threw her own dagger into the necromancer and, while passing Garran, took the concealed dagger from his boot with disturbing familiarity. When Garran demanded her identity she deflected toward the dying necromancer. She subsequently searched the ritual site and voluntarily brought Garran the necromancer's ledger, providing evidence about a second corpse source called the 'tithe pit.' He remains suspicious but recognizes that she is materially aiding his hunt.

## History with player
- Before S01: Seraphina repeatedly followed Garran from afar, sometimes travelling far from the Pale Court to do so.
- S01: Seraphina follows Garran to the necromancer's ruined croft and intervenes when the risen dead begin surrounding him.
- S01: Seraphina kills a risen corpse approaching Garran from behind, revealing herself to him for the first time.
- S01: Seraphina throws a dagger into the necromancer's upper body, disrupting his ritual, then takes Garran's concealed boot dagger as she rushes toward the croft.
- S01: Seraphina wounds the necromancer severely with Garran's dagger. When Garran demands to know who she is, she points out that the necromancer may die before he can be questioned and physically turns the wounded man's attention toward Garran.
- S01: Seraphina finds the necromancer's ledger near the ritual site and brings it to Garran; together its entries and the necromancer's reaction establish 'the tithe pit' as an important lead and additional corpse source.

## Knowledge
- Knows a necromancer is attempting to raise the dead and is actively hunting that threat.
- Now knows from the ledger that the necromancer intended a force of roughly two dozen corpses and that disturbed public graveyards were not the only source.
- Knows the ledger repeatedly identifies an additional corpse source as 'the tithe pit,' marked with a crude crown/line/crosses symbol, though its meaning and location remain unknown.
- Has now seen Seraphina and knows she is highly capable with a dagger, willing to attack the necromancer, and inexplicably knew where he kept a concealed boot dagger.
- Knows Seraphina avoided answering who she is when first asked.
- Does not know Seraphina is a vampire.
- Does not know Seraphina has been following him.
- Does not know Seraphina's feelings for him.
