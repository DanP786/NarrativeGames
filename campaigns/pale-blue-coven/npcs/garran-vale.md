# Sir Garran Vale

- Status: alive
- Nature: human
- Role: sworn knight and monster-hunter
- Location: ruined croft north beyond the old gallows road; engaged with risen dead
- Relationship to Seraphina: Wary; unknown woman who intervened in his fight

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Not yet fully established in play. Seraphina knows him from prolonged observation as a man of authority, seriousness, physical strength and confidence. He carries himself with the discipline of someone who treats duty as obligation rather than performance.

## Traits
- Serious
- Confident
- Dutiful
- Physically formidable by ordinary human standards
- Committed to hunting monsters

## Short-term goals
- Find and stop the human attempting to raise a horde of zombies.
- Survive the encirclement at the ruined croft.
- Determine who the unknown woman with the dagger is and why she is here.

## Long-term goals
- Fulfil his oath to hunt and destroy monsters.

## Methods & lines
To be established through play. His existing oath and conduct establish that he is willing to pursue dangerous supernatural threats personally.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 4 / 100 — the unknown woman has just saved him from a corpse attacking his blind side, but her presence is unexplained.
- Affection: 0 / 100 — no personal relationship yet.
- Respect: 8 / 100 — he has witnessed her kill a risen corpse with speed and precision.
- Loyalty: 0 / 100 — no established bond.
- Fear: 2 / 100 — her unusual speed registers as potentially dangerous, though the fight gives him little time to assess it.
- Suspicion: 28 / 100 — a finely dressed armed woman appearing alone at a necromancer's remote croft in the middle of the night requires explanation.

## Relationship to player
**Wary.** Garran has now seen Seraphina directly for the first time after she emerged from concealment and killed a risen corpse approaching his back. He does not know her name or nature and has no idea she has followed him before.

## History with player
- Before S01: Seraphina repeatedly followed Garran from afar, sometimes travelling far from the Pale Court to do so.
- S01: Garran is hunting the same necromancer whose activities Seraphina used to explain her excursions to Yseld.
- S01: Soldiers at the Pike and Crown report that Garran left town pursuing the necromancer north beyond the old gallows road, apparently without waiting for ordinary soldiers to accompany him.
- S01: Seraphina learns the necromancer is attempting to raise roughly two dozen corpses and believes Garran does not know the full number he may face; she follows his trail north.
- S01: At the ruined croft Garran engages the risen dead alone. Seraphina watches unseen as additional corpses emerge around his flanks and rear.
- S01: Seraphina emerges and kills a risen corpse approaching Garran from behind, revealing herself to him for the first time.

## Knowledge
- Knows a necromancer is attempting to raise the dead and is actively hunting that threat.
- His awareness of the necromancer's roughly two-dozen intended corpses is not established; Seraphina currently believes he does not know the full number.
- Has now seen Seraphina and knows she is armed, highly capable with a dagger, and present at the ruined croft for unknown reasons.
- Does not know Seraphina is a vampire.
- Does not know Seraphina has been following him.
- Does not know Seraphina's feelings for him.
