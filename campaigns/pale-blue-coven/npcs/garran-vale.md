# Sir Garran Vale

- Status: alive
- Nature: human
- Role: sworn knight and monster-hunter
- Location: field around the ruined croft north beyond the old gallows road; clearing remaining risen dead with Seraphina
- Relationship to Seraphina: Wary; newly introduced ally of uncertain origin

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Not yet fully established in play. Seraphina knows him from prolonged observation as a man of authority, seriousness, physical strength and confidence. He carries himself with the discipline of someone who treats duty as obligation rather than performance. In immediate danger he gives short, practical commands and prioritizes the threat at hand over argument.

## Traits
- Serious
- Confident
- Dutiful
- Physically formidable by ordinary human standards
- Committed to hunting monsters

## Short-term goals
- Determine the location and nature of the 'tithe pit' referenced by both the necromancer and his ledger.
- Ensure the disrupted raising cannot resume and deal with any remaining active dead.
- Learn who Seraphina is, why she was at the croft, and whether her explanation for knowing his concealed dagger is credible.

## Long-term goals
- Fulfil his oath to hunt and destroy monsters.

## Methods & lines
- In immediate danger, gives short practical commands and prioritizes the active threat.
- Existing oath and conduct establish that he is willing to pursue dangerous supernatural threats personally.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 10 / 100 — Seraphina has materially aided him and returned his weapon, but offers only a plausible general explanation for locating it.
- Affection: 2 / 100 — her amused confidence registers personally, though no meaningful bond exists.
- Respect: 21 / 100 — her combat effectiveness and practical conduct remain established.
- Loyalty: 0 / 100 — no established bond.
- Fear: 5 / 100 — her speed remains concerning but no new supernatural evidence has appeared.
- Suspicion: 39 / 100 — her claim that a boot is a common hiding place for a knife is plausible enough to weaken that single point, but her wider presence and capabilities remain unexplained.

## Relationship to player
**Wary.** Garran knows Seraphina by name and is clearing the remaining risen dead alongside her. When challenged about how she knew where his concealed boot dagger was, Seraphina answers with amused confidence that hiding a knife there is hardly unique. The explanation is plausible but does not resolve Garran's broader questions about her.

## History with player
- Before S01: Seraphina repeatedly followed Garran from afar, sometimes travelling far from the Pale Court to do so.
- S01: Seraphina follows Garran to the necromancer's ruined croft and intervenes when the risen dead begin surrounding him.
- S01: Seraphina kills a risen corpse approaching Garran from behind, revealing herself to him for the first time.
- S01: Seraphina throws a dagger into the necromancer's upper body, disrupting his ritual, then takes Garran's concealed boot dagger as she rushes toward the croft.
- S01: Seraphina wounds the necromancer severely with Garran's dagger and redirects Garran toward questioning him before death.
- S01: Seraphina finds the necromancer's ledger and brings it to Garran, establishing 'the tithe pit' as an important lead and additional corpse source.
- S01: Seraphina introduces herself by name, offers 'Sera' as what Garran may call her, cleans his dagger and returns it hilt-first.
- S01: While they clear the remaining dead, Seraphina answers Garran's question about his hidden knife by teasing that he is hardly unique in hiding one in his boot.

## Knowledge
- Knows Seraphina's name and that she offered 'Sera' as the name he may call her.
- Knows a necromancer was attempting to raise the dead and has now been killed.
- Knows from the ledger that the necromancer intended a force of roughly two dozen corpses and that disturbed public graveyards were not the only source.
- Knows the ledger repeatedly identifies an additional corpse source as 'the tithe pit,' marked with a crude crown/line/crosses symbol, though its meaning and location remain unknown.
- Knows Seraphina is highly capable with a dagger and willing to attack the necromancer.
- Has heard Seraphina explain her quick discovery of his boot dagger as recognition of a common hiding place rather than specific prior knowledge.
- Does not know Seraphina is a vampire.
- Does not know Seraphina has been following him.
- Does not know Seraphina's feelings for him.
