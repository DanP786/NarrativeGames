## Description, voice & mannerisms
Mara is a striking woman with long, straight black hair. Her voice is low and sultry where Seraphina's is soft. She carries herself with confidence and speaks as though accustomed to being obeyed. On Seraphina's first sight of her, Mara shows easy physical familiarity with Lucien by placing a hand on his arm while addressing Garran.

Mara is observant about appearance and willing to make judgments visible rather than conceal them behind perfect courtesy. On Seraphina's return she appraises the travel-disordered hair and battered formal gown with obvious judgment.

**Unknown and immediately tense.** Mara is a new addition to Veyne Hall during Seraphina's absence. Seraphina first encounters her when Mara places a hand on Lucien's other arm and authoritatively tells Garran he is not welcome. Seraphina responds by removing her own hand from Lucien noticeably quickly.
