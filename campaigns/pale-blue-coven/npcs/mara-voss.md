# Mara Voss

- Status: alive
- Nature: mortal human
- Role: temporary household administrator and organizer at Veyne Hall
- Location: Veyne Hall
- Relationship to Seraphina: Unknown; first meeting tense and competitive

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Mara is a striking woman with long, straight black hair. Her voice is low and sultry where Seraphina's is soft. She carries herself with confidence and speaks as though accustomed to being obeyed. She is observant about appearance and willing to make judgments visible rather than conceal them behind perfect courtesy.

## Background and role
Mara is a mortal gentlewoman from a minor but well-connected merchant-gentry family. She knew Lucien socially before Seraphina joined House Veyne, but they have never been lovers and have no established sexual history.

Mara has practical experience managing a substantial household. During Seraphina's disappearance Lucien repeatedly left Veyne Hall to search for her, Christian was not yet ready to carry full stewardship, and household order began to suffer. Lucien called Mara to Veyne Hall as a temporary administrator and organizer.

Her intended authority covers servants, stores, schedules, guests and routine household administration. It does not place her above Seraphina, give her authority over Seraphina's private rooms or wardrobe, or make her a consort of Lucien. Lucien failed to define these limits tightly enough before Seraphina returned, and Mara overstepped by entering Seraphina's private domestic sphere and arranging her bath and clothing without invitation.

## Traits

## Short-term goals
- Keep Veyne Hall functioning efficiently while her temporary role continues.
- Establish what authority Seraphina intends to exercise now that she has returned.
- Demonstrate competence and standing without directly outranking Seraphina.

## Methods & lines
- Will: speak directly and with authority when she believes household rules are being breached.
- Will: arrange practical household matters without waiting to be asked when she believes they are obvious needs.
- Will: allow a judgmental look or pointed phrasing to communicate criticism.
- Won't: outrank Seraphina within House Veyne merely by virtue of administrative responsibility.
- Won't: claim a sexual relationship with Lucien that does not exist.
- Last resort: not yet established.

## Relationship to player
**Unknown and tense.** Mara's first meeting with Seraphina became territorial almost immediately. Her physical familiarity with Lucien and unsolicited handling of Seraphina's bath, clothing and private rooms provoked visible withdrawal and later jealousy from Seraphina. Seraphina subsequently established that she intends to possess genuine lady-of-the-house authority rather than leave that space by default to Mara.

Mara delivered the sealed Pale Court letter to Lucien's office and Seraphina dismissed her from the room. Mara recognized the instruction, addressed her as "Lady Seraphina," and withdrew. Whether this becomes open rivalry, professional accommodation or something else remains unestablished.
