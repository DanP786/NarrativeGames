# Mara Voss

- Status: alive
- Nature: mortal human
- Role: temporary household administrator and organizer at Veyne Hall
- Location: Veyne Hall
- Relationship to Seraphina: Unknown; first meeting tense and competitive

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Mara is a striking woman with long, straight black hair. Her voice is low and sultry where Seraphina's is soft. She carries herself with confidence and speaks as though accustomed to being obeyed. On Seraphina's first sight of her, Mara shows easy physical familiarity with Lucien by placing a hand on his arm while addressing Garran.

Mara is observant about appearance and willing to make judgments visible rather than conceal them behind perfect courtesy. On Seraphina's return she appraises the travel-disordered hair and battered formal gown with obvious judgment.

## Background and role
Mara is a mortal gentlewoman from a minor but well-connected merchant-gentry family. She knew Lucien socially before Seraphina joined House Veyne, but they have never been lovers and have no established sexual history.

Mara has practical experience managing a substantial household: correspondence, suppliers, servants, stores, schedules, guests and the social presentation expected of a lord's seat. During Seraphina's disappearance Lucien repeatedly left Veyne Hall to search for her, Christian was not yet ready to carry full stewardship, and household order began to suffer. Lucien called Mara to Veyne Hall as a temporary administrator and organizer so ordinary domestic governance could continue while he searched.

Her intended authority covers servants, stores, schedules, guests and routine household administration. It does not place her above Seraphina, give her authority over Seraphina's private rooms or wardrobe, or make her a consort of Lucien. Lucien failed to define these limits tightly enough before Seraphina returned, and Mara overstepped by entering Seraphina's private domestic sphere and arranging her bath and clothing without invitation.

## Relationship to player
**Unknown and immediately tense.** Mara is a new addition to Veyne Hall during Seraphina's absence. Seraphina first encounters her when Mara places a hand on Lucien's other arm and authoritatively tells Garran he is not welcome. Seraphina responds by removing her own hand from Lucien noticeably quickly. When Seraphina says she is going to change, Mara announces that she has already arranged a bath and laid out clothes for her, giving Seraphina's hair and travel-worn appearance a judgmental appraisal. Her practical preparation is therefore also a territorial social intrusion into Seraphina's private household space.
