# Sven

- Status: alive
- Nature: vampire
- Allegiance: The Pale Court
- Location: The Pale Court
- Relationship to Seraphina: familiar coven-mate; sympathetic enough to let her choose her departure

## Description, voice & mannerisms
Sven is a Pale Court vampire familiar with Seraphina from their life in the coven. His fuller appearance and history remain unestablished. Under pressure he is capable of hesitating rather than reflexively enforcing Pale Court authority, particularly where Seraphina's own stated choice is at issue.

## Current situation
During Seraphina and Lucien Veyne's attempted flight from the Pale Court ball, Sven and another Pale Court vampire block a corridor leading toward an exterior door. Sven initially tells Seraphina, "Sera. Don't do this." After she begs him to let her go, he asks her to state whether she truly wants to leave. Seraphina answers, "I want to go! You know what she makes me do! Please."

Sven understands the reference to Lady Yseld's treatment of Seraphina. He chooses not to force Seraphina to remain after she explicitly states her will. He checks his armed companion and gives Seraphina and Lucien a narrow opening to pass toward the exterior door. He does not join them or openly renounce the Pale Court; his intervention is specifically an act of allowing Seraphina to make her own choice.

## Knowledge
- Knows Seraphina personally as a Pale Court vampire/coven-mate.
- Has enough prior awareness of Yseld's use or treatment of Seraphina to understand Seraphina's plea, "You know what she makes me do," without explanation.
- Knows Seraphina is voluntarily attempting to leave the Pale Court with an unfamiliar man/vampire during an alarm.
- Has directly heard Seraphina insist that she wants to go.
- Does not yet have established knowledge of Seraphina's engagement, House Veyne, Lucien's identity, her blood oath to him, or the full private rupture with Yseld unless learned in play.

## GM notes
Sven's decision is not a wholesale betrayal of the Pale Court. He has chosen not to imprison Seraphina against her expressly stated will and has briefly obstructed a fellow Pale Court vampire to give her a chance to leave. This may carry consequences for him if Yseld learns exactly what he did.
