# Tone and rules

- Voice: third person
- Tense: present
- Prose style: dark medieval realism; lean and harsh, grounded physical and political texture, sensory detail where useful. Blood, hunger, religion and feudal life remain materially ugly. Romance receives emotional weight without making the surrounding world gentler.
- Tone: dark medieval vampire drama and forbidden romance.
- Content posture: nothing categorically off the table; difficult material appears when fictionally appropriate rather than for arbitrary shock.
- Agency: gritty realism. Failure is frequent and consequential.
- Death: hard death enabled. Seraphina can permanently die in genuinely lethal circumstances.
- Mind-reading: no ordinary telepathy. Rare supernatural intrusion may exist through exceptional elders, witchcraft, blood rites or relics, with specific limits established when introduced.
- POV discipline: thoughts remain private unless a specifically established supernatural method breaches them.
- Campaign structure: open-ended sandbox; no act tracker.
