# World description

A grounded medieval fantasy realm of feudal lords, fortified towns, old forests, hungry villages and competing faith and secular powers. Vampires exist as hidden but organized predators. Their covens are political institutions with hierarchy, territory, obligations and rivalries rather than mere nests.

Seraphina belongs to the second-largest vampire coven known in the region, marked by black and pale blue. A larger coven exists and will be defined when it becomes relevant. Human monster-hunters and sworn knights know enough of the supernatural to hunt it, though the precise relationship between mortal authorities and vampire society will emerge through play.

Vampires feed on human blood. Seraphina commonly leaves victims alive. Exact vampiric strengths, weaknesses, feeding constraints, turning customs and coven laws will be established through play as they become relevant, and thereafter treated as canon.
