# Narrative

Seraphina began as a young Pale Court vampire secretly in love with Sir Garran Vale, a mortal knight sworn to destroy monsters. Her fixation drew the scrutiny of her maker, Lady Yseld Veyr, and ultimately brought her into Garran's Bellwater investigation, where she revealed her nature, confessed her love, fought beside him and confronted the gulf between her personal moral code and his oath.

Lucien Veyne transformed the central conflict by offering Seraphina an alternative allegiance and a place beside him in founding a new coven. Seraphina chose Lucien, abandoned the Pale Court without yet informing Yseld, entered a blood-bound allegiance with him and accepted his marriage proposal. She later declared that she no longer loved Garran and chose Lucien freely. House Veyne has begun forming around them, with Christian being tested for stewardship, Frederick serving as Lucien's muscle and protector, and mortal businesswoman Elianor Thorne having accepted Seraphina as her future maker once her affairs are arranged.

The Bellwater investigation exposed an organized network collecting human blood from living captives and linked the chain upward to Master Hadrik, though Hadrik's exact role and the final recipient remain unknown. Twelve captives were freed, and a riverside storehouse became the next operational lead. Garran kissed Seraphina during the investigation; she did not reciprocate but did not pull away. Lucien's discovery of the kiss and Seraphina's field disobedience triggered a severe trust rupture: he removed her betrothal ring and coercively confined her, then later acknowledged that obedience under lock could not rebuild trust and restored her freedom of movement within Veyne Hall.

Lucien and Seraphina's consensual private relationship has resumed, but the ring remains withheld as a symbol of unresolved trust. Lucien also severely punished Frederick for failing his protective assignment, nearly beating him to death; Frederick survived and is healing, but his own view of the event is unknown. Seraphina has responded to Lucien's continued possession of the ring with a deliberately playful campaign of temptation, most recently commissioning more striking dresses after successfully forcing Lucien to admit that he cannot resist her.
