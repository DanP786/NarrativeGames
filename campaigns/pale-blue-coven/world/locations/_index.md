# Location index

- Veyne Hall | active | Seat of Lucien Veyne and the emerging House Veyne; Seraphina has returned here, reclaimed her ring and asserted genuine household/coven authority alongside Lucien.
- The Pale Court | active | Stronghold of Seraphina's former coven; second-largest vampire coven in the region. Black and pale blue are its faction colors. Seraphina was forcibly returned here in S02 and escaped with Lucien from a formal ball.
- Old north-road chapel | active lead | Unspecified old chapel chosen by the Pale Court for a truce meeting at midnight three nights after delivery of its letter; physical details and exact loyalties around the site remain unestablished.
- The Lantern Rooms | active | Night-time entertainment house frequented by drinkers and revelers; private rooms and intoxicated patrons make it one of Seraphina's familiar places for discreet feeding.
- The Pike and Crown | active | Busy town tavern near the military quarter, frequented by soldiers, guards, messengers and camp followers; useful for overhearing military gossip.
- Hill-road priory | active | Unnamed priory roughly three miles southwest of a walled river market town, reached by a neglected road into wooded hills. A recent warning scratched at the road fork reads "DON'T SLEEP THERE." Signs near the site indicate recent human activity despite the ominous warning.
