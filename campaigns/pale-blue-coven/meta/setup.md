# Campaign setup

Created: 8 September 2026 (real-world date)
Slug: pale-blue-coven

## Onboarding answers

**Genre / premise:** Vampire coven in a fantasy medieval setting. The PC is a female vampire in the second-largest coven. She has fallen in love from afar with a knight sworn to kill all monsters.

**Voice:** Third person, present tense.

**Prose style:** Dark medieval realism — lean, harsh physical texture in the spirit of grounded medieval political fiction. Blood, hunger, religion and feudal politics feel material and ugly. Romance is not automatically romanticized by the world.

**Length:** Open-ended sandbox. No fixed endpoint and no act tracker.

**Tone / content posture:** Nothing categorically off the table. Dark material may appear when fictionally appropriate.

**Stakes / scope:** Intimate within a dangerous world, blended with coven politics. The emotional camera stays close to Seraphina and the knight while alliances, rivalries, blood debts and obligations within and between covens exert pressure.

**Agency posture:** Gritty realism. Failure is frequent and consequential. Vampirism makes Seraphina formidable, not protected by the story.

**Death rule:** Hard death enabled.

**Mind-reading rule:** Ordinary vampires cannot read thoughts. Rare supernatural intrusion exists: exceptional elders, witchcraft, blood rites or certain relics may breach inner thoughts under specific circumstances.

**Main-thread seed:** Seraphina has fallen in love from afar with a knight whose authority, seriousness, strength, confidence and devotion to duty drew her in. He is sworn to kill all monsters and does not know she is a vampire. She has repeatedly followed him, often straying far from her coven. The opening begins when someone in the coven notices her fixation.

**Character seed:** Seraphina appears 24 but is actually 42, making her one of the youngest vampires in her coven, though she has already proven herself. Honey-blonde hair, blue eyes, small agile build. She favors daggers, black leather and pale-blue fabrics in her faction's colors. She loves experimenting with makeup, including using it to put living color into her skin. She is quiet and observant, physically weak by vampiric standards, and dislikes torturing prey; she usually leaves feeding victims alive. Her coven took her after killing most of her village. She does not resent the escape from her former life: she had been married off to a lazy farmer who drank too much.

**Coven standing:** Useful outsider. Seraphina is accepted and proven, but her restrained feeding and unexplained excursions keep her outside the inner circle.

**Maker relationship:** Cold maker. Turning Seraphina was practical rather than affectionate. Her maker expects competence and obedience, not intimacy.

**Opening situation:** Seraphina returns after another long absence spent following the knight. Someone in the coven has discovered that she repeatedly follows the same mortal monster-killer and is waiting for her.
