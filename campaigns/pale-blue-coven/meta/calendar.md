# Calendar

- Date: late Harvestwane, Year 612 of the Crown Reckoning; the day after Seraphina left Veyne Hall alone following her painful private reckoning with Lucien
- Season: early autumn
- Time of day: late afternoon; the short autumn daylight is beginning to fail
- Current location: Edric's rough timber hut in a small woodland clearing far beyond Veyne Hall
- Notable upcoming: Seraphina wandered through the previous night without choosing a destination and took shelter at dawn in Edric's isolated woodland hut. She slept through the daylight after voluntarily surrendering her original fighting daggers to Edric's keeping and telling him that only one man might be looking for her and that the man would not hurt him. Lucien still possesses the betrothal ring; the engagement has not formally ended, but Seraphina has said she does not know whether she wants the ring and the intended marriage is no longer secure. Frederick remains badly beaten but healing. Christian's prospective stewardship depends on his handling of retainer welfare and a household winter-solstice celebration. Elianor Thorne remains mortal while arranging her affairs before a planned turning by Seraphina. Lady Yseld and the Pale Court have not been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance as far as established. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Master Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
