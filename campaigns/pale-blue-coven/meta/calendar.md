# Calendar

- Date: late Harvestwane, Year 612 of the Crown Reckoning; several days have passed since Seraphina's black-lace confrontation with Lucien
- Season: early autumn
- Time of day: deepening night
- Current location: country roads beyond Veyne Hall; Seraphina is wandering without an established destination
- Notable upcoming: After confronting Frederick about Bellwater and later having a painful private reckoning with Lucien over trust, intimacy and the meaning of the betrothal ring, Seraphina leaves Veyne Hall alone at night. She wears black travelling clothes and a black cloak and carries her original fighting daggers, leaving Lucien's red armour and ivory-handled daggers behind. She continues down country roads without deliberately choosing a destination. Lucien still possesses the betrothal ring; the engagement has not formally ended, but Seraphina has said she does not know whether she wants the ring and the intended marriage is no longer secure. Frederick remains badly beaten but healing and has spoken with Seraphina about Bellwater and the conflicting orders that led to his punishment. Christian's prospective stewardship depends on his handling of retainer welfare and a household winter-solstice celebration. Elianor Thorne remains mortal while arranging her affairs before a planned turning by Seraphina. Lady Yseld and the Pale Court have not been informed of Seraphina's changed allegiance as far as established. The Bellwater blood-substitution operation, riverside-storehouse lead and Master Hadrik investigation remain unresolved.
