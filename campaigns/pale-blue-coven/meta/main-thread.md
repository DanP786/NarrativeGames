# Main thread

- Status: sketch
- Central question: What survives when Seraphina's love for a man sworn to destroy her kind collides with her obligations to the coven that made her?
- PC want: To remain close to the knight she has come to love from afar, and perhaps someday be seen by him as something other than a monster.
- Obstacle: The knight's oath and convictions; Seraphina's vampiric nature; the coven's expectations and growing notice of her excursions.
- Antagonist / pressure: Not yet hardened. The coven's scrutiny and the knight's monster-hunting duty are the immediate opposing forces.
- Establishing arc: Reveal who has noticed Seraphina's fixation, bring the knight or his work into direct contact with her hidden existence, and force Seraphina to make her desire concrete through action.
