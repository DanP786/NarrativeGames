# Main thread

- Status: active
- Central question: What kind of freedom and loyalty can Seraphina build after leaving the Pale Court when love, vampiric hierarchy and her own learned habits of submission keep testing whether her choices are truly hers?
- PC want: To determine what she chooses when neither Pale Court duty nor Lucien's authority is deciding for her; House Veyne and marriage to Lucien remain possible parts of that future, but she has stepped away from both rather than continue trying to prove herself on undefined terms.
- Obstacles: Lady Yseld and the Pale Court remain an eventual threat; Lucien's possessiveness and capacity for coercive violence have damaged the trust Seraphina chose to place in him; Seraphina's learned deference and history of being used blur the boundary between chosen submission and imposed obedience; Garran and the unresolved Bellwater blood network remain dangerous external threads; Seraphina has now left the protection and resources of Veyne Hall without choosing a destination.
- Antagonist / pressure: No single antagonist has fully hardened. Yseld represents the old coven and its claims, Lucien's worst instincts threaten the freedom he promises, the Hadrik blood network remains unresolved, and Seraphina's departure forces the central question of whether House Veyne is truly a chosen home rather than merely another hierarchy.
- Current direction: After Lucien's handling of the trust rupture made Seraphina's chosen intimacy feel insufficient and damaged the meaning of the betrothal ring, Seraphina left Veyne Hall alone. She deliberately left Lucien's red armour and ivory-handled daggers behind, took her old weapons, wandered without a destination, accepted daylight shelter from a mortal woodsman named Edric, and resumed travelling after dusk. She has now reached a larger road west of Edric's woodland hut. The engagement has not been formally ended, but Seraphina does not know whether she wants the ring and has not chosen whether she will return.
