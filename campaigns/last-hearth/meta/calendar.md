Date: 27 Frostwane, year 417 of the March Reckoning.
Season: late autumn, snow.
Time: late afternoon, before opening.
Location: Inn common room, Dunreach.
Today: Roderic surgeon review completed, fever worse; Harl intended approach Edmund about work; Gerard intended inquire at inn about escorts; Thomas intended ask stationer's worker for walk. Tessa reciprocal chore cover completed today; Tessa out at cobbler. Lucel permitted inn common room only, return before opening. Winter supply wagons expected within a week of 24th; Edmund hopes for another afternoon together in a few days.
