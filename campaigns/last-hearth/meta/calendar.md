Date: 27 Frostwane, year 417 of the March Reckoning.
Season: late autumn, snow.
Time: early evening, open for business.
Location: Copper Lantern, Dunreach.
Today: Roderic surgeon review completed, fever worse but retaining small sips since midday; Harl did not approach Edmund; Gerard discussed timber escort preliminarily with Harl; Thomas intended ask stationer's worker for walk. Tessa reciprocal chore cover completed today; boots repaired and borrowed shoes returned. Lucel returned from permitted inn visit before opening. Tonight Willem first nursing watch, Harl to sleep. Tomorrow Willem or Harl intends bring news. Winter supply wagons expected within a week of 24th; Edmund hopes for another afternoon together in a few days.
