Date: 27 Frostwane, year 417 of the March Reckoning.
Season: late autumn, snow.
Time: early morning.
Location: Copper Lantern, Lucel's bedroom, Dunreach.
Today: Roderic surgeon review; Harl intends approach Edmund about work; Gerard intends inquire at inn about escorts; Thomas intends ask stationer's worker for walk. Tessa reciprocal chore cover afternoon 28th. Winter supply wagons expected within a week of 24th; Edmund hopes for another afternoon together in a few days.
