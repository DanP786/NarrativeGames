Dunreach is a fortified human frontier market town beneath the northern mountains, ringed with timber walls and a stone gatehouse. Soldiers, hunters, merchants and labourers sustain its economy. Beyond farmland lie ancient forests and dangerous supernatural creatures. Rare witches and certain monsters possess limited mental intrusion. Copper pennies buy small necessities, twelve copper to a silver mark. Indenture is enforceable by town magistrates; terms vary. The Copper Lantern is a working brothel with common room, upstairs rooms, kitchen and staff quarters, owned by Mistress Odran.
