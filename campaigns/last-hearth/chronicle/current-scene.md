# S01 — Copper Lantern, 23–24 Frostwane
T01–08: Lucel helps Tessa dress; serves regular Bren, receives grey wool gloves, discusses late wife Edith, daughter Sophie expecting third child, fortnight trial winter stay with Sophie, rising flour prices and unsafe wagon roads. Bren pays six copper, one to Lucel (8 savings); Odran permits gloves.
T09–20: Hunters Willem (Bellwick), Roderic (Greyford), Harl (north of Saltmere) arrive. Roderic wounded by bipedal nonhuman creature tracked to abandoned charcoal burner's cottage in northern woods; killed and burned. Three parallel cuts, swelling, bruising, one grey edge newly developed. Odran warns Lucel of earlier alleged dog bite fatality and companion fever; Lucel never touches wound, reports findings. Odran insists hunters seek surgeon at residence tonight; they leave, Willem promising to return next evening and kissing Lucel's knuckles.
T21–25: Lucel meets Edmund Vale, southern cloth merchant, accepts wine, introduces herself. He asks about town unease, she redirects to private company. Edmund pays Odran, Lucel leads upstairs; private evening offscreen.
T26–29: Edmund asks about travel; Lucel explains indenture, knows debt recorded and over 15000 copper outstanding. Edmund converts to approximately 150 gold (10 copper/silver, 10 silver/gold), surprised, asks about childhood work. Lucel asks him not to worry about debt; he respects boundary.
T30–33: Edmund arranges additional overnight payment, affectionate companionship and uneventful sleep. Morning 24 Frostwane, snow blankets town.
T34–39: Edmund may be stranded all winter, hopes to visit again. They share bread, eggs and tea on his account. Lucel explains daytime chores, no days off, offers to find him between errands. He welcomes daytime meeting near warehouses around midday. Lingering farewell kiss; he departs for business and settles breakfast.
T40–43: Lucel strips bed, washes dishes, cleans room, makes bed, completes laundry and replenishes water, washes herself. Odran settles Edmund's service earnings: 20 copper evening, 20 overnight; wine and breakfast separate. 6 copper personal, 6 upkeep, 28 net debt repayment. Savings 14, debt 15068.
T44–47: Lucel confirms Edmund eager to return. Odran supplies basket and 12 copper house money for two soap blocks and six candles, return before midafternoon. Lucel applies hand salve, wears Bren's gloves. Soap costs 6 (one more than usual), candles 5, one copper change. Meets Edmund at warehouses shortly after midday.
T48–52: Edmund secured storage at increased cost, two wagon masters refuse southern road. Lucel skipped haggling to make time. They share tea at nearby tavern on his account. Lucel enjoys walks and checking on people, few close friends outside house, stigma. Edmund unmarried, childless, mother alive, younger married sister with two sons; sister tries matchmaking; business travel occupies him.
T53–57: Lucel concerned whether Edmund is looked after. Housekeeper and mother provide practical care. Lucel says girls care for each other despite mistreatment, Odran charges abusive patrons especially if girls cannot work, girls cannot intervene or refuse paying customers. Edmund condemns arrangement; relieved Lucel came voluntarily. They hold hands, welcome further voluntary meetings.
T58–64: Edmund can continue local wool purchases and cloth sales, costs rise but no immediate ruin. Lantern winter trade guards and hunters, fewer travellers and seasonal workers, all upkeep tracked. Edmund escorts Lucel back, agrees visit tomorrow evening (25 Frostwane). She kisses cheek, he forehead. Lucel returns purchases and one copper change, reports soap increase. Odran worries supply wagons, asks common room prepared.
T65–68: Girls gossip by kitchen hearth: boastful horse owner, exhausted guard reporting longer watches, payment dispute. Player requests longer prose. Lucel tells them Edmund lovely, experienced, paid to sleep in arms; sympathetic to loneliness. Teases Tessa not to poach him, tells tomorrow's planned visit; Tessa advises tell Odran (not yet done). They dress together, Lucel low neckline, Tessa mended blue dress, wonders wounded hunter's recovery.
T69–76: Impatient wagon hauler Garran, late thirties, coarse brown beard, leather coat, pays for private company. Lucel brings hot cider on his bill, massages sore back. Fifteen years hauling through Dunreach; bad roads, foreman threatens wage cuts. Wife and two young sons, eldest potential joiner apprentice, cost obstacle; wife unaware wage threat. Lucel says she has worked two years. Private time offscreen. Garran leaves two copper personal tip on bedside table, apologises for temper, hopes return when spare coin. Tip not explicitly collected. Willem asks downstairs for Lucel.
T77–83: Lucel washes, dresses, changes sheets, returns to common room. Willem, Harl, Roderic present; Roderic pale, alert, fresh forearm sling. Surgeon cleaned wound and removed unhealthy flesh, keep dry, return tomorrow, no strenuous use/hunting. Harl buys three stews and three hot ciders, later another cider. Lucel secretly gives Roderic extra meat, sits beside Willem. Willem fetched surgeon supplies, tended equipment; Harl seeks possible timber-road patrol work, nothing agreed, forbids Roderic hunting. Roderic worries about earning and gives Lucel one copper tip, collected (15 personal savings). Declines further alcohol, drinks water, good hand trembles slightly; Harl notices and wants him rested. Willem pays for private evening, not overnight.
T84–87: Lucel leads Willem upstairs; private time offscreen. He misses having someone to come home to, asks about travel. Lucel says wishes to see outside Dunreach every day, reveals indenture and mistress estimates twenty years to repay. Willem shocked, no means to buy freedom, offers stories and spring outing subject to permission. Asks whether she has ever left gates.
T88–91: Lucel explains mistress raised her, mother's unpaid debts, expects no purchase of freedom, has never been beyond gates. Willem describes childhood wagon trip, stream south of town with alder trees, proposes spring picnic with bread, cheese and blanket, asks her to seek mistress permission in spring; Lucel agrees. He tells stories of Bellwick, orchard, fairs, pigs, childhood mishaps. They kiss until Odran knocks to end paid time. Willem dresses, asks whether he can return just for conversation if short of coin; Lucel warmly agrees. He bids farewell, reminds her of picnic, Harl calls from downstairs. Tessa knocks and invites Lucel to hearth.
T92–94: Lucel washes and half dresses, invites Tessa in to help change sheets. Tessa helps, remarks Willem looked pleased. Downstairs Odran calls Lucel to new patron seeking overnight company. Alistair Venn, early forties, neatly trimmed dark hair grey temples, charcoal wool coat, leather gloves, measured cultivated voice, tired, horse dealer visiting to inspect breeding stock and arrange purchases before winter; previous visits twelve years ago with father (wagon wheel broke) and three summers ago; admires sturdy northern-valley horses. First Lantern visit. Lucel sits, welcomes him, touches arm, says lifelong Dunreach resident and asks about prior visits. Odran confirms payment settled until morning, amount undisclosed. Venn rests hand on hers and asks whether she enjoys hearing travellers' stories. Late evening 24 Frostwane; in common room with Venn. Garran and Willem bookings complete, earnings not yet allocated; Venn booking paid, not yet begun upstairs. Personal savings 15 copper, two copper Garran tip uncollected or location unconfirmed after linen changes; debt 15068 pending settlements. Edmund visit and Roderic surgeon followup tomorrow. NPC and other state files require reconciliation on next save.
