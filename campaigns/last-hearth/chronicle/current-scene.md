# S01 opening — Copper Lantern, dusk
Early snow; Lucel preparing for work. Tessa asks for dress help, mentions Bren. Odran opens house.
T01–02: Lucel helps Tessa, jokes about old patrons and town morale; both descend. Odran assigns Lucel to Bren.
T03–04: Bren gives grey wool gloves, recalls late wife Edith; Lucel thanks him and tries them on. He orders mulled wine.
T05–06: Lucel praises Odran, promises more market visits, agrees to keep Bren company, asks about daughter Sophie. Bren invites her to workshop behind butcher, mentions rising flour prices and unsafe wagon roads. Sophie expecting third child and offers him winter lodging.
T07–08: Lucel reassures Bren; he plans fortnight trial stay. Three hunters arrive, one injured. Lucel listens another half-hour; Bren leaves customary house payment and asks for coat help. Odran gestures towards hunters.
T09–11: Lucel escorts Bren out, meets hunters Willem, Roderic and Harl; tears bread for injured Roderic. Origins Greyford (Roderic), Bellwick (Willem), north of Saltmere (Harl). Lucel lifelong resident.
T12–13: Roderic reports fighting something in woods, delayed by watch until surgeon closed; asks fresh linen. Lucel gathers basin, boiled water and linen. Odran intercepts.
T14–15: Odran warns of past patron with purported dog bite dying overnight and companion developing fever; orders Lucel not to touch peculiar wound, fetch her. Insists payment before upstairs with Willem. Bren's six copper settled, one to Lucel (8 savings); Odran permits gloves.
T16: Lucel returns to hunters, asks what wounded Roderic on Mistress's instruction. Roderic says claw through sleeve from bipedal nonhuman creature tracked to abandoned charcoal burner's cottage in northern woods; killed, burned per Willem. Harl discourages discussion. Roderic removes own dressing, reveals three parallel cuts, bruising and swelling, middle cut grey edges not present this morning. Lucel has not touched wound.
