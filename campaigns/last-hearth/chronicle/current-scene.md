# S01 — Copper Lantern, 23–24 Frostwane
T01–08: Lucel helps Tessa dress; serves regular Bren, receives grey wool gloves, discusses late wife Edith, daughter Sophie expecting third child, fortnight trial winter stay with Sophie, rising flour prices and unsafe wagon roads. Bren pays six copper, one to Lucel (8 savings); Odran permits gloves.
T09–20: Hunters Willem (Bellwick), Roderic (Greyford), Harl (north of Saltmere) arrive. Roderic wounded by bipedal nonhuman creature tracked to abandoned charcoal burner's cottage in northern woods; killed and burned. Three parallel cuts, swelling, bruising, one grey edge newly developed. Odran warns Lucel of earlier alleged dog bite fatality and companion fever; Lucel never touches wound, reports findings. Odran insists hunters seek surgeon at residence tonight; they leave, Willem promising to return next evening and kissing Lucel's knuckles.
T21–25: Lucel meets Edmund Vale, southern cloth merchant, accepts wine, introduces herself. He asks about town unease, she redirects to private company. Edmund pays Odran, Lucel leads upstairs; private evening offscreen.
T26–29: Edmund asks about travel; Lucel explains indenture, knows debt recorded and over 15000 copper outstanding. Edmund converts to approximately 150 gold (10 copper/silver, 10 silver/gold), surprised, asks about childhood work. Lucel asks him not to worry about debt; he respects boundary.
T30–32: Lucel agrees to overnight company; Edmund arranges additional payment with Odran, amount and Lucel share not disclosed, returns upstairs. Affectionate companionship, Edmund thanks her for peaceful evening.
T33: Lucel strokes Edmund until he sleeps, then sleeps herself. Uneventful night. Morning 24 Frostwane; snow blankets town, Edmund wakes beside her; house beginning morning chores. Payment settlement still pending.
T34: Lucel asks whether Edmund could be stuck all winter. He confirms possibility if mountain roads close to laden wagons, prefers waiting to abandoning goods and arrangements; hopes to visit her again.
T35: Lucel welcomes future visits, says whole nights with patrons rare and enjoyed this one, offers tea/breakfast. Edmund requests tea, bread and eggs if available, invites her to share and offers payment for both.
T36: Lucel kisses Edmund, dresses and fetches two breakfasts (bread, eggs, tea) on his account. Returns to room where he has dressed and cleared table. He invites her to sit and asks about her daytime routine.
T37: Lucel explains daytime chores, daily washing of linens. Edmund sympathises with winter washing, recalls father's cloth business and asks whether she gets days to herself.
T38: Lucel says no days off but offers to find him between errands. Edmund welcomes daytime meeting near warehouses around midday, subject to business and her duties; offers to share a warm drink.
T39: Lucel gives Edmund lingering farewell kiss; he reciprocates and departs for business, stopping downstairs to settle breakfast. Lucel remains upstairs with breakfast tray and used linens; personal share pending.
T40: Lucel strips bed and takes linens to laundry, returns for breakfast tray and washes dishes including accumulated evening dishes. About half an hour passes; dishes clean, laundry pending.
T41: Lucel cleans room, makes bed with fresh linens, helps communal laundry until late morning; washing nearly done. Water and soap running low.
T42: Lucel finishes laundry, replenishes water, washes and tidies herself, reports to Odran near midday. Odran approves Edmund's patronage, offers market errand for soap and candles, asks Lucel to sit for earnings settlement; amounts not yet disclosed.
T43: Odran settles Edmund's service earnings: 20 copper evening, 20 overnight; wine and breakfast separate. 6 copper personal, 6 upkeep, 28 net debt repayment. Savings 14, debt 15068. Odran praises good earnings and asks whether Edmund intends to return.
T44: Lucel confirms Edmund eager for further company while in town. Odran encourages repeat business, offers basket and separate house purse of 12 copper for two blocks laundry soap and six tallow candles, return change; asks her back before midafternoon.
T45: Lucel applies hand salve, dons warm clothes and Bren's gloves, takes basket and house purse to market around midday. Soap seller quotes six copper for two blocks, one more than usual; no purchase yet.
T46: Lucel buys both soap blocks for 6 copper house money, leaving 6. Chandler offers six stout tallow candles for 5 copper; awaiting purchase.
T47: Lucel buys candles for 5 copper, leaving one house penny; proceeds to warehouses shortly after midday. Finds Edmund finishing conversation with warehouse keeper near loading bay; he greets her warmly and offers nearby warm drink.
T48: Lucel accepts warm drink and asks about business. Edmund says storage secured at increased cost, two wagon masters refuse southern road until conditions improve. Offers arm and asks how much time she has.
T49: Lucel says she skipped haggling to make time, takes Edmund's arm. He amusedly escorts her to nearby tavern, finds fireside table, offers hot spiced cider or tea and lunch.
T50: Lucel chooses tea. Edmund orders pot on his account, remarks on enjoying ordinary conversation and asks what she likes doing when not working.
T51: Lucel says she enjoys walks and checking on people outside winter; winter mostly chores. Edmund contrasts her settled life with his itinerant acquaintances and asks if she has close friends outside the Lantern.
T52: Lucel says few close friends, recognises stigma of profession, asks about Edmund's family and wife. Edmund says unmarried, childless, mother alive, younger married sister with two sons; sister tries matchmaking but business travel keeps him occupied. Asks whether Lucel was concerned about a wife.
T53: Lucel shyly apologises and explains concern for whether Edmund is looked after at home. He reassures her, holds her hand, says housekeeper and mother look after practical needs, distinguishes having someone waiting for him; asks who cares for her when unwell.
T54: Lucel says Lantern girls care for each other despite mistreatment. Edmund expresses approval of solidarity, criticises customers' entitlement, asks whether Odran intervenes with cruel patrons.
T55: Lucel says Odran charges abusive patrons especially when girls cannot work for days, and girls are not allowed to intervene. Edmund disturbed, asks whether Lucel can refuse a returning patron who hurt her.
T56: Lucel confirms they do not refuse paying customers. Edmund condemns arrangement, then asks if she came to meet him today of her own accord, distinguishing paid company from voluntary meeting.
T57: Lucel confirms voluntary visit and enjoyment of their time, places hand on his. Edmund relieved, reciprocates handholding and welcomes further voluntary meetings; offers escort back when she needs to leave.
T58: Lucel accepts escort offer, holds hand and asks about viability of his business if stranded. Edmund explains local wool purchases and cloth sales remain possible but storage, lodging and wages accumulate while southern sales delayed; not facing immediate ruin. Asks about Lantern winter trade.
T59: Lucel describes winter clientele primarily guards and hunters with steady incomes, fewer travellers and seasonal workers. Edmund asks whether Odran reduces upkeep charges during lean months.
