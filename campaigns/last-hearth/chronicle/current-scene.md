# S01 opening — Copper Lantern, dusk
Early snow; Lucel preparing for work. Tessa asks for dress help, mentions Bren. Odran opens house.
T01–02: Lucel helps Tessa, jokes about old patrons and town morale; both descend. Odran assigns Lucel to Bren.
T03–04: Bren gives grey wool gloves, recalls late wife Edith; Lucel thanks him and tries them on. He orders mulled wine.
T05–06: Lucel praises Odran, promises more market visits, agrees to keep Bren company, asks about daughter Sophie. Bren invites her to workshop behind butcher, mentions rising flour prices and unsafe wagon roads. Sophie expecting third child and offers him winter lodging.
T07–08: Lucel reassures Bren; he plans fortnight trial stay. Three hunters arrive, one injured. Lucel listens another half-hour; Bren leaves customary house payment and asks for coat help. Odran gestures towards hunters.
T09: Lucel pockets Bren's house payment (six copper held for settlement, not personal funds), helps coat, walks him to door and wishes safe journey. Approaches hunters with greeting. Dark-haired scarred injured man greets her; young blond Willem offers seat and asks her name; older auburn-bearded man chides Willem while eating.
T10: Lucel introduces herself, sits crossing legs, asks names. Willem introduces himself, Roderic (scarred) and Harl (older). Willem asks if Lucel is native to Dunreach; Roderic says Willem has never been here before. Roderic struggles to tear bread one-handed; blood staining dressing.
T11: Lucel tears Roderic's bread for him, says she has lived here all her life and asks origins. Roderic grateful, from Greyford south of mountains, away twelve years. Willem from Bellwick, pig farmer father. Harl from north of Saltmere on east coast. Willem asks whether Lucel is curious about elsewhere; Roderic says after their week staying put seems sensible.
T12: Lucel jokes that tax collectors are terrifying and asks whether wound seen by healer. Roderic says gatekeeper and watch captain questioned party until surgeon closed; Harl plans morning visit. Faint swelling near wrist, bleeding through dressing. Roderic claims cut fighting something in woods; Willem makes sardonic remark, Harl silences him with look. Roderic requests fresh clean linen.
T13: Lucel laughs, excuses herself to gather clean linen strips, basin and boiled water for practical wound care. Odran intercepts her in back room, asks what supplies are for and whether hunter bleeding.
