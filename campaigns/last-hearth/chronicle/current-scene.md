# S01 — Copper Lantern, 23–26 Frostwane

T09–20: Hunters Willem (Bellwick), Roderic (Greyford), Harl (north of Saltmere) arrive. Roderic wounded by bipedal nonhuman creature tracked to abandoned charcoal burner's cottage in northern woods; killed and burned. Three parallel cuts, swelling, bruising, one grey edge newly developed. Odran warns Lucel of earlier alleged dog bite fatality and companion fever; Lucel never touches wound, reports findings. Odran insists hunters seek surgeon at residence tonight; they leave, Willem promising to return next evening and kissing Lucel's knuckles.

T95–108: Venn tells stories of stallion throwing hat-wearing nobleman, southern coast, inherited horse trade eleven years ago, stables and grazing outside southern market town. Wife Eleanor died consumption three winters ago after seventeen years marriage; son under twenty helps stables, daughter married last summer. Wife humorous and thoughtful, read by fire. Lucel comforts him; he asks who cares for her. Tessa closest friend, both grew up here and indentured; Lucel reports Tessa wants rich husband, girls come and go saving enough. Venn surprised twenty years indenture, no offer. Lucel dreams of Seareach and seeing sea, warmth, overseas visitors. Venn describes harbour spices dyes languages, gull stole breakfast on trip with Eleanor. Private time offscreen, sleeps cuddling. Morning 25th breakfast at his expense.

Mother died when Lucel six, girl kept out of front room, never saw men's faces. Odran dispelled dreams. Edmund compares father's inherited signet, asks what Lucel wants.
