# Scene 1 — Second Hand Arms
Afternoon. A local thug in a battered synthetic leather vest enters, smells of cigarettes, approaches V and asks for a new gun, budget 600 eddies. Wants something reliable and says his last gun jammed. Len is in the rear office. V asks about situations and discusses categories without describing crimes. Customer prefers something compact, appreciates her not pushing the most expensive stock, reiterates 600 eddies and asks what she has. No sale yet. OOC correction: V wears second-hand black combat trousers, boots and grey band crop top.
V recommends a personally reworked basic Unity pistol, explaining its improved reliability. Customer initially skeptical, then interested; Len vouches for V's work from the office. Customer asks price and whether a jam qualifies for a refund.
V quotes advertised 450 eddies and expresses confidence. Customer agrees to buy, offers remaining 150 for dinner together, asks her name and when she finishes as though agreement is assumed. V has not accepted invitation or extra money.
V says “I’m flattered. But I don’t date”. Customer presses, asks if she has boyfriend. Len tells him she finishes at six without checking with her. Customer announces he will return at six. No agreement to date.
