# Scene 1 — Second Hand Arms
Afternoon. A local thug in a battered synthetic leather vest enters, smells of cigarettes, approaches V and asks for a new gun, budget 600 eddies. Wants something reliable and says his last gun jammed. Len is in the rear office. V asks about situations and discusses categories without describing crimes. Customer prefers something compact, appreciates her not pushing the most expensive stock, reiterates 600 eddies and asks what she has. No sale yet.
