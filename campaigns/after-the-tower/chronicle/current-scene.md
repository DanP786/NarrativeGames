# Scene 2 — Rear alley to Japantown
Previous day: local thug bought V's reworked Unity for 450 eddies, offered dinner, ignored her refusal, learned shift end from Len. V avoided him by leaving through rear exit at 18:00. He waited nearly an hour; Len eventually told him she had gone home.
Next day 18:04: V exits rear door wearing tight green catsuit and black combat boots. Thug waits, approaches, guides her by waist to dark red Quadra. V enters passenger seat because she considers refusal too dangerous. No romantic consent inferred. Thug introduces himself as Dario, drives towards Japantown and asks V's name.
Retcon: V answers “Just you” when asked who gives her trouble. Dario touches her thigh without asking; V is indifferent. He asks her name again and she answers “V”. He interferes with her clothing without permission and resumes resting his hand on her thigh. Car crosses into Westbrook en route to Japantown.
V answers question about her employment with “Shitty circumstances”. Dario unilaterally declares her his girl and promises to look after her with meals, rides and protection. He parks outside a small occupied Japantown restaurant with a flickering red neon sign. V has not agreed to a relationship.
V privately decides to use Dario for material support and takes his hand. He interprets this as a change of heart, without knowing her motives. Proprietor knows Dario by name; seats them at his usual booth overlooking street. Dario offers unrestricted menu choice at his expense; removes jacket, revealing holstered pistol. Asks where V lives. No address disclosed.
