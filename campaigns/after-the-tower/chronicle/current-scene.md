# Scene 2 — Rear alley to Japantown
Previous day: local thug bought V's reworked Unity for 450 eddies, offered dinner, ignored her refusal, learned shift end from Len. V avoided him by leaving through rear exit at 18:00. He waited nearly an hour; Len eventually told him she had gone home.
Next day 18:04: V exits rear door wearing tight green catsuit and black combat boots. Thug waits, approaches, guides her by waist to dark red Quadra. V enters passenger seat because she considers refusal too dangerous. No romantic consent inferred. Thug introduces himself as Dario, drives towards Japantown and asks V's name.
V answers “Just you” when asked who gives her trouble and identifies herself as V. Dario touches her thigh and interferes with her clothing without permission. V is indifferent. They arrive outside a small Japantown restaurant; proprietor recognises Dario and waves. Dario says they know him here. V remains in passenger seat.
