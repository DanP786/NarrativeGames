# Senna

## Status
Alive. Older Vahn-rii woman, matriarch-tier — custodian of new women, keeper of arrival-rites, reader of the will of the deep places. Both wisewoman and headwoman. **Widowed bonded mate:** Senna was bonded in the sacred deep places when younger; her mate later died. The bond did not vanish with his death, but survives as an absence she has learned to carry. She has not taken another mate.

Day 2 (S03): declared Mira's sealed implant awake and forbade the caves while it is so; carried the verdict to Thaal. S05 (~Day 9): Korren defied the verdict, the bond was made, and the party returned with two more women. At the terrace Senna found the made-thing on a second woman (Mari) and began reasoning toward an outside hand acting through the deep places.

## Description
Tall as Vahn-rii women run, no longer young. Long rope of grey-bronze hair over one shoulder. Arms marked head-to-wrist in fine dyed/scarred traceries (older patterns than natural luminescence). Wrap crossing both shoulders. No weapon Mira has seen. Dry grip, warm and stronger than her size promises.

## Voice & mannerisms
Quiet, lower than expected for her frame; never raises it; lands harder than the chief's at half the volume. Speaks as if she has had every conversation in front of her at least once. Pointedly does not look at Korren when she moves against his wants — the absence of the look is the statement.

## Relationship to Mira
Friendly/protective, deepened toward guardianship. Senna has repeatedly put her body and standing between Mira and danger, but her protection can shade into pity in ways Mira dislikes. Her core counsel remains: a hidden thing cannot simply be assumed helpful; Mira may have been made easy to keep; and "Good is not the same as safe… Keep something of yourself, even from him."

## Goals & pressures
- Read and protect new women; the man's claim is the man's, the woman's readiness is the women's.
- Understand the made-things before more women are bonded.
- Hold the camp together without letting fear become a purge.
- Keep women's reasoning from men where it serves the women.
- Her own experience of surviving a mate's death makes her unwilling to romanticize the bond as painless or harmless, while also preventing her from treating it as unreal.

## History with Mira
- Day 1: received, fed, wrapped Mira; felt something at the nape, said nothing.
- Day 2: named the sealed thing; mapped it; found it awake; refused Korren's proposed cut; forbade the caves while the made-thing remained unexplained.
- S05 terrace: found the same made-thing on Mari and reasoned that the women were altered deliberately by an outside hand.
- S07: privately learned Mira's mission/screening/implant-site memories and cave-spore hypothesis; participated in the six-woman meeting; re-dressed Mira's injured foot; later helped Mira leave the meeting when she could no longer tolerate the room.

## Knowledge
- Knows Vahn-rii custom around new women and sacred bonding in full, including from personal experience as a bonded woman.
- Knows the implant is real, awake/reactive, and present in analogous form among the human women; knows Mira's current spore hypothesis but not whether it is correct.
- Does not know the hidden experiment, true selection criteria, or that implants do not cause/control the bond.
