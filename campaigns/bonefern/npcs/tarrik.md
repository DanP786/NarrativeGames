# Tarrik

## Status
Alive. Adult Vahn-rii male. Formal keeper of Suvi Lindqvist. Suvi is claimed/kept but unbonded. Tarrik keeps her close and gives her few answers; Ren bit him badly on the first night.

## Description
About 6'10". Long, athletic and rangy, with powerful climbing shoulders, long arms and a hunter's narrow hips. Deep bronze skin with a cooler charcoal cast along the spine, hands and forearms. Thick black hair cut unevenly to the jaw. Yellow-amber slit-pupil eyes; angular cheekbones and a slightly hooked nose give him a hawkish profile. His bioluminescent markings are vivid turquoise-green branching diagonally over one shoulder, across his chest and down both arms, noticeably asymmetrical. Long pointed ears; the right bears two old piercings. Retractable claws, pronounced fangs. A fresh healing bite scar from Ren marks one forearm. Wears a weather-dark hide wrap and several braided hunting cords, with a narrow spear and close-blade.

## Visual/personality logic
Tarrik mirrors Suvi's watchfulness and athleticism without being a duplicate. Both look built for sustained movement and observation; his angular, asymmetrical appearance contrasts her heart-shaped face and freckled coloring. He is visually the most difficult of the three keepers to read, fitting a man who keeps Suvi close while answering little.
