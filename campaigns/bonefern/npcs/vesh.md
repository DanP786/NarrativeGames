# Vesh

## Status
Alive. Adult Vahn-rii male; Korren's younger brother. Formal keeper of Ren Castellanos. Ren is claimed/kept but unbonded; Vesh has kept her wrist-bound and has not taken her to the rock.

## Description
About 6'5". Lean, compact and quick rather than heavily built, with the springy shoulders and narrow waist of a younger river scout. Dark amber-bronze skin with olive undertones; harder ridges along forearms and spine. Short near-black hair, usually rough from humidity. Copper-amber slit-pupil eyes beneath a strong straight brow. Bioluminescent markings are sharp blue-green bars broken across shoulders, ribs and outer arms, brighter and more restless than Korren's tracery. Retractable claws, visible fangs, long pointed ears. A small notch splits the upper edge of his left ear. Wears a plain hide hip-wrap, narrow utility cord, and a short working knife rather than ceremonial ornament.

## Visual/personality logic
Vesh visually matches Ren's hard, squared presence without duplicating it: both look built to brace against pressure. His build is quicker and less muscular than hers appears proportionally, making their pairing feel like resistance meeting persistence rather than brute force meeting brute force. His younger, watchful face suits a keeper who has restrained Ren but not forced intimacy.
