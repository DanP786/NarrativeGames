# Asho

## Status
Alive. Adult Vahn-rii male. Formal keeper of Liesel Varga. Liesel is claimed/kept but unbonded; Asho guards her and has left her untouched.

## Description
About 6'2", among the shorter adult Vahn-rii males but still towering over Liesel. Long-limbed and fine-boned for a Kael'tha male, with controlled strength rather than bulk. Warm bronze skin, smoother across the face with dark armour-ridging at spine and forearms. Shoulder-length black hair worn loosely tied at the nape. Pale amber slit-pupil eyes that can look almost gold in filtered daylight. His bioluminescence is subdued sea-green: thin, symmetrical lines along throat, collarbones and wrists rather than broad marks. Long pointed ears, retractable claws, modest fangs. A narrow pale scar crosses one eyebrow. Dresses simply in soft dark hide with a small bone clasp and carries a long utility blade.

## Visual/personality logic
Asho's quiet, restrained appearance complements Liesel's pale, slim, withdrawn presence. He is the least visually aggressive of the three unbonded keepers: still unmistakably predatory and alien, but precise and contained. Their contrast is dark/warm against pale/fair, while the shared narrow silhouettes make them visually coherent together.
