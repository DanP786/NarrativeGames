# Mira Holloway

## Vitals
- **Age:** 26
- **Heritage:** Puerto Rican.
- **Origin:** Earth-born, colony-track. Spent most of her adult life shipboard.
- **Last assignment:** Filtration tech, colony ship *Helia-Voss*. Water systems primary, air systems secondary. Three years on that ship; six years shipboard total.
- **Build:** 5'2". Hourglass — large breasts, narrow waist, wide hips, thick thighs, fine ankles and wrists. The kind of body that did not fit standard-issue jumpsuits well.
- **Skin:** Warm brown/olive. Holds colour easily.
- **Hair:** Black, wavy — curls tighter when wet, frizzes in humid air. Worn long, past the shoulder blades.
- **Eyes:** Dark brown, large and expressive — they give her away.
- **Marks:** Small sealed implant at the base of her skull, hidden under the hair, known since Day 2 when Senna found it. Mira knows the device is real but does not know its full purpose. **GM canon:** the implant does not cause or control the mating bond; the cave spores are the genuine native bond mechanism. The implant is experiment infrastructure and includes translation.
- **Former implant mark:** faint inner-bicep scar from a cosmetic implant removed during an ordinary pre-boarding medical exam. Mira remembers the human convention that cosmetic implants commonly sat at the inner bicep, general medical devices above the breast near the heart, and neurological/brain-related devices at the base of the skull.

## Backstory
Mira grew up in a huge Puerto Rican family before entering the colony/shipboard track; the later institutional and shipboard years left her sharply aware of the difference between being surrounded by family and being socially alone. Tested into a technical track at fourteen and into filtration at seventeen. She liked the work because it was unsupervised and quiet: a malfunctioning regulator did not care that she had not said good morning. The *Helia-Voss* was her third ship. She worked night shift, where she made few friends, kept her quarters tidy, kept her head down, and ate alone more often than not. She was three months from rotating off when she remembers being in a transfer corridor and seeing a face she could not place. She does not remember anything between then and waking on the planet.

Before boarding, crew underwent ordinary medical and psychological/mind assessments. People dependent on necessary medical or neurological implants were disallowed aboard. Mira had a cosmetic implant removed as part of the pre-voyage process. Thousands were aboard the ship; she had seen Suvi around during night-shift life but had never spoken with her.

## Personality
Shy, quiet, watchful. Reads rooms before she walks into them. Speaks less than she thinks. Hates being looked at. Underneath that — and she does not always like this about herself — she is reflex-fast: when something startles her she moves before her brain has finished. Under real pressure she can push past the shyness and speak to effect, but confrontation costs her and she can also collapse back into silence when a room turns on her.

She has had two relationships, both short, both with men. Neither prepared her for any of this.

## Current state (~Day 70–75, Vahn-rii village)
- **Bonded mate to Korren.** Mira presently chooses life with him and describes their relationship plainly: Korren dominates and she follows; she has learned to live beside him rather than the reverse.
- Mira likes much of being kept/claimed by Korren and experiences his constancy as safety, but the beginning of his keeping preceded meaningful choice and the cave changed her physically. Her agency question remains unresolved: she wants him and usually does not want to say no, yet she cannot cleanly prove to herself how much freedom exists inside that wanting.
- She and Korren have built roughly two months of shared domestic routine. He knows where her filtration water belongs and how she works; their lodge is home in the practical, daily sense as well as his dwelling.
- Mira has become increasingly willing to defend Vahn-rii people/custom and individual men when she thinks the human women flatten them into a single threat, while still recognizing that Mari's accidental bond and fear are real and that Ada's refusal deserves protection.
- Mira has felt socially cast out by the other human women. After hoping the six stranded women would support one another, she went roughly two months without a substantive conversation with any of them. Liesel has now reopened contact and apologized for her own absence; Mira accepted the opening but remains bitter that this came only after Liesel herself voluntarily bonded with Asho.
- Mira wants answers about the women's arrival, their implants/scars, the cave mechanism, and the hidden hand behind their placement. She distinguishes what she saw, what she thinks, and what she does not know.

## Goals
- Survive and build a life that contains more than survival.
- Understand why the six women were brought here and what the made thing at her nape is for.
- Keep a foothold of competence and standing that is hers, especially through water/filtration work.
- Preserve what she genuinely wants with Korren while remaining able to ask whether wanting and choosing are the same thing.
- Recover some form of human companionship without pretending the earlier abandonment did not hurt.
