# Main thread

**Status:** active, open-ended — post-two-month consolidation.

## Central question
What does a woman become when everything that defined her is taken and the world replaces it with appetite, weather, custom, and a body it will see?

For Mira the question remains doubled: she loves Korren and continues choosing life with him, while also knowing that the beginning of his keeping preceded meaningful choice and that the cave changed her in ways she cannot cleanly separate from herself. Neither truth cancels the other.

## Current human spectrum (~Day 70–75)
The six women no longer fit a single response to Vethaal or the Vahn-rii:
- **Mira:** bonded with Korren; actively chooses their shared life while continuing to want answers about the bond, implants, selection, and arrival.
- **Mari:** bonded with Drenn by accident in the cave; remains frightened/conflicted and has not conveniently converted to acceptance.
- **Suvi:** voluntarily chose to bond with Tarrik after already liking him, strongly because she wanted physical intimacy with him and accepted the Vahn-rii rite required before it.
- **Liesel:** voluntarily chose to bond with Asho after his restraint and protection made her feel safe over time.
- **Ren:** remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened over two months; less continuously armored, without that being treated as consent to sex/bond.
- **Ada:** remains unbonded/unclaimed under her recognized refusal conditions unless later play changes it.

This widening spectrum is important evidence in-world: attraction, safety, resistance, fear, and bonding are not presenting identically across the six women. Voluntary pre-cave attachment now clearly exists in multiple cases, while Mari remains the strongest counterexample to any claim that bond automatically creates willing acceptance.

## Hidden truth — GM only
The six were deliberately selected from thousands: healthy women of childbearing age plus psychological predisposition toward accepting/settling into being controlled, claimed, or kept under suitable circumstances. The experiment tests native spore-mediated human/Kael'tha mating bonds and whether bonded pairs can reproduce. The spores/bond are genuinely native. The implants do not cause or control the bond and do not mind-control the women; they are experiment infrastructure including translation.

## Direction after the skip
The two voluntary new bonds complicate rather than solve the central question. Suvi and Liesel had distinct reasons to choose their partners before bonding; Ren's softening occurs without bonding; Mari remains distressed despite being bonded. Future evidence should preserve these distinctions and avoid treating any one woman's experience as a universal rule. The origin/experiment thread should continue to harden through discoverable evidence rather than revelation by fiat.
