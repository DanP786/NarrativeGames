# Current scene — foreign Kael'tha chief's lodge, ~Day 71–76, late afternoon

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira, gave her access to stream water, and brought her to their village because they refuse to cross the Vahn-rii boundary and want their leadership to arrange her return. Both know Korren's name and react negatively/warily.
- En route, Mira learned that Korren has a reputation here for repeated border crossings and once injured three of this people's men while forcing his way out rather than submitting to detention. She also learned that years ago Korren sought the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate; the chief refused him. Whether the daughter wanted Korren and the exact nature of Korren's response remain unestablished.
- Mira is now before the foreign chief. After learning she is Korren's mate, he asks whether Korren brought her into his territory. Mira answers plainly: "No, I got lost." She thereby makes clear that this border crossing is hers, accidental, and not another Korren incursion.
- The chief accepts that answer provisionally rather than accusing Mira of lying. He questions how far she wandered and whether Korren knows where she is. Mira's escorts can corroborate that they found her alone, exhausted, scratched, and lost deep inside their territory.
- The chief recognizes the practical danger: Korren may search for Mira and cross the boundary if he finds her trail. He must decide how to return or signal Mira without turning her accidental presence into a fresh border confrontation.
- Neither escort has harmed Mira. She is tired, scratched, emotionally raw, and far from home, but has drunk water and is now inside an inhabited settlement.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking.

## Continuity
- Mira/Korren remain bonded mates sharing the lodge and an established daily routine.
- Mira is 26, fully adult, 5'2", Puerto Rican, and notably small beside Kael'tha adults.
- Mira's river-stone mate-bead cord remains visibly worn over her light dress. It was tied on by Korren before she left for the meeting.
- Kael'tha territorial boundaries can restrict members of one people from entering another's land without consequence. This foreign people will not casually cross into Vahn-rii territory.
- Kael'tha cultures recognize established mating/claim signals, but customs vary; do not assume this village shares every Vahn-rii rule.
- Mira may plausibly be pregnant, but this remains unconfirmed and has no supernatural or bond-based signal.
- No telepathy exists. The bond is physical pull plus audible harmonic and does not allow Korren to track Mira remotely.
