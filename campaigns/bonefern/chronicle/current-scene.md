# Current scene — Korren's lodge, Day 10 midday

- Mira wakes from her rest and stretches beneath the light fur; flexing her right foot wakes the pain beneath Senna's dressing again. Korren wakes with her and reminds her not to put weight on it yet.
- Korren raises the private thing he marked at council. Mira explains that Thaal asking how Korren had "made" her forced her to feel the difference between what the camp sees now and how little choice she had at the beginning. She tells Korren she chose the cave and chooses him now, but cannot make the beginning into a choice after the fact. Korren acknowledges the first taking was his decision, not hers.
- Mira says, "I don't want anything to change." Korren understands this as a present choice and takes her back into his arms deliberately. Their quiet intimacy follows; afterward Korren protects her injured foot.
- Senna comes to the lodge for the promised private conversation about Mira's neck. Mira grants it with a nod; Korren protects her privacy while she dresses, then remains outside.
- Mira dresses in the river-green mated-wife clothing, braces her back against the bed structure, and keeps her injured foot extended without weight. Senna enters alone and asks what Mira has learned since Senna first found the made thing.
- Mira says she believes the device translates language, then corrects her certainty when pressed: she does not know for sure. Her inference is that both the implanted object and her effortless knowledge of Vahn-rii speech are new, and devices like this are not inherently strange where she came from.
- Asked whether she chose the implant, Mira says no, and adds that she did not choose to be on Vethaal either. She then gives Senna her fullest account yet of the human mission: the six women were crew aboard a transport in the sky, with different technical duties keeping ship and people alive; the mission was to find a planet humans could inhabit; strict mission rules forbade settlement on a world already inhabited by intelligent life; trained first-landing personnel, not workers like Mira and the other women, were supposed to make planetary landfall. Mira says plainly that the women are frightened, unprepared for survival on this planet, and should never have been put here.
- Senna understands the implications in her own terms: Mira's people were seeking an empty world, Vethaal's peoples make this world forbidden to them, and therefore the six women's arrival cannot be the mission operating as intended. Combined with the unchosen hidden implants and deliberate dispersal, this strengthens Senna's suspicion of an outside hand acting against both the women and the intended rules of their own people. She asks the next unavoidable question: if the women were not meant to land, who aboard Mira's sky-ship had the power to put them here?

## Carry-forward
- Ada is under Senna's roof; the other human women have begun converging there.
- Senna is alone with Mira in Korren's lodge. She now knows the broad human colony mission, the intelligent-life prohibition, that these six women were ship workers rather than trained first-landing personnel, and that Mira says none of them chose this landing.
- Senna knows Mira did not choose the implant and that implanted technology itself is plausible where Mira comes from; she does not yet know the spore mechanism.
- Senna has asked who aboard the human ship had the ability/power to put the six women on Vethaal despite their mission rules.
- Mira's foot is dressed with moss-pad and salve; Senna's instruction remains: walk on it slow, not far, not today.
- Korren is outside and does not know the spore mechanism or Mira's private conversation with Ada; Senna and Mira's current conversation is private from him.
