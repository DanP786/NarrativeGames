# Current scene — foreign healer's lodge, ~Day 71–76, late afternoon

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira, gave her access to stream water, and brought her to their village because they refuse to cross the Vahn-rii boundary and want their leadership to arrange her return. En route Mira learned Korren's reputation here includes repeated border crossings, injuring three men while forcing his way out of detention, and years ago seeking the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate before the chief refused him. Exact circumstances of the daughter incident remain unestablished.
- Mira clarified to the chief that Korren did not bring her here; she became lost and crossed the boundary unknowingly. The chief intends to keep Mira in the village overnight and post watchers toward Vahn-rii approaches because Korren may follow her trail.
- The chief's mate **Sahra** initially mistook Mira for a child, then accepted that Mira is a 26-year-old adult human. She has taken responsibility for Mira's immediate care and brought her next door to female village healer **Orenna**.
- Orenna finds Mira's jungle injuries superficial. Sahra washes Mira and her hair while Orenna examines her. Sahra remarks that her daughter loved having her hair washed when younger.
- Mira reports two moons since her last menstrual bleeding and says Senna examined her that morning and thought pregnancy possible. Orenna performs a more thorough clinical reproductive examination with Mira's consent and becomes personally confident Mira is pregnant, while acknowledging she has never examined a human and cannot know viability, compatibility, or exact gestation.
- After the examination, Orenna retrieves a clean dress that can fit Mira and explains frankly that it is a **child's dress**, because ordinary adult Kael'tha clothing is much too large for Mira. It is offered for cleanliness and comfort while Mira's own light dress is dirty from the jungle; it does not imply Mira is considered a child after her age was clarified.
- Mira reaches for her river-stone Vahn-rii mate-bead cord to put it back on. Sahra places her hand gently but deliberately over Mira's before she can take it and says: **"I would like to know more about your story with Korren before you put these on."** Sahra's concern is informed by her family's unresolved prior history with Korren and by learning that Mira may be carrying his child. She does not seize or destroy the beads; they remain Mira's property and within reach.
- This creates an immediate agency-sensitive conversation: Mira has chosen to continue wearing Korren's beads in her established life, while Sahra knows a different, still-unexplained side of Korren's history. Sahra may ask questions or tell what she witnessed, but she must not be treated as omniscient about Mira/Korren or as automatically correct about the old daughter incident.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking.
