# Current scene — Korren's lodge, Day 10 midday

- Mira wakes from her rest and stretches beneath the light fur; flexing her right foot wakes the pain beneath Senna's dressing again. Korren wakes with her and reminds her not to put weight on it yet.
- Korren raises the private thing he marked at council. Mira explains that Thaal asking how Korren had "made" her forced her to feel the difference between what the camp sees now and how little choice she had at the beginning. She tells Korren she chose the cave and chooses him now, but cannot make the beginning into a choice after the fact. Korren acknowledges the first taking was his decision, not hers.
- Mira says, "I don't want anything to change." Korren understands this as a present choice and takes her back into his arms deliberately. In the quiet afterward he affectionately catalogues the human features he finds alien and appealing, including her extreme smallness and fuller proportions.
- Their teasing turns into familiar consensual intimacy; afterward Korren settles Mira carefully so her injured foot is protected. Someone approaches the lodge.
- Korren rises, draws the fur securely over Mira, fastens his hide hip-wrap and goes to the entrance, deliberately blocking the visitor's sightline into the sleeping space.
- Mira sits upright in the furs, gathering the cover around her chest and lap while keeping her dressed right foot clear. From the bed she can see Korren filling the entrance but not yet the visitor beyond him; she listens for the visitor to answer.
