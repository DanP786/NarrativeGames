# Current scene — Korren's lodge, Day 10 midday

- Mira wakes from her rest and stretches beneath the light fur. The stretch feels good through her back and limbs, but flexing her right foot wakes the pain beneath Senna's dressing again.
- Her wince wakes Korren enough to react: his arm tightens around her waist, broad hand spreading over her stomach and drawing her back against him. He checks the foot without getting up, reminds her not to put weight on it yet, and remains awake behind her as the lodge settles into midday heat.
- Korren raises the private thing he marked at council: Mira took her hand from him before the cliff crisis. Mira initially thinks he means her running to Ada and asks, "To go to Ada. Should I not have?" Korren distinguishes the two moments: he does not condemn her going to Ada; he is asking why she withdrew from him at Thaal's fire before anyone shouted.
- Mira gives him the best answer she can: hearing Thaal ask how Korren had "made" her so forced her to feel the difference between what the camp sees now and how little choice she had at the beginning. She tells Korren she did choose the cave and chooses him now, but she cannot make the beginning into a choice after the fact. She does not disclose the spore mechanism or her private human-tongue conversation with Ada. Korren hears her without releasing her and recognizes that his public answer to Thaal did not erase what came before it.

## Carry-forward
- Ada is under Senna's roof; the other human women have begun converging there.
- Senna intends to speak privately with Mira about her neck.
- Mira's foot is dressed with moss-pad and salve; Senna's instruction remains: walk on it slow, not far, not today.
- Korren's private later has opened into a direct reckoning over Mira's early lack of choice versus her present choice of him.
