# Current scene — Korren and Mira's lodge, ~Day 71–76, humid morning

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- The morning after Mira and Korren's love-language conversation, Korren is away from the lodge and Mira is tidying their shared home in the humid heat.
- Mira wears a light cloth dress she made for the weather: low at front and back, with slits at either side of the skirt for airflow and movement. Her curly hair is piled in a loose nest atop her head.
- Senna entered because Korren summoned her without telling Mira, asking Senna to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna clarified Korren only suspects from small changes he noticed; the bond provides no supernatural pregnancy knowledge.
- Mira consented to Senna's examination. Mira reports she has not menstruated at all since arriving, although stress, weight loss, increased exertion, and changed food are plausible competing explanations. Her cycle was regular before arrival and tracked aboard ship. She has not noticed a definite recent change in her reaction to meat smells.
- Mira voiced fear about the size and safety of a possible human/Vahn-rii child. Senna explained Vahn-rii newborns are small but admitted she has no precedent for a mixed pregnancy and cannot promise how one would develop.
- Mira complies with the physical examination and lies back on the sleeping furs. Senna examines her conservatively and non-invasively first: pulse, eyes, skin, abdomen, and gentle palpation low over the belly, comparing what she feels against her experience with Vahn-rii pregnancies while acknowledging human anatomy may differ.
- Senna finds enough subtle signs, together with Mira's prolonged absent cycle, to consider pregnancy genuinely plausible, but not enough to claim certainty from this single early examination. She tells Mira she cannot honestly call it confirmed yet and wants to watch for change over the coming days/weeks rather than invent certainty.
