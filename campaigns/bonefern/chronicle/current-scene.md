# Current scene — Korren and Mira's lodge, ~Day 70–75, late afternoon

Two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Mira returned from fetching water with Liesel. Their first substantive conversation in roughly two months reopened Mira's hurt over being socially isolated after bonding with Korren. Liesel apologized for her own absence and asked to begin coming to Mira now; Mira accepted with a strained smile despite bitterness that Liesel reaches out only after bonding herself.
- Korren spent part of the afternoon counseling Drenn about Mari. He told Drenn he cannot make Mari want their accidental bond and that if every no costs her something, it is not a word Drenn has allowed her to keep.

- Mira told him, "Stop teasing me," and gave his chest the lightest possible tap. Korren teased that she strikes poorly and should practice.
- Mira replies, "I'm not a warrior. I'm a technician," and turns back toward the fire, reclaiming the cooking task and her composure.
- Korren allows her to turn and does not recapture her. He remains nearby and answers that he knows what she is good at, his attention briefly settling on the practical arrangement around her fire/water work rather than treating her competence as lesser because it is not fighting.
- The domestic teasing settles into companionable proximity beside the fire. Food preparation remains underway.
