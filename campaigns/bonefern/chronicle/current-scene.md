# Current scene — Korren and Mira's lodge, ~Day 70–75, late afternoon

Two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Mira returned from fetching water with Liesel. Their first substantive conversation in roughly two months reopened Mira's hurt over being socially isolated after bonding with Korren. Liesel apologized for her own absence and asked to begin coming to Mira now; Mira accepted with a strained smile despite bitterness that Liesel reaches out only after bonding herself.
- Korren spent part of the afternoon counseling Drenn about Mari. He told Drenn he cannot make Mari want their accidental bond and that if every no costs her something, it is not a word Drenn has allowed her to keep. He advised Drenn that sometimes he must not go to Mari merely because he feels her distress, and must learn which fear asks for his hand and which worsens when he gives it.
- Mira and Korren shared a playful domestic exchange while she cooked. When Mira asked about Drenn, Korren explained that Drenn wants a rule for keeping Mari close without worsening her fear, but must instead learn to listen even when she says what he does not want to hear.
- Mira challenges the framing: "You're focusing too much on the bond. Drenn should just be helpful, supportive. Like you taking my bucket without asking."
- Korren takes the comparison seriously rather than dismissing it. He recognizes Mira is distinguishing ordinary acts of care from constantly treating every interaction as a problem of bond, claim, fear, or permission. He notes that he took Mira's bucket because it was heavy and he knew where she wanted it, not because the bond required an answer.
- Korren considers that Drenn may need to learn Mari's ordinary life and needs rather than approaching her chiefly as a bonded woman whose fear must be solved. He intends to tell Drenn this when next they speak, while understanding that helpfulness still has to respect what Mari does and does not want from him.
- Food continues cooking; the conversation remains serious but domestic and collaborative rather than confrontational.
