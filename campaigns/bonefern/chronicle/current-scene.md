## Immediate scene

- Mira wears a light cloth dress she made for the weather: low at front and back, with slits at either side of the skirt for airflow and movement. Her curly hair is piled in a loose nest atop her head.
- Senna entered because Korren summoned her without telling Mira, asking Senna to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna clarified Korren only suspects from small changes he noticed; the bond provides no supernatural pregnancy knowledge.
- Mira consented to Senna's examination. Mira reports she has not menstruated at all since arriving, although stress, weight loss, increased exertion, and changed food are plausible competing explanations. She says her cycle was regular before arrival and that shipboard women had to track their cycles. She has not noticed a definite recent change in her reaction to meat smells.
- The possibility becomes emotionally real enough that Mira blurts out concern about fetal/birth size: "How big are your babies? I can't push a 7 foot alien out!"

However, she does not pretend to know whether a human/Vahn-rii pregnancy is possible, how a mixed child would develop, or what risks Mira's smaller human body might face; there is no known precedent available to Senna. She treats that uncertainty seriously rather than reassuring Mira falsely.
- Senna still has not established pregnancy. The next step is the physical examination Mira already consented to, with Senna able to look for signs but not claim certainty beyond what her methods support.
