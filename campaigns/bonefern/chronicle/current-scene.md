# Current scene — foreign healer's lodge, ~Day 71–76, late afternoon

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira, gave her access to stream water, and brought her to their village because they refuse to cross the Vahn-rii boundary and want their leadership to arrange her return. En route Mira learned Korren's reputation here includes repeated border crossings, injuring three men while forcing his way out of detention, and years ago seeking the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate before the chief refused him. Exact circumstances of the daughter incident remain unestablished.
- Mira clarified to the chief that Korren did not bring her here; she became lost and crossed the boundary unknowingly. The chief intends to keep Mira in the village overnight and post watchers toward Vahn-rii approaches because Korren may follow her trail.
- The chief's mate **Sahra** has taken responsibility for Mira's immediate care and brought her next door to female village healer **Orenna**. Orenna finds Mira's jungle injuries superficial. Sahra washes Mira and her hair; Mira relaxes under the caretaking. Sahra mentions that her daughter loved having her hair washed when younger.
- Mira reports two moons since her last menstrual bleeding. Orenna performs a more thorough clinical reproductive examination with Mira's consent and becomes personally confident Mira is pregnant, while acknowledging she has never examined a human and cannot know viability, compatibility, or exact gestation.
- Orenna provides Mira a clean Kael'tha child's dress because adult clothing is too large. Mira's own dirty dress remains her property.
- Sahra questions Mira about Korren. Mira says the mate-beads mean "that I'm mated," that she and Korren mated three days after he found her, and that Thaal had not allowed it. Mira also clarifies she chose to leave with Korren for the cave but did not know what the cave would physically do to her.
- Sahra reacts strongly to the combination of Mira's small size, rapid mating, Thaal's prohibition, unknown cave effects, and Orenna's belief that Mira may now be carrying Korren's child. She picks up Mira's mate-bead cord and says: **"I will not give these back until he has earned the right to give them to you."** This is Sahra's protective judgment and an assertion of household/elder authority, not objective proof that Korren lacks a valid bond or that Mira's present choice is false. The beads remain intact in Sahra's possession; Mira has not yet consented to Sahra keeping them or demanded them back.
- Sahra immediately asks Mira: **"Are there more like you?"** She does not yet know there are exactly six human women total, nor their individual bond/claim statuses.
- If Mira answers fully, relevant truth is exactly six human women including Mira: Mira bonded Korren; Mari bonded Drenn accidentally and remains frightened/conflicted; Suvi voluntarily bonded Tarrik after already liking him; Liesel voluntarily bonded Asho after trust/safety developed; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed. Mira must choose what to disclose and how to frame it.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking.
