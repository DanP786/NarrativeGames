# Current scene — foreign healer's lodge, ~Day 71–76, late afternoon

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira, gave her access to stream water, and brought her to their village because they refuse to cross the Vahn-rii boundary and want their leadership to arrange her return. En route Mira learned Korren's reputation here includes repeated border crossings, injuring three men while forcing his way out of detention, and years ago seeking the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate before the chief refused him. Exact circumstances of the daughter incident remain unestablished.
- Mira clarified to the chief that Korren did not bring her here; she became lost and crossed the boundary unknowingly. The chief intends to keep Mira in the village overnight and post watchers toward Vahn-rii approaches because Korren may follow her trail.
- The chief's mate **Sahra** initially mistook Mira for a child, then accepted that Mira is a 26-year-old adult human. She has taken responsibility for Mira's immediate care and brought her next door to the female village healer **Orenna**.
- Orenna finds Mira's jungle injuries superficial: scratches, abrasions, dirt, and overuse rather than major injury. Her old foot injury has had roughly two months of ordinary healing.
- While Orenna examines the wounds, Sahra begins washing the uninjured dirt and sweat from Mira with clean water. She eventually washes Mira's long dark hair; Mira visibly relaxes under the familiar caretaking sensation. Sahra remarks that her daughter loved having her hair washed when she was younger, introducing a tender personal note without yet resolving the daughter's history with Korren.
- For a fuller medical examination and because Orenna has never seen a human body, Orenna removes/sets aside Mira's mate-bead cord and light dress with Mira permitting the examination. The scene remains clinical/nonsexual. Orenna observes basic human anatomical differences and asks many practical questions about normal human size, body temperature, pulse, menstruation, pregnancy, diet, prior injuries, reproductive cycles, and what changes Mira has noticed since arriving.
- When Orenna asks how long it has been since Mira last bled / had her normal monthly cycle, Mira answers, **"Two moons."** This is consistent with what Mira told Senna earlier: she has not menstruated since arrival despite previously regular tracked cycles.
- Orenna now has a medically relevant reason to pay closer attention to Mira's abdomen and reproductive history, but pregnancy remains unconfirmed. She has no human/Vahn-rii precedent and cannot diagnose pregnancy by intuition or species-specific assumptions alone.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking.

Senna earlier found absent menstruation after previously regular cycles plus subtle low abdominal fullness/warmth suggestive but not diagnostic; no fetus was palpated and human/Vahn-rii compatibility is unknown.
