# Current scene — deep jungle beyond Vahn-rii village, ~Day 71–76, mid-afternoon

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Ada admitted the exclusion was deliberate and rooted in distrust/fear that information shared with Mira might reach Korren or the Vahn-rii.
- Overwhelmed by hurt, humiliation, and anger, Mira left the meeting and walked blindly beyond the village into the jungle. She did not track her turns or route and became disoriented beyond the settled paths.
- Mira initially chose a direction she believed led back, but it took her farther away. She then changed direction repeatedly, each correction based on uncertain impressions rather than navigation skill. Over roughly three hours she has compounded the mistake and no longer has a reliable sense of the village's direction.
- It is now around mid-afternoon. Mira is hot, sweaty, physically tired, scratched by vegetation, emotionally depleted, and without her usual water vessels or field kit. She finally stops rather than continuing to blunder through the jungle.
- Mira breaks down crying for several overlapping reasons: the deliberate two-month exclusion by the other women; humiliation and betrayal around today's meeting; fear from being genuinely lost; regret at leaving without thinking; and the unresolved possibility that she may be carrying a child while alone and unprepared in the jungle. These emotions coexist without resolving what Mira ultimately wants about any possible pregnancy.
- No one has yet reached Mira in established canon. Korren cannot locate her through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking.

## Continuity
- Mira/Korren remain bonded mates sharing the lodge and an established daily routine.
- Mira now knows the other five held daily women's meetings without her for roughly two months.
- Mira is untrained in wilderness survival/navigation. Her repeated direction changes have worsened her disorientation.
- Mira's mate-bead cord remains visibly worn over her light dress.
- Mira may plausibly be pregnant, but this remains unconfirmed and has no supernatural or bond-based signal. Her fear while lost does not settle her feelings about pregnancy.
- No telepathy exists. The bond is physical pull plus audible harmonic and does not allow Korren to track Mira remotely.
