Two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Drenn remains at/near Korren and Mira's lodge waiting to speak with Korren about Mari. His statement that Mira chooses to keep following Korren has reopened Mira's private unresolved agency question.
- Mira met newly bonded Liesel while fetching water. Liesel proudly showed her mating beads; Mira complimented Asho's choice. Their light friend-talk turned serious when Mira reminded Liesel how judged/cast out Mira felt after her own bond.

Liesel acknowledged that the women were so focused on whether Korren was dangerous that Mira defending him became another reason not to trust her.

Mira says she came from a huge family, then had no one aboard ship because night shift made friendship difficult. She had hoped the six women would support one another after being stranded here, but instead she was singled out as untrustworthy.
- Mira states the concrete consequence: she has not had a single conversation with any of the other human women in the two months since the Day 10 rupture/meeting period.

- Liesel does not claim to speak for Ada, Mari, Ren, or Suvi. She accepts responsibility only for her own failure to approach Mira. She tells Mira she should have come to her sooner and that being afraid did not require leaving Mira alone.
- The two continue carrying the bucket together toward Mira and Korren's lodge. This is the first substantive human-woman conversation Mira has had in roughly two months.
