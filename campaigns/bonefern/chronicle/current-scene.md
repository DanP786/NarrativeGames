# Current scene — Korren's lodge, Day 10 midday

- Mira wakes from her rest and stretches beneath the light fur; flexing her right foot wakes the pain beneath Senna's dressing again. Korren wakes with her and reminds her not to put weight on it yet.
- Korren raises the private thing he marked at council: Mira took her hand from him before the cliff crisis. Mira explains that Thaal asking how Korren had "made" her forced her to feel the difference between what the camp sees now and how little choice she had at the beginning. She tells Korren she chose the cave and chooses him now, but cannot make the beginning into a choice after the fact. She does not disclose the spore mechanism or her private human-tongue conversation with Ada.
- Korren acknowledges that he found her and decided she was his, and that he had thought the question was whether he kept her well afterward rather than whether the first taking was his to decide. He removes the weight of his arm and leaves Mira the choice to restore it.
- Mira turns to face him and says, "I don't want anything to change." Korren understands this as a present choice, not permission to erase the past. He takes her back into his arms deliberately; their relationship remains possessive and physically close by Mira's expressed choice.
- In the quiet afterward, Korren describes what remains strikingly alien and beloved to him about Mira's human body: her extreme smallness even compared with Vahn-rii women, her softness rather than pebbled hide, her rounded proportions, fine wrists and ankles, large expressive eyes, dark hair without bioluminescent tracery, and the way her whole hand disappears within his. He frames these differences as things he likes rather than defects, while Mira hears herself described as the alien for once.
