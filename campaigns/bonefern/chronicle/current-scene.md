## Immediate scene
- Mira fled the Vahn-rii women's meeting after learning the other five human women had met daily for roughly two months while excluding her. She became lost, crossed into another Kael'tha territory, and was brought to the foreign village.
- Chief's mate **Sahra** and healer **Orenna** took Mira into their care. Orenna correctly identified an early pregnancy despite no human/Kael'tha precedent. Several days later Mira suffered a miscarriage/early pregnancy loss. Cause remains unknown. She is now physically recovering; bleeding and pain have lessened and Orenna continues monitoring her.
- Sahra learned Mira and Korren mated three days after he found her despite Thaal and Senna opposing the cave; Mira chose to leave with Korren but did not know what the cave would physically do. Sahra judged Mira's broader situation a failure of protection and offered to treat Mira as a daughter/member of this tribe. Mira has not formally accepted permanent residence or daughter-status.
- Sahra holds Mira's intact river-stone mate-bead cord and says Korren must earn the right to give it to Mira again. Mira has not consented to Sahra keeping it.
- **Old daughter incident:** years ago the chief and Sahra refused Korren as prospective mate for their adult daughter. Their daughter chose Korren and left with him anyway. They departed in haste; she suffered an accident during that hurried departure/journey and died. Korren did not deliberately harm or kill her. Exact mechanics, aftermath, bond status, and Korren's precise responsibility/rescue attempts remain unestablished.
- The chief would genuinely allow Korren a path to join this tribe. He believes Korren's central fault is that he hears boundaries/orders but decides whether another person's reason outweighs his own. He is willing to teach Korren restraint, answerability, communal boundaries, and accepting judgment/no when he disagrees.
- Boundary-watchers have repeatedly reported **movement/sign on the far side of the border**, but nobody has crossed into this tribe's territory. It is still not directly confirmed as Korren. The restraint is notable given his history, but identity/motive remain unconfirmed.
- Once Mira is well enough for gentle activity, **Sahra shows her around the foreign village.** The settlement is materially and visibly different from Vahn-rii: **larger, more permanent, more prosperous-looking, and substantially more decorative.** It is not merely a temporary/scouting-style collection of shelters. Homes and communal structures are deliberately integrated into large living trees with substantial platforms, fixed walkways, carefully lashed or fitted supports, weather protection, storage, and established household spaces.
- Decoration is common rather than exceptional: carved or patterned wood/bark, woven panels, dyed fibers, beadwork, polished stone, painted markings, decorative knots, planted/encouraged flowering or luminous growth, and household objects made beautiful as well as useful. Exact motifs can be established in play as needed. The village feels generations-deep and intentionally inhabited.
- The settlement has more people and more specialized/shared spaces than Mira is accustomed to in Vahn-rii: broad communal platforms, cooking/work areas, storage, craft spaces, places where children gather/play, and paths/walkways clearly maintained as village infrastructure. This does **not** establish that the tribe is universally culturally superior; it establishes greater permanence, scale, material investment, and decorative emphasis at this settlement.
- Mira remains conspicuously small among them and is still wearing foreign child-sized clothing because adult garments do not fit. Sahra's tour is partly practical orientation and partly an affectionate attempt to show Mira what the offered life/tribal belonging here would actually mean rather than leaving it abstract.
- Mira/Korren's bond remains intact. Korren cannot locate Mira through it; no telepathy exists. No foreign/Vahn-rii confrontation has yet occurred at the border.
