# Current scene — foreign healer's lodge, ~Day 71–76, late afternoon

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira and brought her to their village. En route Mira learned Korren's reputation here includes repeated border crossings, injuring three men while forcing his way out of detention, and years ago seeking the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate before the chief refused him. Exact circumstances of the daughter incident remain unestablished.
- The chief intends to keep Mira in the village overnight and post watchers toward Vahn-rii approaches because Korren may follow her trail.
- Chief's mate **Sahra** brought Mira to female healer **Orenna**. Orenna finds Mira's jungle injuries superficial. Sahra washes Mira and her hair and mentions that her daughter loved having her hair washed when younger.
- Mira reports two moons since her last menstrual bleeding. Orenna performs a clinical reproductive examination with Mira's consent and becomes personally confident Mira is pregnant, while acknowledging she has never examined a human and cannot know viability, compatibility, or exact gestation.
- Orenna provides Mira a clean Kael'tha child's dress because adult clothing is too large.
- Sahra questions Mira about Korren. Mira says the mate-beads mean "that I'm mated," that she and Korren mated three days after he found her, that Thaal had not allowed it, and that although Mira chose to leave with Korren for the cave she did not know what the cave would physically do to her. Mira clarifies that Senna also opposed the cave because of the unknown made thing/implant in Mira's neck, and that Mira herself told Korren they were going and left secretly with him.
- Sahra learns there are exactly six human women total. Three others are bonded/mated: Suvi and Liesel chose their bonds; Mari's happened accidentally. Mira is the smallest human woman, but even the largest is smaller than Sahra.
- Sahra is struck by the contradictory truth: Korren defied healer and chief, but he did so after Mira herself chose to go. She nevertheless judges the broader situation a failure of protection. Sahra tells Mira: **"Vahn-rii did not protect you. Your people did not protect you. Now you carry a child."** This directly touches Mira's long-standing pain: she has wanted the human women to acknowledge both that the beginning of her relationship lacked meaningful protection/choice and that her present love/choice of Korren is real, rather than reducing her either to victim or advocate for the bond.
- Sahra then escalates from temporary hospitality to a unilateral protective stance: **"I will not return you. If Korren wants you he will have to prove himself. He will join our tribe. He does not listen to his chief; that is dangerous."** This is Sahra's declaration, not yet an established ruling of the foreign chief and not something Mira has agreed to. Sahra's concern is sincere but her proposed solution itself risks overriding Mira's agency by deciding where Mira will live and what Korren must do without first asking Mira.
- Sahra continues holding Mira's intact river-stone mate-bead cord. Mira has not yet demanded it back or consented to its retention.
- The foreign chief earlier intended only to keep Mira overnight and arrange her return; Sahra's new position goes beyond that plan. The chief has not yet heard or endorsed Sahra's declaration.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking. He may plausibly follow physical trail/sign but has not yet been established at the boundary.

## Continuity
- Mira/Korren remain bonded mates sharing an established life despite Sahra's contrary protective declaration and possession of the physical mate-beads.
- Mira is 26, fully adult, 5'2", Puerto Rican, and notably small beside Kael'tha adults.
- Mira is emotionally raw after discovering the other five human women excluded her from daily meetings for two months. Sahra's acknowledgment of Mira's lack of protection at the beginning therefore lands with unusual force, even though Sahra's proposed solution may reproduce the same problem of others making choices for Mira.
- Mira wears a clean Kael'tha child's dress supplied by Orenna. Her own dirty light dress remains her property. Her river-stone mate-bead cord is intact and physically held by Sahra.
- **Pregnancy remains objectively unconfirmed**, but two healers have independently found it plausible and Orenna is personally confident after a more thorough exam. No supernatural or bond-based signal exists. Human/Kael'tha compatibility and pregnancy safety remain unknown.
- No telepathy exists. The bond is physical pull plus audible harmonic and does not allow Korren to track Mira remotely.
