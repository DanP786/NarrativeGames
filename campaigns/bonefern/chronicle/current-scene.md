## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira, gave her access to stream water, and brought her to their village because they refuse to cross the Vahn-rii boundary and want their leadership to arrange her return. En route Mira learned Korren's reputation here includes repeated border crossings, injuring three men while forcing his way out of detention, and years ago seeking the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate before the chief refused him. Exact circumstances of the daughter incident remain unestablished.

- The chief's mate **Sahra** has taken responsibility for Mira's immediate care and brought her next door to female village healer **Orenna**. Orenna finds Mira's jungle injuries superficial. Sahra washes Mira and her hair; Mira relaxes under the caretaking. Sahra mentions that her daughter loved having her hair washed when younger.
- Mira reports two moons since her last menstrual bleeding. Orenna performs a more thorough clinical reproductive examination with Mira's consent and becomes personally confident Mira is pregnant, while acknowledging she has never examined a human and cannot know viability, compatibility, or exact gestation.
- Orenna provides Mira a clean Kael'tha child's dress because adult clothing is too large. Mira's own dirty dress remains her property.
- When Mira reaches for her river-stone mate-bead cord, Sahra gently covers Mira's hand and asks to understand her story with Korren before Mira puts the beads back on. Mira says the beads mean **"that I'm mated."**
- Sahra asks how long after Korren found Mira they mated. Mira pauses and answers quietly: **"Three days."** Sahra then asks whether Mira's chief allowed this. Mira answers: **"No."**
- These answers materially increase Sahra's concern because from her perspective Korren found an unknown, unusually small foreign woman and mated her within three days despite his own chief not allowing it. Sahra does not yet know the full Day1–5 sequence: Korren formally claimed responsibility immediately; Mira chose the cave path after the dispute; Thaal had forbidden the cave because of Senna's medical concern; Korren and Mira left secretly; attraction and attachment existed before the cave; the cave bond then intensified Mira's desire; Mira's present relationship with Korren includes genuine chosen attachment alongside unresolved questions about the unchosen beginning.

- Mira's beads remain within reach under/near Sahra's hand; Sahra has not confiscated them and has already indicated she will move her hand if Mira asks.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking.
