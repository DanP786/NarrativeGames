They departed in haste; she suffered an accident during that hurried departure/journey and died. Korren did not deliberately harm or kill her. Exact mechanics, aftermath, bond status, and Korren's precise responsibility/rescue attempts remain unestablished.

He believes Korren's central fault is that he hears boundaries/orders but decides whether another person's reason outweighs his own. He is willing to teach Korren restraint, answerability, communal boundaries, and accepting judgment/no when he disagrees.
- Boundary-watchers have repeatedly reported **movement/sign on the far side of the border**, but nobody has crossed into this tribe's territory. It is still not directly confirmed as Korren. The restraint is notable given his history, but identity/motive remain unconfirmed.
- Sahra has shown Mira around the foreign village. It is materially larger, more permanent, more prosperous-looking, and substantially more decorative than Vahn-rii, with generations-deep infrastructure, fixed platforms/walkways, rainwater systems, storage, specialized communal/craft areas, carved/patterned structures, dyed fibers, beadwork, polished stone, and deliberately encouraged flowering/luminous growth. This establishes different scale/permanence/material investment, not universal cultural superiority.

I think I had a warmer welcome here than at Vahn-rii."** This is significant because Mira has consistently defended Vahn-rii nuance and values what they gave her; she is not declaring Vahn-rii wholly bad. She is acknowledging that this foreign tribe, despite initially finding her as an unknown outsider, has offered direct care, protection, curiosity, food, medical attention, and explicit belonging in a way Mira did not experience on arrival among Vahn-rii.
- Sahra receives the statement without demanding that Mira renounce Vahn-rii or Korren. Her offer remains that Mira can belong here without first proving hatred of where she came from. This contrast matters deeply after Mira's recent exclusion by the other human women.
- Mira remains conspicuously small among this people and wears foreign child-sized clothing because adult garments do not fit.
- Mira/Korren's bond remains intact. Korren cannot locate Mira through it; no telepathy exists. No foreign/Vahn-rii confrontation has yet occurred at the border.
