# Current scene — chief's household, foreign village, ~Day 71–76, evening

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira and brought her to their village. En route Mira learned Korren's reputation here includes repeated border crossings, injuring three men while forcing his way out of detention, and years ago seeking the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate before the chief refused him.
- Chief's mate **Sahra** brought Mira to female healer **Orenna**. Orenna found Mira's jungle injuries superficial. Sahra washed Mira and her hair. Mira reported two moons since her last menstrual bleeding; after a clinical reproductive examination Orenna became personally confident Mira is pregnant, while acknowledging she has never examined a human and cannot know viability, compatibility, or exact gestation. Orenna provided Mira a clean Kael'tha child's dress because adult clothing is too large.
- Sahra learned Mira and Korren mated three days after he found her despite Thaal and Senna opposing the cave; Mira chose to leave with Korren but did not know what the cave would physically do. Sahra learned there are exactly six human women total, three others bonded/mated: Suvi and Liesel by choice, Mari accidentally.
- Sahra judged Mira's broader situation a failure of protection and told her: **"Vahn-rii did not protect you. Your people did not protect you. Now you carry a child."** This touched Mira's long-standing pain around wanting both the unprotected beginning and her present love/choice acknowledged.
- Sahra holds Mira's intact river-stone mate-bead cord and says Korren must earn the right to give it to Mira again. Mira has not consented to Sahra keeping it.
- Sahra openly declared at the chief's fire that Mira should stay as part of their tribe/daughter and Korren must prove himself worthy to join them. Mira has not accepted permanent residence or daughter-status. The chief insists Mira's wishes matter and will not treat Sahra's declaration as Mira's consent.
- **Old daughter incident:** years ago the chief and Sahra refused Korren as prospective mate for their adult daughter. Their daughter chose Korren and left with him anyway. They departed in haste; she suffered an accident during that hurried departure/journey and died. Korren did not deliberately harm or kill her. Exact mechanics, aftermath, bond status, and Korren's precise responsibility/rescue attempts remain unestablished.
- The chief says he would genuinely allow Korren a path to join. He believes Korren's central fault is that he hears boundaries/orders but decides whether another person's reason outweighs his own. Rather than merely punish/exclude him, the chief is willing to **teach Korren** restraint, answerability, communal boundaries, and accepting judgment/no when he disagrees. This would be a real path, not a rigged test.
- After the difficult conversation, Sahra moves Mira's freshly tended hair out of her face and softly tells her: **"Eat. Then you rest."** Mira is allowed to stop explaining herself. She eats the food Sahra prepared at the fire and is then taken into the chief's household to rest as a protected guest for the night. Sahra's care is maternal/affectionate, but Mira's long-term tribal status remains undecided.
- Sahra's assertion that Mira is with child reflects Orenna's strong medical judgment, not omniscient certainty. Pregnancy remains objectively unconfirmed and unprecedented across species.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking. He may plausibly follow physical trail/sign but has not yet been established at the boundary.
