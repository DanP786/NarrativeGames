# Current scene — foreign healer's lodge, ~Day 71–76, late afternoon

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira and brought her to their village. En route Mira learned Korren's reputation here includes repeated border crossings, injuring three men while forcing his way out of detention, and years ago seeking the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate before the chief refused him. Exact circumstances of the daughter incident remain unestablished.
- The chief intends to keep Mira in the village overnight and post watchers toward Vahn-rii approaches because Korren may follow her trail.
- Chief's mate **Sahra** brought Mira to female healer **Orenna**. Orenna finds Mira's jungle injuries superficial. Sahra washes Mira and her hair and mentions that her daughter loved having her hair washed when younger.
- Mira reports two moons since her last menstrual bleeding. Orenna performs a clinical reproductive examination with Mira's consent and becomes personally confident Mira is pregnant, while acknowledging she has never examined a human and cannot know viability, compatibility, or exact gestation.
- Orenna provides Mira a clean Kael'tha child's dress because adult clothing is too large.
- Sahra questions Mira about Korren. Mira says the mate-beads mean "that I'm mated," that she and Korren mated three days after he found her, that Thaal had not allowed it, and that although Mira chose to leave with Korren for the cave she did not know what the cave would physically do to her.
- Sahra takes temporary possession of Mira's mate-bead cord and says Korren must earn the right to give them to Mira again. This is Sahra's protective judgment, not objective proof against Mira's bond/present choice. Mira has not yet demanded the beads back.
- Sahra learns there are exactly six human women total in Vahn-rii lands. Mira tells her **three of the other five are mated/bonded**: two chose to mate/bond, while the third bond happened accidentally. This corresponds to Suvi choosing Tarrik, Liesel choosing Asho, and Mari accidentally bonding Drenn. Mira does not in this exchange detail Mari's continuing fear/conflict unless asked.
- Sahra asks whether the other humans are all as small as Mira. Mira answers: **"I'm the smallest. The biggest is still smaller than you."** Sahra therefore understands that Mira is unusually small even among humans, but that all six human women are smaller than Kael'tha women like Sahra.
- Sahra returns to the timing and asks: **"And they allowed Korren to take you to the caves after three days?"** This refers to the Vahn-rii community/leadership. The accurate answer is no: Thaal explicitly prohibited the cave after Senna's concern about Mira's nape implant; Korren and Mira left secretly at night after Mira chose to go with him. Sahra does not yet know these details beyond Mira's earlier statement that the chief did not allow it.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking.

## Continuity
- Mira/Korren remain bonded mates sharing the lodge and an established daily routine despite Sahra holding the physical mate-beads.
- Mira is 26, fully adult, 5'2", Puerto Rican, and notably small beside Kael'tha adults.
- Mira is tired, scratched, emotionally raw, and physically depleted, though washed, hydrated, ambulatory, and under care.
- Mira wears a clean Kael'tha child's dress supplied by Orenna. Her own dirty light dress remains her property. Her river-stone mate-bead cord is intact and physically held by Sahra.
- **Pregnancy remains objectively unconfirmed**, but two healers have independently found it plausible and Orenna is personally confident after a more thorough exam. No supernatural or bond-based signal exists. Human/Kael'tha compatibility and pregnancy safety remain unknown.
- No telepathy exists. The bond is physical pull plus audible harmonic and does not allow Korren to track Mira remotely.
