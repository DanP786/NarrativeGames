# Current scene — two-month time skip (~Day 70–75)

Two months pass after the Day 10 women's meeting and Mira's return home with Korren. The Vahn-rii village has had time to absorb the six sky-women into daily life unevenly rather than all at once.

## Established time-skip canon
- Mira and Korren remain bonded mates, sharing their lodge. Mira's injured foot has had ordinary healing time.
- **Suvi + Tarrik:** Suvi voluntarily chooses the deep-place rite and bonds with Tarrik. Her choice is driven strongly by wanting to become physically intimate with him; she already liked him before the cave and chooses the Vahn-rii path knowingly rather than being taken there ignorant. The bond follows the choice rather than creating the initial attraction.
- **Liesel + Asho:** Liesel voluntarily chooses the deep-place rite and bonds with Asho. Her reason is different from Suvi's: over time Asho's restraint and keeping make her feel safe with him, and she chooses from that accumulated trust. The bond follows an established sense of safety.
- **Ren + Vesh:** Ren remains unbonded. Over the two months she becomes softer in manner and less continuously combative/armored around the village and Vesh. This is character development, not proof of consent to bond, sex, or permanent claiming.
- **Mari + Drenn:** Mari does not undergo a convenient reconciliation or personality turn. She remains substantially as she was: bonded, frightened/conflicted, and disturbed by bodily responses she did not choose. Drenn/Mari remain the accidental-bond case; Drenn did not intentionally engineer her cave exposure.
- **Ada:** no new change supplied in the skip; remains unbonded and unclaimed under the standing conditions/refusal protections unless changed later in play.
