# Current scene — Korren and Mira's lodge, ~Day 70–75, late afternoon

Two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Mira returned from fetching water with Liesel. Their first substantive conversation in roughly two months reopened Mira's hurt over being socially isolated after bonding with Korren. Liesel apologized for her own absence and asked to begin coming to Mira now; Mira accepted with a strained smile despite bitterness that Liesel reaches out only after bonding herself.
- Korren spent part of the afternoon counseling Drenn about Mari. He told Drenn he cannot make Mari want their accidental bond and that if every no costs her something, it is not a word Drenn has allowed her to keep. He advised Drenn that sometimes he must not go to Mari merely because he feels her distress, and must learn which fear asks for his hand and which worsens when he gives it.
- Mira and Korren shared a playful domestic exchange while she cooked. Mira then reframed Drenn's problem: rather than focusing constantly on the bond, Drenn could be ordinarily helpful and supportive, like Korren taking Mira's heavy bucket because he knew where it belonged. Korren accepted the distinction and said Drenn should learn what Mari needs when nothing is wrong.
- Mira smiles and introduces a human relationship concept: "Where I'm from we have five love languages, ones we tend to perform and ones we like to receive."
- Korren initially takes "languages" literally, then understands Mira means different ways people show and recognize care. The idea catches his attention because it offers a practical way to think about ordinary keeping/care without making every gesture about the sacred bond.
- Korren asks Mira to name the five and, characteristically, immediately wants to know which one she uses and which one she wants from him. Food continues cooking while the conversation becomes an intimate exchange of human and Vahn-rii relationship ideas.
