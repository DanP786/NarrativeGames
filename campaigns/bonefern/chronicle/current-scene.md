# Current scene — leaving foreign Kael'tha chief's lodge for healer, ~Day 71–76, late afternoon

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira, gave her access to stream water, and brought her to their village because they refuse to cross the Vahn-rii boundary and want their leadership to arrange her return. Both know Korren's name and react negatively/warily.
- En route, Mira learned that Korren has a reputation here for repeated border crossings and once injured three of this people's men while forcing his way out rather than submitting to detention. She also learned that years ago Korren sought the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate; the chief refused him. Whether the daughter wanted Korren and the exact nature of Korren's response remain unestablished.
- Mira clarified to the chief that Korren did not bring her here; she became lost and crossed the boundary unknowingly. The chief intends to keep Mira in the village overnight rather than send her through jungle after dark and to post watchers toward Vahn-rii approaches because Korren may follow her trail.
- The chief's mate arrived, initially mistook 5'2" Mira for a child, then accepted that Mira is a 26-year-old adult human. She states that she knew Korren when he wanted her daughter, but that history has not yet been explored further.
- Seeing Mira exhausted, scratched, dirty, and physically small by Kael'tha standards, the chief's mate decides that if Mira is staying, she will personally take her to the village healer. She says, "I will not return one so fragile in this state," steps beside Mira, and takes Mira's hand. The gesture is protective/practical rather than coercive or romantic.
- The chief accepts his mate taking responsibility for Mira's immediate care while he handles the border/Korren problem. Mira is now being led toward the foreign healer, creating the possibility that a healer unfamiliar with humans may notice or ask about the same uncertain bodily signs Senna examined earlier; pregnancy remains unconfirmed and must not become certain merely from another healer's examination.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking.

Senna earlier found absent menstruation after previously regular cycles plus subtle low abdominal fullness/warmth suggestive but not diagnostic; no fetus was palpated and human/Vahn-rii compatibility is unknown.
