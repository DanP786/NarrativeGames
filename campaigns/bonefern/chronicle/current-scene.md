# Current scene — Sahra's fire, foreign village, ~Day 71–76, early evening

Roughly two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Earlier today Senna visited because Korren had privately summoned her to see whether Mira might be pregnant. Senna found pregnancy plausible but unconfirmed. Mira has told none of the other human women.
- At the women's meeting, Ada revealed that the other five human women have been gathering every day throughout the roughly two months in which nobody invited Mira or substantively spoke with her. Overwhelmed, Mira fled into the jungle, became disoriented, and wandered for roughly three hours into another Kael'tha people's territory.
- Two foreign Kael'tha males found Mira and brought her to their village. En route Mira learned Korren's reputation here includes repeated border crossings, injuring three men while forcing his way out of detention, and years ago seeking the chief's adult daughter as a mate/prospective mate before the chief refused him.
- Chief's mate **Sahra** brought Mira to female healer **Orenna**. Orenna found Mira's jungle injuries superficial. Sahra washed Mira and her hair and mentioned that her daughter loved having her hair washed when younger.
- Mira reported two moons since her last menstrual bleeding. Orenna performed a clinical reproductive examination with Mira's consent and became personally confident Mira is pregnant, while acknowledging she has never examined a human and cannot know viability, compatibility, or exact gestation.
- Orenna provided Mira a clean Kael'tha child's dress because adult clothing is too large.
- Sahra questioned Mira about Korren. Mira said the mate-beads mean "that I'm mated," that she and Korren mated three days after he found her, that Thaal had not allowed it, and that although Mira chose to leave with Korren for the cave she did not know what the cave would physically do to her. Mira clarified Senna also opposed the cave because of the unknown made thing/implant in Mira's neck, and that Mira herself told Korren they were going and left secretly with him.
- Sahra learned there are exactly six human women total. Three others are bonded/mated: Suvi and Liesel chose their bonds; Mari's happened accidentally. Mira is the smallest human woman, but even the largest is smaller than Sahra.
- Sahra judged Mira's broader situation a failure of protection and told her: **"Vahn-rii did not protect you. Your people did not protect you. Now you carry a child."** This directly touched Mira's long-standing pain: she has wanted others to acknowledge both the unprotected/unchosen beginning and the reality of her present attachment and choices.
- Sahra has Mira's intact river-stone mate-bead cord and has said Korren must earn the right to give it to Mira again. Mira has not consented to Sahra keeping it.
- Sahra led Mira to the chief's household fire, fed her, and openly declared: **"Mira is staying here. As part of our tribe. If Korren wants her he must prove himself worthy to join us. She is with child. I will not hand her over."** Sahra's statement reflects her protective position; Mira has not accepted permanent residence or daughter-status.
- The chief insists Mira's own wishes matter and does not treat Sahra's declaration as Mira's consent. He nevertheless says Korren will not simply walk into the village and take Mira away because he believes she is his. He raises the unresolved history of Korren and their daughter.
- **The old daughter incident is now established:** years ago, the chief and Sahra refused Korren as a prospective mate for their adult daughter. Their daughter nevertheless **left with Korren**. They departed in haste. During that hurried departure/journey, **the daughter suffered an accident that led to her death**. The death was accidental; do not retcon it into Korren deliberately harming or killing her. Exact mechanics of the accident, immediate aftermath, whether they had reached a cave/bonded, and Korren's precise responsibility or attempts to save her remain unestablished unless subsequently specified.
- This history explains Sahra's especially intense reaction to Mira: she is seeing another woman who chose to leave with Korren against authority, again in haste/defiance, and now believes Mira is carrying his child. Sahra's protectiveness is therefore shaped by grief and fear of repetition, not merely hostility to Korren.
- Sahra's statement that Mira **is with child** reflects Orenna's strong medical judgment, not omniscient certainty. Pregnancy remains objectively unconfirmed and unprecedented across species.
- Korren cannot locate Mira through the bond; there is no telepathy or supernatural tracking. He may plausibly follow physical trail/sign but has not yet been established at the boundary.

## Continuity
- Mira/Korren remain bonded mates sharing an established life despite Sahra's protective claim and possession of the physical mate-beads.
- Mira is 26, fully adult, 5'2", Puerto Rican, and notably small beside Kael'tha adults.
- Mira is emotionally raw after discovering the other five human women excluded her from daily meetings for two months. Sahra's acknowledgment and offer of daughter-like belonging therefore carry unusual emotional weight.
- Mira wears a clean Kael'tha child's dress supplied by Orenna. Her own dirty light dress remains her property. Her river-stone mate-bead cord is intact and physically held by Sahra.
- **Pregnancy remains objectively unconfirmed**, but two healers have independently found it plausible and Orenna is personally confident after a more thorough exam. No supernatural or bond-based signal exists. Human/Kael'tha compatibility and pregnancy safety remain unknown.
- No telepathy exists. The bond is physical pull plus audible harmonic and does not allow Korren to track Mira remotely.
