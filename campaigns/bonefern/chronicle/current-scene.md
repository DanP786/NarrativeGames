# Current scene — Korren and Mira's lodge, ~Day 70–75, late afternoon

Two months have passed since Day 10. Suvi has voluntarily bonded with Tarrik; Liesel has voluntarily bonded with Asho; Ren remains unbonded with Vesh but has softened; Ada remains unbonded/unclaimed; Mari remains bonded with Drenn and substantially frightened/conflicted.

## Immediate scene
- Mira returned from fetching water with Liesel. Their first substantive conversation in roughly two months reopened Mira's hurt over being socially isolated after bonding with Korren. Liesel apologized for her own absence and asked to begin coming to Mira now; Mira accepted with a strained smile despite bitterness that Liesel reaches out only after bonding herself.
- Korren spent part of the afternoon counseling Drenn about Mari. He told Drenn he cannot make Mari want their accidental bond and that if every no costs her something, it is not a word Drenn has allowed her to keep. He advised Drenn that sometimes he must not go to Mari merely because he feels her distress, and must learn which fear asks for his hand and which worsens when he gives it.
- Mira occupied herself preparing food. Korren came inside and the two shared a playful domestic exchange after he caught her bent over their stores. Mira told him she is a technician rather than a warrior; Korren teased that she strikes like a leaf while acknowledging her practical competence.
- Mira laughs in mock offense, turns back to the cooking, and asks, "How was your talk with Drenn?"
- Korren's amusement quiets. He tells Mira that Drenn listened, though he did not like all he heard. Korren says Drenn wants a way to keep Mari close without making her fear worse, but there is no simple rule for that: he will have to listen to her even when what she says is not what he wants.
- Korren does not present Mari's fear as a problem Mira must solve and does not ask Mira to intervene. The conversation shifts from teasing into the more difficult subject of how a bond can exist where one person does not welcome it.

- Mira is wearing her deep river-green Vahn-rii mated-wife outfit and mate-bead cord.
- Mira is a trained filtration technician; practical systems/water work are central parts of her competence and identity.
