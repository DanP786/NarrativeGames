- Chief's mate **Sahra** and healer **Orenna** took Mira into their care. Orenna correctly identified an early pregnancy despite no human/Kael'tha precedent. Several days later Mira suffered a miscarriage/early pregnancy loss. Cause remains unknown. She is now physically recovering; bleeding and pain have lessened and Orenna continues monitoring her.

Sahra judged Mira's broader situation a failure of protection and offered to treat Mira as a daughter/member of this tribe. Mira has not formally accepted permanent residence or daughter-status.

They departed in haste; she suffered an accident during that hurried departure/journey and died. Korren did not deliberately harm or kill her. Exact mechanics, aftermath, bond status, and Korren's precise responsibility/rescue attempts remain unestablished.

He believes Korren's central fault is that he hears boundaries/orders but decides whether another person's reason outweighs his own. He is willing to teach Korren restraint, answerability, communal boundaries, and accepting judgment/no when he disagrees.
- Boundary-watchers have repeatedly reported **movement/sign on the far side of the border**, but nobody has crossed into this tribe's territory. It is still not directly confirmed as Korren. The restraint is notable given his history, but identity/motive remain unconfirmed.
- Sahra has shown Mira around the foreign village. It is materially larger, more permanent, more prosperous-looking, and substantially more decorative than Vahn-rii, with generations-deep infrastructure, fixed platforms/walkways, rainwater systems, storage, specialized communal/craft areas, carved/patterned structures, dyed fibers, beadwork, polished stone, and deliberately encouraged flowering/luminous growth.
- Mira told Sahra: **"Thank you. I think I had a warmer welcome here than at Vahn-rii."** Sahra did not demand Mira renounce Vahn-rii or Korren. Mira privately recognizes she could live here, without yet deciding that she will.
- After eating, Mira insists on doing a little ordinary work and **washes the dishes**, then cleans/wipes a household table. While she is at the washing area, a local Kael'tha woman arrives carrying a folded pile of newly made/altered clothing and sets it on the table Mira just cleaned. **The clothing is for Mira.**
- Sahra comes over to inspect the pile. Unlike the borrowed child's dress Mira has been wearing, these are **adult women's outfits deliberately sized and proportioned for Mira's human body**: short enough for her height and cut with room for her bust/hips rather than merely giving her children's garments. They use this tribe's more decorative textile/craft tradition—dyed fibers, careful edging, patterned weaving/trim, small decorative details—while remaining practical for the wet-hot climate. Exact individual colors/cuts can be established in narration.
- The clothes are a material sign that members of the village have begun making room for Mira as an adult woman rather than treating her presence as a one-night emergency. They do not by themselves mean Mira has accepted tribal membership, and no one should treat the gift as creating an obligation to stay.
- Mira/Korren's bond remains intact. Korren cannot locate Mira through it; no telepathy exists. No foreign/Vahn-rii confrontation has yet occurred at the border.
