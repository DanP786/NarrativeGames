# World description

Rome, 18 BC, under Augustus. The civil wars are within living memory. Augustus' regime presents restored order while concentrating extraordinary authority around the princeps, his family, magistracies, patronage networks and the Senate. Elite life is governed as much by family standing, obligation, reputation and access as by written law.

The campaign treats Roman social structures materially: citizenship and status matter; slavery is ubiquitous and coercive; households are economic and political institutions; marriage and dowry bind families; religion is embedded in public and domestic life. Historical details should be handled carefully, with uncertainty acknowledged where Roman practice varied by date, status, marriage form or surviving evidence.

The initial camera remains close to the Valerian household in Rome and expands only as Aemilia's life requires it.
