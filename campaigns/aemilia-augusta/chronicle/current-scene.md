# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Aemilia and Lucius are speaking with unusual directness about love, their future marriage, and Lucius' continuing sexual access to Livia, an enslaved woman of the household.
- After Lucius asks Aemilia to say plainly if she does not want him to continue seeking Livia once their marriage changes, Aemilia stands and paces with her wine, lifting her skirt for ease of movement more casually than she would in public. She says, 'I do not dare to tell my husband how to live.'
- Lucius recognizes the distinction between Aemilia issuing him an order and Aemilia telling him what she wants. He does not surrender his formal authority, but insists that the private counsel he trained her to give him is meaningless if she must conceal her own wishes whenever they concern him personally.
- The exchange remains nonsexual while Aemilia is seventeen. Lucius leaves the decision in his own hands as Roman husband and household head, but asks Aemilia for the truth of what she wants rather than obedience disguised as indifference.
