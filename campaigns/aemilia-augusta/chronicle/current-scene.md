# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening.
- Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from her eighteenth birthday. Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Their marriage remains privately unconsummated. Publicly Lucius behaves as an established husband and protects the secret without hesitation.
- It is publicly known that Lucius has fathered children by adult enslaved women in his household; these do not satisfy the dynastic expectation for legitimate heirs from Aemilia.
- At an elite gathering, Gaius Fabius Crispus probes Aemilia about the marriage and her unusual access to Lucius' political thinking. Aemilia deflects him without giving a direct lie or admission, and Lucius privately tells her he is proud of the maneuver.
- Aemilia then tells Lucius that society expects him to end their long-childless marriage soon, and that even her own family expects divorce.
- Lucius treats the expectation as politically real rather than dismissing it. He tells Aemilia that her family has already sounded him out indirectly about his intentions and that he has given them no encouragement. He makes clear that he has no present intention of divorcing her merely because outsiders think the marriage has failed to produce heirs quickly enough.
- The approaching end of Aemilia's childhood now sits explicitly between them: the reason Lucius has privately accepted childlessness is nearing its end, while neither has yet spoken plainly tonight about what adulthood will change between them.
