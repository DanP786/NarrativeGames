- Aemilia then explains why her father did not ultimately choose Scipio. The proposed Scipio match offered an excellent old name and senatorial connection, but Marcus Aemilius Lepidus judged the Valerian match more advantageous for Aemilia's long-term security: Lucius's household was wealthier and more politically stable, Lucius himself was a stronger young dynastic prospect, and the Valerians were prepared to accept Aemilia's substantial dowry on terms her father preferred. Scipio was therefore passed over before any completed betrothal. Aemilia had no meaningful deciding voice in this choice at twelve; it was a calculation made by her father among elite family interests.
