# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is Aemilia's eighteenth birthday, late evening, at the Valerian domus in Rome, after the final guest has departed from her successful birthday gathering.
- Aemilia and Lucius are alone at their prepared household bath. She explicitly invited him to bathe with his wife; the attendants have been dismissed.
- After asking Lucius what he thinks of her adult body and hearing him tell her she is beautiful, Aemilia moves close and straddles him in the bath. She tells him, 'I have dreamed of this moment.'
- Lucius understands this as an intimate and emotionally significant declaration after nearly six years of deliberately maintained boundaries. He responds with tenderness and seriousness rather than treating her anticipation as an obligation or rushing beyond what she has chosen. The scene remains sensual but non-graphic.
- Lucius' promises are now in effect: from this birthday onward he will not seek sexual access to Livia or any other women, and he and Aemilia intend to try for legitimate children together.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
