# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. Some time has passed since Aemilia first missed an expected menstrual cycle and told Lucius she believed she might be pregnant; the exact interval and pregnancy status remain to be established through the present examination.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage is established. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children.
- The household's mock gladiatorial games have become a recurring recreational tradition: children may participate or preside, household staff may watch and cheer when duties permit, and strict rules prevent deliberate injury or imitation of lethal outcomes. Lucius promised Aemilia as much of the noise, ceremony and excitement of a Roman spectacle as can be safely recreated without its harsh realities.
- On a deliberately quiet day, Demetrios arrives privately at the Valerian domus accompanied by a trusted experienced midwife whom he is willing to recommend. The visit is kept discreet.
- Lucius is present with Aemilia for the consultation. Pia is the only servant Aemilia has trusted with knowledge of her suspected pregnancy and remains with her. No wider announcement has been made to the household, the children or either extended family.
- Demetrios makes clear that an early pregnancy cannot be established with modern certainty in their period from a single missed bleeding; the midwife will rely on the history of continued missed courses and other bodily changes, along with an appropriate non-graphic examination and experienced judgment. The consultation is about to begin.
- The household's practical health routines based on Demetrios' earlier advice remain in force.
