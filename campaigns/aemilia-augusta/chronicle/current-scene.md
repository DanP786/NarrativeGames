# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is Aemilia's eighteenth birthday, late evening, at the Valerian domus in Rome, after the final guest has departed from her successful birthday gathering.
- Aemilia and Lucius are alone at their prepared household bath after she explicitly invited him to bathe with his wife and Lucius dismissed the attendants.
- Aemilia stands before Lucius unclothed while her elaborate birthday hairstyle and decorations remain intact and asks, 'What do you think?' She is explicitly asking what he thinks of her adult body.
- Lucius understands the question and answers as her husband rather than retreating into another joke: he finds her beautiful. His response remains affectionate and restrained, recognizing that this is the first time the long-protected boundary between them has changed without presuming that her display commits her to any further act.
- Lucius' promises are now in effect: from this birthday onward he will not seek sexual access to Livia or any other women, and he and Aemilia intend to try for legitimate children together.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
