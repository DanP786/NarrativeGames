# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening.
- Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from her eighteenth birthday. Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Their marriage remains privately unconsummated. Publicly Lucius behaves as an established husband and protects the secret without hesitation.
- At an elite private gathering in Rome, an older matron probes Aemilia about the marriage's lack of children. Aemilia answers lightly that Venus will bless them with a child. Lucius deflects further scrutiny with jokes about hoping for a daughter and valuing his wife's opinions.
- Gaius Fabius Crispus later approaches Aemilia near the household shrine while Lucius is occupied. He says rumours are forming around the long-childless marriage and tests whether it conceals a private arrangement.
- Asked whether Lucius has been kind, Aemilia smiles and answers, 'Unimaginably kind.' Crispus presses the political/familial point: the childless household gives rivals material for gossip and lacks advantages attached to parenthood. He notes that Aemilia has never been pregnant.
- When Crispus suggests Lucius' kindness may itself be the problem, Aemilia turns the implication back on him: 'A man must be unkind to have children?' Crispus is forced to retreat from the insinuation and clarify that he means excessive consideration may conflict with dynastic duty, not that cruelty is literally required.
- Lucius arrives during the exchange and hears enough to understand Crispus has been probing the private marriage. Rather than immediately silencing Aemilia, he gives her room to finish handling Crispus herself while making his presence unmistakable.
