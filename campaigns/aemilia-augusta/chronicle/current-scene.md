# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is approximately three months pregnant. The pregnancy is progressing without an established complication.
- Aemilia and Lucius are at the Roman residence of Lucius's elder brother Marcus Valerius Messalla Minor and his wife Servilia for a birthday gathering for one of their sons. Their sons are Marcus, about ten, and Lucius, about five; which boy is celebrating remains unestablished.
- A private message has been sent to Aemilia's parents, Marcus Aemilius Lepidus and Cornelia, telling them directly of the pregnancy so they will not learn through Valerian gossip.
- Servilia noticed Aemilia's pregnancy on arrival and received direct confirmation. Aemilia then made the pregnancy unmistakable to watching relatives and redirected attention toward the birthday child.
- Lucius's father, Marcus Valerius Messalla, has learned directly that Aemilia is about three months pregnant. The timing convinces him that Lucius truly did deliberately postpone conception until Aemilia was an adult; Marcus grudgingly acknowledges that Lucius's judgment may have been right.
- Marcus immediately responds to the pregnancy in his characteristic dynastic and authoritative manner by stating, 'I will send for the finest physicians.' He treats securing elite medical attention as a family resource he can command for Aemilia and the hoped-for legitimate Valerian child. He does not yet know that Lucius and Aemilia already trust Demetrios and an experienced midwife, or the extent of Demetrios's influence on their household health practices; their response to Marcus's offer remains to be played.
- Aemilia and Lucius remain physically close in the family gathering. Lucius remains faithful to his promise. The pregnancy is their hoped-for legitimate child, though its outcome cannot be guaranteed.
- Aemilia's gentler hairstyle preference remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck, with more elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
