# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, night. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Pia's first genuine day free ordinary duties remained intact through the day; a formal standing weekly instruction remains pending. Aemilia's unfamiliar hairstyle has been released after causing scalp discomfort.
- Marcus Aemilius Lepidus visited earlier today, withdrew prior pressure to end Aemilia's marriage, accepted her happiness and judgment, and partially reconciled with her. Cornelia wants to attend Aemilia's confinement/birth; Aemilia wants her there.
- Aemilia ordered a one-month urban-domus discipline trial after questioning Philargyrus: no punitive beating/striking; immediate protective force permitted; immediate modest rewards for competent work; simple tracking of serious offenses, responses and rewards. Lucius explicitly approved the month conditionally.

- During the afternoon/evening, Eros privately told Lucius the substance of her conversation with Aemilia. Lucius now knows about Eros's inability to meaningfully refuse his former sexual access, her conditional wish for freedom, the Menophilus/Chryseros/Thais allegations, Aemilia's former jealousy, and Aemilia's statement that the only person she has to fear is her husband. Eros also conveyed her judgment that Aemilia is kind and sincere but sheltered and unlikely to prevail if Lucius says no. Lucius did not retaliate against Eros for speaking.
- Publius workshop crisis remains active. Earlier figures: Aufidius 12,800; other pressing outside debts 6,200 and 3,600; Valerian due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600; receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. The exact outcome of today's creditor deadline remains unestablished on-screen and must not be assumed.
- At bedtime Lucius enters their bedchamber and asks Aemilia, **“You are afraid of me?”** He reveals Eros came to him. Aemilia scowls, says **“Of course she did,”** stands with one hand on her pregnant abdomen and keeps several paces between them.
- Lucius explains Eros came because he holds final household authority and she does not trust Aemilia's goodwill alone to survive disagreement with him. Aemilia objects that nothing is presently being decided. Lucius agrees no immediate ruling is being made but explains that Eros has little control over when others decide her life and therefore wanted him informed beforehand.
- Aemilia feels personally betrayed and humiliated by Eros's disclosure. She had approached Eros hoping to understand enslaved lives, improve what she could, and lay down her longstanding jealousy toward the woman who had shared Lucius's bed and borne his children before Aemilia. She feels her private vulnerability was carried directly to Lucius and that she was made a fool of. These are Aemilia's internal feelings and Lucius does not automatically know them.
- Aemilia explains her statement about fearing Lucius: **“I… I meant it in the context that you control part of my life, for her there are more.”** Lucius accepts the distinction between his real social/marital authority over parts of Aemilia's life and Eros's much broader vulnerability as an enslaved woman, though he remains troubled by the word fear and by Aemilia keeping her distance.
- Aemilia exhales, searches the room as though for an answer, and asks again, **“Why is she coming to you?”** This time the question carries personal bewilderment and hurt rather than merely asking what decision Eros wanted.
- Lucius answers more directly: Eros came to him because she does not yet trust Aemilia enough to place her safety, her children's future, or accusations against supervisors solely in Aemilia's hands. Eros has known Aemilia chiefly as the young mistress of the house and Lucius's wife, not as a proven protector with independent final authority. Lucius says Eros was protecting herself by ensuring the person who can ultimately permit, reverse, or punish household changes heard her account directly. He does not characterize this as Eros tricking Aemilia, nor does he demand that Aemilia forgive the breach of confidence. He recognizes that Eros repeated things Aemilia reasonably experienced as private.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia stands several paces from Lucius, hand near/on her abdomen, visibly searching for sense in Eros's choice. Lucius has told her plainly that Eros went to him because she does not yet trust Aemilia's protection or authority enough to rely upon it alone. Aemilia's feeling of betrayal remains unresolved and has not yet been fully stated aloud.
