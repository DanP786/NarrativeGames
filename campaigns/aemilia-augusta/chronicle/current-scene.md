# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, morning. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Publius workshop crisis active. Figures: Aufidius 12,800; other pressing outside debts 6,200 and 3,600; Valerian due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600. Receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. Forced liquidation likely destroys value/may not cover Valerian claim. Lucius leans controlled rescue but has not executed it; Aufidius standstill on two seized enslaved craftsmen expires today.
- Pia's first genuine weekly day free ordinary labor is today. Another enslaved household woman dressed Aemilia/did hair; usual clothing, visibly different hair. Pia genuinely off ordinary duties. Steward meeting remains due later.
- Aemilia's father Marcus Aemilius Lepidus, 49, senator/former praetor, arrived uninvited/without prior message. Previously pressured Aemilia to end marriage and privately explored dowry/remarriage contingencies during long childless period.
- Marcus explicitly reverses prior course: pregnancy removes central childlessness premise; marriage should continue; he will not pursue/encourage dissolution or another match on old grounds. He admits discounting Aemilia's judgment and says hers proved better. Aemilia says she is happy in Valerian household; Marcus accepts this and says he should have weighted it more.
- Lucius arrives and corrects Marcus's implication that Aemilia's life/place is Marcus's to grant or withdraw. He asserts Aemilia is his wife, marriage continues because he intends it and she chooses it, and renewed natal-family attempts to remove/reassign her would be resisted. Aemilia deliberately remains silent so as not to undermine Lucius's public assertion of marital/household authority; her own wish has already been clearly stated.
- Marcus says he remains her father and had duties/interests in her future, but concedes he pressed beyond justified facts. He is not here to reclaim her, demand dowry, arrange another husband, or reopen dissolution. Lucius answers 'Good.' Present dispute over continuation is closed, though neither man concedes every theoretical authority point.
- Aemilia then asks Marcus: 'Father, was there anything else you wanted to discuss?'
- Marcus says yes: the pregnancy itself and arrangements for Aemilia's confinement/childbirth. This is not a hidden attempt to remove her from Lucius. Cornelia wants to be involved and Marcus wants clarity about who is attending Aemilia and what preparations the Valerian household has made.
- Marcus frames childbirth as a serious physical risk rather than a ceremonial inevitability. He asks Lucius/Aemilia whether a trusted midwife has been retained and who the physician is. This creates an opening for the already-established unnamed trusted midwife and Demetrios, but Marcus does not yet know their details unless told.
- Marcus also states Cornelia will expect access to her daughter as the pregnancy advances and particularly around confinement. He does not demand Aemilia return to the Aemilian house; exact location/attendance arrangements remain for discussion.
- Lucius does not object to concern for Aemilia's safety but remains alert to any attempt to turn maternal attendance into natal-family control. He lets Aemilia answer first because the questions concern her attendants and pregnancy arrangements and because she has direct knowledge of them.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia, Marcus and Lucius are together. Marcus has raised the second purpose of his visit: childbirth preparations, identity of midwife/physician, and Cornelia's expected involvement later in pregnancy/confinement. No demand to move Aemilia has been made. Lucius is present and allows Aemilia first answer. Publius deadline active; steward meeting later; Pia off duty.
