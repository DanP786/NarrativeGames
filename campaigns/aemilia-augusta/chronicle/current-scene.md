# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Lucius has promised that once Aemilia is an adult they will try to have children together, while refusing to promise outcomes beyond human control.
- His teasing about their future children's opinions finally disarms Aemilia. She scoffs and lightly slaps his arm. Lucius immediately catches her wrist in a familiar, playful response and teases, 'You dare strike the head of this household?'
- The gesture is nonsexual and not punitive: Lucius is deliberately playing with the real hierarchy between them because years of private familiarity allow the joke. Aemilia remains seventeen; no erotic conduct occurs.
