# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is seventeen; exactly 34 days remain until her eighteenth birthday. Later the same day at the villa of Lucius' father in Rome.
- Lucius and Aemilia stand together before Lucius' father after answering his joint summons. The elder Valerian argues that nearly six years without legitimate issue, contrasted with Lucius' children by enslaved women, indicates Aemilia is barren. Lucius rejects that conclusion without revealing that the marriage remains unconsummated.
- Lucius' father has called Aemilia physically defective and useless if she cannot provide an heir, and has urged Lucius to divorce and remarry. Lucius firmly refuses divorce and rejects the unproven premise of infertility while defending his authority over his own marriage.
- When asked why Lucius keeps her, Aemilia quietly says, 'I cannot speak my husband's mind,' refusing to expose the private marriage arrangement or presume to answer for him.
- The elder Valerian now escalates to an ultimatum: 'You will tell me now or I will cast you out.' In context, the threat is treated as a serious threat of familial and dynastic exclusion—withdrawal of paternal support, inheritance expectations, connections and standing within the elder Valerian's branch—not as magical or automatic erasure of Lucius' independently held adult household, property, marriage or legal identity.
- Lucius recognizes that silence now carries material consequences. He does not immediately surrender the full private truth, but the confrontation has become climactic: he must choose whether to reveal some part of his deliberate responsibility for the childlessness, accept the threatened rupture, or find another explanation strong enough to satisfy his father without sacrificing Aemilia's privacy.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
