# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, night. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Pia's first genuine day free ordinary duties remained intact through the day; a formal standing weekly instruction remains pending. Aemilia's unfamiliar hairstyle has been released after causing scalp discomfort.
- Marcus Aemilius Lepidus visited earlier today, withdrew prior pressure to end Aemilia's marriage, accepted her happiness and judgment, and partially reconciled with her. Cornelia wants to attend Aemilia's confinement/birth; Aemilia wants her there.
- Aemilia ordered a one-month urban-domus discipline trial after questioning Philargyrus: no punitive beating/striking; immediate protective force permitted; immediate modest rewards for competent work; simple tracking of serious offenses, responses and rewards. Lucius explicitly approved the month conditionally.
- Aemilia's private conversation with Eros established that Lucius's past sexual access was not something Eros always wanted and refusal was not meaningfully available; Eros prefers remaining with her children to sale/transfer but would choose freedom if she could remain near/protect them. Aemilia promised no retaliation and said she and Lucius do not wish to separate Eros from Fortunata/Felix; no manumission promise was made.
- Eros identified Menophilus as an abusive delegated supervisor, Chryseros as a plausible replacement, and Thais as a lesser concern for favoritism/punitive manipulation. These allegations remain unverified.
- During the afternoon/evening, Eros privately told Lucius the substance of her conversation with Aemilia. Lucius now knows about Eros's inability to meaningfully refuse his former sexual access, her conditional wish for freedom, the Menophilus/Chryseros/Thais allegations, Aemilia's former jealousy, and Aemilia's statement that the only person she has to fear is her husband. Eros also conveyed her judgment that Aemilia is kind and sincere but sheltered and unlikely to prevail if Lucius says no. Lucius did not retaliate against Eros for speaking.
- Publius workshop crisis remains active. Earlier figures: Aufidius 12,800; other pressing outside debts 6,200 and 3,600; Valerian due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600; receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. The exact outcome of today's creditor deadline remains unestablished on-screen and must not be assumed.
- At bedtime Lucius entered their bedchamber and asked Aemilia, **“You are afraid of me?”** He revealed Eros came to him. Aemilia learned Eros had disclosed substantially the whole private conversation.
- Lucius explained Eros came because he holds final household authority and she does not trust Aemilia's goodwill alone to survive disagreement with him. Aemilia feels personally betrayed and humiliated because she had approached Eros hoping to understand enslaved lives, improve what she could, and lay down longstanding jealousy toward the woman who shared Lucius's bed and bore his children before Aemilia.
- Aemilia explained her statement about fearing Lucius as referring to his control over part of her life, contrasted with the many people who hold power over Eros. Lucius accepted the distinction.
- Lucius told Aemilia that Eros does not yet trust her enough to place her safety, children's future, or accusations against supervisors solely in Aemilia's hands. He acknowledged Eros repeated things Aemilia reasonably experienced as private but explained Eros was protecting herself rather than Aemilia's dignity.
- Aemilia said, **“What wounds me is that you defend her,”** and tried to leave the bedchamber. Lucius caught her by the arm before she reached the door. His restraint was deliberate and physical but not painful or violent; he stopped her departure and insisted they not end the exchange immediately after she had said she feared him.
- Aemilia lowers her head, turns back to face Lucius, and clarifies: **“I do not fear you. I fear what disappointing you will bring. I fear if I displease you I will be confined from you again.”** Her fear is therefore not principally that Lucius will beat or physically injure her, but that his authority and affection can be withdrawn: that displeasing him could mean renewed emotional/domestic exclusion resembling the years when their childhood marriage imposed distance and boundaries between them.
- Lucius immediately loosens and releases her arm after hearing this. He recognizes that physically stopping her while demanding an explanation undercut his own insistence that her willingness and refusal matter. He does not promise that Aemilia will never face disagreement, limits, or consequences, but distinguishes those from deliberately withholding his company or marital affection as punishment. He tells her he will not use exclusion from him as a disciplinary instrument to train her obedience. The old childhood boundary was not punishment for displeasing him, though Aemilia's experience of years of denied adult marital closeness has plainly left a fear he had not understood.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Near the bedchamber door, Lucius has released Aemilia's arm. Aemilia has finally named the core fear: not physical harm, but losing Lucius's closeness and being shut away from him if she disappoints or displeases him. Lucius is taking that fear seriously without pretending he lacks authority or that their marriage will never contain consequences or conflict.
