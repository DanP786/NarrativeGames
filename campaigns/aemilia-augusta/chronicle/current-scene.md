Receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. Forced liquidation likely destroys value/may not cover Valerian claim. - Pia's first genuine weekly day free ordinary labor is today. Another enslaved household woman dressed Aemilia/did hair; usual clothing, visibly different hair. Pia genuinely off ordinary duties.
- Marcus Aemilius Lepidus's unannounced visit has concluded. He withdrew prior pressure to end Aemilia's marriage, accepted her happiness and judgment, and agreed the marriage should continue. Aemilia and Marcus partially reconciled. Cornelia wants to be present during Aemilia's confinement/birth; Aemilia wants her there. Marcus has been told about the trusted unnamed midwife and Demetrios.
- After Marcus leaves, Aemilia and Lucius share an affectionate, impulsive adult marital interlude, non-explicitly narrated. Lucius then returns toward the urgent Publius/Aufidius matter before today's standstill expires.
- Aemilia conducts a private household-discipline inquiry with senior steward Gaius Philargyrus. She learns physical punishment occurs several times in an ordinary month and that lesser delegated supervisors sometimes strike enslaved subordinates without reporting each incident.
- Aemilia orders a one-month trial in the urban domus under Philargyrus's immediate administration: no punitive beating or striking by anyone. Necessary immediate force to stop assault or protect someone from bodily danger remains permitted, including protection of Aemilia and her pregnancy, but may not become punishment afterward.
- Aemilia orders immediate modest rewards for competent ordinary work rather than vague future promises or rewarding exhaustion. Philargyrus will track offenses, responses and rewards so the trial can be assessed. He will inform Lucius because Lucius retains ultimate household authority; unless countermanded, implementation begins immediately.
- Pia's weekly day free ordinary duties still needs formal standing instruction; Pia remains off duty today.
- Aemilia summons Eros, adult enslaved woman and mother of Fortunata (~4) and Felix (~2) by Lucius. Aemilia has her close the door and invites her to sit. Their first substantive private conversation is cautious because of the stark power imbalance.
- Aemilia tells Eros Pia suggested speaking with a servant about things Aemilia does not see and admits she has accepted ignorance until now. Eros explains that servants conceal quarrels, injuries, misconduct and complaints from masters, and that ordering truth does not make candor safe.
- Aemilia acknowledges Eros has particular reason to tell her what she thinks Aemilia wants to hear. Eros says she loves Fortunata and Felix without hesitation and explains that her lack of legal control over their futures makes her especially careful around Aemilia and Lucius.
- Aemilia asks: 'Then if I asked whether you love my husband you would not answer truthfully?'
- Eros does not give a simple yes/no. She says she would answer as truthfully as she safely could, but 'love' is a dangerous word in her position because Lucius was her master and could summon her; affection, gratitude, fear, dependence, familiarity and the fact that he fathered her children can coexist without making the relationship freely chosen.
- Eros says she does care what happens to Lucius and does not wish him harm. She is grateful that he acknowledges and shows care toward Fortunata and Felix. She also states carefully that she was never in a position to decide whether Lucius came to her, so she cannot describe that history as though she had the same choice Aemilia has in their adult marriage.
- Eros does not claim Lucius was uniquely cruel or that she secretly hated/loved him; those stronger conclusions remain unestablished. Her answer establishes emotional complexity under coercive ownership rather than retroactively converting sexual access into a consensual romance.
- Eros's exact age beyond adulthood, appearance, origin and ordinary duties remain unestablished unless they emerge naturally in play.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia and Eros sit alone behind a closed door. Aemilia has directly raised Eros's feelings toward Lucius. Eros has distinguished caring for him and gratitude for his treatment of the children from the freedom required to describe their past sexual relationship as an ordinary chosen love affair. Lucius remains occupied with Publius/Aufidius; Pia remains off duty.
