# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is Aemilia's eighteenth birthday, late evening, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Aemilia's large birthday gathering brings together members of her Aemilian family and Lucius' Valerian family, their friends, Lucius' colleagues and other important guests. The gathering is both a family celebration and a conspicuous elite social event.
- Aemilia has a genuinely wonderful evening. She is visibly happy and confident as hostess, moving among relatives, friends and politically useful guests while household slaves manage the practical burden of the feast. The celebration succeeds socially: no public quarrel or scandal mars it, and guests see Aemilia occupying the place of Lucius' adult wife and mistress of the household with ease.
- Lucius remains publicly attentive without monopolizing her celebration. He receives important guests beside her when appropriate and otherwise allows the evening to belong to Aemilia. The contrast with the confrontation at his father's villa 34 days earlier is marked: tonight Aemilia is celebrated rather than interrogated.
- Lucius' earlier promises now take effect: from this birthday onward he will not seek sexual access to Livia or any other women, and he and Aemilia intend to try for legitimate children together. No sexual activity is assumed merely because the date has arrived.
- As the evening advances, guests gradually begin to depart and the household moves toward the quieter aftermath of the feast. The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
