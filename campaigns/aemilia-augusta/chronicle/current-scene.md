# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, the following morning. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Publius originally received 60,000 sesterces for workshop capital; 18,000 is presently due to the Valerian household as an overdue scheduled payment plus interest/arrears; earlier payments were made; workshop assets are pledged. Aemilia proposed investigating all creditors and potentially consolidating claims to preserve the productive workshop.
- Numerius Aufidius Crispus seized two adult enslaved metalworkers from Publius's workshop. One was struck while resisting removal but remains alive. Aufidius claims secured priority over Valerian interests, disputed/unverified. He prefers prompt certainty on his debt. Lucius secured a standstill through today.
- Aemilia learned Valerian household punishes enslaved people and she had been shielded. Pia disclosed being struck when younger and structural sexual vulnerability, though no one sexually forced Pia. Steward meeting ordered for today.

- Creditor schedule: Aufidius gross 14,400 less documented 1,600 bronze adjustment = 12,800 demanded; second trade creditor 6,200; smaller private obligation 3,600; Valerian presently due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600.
- Publius has ~21,000 customer receivables, only ~12,000 judged promptly collectible; remaining ~9,000 doubtful/delayed rather than established loss. Forced-sale value of stock/tools/workshop is materially worse than continued productive use.
- Workshop appears capable of earning out over time if kept operating but cannot satisfy pressing creditors now. Piecemeal seizure probably destroys value. Aufidius priority over craftsmen remains legally ambiguous; no safe legal resolution before today's deadline. Bronze dispute real but limited.
- Aemilia proposes Valerians satisfy/secure outside creditors, preserve workshop, consolidate debt under Valerian control and charge more interest. Lucius accepts coherent in principle but requires controls: no new borrowing without approval, direct account visibility, receipts first against Valerian exposure, adequate security.
- Earlier Valerian repayment history substantially regular. Crisis worsened after customer receipts slowed and bronze/rework trouble. No obvious large personal diversion/hidden second business. Publius continued commissions and bought inputs on credit without enough deposits/short terms, effectively financing customers with creditors' money. Aemilia identifies this borrowing spiral herself.
- Aemilia notes liquidation may fail even to cover Valerian claim. Lucius agrees but warns avoiding recognition of existing loss is not proof more lending is profitable. Rescue adds ~22,600 outside exposure to Valerian risk.
- When Lucius asks whether she would still rescue Publius, Aemilia says her answer may be naive. Lucius values the admission, distinguishes humane motive from calculation, and says he is inclined toward controlled rescue based on prior repayments/productive value, not mercy or certainty. Rescue not yet executed.
- Aemilia stands, steps between Lucius's knees while he remains seated, places both hands on his shoulders and smiles down at him. She tells him, 'Your answer was definitely more senatorial than mine would have been.' The gesture is private adult marital affection and does not alter the pending business deadline.
- Lucius receives the teasing warmly. He answers that a senatorial answer often means finding a respectable argument for doing what one already thinks necessary, but in this case the discipline matters: if they intervene, they must be able to explain the decision as protection of Valerian assets and profitable control rather than sentimental rescue of Publius. He is amused by her closeness but remains conscious they still need to act before Aufidius's standstill expires.
