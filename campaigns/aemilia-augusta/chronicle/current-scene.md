# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is later in the morning after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Aemilia's birthday gathering was a genuine social success. After the final guest departed, she invited Lucius to share a prepared private bath. Their intimacy continued afterward; the night itself remains undescribed rather than retroactively detailed.
- After their playful first waking together, Aemilia fell asleep again against Lucius. She later wakes to the ordinary machinery of an elite household: Lucius is already up and being dressed by attendants in their room.
- Aemilia rises to be made presentable. Pia, her longtime enslaved personal attendant who came with her from her father's household, begins repairing her disordered hair. Pia understands from the unprecedented shared night and morning that the nearly six-year marital boundary has changed, and discreetly asks how Aemilia feels.
- Aemilia smiles and answers, 'I am well, thank you, Pia.' Pia accepts the answer without prying for intimate details and continues tending her hair with the familiar competence of years of personal service.
- Their marriage has now entered its adult phase by mutual choice. Lucius' fidelity promise remains in effect: he will not seek sexual access to Eros or any other women. He and Aemilia intend to try for legitimate children together, but no pregnancy or reproductive outcome is assumed.
- Lucius has three surviving children born to enslaved women: Hilarus, age six, whose mother is Daphne; Fortunata, age four, whose mother is Eros; and Felix, age two, whose mother is Eros. They remain enslaved members of the household and are not legitimate dynastic heirs of Lucius' marriage to Aemilia.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
