# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Aemilia has asked Lucius to be faithful to her after her eighteenth birthday, and Lucius has agreed without exceptions while making clear that Livia, Valeria and his other children will remain protected members of the household.
- Aemilia stops pacing and fixes Lucius with a stern look. She had built up an entire angry speech, but his immediate agreement has deprived her of the argument she expected. Instead she downs the rest of her wine and releases the handful of skirt she had been carrying, throwing the fabric out with visible indignation.
- Lucius reads the visible frustration and humor of the moment without claiming knowledge of the unspoken speech. He does not retract or qualify his promise. Their exchange remains private, familiar and nonsexual while Aemilia is seventeen.
