# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is later in the morning after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Earlier Aemilia encountered Eros and learned that Eros' two-year-old son Felix had been feverish during the night, though somewhat improved by morning. Aemilia relieved Eros of her duties for the day so she could remain with her son. Felix's outcome remains unresolved.
- Aemilia has joined Lucius and the Greek physician Demetrios in a private consultation about Demetrios' efforts to reduce sickness through cleaner and healthier domestic practices. Lucius intends to listen despite senatorial dismissal and has included Aemilia because implementation intersects strongly with her household authority.
- Demetrios' approach is empirical but historically bounded: he has no germ theory or modern microbiology. He reasons through contemporary ideas of regimen, environment, corrupted air or water and bodily balance, while observing that cleaner water and vessels, waste removal, washing, clean linens and dressings, food storage, ventilation and reduced crowding around some illnesses can sometimes improve health. He also advocates particular cleanliness around childbirth while making no claim that it can eliminate pregnancy or childbirth risks.
- Aemilia accepts responsibility for implementing what proves useful, telling Lucius dutifully, 'I will see to this, husband,' then directs Demetrios, 'This way.' Lucius leaves the practical inspection in her hands.
- Aemilia and Demetrios begin a household inspection, starting with water as Demetrios proposed. His role is advisory; Aemilia retains domestic authority over how recommendations are implemented. The inspection is expected to proceed through water storage and handling, kitchens and food stores, waste, bedding and laundry, servants' quarters and practices around sickness.
- Demetrios does not assume Aemilia is currently pregnant and has not been told the private reproductive history of the marriage. Lucius knows Aemilia and he intend to try for legitimate children and understands the personal relevance of her questions.
- This is the first day of Aemilia and Lucius' adult marital phase. Lucius' fidelity promise remains active. No pregnancy or reproductive outcome is assumed.
