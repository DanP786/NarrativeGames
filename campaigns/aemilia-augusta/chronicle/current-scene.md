- Lucius arrives and asserts Aemilia's place as his wife is not Marcus's to grant/withdraw. Aemilia strategically remains silent during husband/father authority exchange. - Aemilia asks: 'Mother wishes to be here at the birth?'

Marcus says Cornelia has borne children herself and does not regard childbirth lightly; her concern intensified after hearing Aemilia is pregnant.
- Marcus makes clear this is Cornelia's wish rather than a paternal command. He says Cornelia would likely have come today herself had he not insisted on settling his own business with Aemilia first. He expects Cornelia to ask Aemilia directly rather than assume permission.
- Lucius does not object to Cornelia's presence in principle. He leaves the immediate answer to Aemilia while retaining authority over household arrangements and access when confinement actually occurs.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia has learned that Cornelia genuinely wishes to be present for her confinement/birth if Aemilia wants her there. Marcus frames this as Cornelia's maternal wish, not an order. Lucius is present and has not objected. Publius deadline active; steward meeting later; Pia off duty.
