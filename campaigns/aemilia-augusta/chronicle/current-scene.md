# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is later in the morning after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Aemilia's birthday gathering was a genuine social success. After the final guest departed, she invited Lucius to share a prepared private bath. Their intimacy continued afterward; the night itself remains undescribed rather than retroactively detailed.
- After sleeping again and then being dressed, Aemilia is freshly presentable and walking through the Valerian villa as the household settles into its daytime rhythm.
- In an interior passage Aemilia encounters Eros carrying a jug of water. Rather than address the changed marital circumstances, Aemilia asks after Eros' two-year-old son Felix, saying she had been told he had a fever. This establishes that Felix has recently been feverish and that Aemilia receives information about the health of children in her household.
- Eros reports that Felix's fever was worse during the night but has eased somewhat this morning; he has taken some water but remains tired. The illness remains active and its outcome is unresolved.
- The encounter occurs on the first morning after Lucius' fidelity promise to Aemilia has become active. Eros and her children remain protected members of the household and are not to be punished, discarded or displaced because of the change in the marriage.
- Lucius and Aemilia intend to try for legitimate children together, but no pregnancy or reproductive outcome is assumed.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
