# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Aemilia and Lucius are discussing what her approaching adulthood will mean for their privately unconsummated marriage.
- After asking whether Lucius loves Livia, Aemilia accepts his invitation to ask the more personal question beneath it and asks whether he loves Aemilia herself.
- Lucius answers plainly that he does. He distinguishes that love from duty, ownership, dynastic usefulness and the affection he has for his children, grounding it instead in the years of shared household life, argument, education, trust and companionship between them.
- Lucius does not turn the admission into sexual contact while Aemilia remains seventeen. The declaration changes what has previously been deeply felt but largely unnamed between them.
