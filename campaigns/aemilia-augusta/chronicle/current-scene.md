# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. It is the morning after Aemilia told Lucius she has missed an expected menstrual cycle and believes she may be pregnant.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage is established. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children. Pregnancy is possible but not yet firmly established; no physician or midwife has examined Aemilia.
- A long-planned gladiatorial spectacle is being held in Rome today. Aemilia voluntarily agrees to stay home after Lucius voices concern about crowds, heat, jostling and a long public day; Lucius remains home with her.
- They turn the disappointment into private household play in the peristyle: Hilarus, six, and Fortunata, four, stage mock gladiatorial games, with two-year-old Felix jokingly cast as their presiding 'emperor.' Aemilia formally opens the games with a playful speech stressing courage, fairness and victory without cruelty.
- Fortunata immediately charges Hilarus as the mock contest begins. Lucius jokes that Aemilia has created a monster.
- Smiling as she sits beside Lucius and Felix, Aemilia answers, 'And soon we shall our own little monsters,' openly connecting the household play to her hope that she is carrying Lucius' legitimate child. Lucius receives the remark with private tenderness while the pregnancy remains unconfirmed.
- The household's practical health routines based on Demetrios' advice remain in force. The possible pregnancy has not been announced to either family or to the children.
