# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, some weeks after Demetrios and the trusted midwife judged Aemilia strongly likely to be pregnant. Her pregnancy is progressing without an established complication; exact gestational dating remains uncertain by ancient standards.
- Aemilia and Lucius have made no formal announcement of the pregnancy. Nevertheless, the Valerian household has effectively inferred it. Changes in Aemilia's food and drink, unusual attention to what she is served, avoidance of unnecessary illness exposure and other protective adjustments are visible to servants accustomed to noticing household routine.
- Women who attend Aemilia in the baths have also begun to notice the first subtle change in her previously flat belly. Because the household has long been alert to the question of whether Lucius's wife would bear a legitimate child, small signs are being watched closely. No servant has been formally told beyond Pia, but discreet certainty and gossip are spreading below stairs.
- The household's response remains constrained by status: servants do not openly congratulate Aemilia before their masters announce anything, and most behave as though they have noticed nothing. Their increased care around her nevertheless makes the collective inference difficult to conceal.
- Lucius remains faithful to his promise. He and Aemilia continue their adult married life with the midwife's cautions in mind. The pregnancy is their hoped-for legitimate child, though its outcome cannot be guaranteed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
