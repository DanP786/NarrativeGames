# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is evening on the day after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Earlier Aemilia encountered Eros and learned that Eros' two-year-old son Felix had been feverish during the night, though somewhat improved by morning. Aemilia relieved Eros of her duties for the day so she could remain with her son. Felix's outcome remains unresolved.
- Aemilia spent most of the day accompanying the Greek physician Demetrios through a practical inspection of the Valerian domus. They examined actual household routines involving water, kitchens and food stores, waste, laundry and bedding, servants' quarters, ventilation and practices around sickness.
- Demetrios' approach is empirical but historically bounded: he has no germ theory or modern microbiology. He reasons through contemporary ideas of regimen, environment, corrupted air or water and bodily balance, while advocating practical cleanliness where his observations suggest it reduces sickness. He also advocates particular cleanliness around childbirth while making no claim that it can eliminate pregnancy or childbirth risks.
- Aemilia has insisted Demetrios remain as a guest for food and a bed for the night. Lucius, Aemilia and Demetrios now attend an evening dinner together at the domus. The meal is private household hospitality rather than a large formal banquet, allowing the day's inspection and recommendations to be discussed directly.
- Aemilia retains authority over implementation of Demetrios' recommendations. Detailed permanent changes to household routines have not yet been enumerated or ordered in canon.
- Demetrios has not been told the private reproductive history of Lucius and Aemilia. Whether he learns of Felix's current fever remains unresolved unless it arises directly in play.
- This is the first day of Aemilia and Lucius' adult marital phase. Lucius' fidelity promise remains active. No pregnancy or reproductive outcome is assumed.
