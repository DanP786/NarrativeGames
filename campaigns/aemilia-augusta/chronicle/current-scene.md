# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is seventeen; exactly 34 days remain until her eighteenth birthday. Later the same day at the villa of Lucius' father in Rome.
- Lucius and Aemilia stand together before Lucius' father after answering his joint summons. Lucius' father is now established as an older, senior Valerian aristocrat with a blunt, dynastic outlook: legitimate continuity of the family line matters to him more than delicacy. His personal name and exact offices remain unestablished.
- Lucius' father says he has received word from Aemilia's father concerning Lucius' refusal to end the marriage. Aemilia's father remains personally unnamed in canon; rather than invent a cognomen retroactively, the elder Valerian refers to him as Aemilia's father / his Aemilian correspondent.
- The elder Valerian argues from the public evidence available to him: nearly six years of marriage without legitimate issue, while Lucius has demonstrably fathered children by enslaved women. He calls those children bastards in blunt social shorthand and concludes aloud that Aemilia is barren.
- Lucius immediately rejects the conclusion without revealing the private truth that the marriage remains unconsummated. He states that there is no evidence Aemilia is barren and that the absence of children is not grounds for his father to pronounce a diagnosis on his wife. He does not expose Aemilia's body, private marital history, or the promise he made when she was twelve.
- This creates a direct conflict between Lucius' protection of the marriage's secret and the dynastic logic of his father, who has every public reason to believe the marriage has been consummated for years.
- No sexual or erotic content occurs.
