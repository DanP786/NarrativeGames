# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is seventeen; exactly 34 days remain until her eighteenth birthday. Later the same day at the villa of Lucius' father in Rome.
- Lucius and Aemilia stand together before Lucius' father after answering his joint summons. The elder Valerian argues that nearly six years without legitimate issue, contrasted with Lucius' children by enslaved women, indicates Aemilia is barren. Lucius rejects that conclusion without revealing that the marriage remains unconsummated.
- After crudely asking whether Aemilia is physically defective, Lucius' father escalates: 'A wife who can give you no heir is useless. Cut her loose and remarry.' He treats marriage explicitly as a dynastic instrument and Aemilia's presumed infertility as sufficient reason to discard her.
- Lucius rejects both premises. He states that his father has not established that Aemilia cannot bear children and, more decisively, that he will not divorce her. He emphasizes that the marriage is his to govern and refuses to permit his father to reduce Aemilia's value to an unproven fertility judgment. He still does not reveal the private fact that the marriage remains unconsummated.
- Lucius' refusal is firm enough to turn the conversation from advice into an intergenerational authority conflict. The elder Valerian has legitimate family/dynastic interests but does not control Lucius' adult household simply by paternal command. Aemilia remains present and free to speak.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
- No sexual or erotic content occurs.
