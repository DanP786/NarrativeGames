# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is approximately three months pregnant. The pregnancy is progressing without an established complication.
- Aemilia and Lucius have arrived at the Roman residence of Lucius's elder brother Marcus Valerius Messalla Minor and his wife Servilia for a birthday gathering for one of their sons. Their sons are Marcus, about ten, and Lucius, about five; which boy is celebrating remains unestablished.
- Before leaving home, Aemilia chose not to disguise the pregnancy. Pia dressed her comfortably and elegantly so the slight three-month curve of her abdomen and changed figure are allowed to show naturally. Her movement is also more measured than before pregnancy.
- A private message has been sent to Aemilia's parents, Marcus Aemilius Lepidus and Cornelia, telling them directly that Aemilia is about three months pregnant, progressing well so far, and that the delay in telling them was due to early caution. This was done so the Aemilii would not learn through Valerian family gossip.
- On arrival, Servilia notices Aemilia's pregnancy almost immediately and embraces her warmly. Asked how far along she is, Aemilia answers, 'Three months, I believe.' Servilia now has direct confirmation from Aemilia. She understands the qualification as ordinary uncertainty in dating an early pregnancy and responds with experienced maternal warmth rather than pressing for false precision.
- Lucius remains beside Aemilia and visibly pleased, while allowing the women their exchange rather than speaking for his wife.
- Aemilia's gentler hairstyle preference remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck, with more elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Lucius remains faithful to his promise. The pregnancy is their hoped-for legitimate child, though its outcome cannot be guaranteed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
