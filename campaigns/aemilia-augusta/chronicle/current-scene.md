# Current scene

- S01, wedding night, Valerian domus in Rome.
- Aemilia, age twelve, and Lucius Valerius Messalla, age twenty-four, are alone in the bedchamber after the ceremonies.
- Lucius has told Aemilia he will not consummate the marriage while she is a child. He intends to educate her in Latin, Greek, history, accounts, law and eventually household political business, while retaining final authority as household head.
- Aemilia has questioned how delayed children affect Augustan marriage policy, inheritance and her legal position. Lucius says she has been taught several complicated legal matters as frightening simplifications and promises she will learn the actual rules.
- Lucius has framed the delay of children and her expanded education as an unusual gift rather than rejection or punishment, and expects the marriage eventually to produce heirs once she is older.
- Aemilia repeats something she has been told: that men have desires. Lucius confirms the Roman marital double standard and says he will avoid public humiliation of her while keeping adult matters entirely separate from her childhood.
- At Lucius' dry remark that she needs sleep rather than an education in Roman men's appetites, Aemilia nearly laughs. Lucius notices and invites her to laugh openly in private.
- Aemilia smiles instead and says she looks forward to learning what Lucius permits. Lucius catches the significance of 'permit': he accepts that his authority sets real limits, but tells her his purpose is not merely to teach permitted answers. He wants her eventually to form judgments of her own and bring disagreement to him privately rather than simply anticipating what will please him.
