# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. The discreet family-and-business meeting at Lucius and Aemilia's domus has continued through supper. It is now later in the day. Aemilia remains approximately three months pregnant, with no established complication.
- The private conversation after the earlier Valerian birthday gathering remains settled between Aemilia and Lucius: Aemilia learned that during the childhood-boundary years Lucius exercised sexual access to six adult enslaved women in total, including Eros and Daphne; she distinguishes those years from their adult marriage and told him she forgives what came before. Lucius's fidelity promise from Aemilia's eighteenth birthday remains absolute, including during any period when pregnancy, childbirth, illness or medical caution prevents marital intimacy.
- The day's deliberately deniable meeting has brought Terentia and thirteen-year-old Valeria Tertia to Aemilia as family guests while Lucius independently received Gaius Vibius Rufus, approximately twenty-two, a wealthy rising equestrian from a non-senatorial family. Terentia knows Aemilia suggested Rufus as a possible husband for Valeria. Valeria has not been told she is evaluating Rufus as a prospective husband, and Rufus has not been told he is being observed as one. Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told of the possibility.
- Aemilia introduced Rufus to Terentia and Valeria and encouraged Valeria to test his reputed ability to find things. Valeria challenged him to identify and, if possible, obtain an unidentified Greek poem about Atalanta once quoted by her tutor. Rufus did not bluff knowledge, questioned Valeria carefully about what she remembers, and accepted the challenge. He told her that if he finds a purchasable copy he will tell her the cost before buying it because the choice of what it is worth should be hers.
- Aemilia invited Rufus to stay for supper. Before the meal, Lucius turned the gathering toward the legitimate business that brought Rufus to the house, drawing out Rufus's methods for judging commercial information, comparing independent reports, following credit and contractual relationships, and distinguishing firsthand knowledge from reputation. This demonstrated why Lucius values Rufus while preserving the meeting's innocent business explanation.
- Terentia's assessment so far is favorable but cautious. She notes that Rufus admits ignorance, does not flatter Valeria, speaks to her rather than through her elders, becomes interested rather than irritated when she makes things difficult, and gives her agency over her own desired purchase. Terentia thinks Valeria already likes talking to him but distinguishes this from liking him as a prospective husband. She wants to see how Rufus handles disagreement and how Valeria behaves once novelty wears off. Rufus's equestrian status remains the principal dynastic obstacle; Terentia has said that if he were a senator's son they would likely already be discussing terms.
- Aemilia and Terentia privately joked about the matchmaking during an oleander inspection. Aemilia called Rufus 'merely a suggestion.' Terentia teased that Aemilia's definition of suggestion is remarkably energetic. When Terentia joked that dealing with Marcus might require more wine, Aemilia answered, 'Our stores are always available to you, socrus mea carissima,' an affectionate 'my dearest mother-in-law.'
- During the business discussion an enslaved household attendant silently brought Aemilia a chair and water without being asked, reflecting established pregnancy precautions and familiarity with her habits. Aemilia continued listening. She later exchanged a small private smile with Lucius while he questioned Rufus.
- Supper has since taken place without establishing a betrothal, disclosing the matchmaking plan to Valeria or Rufus, or informing Marcus. Rufus's poem challenge remains unresolved and gives him a natural reason for future contact.
- CURRENT MOMENT: later that day, Aemilia and Valeria Tertia sit together in the shade at the domus, away from the main heat and bustle. The gathering has relaxed after supper. Valeria still does not know that Aemilia and Terentia arranged the day partly to observe Rufus as a possible match.
- Valeria asks whether Aemilia truly believes Rufus will find the unidentified Atalanta poem. Aemilia answers, 'If it exists? I think he will, yes.' Valeria admits she had partly expected Rufus to laugh at the challenge or offer to buy her something easier instead. His taking her seriously has therefore made an impression. She then asks Aemilia how long she has known Rufus.
- Aemilia answers, 'Four years. I wasn't much older than you when I first met him.' This establishes that Aemilia first met Rufus around age fourteen, when Rufus was approximately eighteen, through the circles around Lucius's affairs. Valeria immediately recognizes that Aemilia has therefore known Rufus long enough for her favorable judgment to rest on repeated acquaintance rather than today's performance alone. She asks whether Rufus treated Aemilia seriously when she was that age as well.
- Aemilia's pregnancy is openly known among close Valerian relatives. Her gentler hairstyle preference remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck, with elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
