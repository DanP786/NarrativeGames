# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. It is the morning after Aemilia told Lucius she has missed an expected menstrual cycle and believes she may be pregnant.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage is established. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children. Pregnancy is possible but not yet firmly established; no physician or midwife has examined Aemilia.
- A long-planned gladiatorial spectacle is being held in Rome today. Lucius had refused to take Aemilia while she was a child and promised she could attend after turning eighteen, so the outing had been anticipated for years. Aemilia voluntarily agrees to stay home after Lucius voices concern about crowds, heat, jostling and a long public day; Lucius remains home with her.
- They turn the disappointment into private household play in the peristyle: Hilarus, six, and Fortunata, four, stage mock gladiatorial games, with two-year-old Felix jokingly cast as their presiding 'emperor.'
- Aemilia sits with Felix and Lucius overlooking the improvised arena. Lucius establishes strict rules against real injury and gives Aemilia the honor of opening the games.
- Aemilia delivers a deliberately grand, playful opening address to the tiny household audience, praising the courage of Hilarus and Fortunata, reminding them that the greatest fighter is one who can win without cruelty, and formally inviting the two 'warriors' to begin. The children receive it with excitement; the mock contest is ready to start.
- The household's practical health routines based on Demetrios' advice remain in force. The possible pregnancy has not been announced to either family or to the children.
