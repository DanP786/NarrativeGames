# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC. Roughly a week has passed at the Valerian country villa since Aemilia and Lucius first resumed marital intimacy; the pregnancy loss is now more than two weeks in the past, though exact day count remains loose. Aemilia is eighteen and has returned with Lucius to the Roman domus.
- The severe marital breach remains canon: Lucius twice physically stopped Aemilia leaving an argument and the second restraint broke his explicit promise not to do so again. He later admitted **“I was wrong.”** The breach is actively being repaired but is not erased.
- During the week at the villa, Aemilia consistently welcomed Lucius's touch and took comfort in his devotion. She talked freely with him and initiated or returned affectionate touch. She still does not smile freely and does not laugh. Their adult marital intimacy has resumed voluntarily. No new pregnancy is established.
- Aemilia remains in physical recovery from severe blood loss and prolonged weakness. The quiet week improved her rest and endurance, but she is not fully restored to her former strength. No fever, renewed hemorrhage or new medical complication is established.
- Pia remains missing. The initial discreet information-only inquiry produced its first uncertain lead: a witness along the route associated with Pia's genuine leave recalls a woman broadly matching Pia's description with unfamiliar men and remembers that she did not appear to be moving with them freely. The witness cannot establish identity with certainty or say where the group ultimately went.
- On hearing this lead, Aemilia raises her voice for the first time anyone present has known her to do so: **“Then find her! I do not just want information if she is taken against her will. You know I was concerned about her.”** The outburst is directed at the urgency of Pia's possible danger rather than a general abandonment of Aemilia's controlled manner.
- Aemilia thereby changes the standing Pia instruction. If Pia may have been taken against her will, the household is now ordered to actively find and recover her rather than merely gather information. Her earlier condition concerning a voluntary escape remains conceptually distinct: the escalation is triggered specifically by evidence suggesting coercion.
- The steward immediately accepts the correction and treats the matter as urgent. Lucius supports the active search rather than overriding Aemilia. The household may now use Valerian resources, inquiries and connections to trace the witness lead, identify the unfamiliar men and follow likely onward routes or transactions. No exact captor or destination is yet known.
- OOC GM canon remains: Pia did not deliberately flee Aemilia. During genuine leave she was unlawfully seized and subsequently held/sold onward as enslaved property despite already belonging to the Valerian household. Aemilia, Lucius and the household do not know this hidden truth; it must be uncovered through plausible investigation.

- Eros remains in Rome and no longer has discretionary direct/private access to Lucius; necessary child or household matters pass through ordinary channels or occur with others present. The restriction has not lapsed.
- Household social knowledge remains distinct from private fact. The full marital breach is known only to Aemilia, Lucius, Cornelia and Terentia in differing detail. Household members can observe Aemilia and Lucius returning together and functioning as husband and wife, but cannot legitimately infer that grief or the marriage is completely healed.

Aemilia told him she was glad he rather than Scipio became her husband.
