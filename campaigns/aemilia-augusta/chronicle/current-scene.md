# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, the following morning. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Publius originally received 60,000 sesterces for workshop capital; 18,000 is presently due to the Valerian household as an overdue scheduled payment plus interest/arrears; earlier payments were made; workshop assets are pledged. Aemilia proposed investigating all creditors and potentially consolidating claims to preserve the productive workshop.
- Another creditor, Numerius Aufidius Crispus, seized two adult enslaved metalworkers from Publius's workshop. One was struck while resisting removal but remains alive. Aufidius claims secured priority over Valerian interests, still disputed/unverified. He does not particularly want the craftsmen and prefers prompt certainty on his debt.
- During negotiation at the Valerian domus, Aemilia used hospitality and calibrated charm to learn Aufidius's interests. Lucius then secured a standstill: neither craftsman would be sold before the following day while documents were exchanged and reviewed, provided the Valerians acted promptly. That day has now arrived.
- Aufidius admitted Publius complained about part of a bronze delivery and received a limited adjustment; the extent and cause of the alleged defects remain disputed. He is to provide his complete account/security papers and records of that adjustment for comparison with Publius's books and other creditor claims.
- Priscus was ordered to work urgently. The financial review must establish creditor names, amounts, securities, due dates, priority claims, receivables, stock and realistic earning capacity before Lucius decides whether to guarantee/assume an outside claim, consolidate debts, purchase assets, restructure, or allow enforcement to proceed. Exact outside figures remain to be adjudicated from the documents rather than assumed.
- Aemilia has learned the Valerian household itself punishes enslaved people and that she had been shielded from harsher administration. Pia disclosed that she was physically struck when younger and has lived with the structural sexual vulnerability of enslavement, though no one has sexually forced Pia. Aemilia ordered a meeting with the household steward for today to investigate treatment within the household.
- Aemilia also granted Pia one genuine weekly day free from ordinary labor, with discretion to rest or make ordinary outings subject to household knowledge/security expectations. Lucius advised formalizing the privilege in the steward's standing instructions and arranging replacement attendant coverage. This remains to be done.
- The previous day's private marital affection between Aemilia and Lucius ended without further public consequence; their intimacy remains affectionate and non-explicit.
- THIS MORNING: the commercial deadline is no longer abstract. Priscus has had the night and early morning to assemble what can be assembled. A bundle of tablets and documents has arrived for Lucius's review, including material from Aufidius and Publius's side, but the contents have not yet been established in play. Because Aemilia was promised a seat in the review, Lucius has had the papers brought to a working room rather than deciding alone.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia and Lucius are about to examine Priscus's compiled accounts and creditor schedule. The standstill on the two craftsmen lasts only through today, so any gaps or unpleasant figures matter immediately.
