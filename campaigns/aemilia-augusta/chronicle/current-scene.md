# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, deep night. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Earlier today Marcus Aemilius Lepidus withdrew pressure to end Aemilia's marriage and partially reconciled with her. Cornelia wants to attend Aemilia's confinement/birth; Aemilia wants her there. Pia's first genuine day free ordinary duties remained intact; formal weekly instruction remains pending.
- Aemilia's one-month urban-domus discipline trial is approved conditionally by Lucius: no punitive beating/striking, protective force permitted, modest rewards, simple tracking.
- Eros privately told Lucius substantially the whole of her earlier conversation with Aemilia, including Eros's lack of meaningful refusal during Lucius's former sexual access, her wish for freedom if she can remain near/protect her children, Menophilus/Chryseros/Thais, Aemilia's former jealousy, Aemilia's statement that the only person she fears is her husband, and Eros's judgment that Aemilia is kind/sincere but sheltered and unlikely to prevail if Lucius says no.
- At bedtime this disclosure caused a severe marital argument. Aemilia felt betrayed and believed Eros was manipulating Lucius. Lucius conceded Eros breached unnecessary private confidences and that he mishandled the disclosure but would not conclude without evidence that Eros intends to destroy/displace Aemilia.
- During the argument Aemilia twice tried to leave and Lucius physically stopped her twice. The second time came immediately after he explicitly promised not to stop her again: he drove the door shut and forced her back against it, avoiding her abdomen but coercively confining her. He later cleared the door and admitted **“I was wrong.”** This serious marital boundary breach remains unresolved.
- Aemilia eventually became tearful and defeated, apologized only to end the argument, and developed abdominal pain strong enough to make standing difficult. Lucius ended the argument and wanted her to rest; Aemilia insisted **“I'm fine”** and moved slowly to bed. No bleeding had yet been established then.
- Later that same night Aemilia wakes with significant abdominal pain and vaginal bleeding. Amount/color, clots/tissue, exact pain pattern and pregnancy outcome remain unestablished. This is an alarming symptom at approximately four months and is treated as an emergency without assuming miscarriage or fetal death.
- Lucius calls for light, Demetrios, the trusted midwife, clean water and linens. Aemilia weeps, clutches her stomach and says **“I don't know”** when asked where/how much. Lucius does not press her and stays beside her without promising safety or assigning cause.
- Demetrios arrives. Aemilia lies awake, nearly motionless, crying and clutching her abdomen. Demetrios orders light, clean cloths/water and reduced crowding; he begins assessment cautiously and asks to examine her abdomen/bleeding with the midwife's assistance. His methods are ancient and limited and he does not claim diagnostic certainty.
- When Demetrios asks about the pain and whether Aemilia has felt the child move, Aemilia gives no verbal answer. She only cries. Internally she believes she understands what is happening to her body and fears she is losing the pregnancy; this is Aemilia's conviction/fear, not yet an established medical outcome.
- Demetrios recognizes that questioning is not presently yielding useful answers and does not demand speech from her. He proceeds from observable signs and asks Lucius/attendants only for facts they actually witnessed: onset of pain, first observed bleeding, whether Aemilia fainted/vomited/fevered, and whether any tissue or heavy blood loss has been seen. Unestablished signs remain unestablished rather than invented.
- Demetrios tells Aemilia plainly that bleeding and pain at this stage are grave signs but that he cannot yet tell her the outcome merely from those signs. He needs to examine her and the midwife needs to assess the bleeding. He avoids false reassurance while refusing to pronounce the child lost before evidence establishes it.
- Lucius remains beside Aemilia but does not force comfort or demand she answer. He is frightened and recognizes her crying may reflect the same conclusion he fears. He yields medical control to Demetrios and does not assign blame or causation.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Deep night. Aemilia lies crying and clutching her abdomen, convinced/fearing she knows what the bleeding means. Demetrios is at bedside and preparing/continuing an urgent examination; the trusted midwife is expected imminently. Pregnancy outcome and maternal trajectory remain unresolved.
