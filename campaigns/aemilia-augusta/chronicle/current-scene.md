# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is the morning after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Aemilia's birthday gathering was a genuine social success. After the final guest departed, she invited Lucius to share a prepared private bath. Their intimacy continued afterward, and Aemilia wakes beside Lucius in their bed. The night itself remains undescribed rather than retroactively detailed.
- Lucius greets her with, 'Good morning, wife,' and teases that she survived eighteen after all. Aemilia sleepily answers that she did. The household is stirring outside, but they remain together in bed for an unhurried morning.
- Aemilia yawns and observes, 'You have never woken beside me before.' Lucius answers that he has never woken beside anyone before. This establishes that, despite his previous sexual access to enslaved women, he did not share the marital/domestic intimacy of sleeping through the night and waking beside them. He treats waking beside Aemilia as new to him as well.
- This marks the end of the nearly six-year period in which Lucius deliberately maintained a marital boundary while Aemilia was a child. Their marriage has now entered its adult phase by mutual choice.
- Lucius' fidelity promise remains in effect: he will not seek sexual access to Livia or any other women. He and Aemilia intend to try for legitimate children together, but no pregnancy or reproductive outcome is assumed.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
