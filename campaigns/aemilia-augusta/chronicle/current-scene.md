# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after Aemilia and Lucius' late-night conversation and around the period of her father's renewed inquiries into the childless marriage. Aemilia remains seventeen, closer to her eighteenth birthday.
- Daytime in the courtyard of the Valerian domus. Aemilia and Lucius watch his three surviving children play: two boys and Valeria, approximately four, daughter of Livia.
- The conversation has shifted from the children to the material fortunes of the domus. Aemilia observes that Lucius has amassed great wealth since their marriage. Lucius acknowledges her dowry and connections as important foundations but also credits estates, credit, obligations, patronage, information and calculated risk; no precise fortune is established.
- Lucius notes Aemilia's increasing usefulness as a wife who remembers politically and financially useful names, then recognizes that her observation is leading toward an argument and asks her to continue.
- Aemilia rises, faces Lucius directly and fans herself in the warm courtyard air. Lucius recognizes the deliberate change in posture and waits for her argument rather than supplying it for her.
- The three children continue playing nearby under household supervision. The two boys' names, exact ages, mothers and individual personalities remain unestablished. Aemilia remains seventeen; no sexual or erotic content occurs.
