# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. It is the morning after Aemilia told Lucius she has missed an expected menstrual cycle and believes she may be pregnant.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage is established. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children. Pregnancy is possible but not yet firmly established; no physician or midwife has examined Aemilia.
- After Aemilia gives up a long-awaited public gladiatorial outing because Lucius fears the practical hazards of crowds, heat and jostling during a possible pregnancy, they create private mock gladiatorial games in the peristyle. Hilarus and Fortunata fight with safe practice weapons while Felix jokingly presides.
- The mock games become a recurring household tradition. All children of the household may participate or preside according to age and ability. Household staff are encouraged to watch when duties permit, and during the games they are explicitly allowed to cheer, laugh, shout encouragement and take sides rather than maintaining ordinary quiet decorum.
- The contests remain recreational: practice weapons, clear rules, no deliberate injury, immediate stopping when someone falls or yields, and no imitation of executions or lethal outcomes. Lucius promises Aemilia that he will give her as much of the noise, ceremony, rivalry and excitement of a Roman spectacle as can reasonably be recreated inside the villa without its harsh realities. The games are for fun.
- The first games begin with Aemilia's playful formal opening speech stressing courage, fairness and victory without cruelty. The household responds enthusiastically, establishing the tone for later contests.
- The household's practical health routines based on Demetrios' advice remain in force. The possible pregnancy has not been announced to either family or to the children.
