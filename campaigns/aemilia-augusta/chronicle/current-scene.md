# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is approximately three months pregnant. The pregnancy is progressing without an established complication.
- Aemilia and Lucius have made no formal public announcement, though their household effectively knows. They are preparing to attend a birthday gathering for one of Lucius's nephews at the household of his elder brother Marcus Valerius Messalla Minor and Servilia.
- While dressing, Aemilia protests that she is 'hardly showing.' Lucius observes that experienced female relatives may notice these changes even without an obvious belly.
- Aemilia scoffs at Lucius's prediction. Pia, whose duties include dressing Aemilia and therefore make changes in clothing fit impossible to miss, adds another practical sign: Aemilia's breasts have grown enough that the upper fit and drape of her clothing have changed. Pia matter-of-factly notes that women who know Aemilia well may notice that too. The observation is treated as an ordinary pregnancy-related bodily change rather than sexualized.
- Aemilia's gentler hairstyle preference remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck, with more elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Lucius remains faithful to his promise. The pregnancy is their hoped-for legitimate child, though its outcome cannot be guaranteed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
