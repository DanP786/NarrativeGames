# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after S01. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant. Morning at Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus in Rome; Lucius is away.
- Earlier Aemilia received Tullia concerning Publius's debt and independently checked Lucius's household records. The Valerian records substantially support Tullia's description: Publius originally received 60,000 sesterces for workshop capital; 18,000 is presently due as an overdue scheduled payment plus interest/arrears; earlier payments were made; workshop assets are pledged; Gnaeus Fabius Priscus appears to be enforcing within ordinary delegated authority without a fresh personal review by Lucius.
- While replacing those records, Aemilia found and deliberately read private tablets belonging to Lucius. They showed that during her later adolescence her father Marcus Aemilius discreetly explored the financial consequences of ending her still-childless marriage and the possibility of another future match, while Lucius secretly monitored those efforts and withheld the matter from Aemilia. The inquiries never advanced to an established replacement match.
- Aemilia also discovered older premarital due-diligence reports compiled about her when she was about twelve. They covered her birth and family standing, contemplated dowry, health, family reproductive history, education, domestic/religious instruction, temperament and absence of known scandal. Informants had noticed her tendency to listen closely to political/estate conversation and remember names and disputes. Lucius's notes rejected confident predictions of a child's future fertility or adult character and stated that he would judge Aemilia himself once he knew her.
- Aemilia carefully returned every tablet and surrounding account record to the place and arrangement in which she found it, taking nothing and deliberately leaving no sign that she read the private packet.
- Pia remained outside the office throughout Aemilia's reading and did not see the material. On leaving, Aemilia tells Pia, 'We never entered the study.' The instruction is an explicit command to conceal their presence there. Pia answers simply, 'No, domina,' accepting the fiction without asking what Aemilia found.
- Aemilia gathers her skirts and leaves the study area with Pia. Lucius does not know that Aemilia has discovered or read the private reports, and Pia knows only that Aemilia entered Lucius's study, spent time alone there, and now requires that fact concealed.
- Aemilia privately carries both the financial information relevant to Publius and the personal knowledge concerning her father's contingency planning and Lucius's secret surveillance/premarital investigation.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia and Pia are moving away from Lucius's study. Aemilia has deliberately chosen secrecy for the moment and has enlisted Pia in concealing that they entered the room. Lucius remains away from the domus.
