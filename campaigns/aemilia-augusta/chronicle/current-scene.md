# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. The current scene is private in Lucius' study at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Over the preceding months Aemilia has made Demetrios' practical health recommendations durable household routines involving water, food, waste, linens, washing and care around illness.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage has settled into an established private rhythm. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children together.
- Aemilia's expected monthly bleeding has failed to begin at its usual time. Pregnancy is possible but not yet firmly established; no physician or midwife has examined her.
- Aemilia tells Lucius she believes she is carrying their child. Lucius responds with visible happiness and tenderness while cautioning that they should tell no one yet and arrange for a trustworthy attendant or practitioner.
- Aemilia happily wraps her arms around Lucius' shoulders and embraces him. Lucius returns the embrace, allowing himself private happiness at the possibility of their child after their long wait.
- The private reason for the marriage's earlier childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
