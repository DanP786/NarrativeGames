# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is Aemilia's eighteenth birthday, late evening, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Aemilia's large birthday gathering has been a genuine social success, bringing together her Aemilian family, Lucius' Valerian family, friends, Lucius' colleagues and other important guests. Aemilia has had a wonderful evening and has been visibly happy and confident as hostess.
- As the last substantial cluster of guests prepares to depart, Lucius teasingly observes that Aemilia appears to have survived becoming eighteen. Aemilia answers, 'Not quite yet, husband,' teasing him in return.
- Lucius understands the double edge of the remark: the birthday evening is literally not over, and this is also the date on which the long-established terms of their marriage change. He responds with private amusement rather than presuming what Aemilia wants next, reminding her that there are still guests in the house and leaving the next move to her.
- Lucius' promises are now in effect: from this birthday onward he will not seek sexual access to Livia or any other women, and he and Aemilia intend to try for legitimate children together. No sexual activity is assumed merely because the date has arrived.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
