# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is approximately three months pregnant. The pregnancy is progressing without an established complication.
- Aemilia and Lucius are preparing to attend a birthday gathering for one of Lucius's nephews at the household of his elder brother Marcus Valerius Messalla Minor and Servilia. Their own household effectively knows of the pregnancy despite no formal public announcement.
- Aemilia has decided not to disguise the pregnancy at the gathering. Pia dresses her comfortably and elegantly so the slight three-month curve of her abdomen and changed figure are allowed to show naturally rather than being concealed.

- Aemilia's gentler hairstyle preference remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck, with more elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Lucius remains faithful to his promise. The pregnancy is their hoped-for legitimate child, though its outcome cannot be guaranteed.
