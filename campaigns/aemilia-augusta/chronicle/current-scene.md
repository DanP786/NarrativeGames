# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after Aemilia and Lucius' late-night conversation. Aemilia remains seventeen and is now somewhat closer to her eighteenth birthday; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Morning at the Valerian domus in Rome. Aemilia's father has sent two physicians, accompanied by a household freedman, to investigate why Aemilia has supposedly failed to conceive during her long marriage.
- Lucius has not allowed the physicians access to Aemilia and privately asks how she wants the intervention handled. Aemilia immediately identifies the danger: 'If they examine me they will learn I am untouched.'
- Lucius agrees that no intimate physical examination will be permitted. He also cautions against assuming that ancient physicians could reliably prove sexual history from anatomy alone; the greater danger is the physicians' questioning, interpretation and subsequent report creating suspicion around a marriage publicly believed consummated.
- Lucius decides the physicians may ask Aemilia ordinary questions about her general health and menstrual history only if she agrees, but they will not perform an intimate examination. He intends to justify the limit publicly as a husband's refusal to permit unnecessary intrusion into his wife's body, preserving both Aemilia's dignity and the household secret without confessing the true reason for childlessness.
- Aemilia remains seventeen; no sexual or erotic content occurs and no intimate examination is depicted.
