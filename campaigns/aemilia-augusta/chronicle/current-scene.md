# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, morning. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Publius workshop crisis active. Figures: Aufidius 12,800; other pressing outside debts 6,200 and 3,600; Valerian due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600. Receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. Forced liquidation likely destroys value/may not cover Valerian claim. Lucius leans controlled rescue but has not executed it; Aufidius standstill on two seized enslaved craftsmen expires today.
- Pia's first genuine weekly day free ordinary labor is today. Another enslaved household woman dressed Aemilia/did hair; usual clothing, visibly different hair. Pia genuinely off ordinary duties.
- Marcus Aemilius Lepidus's unannounced visit has concluded. He withdrew prior pressure to end Aemilia's marriage, accepted her happiness and judgment, and agreed the marriage should continue. Aemilia and Marcus partially reconciled. Cornelia wants to be present during Aemilia's confinement/birth; Aemilia wants her there. Marcus has been told about the trusted unnamed midwife and Demetrios.
- After Marcus leaves, Aemilia and Lucius share an affectionate, impulsive adult marital interlude, non-explicitly narrated. Lucius then returns toward the urgent Publius/Aufidius matter before today's standstill expires.
- Aemilia conducts a private household-discipline inquiry with senior steward Gaius Philargyrus. She learns physical punishment occurs several times in an ordinary month and that lesser delegated supervisors sometimes strike enslaved subordinates without reporting each incident.
- Aemilia orders a one-month trial in the urban domus under Philargyrus's immediate administration: no punitive beating or striking by anyone. Necessary immediate force to stop assault or protect someone from bodily danger remains permitted, including protection of Aemilia and her pregnancy, but may not become punishment afterward.
- Aemilia orders immediate modest rewards for competent ordinary work rather than vague future promises or rewarding exhaustion. Philargyrus will track offenses, responses and rewards so the trial can be assessed. He will inform Lucius because Lucius retains ultimate household authority; unless countermanded, implementation begins immediately.
- Pia's weekly day free ordinary duties still needs formal standing instruction; Pia remains off duty today.
- Aemilia's private conversation with Eros has ended. Eros disclosed that Lucius's past sexual access was not something she always wanted and that refusal was not meaningfully available; she prefers remaining with her children to sale/transfer but would choose freedom if she could remain near/protect them. Aemilia promised no retaliation for candor and said she and Lucius do not wish to separate Eros from Fortunata/Felix; no manumission promise was made.
- Eros identified **Menophilus**, an enslaved senior cellar/store servant, as abusing delegated authority through arbitrary striking and intimidation. She identified **Chryseros**, an enslaved stores clerk/assistant, as a plausible replacement, and **Thais**, a senior enslaved woman in laundry/linen work, as a lesser concern for favoritism and punitive manipulation of assignments, sleep and small comforts. Eros cautioned that allegations require corroboration.
- Aemilia disclosed to Eros that Lucius refused adult marital intimacy with Aemilia until eighteen and that Aemilia had once been jealous that Eros bore his children. Their conversation sharpened the distinction between Aemilia's constrained freeborn status and Eros's enslavement.
- After dismissing Eros, Aemilia enters service/work areas she has never previously seen despite roughly six years in the household. She walks silently at first, observing storage, laundry-related work, repair, cleaning, supply handling and the hierarchy among enslaved workers.
- Aemilia continues moving freely through the service side rather than conducting a formal inspection with Philargyrus. She discovers practical details normally invisible upstairs: damaged household goods are repaired repeatedly; leftovers and usable scraps are sorted rather than casually discarded; water, lamp oil, linens and serving goods require continual carrying/counting/cleaning; sleeping and working spaces for servants are more cramped and materially spare than elite rooms; household labor continues through overlapping tasks rather than a neat separation of roles.
- She begins asking ordinary practical questions rather than immediately asking about abuse: what a worker is doing, where particular stores go, how often linens are washed or repaired, who assigns a task, what happens to broken objects, how late certain work normally continues, and who is responsible when something is missing or spoiled.
- Answers are initially deferential and guarded. Workers tend to give factual descriptions of duties and direct responsibility upward rather than volunteer grievances. Some glance toward supervisors or other workers before answering, reinforcing Eros's warning that hierarchy shapes what can safely be said.
- Aemilia learns by questioning that undesirable tasks and late work can be assigned through delegated supervisors and that access to small comforts—better sleeping places, preferred work, first choice of leftovers or similar household advantages—can function as informal rewards or penalties. This broadly corroborates the mechanism Eros described but does not yet independently establish that Thais abuses it for personal favoritism.
- Aemilia does not reveal Eros as a source and does not yet accuse Menophilus or Thais. Menophilus, Chryseros and Thais are not yet formally identified by sight to Aemilia.
- Aemilia's presence itself continues to distort what she can observe: overt misconduct disappears while she is nearby, and direct questions produce answers calibrated for the domina. Nevertheless, she is gaining a more concrete map of how authority and material privilege actually move through the household.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia is moving through the service areas asking practical questions of enslaved workers. She is receiving cautious factual answers and beginning to understand delegated task assignment, working conditions and informal privileges without yet exposing Eros or confronting any named supervisor. Lucius remains occupied with Publius/Aufidius; Pia remains off duty.
