# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after S01. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant. The pregnancy is more visibly apparent but no complication is established.
- Morning at Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus in Rome. Lucius is away from the domus. The discreet Rufus-Valeria gathering is now several weeks past. Terentia remains cautiously favorable; Valeria and Rufus remain unaware of the matchmaking purpose; Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told. Rufus's Atalanta-poem search remains unresolved.
- Aemilia's standing within the household has continued to consolidate. Her pregnancy makes her dynastic importance more visible to visitors and dependents as well as to the family.
- Aemilia receives Tullia in the tablinum with Pia attending. Tullia is an adult married Roman woman in her thirties from a respectable senatorial-connected family, known to Aemilia by sight from elite social and religious occasions but not as an intimate. Tullia's own attendant remains near the doorway.
- Tullia says she came because she would rather put the matter before the mistress of the Valerian house before involving either woman's husband.
- Aemilia responds with visible concern rather than satisfaction at being approached: 'What can I help you with, Tullia?'
- Tullia explains that one of her husband's freedmen, Publius, has been pressed for payment on an obligation by a Valerian household agent. The debt itself is not denied. The immediate problem is that the agent has threatened, if payment is not produced promptly, to seize pledged workshop equipment and stock that Publius says are necessary to keep his business operating. Tullia says Publius's wife came to her in distress because seizure would likely prevent the household from earning enough to discharge the obligation at all.
- Tullia does not ask Aemilia to erase the debt. She asks whether Aemilia can have the collection paused long enough for the accounts and pledged property to be examined before the matter escalates to Lucius and Tullia's husband. She is explicitly trying to solve a household problem through the two women before it becomes a dispute between men and houses.
- The identity of the Valerian agent, the amount and origin of the debt, whether the threatened seizure is contractually justified, and whether Publius's account is truthful have not yet been established. Aemilia has not committed herself.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Tullia has made her request. She wants Aemilia to intervene for a temporary pause in collection while the obligation is examined, not to forgive the debt. Pia remains present. Lucius is away.
