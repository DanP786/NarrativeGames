# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening.
- Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from her eighteenth birthday. Lucius is approximately thirty.
- At an elite gathering, Aemilia and Lucius pursue a political/patronage opportunity cultivated over several years.
- Marcus Calpurnius Varro has introduced them to Titus Claudius Marcellus, a reserved equestrian financier and estate creditor whom Lucius has wanted direct access to for years.
- Aemilia used modest practical competence about estate accounts to make Marcellus comfortable, giving Lucius a natural opening into the substantive conversation.
- Once Lucius and Marcellus begin discussing estates, debts and financing, Aemilia follows the discipline Lucius taught her: she listens closely but offers no substantive political or financial advice publicly. Her silence protects both Lucius' standing and the usefulness of her private influence.
- Lucius uses the opening to test Marcellus about distressed estates around Capua without yet revealing his full purpose. Marcellus responds cautiously but begins supplying enough detail to confirm that Lucius' interest is well founded.
- Marcellus notices that Aemilia continues to follow the conversation despite not inserting herself into it. He does not yet know how much of what she hears will later be discussed privately with Lucius.
