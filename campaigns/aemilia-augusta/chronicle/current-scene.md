# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, more than a week after Aemilia's pregnancy loss; exact day count remains loose. Aemilia is eighteen, at the Valerian domus in Rome. It is the afternoon of the second day of Aemilia's return to household duties.
- Earlier events remain active canon: Eros's disclosure caused a severe marital argument; Lucius twice physically stopped Aemilia leaving and the second restraint broke his explicit promise not to stop her again. He later admitted **“I was wrong.”** The marital breach remains unresolved.
- Aemilia lost her approximately four-month pregnancy after hours of pain and bleeding in the shared bedchamber despite attendance by Demetrios and the trusted midwife. No medical cause was established. She then remained unconscious for about a day from severe weakness/blood loss before waking.
- Pia failed to return from her first genuine day of leave and remains absent. Her reason and whereabouts/fate remain unestablished.
- After grief-driven withdrawal, Cornelia's visit and a second withdrawal after sending her mother home, Aemilia returned to household duties. She now works in a changed manner: controlled and soft-voiced but without her former warmth, dimpling smiles or instinct to cushion commands. This reserve applies broadly to everyone, including Eros.
- Aemilia remains physically weakened after major blood loss, prolonged inactivity and limited eating. She is now on her second day back at household work. No fever, renewed hemorrhage or new medical complication is established.
- Earlier at midday Terentia, Lucius's stepmother and matron of the elder Messalla household, visited. During the visit Aemilia disclosed the unresolved marital breach involving Eros and Lucius, including Lucius physically stopping her twice and breaking his promise not to stop her again. Terentia did not establish a causal link between the argument/restraint and the later miscarriage, but took the broken promise and physical restraint seriously.
- Terentia advised that Aemilia need not punish Eros or her children, but mercy does not require confidence or warmth; Lucius remains responsible for his own choices. Aemilia has already told Lucius what hurt her and need not repeatedly chase him for acknowledgment. If the marriage is to recover, Lucius must eventually choose to address the breach rather than allow ordinary married life to erase it.
- Aemilia changed the subject to Valeria Tertia. Valeria is well, remains unmarried and unaware that her parents are considering Gaius Vibius Rufus as a possible husband. Marcus is investigating Rufus as a dynastic prospect while Terentia scrutinizes his temperament and motives. Terentia is cautiously favorable. No engagement has been made.
- As Terentia prepared to leave, she invited Aemilia to visit the elder Messalla household when strong enough, saying Valeria would be glad to see her. Aemilia took Terentia's offered hand and said, **“I would like that.”** Terentia departed without reopening the marital dispute.
- After Terentia left, Aemilia instinctively reached to stroke her stomach, caught herself when she realized the old pregnancy habit, and stopped the gesture. She then turned back to her household work.
- Later in the afternoon Aemilia went to find the household steward herself rather than summoning him. She asked, **“Is there any word on Pia?”** The steward said there was still no confirmed word: Pia has neither returned nor sent a message that has reached the household. Pia remains missing; no sightings, witnesses, search result, reason or fate have been established. When asked if she wanted anything further done, Aemilia said, **“No I… just hope she is safe.”**
- On trying to return from the working part of the domus, Aemilia reaches a set of steps and discovers she is too weak to climb them. She stops, leans against the wall, and tells a concerned nearby worker, **“I’m just resting.”** Although not sufficiently recovered, she pushes herself to climb anyway. After several steps her legs shake, breathing becomes hard and one knee nearly folds.
- Aemilia tells the worker, **“Get Lucius.”** While the summons is carried, she rests her head against the hand braced on the wall, closes her eyes and deliberately breathes slowly. Lucius arrives promptly, dismisses unnecessary onlookers, comes close without seizing her and asks permission to carry her.
- In answer, Aemilia reaches one hand toward Lucius but does not move her feet because she fears falling. Lucius takes the offered hand, warns her before touching further, and lifts her securely from the steps rather than making her attempt another step.
- While Lucius carries her upward, Aemilia voluntarily rests her head against his chest, says nothing and closes her eyes in the warmth of being held. This is the first voluntary physical closeness she has shown him in the immediate post-loss period after the unresolved marital breach. Lucius notices the gesture but does not treat it as resolution or press for discussion.
- Aemilia gives Lucius no instruction about where to take her. Remembering her visible distress around the bed where the pregnancy loss occurred, Lucius carries her to a nearby private sitting room with a couch instead, has the room cleared, and lowers her carefully onto the couch. He tells her she is not returning to the accounts today and asks whether she wants him to stay.
- Before Lucius can fully withdraw his hand after settling her, Aemilia grabs it. She does not answer verbally. Lucius takes the gesture as a request for his presence, not as forgiveness or resolution. He stays beside the couch, lets her keep his hand, and closes his fingers gently around hers without pressing her to speak.
