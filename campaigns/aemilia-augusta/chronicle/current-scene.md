# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, more than a week after Aemilia's pregnancy loss; exact day count remains loose. Aemilia is eighteen, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Earlier events remain active canon: Eros's disclosure caused a severe marital argument; Lucius twice physically stopped Aemilia leaving and the second restraint broke his explicit promise not to stop her again. He later admitted **“I was wrong.”** The marital breach remains unresolved.
- Aemilia lost her approximately four-month pregnancy after hours of pain and bleeding despite attendance by Demetrios and the trusted midwife. No medical cause was established. She then remained unconscious for about a day from severe weakness/blood loss before waking.
- Pia failed to return from her first genuine day of leave and remains absent. Her reason and whereabouts/fate remain unestablished. Aemilia experiences Pia's disappearance as the loss of her only safety net. Any search and its results remain unestablished unless played.
- Cornelia stayed for several days and briefly helped Aemilia begin sitting upright and eating a little. After Aemilia disclosed the Eros/Lucius conflict and felt Cornelia was also explaining/excusing Eros because she was enslaved, Aemilia sent Cornelia home and withdrew again into silence and isolation.
- After a further period of withdrawal, Aemilia eventually rises of her own accord. She dresses herself, abandons an unsuccessful attempt at her usual hair arrangement, and simply pins a few front strands behind her head. She then leaves the bedchamber and resumes household duties neglected during the crisis and recovery.
- Aemilia works, but her manner has changed. The warmth and easy softness that previously accompanied her household authority are absent. Her dimples do not show. She does not soften instructions to make them easier to receive, although her voice remains controlled and does not become shouting, cruelty or theatrical severity.
- The change is visible to household staff because it alters the social texture of familiar authority: Aemilia listens, decides and gives answers without the small smiles, reassurance or conversational warmth that once cushioned commands. Staff are not assumed to know why beyond the obvious facts that their mistress has recently lost a pregnancy, been gravely ill/weak, and withdrawn from household life.
- Aemilia's return is functional rather than healed. She applies herself to accumulated routine domestic work—stores, linens, work assignments, household requests and records—without assuming every matter is unresolved or that the house ceased operating in her absence.
- Aemilia's previously approved one-month urban-domus discipline trial without punitive striking, with modest rewards/records, remains active background canon, but its exact interim administration during her withdrawal is unestablished and may require review when encountered.
- Aemilia remains physically weakened from major blood loss, prolonged inactivity and limited eating. Sustained work is possible but taxing. No fever, renewed hemorrhage or other new medical complication is established.
- Her simpler hair and subdued manner make Pia's absence materially visible in Aemilia's routine. Pia remains missing; reason, whereabouts/fate and any search/results remain unestablished unless played.
- Lucius has not yet been brought back into direct conversation after Cornelia's departure and does not know the exact conversation between Aemilia and Cornelia unless told. Whether he observes Aemilia's changed household manner remains in play.
- The pregnancy is over. Exact sex/appearance and handling of the lost child remain unestablished. The exact outcome of the Publius/Aufidius creditor deadline remains unestablished.
