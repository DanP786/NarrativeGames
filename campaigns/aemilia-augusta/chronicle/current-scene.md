# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is Aemilia's eighteenth birthday, late evening, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Aemilia's large birthday gathering has been a genuine social success, bringing together her Aemilian family, Lucius' Valerian family, friends, Lucius' colleagues and other important guests. Aemilia has had a wonderful evening and has been visibly happy and confident as hostess.
- As the gathering winds down, Lucius teasingly asks what the mistress of the house intends to do with the remainder of her birthday. Aemilia privately decides that she wants a bath. She has not yet said this aloud, so Lucius does not know her thought or react to it.
- Household slaves continue clearing the feast and seeing off the remaining guests. A bath can be prepared in the household as an ordinary end to a long, crowded evening; no further intention is assumed until Aemilia expresses or acts on it.
- Lucius' promises are now in effect: from this birthday onward he will not seek sexual access to Livia or any other women, and he and Aemilia intend to try for legitimate children together.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
