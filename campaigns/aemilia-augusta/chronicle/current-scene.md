# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Lucius has told Aemilia plainly that he loves her. Aemilia then looks down into her cup, shifts uneasily and says, 'And you still seek Livia's company.'
- This is the first time Aemilia voices the jealousy she has previously tried to suppress through prayer and self-discipline. Her discomfort is observable; the exact private history of those prayers remains unknown to Lucius unless she tells him.
- Lucius does not shame her for jealousy or pretend Roman custom makes the feeling meaningless. He acknowledges that he has continued to seek Livia sexually during the years in which he refused to involve Aemilia while she remained a child, and that his freedom to do so existed because of the unequal power he held over both women in different ways.
- Lucius makes no claim that Livia freely chose the arrangement and does not romanticize it. He recognizes that Aemilia is asking whether his conduct will continue once their marriage changes, and asks her to state what she wants from him rather than forcing her to hide the question behind household convention.
