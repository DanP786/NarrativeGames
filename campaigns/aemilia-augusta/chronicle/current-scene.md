- Aemilia now explains further: **“It was the mother of two of his children. She told him everything we had discussed in a meeting. I wanted to improve the lives of the slaves here. Cornelia therefore knows Lucius would not let Aemilia leave but not yet exactly how he prevented her.

The temporal sequence—argument/restraint, pain beginning, later bleeding and loss—is real canon, but causation cannot be asserted. Cornelia may react strongly to the sequence while distinguishing what she knows from what she fears.
