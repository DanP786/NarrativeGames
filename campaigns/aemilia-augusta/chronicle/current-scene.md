# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after S01. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant. The pregnancy is more visibly apparent but no complication is established.
- Morning at Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus in Rome. Lucius is away from the domus.
- Earlier this morning Aemilia received Tullia regarding Publius, a freedman of Tullia's husband who owes Lucius's household money. Aemilia promised only to bring the matter to Lucius, then independently checked the household records.
- The Valerian record shows Publius originally received 60,000 sesterces for workshop capital. The present 18,000 demand is an overdue scheduled payment plus accrued interest/arrears, with a substantial balance still remaining. Publius made earlier payments. Specified workshop equipment and stock are pledged. Gnaeus Fabius Priscus appears to be enforcing the security within ordinary delegated authority; the record does not show a fresh personal review by Lucius of Publius's request for more time.
- While carefully putting the Publius material back where she found it in Lucius's office, Aemilia accidentally dislodges a narrow wax tablet packet that had been tucked behind a group of ordinary account records rather than filed with them. It falls partly open when it strikes the writing table. Aemilia does not deliberately break a seal or force a locked container.
- What is immediately visible is plainly not an ordinary household account. The outer notation is in Lucius's hand and marks the material as private. A visible line names Marcus Aemilius Lepidus, Aemilia's father, alongside a reference to inquiries made concerning Aemilia's marriage and the disposition of her dowry in the period before her eighteenth birthday. Another visible fragment refers to Lucius having obtained copies or reports without Marcus Aemilius knowing that Lucius possessed them.
- The accidental glimpse establishes only that Lucius privately collected information concerning Aemilia's father, her marriage, and her dowry before she turned eighteen, and that the material was sensitive enough to keep separate from ordinary accounts. It does NOT yet establish what Marcus Aemilius intended, what Lucius concluded, whether wrongdoing occurred, or the full contents of the packet.
- Aemilia now faces a genuine boundary: she can close and replace the packet after the accidental glimpse, deliberately read further, or confront/ask Lucius about it when he returns. Pia is nearby but has not been established as having read the writing.
- CURRENT MOMENT: In Lucius's office, the Publius accounts have been put away. A private packet accidentally dislodged from behind the records lies partly open before Aemilia, with enough visible text to connect her father, her marriage, and her dowry to secret inquiries made before she turned eighteen. Lucius remains away.
