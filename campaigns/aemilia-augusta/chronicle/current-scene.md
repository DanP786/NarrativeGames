# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after Aemilia and Lucius' late-night conversation and around the period of her father's renewed inquiries into the childless marriage. Aemilia remains seventeen, closer to her eighteenth birthday.
- Daytime in the courtyard of the Valerian domus. Aemilia watches the three surviving children Lucius fathered by adult enslaved women during the years he postponed the adult terms of their marriage: two boys and one girl.
- Valeria, approximately four and the daughter of Livia, is the girl. The two surviving boys are now established as Lucius' sons by adult enslaved women of the household; their names, exact ages, mothers and individual personalities have not yet been established and should emerge in play rather than be retroactively assumed.
- The children play together under household supervision. Their presence carries the familiar contradiction for Aemilia: she knows and cares for them as children of the household, while they also embody Lucius' already-lived experience of fatherhood and the motherhood she has told him she wants for herself.
- Aemilia remains seventeen; no sexual or erotic content occurs.
