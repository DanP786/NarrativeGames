# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Time has advanced: Aemilia is now approximately three months pregnant. The pregnancy is progressing without an established complication.
- Aemilia and Lucius have still avoided a formal public announcement, though the Valerian household has effectively inferred the pregnancy from changed routines and Aemilia's increasingly noticeable physical changes. Pia remains the only servant formally entrusted with the secret.
- An invitation arrives from Lucius's elder brother Marcus Valerius Messalla Minor and his wife Servilia for the birthday gathering of one of their sons. Their sons are Marcus, about ten, and Lucius, about five; which boy's birthday is being celebrated has not yet been established. The gathering will bring Aemilia into close contact with Lucius's Valerian relatives, making continued concealment of a three-month pregnancy socially difficult even without an announcement.
- Aemilia's recent preference for gentler hairstyles remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck for ordinary comfort, with more elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Lucius remains faithful to his promise. The pregnancy is their hoped-for legitimate child, though its outcome cannot be guaranteed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
