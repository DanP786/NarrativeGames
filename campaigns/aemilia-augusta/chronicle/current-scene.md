# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC. Roughly a week has passed at the Valerian country villa since Aemilia and Lucius first resumed marital intimacy; the pregnancy loss is now more than two weeks in the past, though exact day count remains loose. Aemilia is eighteen.
- The severe marital breach remains canon: Lucius twice physically stopped Aemilia leaving an argument and the second restraint broke his explicit promise not to do so again. He later admitted **“I was wrong.”** The breach is actively being repaired but is not erased.
- During the week at the villa, Aemilia consistently welcomes Lucius's touch and takes comfort in his devotion. She talks freely with him and initiates or returns affectionate touch. She does not yet smile freely and does not laugh. Lucius does not demand those signs as proof of recovery and does not treat renewed physical closeness as erasing grief or the breach.
- Their adult marital intimacy has resumed voluntarily. No new pregnancy is established.
- Aemilia remains in physical recovery from severe blood loss and prolonged weakness. The quiet week improves her rest and endurance, but she is not established as fully restored to her former strength. No fever, renewed hemorrhage or new medical complication is established.
- Pia remains missing. Before the villa retreat Aemilia ordered a discreet information-only inquiry seeking sightings and information about injury, detention or other circumstances without automatically ordering Pia seized or punished if found.
- During the week in Rome, that inquiry produces its first uncertain lead: a person questioned along the route associated with Pia's leave recalls a woman broadly matching Pia's description in the company of unfamiliar men and remembers the encounter because the woman did not appear to be moving with them freely. The witness cannot establish Pia's identity with certainty and cannot say where the group ultimately went. This is a lead, not proof, and the household has not yet concluded that Pia was abducted or sold.
- OOC GM canon remains: Pia did not deliberately flee Aemilia. During genuine leave she was unlawfully seized and subsequently held/sold onward as enslaved property despite already belonging to the Valerian household. Aemilia, Lucius and the household do not know this hidden truth; it must be uncovered through plausible investigation.

- Eros remains in Rome and no longer has discretionary direct/private access to Lucius; necessary child or household matters pass through ordinary channels or occur with others present. The restriction has not lapsed during the villa week.
- Household social knowledge remains distinct from private fact. The full marital breach is known only to Aemilia, Lucius, Cornelia and Terentia in differing detail. Household members can observe Aemilia and Lucius returning together and functioning as husband and wife, but cannot legitimately infer that grief or the marriage is completely healed.
- The prior villa conversations remain canon: Aemilia and Lucius discussed authority, obedience, sexual autonomy, Eros, the old Scipio marriage prospect, Aemilia's childhood willingness to become Lucius's wife, and Lucius's distinction between long-growing affection and adult sexual desire. Aemilia told him she was glad he rather than Scipio became her husband.

- On arrival Lucius remains attentive without publicly fussing over Aemilia. The household receives its domina back with the visible knowledge that she left in grief and weakness and now returns still subdued but physically closer to her husband. Aemilia's lack of free smiles/laughter remains noticeable to those who knew her former manner.
