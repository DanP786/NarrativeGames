# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after S01. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant. The pregnancy is more visibly apparent but no complication is established.
- Morning at Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus in Rome. The discreet Rufus-Valeria gathering is now several weeks past. Terentia remains cautiously favorable; Valeria and Rufus remain unaware of the matchmaking purpose; Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told. Rufus's Atalanta-poem search remains unresolved.
- Aemilia's standing within the household has continued to consolidate. Her pregnancy makes her dynastic importance more visible to visitors and dependents as well as to the family.
- Pia is attending Aemilia during the morning routine when a household servant brings word that an elite married woman of respectable standing has arrived and specifically asks to speak with Aemilia rather than Lucius. She is not one of Aemilia's established close relatives. The visitor says the matter concerns a household obligation and asks for a private audience.
- The request is unusual enough to matter: someone outside the immediate family has deliberately identified Aemilia as a person through whom something might be accomplished. No details of the requested favor have yet been established, and Aemilia has not decided whether to receive the woman privately, receive her with witnesses, ask her business first, or refer her to Lucius.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia is in her morning rooms with Pia. A servant waits for her answer regarding the unexpected female petitioner below.
