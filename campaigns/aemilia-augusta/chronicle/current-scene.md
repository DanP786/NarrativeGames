# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after Aemilia and Lucius' late-night conversation and around the period of her father's renewed inquiries into the childless marriage. Aemilia remains seventeen, closer to her eighteenth birthday.
- Daytime in the courtyard of the Valerian domus. Aemilia and Lucius watch his three surviving children play nearby under household supervision.
- Their conversation has turned to the growth of Valerian wealth since the marriage. Lucius has acknowledged Aemilia's dowry, connections and increasing usefulness as an observant political wife while emphasizing estates, credit, obligations, patronage, information and calculated risk.
- Facing Lucius and fanning herself, Aemilia begins making a case for her own material contribution. She points to several lesser transactions and opportunities over the preceding years in which her private observations or social handling materially helped Lucius: occasions when she noticed a useful inconsistency in household or estate information, drew a socially useful man into greater openness through charm and apparently harmless conversation, or recognized which introduction or relationship Lucius could profitably pursue. These examples establish a pattern of real indirect contribution without retroactively assigning her sole control of major transactions beyond her novice Politics & Patronage and adept household skills.
- Lucius does not dismiss the examples. He distinguishes between originating useful intelligence or access and actually completing a Roman elite transaction through capital, legal authority, negotiation and enforcement. He recognizes that some gains would not have existed in the same form without Aemilia's contribution, while resisting the claim that any substantial deal was literally hers alone.
- The three surviving children remain nearby: Valeria, approximately four, and two boys whose names, exact ages, mothers and individual personalities remain unestablished. Aemilia remains seventeen; no sexual or erotic content occurs.
