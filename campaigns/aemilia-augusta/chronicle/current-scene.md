# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. Enough time has passed since Aemilia's first missed expected menstrual cycle for a trusted midwife to assess her after continued absence of her courses; exact gestational dating remains uncertain by ancient standards.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage is established. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children.
- The household's mock gladiatorial games have become a recurring recreational tradition: children may participate or preside, household staff may watch and cheer when duties permit, and strict rules prevent deliberate injury or imitation of lethal outcomes.
- On a deliberately quiet day, Demetrios arrives privately with a trusted experienced midwife. Lucius is present with Aemilia; Pia is the only servant Aemilia has trusted with knowledge of her suspected pregnancy. No wider announcement has been made.
- After history and a discreet non-graphic examination, the midwife finds continued missed courses, bodily changes and examination signs strongly consistent with pregnancy. She tells Aemilia that she believes Aemilia is carrying a child, while Demetrios cautions that ancient medicine cannot give absolute early certainty or guarantee the outcome.
- Aemilia beams at Lucius on hearing the midwife's judgment. Lucius's composure gives way to visible joy and tenderness; he takes Aemilia's hand and openly acknowledges that this is the child they hoped to make together. Pia and the medical attendants witness the private moment.
- The pregnancy is now treated within this trusted circle as strongly established by the best available contemporary evidence, though its outcome remains uncertain. It has not yet been announced to the household, children or extended families.
- The household's practical health routines based on Demetrios' earlier advice remain in force.
