# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is seventeen; exactly 34 days remain until her eighteenth birthday. Later the same day at the villa of Lucius' father in Rome.
- Lucius and Aemilia stand before Lucius' father after his ultimatum that Lucius explain the nearly six-year absence of legitimate issue or face familial/dynastic exclusion. The elder Valerian has presumed Aemilia barren, called her defective/useless, and demanded divorce and remarriage.
- Before Lucius answers the ultimatum, Aemilia gives him one minute nod. Lucius reads the gesture as permission to disclose the private fact necessary to clear her of the accusation; he does not treat it as permission to expose unnecessary intimate detail.
- Lucius tells his father plainly that Aemilia has never been pregnant because he deliberately chose not to attempt to father a child with her while she was still a child. He takes responsibility for the nearly six years without legitimate issue and states that the decision was his, not evidence of infertility or physical defect in Aemilia.
- Lucius does not describe sexual acts or Aemilia's body. He explains the decision in dynastic terms his father can understand: pregnancy and childbirth posed grave risks to a very young wife, and he judged that preserving his wife until greater maturity was preferable to risking her life for an earlier heir. Aemilia remains seventeen and no sexual or erotic content occurs.
- The elder Valerian now knows the central secret previously concealed from both families: the marriage has not produced children because Lucius intentionally delayed attempting conception while Aemilia was a child. His reaction to that disclosure remains unresolved.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
