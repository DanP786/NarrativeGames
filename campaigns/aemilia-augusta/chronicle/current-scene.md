# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Aemilia and Lucius are discussing what her approaching adulthood will mean for their privately unconsummated marriage.
- After asking what will happen to Livia, the enslaved mother of Lucius' daughter Valeria, Aemilia asks Lucius directly, 'Do you love her?'
- Lucius answers that he does not love Livia as he understands love between himself and Aemilia. He acknowledges familiarity, responsibility toward Livia, and affection for Valeria, while refusing to turn his ownership of Livia or their sexual history into a romantic story.
- Lucius recognizes that Aemilia's question is personal rather than merely administrative, but he does not presume to know whether it comes from jealousy, fear, curiosity or some combination unless Aemilia chooses to say so.
