# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after S01. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant. The pregnancy is more visibly apparent but no complication is established.
- Morning at Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus in Rome. Lucius is away from the domus. The discreet Rufus-Valeria gathering is now several weeks past. Terentia remains cautiously favorable; Valeria and Rufus remain unaware of the matchmaking purpose; Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told. Rufus's Atalanta-poem search remains unresolved.
- Aemilia receives Tullia in the tablinum with Pia attending. Tullia is an adult married Roman woman in her thirties from a respectable senatorial-connected family, known to Aemilia by sight from elite social and religious occasions but not as an intimate. Tullia's own attendant remains near the doorway.
- Tullia explains that one of her husband's freedmen, Publius, has been pressed for payment on an obligation by a Valerian household agent. The debt itself is not denied. The agent has threatened to seize pledged workshop equipment and stock if payment is not produced promptly. Publius and his wife say this would prevent the workshop from continuing to earn.
- Aemilia promises to take the matter to Lucius when he returns, saying the household would not want a man's livelihood stripped without consideration. She does not forgive the debt, declare the agent wrong, order collection stopped herself, or accept Publius's account as proven.
- Aemilia asks for more detail. Tullia says Publius told her the payment presently demanded is 18,000 sesterces. The obligation arose from money advanced for workshop stock and operating expenses, secured in part against equipment and stock. According to Publius, his metalworking workshop has been squeezed by delayed customer payments and a costly batch of poor-quality metal, leaving a viable business without enough liquid money to meet the present demand. Tullia can vouch for the workshop's existence and Publius's long reliable service to her husband's household but not for his books or every explanation.
- Asked which Valerian agent is involved, Tullia identifies him as Gnaeus Fabius Priscus, a freeborn professional agent used by Lucius's household for some urban debts and commercial obligations. Tullia knows the name from the notice delivered to Publius and has not personally dealt with Priscus.
- Aemilia asks whether the 18,000 sesterces is the full amount owed or merely the current installment. Tullia says her understanding from Publius is that 18,000 is the amount presently fallen due, not the whole original obligation. She believes Publius described a larger balance remaining afterward, but she does not know its exact size and refuses to guess. She also does not know from firsthand inspection whether the 18,000 consists purely of scheduled principal or includes interest or arrears. The Valerian accounts can establish this precisely.
- The full original amount and exact contractual terms, remaining balance, composition of the 18,000 due, whether seizure is contractually justified, whether Publius's business account is accurate, and whether Priscus acted within Lucius's standing instructions remain unverified.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Tullia has clarified that the 18,000 sesterces is, according to Publius, the current amount due rather than the entire debt. Aemilia is methodically separating what Tullia knows from what must be checked against Lucius's records.
