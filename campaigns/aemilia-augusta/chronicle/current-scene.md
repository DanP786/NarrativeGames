# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, morning. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Publius workshop crisis active. Figures: Aufidius 12,800; other pressing outside debts 6,200 and 3,600; Valerian due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600. Receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. Forced liquidation likely destroys value/may not cover Valerian claim. Lucius leans controlled rescue but has not executed it; Aufidius standstill on two seized enslaved craftsmen expires today.
- Pia's first genuine weekly day free ordinary labor is today. Another enslaved household woman dressed Aemilia/did hair; usual clothing, visibly different hair. Pia genuinely off ordinary duties. Steward meeting remains due later.
- Aemilia's father Marcus Aemilius Lepidus, 49, senator/former praetor, arrived uninvited/without prior message. Previously pressured Aemilia to end marriage and privately explored dowry/remarriage contingencies during long childless period.
- Marcus explicitly reverses prior course: pregnancy removes central childlessness premise; marriage should continue; he will not pursue/encourage dissolution or another match on old grounds. He admits discounting Aemilia's judgment and says hers proved better. Aemilia says she is happy in Valerian household; Marcus accepts this.
- Lucius arrives and asserts Aemilia's place as his wife is not Marcus's to grant/withdraw. Aemilia strategically remains silent during husband/father authority exchange. Marcus concedes he pressed beyond justified facts and is not here to reclaim her, demand dowry, arrange another husband, or reopen dissolution.
- Marcus raises pregnancy/confinement. Cornelia wants involvement; Marcus wants clarity about midwife, physician and preparations. No demand to move Aemilia has been made.
- Aemilia asks whether Cornelia wants to be at the birth. Marcus says yes, if Aemilia will have her. Cornelia wants to be present during confinement/nearby in labor, understands childbirth's dangers from having borne children, and would have come today had Marcus not insisted on first settling his private business. Marcus says Cornelia will ask Aemilia herself.
- Lucius says he has no objection to Cornelia being present provided Aemilia wants her.
- Aemilia visibly softens at Lucius leaving the choice to her and teases him affectionately in front of Marcus: 'You may regret saying that, my love.' The endearment openly displays the warmth of the marriage Marcus had previously underestimated.
- Lucius answers with dry humor rather than retreating. He says he expects Cornelia may reorganize half the household and interrogate Demetrios, but he would rather endure an overbearing mother-in-law than have Aemilia frightened or deprived of someone she wants during confinement. He retains authority over actual household arrangements and makes clear Cornelia will not command his domus unchecked.
- Marcus is dryly amused by the warning and confirms Cornelia is likely to be formidable once admitted. He does not object to Aemilia's affectionate address to Lucius; it reinforces the reality of the marriage he has just agreed to accept.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia, Marcus and Lucius remain together. Cornelia's possible presence at confinement is welcomed in principle if Aemilia wants it, with details/boundaries not yet fixed. Marcus still wants to know what midwife/physician arrangements already exist. Publius deadline active; steward meeting later; Pia off duty.
