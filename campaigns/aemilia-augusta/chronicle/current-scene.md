# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. Enough time has passed since Aemilia's first missed expected menstrual cycle for a trusted midwife to assess her after continued absence of her courses; exact gestational dating remains uncertain by ancient standards.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage is established. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children.
- On a deliberately quiet day, Demetrios arrives privately with a trusted experienced midwife. Lucius is present with Aemilia; Pia is the only servant Aemilia has trusted with knowledge of her suspected pregnancy. No wider announcement has been made.
- After history and a discreet non-graphic examination, the midwife finds continued missed courses, bodily changes and examination signs strongly consistent with pregnancy. The pregnancy is now treated within this trusted circle as strongly established by the best available contemporary evidence, though its outcome remains uncertain.
- Aemilia asks for comprehensive guidance. The midwife and Demetrios advise nourishing food and good water, moderate ordinary movement, rest as needed, avoiding falls/violent exertion/needless exposure to illness, caution with strong remedies, and urgent attention for significant bleeding, severe persistent pain, fever, fainting or inability to keep down food and water. Household cleanliness measures remain especially important around eventual childbirth.
- Lucius asks specifically about marital intimacy. The midwife says that in an uncomplicated pregnancy with no bleeding, pain or other warning signs she sees no reason for husband and wife to abstain entirely; gentleness, Aemilia's comfort and stopping if anything causes pain or bleeding are the practical rules. If concerning symptoms develop, they should abstain and summon her or Demetrios. Demetrios agrees while noting that ancient medical opinions differ and certainty is impossible.
- The pregnancy has not yet been announced to the household, children or extended families.
