# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is seventeen; she now establishes that exactly 34 days remain until her eighteenth birthday. Daytime in the courtyard of the Valerian domus; Lucius' three surviving children play nearby under household supervision.
- Aemilia has made a deliberate case that her observations, intelligence and social handling materially contributed to some profitable Valerian opportunities. Lucius accepts the contribution but distinguishes it from his capital, legal authority, negotiation and enforcement, then jokes that his 'expensive wife' should name the payment she wants.
- Aemilia reacts with mock insult to 'expensive' and says, 'There is only one price I ask, and we are 34 days away.' In context Lucius understands this as a reference to her eighteenth birthday and the promises already made concerning the adult phase of their marriage: fidelity from that birthday onward and their intention to try for legitimate children together once she is an adult.
- Lucius does not sexualize the exchange or renegotiate the promise. He treats the exact count as characteristic of Aemilia, confirms that he remembers what he promised, and makes clear that wealth or her usefulness did not purchase that promise.
- The three children remain nearby. Valeria is approximately four; the two boys' names, exact ages, mothers and individual personalities remain unestablished. No sexual or erotic content occurs.
