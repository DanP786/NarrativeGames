# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after S01. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant. The pregnancy is more visibly apparent but no complication is established.
- Morning at Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus in Rome. Lucius is away from the domus. The discreet Rufus-Valeria gathering is now several weeks past. Terentia remains cautiously favorable; Valeria and Rufus remain unaware of the matchmaking purpose; Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told. Rufus's Atalanta-poem search remains unresolved.
- Aemilia's standing within the household has continued to consolidate. Her pregnancy makes her dynastic importance more visible to visitors and dependents as well as to the family.
- An elite married woman of respectable standing, not one of Aemilia's established close relatives, has arrived and specifically asked to speak with Aemilia rather than Lucius. She says the matter concerns a household obligation and asks for a private audience.
- Aemilia orders that the visitor be settled in the tablinum while she finishes dressing. Pia accompanies her as attendant.
- Aemilia enters the tablinum. The visitor is established as Tullia, an adult married Roman woman in her thirties from a respectable senatorial-connected family, known to Aemilia by sight from elite social and religious occasions but not as an intimate. She is dressed well but soberly and has brought no conspicuous entourage into the room; her own attendant remains near the doorway.
- Tullia rises when Aemilia enters and greets her with the courtesies appropriate to Aemilia's rank and household position. Her eyes briefly register Aemilia's now-visible pregnancy before returning to her face; she does not comment on it.
- Pia remains behind and slightly to Aemilia's side in an attendant's position. The tablinum setting makes the audience private enough for discretion while still visibly part of formal household business.
- Tullia has not yet stated the substance of her request. After the formal greeting, she says she is grateful Aemilia agreed to receive her and that she came because the matter is one she would rather place before 'the mistress of this house' before involving either woman's husband.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia has entered and received Tullia in the tablinum. Tullia is standing before her and has just explained why she sought Aemilia first, but has not yet disclosed what the household obligation is.
