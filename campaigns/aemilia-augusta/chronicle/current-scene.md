- Publius workshop crisis active. Figures: Aufidius 12,800; other pressing outside debts 6,200 and 3,600; Valerian due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600. Receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. Forced liquidation likely destroys value/may not cover Valerian claim. - Pia's first genuine weekly day free ordinary labor is today. Another enslaved household woman dressed Aemilia/did hair; usual clothing, visibly different hair. Pia genuinely off ordinary duties.
- Marcus Aemilius Lepidus's unannounced visit has concluded. He withdrew prior pressure to end Aemilia's marriage, accepted her happiness and judgment, and agreed the marriage should continue. Aemilia and Marcus partially reconciled. Cornelia wants to be present during Aemilia's confinement/birth; Aemilia wants her there. Marcus has been told about the trusted unnamed midwife and Demetrios.
- After Marcus leaves, Aemilia and Lucius share an affectionate, impulsive adult marital interlude, non-explicitly narrated. Lucius then returns toward the urgent Publius/Aufidius matter before today's standstill expires.
- Aemilia conducts a private household-discipline inquiry with senior steward Gaius Philargyrus. She learns physical punishment occurs several times in an ordinary month and that lesser delegated supervisors sometimes strike enslaved subordinates without reporting each incident.
- Aemilia orders a one-month trial in the urban domus under Philargyrus's immediate administration: no punitive beating or striking by anyone. Necessary immediate force to stop assault or protect someone from bodily danger remains permitted, including protection of Aemilia and her pregnancy, but may not become punishment afterward.
- Aemilia orders immediate modest rewards for competent ordinary work rather than vague future promises or rewarding exhaustion. Philargyrus will track offenses, responses and rewards so the trial can be assessed. He will inform Lucius because Lucius retains ultimate household authority; unless countermanded, implementation begins immediately.
- Pia's weekly day free ordinary duties still needs formal standing instruction; Pia remains off duty today.
- Aemilia's private conversation with Eros has ended. Eros disclosed that Lucius's past sexual access was not something she always wanted and that refusal was not meaningfully available; she prefers remaining with her children to sale/transfer but would choose freedom if she could remain near/protect them. Aemilia promised no retaliation for candor and said she and Lucius do not wish to separate Eros from Fortunata/Felix; no manumission promise was made.
- Eros identified **Menophilus**, an enslaved senior cellar/store servant, as abusing delegated authority through arbitrary striking and intimidation. She identified **Chryseros**, an enslaved stores clerk/assistant, as a plausible replacement, and **Thais**, a senior enslaved woman in laundry/linen work, as a lesser concern for favoritism and punitive manipulation of assignments, sleep and small comforts. Eros cautioned that allegations require corroboration.
- Aemilia disclosed to Eros that Lucius refused adult marital intimacy with Aemilia until eighteen and that Aemilia had once been jealous that Eros bore his children. Their conversation sharpened the distinction between Aemilia's constrained freeborn status and Eros's enslavement.
- After dismissing Eros, Aemilia enters service/work areas she has never previously seen despite roughly six years in the household. She walks silently at first, observing storage, laundry-related work, repair, cleaning, supply handling and the hierarchy among enslaved workers.
- Aemilia continues moving freely through the service side rather than conducting a formal inspection with Philargyrus. She discovers practical details normally invisible upstairs and begins asking ordinary practical questions. Workers give cautious factual answers and often direct responsibility upward or glance toward supervisors before answering.
- Aemilia learns that undesirable tasks and late work can be assigned through delegated supervisors and that access to small comforts can function as informal rewards or penalties. This broadly corroborates the mechanism Eros described but does not independently establish abuse by Thais.
- In an open service yard, Aemilia encounters several adult male household workers splitting and stacking firewood. She pauses and watches longer than an ordinary inspection requires, and the men become plainly conscious of the pregnant domina's sustained attention.
- Aemilia realizes she has been distracted by the physical sight of the adult men's muscles working during the strenuous labor. She blushes when one respectfully addresses her, smiles without explanation, and moves on rather than prolonging the moment.
- The workers do not presume flirtation or invitation from the mistress. Her attention is socially consequential because of the hierarchy, so they return to work rather than responding familiarly. No names, ages, origins, legal histories or individual appearances for the wood-yard workers are established.
- Aemilia does not reveal Eros as a source and has not yet accused or confronted Menophilus or Thais. Menophilus, Chryseros and Thais are not yet formally identified by sight to Aemilia.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia has left the wood-splitting yard after a brief blush and smile and continues her informal exploration of the service side of the domus. Lucius remains occupied with Publius/Aufidius; Pia remains off duty.
