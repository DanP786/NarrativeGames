# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, a difficult conversation about fidelity, children, jealousy and the costs of Lucius' decision to postpone the adult terms of their marriage has eased into familiar private teasing.
- After Lucius jokes that his household authority may barely survive Aemilia's demands and light slap to his arm, Aemilia gives him an exaggerated false laugh, rolls her eyes and says, 'Good night, husband.'
- Lucius accepts the dismissal with dry amusement rather than prolonging the exchange. He answers, 'Good night, wife,' and lets her leave the study without adding another demand or argument.
- The conversation closes with two concrete promises now established for Aemilia's adulthood: Lucius will be faithful to her from her eighteenth birthday onward, and they will try to have children together. Livia and Lucius' existing children remain protected members of the household.
- Aemilia remains seventeen; no sexual contact or erotic conduct occurs.
