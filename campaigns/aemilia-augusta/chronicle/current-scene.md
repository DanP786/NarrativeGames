# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is later in the morning after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Aemilia's birthday gathering was a genuine social success. After the final guest departed, she invited Lucius to share a prepared private bath. Their intimacy continued afterward; the night itself remains undescribed rather than retroactively detailed.
- After sleeping again and then being dressed, Aemilia is freshly presentable and moves through the ordinary business of the Valerian household.
- Earlier in the morning Aemilia encountered Eros and learned that Eros' two-year-old son Felix had been feverish during the night, though somewhat improved by morning. Aemilia relieved Eros of her duties for the day so she could remain with her son. Aemilia privately prays that the fever does not become a serious illness. Felix's outcome remains unresolved.
- Later, a household attendant informs Aemilia that Lucius has received a Greek physician for a private meeting and has asked that Aemilia join them. The physician's identity, purpose and subject of consultation are not yet established in canon.
- This is the first day of Aemilia and Lucius' adult marital phase. Lucius' fidelity promise remains active, and they intend to try for legitimate children together, but no pregnancy or reproductive outcome is assumed.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
