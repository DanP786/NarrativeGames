# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Aemilia has finally voiced the cost she associates with Lucius' decision to postpone the adult terms of their marriage: he fathered seven children by enslaved women during those years, three of whom survive, while she witnessed births, deaths and grieving mothers from which she felt personally excluded.
- Lucius acknowledges that he protected Aemilia from dangerous early pregnancy but also denied her an experience she wanted while remaining free to become a father himself. He says he would still have protected twelve-year-old Aemilia from pregnancy but no longer pretends the decision cost her nothing.
- Aemilia walks directly up to Lucius and states, rather than asks, 'You will give me children.' This is the clearest statement yet that she personally wants motherhood and legitimate children with Lucius, not merely that she accepts dynastic expectation.
- Lucius accepts the desire and tells her that, once she is an adult, they will try to have children together. He does not promise that conception, survival or childbirth can be guaranteed, given what both have witnessed in the household, but he promises he will not deliberately keep that part of marriage from her after adulthood.
- Aemilia remains seventeen; the exchange concerns their future and contains no sexual contact or erotic conduct.
