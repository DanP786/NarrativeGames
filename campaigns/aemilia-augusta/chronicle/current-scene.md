# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is seventeen; exactly 34 days remain until her eighteenth birthday. Later the same day at the villa of Lucius' father in Rome.
- Lucius and Aemilia stand together before Lucius' father after answering his joint summons. The elder Valerian argues that nearly six years without legitimate issue, contrasted with Lucius' children by enslaved women, indicates Aemilia is barren. Lucius rejects that conclusion without revealing that the marriage remains unconsummated.
- Lucius' father has called Aemilia physically defective, useless if she cannot provide an heir, and urged Lucius to divorce and remarry. Lucius firmly refuses divorce and rejects the unproven premise of infertility while defending his authority over his own marriage.
- When the elder Valerian asks Aemilia herself why Lucius is determined to keep her, Aemilia answers quietly, 'I cannot speak my husband's mind.' Her answer is formally deferential and socially difficult to attack: she neither exposes the marriage's secret nor presumes to explain Lucius' motives for him.
- The elder Valerian recognizes that the answer returns the question to Lucius rather than giving him leverage over Aemilia. Lucius does not contradict her or make her perform a defense of her worth; he accepts responsibility for explaining his own decision while continuing to guard the private history of the marriage.
- The central pressure remains unresolved: the elder Valerian wants a credible explanation for nearly six years without legitimate issue, while Lucius cannot fully answer without revealing that he deliberately left the marriage unconsummated while Aemilia was a child.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
