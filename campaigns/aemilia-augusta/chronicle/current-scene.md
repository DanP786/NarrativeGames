# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. The current scene is private in Lucius' study at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Over the preceding months Aemilia has made Demetrios' practical health recommendations durable household routines involving water, food, waste, linens, washing and care around illness.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage has settled into an established private rhythm. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children together.
- Aemilia's expected monthly bleeding has failed to begin at its usual time. Pregnancy is possible but not yet firmly established; no physician or midwife has examined her.
- Aemilia tells Lucius she believes she is carrying their child. They embrace and allow themselves private happiness while agreeing not to announce anything yet. Aemilia also reminds him that she remembers Demetrios' advice about healthy pregnancy and childbirth practices.
- Smiling and half laughing, Aemilia says, 'I am not barren.' The remark carries the weight of years in which outsiders treated the childless marriage as possible evidence of her infertility even though Lucius knew they had not been attempting conception.
- Lucius knows Aemilia was never proven barren and that the earlier absence of pregnancy resulted from his deliberate refusal to attempt conception while she was a child. He receives her relief and happiness without treating the present possible pregnancy as medically certain yet.
- The private reason for the marriage's earlier childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
