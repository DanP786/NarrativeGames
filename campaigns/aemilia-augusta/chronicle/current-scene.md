# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. It is night in Aemilia and Lucius' private rooms at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Over the preceding months Aemilia has made Demetrios' practical health recommendations durable household routines involving water, food, waste, linens, washing and care around illness. The reforms require continuing supervision but are now part of the domus' ordinary management.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage has settled into an established private rhythm. Lucius has a notably frequent sexual appetite, remains faithful to his promise, and regularly seeks consensual intimacy with his adult wife. Their intimate life remains non-graphically narrated.
- Tonight Aemilia dismisses her attendants before they undress her. Alone with Lucius, she sits removing her own jewellery and undoing her elaborate hair. Lucius comes behind her, kisses her neck and begins helping loosen the outer layers and fastenings of her clothing while she continues removing hairpins.
- Aemilia tells him, 'You have not disappointed your wife.' Lucius receives the remark as intimate praise and responds with familiar dry confidence and affection rather than turning the exchange into explicit description.
- No pregnancy or reproductive outcome has yet been established. Aemilia and Lucius continue intending legitimate children together.
- The private reason for the marriage's earlier childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
