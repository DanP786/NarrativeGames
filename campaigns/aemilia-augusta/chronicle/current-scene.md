# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, morning. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Publius workshop crisis active. Figures: Aufidius 12,800; other pressing outside debts 6,200 and 3,600; Valerian due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600. Receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. Forced liquidation likely destroys value/may not cover Valerian claim. Lucius leans controlled rescue but has not executed it; Aufidius standstill on two seized enslaved craftsmen expires today.
- Pia's first genuine weekly day free ordinary labor is today. Another enslaved household woman dressed Aemilia/did hair; usual clothing, visibly different hair. Pia genuinely off ordinary duties. Steward meeting remains due later.
- Marcus Aemilius Lepidus arrived uninvited. He explicitly reversed prior pressure to end Aemilia's marriage: pregnancy removes central childlessness premise; he will not pursue dissolution/remarriage on old grounds. He admits discounting Aemilia's judgment. Aemilia says she is happy here; Marcus accepts this.
- Lucius arrives and asserts Aemilia's place as his wife is not Marcus's to grant/withdraw. Aemilia strategically stays silent during authority exchange. Marcus concedes he is not here to reclaim her, demand dowry, arrange another husband, or reopen dissolution.
- Marcus raises pregnancy/confinement. Cornelia wants involvement. Aemilia learns her mother wants to be present during confinement/nearby for labor if Aemilia will have her. Lucius agrees provided Aemilia wants her. Aemilia confirms she wants Cornelia present.
- Aemilia tells Marcus established arrangements: trusted experienced unnamed midwife is principal practical birth attendant; Demetrios is involved as physician/support. They have already advised her; Demetrios advocates particular cleanliness around childbirth. No complication currently established. Marcus is reassured but serious; Cornelia will want to meet both.
- Aemilia tells Marcus it is good he came. Marcus says he is glad too; easier would have been to bury prior words under manners, but worse. He says he will tell Cornelia Aemilia wants her at confinement and warns steward deserves notice.
- Aemilia answers, 'As will I,' smiling broadly enough that her dimples show. She rises and moves deliberately to Lucius's side. The movement is socially legible: affectionate and marital, reinforcing without speech that she has chosen her husband's household while remaining reconciled with her father.
- Lucius receives her beside him naturally rather than making a theatrical claim; a light hand at her back/waist acknowledges her place and pregnancy without possessive display. Marcus sees the gesture and the ease between them. It supplies further direct evidence that the marriage he once judged from outside has an established private warmth and partnership.
- Marcus accepts the image without renewed challenge. His leave-taking is now civil. He intends to tell Cornelia that Aemilia welcomes her presence and that birth attendants are already arranged.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia stands beside Lucius, smiling, as Marcus prepares to take his leave after partial reconciliation. Marriage pressure withdrawn; Cornelia welcomed for confinement; Demetrios/midwife arrangements communicated. Publius deadline active; steward meeting later; Pia off duty.
