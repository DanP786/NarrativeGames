# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is approximately three months pregnant. The pregnancy is progressing without an established complication.
- Aemilia and Lucius are at the Roman residence of Lucius's elder brother Marcus Valerius Messalla Minor and his wife Servilia for a birthday gathering for one of their sons. Their sons are Marcus, about ten, and Lucius, about five; which boy is celebrating remains unestablished.
- A private message has been sent to Aemilia's parents, Marcus Aemilius Lepidus and Cornelia, telling them directly of the pregnancy so they will not learn through Valerian gossip.
- Lucius's father, Marcus Valerius Messalla, has learned directly that Aemilia is about three months pregnant. The timing convinces him that Lucius truly did deliberately postpone conception until Aemilia was an adult; Marcus grudgingly acknowledges that Lucius's judgment may have been right.
- Marcus recognizes Demetrios by reputation and calls him a fanatic for his persistent advocacy of stricter household cleanliness. After Aemilia quietly reports fewer illnesses since adopting Demetrios's practices, Marcus says he will hear Demetrios out and nothing more, adding that 'your mother' will decide whether the physician is worth disrupting the household. By 'your mother' Marcus means Terentia, Lucius's stepmother; the usage bristles Lucius, whose biological mother Claudia Pulchra died when he was about ten.
- Terentia, approximately forty-five, Marcus's second wife and current matron of the elder Messalla household, joins them with a wine cup in hand after hearing her name. Seeing Aemilia's pregnancy, she asks whether this is Aemilia's first pregnancy, remarks to Lucius that it has taken six years, and tells Aemilia she will pray to the gods that the child is born. Terentia does not yet know the private reason for the six-year delay unless told.
- Aemilia changes the subject to the oleander recently planted in Marcus and Terentia's gardens. Terentia draws Aemilia away from Lucius and the men for a more private female conversation. Their exchange becomes openly affectionate and teasing. Terentia tells Aemilia she has always been her favourite, excluding Terentia's own biological children Decimus and Valeria Tertia.
- Terentia and Aemilia settle together on a couch at the edge of Servilia's gathering. The conversation turns to potential husbands for Valeria Tertia, Terentia's thirteen-year-old daughter. Aemilia suggests an unconventional prospect Terentia and Marcus may not have seriously considered: Gaius Vibius Rufus, about twenty-two, from a prosperous but non-senatorial equestrian family. Aemilia has met him repeatedly through Lucius's business and patronage network. He is building substantial wealth through Italian estates, contracts, credit and commercial connections and has become a useful contact for Lucius. His family lacks senatorial ancestry, making the match socially downward in pedigree for a Valerian daughter, but his growing fortune, practical competence and network could make him strategically useful. Terentia is intrigued rather than immediately persuaded and asks Aemilia why she thinks him suitable for Valeria personally, not merely useful to Lucius.
- Aemilia and Lucius remain physically affectionate and secure despite the temporary separation. Lucius remains faithful to his promise. The pregnancy is their hoped-for legitimate child, though its outcome cannot be guaranteed.
- Aemilia's gentler hairstyle preference remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck, with more elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
