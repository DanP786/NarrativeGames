# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, deep night. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Earlier today Marcus Aemilius Lepidus withdrew pressure to end Aemilia's marriage and partially reconciled with her. Cornelia wants to attend Aemilia's confinement/birth; Aemilia wants her there. Pia's first genuine day free ordinary duties remained intact; formal weekly instruction remains pending.
- Aemilia's one-month urban-domus discipline trial is approved conditionally by Lucius: no punitive beating/striking, protective force permitted, modest rewards, simple tracking.
- Eros privately told Lucius substantially the whole of her earlier conversation with Aemilia, including Eros's lack of meaningful refusal during Lucius's former sexual access, her wish for freedom if she can remain near/protect her children, Menophilus/Chryseros/Thais, Aemilia's former jealousy, Aemilia's statement that the only person she fears is her husband, and Eros's judgment that Aemilia is kind/sincere but sheltered and unlikely to prevail if Lucius says no.
- At bedtime this disclosure caused a severe marital argument. During it Aemilia twice tried to leave and Lucius physically stopped her twice. The second time came immediately after he explicitly promised not to stop her again: he drove the door shut and forced her back against it, avoiding her abdomen but coercively confining her. He later cleared the door and admitted **“I was wrong.”** The marital breach remains unresolved.
- Aemilia eventually became tearful and defeated, apologized only to end the argument, and developed abdominal pain strong enough to make standing difficult. She insisted she was fine and went slowly to bed.
- Later the same night Aemilia wakes with significant abdominal pain and vaginal bleeding at approximately four months pregnant. Lucius summons Demetrios and the trusted midwife. Aemilia weeps and largely cannot answer questions.
- Demetrios performs a cautious external examination and refuses to claim fetal viability from abdominal touch. The trusted midwife then examines Aemilia and the ongoing bleeding with clean hands/cloths and as much modesty as possible.
- The midwife concludes from the combination of ongoing bleeding, recurrent labor-like/cramping pain, and her physical examination that Aemilia is losing the pregnancy: the process has begun and cannot realistically be stopped with the means available to them. This is now established in play as a pregnancy loss at approximately four months.
- The midwife does **not** claim to know why it happened. No causal link is established to Aemilia's emotions, the argument, Lucius's physical restraint, ordinary activity, or any moral fault. Demetrios likewise refuses causal certainty. The earlier restraint avoided direct abdominal pressure as established, but temporal proximity may carry intense guilt without proving causation.
- The immediate medical priority becomes Aemilia's safety through the loss: monitor the amount of bleeding, keep her in bed, use clean linens/water, watch for faintness, weakness, fever, worsening hemorrhage or retained material, and allow the process to continue rather than promising it can be reversed. Ancient limitations remain severe; maternal danger is real.
- The midwife tells Aemilia plainly but gently that the pregnancy is being lost and that there is nothing Aemilia can do now to hold it inside. She emphasizes only what she can know: they must attend to Aemilia and the bleeding. She does not use modern terminology or falsely precise gestational claims.
- Lucius hears the conclusion at bedside. He is visibly devastated but does not make the moment about his guilt or demand reassurance. He does not blame Aemilia. His prior experience of four infant/early-child deaths gives him familiarity with child loss but not with witnessing Aemilia lose their first legitimate pregnancy.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Deep night. Aemilia lies in bed crying and bleeding. The trusted midwife has concluded that Aemilia is losing the pregnancy at approximately four months and that the process cannot be stopped with available means. Demetrios and the midwife are now focused on keeping Aemilia safe through the loss. The loss is established; its duration, amount of bleeding, completion, maternal complications and emotional aftermath remain unresolved.
