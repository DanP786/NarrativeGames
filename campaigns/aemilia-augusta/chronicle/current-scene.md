# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia remains approximately three months pregnant, with no established complication.
- The deliberately deniable family-and-business gathering at Lucius and Aemilia's domus has ended for the night. Terentia and thirteen-year-old Valeria Tertia attended as Aemilia's family guests; Gaius Vibius Rufus, approximately twenty-two, attended independently through Lucius's business relationship. Terentia knows Aemilia suggested Rufus as a possible husband for Valeria. Valeria and Rufus were not told they were being observed as a possible match, and Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told.
- During the visit Valeria challenged Rufus to locate an unidentified Greek poem about Atalanta once quoted by her tutor. Rufus accepted without bluffing knowledge, questioned her seriously, and said that if he finds a purchasable copy he will tell Valeria the cost before buying it because the choice of its worth should be hers. The challenge remains unresolved and creates a natural reason for future contact.
- Terentia's assessment of Rufus is favorable but cautious. Rufus's equestrian rather than senatorial status remains the principal dynastic obstacle.
- Before supper, Aemilia and Valeria spoke privately. Valeria's curiosity about Rufus became explicitly her own, though she still does not know the matchmaking context. Their conversation broadened into Aemilia's practical lessons about status, incentives, marriage, and relational power. Aemilia told Valeria that Lucius taught her to notice what people gain from interactions and to notice power in everything. She distinguished Lucius's final household authority from the practical power she possesses, and encouraged Valeria to judge a prospective husband's response when his authority is challenged. Valeria distilled the lesson as learning when and how to challenge and how to win an argument without making a husband feel defeated.
- Valeria wondered whether Lucius would teach her directly as he taught Aemilia. Aemilia promised, 'I can promise you I'll ask him.'
- Supper passed without any betrothal proposal or disclosure of the matchmaking plan. The guests departed or withdrew for the night.
- Aemilia and Lucius retire to their private rooms. Pia has already helped Aemilia out of her formal day attire before leaving the couple to their privacy. Aemilia describes the gathering as 'one of my less subtle days.' Lucius is amused and notes that Terentia plainly understood what Aemilia was doing, while Valeria had begun watching Rufus and the married couple by supper.
- Aemilia tells Lucius that Valeria wishes to learn what he taught Aemilia. Lucius agrees in principle. Aemilia candidly recounts that she told Valeria Lucius taught her to look for what people gain, notice power in ordinary relationships, distinguish formal authority from practical influence, and judge a husband partly by how he reacts when his authority is challenged. She tells him Valeria concluded that a wife must learn how to win an argument without making her husband feel defeated, and that Aemilia told Valeria Lucius would be proud. Lucius says he is.
- Aemilia remarks that Lucius's father would be furious she said such things. Lucius agrees Marcus would call it teaching Valeria to defy her husband, while Terentia in practice continues managing Marcus. Lucius remains willing to teach Valeria discreetly.
- Aemilia removes the remaining simple dress over her head while preparing for bed and asks Lucius directly, 'Am I a good wife?' The question follows their discussion of Marcus's conventional ideal of an obedient wife and therefore asks Lucius to distinguish his own judgment of Aemilia from his father's standard.
- Lucius answers without adopting Marcus's definition. He tells Aemilia that she is a good wife to him not because she never disagrees, but because she is loyal to their household, thinks seriously, tells him when she believes he is wrong, understands when an issue is his to decide, and has made herself someone whose judgment he wants before deciding. He does not pretend their marriage is formally equal, but neither does he regard passive obedience as the measure of her worth as a wife.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia and Lucius are alone in their private room at night, preparing for bed after discussing Valeria's education, Marcus's expectations, and Lucius's own judgment of Aemilia as his wife.
- Aemilia's pregnancy is openly known among close Valerian relatives. Her gentler hairstyle preference remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck, with elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
