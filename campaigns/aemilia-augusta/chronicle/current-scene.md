# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is later in the morning after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Earlier Aemilia encountered Eros and learned that Eros' two-year-old son Felix had been feverish during the night, though somewhat improved by morning. Aemilia relieved Eros of her duties for the day so she could remain with her son. Felix's outcome remains unresolved.
- A household attendant later informed Aemilia that Lucius had received a Greek physician privately and asked her to join them. Aemilia accepts the invitation and enters the meeting with Lucius and the physician, Demetrios.
- Lucius explains that Demetrios has been urging men of the senatorial elite to adopt cleaner and healthier habits intended to reduce sickness, but that his advice has largely been ignored or dismissed. Lucius has invited him privately because he intends to listen rather than dismiss him.
- Demetrios' approach is empirical but historically bounded: he has no germ theory or modern microbiology. He reasons through contemporary ideas of regimen, environment, corrupted air or water and bodily balance, while observing that practical measures involving cleaner water and vessels, waste removal, washing, clean linens and dressings, food storage, ventilation and reduced crowding around some illnesses can sometimes improve health. He does not claim these measures prevent every disease.
- Lucius has deliberately included Aemilia because practical changes to the running of the domus would intersect strongly with her household authority. Demetrios has not yet been established as knowing about Felix's fever or the private reproductive history of the marriage.
- This is the first day of Aemilia and Lucius' adult marital phase. Lucius' fidelity promise remains active, and they intend to try for legitimate children together, but no pregnancy or reproductive outcome is assumed.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; any wider consequences remain unresolved.
