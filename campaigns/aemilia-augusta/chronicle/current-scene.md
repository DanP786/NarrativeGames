# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, morning. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Publius workshop crisis active. Figures: Aufidius 12,800; other pressing outside debts 6,200 and 3,600; Valerian due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600. Receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. Forced liquidation likely destroys value/may not cover Valerian claim. Lucius leans controlled rescue but has not executed it; Aufidius standstill on two seized enslaved craftsmen expires today.
- Pia's first genuine weekly day free ordinary labor is today. Another enslaved household woman dressed Aemilia/did hair; usual clothing, visibly different hair. Pia genuinely off ordinary duties.
- Marcus Aemilius Lepidus's unannounced visit has concluded. He withdrew prior pressure to end Aemilia's marriage, accepted her happiness and judgment, and agreed the marriage should continue. Aemilia and Marcus partially reconciled. Cornelia wants to be present during Aemilia's confinement/birth; Aemilia wants her there. Marcus has been told about the trusted unnamed midwife and Demetrios.
- After Marcus leaves, Aemilia and Lucius share an affectionate, impulsive adult marital interlude, non-explicitly narrated. Lucius then returns toward the urgent Publius/Aufidius matter before today's standstill expires.
- Aemilia is conducting a private household-discipline inquiry with senior steward Gaius Philargyrus. His legal status, age, origin and detailed personal history remain unestablished.
- Philargyrus has explained that minor mistakes occur constantly; deliberate offenses include theft, falsified accounts/stores, repeated disobedience, deliberate damage, serious fighting, unauthorized absence, attempted flight/aiding flight, and serious deception. Matters requiring his direct discipline arise every few days; physical punishment occurs several times in an ordinary month. Lesser delegated superiors sometimes strike/correct people without reporting each incident.
- Aemilia and Philargyrus discuss rewards versus fear. Rewards already exist but Philargyrus regards physical punishment as one sanction for serious/repeated offenses. He acknowledges arbitrary/excessive violence produces concealment, resentment, damage, poor work and flight.
- Aemilia orders a one-month trial in the **urban domus under Philargyrus's immediate administration**: no punitive beating or striking by anyone during the trial. Immediate force remains permissible only where necessary to stop an ongoing assault, prevent immediate bodily harm, or protect Aemilia/pregnancy or another person from an immediate danger; such force is not to be used as punishment afterward.
- Aemilia orders immediate, concrete rewards for good ordinary work: rewards are to recognize doing expected duties well, not exceptional exhaustion or servants sacrificing sleep by working through the night. Philargyrus is to use modest practical rewards within household means rather than vague future promises. No automatic promise of manumission is created.
- The trial's purpose is observational: Philargyrus is to watch whether disobedience, theft, fighting, poor work, unauthorized absence or other misconduct increases, decreases or changes. Serious incidents are to be brought upward rather than answered with a beating.
- Aemilia explicitly says the experiment must not compromise the safety of her pregnancy. Philargyrus interprets this broadly as preserving ordinary guards, access control, supervision, safe food/water, escort where appropriate and immediate protective restraint/force if someone creates an actual danger. Pregnancy safety is not a pretext for routine punitive violence.
- Philargyrus accepts the instruction but notes that because Lucius is ultimate household authority and the policy changes established disciplinary practice, he will inform Lucius of Aemilia's order rather than conceal it. Unless Lucius countermanded it, Philargyrus will implement it immediately in the urban house. Aemilia has not purported to extend the order automatically to distant Valerian estates/worksites outside Philargyrus's direct administration.
- Implementation requires Philargyrus to tell delegated supervisors clearly that cuffs, blows and beatings as correction are suspended, because otherwise the order would fail below his notice. He will keep a simple record of serious offenses, responses and rewards during the month so the experiment can actually be assessed.
- Pia's weekly day free ordinary duties still needs to be formalized as a standing instruction; Pia remains off duty today.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia has issued her first substantial household-discipline experiment: one month without punitive striking in the urban domus, paired with immediate rewards for competent ordinary service and basic tracking of outcomes, while preserving necessary immediate protective force and pregnancy safety. Philargyrus has accepted implementation subject to Lucius's ultimate authority and awaits any further instructions. Lucius remains occupied with Publius/Aufidius.
