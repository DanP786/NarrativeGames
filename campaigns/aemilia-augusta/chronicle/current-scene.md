# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, the morning after Aemilia first felt repeated possible fetal movement. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- The previous day Aemilia received Tullia concerning Publius's debt and independently checked Lucius's household records. Publius originally received 60,000 sesterces for workshop capital; 18,000 is presently due as an overdue scheduled payment plus interest/arrears; earlier payments were made; workshop assets are pledged; Gnaeus Fabius Priscus appears to have been enforcing within ordinary delegated authority.
- While replacing those records, Aemilia deliberately read private tablets belonging to Lucius. They showed that during her later adolescence her father Marcus Aemilius discreetly explored the financial consequences of ending her still-childless marriage and another possible future match, while Lucius secretly monitored those efforts and withheld the matter from Aemilia. No established replacement match resulted.
- Aemilia also read premarital due-diligence reports about herself at about twelve. Lucius's annotations rejected confident predictions about a child's fertility or adult character and showed particular interest in reports that quiet Aemilia listened closely to adult political and estate discussions and remembered details.
- Aemilia restored the records. Pia did not read them. Aemilia ordered Pia, 'We never entered the study.' Pia accepted the command and knows only that Aemilia entered the study, spent time alone, and wants the fact concealed. Lucius still does not know of this concealment order.
- Aemilia confessed to Lucius that she read both sets of private reports. Lucius said she crossed a boundary and asked her not to read his private papers again, while also apologizing for withholding her father's inquiries once she was old enough to understand them. He accepted her apology and refused further punishment or humiliation.
- Aemilia explained she had already inferred that Marcus would investigate dowry recovery and remarriage because Marcus had asked her to end the marriage. Her principal concern is whether Marcus still seeks dissolution. Lucius says he has no current evidence that Marcus does, but promises to tell Aemilia if renewed efforts come to his attention.
- At supper Aemilia proposed investigating Publius's other creditors and potentially consolidating claims to preserve the productive workshop and gain control over recovery. Lucius sees possible commercial merit but wants full accounts first. He has decided Priscus is to pause collection while creditor amounts, securities, receivables, stock and earning capacity are examined. Aemilia will sit with Lucius when the figures arrive before he decides whether to restructure, consolidate, or proceed against the pledged workshop.
- During the evening Aemilia felt two unfamiliar painless flutters in her belly and interpreted them as their child. Lucius could not necessarily feel the subtle movement externally but became openly moved. Aemilia said, 'our child,' and Lucius echoed her words with unusual tenderness.
- The excitement led to affectionate kissing and mutually welcomed marital intimacy between the adult spouses; the sexual encounter remained off-page. No pain, bleeding or other complication was established.
- Afterward their conversation turned playfully to obedience and authority. Aemilia said she tries always to obey. Lucius distinguished obedience from silence or thoughtlessness: he expects her to think, advise and disagree, while generally respecting his final household authority. When she asked his next decision, he told her to eat a little more and rest, then reaffirmed she would participate in reviewing Publius's accounts. Aemilia ate several bites with conspicuous indignation; Lucius was amused without demanding cheerful compliance.
- The following morning Aemilia wakes with Lucius beside her, kissing her neck affectionately. His hand rests over her approximately four-month pregnant stomach. The contact is tender and possessive but non-explicit; he is awake before her and appears preoccupied with the place where she felt the child's first flutters the previous evening.
- In that morning closeness Lucius's possessiveness rises again. With his hand still resting over Aemilia's pregnant belly, he tells her, 'I am going to give you many children.' The statement is intimate, dynastic and hopeful rather than a literal guarantee: Lucius already knows conception, safe childbirth and surviving children cannot be commanded. His tone nevertheless carries the confidence of a husband imagining a large legitimate family with Aemilia and taking fierce pleasure in the visible proof of the first pregnancy.
- Aemilia rolls onto her back and looks up at Lucius smiling. In response to the previous day's revelations that Marcus once explored ending the marriage, Lucius speaks with unusual finality about their marriage. He says Marcus may once have considered another future for her, and other relatives may have opinions, but neither inquiry nor family preference decides the marriage now. Lucius states that he does not intend to release Aemilia from the marriage, seek another wife, or allow her to be quietly transferred into another dynastic arrangement; he understands Aemilia herself has chosen this marriage as an adult as well. He frames its permanence as their established commitment and household reality, not as a legal claim that Roman marriage is literally indissoluble.
- Lucius kisses Aemilia and tells her, 'You are mine.' In context the words are possessive and hierarchical but affectionate: they follow his insistence that others will not decide the fate of their marriage. He keeps his hand over their child and immediately treats the claim as marital belonging rather than erasing Aemilia's adult choice or independent judgment. His possessiveness is reciprocal in emotional implication—he is binding himself to the marriage as firmly as he claims her within it—even though Roman household hierarchy remains unequal.
- Aemilia giggles and wraps both arms around Lucius in response. He accepts the embrace with visible satisfaction, lowering himself carefully enough not to press against her pregnant belly and holding her close. Her spontaneous affection turns his possessive declaration into a mutually affectionate moment rather than a demand requiring a verbal answer.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Morning light fills their private room. Aemilia has answered Lucius's possessive 'You are mine' by laughing softly and wrapping her arms around him. Lucius is holding her close, pleased by the unforced response, while remaining careful of her pregnancy. The morning's household business has not yet begun on-page.
