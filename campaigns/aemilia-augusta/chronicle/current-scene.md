# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after Aemilia and Lucius' late-night conversation. Aemilia remains seventeen and is now somewhat closer to her eighteenth birthday; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Morning at the Valerian domus in Rome. Aemilia's father has followed through on his concern about the marriage's long childlessness by sending physicians to examine his daughter and determine why she has supposedly failed to conceive.
- The physicians' arrival creates an immediate threat to the central household secret: Aemilia has not conceived because Lucius has deliberately left the marriage unconsummated while she is a child, a fact concealed from both families and elite society.
- Lucius is informed before any examination takes place. He refuses to let visiting physicians simply take physical access to Aemilia on her father's authority inside the Valerian household. He receives them formally and requires them to explain what they intend to ask or do before deciding what access, if any, they will receive.
- Lucius must protect Aemilia from an unwanted or revealing examination without publicly exposing the true reason for the marriage's childlessness. Aemilia has not yet chosen how she wants to handle her father's intervention.
- Aemilia remains seventeen; no sexual or erotic content occurs.
