# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after Aemilia and Lucius' late-night conversation and around the period of her father's renewed inquiries into the childless marriage. Aemilia remains seventeen, closer to her eighteenth birthday.
- Daytime in the courtyard of the Valerian domus. Aemilia and Lucius watch his three surviving children play: two boys and Valeria, approximately four, daughter of Livia.
- After joking about Valeria learning cunning, Lucius tells Aemilia that she too had time to grow and learn, more than others expected him to give her, and that he thinks it was well spent.
- Aemilia shifts the conversation to the material fortunes of the domus, observing, 'You have amassed great wealth since your marriage.' Lucius accepts the substance but distinguishes between wealth simply acquired and wealth made useful: marriage brought Aemilia's substantial dowry and strengthened connections, while the household's later gains also came through years of estates, obligations, patronage, credit, information and calculated risks. No precise fortune is established.
- Lucius treats Aemilia's remark as potentially leading somewhere and invites her to continue rather than assuming her conclusion.
- The two surviving boys remain nearby; their names, exact ages, mothers and individual personalities are still unestablished. Aemilia remains seventeen; no sexual or erotic content occurs.
