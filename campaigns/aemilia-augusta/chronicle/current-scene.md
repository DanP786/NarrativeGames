# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, morning. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Publius workshop crisis active. Figures: Aufidius 12,800; other pressing outside debts 6,200 and 3,600; Valerian due 18,000; pressing total ~40,600. Receivables ~21,000/~12,000 promptly collectible. Forced liquidation likely destroys value/may not cover Valerian claim. Lucius leans controlled rescue but has not executed it; Aufidius standstill on two seized enslaved craftsmen expires today.
- Pia's first genuine weekly day free ordinary labor is today. Another enslaved household woman dressed Aemilia/did hair; usual clothing, visibly different hair. Pia genuinely off ordinary duties.
- Marcus Aemilius Lepidus's unannounced visit has concluded. He withdrew prior pressure to end Aemilia's marriage, accepted her happiness and judgment, and agreed the marriage should continue. Aemilia and Marcus partially reconciled. Cornelia wants to be present during Aemilia's confinement/birth; Aemilia wants her there. Marcus has been told about the trusted unnamed midwife and Demetrios.
- After Marcus leaves, Aemilia and Lucius share an affectionate, impulsive adult marital interlude, non-explicitly narrated. Lucius then returns toward the urgent Publius/Aufidius matter before today's standstill expires.
- Aemilia conducts a private household-discipline inquiry with senior steward Gaius Philargyrus. She learns physical punishment occurs several times in an ordinary month and that lesser delegated supervisors sometimes strike enslaved subordinates without reporting each incident.
- Aemilia orders a one-month trial in the urban domus under Philargyrus's immediate administration: no punitive beating or striking by anyone. Necessary immediate force to stop assault or protect someone from bodily danger remains permitted, including protection of Aemilia and her pregnancy, but may not become punishment afterward.
- Aemilia orders immediate modest rewards for competent ordinary work rather than vague future promises or rewarding exhaustion. Philargyrus will track offenses, responses and rewards so the trial can be assessed. He will inform Lucius because Lucius retains ultimate household authority; unless countermanded, implementation begins immediately.
- Pia's weekly day free ordinary duties still needs formal standing instruction; Pia remains off duty today.
- Aemilia summons Eros, adult enslaved woman and mother of Fortunata (~4) and Felix (~2) by Lucius. Their exact personal relationship has not previously been established. Eros arrives respectfully. Aemilia has her close the door and invites her to sit; Eros complies cautiously.
- Aemilia tells Eros nothing is wrong. She explains that Pia suggested she speak with a servant to understand household goings-on from below, including things Aemilia is not ordinarily privy to, and admits: 'I have accepted ignorance until now.'
- Eros is visibly cautious about the request. She understands that a domina asking for candid information about superiors, discipline and concealed household conduct places an enslaved speaker at risk if her words are repeated or interpreted as accusation. She does not assume Aemilia's good intentions eliminate that danger.
- Eros responds that there are many things mistresses and masters do not see because servants deliberately ensure they do not see them. She indicates that people learn which noises to swallow, which injuries to explain differently, which quarrels to settle before a superior arrives, and which complaints are dangerous to make.
- Eros also tells Aemilia, respectfully, that if she truly wants such knowledge, merely ordering someone to tell the truth is insufficient: an enslaved person must believe the answer will not later be used against them or another servant. This establishes Eros as cautious and perceptive without yet establishing broad trust, hostility or intimacy with Aemilia.
- Eros's exact age beyond adulthood, appearance, origin and ordinary duties remain unestablished unless they emerge naturally in play.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia and Eros sit alone behind a closed door. Aemilia has admitted her ignorance and asked, indirectly, to see the household from below. Eros has explained why servants conceal much of that world and why candid speech itself carries risk. She awaits Aemilia's response. Lucius remains occupied with Publius/Aufidius; Pia remains off duty.
