# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. It is now the morning after Aemilia told Lucius she has missed an expected menstrual cycle and believes she may be pregnant.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage is established. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children. Pregnancy is possible but not yet firmly established; no physician or midwife has examined Aemilia.
- A long-planned gladiatorial spectacle is being held in Rome today. Lucius had refused to take Aemilia to gladiatorial games while she was a child and promised that after she turned eighteen she could attend; arrangements for this outing have therefore been anticipated for a long time.
- On the morning of the event, Lucius tries to persuade Aemilia not to attend because of the newly suspected pregnancy. His concern is precautionary rather than based on evidence that merely watching the games would harm a pregnancy: the outing means crowds, heat, jostling, travel through Rome and a long public day before they know whether she is carrying a child.
- Lucius does not yet make the decision for Aemilia. He argues that the games can be attended another time and that yesterday's news changes the circumstances; Aemilia has not yet answered him.
- The household's practical health routines based on Demetrios' advice remain in force.
- The private reason for the marriage's earlier childlessness remains known to Lucius' father; the possible pregnancy has not yet been announced to either family.
