# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Several days have passed since the Valerian family birthday gathering. It is late morning at Lucius and Aemilia's domus in Rome. Aemilia remains approximately three months pregnant, with no established complication.
- The private conversation after the birthday gathering is settled between Aemilia and Lucius: Aemilia learned that during the childhood-boundary years Lucius exercised sexual access to six adult enslaved women in total, including Eros and Daphne; she distinguishes those years from their adult marriage and told him she forgives what came before. Lucius's fidelity promise from Aemilia's eighteenth birthday remains absolute, including during any period when pregnancy, childbirth, illness or medical caution prevents marital intimacy.
- At the birthday gathering Aemilia and Terentia privately discussed possible husbands for thirteen-year-old Valeria Tertia. Aemilia proposed Gaius Vibius Rufus, approximately twenty-two, a wealthy and rapidly rising equestrian from a non-senatorial family who is useful within Lucius's business and information network. Aemilia judges Rufus kind, clever, perceptive, not controlling but reluctant to concede once persuaded. Her report that he has never attended a brothel and only occasionally attends public bathhouses remains secondhand rather than independently established.
- Aemilia and Terentia agreed on a deliberately deniable way to observe Rufus. Terentia and Valeria Tertia would visit Aemilia as family while Lucius independently happened to receive Rufus. Lucius subsequently agreed to the arrangement after Aemilia argued that Rufus offers a new equestrian network of wealth, credit, contracts and information, whereas her unmarried brother Publius would largely reinforce an Aemilian alliance the Valerii already possess.
- Lucius has insisted the encounter remain exploratory: if Valeria dislikes Rufus, Terentia finds him unsuitable, or Rufus behaves as though Valeria is merely a route into the Valerian family, the possibility goes no further. Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been asked to consider Rufus and need not be convinced merely for Terentia and Valeria to visit family.
- Today the plan is carried out. Aemilia receives Terentia and Valeria Tertia as ordinary family guests in the domestic reception rooms. Terentia knows the real purpose and must manage her interest without making the meeting obvious. Valeria Tertia has not been told that Rufus is being tested as a prospective husband unless Terentia chooses to tell her during play.
- Lucius separately receives Gaius Vibius Rufus on legitimate business/social grounds. Rufus has not been told he is being observed as a possible marriage candidate. He arrives as Lucius's equestrian associate, not as a declared suitor.
- The household arrangement allows the two parties to encounter one another naturally without a formal marriage negotiation. The first shared interaction has not yet occurred; Aemilia has the opportunity to shape how the guests are brought together.
- Aemilia's pregnancy is now openly known among close Valerian relatives. Her gentler hairstyle preference remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck, with elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
