# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is seventeen; exactly 34 days remain until her eighteenth birthday. Daytime in the courtyard of the Valerian domus; Lucius' three surviving children play nearby under household supervision.
- After Aemilia makes a case for her material contribution to Valerian prosperity, Lucius jokes that his expensive wife should name her price. Aemilia says there is only one price she asks and reminds him they are 34 days from her eighteenth birthday. Lucius confirms he remembers his promises: fidelity from that birthday onward and that they will try for legitimate children once she is an adult. He stresses that these promises were not purchased by her dowry, cleverness or usefulness.
- Aemilia tells him, 'I hope you are ready for the woman you have been shaping for this very day.' Lucius takes the challenge seriously without sexualizing it. He rejects the idea that he simply manufactured her to specification: he chose tutors, opened doors, demanded judgment and gave her unusual room to grow, but Aemilia herself formed the judgments and character that resulted.
- Lucius acknowledges that adulthood will change the terms of their marriage and that he cannot reasonably expect the educated, observant wife he deliberately cultivated to become conveniently passive when that day arrives. He remains confident in his formal household authority but admits, with dry humour, that he expects Aemilia to test it.
- The three children remain nearby. Valeria is approximately four; the two boys' names, exact ages, mothers and individual personalities remain unestablished. No sexual or erotic content occurs.
