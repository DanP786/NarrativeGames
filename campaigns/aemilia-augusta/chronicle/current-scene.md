# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Lucius has promised that once Aemilia is an adult they will try to have children together. Their difficult conversation has eased into familiar private teasing.
- After Aemilia lightly slaps Lucius' arm and he catches her wrist, joking that she has struck the head of the household after drinking his wine, demanding his fidelity and demanding his children, Aemilia answers dryly, 'However will you manage?'
- Lucius continues the joke rather than treating the gesture as insubordination. He releases her wrist and remarks that he has survived senators, creditors and Aemilia's opinions and expects his authority may endure the night, though perhaps only narrowly. The exchange reflects their unusual private familiarity without erasing the genuine hierarchy of their marriage.
- Aemilia remains seventeen; the physical contact and teasing are nonsexual and no erotic conduct occurs.
