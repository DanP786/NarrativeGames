# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Aemilia has asked Lucius to be faithful to her after her eighteenth birthday, and Lucius has agreed without exceptions while making clear that Livia, Valeria and his other children will remain protected members of the household.
- Frustrated by how readily Lucius agreed, Aemilia sets her cup down hard, nearly unleashes the speech she had prepared, then restrains herself and asks whether fidelity had been Lucius' plan all along—whether he has granted her something she never needed to ask for.
- Lucius answers that he had been considering what their adult marriage should become, including ending his sexual use of women in the household, but had not settled the matter into a promise before Aemilia spoke. He deliberately asked what Aemilia wanted because he wanted her wishes to have real causal weight rather than arranging everything privately and later presenting his decision as a gift.
- His fidelity promise therefore remains a choice he made in response to Aemilia's stated desire, not a preplanned concession or test with a predetermined outcome. Their hierarchy remains intact, but this becomes a concrete instance in which Aemilia's private judgment changes Lucius' conduct.
- Aemilia remains seventeen; the scene contains no sexual contact or erotic conduct.
