# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Thirty-four days have passed since the confrontation at the villa of Lucius' father. It is now Aemilia's eighteenth birthday, evening, at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Aemilia is now eighteen. She has arranged a large birthday gathering drawing together members of her own Aemilian family and Lucius' Valerian family, their friends, Lucius' colleagues and other important people. The scale makes the occasion both a family celebration and a conspicuous elite social event.
- The house is crowded and status-conscious: guests arrive with attendants, gifts and expectations; household slaves manage lamps, wine, food, cloaks and movement through the reception rooms. Aemilia is publicly visible as Lucius' adult wife and hostess rather than merely the young bride Rome has watched remain childless.
- Lucius remains close enough to receive important arrivals with her while allowing Aemilia visible authority over the gathering she is hosting. His earlier promises now take effect: from this birthday onward he will not seek sexual access to Livia or any other women, and he and Aemilia intend to try for legitimate children together. No sexual activity is assumed to occur merely because the date has arrived.
- The private reason for the marriage's previous childlessness is known to Lucius' father after their confrontation 34 days earlier. Whether he has told anyone else, and the consequences for paternal support, inheritance expectations and wider gossip, remain unresolved.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
