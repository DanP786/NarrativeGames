# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is seventeen; exactly 34 days remain until her eighteenth birthday. Later the same day at the villa of Lucius' father in Rome.
- Under threat of familial/dynastic exclusion, and after receiving Aemilia's minute nod permitting the necessary disclosure, Lucius has told his father that Aemilia has never been pregnant because he deliberately chose not to attempt to father a child with her while she was still a child. He takes sole responsibility and refuses to let his father label Aemilia barren or defective.
- Lucius' father reacts with furious disbelief. In coarse language he contrasts Lucius' acknowledged children by enslaved women with his refusal to seek legitimate issue from his high-status Roman wife, calls Lucius an idiot, and orders him to go home and immediately consummate the marriage.
- Lucius does not comply. Aemilia remains seventeen, exactly 34 days from eighteen, and Lucius' established boundary remains intact: he will not sexually involve her while she is a child. He answers his father in nonsexual terms that he has waited nearly six years by deliberate choice and will not abandon that decision with thirty-four days remaining merely because his father has shouted an order.
- The elder Valerian now knows the private reason for the childlessness but does not accept Lucius' reasoning. The immediate dispute has shifted from whether Aemilia is infertile to whether Lucius' paternal family can compel him to change the timetable of his own marriage. Lucius remains prepared to leave with Aemilia rather than comply.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
- No sexual or erotic content involving Aemilia occurs.
