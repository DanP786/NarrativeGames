# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night Aemilia and Lucius return from a successful elite gathering to the Valerian domus. Lucius' young daughter Valeria runs to meet him before being carried back to bed by her mother Livia.
- Seeing Lucius' children consistently causes Aemilia private emotional pain. She prays against jealousy and remains genuinely gentle toward the children, but she wants children of her own with Lucius and longs to see him show that same paternal affection toward children born to their marriage.
- After Valeria leaves, Aemilia gives the slightest sigh and follows Lucius into his study. Lucius can observe the sigh and her subdued manner but cannot know the prayer or exact longing she has not voiced.
- In the privacy of the study, Lucius turns first to the night's business rather than presuming upon Aemilia's mood. He asks for her judgment of Marcellus now that they are behind closed doors, restoring the private role she deliberately concealed in public.
