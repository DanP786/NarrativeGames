# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening.
- Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from her eighteenth birthday. Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Their marriage remains privately unconsummated. Publicly Lucius behaves as an established husband and protects the secret without hesitation.
- It is publicly known that Lucius has fathered children by adult enslaved women in his household; these do not satisfy the dynastic expectation for legitimate heirs from Aemilia.
- At an elite gathering, Aemilia and Lucius discuss the widespread expectation that their childless marriage will end. Lucius tells her he has no present intention of divorcing her and notes that they have effectively avoided discussing what her approaching adulthood will change.
- Aemilia replies that there have been more pressing matters and subtly indicates an elite man across the room connected to a political/patronage strategy she and Lucius have discussed and cultivated over several years.
- The identity and exact details of this long-game target were not previously established in campaign canon, so they remain deliberately unspecified until play defines them.
- Lucius understands Aemilia's signal immediately. In a familiar, publicly marital gesture, he moves her hair over her shoulder and rests his hand affectionately at the back of her neck, then turns his attention with hers toward the political target. He accepts the change of subject and shifts into their practiced private collaboration.
