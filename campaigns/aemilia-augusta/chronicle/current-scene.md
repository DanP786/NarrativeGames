# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after S01. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant. The pregnancy is more visibly apparent but no complication is established.
- Morning at Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus in Rome. Lucius is away from the domus. The discreet Rufus-Valeria gathering is now several weeks past. Terentia remains cautiously favorable; Valeria and Rufus remain unaware of the matchmaking purpose; Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told. Rufus's Atalanta-poem search remains unresolved.
- Aemilia's standing within the household has continued to consolidate. Her pregnancy makes her dynastic importance more visible to visitors and dependents as well as to the family.
- An elite married woman of respectable standing, not one of Aemilia's established close relatives, has arrived and specifically asked to speak with Aemilia rather than Lucius. She says the matter concerns a household obligation and asks for a private audience.
- Aemilia orders that the visitor be settled in the tablinum while she finishes dressing. The choice gives the visitor privacy from casual household traffic without making the audience clandestine.
- When Pia asks whether Aemilia wants her present, Aemilia nods and asks, 'What could she possibly want from me?' Pia does not pretend to know the visitor's purpose. She points out that asking specifically for Aemilia, while Lucius is away, suggests the woman wants something a respectable matron can do more easily or discreetly than a male patron: a word to another woman, access to the household, an introduction, intercession in a domestic matter, or influence that does not need to look like formal patronage. Pia also notes that the visitor may simply believe Aemilia can reach Lucius without approaching him directly. These are possibilities, not established knowledge.
- Pia will accompany Aemilia into the tablinum unless Aemilia dismisses her, remaining in an attendant's position rather than participating as an equal in the interview.
- No details of the visitor's requested favor have yet been established.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia is finishing her morning preparation with Pia before going to the tablinum to meet the unexpected petitioner.
