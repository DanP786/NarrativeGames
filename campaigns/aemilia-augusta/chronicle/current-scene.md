Philargyrus did not personally deliver Pia to Naso; according to Naso, he sent word beforehand that two men would bring a woman whose disposal was authorized and that Naso could treat the transaction as legitimate.

Whether Philargyrus knew Pia was stolen, knew she belonged to the Valerian household, or was himself acting for another person remains unestablished.
