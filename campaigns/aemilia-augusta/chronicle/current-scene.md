# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. A trusted midwife and Demetrios have judged Aemilia strongly likely to be pregnant after continued missed courses, bodily changes and a discreet examination; exact gestational dating remains uncertain by ancient standards.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage is established. Lucius remains faithful to his promise. The pregnancy is the legitimate child they have been trying for, though its outcome cannot be guaranteed.
- Earlier today Demetrios and the midwife gave comprehensive pregnancy guidance: nourishing food and good water, moderate ordinary movement, rest as needed, avoiding falls/violent exertion/needless illness exposure, caution with strong remedies, warning signs requiring urgent attention, and strict cleanliness around eventual childbirth. In an uncomplicated pregnancy, marital intimacy need not cease, but Aemilia's comfort and any warning signs govern.
- The pregnancy remains known only to Aemilia, Lucius, Pia, Demetrios and the trusted midwife. It has not yet been announced to the household, children or extended families.
- That evening Aemilia and Lucius lie together in bed. Lucius rests a hand over Aemilia's still-early belly and strokes it gently, privately absorbing that their hoped-for child may already be growing there. His manner is tender and unusually unguarded rather than sexual.
- Aemilia observes, 'It didn't take long.' Lucius answers with dry private humour that they were hardly negligent in trying, then turns more serious: after years of deliberately preventing the possibility, the speed with which pregnancy followed their decision to try strikes him deeply. He emphasizes that whatever happens, no one may again call her barren on the evidence of those waiting years.
- The household's practical health routines based on Demetrios' earlier advice remain in force, and the recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
