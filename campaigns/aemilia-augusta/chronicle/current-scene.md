# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, some weeks after Demetrios and the trusted midwife judged Aemilia strongly likely to be pregnant. Her pregnancy is progressing without an established complication; exact gestational dating remains uncertain by ancient standards.
- Aemilia and Lucius have made no formal announcement of the pregnancy. Nevertheless, the Valerian household has effectively inferred it from changed food and drink, protective adjustments, and the first subtle physical changes noticed by women attending Aemilia in the baths. Pia remains the only servant formally entrusted with the secret.
- Household servants continue the courtesy of pretending ignorance, but their increased care around Aemilia makes their collective inference obvious.
- During an otherwise ordinary day of household duties, Aemilia becomes tired enough to sit and rest. The fatigue is not accompanied by any established warning sign or complication. Nearby servants quietly make space and bring what she needs.
- Pia comes to Aemilia while she rests. Aemilia asks her to remove her hair fastenings because they feel too tight today. Pia immediately begins taking out the pins and loosening the elaborate arrangement, letting the weight and tension off Aemilia's scalp without making a fuss over it. Her familiarity allows her to recognize that Aemilia wants relief rather than ceremony.
- Lucius remains faithful to his promise. He and Aemilia continue their adult married life with the midwife's cautions in mind. The pregnancy is their hoped-for legitimate child, though its outcome cannot be guaranteed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
