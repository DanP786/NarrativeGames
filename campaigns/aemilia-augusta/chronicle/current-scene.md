# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. Enough time has passed since Aemilia's first missed expected menstrual cycle for a trusted midwife to assess her after continued absence of her courses; exact gestational dating remains uncertain by ancient standards.
- Aemilia and Lucius' adult marriage is established. Lucius remains faithful to his promise, and they have been trying for legitimate children.
- On a deliberately quiet day, Demetrios arrives privately with a trusted experienced midwife. Lucius is present with Aemilia; Pia is the only servant Aemilia has trusted with knowledge of her suspected pregnancy. No wider announcement has been made.
- After history and a discreet non-graphic examination, the midwife finds continued missed courses, bodily changes and examination signs strongly consistent with pregnancy. The pregnancy is now treated within this trusted circle as strongly established by the best available contemporary evidence, though its outcome remains uncertain.
- Aemilia asks the midwife and Demetrios to tell her everything she should know going forward. They advise ordinary nourishing food and adequate water, avoiding spoiled food and excessive wine, continuing moderate everyday movement rather than unnecessary confinement, resting when unusually fatigued, avoiding falls, violent exertion and needless exposure to illness, and promptly reporting significant bleeding, severe persistent abdominal pain, fever, fainting, inability to keep down food or drink, or other alarming changes.
- Demetrios reinforces the household cleanliness practices already established: clean water vessels, clean food preparation, hand and linen washing, less crowding around illness, and especially clean hands, cloths, bedding, instruments and room around childbirth. He explicitly refuses to promise these measures can guarantee a safe pregnancy or delivery.
- The midwife advises continued observation over the coming months, with later fetal movement providing stronger practical evidence of a living pregnancy. She and Demetrios discourage unnecessary remedies, purges and potent drugs without clear reason. They will continue to attend Aemilia as needed and prepare for birth well before labor begins.
- Lucius and Pia hear the guidance. The pregnancy has not yet been announced to the household, children or extended families.
