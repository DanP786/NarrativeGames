# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC, around five and a half years after the wedding-night opening. Aemilia is seventeen, a few months from eighteen; Lucius is approximately thirty.
- Late at night in Lucius' private study, Aemilia and Lucius are speaking with unusual directness about love, their future marriage, and Lucius' continuing sexual access to Livia, an enslaved woman of the household.
- Aemilia paces to control a much larger angry and jealous outburst she does not want to direct disrespectfully at her husband. When Lucius insists she tell him what she wants rather than hiding behind obedience, she says plainly, 'I want you to be faithful after my eighteenth birthday.'
- Lucius recognizes that Aemilia is asking for a standard Roman husbands of his rank are not ordinarily required to observe. He does not mock or dismiss the request. Because he has spent years demanding her honest judgment, he treats the request as legitimate private counsel about the terms on which she wants their adult marriage to proceed.
- Lucius agrees: from Aemilia's eighteenth birthday onward he will not seek sexual access to Livia or other women. He frames this as a promise he chooses to make to Aemilia, not as a surrender of his formal household authority. Livia and Lucius' children remain members of the household and are not to be punished, displaced or treated as blameworthy because of that promise.
- Aemilia remains seventeen; no sexual contact or erotic conduct occurs in the scene.
