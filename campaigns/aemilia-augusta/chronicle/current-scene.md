# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after S01. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant. The pregnancy is more visibly apparent but no complication is established.
- Morning at Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus in Rome. Lucius is away from the domus. The discreet Rufus-Valeria gathering is now several weeks past. Terentia remains cautiously favorable; Valeria and Rufus remain unaware of the matchmaking purpose; Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told. Rufus's Atalanta-poem search remains unresolved.
- Aemilia's standing within the household has continued to consolidate. Her pregnancy makes her dynastic importance more visible to visitors and dependents as well as to the family.
- Pia is attending Aemilia during the morning routine when a household servant brings word that an elite married woman of respectable standing has arrived and specifically asks to speak with Aemilia rather than Lucius. She is not one of Aemilia's established close relatives. The visitor says the matter concerns a household obligation and asks for a private audience.
- Aemilia looks to Pia, conscious that Lucius is away, then nods and orders, 'Settle her in the tablinum. I will be there shortly.' Aemilia therefore chooses to receive the petitioner herself in the household's business/reception room rather than defer the matter until Lucius returns. The choice gives the visitor privacy from casual household traffic without making the audience clandestine.
- Pia acknowledges the instruction and moves to see it carried out. The visitor is being shown into the tablinum while Aemilia completes her morning preparation.
- No details of the visitor's requested favor have yet been established.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia remains in her morning rooms for the few minutes needed to finish dressing before going to the tablinum to meet the unexpected petitioner herself.
