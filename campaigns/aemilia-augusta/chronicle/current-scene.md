# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. It is later in the morning after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday at the Valerian domus in Rome.
- Earlier Aemilia encountered Eros and learned that Eros' two-year-old son Felix had been feverish during the night, though somewhat improved by morning. Aemilia relieved Eros of her duties for the day so she could remain with her son. Felix's outcome remains unresolved.
- Aemilia has joined Lucius and the Greek physician Demetrios in a private consultation about Demetrios' efforts to reduce sickness through cleaner and healthier domestic practices. Lucius intends to listen despite senatorial dismissal and has included Aemilia because implementation would intersect strongly with her household authority.
- Demetrios' approach is empirical but historically bounded: he has no germ theory or modern microbiology. He reasons through contemporary ideas of regimen, environment, corrupted air or water and bodily balance, while observing that cleaner water and vessels, waste removal, washing, clean linens and dressings, food storage, ventilation and reduced crowding around some illnesses can sometimes improve health.
- After learning that Demetrios believes children particularly benefit from avoiding preventable sources of illness, Aemilia asks, 'And what of pregnancy?' Demetrios answers that a pregnant woman likewise benefits from good water, sound food, clean surroundings, moderate activity and avoiding needless exposure to sickness, but he cannot promise these things prevent miscarriage, difficult labour, childbed fever or death. He further argues from observation that cleanliness of hands, cloths, instruments and the birth room matters especially during and after delivery, though he explains this through contemporary concepts rather than germ theory.
- Demetrios does not assume Aemilia is currently pregnant and has not been told the private reproductive history of the marriage. Lucius knows Aemilia and he intend to try for legitimate children and therefore understands the personal relevance of her question.
- This is the first day of Aemilia and Lucius' adult marital phase. Lucius' fidelity promise remains active. No pregnancy or reproductive outcome is assumed.
