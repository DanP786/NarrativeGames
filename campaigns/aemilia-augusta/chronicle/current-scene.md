# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia remains approximately three months pregnant, with no established complication. The deliberately deniable family-and-business gathering at Lucius and Aemilia's domus has reached supper.
- Terentia and thirteen-year-old Valeria Tertia are present as Aemilia's family guests. Gaius Vibius Rufus, approximately twenty-two, is independently present through Lucius's business relationship. Terentia knows Aemilia suggested Rufus as a possible husband for Valeria; Valeria and Rufus do not know they are being observed as a possible match. Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told.
- Earlier in the visit, Valeria challenged Rufus to locate an unidentified Greek poem about Atalanta once quoted by her tutor. Rufus accepted without bluffing knowledge, questioned her seriously, and said that if he finds a purchasable copy he will tell Valeria the cost before buying it because the choice of its worth should be hers. The challenge remains unresolved and creates a natural reason for future contact.
- Lucius also drew Rufus into legitimate business discussion about judging commercial information, independent reports, credit and contractual relationships, firsthand knowledge, reputation, and motives. This demonstrated why Lucius values Rufus while preserving the innocent business explanation for his presence.
- Terentia's assessment of Rufus is favorable but cautious. She notes that he admits ignorance, does not patronize Valeria, becomes interested rather than irritated when challenged, and gives Valeria some practical agency. She wants to see how he handles disagreement and how Valeria behaves after novelty fades. Rufus's equestrian rather than senatorial status remains the principal dynastic obstacle; Terentia has said that if he were a senator's son they would likely already be discussing terms.
- Before supper, Aemilia and Valeria spoke privately in the shade. Valeria's curiosity about Rufus is now explicitly her own, though she still does not know the matchmaking context. Their conversation broadened into Aemilia's practical lessons about status, incentives, marriage, and relational power.
- Aemilia told Valeria that Lucius taught her there is always something to be gained in every interaction and to notice power in everything. She explained that Valeria will one day be a domina with household, purchasing, social, and patronage influence of her own. Using the enslaved servant Syria and plants shading one another as examples, she distinguished unequal authority from the ability of a subordinate person or thing to affect someone stronger. This did not equate an enslaved servant's constrained influence with a domina's coercive authority.
- On marriage, Aemilia said Lucius has the final say and she does not think she has power over him, though she possesses real power of her own. Asked what a wife's greatest power is, Aemilia replied that it depends on the kind of man her husband is and asked how he responds when his authority is challenged. She cautioned that men do not care to be told they are wrong too often. Valeria distilled the lesson as learning when and how to challenge a husband and how to win an argument without making him feel defeated. Aemilia smiled and said, 'Now you're getting it. Lucius would be proud.'
- Valeria then wondered whether Lucius would teach her directly as he taught Aemilia. Aemilia promised, 'I can promise you I'll ask him.' Valeria jokingly recognized the distinction between Aemilia promising to ask and commanding Lucius's answer.
- CURRENT MOMENT: it is time for supper. The household summons the guests from the shaded garden conversation and the company gathers to dine. Lucius, Terentia, Valeria Tertia, Rufus, and Aemilia are all present. The arrangement remains socially innocent on its face: family hospitality and Lucius's business guest sharing the same meal. No betrothal has been proposed, the matchmaking purpose remains undisclosed to Valeria and Rufus, and Marcus has not been informed.
- Aemilia's pregnancy is openly known among close Valerian relatives. Her gentler hairstyle preference remains established: soft, low-tension braids kept off her neck, with elaborate arrangements reserved for occasions when needed.
- Demetrios's household health routines remain in force. The recurring mock gladiatorial games remain a household tradition.
