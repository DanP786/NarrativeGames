# Current scene

- S02, approximately 12 BC, several weeks after S01. Aemilia is eighteen and approximately four months pregnant. Morning at Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus in Rome; Lucius is away.
- Earlier Aemilia received Tullia concerning Publius's debt and independently checked Lucius's household records. While replacing them, she found and deliberately read a private packet showing that during her later adolescence her father Marcus Aemilius discreetly explored the financial consequences of ending her still-childless marriage and the possibility of another future match, while Lucius secretly monitored those efforts and withheld the matter from Aemilia.
- Pia remains outside Lucius's office at Aemilia's instruction and has not read the private material.
- Continuing through the material, Aemilia discovers a second, older set of reports dating from before her marriage, when she was about twelve. These are not romantic observations and contain no sexualized assessment of the child. They are due-diligence reports concerning the proposed Aemilian-Valerian marriage and specifically Aemilia as the prospective bride.
- The reports record her legitimate birth and family standing, the scale and composition then contemplated for her dowry, her health as known to the household, childhood illnesses without any established chronic infirmity, the survival and reproductive history of women in her maternal and paternal family insofar as informants could report it, her education at that age, religious/domestic instruction, temperament as observed by tutors and household dependents, and whether there was any known scandal or impediment that might make the match dangerous to the Valerii.
- The reports characterize twelve-year-old Aemilia as observant, quiet in formal company, strongly attached to familiar attendants and family, dutiful in expected domestic/religious instruction, literate to the conventional level expected of an elite girl, and prone to asking more questions in private than she did before senior men. They note no known scandal and no established health reason to doubt eventual childbearing, while acknowledging that predictions about future fertility from a child are uncertain.
- Several observations clearly came from people inside or adjacent to the Aemilian household without young Aemilia's knowledge. One informant reported that she listened closely when political or estate matters were discussed nearby even when she was not included; another described her as more likely to remember an overheard name or disagreement than adults assumed. These traits predate Lucius's later education of her.
- A short note in Lucius's hand shows that he read the premarital reports before agreeing to the match. His annotation rejects treating speculative judgments about a twelve-year-old girl's future fertility as reliable and focuses instead on family alliance, dowry terms, health, and whether the proposed bride was being misrepresented. Another note indicates that after the marriage was settled he intended to judge Aemilia's character himself rather than rely indefinitely on servants' reports.
- The existence of these reports establishes that Lucius investigated Aemilia and her household before marrying her, as part of elite dynastic due diligence, and that information about her private childhood behavior was gathered without her knowledge. It does not establish that Lucius chose the match from affection, that he knew her personally beforehand beyond whatever ordinary elite acquaintance existed, or that the reports contained sexual content.
- The contrast is now sharper for Aemilia: before she entered the Valerian household, adult men had already reduced parts of her health, dowry, family, temperament and future dynastic usefulness to written information for a marriage decision; Lucius nevertheless showed skepticism toward claims that a child's fertility or adult character could be confidently predicted.
- Lucius does not know Aemilia has found or read either set of private reports.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia stands alone in Lucius's office reading reports compiled about her before she was married at twelve. Pia waits outside. The packet now exposes not only later surveillance of her father but the premarital investigation through which the Valerii assessed Aemilia herself before the marriage was agreed.
