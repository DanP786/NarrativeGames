- Marcus intentionally came before Cornelia/family because he wanted private conversation. Cornelia is well, knows he came, wanted to visit and has been concerned for Aemilia.
- Marcus acknowledges that during long childless years he believed Lucius was failing marriage's dynastic purpose and considered ending/replacing arrangement. Pregnancy removes central premise. He explicitly reverses course: marriage should continue; he will not pursue/encourage dissolution or another match on old childlessness grounds.
- Marcus also acknowledges Lucius could have ended marriage but did not, and that Aemilia repeatedly said she wanted to remain. Marcus admits he discounted her judgment due youth/attachment and says on this matter her judgment was better than his.
- Aemilia answers simply: 'I am happy here, Father.'
- Marcus takes the statement seriously rather than dismissing it as childish attachment. He says he can see that she is settled in the Valerian household and that her happiness matters to him, though he does not elevate happiness above all Roman family/dynastic concerns. He admits he should have listened more carefully when she said the same thing before.
- Marcus remains emotionally restrained. He does not suddenly become a modern indulgent father or renounce paternal/dynastic expectations. His concession is narrower and meaningful: Aemilia's lived judgment about her own marriage now carries more weight with him than it did before.
- CURRENT MOMENT: Aemilia and Marcus remain seated privately. Aemilia has told him she is happy here; Marcus has accepted that as real and acknowledged he should have listened before. His original marriage objection has been withdrawn. Lucius is expected shortly after urgent business. Publius deadline active; steward meeting later; Pia off duty.
