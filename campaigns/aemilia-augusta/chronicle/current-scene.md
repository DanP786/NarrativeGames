# Current scene

- S01, approximately 12 BC. Aemilia is seventeen; exactly 34 days remain until her eighteenth birthday. Later the same day at the villa of Lucius' father in Rome.
- Lucius and Aemilia stand together before Lucius' father after answering his joint summons. The elder Valerian argues that nearly six years without legitimate issue, contrasted with Lucius' children by enslaved women, indicates Aemilia is barren. Lucius rejects that conclusion without revealing that the marriage remains unconsummated.
- Pressing harder, Lucius' father points out that there has not even been a reported pregnancy and asks Aemilia directly, 'Are you deformed, girl?' The question is a humiliating challenge to her bodily fitness for dynastic motherhood, made from ignorance of the private marriage arrangement.
- Lucius immediately intervenes before Aemilia is required to answer. His manner becomes markedly formal and cold. He tells his father that Aemilia will not be interrogated about her body and repeats that his father has no evidence she is barren or physically defective. He frames the boundary as his authority over his own marriage and household rather than exposing the secret or asking Aemilia to defend herself.
- The confrontation is now openly between two adult Valerian men over the elder father's claimed dynastic interest and Lucius' authority to govern his own marriage. Aemilia remains present and may choose to speak despite Lucius' intervention.
- Lucius' father's personal name and exact offices remain unestablished. Aemilia's father also remains personally unnamed in canon.
- No sexual or erotic content occurs.
