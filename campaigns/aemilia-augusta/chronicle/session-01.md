# Session 01

## Opening
18 BC, Rome. Twelve-year-old Aemilia has married twenty-four-year-old Lucius Valerius Messalla and entered the Valerian household with a substantial dowry and attendants from her natal home. The session opens in their bedchamber after the wedding ceremonies. Lucius intends to tell her that he will not consummate the marriage while she is a child and to begin defining an unusual plan: he will remain the household authority, but intends to educate her far beyond what her father permitted and eventually expose her to the political and legal business of his domus.
