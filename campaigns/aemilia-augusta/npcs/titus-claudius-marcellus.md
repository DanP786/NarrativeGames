# Titus Claudius Marcellus

## Description
An older equestrian financier and estate creditor with discreet connections across elite households and provincial commercial arrangements. Reserved rather than ostentatious.

## Voice and mannerisms
Economical speech. Watches before committing himself. Dislikes obvious flattery and men who reveal too quickly what they want.

## Traits
- Reserved
- Financially astute
- Discreet
- Observant

## Current goals
- Protect his network of obligations and information.
- Deal with aristocratic households without becoming publicly entangled in their rivalries.

## Relationship to player
Newly introduced to Aemilia and Lucius through Marcus Calpurnius Varro. He immediately notices that Aemilia appears attentive to business language rather than merely decorative at her husband's side.

## Knowledge
Possesses unusually broad practical knowledge of elite debts, estate financing and some provincial commercial arrangements. He does not yet know why Lucius has wanted access to him.
