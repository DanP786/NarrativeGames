# Celer

## Description
Adult male hired messenger and low-level go-between working around the Porta Capena / Appian departure quarter. Known chiefly by the byname **Celer**. Exact age, legal status and family background remain unestablished. He moves between wine-shops, cart contacts, messengers and commercial intermediaries rather than maintaining a fixed office or known residence.

## Current status
Alive and under Valerian control in Rome after Lucius's men locate him through the modest wine-shop/food-house near Porta Capena identified by Gaius Vettius Philargyrus. He is taken discreetly and brought for questioning without public disturbance.

## Established role in Pia's disappearance
Celer carried the specific targeting information about Pia to Philargyrus: that she was a household attendant, would be away from her mistress on legitimate leave, and could be intercepted away from the Valerian domus.

Under Lucius's questioning, after being confronted with Philargyrus's account and the messenger reimbursement in Naso's records, Celer identifies the original household-informed source as **Eros**, an enslaved woman of the Valerian household and mother of two of Lucius's children.

Celer's identification is not merely a guessed name. He says Eros dealt with him directly on the information that mattered: Pia's identity as Aemilia's close attendant, the fact that Pia had genuine leave, and the time/route by which she could be met away from the house. Celer knows her as belonging to the Valerian household and as mother of children by its master; his description and contextual details are sufficient for Lucius to know whom he means.

Celer says the arrangement presented to him was to have Pia intercepted and kept away long enough to create a private disappearance/separation from her mistress. He does **not** say Eros instructed him to sell Pia, send her south, or place her into a commercial slave chain. Celer passed the opportunity onward through Philargyrus; from there the scheme became entangled with men who treated Pia as disposable property and moved her toward sale. Exactly where Celer's own culpability shifts from messenger to knowing facilitator remains open to legal/investigative judgment.

Whether money, favors or some other consideration passed directly between Eros and Celer is not yet firmly established; Lucius has not yet completed corroboration of every part of Celer's account.

## Voice and manner
Street-practical, wary and quick to distinguish what he carried, what he arranged and what others later did. He is much less socially secure than Philargyrus and understands that a senatorial household can make his position extremely dangerous. Under pressure he tries to minimize his responsibility but gives concrete identifying detail when confronted with corroborating evidence.

## Knowledge constraints
- Knows Eros supplied the targeting information about Pia's genuine leave and accessibility away from the household.
- Knows Eros wanted Pia removed from Aemilia's immediate presence for a period/private disappearance.
- Does not establish that Eros ordered onward sale.
- Knows he passed the information/opportunity to Philargyrus.
- Does not automatically know every later stage through Naso/Faustus.
- Is not established as one of the two men who physically seized/delivered Pia.

## GM constraints
- Celer is the evidentiary bridge from the commercial chain back into the Valerian household.
- His testimony establishes Eros's deliberate facilitation of the initial interception, consistent with hidden canon, but must **not** transform Eros into the architect of Pia's onward sale.
- The pregnancy loss remains unrelated and medically unresolved.
- Further corroboration can refine method/payment/which physical interceptors were used, but Lucius now has a concrete named source rather than mere suspicion.
