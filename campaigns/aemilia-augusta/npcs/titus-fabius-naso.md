# Titus Fabius Naso

## Description
Roman freedman operating in Rome as a commercial intermediary in the sale, transport and placement of enslaved laborers. His exact age, former owner and family circumstances are not yet established.

## Status
Alive and located in Rome. Valerian searchers have found him through one of his regular business contacts before he could disappear and have placed him under sufficient pressure/control to prevent him simply leaving. A small bundle of business tablets and memoranda connected with recent transactions has been secured.

## Role in Pia's disappearance
The holding yard through which a woman strongly matching Pia passed identifies Naso as the intermediary who arranged her onward transfer. This is established. Naso is **not** yet established as the person who ordered Pia's original interception, as having known she already belonged to the Valerian household, or as having any direct contact with Eros.

His precise account of who delivered authority to transfer the woman, where she was sent, what money changed hands, and what records survive has not yet been established. These facts must emerge through interrogation and examination of his records rather than assumption.

## Voice and manner
Not yet established. Must be developed consistently when he first speaks.

## Knowledge
He necessarily knows something about the onward transaction he arranged, but the exact limits of his knowledge are unestablished. He does not automatically know the full chain behind Pia's interception.

## Relationship to Aemilia
None personally established. He is now implicated in the onward transfer of Aemilia's missing enslaved attendant and faces the authority and resources of the Valerian household.

## GM constraints
- Do not retroactively make Naso the architect of the original interception without evidence.
- Do not establish a direct Naso–Eros link merely for convenience.
- Use his business records and testimony to move the investigation one plausible link at a time.
- Pia's current location is still unknown at the moment Naso is found.
