# Eros

## Description
Adult enslaved woman of the Valerian household and mother of two of Lucius' surviving children: Fortunata, approximately four, and Felix, approximately two.

## Status
Enslaved within the Valerian domus. Her position is constrained by ownership and household hierarchy; her sexual relationship with Lucius occurred within that coercive social structure and is not treated as evidence of free consent.

## Relationship to player
Lives under Aemilia's authority as mistress of the household. The exact personal relationship between Eros and Aemilia has not yet been established in play.

## Relationship to Lucius
Mother of Fortunata and Felix, two of Lucius' acknowledged surviving children. Lucius continued seeking sexual access to Eros during the years in which he maintained a marital boundary with Aemilia. From Aemilia's eighteenth birthday onward, his promise of fidelity to Aemilia means he will no longer seek sexual access to Eros. Eros and her children are not to be punished, discarded or displaced because of that change.

## Knowledge
Knows the household from within. Further knowledge and loyalties remain to be established through play.
