# Livia

## Description
Adult enslaved woman of the Valerian household and mother of Lucius' young daughter Valeria.

## Status
Enslaved within the Valerian domus. Her position is constrained by ownership and household hierarchy; any sexual relationship producing Valeria occurred within that coercive social structure and is not treated as evidence of free consent.

## Relationship to player
Lives under Aemilia's authority as mistress of the household. The exact personal relationship between Livia and Aemilia has not yet been established in play.

## Relationship to Lucius
Mother of one of Lucius' acknowledged children. The exact continuing personal relationship between Livia and Lucius has not yet been established in play.

## Knowledge
Knows the household from within. Further knowledge and loyalties remain to be established through play.
