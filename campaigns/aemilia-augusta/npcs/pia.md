# Pia

## Description
Adult enslaved woman and longtime personal attendant to Aemilia. Pia came with Aemilia from her father's household when Aemilia married into the Valerian domus and has remained one of the most familiar figures in her daily private life.

## Role
Personal attendant and familiar companion within the unequal structure of Roman slavery. She assists with Aemilia's dressing, hair and intimate domestic routine and has unusually close practical knowledge of her mistress's habits and household life.

## Voice and manner
Familiar enough to speak gently and personally when privacy and rank permit, but does not forget that Aemilia is her domina. She can read Aemilia's expressions well after years of attendance.

## Current status
Missing. Pia was given her first genuine day away from ordinary duties shortly before Aemilia's pregnancy loss and failed to return to the Valerian domus. More than a week later no message or confirmed word from her has reached the household. Her whereabouts, reason for absence and fate remain unestablished. No search result, sighting or witness account is established.

## Current knowledge
- Knows that Aemilia and Lucius maintained an unusual private marital boundary for nearly six years.
- From the circumstances of Aemilia's eighteenth birthday, understands that the marriage entered its adult phase.
- What Pia knows about events after she disappeared is unestablished and must not be assumed.
- Does not know Aemilia's unspoken thoughts unless Aemilia expresses them.

## Relationship to Aemilia
Warm and longstanding in daily practice, though structurally unequal because Pia is enslaved and Aemilia is her domina. Pia followed Aemilia from her father's household into the Valerian domus and witnessed her growth from young bride to adult mistress. During Pia's absence Aemilia has struggled with dressing and hair routines Pia normally handled and has explicitly asked the household steward for news, saying she hopes Pia is safe.
