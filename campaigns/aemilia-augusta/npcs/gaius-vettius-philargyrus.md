# Gaius Vettius Philargyrus

## Description
Roman freedman active around commercial introductions in Rome. Exact age, appearance, former owner, household and family circumstances remain unestablished.

## Status
Alive and now under Valerian control inside the Roman domus after being located by men sent to find him discreetly. He is kept separate from Titus Fabius Naso so the two cannot coordinate accounts.

## Established commercial role
Philargyrus is known to Titus Fabius Naso through prior dealings involving small labor placements, transport introductions and short-notice transactions.

Before Pia entered Naso's commercial chain, Philargyrus sent word that two men would bring a woman whose disposal was authorized and that Naso could treat the transaction as legitimate.

Philargyrus's message contained specific advance information: the target was a **household attendant who would be away from her mistress on legitimate leave**, and the men would know where to meet/intercept her before bringing her into the commercial chain.

Naso's records corroborate that Philargyrus received a compensated brokerage/introduction fee connected with the transaction. They also record a messenger reimbursement associated with a man known as **Celer**, whom Naso has seen carrying messages for Philargyrus before.

## Current questioning
Lucius is questioning Philargyrus in the report room after his capture/arrival. Aemilia has not yet entered the questioning at the close of T111.

Philargyrus has not yet established in play who supplied him with the advance information, who engaged him, whether he knew Pia belonged to the Valerian household, or whether he knew the intended interception was unlawful.

## Knowledge constraints
- He knows his own role in passing the advance assurance/information to Naso.
- He knows how he received the information and/or from whom to the extent later established through questioning; this must emerge plausibly in play rather than being retroactively assumed.
- He is **not yet established** as Pia's owner, physical abductor, the person who ordered the seizure, or a knowing handler of stolen Valerian property.
- He did not personally deliver Pia to Naso according to Naso's account.
- No direct Philargyrus–Eros link is established in known evidence.

## Voice and manner
Not yet established. Do not canonize a detailed personality, biography or speaking style until he speaks/acts in play.

## GM constraints
- Philargyrus is the next backward intermediary toward the hidden source of the advance information.
- Do **not** jump directly from Philargyrus to Eros. At least one further plausible evidentiary step/intermediary must establish how the information traveled before Eros can be implicated.
- Celer may serve as a corroborating messenger/contact lead but is not automatically the physical interceptor or the ultimate source.
- Hidden GM canon remains that Eros facilitated Pia's initial interception but did not intend/order Pia's onward sale and did not design the full commercial chain.
