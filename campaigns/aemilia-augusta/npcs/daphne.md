# Daphne

## Description
Adult enslaved woman of the Valerian household and mother of Lucius' surviving six-year-old son Hilarus.

## Status
Enslaved within the Valerian domus. Her position is constrained by ownership and household hierarchy; any sexual relationship with Lucius occurred within that coercive social structure and is not treated as evidence of free consent.

## Relationship to player
Lives under Aemilia's authority as mistress of the household. Further details of their personal relationship remain to emerge in play.

## Relationship to Lucius
