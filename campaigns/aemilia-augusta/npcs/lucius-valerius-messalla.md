# Lucius Valerius Messalla

## Description
Twenty-four-year-old Roman senator's son from an established senatorial family, educated for elite male public life. Newly married to twelve-year-old Aemilia.

## Voice and mannerisms
Controlled and educated. Speaks to make distinctions clear rather than to fill silence. Accustomed to being obeyed within hierarchies and does not disguise authority as equality.

## Traits
- Deliberate
- Hierarchical
- Intellectually curious
- Reputation-conscious

## Short-term goals
- Make Aemilia understand that he will not consummate the marriage while she is a child.
- Establish the basic terms of life in his household without frightening or humiliating her.
- Learn how much she actually understands beneath the behaviour expected of a dutiful daughter.

## Long-term goals
- Build a strong and politically capable domus.
- Educate Aemilia substantially beyond the conventional limits of her upbringing.
- Eventually use her observations and private judgment as an additional source of counsel while retaining final household authority.
- Advance his family's standing in Augustan Rome without becoming expendable to more powerful patrons.

## Methods & lines
- Will: instruct, command, negotiate within elite custom, use patronage, control access, test judgment, reward competence.
- Won't: consummate the marriage while Aemilia is a child; pretend their legal/social power is equal when it is not.
- Last resort: coercive household or political measures when he believes the domus is genuinely threatened; specifics depend on circumstance.

## Disposition toward Aemilia (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 35 / 100 — she is almost unknown to him and has been trained to say what elders expect.
- Affection: 25 / 100 — protective goodwill exists, but intimacy has not been earned.
- Respect: 30 / 100 — reserves judgment; assumes lack of education rather than lack of capacity.
- Loyalty: 45 / 100 — marriage creates a serious household obligation, though personal loyalty is still developing.
- Suspicion: 20 / 100 — no particular reason to suspect deceit, but he expects rehearsed obedience.

## Relationship to player
Neutral. Aemilia is his new wife and a dynastic partner in law and household politics, but personally they are near-strangers. He intends to exercise conventional household authority while deliberately expanding her education and eventual access to information.

## History with player
- S01: Married Aemilia in 18 BC.
- S01 opening: Intends to tell her privately that he will not consummate the marriage while she remains a child.

## Knowledge
He knows her family standing, dowry negotiations and the reputation her family presented during the match. He knows she received a conventional elite female education. He does not know her private thoughts or what personality may emerge outside her father's household.

## What he is hiding
His precise political ambitions, pressures on his family, and the full strategic reasoning behind wanting an unusually educated wife have not yet been revealed.
