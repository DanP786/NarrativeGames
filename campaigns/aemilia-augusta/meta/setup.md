# Campaign setup

Created: 27 September 2026 (real-world date)
Slug: aemilia-augusta

## Onboarding answers

**Genre / premise:** Historically grounded Ancient Rome under Emperor Augustus, focused on Roman law, institutions, social hierarchy, religion, family structures, slavery, citizenship, patronage, marriage, household authority, and politics. Gritty historical realism rather than generic sword-and-sandal adventure.

**Starting date:** 18 BC, during the period of Augustus' marriage legislation.

**Voice:** Third person, present tense.

**Prose style:** Gritty historical realism. Direct, physical, socially observant prose. Rome is crowded, unequal, status-conscious and dangerous. Political, legal, familial and economic realities matter. Little romanticisation; avoid conveniently modern assumptions in historical characters.

**Length:** Open-ended sandbox. No fixed endpoint and no act tracker.

**Tone / content posture:** Nothing categorically off the table when historically and fictionally appropriate. The setting may include slavery, violence, disease, death, coercive social structures, exploitation, political cruelty and severe inequality. Aemilia begins play at age twelve; no sexual activity involving her while she is a child will be depicted. The opening explicitly establishes that Lucius refuses to consummate the marriage while she is a child.

**Stakes / scope:** Begins intimately with Aemilia's new marriage and household, then may expand naturally through family, inheritance, dowry, patronage, reputation, elite society and imperial politics. The campaign explores survival and growth within a new household, her evolving relationship with Lucius as she grows up, and the dynastic/familial purposes behind their marriage.

**Agency posture:** Gritty realism. Choices and failures have lasting consequences; the world does not bend to protect Aemilia.

**Death rule:** Hard death enabled. Disease, violence, political punishment, childbirth once adult, and other genuinely lethal circumstances can kill the PC.

**Mind-reading rule:** None. Thoughts are private. Roman religion, omens, dreams and divination may matter culturally or fictionally but do not grant automatic access to another person's inner state.

**Main-thread seed:** Aemilia enters marriage at twelve as a wealthy, carefully raised and largely docile elite Roman girl. Lucius intends to remain the dominant head of his household by Roman custom, but unusually plans to educate his wife substantially, eventually permit her to listen to meetings and seek her private judgment. The story asks what Aemilia becomes when access to knowledge and influence exceeds what her father prepared her for, what sort of marriage she and Lucius construct over time, and what their household ambitions cost them.

**Character seed:** Aemilia, age twelve, is from a wealthy branch of the gens Aemilia. Her dowry is substantial and her marriage brings enslaved attendants into the Valerian household. Her father provided the education he considered suitable for an elite Roman daughter: household management, textile work, religion and ritual, respectable manners, basic literacy and some literature. She has been docile for much of her life. Her deeper adult personality is deliberately undefined and will develop through play under the influence of her new life and her own choices.

**Husband:** Lucius Valerius Messalla, age twenty-four, from a senatorial family. He received an elite male education in Latin and Greek literature, rhetoric, law, philosophy, history and public affairs. He accepts the Roman hierarchy of his household and expects to hold final authority. His unusual conviction is that an educated, perceptive wife can strengthen his domus. He plans to educate Aemilia and, as she matures, expose her to political and legal discussions generally reserved for men. On their wedding night he explicitly tells her that he will not consummate their marriage while she is a child.

**Opening situation:** Wedding night in Lucius' Roman house. Twelve-year-old Aemilia and her twenty-four-year-old husband stand in their bedchamber after the ceremonies. Lucius explains that he will not bed her until she is older, and begins to explain what he intends their marriage and her education to become.
