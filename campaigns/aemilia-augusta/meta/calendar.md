# Calendar

- Date: approximately 12 BC; several weeks after the end of S01. Aemilia is approximately four months pregnant and eighteen years old.
- Time of day: morning
- Location: Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus, Rome
- Time elapsed since opening: approximately six years
- Notable current: Aemilia's pregnancy is now more visibly apparent but remains without an established complication. The discreet family-and-business gathering involving Terentia, Valeria Tertia and Gaius Vibius Rufus lies several weeks in the past. Terentia knows the matchmaking purpose and is cautiously favorable; Valeria and Rufus do not know they were observed as a possible match, and Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told. Rufus's search for Valeria's unidentified Greek poem about Atalanta remains unresolved. Lucius has agreed in principle to teach Valeria practical social judgment. Lucius's fidelity promise remains in effect.
