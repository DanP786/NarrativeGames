# Calendar

- Date: approximately 12 BC; several days after Aemilia's pregnancy loss, which occurred on the night following the expiration day of Numerius Aufidius Crispus's one-day standstill over Publius's two seized craftsmen. Aemilia is eighteen years old.
- Time of day: unestablished within the current day
- Location: Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus, Rome
- Time elapsed since opening: approximately six years
- Notable current: Aemilia lost her approximately four-month pregnancy after a night of pain and bleeding, then remained unconscious for about a day from severe weakness/blood loss. She survived the immediate crisis and has now remained in bed for several days after waking, increasingly from grief rather than medical order. Pia failed to return from her first genuine day free ordinary duties and remains missing for reasons not yet established. The severe marital breach from Lucius twice preventing Aemilia leaving their argument, including breaking his promise not to stop her again, remains unresolved. Aemilia's one-month no-punitive-striking urban-domus discipline trial had been approved before the crisis, but Aemilia has not resumed ordinary household management during her withdrawal. Marcus Aemilius Lepidus had withdrawn pressure to end Aemilia's marriage and partially reconciled with her shortly before the loss. The exact outcome of the Publius/Aufidius creditor deadline remains unestablished on-screen and must not be assumed.
