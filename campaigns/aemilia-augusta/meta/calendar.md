# Calendar

- Date: approximately 12 BC; roughly a week or more after Aemilia's pregnancy loss, with the exact day count intentionally loose. The loss occurred on the night following the expiration day of Numerius Aufidius Crispus's one-day standstill over Publius's two seized craftsmen. Aemilia is eighteen years old.
- Time of day: unestablished within the current day
- Location: Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus, Rome
- Time elapsed since opening: approximately six years
- Notable current: Aemilia lost her approximately four-month pregnancy after a night of pain and bleeding, then remained unconscious for about a day from severe weakness/blood loss. After waking she spent several days remaining in bed from grief rather than medical order. Cornelia has now stayed at the Valerian domus for several days, keeping Aemilia company mostly through ordinary gossip and familiar conversation. Aemilia remains largely silent but has begun sitting upright and eating a little, marking slow recovery without resolution. Pia failed to return from her first genuine day free ordinary duties and remains missing for reasons not yet established. The severe marital breach from Lucius twice preventing Aemilia leaving their argument, including breaking his promise not to stop her again, remains unresolved. Aemilia's one-month no-punitive-striking urban-domus discipline trial had been approved before the crisis, but Aemilia has not yet resumed ordinary household management. The exact outcome of the Publius/Aufidius creditor deadline remains unestablished on-screen and must not be assumed.
