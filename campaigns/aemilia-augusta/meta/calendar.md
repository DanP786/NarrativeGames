# Calendar

- Date: approximately 12 BC; Aemilia is approximately three months pregnant and several months past her eighteenth birthday
- Time of day: night
- Location: Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus, Rome; the couple have retired to their private rooms
- Time elapsed since opening: approximately six years
- Notable current: The discreet family-and-business gathering involving Terentia, Valeria Tertia and Gaius Vibius Rufus has ended. Terentia knows the matchmaking purpose and is cautiously favorable; Valeria and Rufus do not know they were being observed as a possible match, and Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told. Rufus's search for Valeria's unidentified Greek poem about Atalanta remains unresolved. Lucius has agreed in principle to teach Valeria some of the practical social judgment he taught Aemilia. Aemilia's pregnancy is progressing without an established complication. Lucius's fidelity promise remains in effect.
