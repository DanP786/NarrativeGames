# Calendar

- Date: approximately 12 BC; night after the day on which Aemilia and Lucius faced the expiration of Numerius Aufidius Crispus's one-day standstill over Publius's two seized craftsmen. Aemilia is approximately four months pregnant and eighteen years old.
- Time of day: night / bedtime
- Location: Lucius and Aemilia's Valerian domus, Rome
- Time elapsed since opening: approximately six years
- Notable current: Aemilia's pregnancy is visibly apparent but remains without an established complication. Marcus Aemilius Lepidus visited unannounced today, withdrew his prior pressure to end Aemilia's marriage, accepted her happiness and judgment, and partially reconciled with her. Aemilia wants Cornelia present for confinement/birth. Aemilia investigated household discipline, ordered a one-month urban-domus trial without punitive striking plus modest rewards and records, and Lucius approved the experiment conditionally. Pia has had her first genuine day free ordinary duties, though a formal standing weekly instruction remains pending. Aemilia privately questioned Eros and learned that Lucius's former sexual access was not always wanted and that meaningful refusal was unavailable; Eros also named Menophilus as an abusive delegated supervisor, Chryseros as a possible replacement, and Thais as a lesser concern. During the afternoon/evening Eros privately told Lucius the substance of that conversation, including Aemilia's statement that the only person she has to fear is her husband. Aemilia does not yet know Eros spoke to him. The exact outcome of today's Publius/Aufidius creditor deadline has not yet been established on-screen and must not be assumed. Aemilia is now preparing for bed with her hair released after an uncomfortable substitute arrangement pulled at her scalp through the day.
