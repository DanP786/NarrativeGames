# Main thread

- Status: active
- Central question: What will Aemilia do with the education, household authority and private influence she has acquired inside a society that still formally subordinates her, and how will adulthood, motherhood and dynastic responsibility reshape the marriage in which those capacities were cultivated?
- PC want: Preserve the household she adores and her bond with Lucius while continuing to grow into meaningful judgment and influence, now including the prospect of motherhood.
- Primary obstacle: Elite Roman law, custom, family strategy and reputation impose real limits on female autonomy; Aemilia's influence is strongest precisely where it is least public.
- Relationship thread: Aemilia and Lucius's marriage has entered its adult phase. They love one another, expect a legitimate child, and have explicitly negotiated fidelity, past sexual history and the difference between Lucius's formal authority and Aemilia's practical influence. The relationship remains structurally unequal while increasingly dependent on her judgment.
- Political thread: Aemilia now actively uses private social influence, patronage knowledge and family hospitality rather than merely observing them. Her discreet attempt to place Gaius Vibius Rufus before Terentia and Valeria Tertia as a possible marriage prospect is the clearest current example.
- Family side thread: Valeria Tertia is approaching marriage age. Terentia is cautiously interested in Rufus; Valeria and Rufus remain unaware of the matchmaking test; Marcus Valerius Messalla has not been told. Lucius has agreed in principle to teach Valeria practical social judgment.
