# Personality and reputation

## Starting disposition
Aemilia has been regarded by her family as docile, respectable and properly raised. This describes her conditioning and outward reputation, not a fixed inner personality.

## Development rule
Her deeper temperament, convictions and methods emerge through play. Do not retroactively assign her a hidden rebelliousness, ambition or ideology merely to create drama.

## Reputation
- Aemilian family: Properly raised young daughter; newly transferred into marriage.
- Valerian household: Newly arrived wife; reputation not yet formed.
