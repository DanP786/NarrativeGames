# Personality and reputation

## Developed disposition
Aemilia is no longer merely the docile child her natal family delivered into marriage. Years of education and responsibility have made her observant, intellectually engaged and increasingly capable of forming judgments rather than simply discovering the answer an authority wants. She remains comfortable with much of the household hierarchy in which she was raised, particularly because her experience of the Valerian household has given her belonging, responsibility and access rather than simple exclusion.

She adores her household and loves Lucius deeply. The fact that he exercises substantial authority over her life is not invisible to her; it is part of the complicated structure within which that love developed.

## Reputation
- Aemilian family: A successful elite marriage and a daughter firmly established in the Valerian household.
- Valerian household: Experienced young mistress of the domus; educated, attentive and strongly identified with the household's interests.
- Elite society: Respectable young wife of Lucius Valerius Messalla. Her unusual education is known selectively; the private non-consummation of the marriage is not public knowledge.
