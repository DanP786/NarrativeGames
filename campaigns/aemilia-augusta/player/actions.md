# Significant actions

- S01: Aemilia grows into practical authority over the Valerian domus and implements Demetrios's household-health reforms concerning water, food storage, waste, washing, linens and illness practices.
- S01: Aemilia secures an explicit fidelity promise from Lucius for their adult marriage and later clarifies that it remains binding through pregnancy, childbirth, illness and periods of abstinence.
- S01: After learning the fuller extent of Lucius's past sexual access to enslaved women, Aemilia distinguishes the childhood-boundary years from their adult marriage and tells Lucius she forgives what came before without denying the coercive structure of slavery.
- S01: Aemilia proposes Gaius Vibius Rufus as a possible husband for Valeria Tertia and engineers a deniable social encounter that lets Terentia and Valeria assess him without alerting Rufus or Marcus Valerius Messalla to a formal marriage negotiation.
- S01: Aemilia begins teaching Valeria Tertia practical lessons about incentives, unequal power, influence and judging how a prospective husband responds to disagreement; she secures Lucius's willingness in principle to continue that instruction.
