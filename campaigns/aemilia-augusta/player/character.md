# Aemilia

- Name: Aemilia
- Age: 18
- Status: Freeborn Roman woman of a wealthy elite family; wife of Lucius Valerius Messalla.
- Family: A wealthy branch of the gens Aemilia.
- Current household: Valerian domus, Rome.
- Marriage: Married to Lucius for roughly six years. Their marriage entered its adult phase by mutual choice on Aemilia's eighteenth birthday after Lucius maintained a childhood boundary for nearly six years. Their adult intimate life was frequent and affectionate before the current grief and marital breach.
- Dowry: Substantial; part of the economic and dynastic bond between the Aemilian and Valerian families.

## Appearance
Aemilia is an eighteen-year-old Roman woman with an oval face, warm olive complexion, large brown eyes, dark brown hair, and fine, expressive features rather than a severe cast. She is currently pale and physically depleted after major blood loss. Without Pia's familiar help, her hair has recently been arranged simply, with much of it left loose rather than in the elaborate styles she previously wore for elite occasions. Her established facial visual reference remains the S01 gathering portrait generated during play; incidental details from generated images are not automatically canon.

## Development since the wedding
Lucius followed through on his promise to educate Aemilia. Tutors, household records, conversation and carefully widened access to the business of the domus have given her an education far beyond what her father originally intended. She has learned to read arguments rather than merely words, to follow accounts and legal interests, to understand patronage and political conversations, and to speak privately with Lucius about matters on which he still retains final authority.

Aemilia grew deeply attached to the Valerian household and regards it as her home. She loves Lucius, but their relationship is now strained by an unresolved breach: during a severe argument concerning Eros, Lucius twice physically prevented Aemilia from leaving and the second restraint broke his explicit promise not to stop her again. He admitted **“I was wrong,”** but the dispute has not been directly resolved since the pregnancy loss that followed later that night.

Following the Greek physician Demetrios's inspection, Aemilia spent months turning his practical recommendations into household routines involving cleaner water handling, kitchens and stores, waste removal, linens, washing and practices around illness. The work strengthened her practical authority as mistress of the domus.

## Current state
Approximately 12 BC, more than a week after the loss of Aemilia's first pregnancy at approximately four months. The miscarriage involved hours of pain and significant bleeding; Aemilia remained unconscious for roughly a day from severe weakness/blood loss. No medical cause was established. She has no established fever, renewed hemorrhage or new complication, but remains markedly weak and lacks her former endurance.

After days of grief-driven withdrawal, Aemilia has returned to household work. Her manner is controlled and soft-voiced but notably less warm: she no longer instinctively smiles, reassures or cushions commands as she once did. The reserve applies broadly rather than solely to Eros. On the second day of her return she overexerts herself, becomes unable to climb stairs safely, and calls for Lucius. She voluntarily accepts his help, rests against him while he carries her, and later holds his hand to keep him beside her. This renewed closeness does not resolve the marital breach.

Pia, Aemilia's longtime enslaved personal attendant and most familiar daily companion, has been missing for more than a week after failing to return from a genuine day of leave. No message or confirmed word has reached the household. Aemilia has explicitly said she hopes Pia is safe.

Lucius's fidelity promise remains active. Unknown to Aemilia, during her post-loss withdrawal Eros privately sought Lucius's company in a manner recalling their former sexual relationship; Lucius was tempted but did not resume sexual access or have sex with Eros.
