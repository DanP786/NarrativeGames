# Aemilia

- Name: Aemilia
- Age: 18
- Status: Freeborn Roman woman of a wealthy elite family; wife of Lucius Valerius Messalla.
- Family: A wealthy branch of the gens Aemilia.
- Current household: Valerian domus, Rome.
- Marriage: Married to Lucius for roughly six years. Their marriage entered its adult phase by mutual choice on Aemilia's eighteenth birthday after Lucius maintained a childhood boundary for nearly six years. Their adult intimate life is established and frequent but remains non-graphically described in play.
- Dowry: Substantial; part of the economic and dynastic bond between the Aemilian and Valerian families.

## Appearance
Aemilia is an eighteen-year-old Roman woman with an oval face, warm olive complexion, large brown eyes, dark brown hair, and fine, expressive features rather than a severe cast. For elite public occasions she can wear her hair elaborately braided and pinned with restrained gold ornaments, but during pregnancy she increasingly prefers softer, low-tension braids kept off the back of her neck; elaborate arrangements are reserved for occasions that warrant them. At approximately three months pregnant she is only slightly showing: there is a small early curve and firmness rather than a large belly, her bust has increased enough to alter the drape and fit of clothing, and fatigue sometimes makes her movements more measured. For the recent Valerian family gathering she chose comfortable but elegant matronly draping that followed rather than disguised the slight change in her figure. Her established facial visual reference remains the S01 gathering portrait generated during play; incidental details from generated images are not automatically canon.

## Development since the wedding
Lucius followed through on his promise to educate Aemilia. Tutors, household records, conversation and carefully widened access to the business of the domus have given her an education far beyond what her father originally intended. She has learned to read arguments rather than merely words, to follow accounts and legal interests, to understand patronage and political conversations, and to speak privately with Lucius about matters on which he still retains final authority.

Aemilia has grown deeply attached to the Valerian household and regards it as her home. She loves Lucius. That love has developed within a relationship whose hierarchy remains real: Lucius controls much of the formal shape of their household life, but he has also consistently made room for her mind and judgment and now tells her that her judgment is something he wants before important decisions.

Following the Greek physician Demetrios' inspection, Aemilia spends months turning his practical recommendations into household routines involving cleaner water handling, kitchens and stores, waste removal, linens, washing and practices around illness. The work strengthens her practical authority as mistress of the domus.

## Current state
Approximately 12 BC, several months after Aemilia's eighteenth birthday. Aemilia is approximately three months pregnant with her and Lucius's first legitimate child, as strongly established by the evidence available to Demetrios and an experienced midwife. No complication is established. She experiences ordinary fatigue and has had a brief passing low abdominal pulling sensation on standing too quickly, without established warning signs.

Lucius's fidelity promise remains active. Aemilia has learned that during the six years he maintained the childhood boundary with her he had sexual access to six adult enslaved women in total, including Eros and Daphne. Both spouses explicitly acknowledge the coercive ownership structure involved. Aemilia has told Lucius she forgives what came before in terms of their marriage, while Lucius has promised that fidelity in their adult marriage remains binding through pregnancy, childbirth, illness and periods of abstinence.

Aemilia is increasingly willing to use her education and private influence on behalf of others. She has discreetly suggested Gaius Vibius Rufus as a possible husband for Valeria Tertia, arranged an exploratory encounter with Lucius and Terentia's knowledge, and begun teaching Valeria some of the practical lessons about incentives, authority and influence that Lucius once taught her.
