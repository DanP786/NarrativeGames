# Current scene

- Location: Palace of the White Hart, Veyrhold — council chamber
- Time: late afternoon, 29th of Harvestwane, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: A dispute over grain convoys has become a proxy struggle between Oren and Cassian for practical authority in Veyrhold. Elowen privately proposes to Garrick that neutral House Vale escort the grain.

## Recent beats
- Master Pell reports that two eastern grain convoys have failed to arrive and Veyrhold has roughly eleven days of grain at normal rationing.
- Oren offers royal household troops to escort the next convoy. Cassian objects that this would let one claimant control the food road and proposes a mixed neutral escort instead. Oren argues mixed command would be inefficient.
- Elowen recognizes that whoever visibly secures the grain gains legitimacy and leverage over the city.
- Elowen whispers to Garrick that House Vale could escort the carts because it is technically neutral.
- Garrick considers the idea seriously and identifies both its strength and danger: a single neutral Vale command solves Cassian's concern about claimant control and Oren's concern about divided command, but it also makes Vale visibly responsible for whether the grain arrives and places Vale soldiers on a strategically important road. If the convoy is attacked, House Vale could be drawn into conflict or manipulated into blaming a claimant.
- Garrick decides the proposal is worth making if tightly constrained. Rather than speak for Elowen, he quietly tells her that since she saw the solution, she should present it to the council herself, but only as a practical temporary escort under civic authority, not as a political claim over the grain road.
