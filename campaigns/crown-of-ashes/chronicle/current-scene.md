# Current scene

- Location: Palace of the White Hart, Veyrhold — west gallery
- Time: afternoon, 29th of Harvestwane, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: Three days after Elowen's public proclamation as Vale heir, Garrick brings her to a palace council on unrest, food supply, and rival armed followings. During a recess, Prince Oren approaches Elowen directly and asks what she believes House Vale requires from the eventual king.

## Recent beats
- Garrick and Cassian sealed a thirty-day restraint including Veyne recognition of Elowen's succession, Vale neutrality, and reciprocal advance notice of major marriage alliances.
- Garrick publicly named Elowen his lawful heir. Vale bannermen swore to uphold her succession despite some openly questioning her sex, demeanor, and inexperience.
- On 29 Harvestwane, a broad council gathers at the Palace of the White Hart. Oren and Cassian both attend under separate armed entourages.
- During a recess Oren approaches Elowen while Garrick is occupied nearby. He congratulates her on being named heir and asks what she herself thinks House Vale requires from whichever man eventually wears the Crown.
- Elowen answers simply: 'Peace.' Oren does not dismiss the answer. He presses gently on what peace means in a succession struggle, arguing that peace without a settled Crown is merely delay and that someone must ultimately submit or prevail. He asks Elowen whether House Vale's desired peace means neutrality at any cost or a ruler strong enough to end the contest. The exchange tests whether Elowen can distinguish the desired outcome from the means required to obtain it.
