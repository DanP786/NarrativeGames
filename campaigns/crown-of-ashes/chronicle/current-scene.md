# Current scene

- Location: Veyrhold — Bell Street physician's house, upstairs sickroom
- Time: morning after the Southbank dead-drop surveillance
- Scene significance: climactic
- Situation: Mara survived an attempt to silence her by burning her lamp-oil stall. She has a burned forearm, smoke inhalation, and a head wound. Garrick and Elowen are with her under heavy Vale protection after the physician permits only a brief conversation.

## Recent beats
- Mara tells Garrick she did not know what the men paying her were doing. She never knew the principal's proper name but says she knows his face, explaining why someone may have tried to kill her.
- Elowen goes to Mara's bedside rather than remaining beside Garrick. She presses a cool flannel to Mara's head and tells her it will be all right because Garrick has men guarding both the building and street.
- Mara responds to Elowen's gentler approach. Her fear does not vanish, but her breathing steadies enough to continue speaking. Garrick allows Elowen to handle the immediate rapport rather than crowding the injured woman.
- Mara explains that she was recruited months earlier by a man who paid her to pass packets and watch for simple signals. She believed it was smuggling, bribery, or private noble business and says she never knew about the convoy attack until after it happened.
- She identifies the man who personally gave her instructions as the same Merrow household guard she met after the dead drop. She knows him by the first name 'Bennet.' Vale has not yet verified his full identity.
- Mara says Bennet is not the man she fears most. On two occasions she saw Bennet report in person to a better-dressed older man whom Bennet addressed as 'Master Harrow.' She can describe Harrow but does not know his true name.
- Mara's description: late forties or early fifties, greying dark hair, narrow face, missing the tip of the left little finger, educated speech, and a distinctive gold signet bearing a black tower over three waves. This heraldic or personal device has not yet been identified in canon.
- Mara says the second burned body in her stall may be the man who attacked her: she remembers being struck, smelling lamp oil, then hearing someone else enter and a struggle before losing consciousness. She cannot identify who fought the attacker or who the corpse is.
- Garrick now has a physical description and signet device that can be investigated without immediately exposing Mara's survival.
