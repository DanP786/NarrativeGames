# Current scene

- Location: House Vale's Veyrhold residence — Garrick's solar
- Time: evening, 19th of Harvestwane, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: After Prince Oren's afternoon presentation to the great houses, Garrick and Elowen return to the Vale residence. Oren's faction has proposed marriage between Elowen and Oren's younger brother, Lord Adrian. Garrick is using the offer as Elowen's first direct lesson in political analysis.

## Recent beats
- Rowan Vale died in the cathedral melee after charging Cassian Veyne, who killed him in front of Elowen. Prince Alaric was also killed, leaving the royal succession open.
- Garrick told Elowen that Rowan's death leaves her his provisional heir and that she must begin learning politics and governance.
- At a parley, Garrick and Cassian agreed to seven days of mutual restraint in which neither house attacks the other and House Vale declares for no claimant.
- Prince Oren entered Veyrhold and asserted a rival claim. He deliberately acknowledged Elowen as Garrick's surviving child, signaling awareness of House Vale's succession problem.
- That evening Oren proposed Elowen marry his younger brother, Lord Adrian, with Crown recognition of Elowen's inheritance in exchange for an eventual Vale alliance.
- Garrick asks Elowen what Oren is truly buying rather than what the proposal explicitly promises. Elowen identifies Vale resources and, with effort, the legitimizing value of Garrick's public support. Garrick explains Oren also seeks to remove Elowen from the marriage market before a rival can gain House Vale through her.
- Elowen independently notices that accepting Adrian during the seven-day restraint would violate the substance of Garrick's agreement with Cassian. Garrick confirms this and says he will not accept during the restraint.
- Elowen then asks what happens if she marries Adrian and Oren fails to become king. Garrick treats this as the correct next question. He explains that the marriage's value depends heavily on Oren's success: if Oren loses politically but survives, Adrian may still bring royal blood and useful connections but House Vale could be tied to a defeated faction; if Oren is attainted or killed after losing, Adrian himself could become a dangerous liability or target. If Cassian wins after Vale openly backs Oren through the marriage, Cassian could regard House Vale as an enemy house and challenge the very inheritance guarantees Oren promised. Garrick concludes that Oren is asking House Vale to bear much of the downside while offering rewards contingent on his own victory, which is another reason not to hurry.
