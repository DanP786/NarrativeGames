# Current scene

- Location: House Merrow's Veyrhold townhouse — ballroom, among a cluster of young noblewomen
- Time: evening, 6th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: Elowen has just completed a conspicuous second dance with Adrian. Familiar young noblewomen are teasing her about Adrian and Cassian when Lady Emelie arrives fresh from dancing with Cassian.

## Recent beats
- Elowen's friends press her on whether she enjoyed Adrian's company rather than whether she knows him well enough to marry him.
- Flushed and cringing under the interrogation, Elowen says, 'Please stop.' Her friends begin to relent rather than overriding the request.
- Lady Emelie interrupts the circle before the teasing can fully dissolve. She appears visibly pleased with herself after her dance with Cassian Veyne.
- Emelie does not know the content of Elowen and Cassian's earlier conversation. She has only public observations: they spoke for a conspicuously long time, then Elowen remained at the refreshments while Cassian approached Emelie for a dance.
- Emelie remarks with deliberate casualness that Lord Veyne proved more agreeable company than his reputation suggests and notes that he sought her out personally for the dance.
- Her smugness is socially competitive rather than evidence of a hidden arrangement with Cassian. She is plainly enjoying the status of having attracted the attention of a major claimant and expects the other ladies to notice.
- The interruption shifts the group's attention away from Elowen's embarrassment and toward Emelie's account of her dance, while giving Elowen an opportunity either to stay quiet, question Emelie for information, tease her back, or leave the group.
