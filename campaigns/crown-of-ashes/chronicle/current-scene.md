# Current scene

- Location: House Merrow's Veyrhold townhouse — a quieter side gallery off the ballroom
- Time: later evening, 6th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: Later in the ball, Elowen and her trusted correspondent find a quiet interval away from the main cluster of guests and make good on their whispered information bargain.

## Recent beats
- Elowen had asked her friend for household-level succession intelligence: which undeclared houses are leaning toward Oren or Cassian, what they want, and what they fear. Her friend agreed to share what she personally hears while excluding her father's private correspondence.
- In return, the friend asked why Cassian crossed the ballroom to dance with Emelie and whether Elowen knows of genuine interest between them.
- In the side gallery, the friend reports three pieces of intelligence with source caveats. First, House Dacre is publicly undeclared but her uncle believes Dacre currently leans toward Oren because its elderly lord fears a disputed succession more than Veyne power and places heavy weight on Oren's cleaner legal case; this is family-table reporting, not proof of a commitment.
- Second, she has repeatedly heard that House Corren is receptive to Cassian, chiefly because Corren wants stronger guarantees against raids and believes Cassian is more likely to answer border violence with soldiers rather than councils. She labels this as repeated household gossip from men who deal with Corren retainers, not a formal declaration.
- Third, she says Lord Merrow is being courted by both camps and that her mother believes Merrow wants a concrete Crown appointment or council influence before committing, but she warns this last point is inference rather than something Lord Merrow has stated.
- The friend also says the social rumor of the evening is already moving toward an Adrian-Elowen possibility because of their second dance, though she distinguishes this clearly from evidence of an agreement.
- Elowen pays her side of the bargain by revealing that she herself suggested Cassian dance with Emelie after realizing her long conversation with him was attracting attention. She explains that Cassian recognized the usefulness of redirecting gossip and accepted the suggestion.
- Elowen truthfully says she has no evidence Cassian has genuine romantic interest in Emelie. He noticed Emelie only after Elowen pointed her out, and whatever occurred during their dance is known only to them. Thus Emelie's hopes may be real, but Elowen cannot substantiate them.
- The friend is delighted by the revelation but understands it is part of their private exchange, not permission to humiliate Emelie publicly. The bargain gives Elowen several leads to report to Garrick while preserving the distinction between sourced intelligence, repeated rumor, and inference.
