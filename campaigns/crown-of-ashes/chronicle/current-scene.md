# Current scene

- Location: House Vale's Veyrhold residence — Garrick's solar
- Time: evening, 19th of Harvestwane, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: After Prince Oren's afternoon presentation to the great houses, Garrick and Elowen return to the Vale residence. Oren's faction has proposed marriage between Elowen and Oren's younger brother, Lord Adrian. Garrick is using the offer as Elowen's first direct lesson in political analysis.

## Recent beats
- Rowan Vale died in the cathedral melee after charging Cassian Veyne, who killed him in front of Elowen. Prince Alaric was also killed, leaving the royal succession open.
- Garrick told Elowen that Rowan's death leaves her his provisional heir and that she must begin learning politics and governance.
- At a parley, Garrick and Cassian agreed to seven days of mutual restraint in which neither house attacks the other and House Vale declares for no claimant.
- Prince Oren entered Veyrhold and asserted a rival claim. He deliberately acknowledged Elowen as Garrick's surviving child, signaling awareness of House Vale's succession problem.
- That evening Oren proposed Elowen marry his younger brother, Lord Adrian, with Crown recognition of Elowen's inheritance in exchange for an eventual Vale alliance.
- Garrick asks Elowen what Oren is truly buying rather than what the proposal explicitly promises. Elowen hesitantly attempts the analysis: she identifies House Vale's soldiers and resources, then, with effort, recognizes that Oren also wants Garrick's public support because other houses may follow House Vale. Garrick confirms the core answer and adds that Oren is also trying to secure Elowen before another claimant can use her marriage to gain House Vale. He treats her imperfect answer as the beginning of instruction rather than expecting expertise she was never taught.
