# Current scene

- Location: House Vale's Veyrhold residence — music room
- Time: evening, 24th of Harvestwane, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: Five days have passed since Oren's marriage proposal. Rowan has been buried. Elowen has spent the intervening days accompanying Garrick to meetings and beginning an exhausting political education while House Vale remains publicly neutral. The seven-day restraint with Veyne expires in two days. This evening Elowen has a rare interval at the piano when an unexpected formal delivery arrives from House Veyne.

## Recent beats
- Rowan Vale died in the cathedral melee after charging Cassian Veyne, who killed him in front of Elowen. Prince Alaric also died, leaving the royal succession open.
- Garrick told Elowen that Rowan's death leaves her his provisional heir and began educating her in politics and governance.
- Garrick and Cassian agreed to seven days of mutual restraint in which neither house attacks the other and House Vale declares for no claimant.
- Prince Oren asserted a rival claim and offered a marriage between Elowen and his younger brother Adrian in exchange for an eventual Vale alliance and guarantees concerning Elowen's inheritance.
- Elowen recognized both that accepting during the restraint would violate the substance of Garrick's agreement with Cassian and that marriage to Adrian could endanger House Vale if Oren ultimately lost. Garrick declined to commit to Oren during the unsettled succession.
- Over the following five days Rowan is formally buried. Elowen attends in mourning and continues to suffer intrusive memories of his death, though she performs the expected public rituals without incident.
- Garrick begins bringing Elowen to selected meetings. She remains inexperienced and quiet but is gradually learning to connect bargains, alliances, and dynastic consequences rather than treating each conversation in isolation.
- Political pressure increases as Oren and Cassian gather supporters. Rumours concerning Elowen's marriage circulate despite no announced betrothal, reflecting her newly visible status as Vale's provisional heir.
- On the evening of 24 Harvestwane, Elowen is in the Vale music room at the piano when Garrick enters carrying a sealed formal communication delivered by a Veyne herald. The black wax bears Cassian Veyne's seal. Garrick has not yet opened it.
