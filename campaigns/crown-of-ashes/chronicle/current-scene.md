# Current scene

- Location: House Vale's Veyrhold residence — Garrick's solar
- Time: late morning, 4th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: The ambush evidence review has broadened into Garrick teaching Elowen how Vale's neutrality functions as leverage. Elowen proposes using her own courtship prospects to preserve Vale's importance and gather information.

## Recent beats
- Garrick explains that Oren and Cassian want more than Vale soldiers: they want Vale's legitimacy, resources, roads, fortifications, bannermen, and dynastic alignment through Elowen's marriage.
- Garrick says Vale should remain useful without becoming owned: solve neutral civic problems, preserve its forces and treasury, maintain communication with both camps, and make limited bargains with hard boundaries.
- Elowen asks whether she should court men from either side to remind both factions that her marriage remains an option and perhaps retrieve information that could help Garrick decide.
- Garrick takes the suggestion seriously but distinguishes social courtship from espionage. He agrees that Elowen should be visibly available to conversation and carefully managed attention from more than one political camp, because uncertainty over her eventual marriage preserves leverage. He does not want her making promises, implying secret commitments, or attempting crude seduction for secrets.
- Garrick tells Elowen that useful information can be gathered simply by asking men about their plans, priorities, allies, and expectations and noticing what they boast about, avoid, or assume. Her role is to make people want to persuade her, not to pretend affection she does not feel or put herself in compromising private situations.
- Garrick notes that Adrian is an obvious Oren-aligned possibility because a marriage offer has already existed. On the Veyne side, Cassian's agreement requires notice of his own major marriage alliances but no Cassian-Elowen match has been proposed; Garrick does not manufacture one. He says Elowen may converse with Cassian or Veyne-aligned nobles when propriety and security permit, but she is not to treat Cassian as a suitor unless he actually becomes one.
- Garrick sets boundaries: Elowen may receive and encourage respectable public attention, ask questions, report what she learns, and decline to commit. Any formal offer, private meeting, gift of unusual value, request for secrecy, or attempt to bind Vale must come to Garrick immediately.
