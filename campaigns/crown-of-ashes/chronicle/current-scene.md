## Recent beats
- Elowen's suspicious friend says Elowen must explain later why she appears to know something about Cassian's dance with Emelie.

- In exchange, the friend wants the true reason Cassian crossed the ballroom to ask Emelie to dance and, if Elowen knows it, whether Cassian said anything that suggests genuine interest in Emelie. Elowen has not yet accepted or paid this counterprice.
- Emelie and the other women remain occupied with speculation and do not hear the whispered bargain.
