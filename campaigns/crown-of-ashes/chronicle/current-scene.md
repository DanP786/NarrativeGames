In exchange, Vale must remain neutral between Cassian and Oren for thirty days and give prior notice before a marriage compact binds Elowen to either claimant's immediate family.

- Garrick explains that Oren is already married to Princess Maris of Dathen and therefore offered his unmarried younger brother Adrian to Vale, while Cassian remains personally free to form a new marriage alliance.
- Elowen asks whether Oren has offered Adrian's hand to other houses. Garrick says he has no confirmed evidence of a second formal offer and will not present rumor as fact. He considers it entirely plausible that Oren's agents have sounded out other houses or kept alternatives available, because Adrian is too valuable a dynastic asset to leave idle during a succession struggle. Garrick explains that until a betrothal is concluded, House Vale should assume Oren may redirect Adrian elsewhere if another alliance becomes more useful.
