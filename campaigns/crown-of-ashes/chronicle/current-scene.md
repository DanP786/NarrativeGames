# Current scene

- Location: Vale residence in Veyrhold — Lord Garrick's solar
- Time: afternoon, several days after 7th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: climactic
- Situation: A man believed to have participated in the Vale grain-convoy ambush has been captured alive in Veyrhold. A wounded Vale guard recognized him as the attacker he cut across the face, and the prisoner bears a fresh matching wound. He was seized from servants' lodgings attached to House Dacre's townhouse.

## Recent beats
- Elowen cuts short her courtship ride with Adrian after Garrick's urgent summons. Adrian accepts the interruption without demanding access to Vale intelligence and says they will reschedule.
- Garrick tells Elowen the captive was recognized by one of the wounded convoy guards and has the matching facial wound, making his connection to the ambush substantially stronger than the earlier badge evidence.
- The man was taken from servants' lodgings associated with House Dacre, which is publicly undeclared and had been reported secondhand as possibly leaning Oren because its elderly lord fears civil war and favors Oren's cleaner legal claim.
- Elowen asks whether Vale knows Dacre's motives.
- Garrick says no motive is established and refuses to equate the prisoner's lodging with orders from Lord Dacre. The captive may be a Dacre retainer, a hired man using Dacre property, someone sheltered without the lord's knowledge, or evidence deliberately positioned to implicate Dacre.
- Garrick reports that the prisoner has so far refused to name an employer. He has admitted being present at the convoy attack only after being confronted with the identifying wound, but claims he was hired through an intermediary and never met the person who ultimately ordered the strike. This confession establishes participation, not sponsor.
- The prisoner says his instructions were to strike the escort, ensure Veyne badges were visible, and withdraw rather than fight to annihilation if resistance became costly. This supports the prior inference that the attack itself and its attribution were central objectives.
- He claims payment and instructions passed through a man he knew only as 'Harrow,' whose identity is not yet established. Vale has not verified whether Harrow is real, an alias, or a lie.
- Garrick has not yet confronted House Dacre because doing so could cause the intermediary network to disappear and because word of the arrest is already leaking. He needs to decide whether to conceal the prisoner's survival, pressure Dacre immediately, or use the prisoner to draw out whoever hired him.
