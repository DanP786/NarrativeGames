# Current scene

- Location: Veyrhold, royal capital — Vale residence, Garrick's private solar
- Time: afternoon, 15th of Frostwane, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: Garrick, Elowen, and Cassian privately confront a coordinated rumor campaign mixing a false sexual-assault allegation with the true private fact that Elowen killed an attacker at the tollhouse. Garrick believes Elowen's denial; Cassian knows firsthand it is false and intends to absorb Elowen's killing into his own public account.

## Recent beats
- Elowen worries that admitting she concealed the killing may make Garrick doubt her denial of the sexual rumor. Garrick firmly separates the two matters and says he already believes her; Cassian independently confirms there was no sexual contact, coercion, kiss, or intimate touching during their isolation.
- Elowen identifies another person who could know details of the tollhouse encounter: 'There is one other person. Lord Halvern…'
- Tomas Halvern was physically present during the tollhouse fight and its aftermath and therefore had direct access to more of the true operational details than the abandoned quarry prisoner. He is consequently a necessary investigative lead.
- Neither Garrick nor Cassian treats Halvern's knowledge as proof of betrayal. Halvern fought alongside them, helped them escape, transported the badly wounded Cassian to the physician, and later provided his identity/account while the recovered papers materially supported his story. Accusing him without evidence would be both unjustified and strategically foolish.
- Garrick distinguishes between Halvern himself originating the rumors and information leaking through Halvern's household, servants, messengers, rescuers, or people he later told. He orders that Halvern be approached discreetly as a witness/source of the information chain rather than seized or accused.
- Cassian notes that Halvern could directly know Elowen fought and used a knife at the tollhouse, making his information chain especially important to trace. The false sexual rumor could then have been attached later by someone who acquired genuine details.
- The investigation now has two principal lines: determine what became of the abandoned quarry prisoner and trace every person through whom Tomas Halvern's knowledge of the tollhouse events could have passed.
- Marriage articles remain unsigned and active. Garrick and Cassian are temporarily aligned in protecting Elowen and identifying the source of the smear.
