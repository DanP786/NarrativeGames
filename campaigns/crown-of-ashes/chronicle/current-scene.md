- Time: evening, 29th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: climactic
- Situation: Garrick's warning suggests a second unmarked party, possibly including Bennet Carrow, is moving north behind the expedition with fresh horses. Cassian proposes leaving early, taking a rough secondary route, and using a decoy on the main Greyfen road.

## Recent beats
- Elowen gives Cassian Garrick's private letter in full. Cassian treats the disclosure as operational trust and returns the letter after reading it.
- Cassian proposes a route change and initially suggests a multi-rider decoy, but asks Elowen to decide with him because this is mission strategy rather than immediate battlefield command.

- OOC player direction: use this as an opportunity for Cassian and Elowen themselves to become the two-person diversion, creating genuine isolated forced proximity while the larger joint force proceeds by the secondary route.
- Cassian recognizes that two riders are less conspicuous and can plausibly appear to be outriders or principals traveling ahead, but ordinary soldiers would not necessarily draw pursuit from anyone specifically tracking the expedition's leaders.
- Cassian therefore develops the dangerous version of Elowen's idea: he and Elowen can take the main road together while both escorts quietly take the charcoal road. If the trailing party is watching for the Veyne claimant and Vale heir rather than merely counting soldiers, the decoy becomes convincing.
- This plan would leave Cassian and Elowen temporarily without their house escorts, though Cassian remains armed and highly experienced while Elowen is unarmed and inexperienced. It sharply increases the practical power imbalance and dependence between them.
- Cassian does not yet impose the plan because Elowen's personal exposure is substantial and Garrick explicitly told her to use her judgment. He presents the option and makes clear that if she accepts, she will obey his tactical instructions without debate while they are alone.
- No final acceptance from Elowen has yet occurred.
