- Time: morning, 30th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: climactic
- Situation: Elowen is exposed at the tollhouse as bait while wounded Cassian watches from concealment. The occupants have demonstrated that they know her identity and expected her arrival. A bound person is concealed in the unmarked wagon.

- Elowen reaches the wagon's rear/side just ahead of the sentry's grasp and gets a first clear look beneath the disturbed canvas: the captive is a bound adult man, bruised and gagged, dressed in travel clothes of better quality than a common laborer's. His identity is not yet established.
