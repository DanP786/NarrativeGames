# Current scene

- Location: House Merrow's Veyrhold townhouse — ballroom, among a cluster of young noblewomen
- Time: evening, 6th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: Elowen is among familiar young noblewomen after her second dance with Adrian. Lady Emelie is excitedly considering whether Cassian's dance with her might indicate interest.

## Recent beats
- Emelie recounts that Cassian asked about her family's western vineyards during their dance. The women speculate about whether the attention might have political or romantic meaning, though none has evidence that Cassian intends to court Emelie.
- Elowen tells Emelie she and Cassian would make a good match. When Emelie vulnerably asks whether Elowen truly thinks they would suit, Elowen smiles and answers, 'Of course.'
- Privately, Elowen reasons that Emelie's house is minor enough that even if it declared for Cassian in hope of a marriage, it would not materially strengthen him in the succession. This is Elowen's own assessment, not an established strategic fact known to the room.
- Elowen also privately remembers Emelie's capacity for cruelty and Rowan's death at Cassian's hand, and thinks that Emelie and Cassian deserve each other. None of these private judgments are available to the other women.
- Emelie receives Elowen's endorsement at face value and becomes more hopeful, while still attempting to maintain courtly modesty. She says she would hardly presume anything from one dance but admits she would not object if Cassian asked for another.
- Elowen does not reveal that she was the one who suggested Cassian dance with Emelie. Cassian remains elsewhere in the ballroom and is not privy to the women's speculation.
