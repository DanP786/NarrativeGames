# Current scene

- Location: Veyrhold, royal capital — Vale residence, morning room
- Time: late morning, 15th of Frostwane, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: Several days after Garrick accepted Cassian's marriage proposal in principle, marriage articles are still being negotiated. Garrick and Elowen are together when a newly arrived missive reports a vicious rumor circulating in Veyrhold: that Cassian sexually forced himself on Elowen while they were isolated during the Greyfen/priory ordeal and that the sudden marriage proposal is meant to conceal her dishonor.

## Recent beats
- The rumor is false. Established canon contains no sexual assault or sexual contact between Cassian and Elowen during the expedition/priory period. Their forced proximity involved pursuit, wounds, evidence work, mundane recovery, arguments, humor, and growing reluctant trust.
- Garrick reads the report aloud in Elowen's presence. The allegation is framed as court rumor rather than verified accusation; the missive reports that versions are already moving through servants, lesser courtiers, and political households.
- Elowen reacts physically before she can maintain composure: she drops her teacup. It strikes the table/crockery and spills hot tea across the tabletop and the front of her dress. This visible shock is not evidence for the rumor; Garrick knows Elowen well enough not to draw that conclusion automatically.
- Garrick's anger becomes controlled stillness. Because he already knows Cassian killed Rowan and nevertheless accepted the marriage only under severe strategic necessity, an allegation that Cassian also assaulted Elowen would be potentially settlement-ending if true. He therefore needs the truth directly from Elowen rather than relying on rumor or political convenience.
- The rumor is politically sophisticated: it can damage Cassian as a claimant, recast the marriage as coercive concealment, stain Elowen's sexual reputation in a conservative noble culture, pressure Garrick to break the proposed alliance, and potentially push Vale back toward Oren/Adrian. Its timing shortly after the proposed match makes deliberate sabotage plausible, though no source is yet established.
- Garrick immediately prevents servants from turning Elowen's spilled tea into a spectacle and intends to clear the room before questioning her privately. He will not publicly repeat the allegation unnecessarily.
- The marriage articles remain unsigned. No decision about cancelling, accelerating, or publicly announcing the marriage has yet been made in response to the rumor.
