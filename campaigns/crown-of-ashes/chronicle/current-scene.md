# Current scene

- Location: House Merrow's Veyrhold townhouse — refreshments at the ballroom edge
- Time: evening, 6th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: After visible dances with Julian Merrow and Adrian, Elowen is speaking directly with Cassian Veyne. Their conversation has shifted from politics into music, creating unexpectedly ordinary social ground between members of hostile houses.

## Recent beats
- Cassian explains that he attends balls to observe the alliances people reveal through social choices. He then asks Elowen whether she has always enjoyed balls.
- Elowen answers readily that she loves the music. Cassian notices the greater ease of this personal answer and asks what she plays.
- By ingrained courtly habit, Elowen replies with a light teasing deflection: 'Can a lady not simply enjoy listening?' The line is the sort of flirtatious social jest she would ordinarily use with men at court, but she immediately becomes visibly tense and annoyed with herself because Cassian is not an ordinary court acquaintance.
- Elowen then concedes, 'Piano.' Cassian can observe her jaw tightening after the jest but cannot know precisely why she regrets it.
- Cassian does not exploit the accidental flirtatious tone or embarrass her by calling attention to it. He accepts the answer and asks whether she plays merely because noblewomen are expected to, or because she genuinely likes playing, giving Elowen room to discuss a subject in which she has real confidence.
