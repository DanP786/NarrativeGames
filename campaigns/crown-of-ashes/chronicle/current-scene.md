## Recent beats
- Rowan Vale died in the cathedral melee after charging Cassian Veyne, who killed him in front of Elowen. Prince Alaric also died, leaving the royal succession open.
- Garrick told Elowen that Rowan's death leaves her his provisional heir and began educating her in politics and governance.
- Garrick and Cassian agreed to seven days of mutual restraint in which neither house attacks the other and House Vale declares for no claimant.
- Prince Oren asserted a rival claim and offered a marriage between Elowen and his younger brother Adrian in exchange for an eventual Vale alliance and guarantees concerning Elowen's inheritance.
- Elowen recognized both that accepting during the restraint would violate the substance of Garrick's agreement with Cassian and that marriage to Adrian could endanger House Vale if Oren ultimately lost. Garrick declined to commit to Oren during the unsettled succession.
- Cassian later offered to extend the Vale-Veyne restraint for thirty days. If Garrick publicly names Elowen his lawful heir, Cassian promises recognition of her succession and lawful issue. In exchange, Vale must remain neutral between Cassian and Oren for thirty days and give prior notice before a marriage compact binds Elowen to either claimant's immediate family.
- Garrick explained that Cassian's concern with Elowen's marriage is strategic: as Vale's heir, her husband could gain influence over Vale lands, descendants, bannermen, and armies.
- Elowen asked why Garrick would continue dealing with Cassian after Rowan's death. Garrick admitted his hatred and desire for vengeance but distinguished Rowan's death in armed combat from murder. He refuses to risk Elowen and House Vale in a blood feud merely to satisfy grief.
- After Elowen apologized for questioning him, Garrick told her that private questioning is now part of her duty as his heir; House Vale must speak with one voice publicly, but he wants her to challenge and understand decisions in private.
- Elowen asks whether Garrick therefore intends to accept Cassian's thirty-day extension. Garrick says he intends to accept the core bargain but not Cassian's draft unchanged. He will counter that Vale neutrality lasts thirty days and Cassian recognizes Elowen's succession, but any marriage-notice obligation must be reciprocal: if Cassian seeks or enters a marriage alliance with Oren's immediate blood or another great house during the term, Vale must receive equivalent prior notice. Garrick explains that if Cassian wants warning before Vale changes the balance through marriage, Vale should receive warning before Cassian does the same.
