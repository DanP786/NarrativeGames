# Current scene

- Location: Palace of the White Hart, Veyrhold — council chamber
- Time: late afternoon, 29th of Harvestwane, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: A dispute over grain convoys has become a proxy struggle between Oren and Cassian for practical authority in Veyrhold. Garrick has told Elowen to present her proposed neutral Vale escort to the council herself.

## Recent beats
- Master Pell reports that two eastern grain convoys have failed to arrive and Veyrhold has roughly eleven days of grain at normal rationing.
- Oren offers royal household troops to escort the next convoy. Cassian objects that this would let one claimant control the food road and proposes a mixed neutral escort instead. Oren argues mixed command would be inefficient.
- Elowen recognizes that a single neutral Vale escort could address both objections and whispers the idea to Garrick.
- Garrick identifies the risks but judges the proposal worthwhile if temporary, limited to escort duty, and under civic authority. He tells Elowen to present it herself.
- Elowen initially begins too quietly and is lost beneath the council argument. She gathers herself and speaks again more loudly, proposing that House Vale provide a single-command escort for the next grain convoy while remaining under civic direction and claiming no control over the road or grain.
- The room falls quiet because the newly proclaimed Vale heir has spoken publicly in council. Oren and Cassian both turn their attention to her. Garrick does not rescue or elaborate for her, leaving the proposal to stand in Elowen's own words.
