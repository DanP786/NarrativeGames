## Recent beats
- Rowan Vale died in the cathedral melee after charging Cassian Veyne, who killed him in front of Elowen. Prince Alaric also died, leaving the royal succession open.
- Garrick told Elowen that Rowan's death leaves her his provisional heir and began educating her in politics and governance.
- Prince Oren asserted a rival claim and offered a marriage between Elowen and his younger brother Adrian in exchange for an eventual Vale alliance and guarantees concerning Elowen's inheritance.
- Cassian offered a thirty-day extension of Vale-Veyne restraint: recognition of Elowen's succession in exchange for continued Vale neutrality and advance notice before Elowen is bound by marriage to either claimant's immediate family.
- Garrick secured reciprocal notice concerning any major marriage alliance Cassian might make. Cassian accepted, with the clarification that notice must come before a binding promise rather than merely before public announcement.
- Garrick and Cassian seal the thirty-day compact at Saint Orra. Cassian formally recognizes Elowen as heir to House Vale.
- After Cassian leaves, Garrick tells Elowen he will use the month to proclaim her heir, secure bannermen's oaths, strengthen Vale defenses, investigate marriage possibilities, and judge which claimant is least dangerous.
- When Garrick says Elowen must eventually judge between Cassian, Oren, or neither, Elowen asks whether 'Cassian' means marriage. Garrick clarifies that he means supporting Cassian's claim to the Crown, not marrying him. No marriage between Elowen and Cassian has been proposed by either side. Garrick acknowledges that such a marriage is theoretically possible because both are unmarried and politically valuable, but states that it would presently be an extraordinary and deeply hostile match: Cassian killed Rowan, the houses share a blood feud, and marrying Vale's heir directly to Cassian would bind House Vale to his cause more completely than ordinary political support. Garrick has no current intention of proposing it.
