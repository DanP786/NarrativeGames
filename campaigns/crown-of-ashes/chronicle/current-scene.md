# Current scene

- Location: northern road out of Veyrhold, en route to Greyfen Priory
- Time: early morning, 29th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: The joint Vale-Veyne expedition is underway. Cassian has identified Elowen's sidesaddle as a tactical vulnerability and ordered that she change to an astride saddle and divided riding garments at the next stop.

## Recent beats
- Cassian acknowledges Vale's captain formally commands Vale soldiers and that Cassian cannot simply convert them into Veyne troops, while insisting his battlefield judgment will govern his own instructions in immediate danger.
- Cassian notices Elowen riding sidesaddle and orders that it be replaced at the next stop. His reasoning is tactical: Elowen is unarmed and inexperienced in combat, and sidesaddle makes it harder to remain mounted, maneuver, or flee over broken ground during an attack.
- Cassian treats the saddle change as an instruction rather than a request, reinforcing the intended practical power imbalance while not claiming formal political authority over House Vale.
- Elowen protests, cheeks burning, 'It would be improper for me to ride like that.'
- Cassian does not mock her embarrassment or sexualize the issue. He recognizes that riding astride violates the conventions Elowen has been raised under, but judges the danger more important than propriety.
- Cassian answers that impropriety is survivable and a broken neck is not. He notes they are traveling without banners precisely because ordinary public conventions have already yielded to operational necessity. He says her cloak and divided riding garments will preserve modesty well enough for the road.
- He does not soften the order: at the next suitable stop the saddle will be changed. Elowen has not yet complied or refused.
