# Current scene

- Location: House Merrow's Veyrhold townhouse — ballroom
- Time: evening, 6th of Emberfall, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: Elowen is dancing with Adrian, Prince Oren's brother and the subject of Oren's earlier marriage offer. Adrian has personally asked permission to court her.

## Recent beats
- Adrian distinguishes his own interest from Oren's political marriage offer and asks whether Elowen wants to be courted. Elowen warmly says that she does.
- Adrian then asks formally whether he himself may court her.
- Elowen remembers Garrick's instruction not to give away value without learning or receiving something in return. Because she is untrained in politics and persuasion, she does not execute a sophisticated bargain, but her courtly training lets her phrase a socially graceful condition rather than simply accepting.
- Elowen tells Adrian she would welcome his courtship if he will give her honesty in return: she does not want to hear only what Prince Oren would want her to hear, and if Adrian is to know her as himself, she wants to know what he himself believes about the struggle and his own future.
- The condition does not bind Adrian to reveal secrets or betray Oren, but it establishes a reciprocal expectation and invites candid information. Adrian recognizes the bargain and agrees that he will not knowingly give Elowen his brother's words disguised as his own, while reserving that there may be matters he cannot disclose.
- Adrian's courtship is therefore personally welcomed but remains informal and politically nonbinding; any formal marriage terms still require Garrick. The public dance continues to signal warmth without constituting a Vale alliance.
