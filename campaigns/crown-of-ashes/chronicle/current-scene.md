# Current scene

- Location: Palace of the White Hart, Veyrhold — west gallery
- Time: afternoon, 29th of Harvestwane, Year 312 of the Crown
- Scene significance: charged
- Situation: Three days after Elowen's public proclamation as Vale heir, Garrick brings her to a palace council on unrest, food supply, and rival armed followings. During a recess, Prince Oren approaches Elowen directly and tests her political judgment.

## Recent beats
- Garrick and Cassian sealed a thirty-day restraint including Veyne recognition of Elowen's succession, Vale neutrality, and reciprocal advance notice of major marriage alliances.
- Garrick publicly named Elowen his lawful heir. Vale bannermen swore to uphold her succession despite some openly questioning her sex, demeanor, and inexperience.
- On 29 Harvestwane, a broad council gathers at the Palace of the White Hart. Oren and Cassian both attend under separate armed entourages.
- During a recess Oren approaches Elowen while Garrick is occupied nearby. He asks what she believes House Vale requires from the eventual king. Elowen answers, 'Peace.'
- Oren presses on whether Vale wants neutrality at any cost or a ruler capable of imposing peace.
- Elowen cautiously replies that if peace could be achieved without further bloodshed, House Vale might lean toward whoever can secure it. Her pause and qualifier make clear she is not claiming authority to pledge the house.
- Oren recognizes that Elowen has given him a political criterion rather than an endorsement. He says that is more useful than flattery and asks whether she would apply the same standard to him and Cassian alike: if Cassian could secure peace without further bloodshed and Oren could not, would she advise Garrick accordingly? Garrick remains several paces away and has not yet intervened.
