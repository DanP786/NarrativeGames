# Campaign setup

Created: 14 August 2026 (real-world date)
Slug: crown-of-ashes

## Onboarding answers

**Genre / references:** Brutal grounded medieval fantasy in a Game of Thrones-like register: succession politics, noble houses, war, betrayal, sex, class, violence, and severe consequences. Original world and cast.

**Core romance:** Slow-burn heterosexual enemies-to-lovers. The PC is a young noblewoman; the eventual male love interest is in his late twenties or early thirties and holds substantially greater political/status power. He is one of the most dangerous contenders in a succession crisis. There is already blood between their houses when the story begins, and early in play he will personally make a ruthless political choice that harms Elowen or House Vale, turning inherited hostility into personal enmity. The romance is not guaranteed and must develop through play.

**Voice:** Third person, present tense.

**Prose style:** Cinematic and ruthless — fast, punchy political confrontations, battles, executions and betrayals; modern-readable register with strong sensory detail. Romantic scenes deliberately slow down and give subtext and small behavioural changes room to breathe.

**Length:** Open-ended sandbox. No fixed endpoint and no act tracker.

**Tone / content posture:** Nothing categorically off the table. Graphic violence, executions, torture, sexual content, political cruelty, betrayal, horror, and moral despair may appear when fictionally appropriate.

**Stakes / scope:** Begins with a personal/familial perspective inside a realm-wide succession crisis. The realm's ruler has died and rival factions are moving toward civil war.

**Agency posture:** Merciless realism. Bad decisions can destroy relationships, cost limbs, lose wars, or kill people. Competence helps; nobody has plot armour.

**Death rule:** Hard death enabled. Elowen can die in genuinely lethal circumstances.

**Mind-reading rule:** None. Thoughts are completely private; there is no telepathy or supernatural emotion-reading.

**Main thread:** Elowen wants to remain useful to and loved by the family that raised her to believe fulfilling her place earns both. Her house is drawn into a succession struggle against a powerful claimant whose house already shares blood-feud history with House Vale. The campaign asks what loyalty costs when family, survival, power, and eventually forbidden attachment begin demanding incompatible things.

**Character seed:** Lady Elowen Vale, age 23. A quiet, observant noblewoman raised to be show rather than substance. She is an accomplished singer and pianist, polished for display at noble gatherings. She rarely speaks and has little political skill. She is loyal to her family because that loyalty was deeply taught to her. She has no combat training.

**Confirmed starting skills:** Adept Music & Performance; Novice Courtly Etiquette, Observation, Noble Household Knowledge; notable Untrained Politics, Combat, Persuasion/Public Speaking.

**Opening situation:** The king is dead. The great houses gather in the capital for his funeral, publicly mourning while privately counting soldiers and allies. House Vale arrives already carrying a blood-feud with the house of one of the most dangerous claimants.
