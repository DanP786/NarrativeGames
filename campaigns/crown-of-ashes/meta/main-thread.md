# Main thread

- Status: sketch
- Central question: What does loyalty cost when family, survival, power, and forbidden attachment demand incompatible things?
- PC want: Elowen wants to remain useful to and loved by House Vale by fulfilling the place her family taught her to occupy.
- Antagonist / obstacle: A royal succession crisis is pulling the great houses toward civil war. One of the most dangerous claimants comes from a house already bound to House Vale by blood-feud, and will soon make that inherited conflict personally Elowen's.
- Establishing arc: Bring the claimant or his proxy on-screen; make House Vale's expectations of Elowen concrete; expose the old blood between the houses; force Elowen to encounter the difference between being displayed and having agency.
