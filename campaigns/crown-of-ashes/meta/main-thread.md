# Main thread

- Status: active
- Central question: What does loyalty cost when family, survival, power, and forbidden attachment demand incompatible things?
- PC want: Elowen has grown from an ornamental Vale daughter into Lady Veyne and an active political partner. She wants to preserve Vale interests and her own agency while helping Cassian secure a stable crown rather than merely seize one.
- Antagonist / obstacle: King Edric II is dead and Prince Alaric also died in the cathedral violence. The succession remains unresolved between Lord Cassian Veyne and Prince Oren. Months without a crowned ruler have pushed Ardelane toward a legitimacy crisis: noble indecision, institutional strain and popular unrest are converging, and the people are close to rebellion as demands for a king intensify. The tollhouse evidence also shows an organized coercion/intelligence network mapping and manipulating houses, with a historical payment link to House Dacre but no proof Dacre ordered the attacks.
- Active pressure: Cassian and Elowen are now married, joining Veyne and Vale. Elowen has been cultivating noblewomen and undecided houses through listening, hospitality and a stability/continuity argument. Lady Aramelle Sorne conveyed the western houses' central fear: houses that opposed or hesitated during the succession may be treated as enemies once Cassian becomes king. A credible distinction between succession opposition and treason against a crowned king could unlock western support. Cassian understands that Elowen's social work is producing political intelligence as well as persuasion. The prolonged vacancy now leaves little time for gradual coalition-building before disorder becomes rebellion.
- New leverage: Elowen has obtained letters/declarations of support from two neighboring kingdoms for Cassian's claim. The kingdoms and exact terms are not yet established. Foreign backing could strengthen Cassian's legitimacy and signal regional confidence, but must be used carefully so domestic rivals cannot frame him as a foreign-imposed king.
- Romance vector: Elowen and Cassian are deeply affectionate newlyweds and increasingly function as political partners. Their old Vale-Veyne enmity and the coercive origins of the marriage remain part of their history, but Cassian now explicitly recognizes Elowen's judgment and political contribution. Their relationship is tested less by whether affection exists than by whether love, ambition, fear and power can coexist without Cassian controlling Elowen or Elowen disappearing into his claim.
