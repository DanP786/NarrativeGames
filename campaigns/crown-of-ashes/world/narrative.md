# Narrative

King Edric II is dead. Lady Elowen Vale, twenty-three and raised to ornament her house rather than steer it, arrived in Veyrhold for the funeral as Ardelane's great houses began choosing sides in a succession crisis.

At the funeral, Elowen's elder brother Rowan publicly renounced claimant Lord Cassian Veyne and destroyed House Vale's attempted neutrality. When the confrontation became armed, Rowan used Elowen as a shield; she broke from him and became isolated near the Veyne position. Cassian saved her from an advancing royal guardsman and seized her into his lines. Rowan then charged Cassian despite Garrick's order to stop. Cassian ran Rowan through in front of Elowen.

Prince Alaric was subsequently killed near the royal dais. His death halted the melee without resolving it: armed factions now face one another in a tense stalemate inside the sealed Cathedral of the Nine Bells. Elowen, blood-soaked and in shock beside Rowan's corpse, remains separated from her father and physically within Cassian Veyne's position. The old Vale-Veyne feud is now living blood between Elowen and Cassian.
