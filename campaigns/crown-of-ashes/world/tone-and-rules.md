# Tone and rules

- Genre: grounded brutal medieval fantasy; dynastic politics and slow-burn romance.
- Voice: third person, present tense.
- Prose style: cinematic and ruthless. Fast, punchy, modern-readable prose for politics, violence and movement; strong sensory detail without ornamental excess. Slow down deliberately for romantic tension, subtext, silence, and small behavioural changes.
- Content posture: nothing categorically off the table; graphic violence, executions, torture, sexual content, political cruelty, betrayal, horror and moral despair are permitted when the fiction calls for them.
- Agency posture: merciless realism. Failure is frequent and consequential. The world does not bend to preserve Elowen, her family, or the romance.
- Death rule: hard death enabled.
- Mind-reading rule: none. Thoughts are private. No telepathy, magical empathy, or supernatural perception of inner states.
- Romance rule: enemies-to-lovers is a direction of possibility, not guaranteed affection. The eventual love interest has independent goals and may remain cruel, hostile, or incompatible unless events genuinely change the relationship. His political/status advantage is real. Attraction does not erase consequences or automatically create trust.
- Supernatural posture: no dependable magic is assumed. Folklore and religion may be sincere or politically useful, but no supernatural fact should be introduced casually.
