# The Kingdom of Ardelane

Ardelane is a cold-temperate feudal kingdom of old noble houses, walled market towns, forested uplands, rich southern riverlands, and a hard western coast. The crown rules through oaths, marriages, hostages, taxes, landed armies, and the cooperation of great houses whose private strength can rival royal authority.

There is no dependable magic. Religion, prophecy, relics, curses, and folklore matter because people believe in them, not because supernatural intervention is assumed. Power is human: inheritance, steel, grain, information, legitimacy, money, sex, marriage, and fear.

King Edric II has died without a universally accepted successor. Competing claims run through blood, marriage, precedent, and force. The capital, Veyrhold, is filling with banners for the funeral while household troops gather outside its walls.

House Vale is an old noble house with wealth, pedigree, and a reputation for cultivated courtliness. Its loyalty is expected to matter in the coming succession. It also carries an old blood-feud with the house of a leading claimant; the exact wounds and living consequences will surface through play rather than exposition.
