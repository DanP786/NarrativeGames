# Lord Cassian Veyne

## Description, voice, mannerisms
A tall nobleman in his early thirties, severe in mourning black, carrying a sword even inside the Cathedral of the Nine Bells. He speaks without hurry and does not raise his voice merely to command attention. When challenged, he tends to become more precise rather than louder.

## Traits
- Ruthlessly self-possessed
- Legitimacy-conscious
- Strategically patient until patience costs leverage
- Remembers injuries as obligations, not emotions

## Short-term goals
- Prevent Prince Alaric's succession from becoming accepted fact through ceremony and repetition.
- Force the assembled great houses to acknowledge that the crown remains contested.
- Identify which houses will resist or support his claim.

## Long-term goals
- Take the throne of Ardelane or ensure no settlement leaves House Veyne politically vulnerable.
- Break the coalition of houses capable of excluding him from power.

## Methods & lines
- Will: exploit law and precedent, intimidate, bargain, use hostages and marriages, deploy armed force, punish defiance when strategically useful.
- Won't: surrender a serious claim merely to preserve courtly peace; pretend an enemy is a friend without purpose.
- Last resort: civil war and lethal force against rival claimants or houses that make coexistence impossible.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 10 / 100 — Elowen is a Vale, and the houses share old blood.
- Affection: 15 / 100 — no personal relationship; inherited hostility dominates first impressions.
- Respect: 25 / 100 — knows little of Elowen beyond her status and house.
- Loyalty: 0 / 100 — none.
- Fear: 5 / 100 — does not presently regard Elowen personally as dangerous.
- Suspicion: 60 / 100 — assumes members of House Vale may serve their house's interests against him.
- Resentment: 65 / 100 — House Vale is tied to unresolved blood-feud history with House Veyne.

## Relationship to player
Hostile. Elowen is currently significant to Cassian primarily because she is Lord Garrick Vale's daughter and therefore part of an enemy house. He has no established personal understanding of her yet.

## History with player
- No direct personal history established before King Edric II's funeral.
- Their houses carry an unresolved blood-feud predating their first meaningful interaction.

## Knowledge
- Knows Elowen is Lord Garrick Vale's daughter if he has occasion to identify her by house and position.
- Knows House Vale's political weight and the history of the Vale-Veyne feud.
- Does not know Elowen's private feelings, loyalties beyond ordinary dynastic expectation, or personal ambitions.

## Relationships with other NPCs
- Prince Alaric: rival claimant; Cassian rejects the attempt to establish Alaric's succession as settled through the funeral proclamation.
