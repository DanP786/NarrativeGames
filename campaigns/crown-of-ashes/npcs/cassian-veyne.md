# Lord Cassian Veyne

## Description, voice, mannerisms
A tall nobleman in his early thirties, severe in mourning black. He speaks without hurry and does not raise his voice merely to command attention. When challenged, he tends to become more precise rather than louder.

## Traits
- Ruthlessly self-possessed
- Legitimacy-conscious
- Strategically patient until patience costs leverage
- Remembers injuries as obligations, not emotions

## Short-term goals
- Consolidate his position after Alaric's death without allowing rival factions to unite against him.
- Secure temporary Vale neutrality and prevent immediate Vale-Veyne war.
- Determine the dynastic consequences of Rowan Vale's death, including Elowen's changed political value.

## Long-term goals
- Take the throne of Ardelane or ensure no settlement leaves House Veyne politically vulnerable.
- Break the coalition of houses capable of excluding him from power.

## Methods & lines
- Will: exploit law and precedent, intimidate, bargain, use hostages and marriages, deploy armed force, punish defiance when strategically useful; preserve useful enemies when their survival offers leverage.
- Won't: surrender a serious claim merely to preserve courtly peace; pretend an enemy is a friend without purpose.
- Last resort: civil war and lethal force against rival claimants or houses that make coexistence impossible.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 7 / 100 — Elowen factually confirmed his account despite having strong personal cause to hate him; this is evidence of honesty under pressure, not allegiance.
- Affection: 10 / 100 — no personal relationship; Rowan's killing makes immediate warmth implausible.
- Respect: 29 / 100 — Elowen answered truthfully rather than simply echoing the most hostile version available to her house.
- Loyalty: 0 / 100 — none.
- Fear: 8 / 100 — Elowen herself remains unarmed, but her family has immediate cause for vengeance.
- Suspicion: 66 / 100 — still an enemy noblewoman, though her willingness to state an inconvenient fact slightly complicates his assumptions.
- Resentment: 72 / 100 — the feud remains renewed in living blood by Rowan's attack and death.

## Relationship to player
Hostile and politically watchful. Cassian saved Elowen from a royal guardsman and pulled her into his lines, partly preserving valuable leverage; he later retained her during the withdrawal. Moments after her rescue, Rowan charged Cassian and Cassian killed him at close range in front of Elowen. At the next day's parley, Elowen quietly confirmed that Cassian had not threatened her with a blade. Cassian registers that she did not lie merely to injure him, but he has no reason yet to mistake this for sympathy or trust.

## History with player
- Their houses carry an unresolved blood-feud predating their first meaningful interaction.
- Elowen was present when Rowan publicly renounced Cassian's claim.
- Elowen and Cassian briefly met one another's gaze across the cathedral after Garrick shoved Rowan back.
- Cassian witnessed Rowan mistreat Elowen and use her as cover.
- During the melee, Cassian killed a royal guardsman advancing through Elowen and pulled her behind the Veyne line.
- Rowan then charged Cassian. Cassian met the attack and ran Rowan through the torso at close range. Rowan died in front of Elowen, his blood reaching her gown and hands.
- During the following day's parley, Cassian proposed a mutual public account protecting Elowen from rumours of abduction or voluntary defection. When Garrick accused him of holding Elowen at swordpoint, Cassian denied that specific claim. With Garrick's permission, Elowen quietly confirmed Cassian was right.

## Knowledge
- Knows Elowen is Lady Elowen Vale, Lord Garrick Vale's daughter and Rowan's sister.
- Knows House Vale's political weight and the history of the Vale-Veyne feud.
- Knows Rowan publicly opposed him, mistreated Elowen, used her as cover, and finally attacked him armed.
- Knows Elowen witnessed him kill Rowan and has visible reason to fear or hate him.
- Has observed Elowen as quiet, deferential, nervous, and attentive during the parley.
- Knows Elowen will state an inconvenient fact accurately when directly permitted to answer, even when a lie might damage him.
- Suspects Rowan's death may have increased Elowen's dynastic importance, but Garrick has not confirmed her status as heir.
- Does not know Elowen's private feelings about Rowan, Cassian, her attraction to his appearance, or her fears about succession.

## Relationships with other NPCs
- Prince Alaric: dead rival claimant.
- Rowan Vale: killed him in armed combat after Rowan charged during the cathedral melee.
- Garrick Vale: hostile lord whose son Cassian killed; currently negotiating temporary restraint and political neutrality.
