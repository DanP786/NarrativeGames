# Lord Cassian Veyne

## Description, voice, mannerisms
A tall nobleman in his early thirties, severe in mourning black, carrying a sword even inside the Cathedral of the Nine Bells. He speaks without hurry and does not raise his voice merely to command attention. When challenged, he tends to become more precise rather than louder.

## Traits
- Ruthlessly self-possessed
- Legitimacy-conscious
- Strategically patient until patience costs leverage
- Remembers injuries as obligations, not emotions

## Short-term goals
- Survive and control the armed confrontation without allowing Alaric's faction to seize him.
- Prevent Prince Alaric's succession from becoming accepted fact.
- Use any leverage the melee provides, including Elowen Vale's accidental presence within his lines.
- Ensure Rowan Vale's public challenge carries a visible cost when strategically useful.

## Long-term goals
- Take the throne of Ardelane or ensure no settlement leaves House Veyne politically vulnerable.
- Break the coalition of houses capable of excluding him from power.

## Methods & lines
- Will: exploit law and precedent, intimidate, bargain, use hostages and marriages, deploy armed force, punish defiance when strategically useful; preserve useful enemies when their survival offers leverage.
- Won't: surrender a serious claim merely to preserve courtly peace; pretend an enemy is a friend without purpose.
- Last resort: civil war and lethal force against rival claimants or houses that make coexistence impossible.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 8 / 100 — Elowen is a Vale, and her brother has publicly opposed him.
- Affection: 15 / 100 — no personal relationship; inherited hostility dominates first impressions.
- Respect: 27 / 100 — she has remained composed earlier but is now visibly panicked in a lethal situation for which she has no training.
- Loyalty: 0 / 100 — none.
- Fear: 5 / 100 — does not presently regard Elowen personally as dangerous.
- Suspicion: 61 / 100 — her accidental isolation appears genuine, though she remains an enemy noblewoman.
- Resentment: 68 / 100 — the old blood-feud has been publicly renewed by Rowan's challenge.

## Relationship to player
Hostile and now immediately controlling. Elowen became isolated beside the Veyne position during the cathedral melee. Cassian killed a royal guardsman who was about to strike through her and pulled her behind his own line. Saving her was compatible with preserving a valuable enemy noblewoman and gaining leverage over House Vale; it is not evidence of affection. Elowen is now physically within his control during the crisis.

## History with player
- No direct personal history established before King Edric II's funeral.
- Their houses carry an unresolved blood-feud predating their first meaningful interaction.
- Elowen was present when her brother Rowan publicly renounced Cassian's claim.
- After Rowan was shoved back into the Vale benches, Elowen and Cassian briefly met one another's gaze across the cathedral before she looked away.
- Cassian witnessed Rowan grip Elowen roughly and blame her while venting frustration from Garrick's rebuke.
- Cassian witnessed Rowan shove Elowen ahead of himself as cover when weapons were drawn inside the sealed cathedral.
- During the melee, Cassian killed a royal guardsman advancing through Elowen and seized her by the arm, pulling her behind the Veyne line and ordering her to stay behind him if she wanted to survive.

## Knowledge
- Knows Elowen is Lady Elowen Vale, Lord Garrick Vale's daughter.
- Knows House Vale's political weight and the history of the Vale-Veyne feud.
- Has witnessed Rowan Vale publicly declare House Vale's refusal to kneel to him.
- Has observed Rowan treating Elowen as a safe target for displaced anger and then as physical cover under threat.
- Has seen Elowen panic when isolated in lethal violence; he knows she lacks battlefield composure, not why she feels anything beyond visible fear.
- Does not know Elowen's private feelings, loyalties beyond ordinary dynastic expectation, or personal ambitions.

## Relationships with other NPCs
- Prince Alaric: rival claimant; the cathedral confrontation has become armed bloodshed.
- Rowan Vale: open dynastic enemy whose public bravado Cassian has seen give way to using his unarmed sister as cover.
