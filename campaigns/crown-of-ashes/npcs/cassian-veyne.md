# Lord Cassian Veyne

## Description, voice, mannerisms
A tall nobleman in his early thirties, severe in mourning black. He speaks without hurry and does not raise his voice merely to command attention. When challenged, he tends to become more precise rather than louder.

## Traits
- Ruthlessly self-possessed
- Legitimacy-conscious
- Strategically patient until patience costs leverage
- Remembers injuries as obligations, not emotions

## Short-term goals
- Consolidate his position after Alaric's death without allowing rival factions to unite against him.
- Preserve Vale neutrality while he competes with Prince Oren for support.
- Use recognition of Elowen's succession to stabilize Vale-Veyne relations without surrendering strategic warning over a potential Vale marriage alliance.

## Long-term goals
- Take the throne of Ardelane or ensure no settlement leaves House Veyne politically vulnerable.
- Break the coalition of houses capable of excluding him from power.

## Methods & lines
- Will: exploit law and precedent, intimidate, bargain, use hostages and marriages, deploy armed force, punish defiance when strategically useful; preserve useful enemies when their survival offers leverage.
- Won't: surrender a serious claim merely to preserve courtly peace; pretend an enemy is a friend without purpose.
- Last resort: civil war and lethal force against rival claimants or houses that make coexistence impossible.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 13 / 100 — Elowen has repeatedly acted to preserve Cassian's life during the ambush and aftermath; political distrust remains substantial.
- Affection: 21 / 100 — Shared danger, Elowen's care during his blood loss, and her unexpected teasing while sitting at his bedside are beginning to complicate hostility, but affection remains nascent.
- Respect: 49 / 100 — Elowen stayed functional under pursuit, improvised effectively despite lack of training, and preserved Cassian through the journey to medical care.
- Loyalty: 0 / 100 — none.
- Fear: 8 / 100 — Elowen herself remains inexperienced, but her dynastic value and House Vale's power remain significant.
- Suspicion: 56 / 100 — Her conduct during shared danger marginally reduces suspicion of personal manipulation, though political caution remains.
- Resentment: 64 / 100 — the blood-feud remains real, but immediate mutual dependence and increasingly human exchanges complicate inherited hostility.

## Relationship to player
Hostile and politically watchful, now complicated by forced operational dependence. Cassian saved Elowen from a royal guardsman and pulled her into his lines, partly preserving valuable leverage; he later retained her during the withdrawal. Moments after her rescue, Rowan charged Cassian and Cassian killed him at close range in front of Elowen. Elowen later factually confirmed that Cassian had not threatened her with a blade. Cassian formally recognized Elowen as Garrick's heir as part of a thirty-day political restraint. At the Merrow ball, their first sustained conversation without negotiators or armed men began distinguishing inherited enmity from future choices. During the Greyfen expedition, Cassian and Elowen have been forced into isolated danger together after a diversion plan was betrayed. Cassian exercises substantial tactical power over the inexperienced Elowen, while she retains Vale's political authority and equal claim to evidence. Shared survival has begun producing moments of unguarded humor and care without resolving the feud, Rowan's death, or the political imbalance.

## History with player
- Their houses carry an unresolved blood-feud predating their first meaningful interaction.
- Elowen was present when Rowan publicly renounced Cassian's claim.
- Cassian witnessed Rowan mistreat Elowen and use her as cover.
- During the melee, Cassian killed a royal guardsman advancing through Elowen and pulled her behind the Veyne line.
- Rowan then charged Cassian. Cassian met the attack and ran Rowan through the torso at close range. Rowan died in front of Elowen.
- During the following day's parley, Elowen quietly confirmed Cassian had not threatened her with a blade.
- Cassian subsequently offered formal recognition of Elowen's succession in exchange for extended Vale neutrality and advance notice of marriage alignment. Garrick countered with reciprocal notice concerning Cassian's own major marriage alliances, which Cassian accepted.
- At House Merrow's ball, Elowen and Cassian had their first sustained personal conversation. Elowen admitted she loves playing piano because soldiers do not lose their lives if she plays poorly; Cassian admitted he rides alone for respite and wants the Crown rather than claiming reluctant duty.
- Cassian told Elowen that inherited enemies need not remain enemies forever, though he did not ask forgiveness for Rowan's death or deny the feud.
- During the Greyfen expedition Cassian ordered Elowen out of her sidesaddle for tactical safety and taught her enough astride riding to survive a pursuit.
- Three riders chased them into an abandoned quarry. Cassian ordered Elowen away and fought the pursuers; one wounded him along the ribs. Elowen bound the wound under his instruction.
- A surviving pursuer revealed that Elowen was to be taken alive as political leverage and Cassian killed. Their orders were updated after the diversion plan was made, proving the operation leaked.
- Cassian told Elowen she is badly trained for the life she inherited rather than incapable, and should make Garrick correct the gaps in her education.
- At the tollhouse, Elowen's thrown knife spoiled an archer's shot at Cassian, allowing him to reach the building. Cassian's wound later reopened badly during the fighting.
- Elowen maintained hard pressure on Cassian's wound after he collapsed unconscious and throughout the wagon journey to a priory physician, materially contributing to his survival.
- After Cassian briefly wakes in the priory infirmary and jokes about Elowen reading his last rites, Elowen teases him that she is actually reading a marriage proposal to Lady Fiona, whom he danced with at Merrow's ball. This is a notably casual, playful exchange compared with their earlier hostility.

## Knowledge
- Knows Elowen is Garrick Vale's only surviving child and publicly acknowledged heir.
- Knows House Vale's political weight and the history of the Vale-Veyne feud.
- Knows Elowen witnessed him kill Rowan and has reason to fear or hate him.
- Has observed Elowen as quiet, deferential, nervous, attentive, and increasingly capable of direct political questioning.
- Knows Elowen's marriage is a major strategic lever for House Vale and rival claimants.
- Knows Elowen plays piano and genuinely loves playing.
- Has observed during the Greyfen expedition that Elowen learns quickly under pressure, obeys tactical orders during immediate danger, and can function despite fear and pain.
- Knows Elowen chose to share Garrick's private operational warning with him in full.
- On regaining partial consciousness at the priory, finds Elowen sitting beside him with the recovered papers; she responds to his dry remark by teasing him about a fictional marriage proposal involving Lady Fiona from Merrow's ball.
- Does not know Elowen's private feelings about Rowan, Cassian, her attraction to his appearance, the softness she has begun to feel, or her fears beyond what she outwardly reveals.

## Relationships with other NPCs
- Prince Alaric: dead rival claimant.
- Prince Oren: active rival claimant gathering support in Veyrhold.
- Rowan Vale: killed him in armed combat after Rowan charged during the cathedral melee.
- Garrick Vale: hostile but functioning negotiating counterpart; Cassian has secured a thirty-day restraint and Vale neutrality in exchange for recognition of Elowen's succession.
