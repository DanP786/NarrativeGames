# Lord Cassian Veyne

## Description, voice, mannerisms
A tall nobleman in his early thirties, severe in mourning black. He speaks without hurry and does not raise his voice merely to command attention. When challenged, he tends to become more precise rather than louder.

## Traits
- Ruthlessly self-possessed
- Legitimacy-conscious
- Strategically patient until patience costs leverage
- Remembers injuries as obligations, not emotions

## Short-term goals
- Finalize marriage articles with House Vale that bind Vale to his claim while preserving enough Vale autonomy for Garrick to accept the settlement.
- Prevent Elowen from being married into Oren's coalition through Adrian.
- Identify and counter the coordinated rumor campaign targeting Elowen and the Vale-Veyne match.
- Continue tracing the coercion network exposed by the tollhouse papers without allowing the proven leak to compromise the investigation.
- Recover fully from the tollhouse wound without publicly appearing weak.

## Long-term goals
- Take the throne of Ardelane or ensure no settlement leaves House Veyne politically vulnerable.
- Make the Vale-Veyne marriage durable enough to survive inherited hatred, succession conflict, and future dynastic disputes.

## Methods & lines
- Will: exploit law and precedent, intimidate, bargain, use hostages and marriages, deploy armed force, punish defiance when strategically useful; preserve useful enemies when their survival offers leverage; take politically useful blame onto himself when doing so protects a dynastic asset or ally.
- Won't: surrender a serious claim merely to preserve courtly peace; pretend an enemy is a friend without purpose.
- Last resort: civil war and lethal force against rival claimants or houses that make coexistence impossible.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 16 / 100 — Elowen has repeatedly acted to preserve Cassian's life; political distrust remains substantial.
- Affection: 25 / 100 — Shared danger, care, humor, and now a public attack on Elowen's reputation increasingly engage his protective instincts, though affection remains nascent and unacknowledged.
- Respect: 52 / 100 — Elowen survived the expedition, saved his life, and acted decisively under attack.
- Loyalty: 5 / 100 — prospective marital/dynastic obligation is beginning to produce concrete protective conduct before the marriage is finalized.
- Fear: 8 / 100 — Elowen herself remains inexperienced, but her dynastic value and House Vale's power remain significant.
- Suspicion: 53 / 100 — political caution remains, but nothing in the rumor campaign makes him suspect Elowen.
- Resentment: 61 / 100 — the blood-feud remains real, but shared danger and political partnership continue complicating inherited hostility.

## Relationship to player
Hostile and politically watchful, now becoming prospective spouses through an unequal dynastic bargain. Cassian saved Elowen from a royal guardsman and later killed Rowan after Rowan charged him. Their Greyfen/tollhouse ordeal created forced operational dependence, and Elowen materially saved Cassian's life after his wound reopened. Several days at the priory produced reluctant familiarity and humor. After returning to Veyrhold, Cassian used political necessity to propose marriage directly to Garrick as the core of a Vale-Veyne settlement. Garrick accepted in principle. Elowen is openly angry at the process. When coordinated rumors later target Elowen's sexual reputation and reveal the true fact that she killed an attacker at the tollhouse, Cassian publicly/within Vale's council denies her involvement and takes the killings onto himself and Tomas Halvern, protecting Elowen from a damaging narrative without first asking her.

## History with player
- Their houses carry an unresolved blood-feud predating their first meaningful interaction.
- Elowen was present when Rowan publicly renounced Cassian's claim.
- Cassian witnessed Rowan mistreat Elowen and use her as cover.
- During the melee, Cassian killed a royal guardsman advancing through Elowen and pulled her behind the Veyne line.
- Rowan then charged Cassian. Cassian met the attack and ran Rowan through the torso at close range. Rowan died in front of Elowen.
- During the following day's parley, Elowen quietly confirmed Cassian had not threatened her with a blade.
- Cassian subsequently offered formal recognition of Elowen's succession in exchange for extended neutrality and advance notice of marriage alignment. Garrick countered with reciprocal notice concerning Cassian's own major marriage alliances, which Cassian accepted.
- At House Merrow's ball, Elowen and Cassian had their first sustained personal conversation. Elowen admitted she loves playing piano because soldiers do not lose their lives if she plays poorly; Cassian admitted he rides alone for respite and wants the Crown rather than claiming reluctant duty.
- Cassian told Elowen that inherited enemies need not remain enemies forever, though he did not ask forgiveness for Rowan's death or deny the feud.
- During the Greyfen expedition Cassian ordered Elowen out of her sidesaddle for tactical safety and taught her enough astride riding to survive a pursuit.
- Three riders chased them into an abandoned quarry. Cassian ordered Elowen away and fought the pursuers; one wounded him along the ribs. Elowen bound the wound under his instruction.
- A surviving pursuer revealed that Elowen was to be taken alive as political leverage and Cassian killed. Their orders were updated after the diversion plan was made, proving the operation leaked.
- Cassian told Elowen she is badly trained for the life she inherited rather than incapable, and should make Garrick correct the gaps in her education.
- At the tollhouse, Elowen's thrown knife spoiled an archer's shot at Cassian, allowing him to reach the building. Cassian's wound later reopened badly during the fighting.
- During the tollhouse fighting Elowen killed an attacker with a knife in close struggle. She later maintained hard pressure on Cassian's wound after he collapsed unconscious and throughout the wagon journey to a priory physician, materially contributing to his survival.
- At the priory Elowen teases Cassian about a fictional marriage proposal from Lady Fiona, and they develop the first casual humor of their relationship.
- After returning to Veyrhold, Cassian formally proposes marriage to Elowen through Garrick as a dynastic settlement. Garrick accepts in principle subject to extensive articles.
- In the west gallery afterward, Elowen tells Cassian she is angry with everyone because nobody asked what she wanted. Cassian admits he chose to make the proposal and will not falsely claim he had no choice.
- A false rumor subsequently spreads that Cassian sexually forced himself on Elowen during their isolation and that the marriage conceals her dishonor. Garrick informs Cassian through a trusted channel after Elowen directly denies the allegation.
- A second rumor/report then claims Elowen killed a man during the expedition. This underlying fact is true, though Elowen had not volunteered it on returning home. When the matter is raised in Garrick's presence with Elowen and Cassian present, Cassian immediately states the claim is false and says that he and Lord Tomas Halvern alone killed the attackers, deliberately shielding Elowen's role.

## Knowledge
- Knows Elowen is Garrick Vale's only surviving child and publicly acknowledged heir unless Garrick later produces a lawful son with superior right.
- Knows House Vale's political weight and the history of the Vale-Veyne feud.
- Knows Elowen witnessed him kill Rowan and has reason to fear or hate him.
- Knows Elowen killed an attacker in close combat at the tollhouse and that this was survival rather than an execution or aggressive killing.
- Knows Elowen did not sexually engage with him during their isolation and that the assault rumor is wholly false.
- Knows Elowen's reputation is being targeted by at least two rapidly circulating stories, one wholly fabricated and one built around a true but private detail from the ambush.
- Has observed Elowen as quiet, deferential, nervous, attentive, increasingly capable of direct political questioning, and functional under severe pressure.
- Knows Elowen's marriage is a major strategic lever for House Vale and rival claimants.
- Knows Elowen plays piano and genuinely loves playing.
- Knows Elowen chose to share Garrick's private operational warning with him in full and later saved his life through sustained wound pressure.
- Knows Elowen is angry about the arranged marriage process and specifically knows she questions why he would choose a wife/potential queen who hates him.
- Does not know Elowen's private attraction to his appearance, the softness she has begun to feel, or her private thoughts about Rowan beyond what she outwardly reveals.

## Relationships with other NPCs
- Prince Alaric: dead rival claimant.
- Prince Oren: active rival claimant gathering support in Veyrhold.
- Rowan Vale: killed him in armed combat after Rowan charged during the cathedral melee.
- Garrick Vale: deeply hostile negotiating counterpart who has accepted the Cassian-Elowen marriage in principle only because he judges it Vale's strongest immediate survival settlement.
