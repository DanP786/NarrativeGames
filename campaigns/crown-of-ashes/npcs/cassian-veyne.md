# Lord Cassian Veyne

## Description, voice, mannerisms
A tall nobleman in his early thirties, severe in mourning black, carrying a sword even inside the Cathedral of the Nine Bells. He speaks without hurry and does not raise his voice merely to command attention. When challenged, he tends to become more precise rather than louder.

## Traits
- Ruthlessly self-possessed
- Legitimacy-conscious
- Strategically patient until patience costs leverage
- Remembers injuries as obligations, not emotions

## Short-term goals
- Survive and control the armed confrontation without allowing Alaric's faction to seize him.
- Prevent Prince Alaric's succession from becoming accepted fact.
- Retain Elowen Vale as leverage now that she is physically within his position and her brother is dead.

## Long-term goals
- Take the throne of Ardelane or ensure no settlement leaves House Veyne politically vulnerable.
- Break the coalition of houses capable of excluding him from power.

## Methods & lines
- Will: exploit law and precedent, intimidate, bargain, use hostages and marriages, deploy armed force, punish defiance when strategically useful; preserve useful enemies when their survival offers leverage.
- Won't: surrender a serious claim merely to preserve courtly peace; pretend an enemy is a friend without purpose.
- Last resort: civil war and lethal force against rival claimants or houses that make coexistence impossible.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 5 / 100 — Elowen is now the sister of a man Cassian has personally killed.
- Affection: 10 / 100 — no personal relationship; the killing of Rowan makes immediate warmth even less plausible.
- Respect: 27 / 100 — unchanged; little personal basis exists yet.
- Loyalty: 0 / 100 — none.
- Fear: 8 / 100 — Elowen herself remains unarmed, but her family now has immediate cause for vengeance.
- Suspicion: 68 / 100 — she is an enemy noblewoman whose brother has just attacked him.
- Resentment: 72 / 100 — the feud is now renewed in living blood by Rowan's attack and death.

## Relationship to player
Hostile and immediately controlling. Cassian saved Elowen from a royal guardsman and pulled her into his lines, partly preserving valuable leverage. Moments later Rowan charged him. Cassian killed Rowan at close range in front of Elowen. He has no expectation that she will regard the killing as justified merely because Rowan attacked first. Elowen's value as a political hostage has increased sharply.

## History with player
- Their houses carry an unresolved blood-feud predating their first meaningful interaction.
- Elowen was present when Rowan publicly renounced Cassian's claim.
- Elowen and Cassian briefly met one another's gaze across the cathedral after Garrick shoved Rowan back.
- Cassian witnessed Rowan mistreat Elowen and use her as cover.
- During the melee, Cassian killed a royal guardsman advancing through Elowen and pulled her behind the Veyne line.
- Rowan then charged Cassian. Cassian met the attack and ran Rowan through the torso at close range. Rowan died in front of Elowen, his blood reaching her gown and hands.

## Knowledge
- Knows Elowen is Lady Elowen Vale, Lord Garrick Vale's daughter and Rowan's sister.
- Knows House Vale's political weight and the history of the Vale-Veyne feud.
- Knows Rowan publicly opposed him, mistreated Elowen, and finally attacked him armed.
- Knows Elowen has just witnessed him kill her brother.
- Does not know Elowen's private feelings about Rowan, the killing, her family, or Cassian.

## Relationships with other NPCs
- Prince Alaric: rival claimant; the cathedral confrontation has become armed bloodshed.
- Rowan Vale: killed him in armed combat after Rowan charged him during the cathedral melee.
- Garrick Vale: enemy lord whose son Cassian has now killed and whose daughter is within Cassian's control.
