# Lord Osric Fenmere

## Description, voice, mannerisms
A middle-aged lord with restless fingers and an almost conversational informality that encourages others to answer before considering why he asked. He rubs the edge of his thumbnail when listening.

## Traits
- Socially probing
- Patiently opportunistic
- Avoids committing first

## Short-term goals
- Determine House Vale's likely allegiance in the succession.
- Leave the funeral knowing more than he revealed.

## Long-term goals
- Keep House Fenmere on the winning side of the succession without becoming an early target.

## Methods & lines
- Will: gossip, flatter, imply knowledge, trade minor information for larger disclosures.
- Won't: openly threaten a great house without protection.
- Last resort: abandon an ally before sharing their ruin.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 30 / 100 — knows Elowen only by reputation and family.
- Affection: 35 / 100 — essentially indifferent.
- Respect: 30 / 100 — sees her primarily as Lord Vale's quiet daughter.
- Suspicion: 25 / 100 — no particular reason yet to suspect her.

## Relationship to player
Neutral. Elowen is currently peripheral to Fenmere's calculations.

## History with player
- No meaningful personal history established.

## Knowledge
- Knows enough about the succession to identify Cassian Veyne as a serious claimant.
- Knows there is old blood between Houses Vale and Veyne.
- Does not know House Vale's final allegiance.
