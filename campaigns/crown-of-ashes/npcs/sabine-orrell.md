# Lady Sabine Orrell

## Status
Alive; newly exposed as the person who arranged the dagger warning at Lady Elowen Veyne's pillow.

## Description / social position
A widowed noblewoman of Veyrhold society. Exact age, appearance, house strength and family connections are not yet established.

## Relationship to Cassian Veyne
- Sabine and Cassian had a discreet affair several years before his marriage to Elowen.
- The affair ended without a promise of marriage.
- Cassian did not regard the relationship as continuing and had not seen Sabine privately since well before marrying Elowen.
- Sabine privately allowed the old affair to become entangled with fantasies of a permanent place beside Cassian that he had never promised her.

## Dagger plot
- After Cassian married Elowen, Sabine became jealous and resentful of Elowen's intimate and dynastic place beside him.
- Sabine arranged for a dagger to be placed at/on Elowen's pillow as intimidation and a possessive warning. She claims she did not intend an immediate assassination.
- Sabine did not enter Veyne House herself. She bribed a junior laundress who already had legitimate access through household linen work. The laundress concealed/carried the dagger through ordinary linen circulation and placed it at Elowen's pillow.
- Investigators eventually traced an unexplained payment/gift to the laundress; confession and the intermediary/payment trail exposed Sabine.
- Sabine acted from personal jealousy, not as part of the established Greyfell/courier/priest/Dacre conspiracy. No link between her and those threads is established.

## Current position
- Exposed and secured/detained sufficiently that she cannot immediately repeat the threat.
- Final punishment has not yet been established in play.

## Knowledge limits
- Knows of her own former affair with Cassian and her own dagger scheme.
- Do not give her knowledge of unrelated conspiracy facts without later canon support.
