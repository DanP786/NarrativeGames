# Lord Garrick Vale

## Description, voice, mannerisms
Elowen's father and head of House Vale. A controlled nobleman who dislikes giving information away cheaply. His voice tends toward measured formality; irritation shows first as stillness rather than volume.

## Traits
- Dynastically minded
- Controlled
- Protective of House Vale's standing
- Slow to forget injury

## Short-term goals
- Secure House Vale after Rowan and Alaric's deaths.
- Prevent further bloodshed while House Vale is exposed inside Veyrhold.
- Determine the consequences of Cassian's surviving claim.
- Begin preparing Elowen for her new position as provisional heir.

## Long-term goals
- Preserve House Vale's lands, bloodline, and influence through the succession crisis.
- Resolve the dynastic crisis created by Rowan's death, which leaves Elowen as his only surviving child.
- Exact or secure an answer for Rowan's death without destroying House Vale through reckless vengeance.

## Methods & lines
- Will: negotiate, conceal intentions, arrange political bargains, use family members to advance House Vale.
- Won't: publicly abase House Vale without overwhelming necessity.
- Last resort: violence or open rebellion if House Vale's survival requires it.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 65 / 100 — Elowen has been a dutiful daughter and has given him little reason to distrust her.
- Affection: 66 / 100 — nearly losing Elowen immediately after Rowan's death intensifies his paternal attachment, though it remains entangled with dynastic expectations.
- Respect: 36 / 100 — values her accomplishments and conduct but does not yet regard her as politically capable; necessity now forces him to begin treating her as someone who must learn.
- Loyalty: 78 / 100 — she is now his only surviving child as well as part of House Vale.
- Suspicion: 10 / 100 — does not presently expect her to work against him.
- Resentment toward Cassian Veyne: 95 / 100 — Cassian killed Rowan and held Elowen as leverage during the withdrawal.

## Relationship to player
Friendly. Garrick regards Elowen as his loved and dutiful daughter, now also his only surviving child. Rowan's death makes her survival personally and dynastically more important. His affection remains shaped by a deeply dynastic understanding of family. Garrick has now told Elowen that unless and until he fathers another legitimate son, she is his heir, making her marriage and conduct matters of House Vale's succession rather than merely advantageous alliance.

## History with player
- Raised Elowen within House Vale's expectations of noble femininity, display, obedience, and family loyalty.
- At King Edric II's funeral, Garrick watched Elowen become separated during the melee, saw Rowan die after charging Cassian, and was forced to leave Elowen temporarily within Cassian's lines while the armed withdrawal was negotiated.
- Once permitted to approach, Garrick called Elowen back to him and received her while Vale men prepared to recover Rowan's corpse.
- Hours after Rowan's death, Garrick summoned Elowen and told her that she is now his heir unless he later produces another legitimate son.

## Knowledge
- Knows House Vale's political calculations and the history behind its feud with House Veyne.
- Knows considerably more about the succession than Elowen does.
- Knows Elowen was physically within Cassian's control during the cathedral crisis and appears deeply shocked by Rowan's death.
- Knows he has raised Elowen for an ornamental and marital role rather than rule, and that her sudden position as heir leaves her politically underprepared.
- Does not know Elowen's private thoughts about Rowan, Cassian, or becoming his heir.
