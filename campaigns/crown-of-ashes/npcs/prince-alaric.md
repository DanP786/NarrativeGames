# Prince Alaric

## Status
Dead — killed during the armed melee at King Edric II's funeral, Session 01.

## Description, voice, mannerisms
King Edric II's publicly designated heir. Young enough that older lords watched him for signs of uncertainty. Formal under scrutiny; anger tightened his diction rather than loosening it.

## Traits
- Proud
- Conscious of royal dignity
- Politically pressured
- Sensitive to public challenges to legitimacy

## Final goals
- Secure recognition as King Edric II's successor.
- Defeat Cassian Veyne's challenge to his claim.

## Relationship to player at death
Neutral. Elowen was one member of a politically important house whose allegiance had been forced into opposition to Cassian by Rowan's public declaration. No meaningful personal relationship formed between Alaric and Elowen.

## History with player
- Elowen was present when Alaric was publicly named King Edric II's designated heir.
- Cassian Veyne challenged Alaric's succession during the funeral, and the confrontation escalated into armed bloodshed.
- Alaric was killed near the royal dais during the melee while Elowen was isolated among the Veyne position.

## Final knowledge
- Knew House Vale was politically important and that Rowan Vale had publicly opposed Cassian Veyne.
- Had no access to Elowen's private thoughts or intentions.

## Relationships with other NPCs
- Lord Cassian Veyne: rival claimant whose public challenge precipitated the confrontation that became armed bloodshed.
