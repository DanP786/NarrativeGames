# Prince Alaric

## Description, voice, mannerisms
King Edric II's publicly designated heir. Young enough that older lords still watch him for signs of uncertainty. Formal under scrutiny; anger tightens his diction rather than loosening it.

## Traits
- Proud
- Conscious of royal dignity
- Politically pressured
- Sensitive to public challenges to legitimacy

## Short-term goals
- Complete his father's funeral without allowing his designation as heir to be undermined.
- Secure visible acceptance from the great houses.

## Long-term goals
- Be crowned King of Ardelane and preserve his father's settlement of the succession.

## Methods & lines
- Will: invoke royal law, seek oaths, bargain with houses, use crown authority.
- Won't: publicly concede that Cassian's claim equals his own while he retains viable support.
- Last resort: armed suppression of rival claimants.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 35 / 100 — House Vale has not declared for him.
- Affection: 30 / 100 — no meaningful personal relationship with Elowen established.
- Respect: 35 / 100 — sees Elowen chiefly through her family's standing.
- Loyalty: 10 / 100 — no personal bond.
- Suspicion: 40 / 100 — all undeclared great houses are potential threats during the succession.

## Relationship to player
Neutral. Elowen is presently one member of a politically important but undeclared house.

## History with player
- No meaningful personal history established.

## Knowledge
- Knows House Vale is politically important and has not publicly declared its allegiance.
- May recognize Elowen as Lord Garrick Vale's daughter through court knowledge.
- Does not know her private thoughts or intentions.

## Relationships with other NPCs
- Lord Cassian Veyne: rival claimant who publicly challenged his designation during King Edric II's funeral.
