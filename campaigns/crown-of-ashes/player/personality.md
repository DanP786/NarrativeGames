# Personality and reputation

## How Elowen is perceived
Elowen began the crisis as a quiet, well-bred Vale daughter known chiefly for music and manners. She is now Lady Veyne and increasingly visible as an active political partner to Cassian. Her strength is not loud command: she listens, remembers anxieties, creates social safety, and frames stability in terms people can live with.

At court she can deliberately defer to Cassian in public without appearing diminished. The contrast is politically useful: Cassian presents cold authority and direction while Elowen supplies warmth, accessibility and continuity. Those who spend time with her have reason to understand that the deference is chosen rather than evidence that she lacks judgment.

## Reputation
- House Vale: Garrick's surviving child and an important dynastic bridge to House Veyne; no longer merely ornamental.
- House Veyne: Lady Veyne and Cassian's wife, increasingly functioning as a political partner rather than a decorative consort.
- Western / undecided nobility: emerging as a listener and intermediary who does not demand premature allegiance; her conversations have helped expose fear of post-succession reprisals.
- Foreign courts: has opened friendly cultural/diplomatic correspondence with eight courts; two have returned meaningful written support for Cassian's claim. The identities and exact terms are not established.
- Wider court: increasingly associated with the Vale-Veyne union, social warmth, and deliberate support of Cassian's authority.
