# Significant actions

- S01, Cathedral of the Nine Bells: When Rowan Vale shoved Elowen in front of himself as a shield during the armed confrontation, Elowen twisted free and quietly called her brother a coward. Rowan heard her.
- S01, Cathedral of the Nine Bells: Elowen witnessed Cassian Veyne run Rowan through after Rowan charged him armed. Rowan died in front of her, making the Vale-Veyne feud immediate and personal.
- S01, Greyfen/tollhouse crisis: Elowen acted under pursuit despite having no martial training, bound Cassian's wound under instruction, spoiled an archer's shot at him with a thrown knife, killed an attacker in a close survival struggle, and maintained pressure on Cassian's reopened wound through his collapse and transport to a physician, materially helping save his life.
- S01, Vale-Veyne settlement: Elowen objected openly to being treated as an object of dynastic negotiation. When presented with Cassian Veyne's settlement and Prince Adrian's serious alternative, she chose the Veyne settlement as stronger for House Vale.
- S01, marriage: Elowen married Cassian Veyne on the 16th of Deepfrost, formally joining House Vale and House Veyne. Their political marriage subsequently became a genuine affectionate partnership.
- S01, domestic coalition-building: Elowen cultivated noblewomen through tea, hospitality and listening rather than demands for allegiance. Lady Aramelle Sorne entrusted her with the western houses' central fear of punishment after the succession; Elowen conveyed the insight to Cassian without exceeding her authority or promising pardons, offices or immunity.
- S01, foreign outreach: Elowen independently opened friendly channels to eight foreign courts through letters, gifts and cultural envoys without making binding commitments for Cassian. Two courts returned meaningful written support for Cassian's claim.
- S01, foreign security awareness: Elowen instructed her traveling entertainers to report only naturally visible or heard signs of mobilization, hostile rhetoric, troop gathering, supply activity or court sentiment, giving Cassian another non-invasive source to compare with his own channels.
- S01, Prince Oren's ball: Elowen and Cassian deliberately presented a united political partnership. Elowen used chosen public deference to reinforce Cassian's image as a stable potential king while retaining warmth and social agency. She stayed focused on politics despite discovering Daphne's pregnancy, seeing Garrick repeatedly dance with Georgia, and receiving a covert note from Prince Adrian.
- S01, Prince Oren's ball: Elowen disclosed Adrian's covert warning to Cassian at the first safe opportunity and gave him the physical note once home. The note warns that the council may choose sooner than expected and that unnamed men are attempting to force the matter before winter worsens; its source and motive remain unverified.
