# Lady Elowen Veyne

- Age: 23
- Birth house: House Vale
- Status: Lady Veyne; wife of Lord Cassian Veyne; Lord Garrick Vale's surviving child after Rowan's death
- Current location: Veyne House, Veyrhold
- Current state: Home late at night after Prince Oren's ball, privately debriefing with Cassian before bed.

## Background
Elowen was raised to be show rather than substance: beautiful manners, music, composure, and the quiet accomplishments expected of a daughter displayed by an old house. She is an accomplished singer and pianist. Her family did not educate her to wield politics for herself. Quietness made her observant, but at the campaign's beginning she lacked practical political training.

Rowan's death made Elowen Garrick Vale's surviving child. The succession crisis, shared danger with Cassian, and the Vale-Veyne marriage forced her rapidly into political adulthood. She married Cassian on the 16th of Deepfrost, joining the two hostile houses. Their marriage began as a dynastic settlement negotiated through Garrick without Elowen first being asked, but it has become a deeply affectionate partnership.

## Temperament
Warm, observant and increasingly willing to speak directly when something matters. Elowen still prefers social intelligence, listening and hospitality to overt confrontation. She is capable of deliberate public deference without surrendering private judgment or agency.

## Current political role
- Works beside Cassian as a political partner in his claim to the crown.
- Cultivates noblewomen and undecided houses through hospitality, listening and arguments about stability rather than demanding allegiance.
- Independently opened friendly channels to eight foreign courts without making binding commitments; two have sent meaningful written support for Cassian's claim.
- Uses entertainers as informal cultural envoys who may report naturally visible signs of foreign mobilization or hostile sentiment, but they are not instructed to conduct invasive espionage.
- Helped surface the western houses' fear that losing the succession could make them targets of the eventual king.

## Goals
- Preserve House Vale's interests without disappearing into them.
- Preserve her own agency within marriage and politics.
- Help Cassian secure a stable crown rather than merely seize one.
- Build a marriage in which love, ambition and protection do not become control.

## Limitations
- No formal combat training.
- Political skill has been learned rapidly through lived experience rather than systematic education.
- Her growing influence can expose her to manipulation, retaliation and misread motives.

## Immediate personal circumstances
- Cassian and Elowen are deeply affectionate newlyweds and political partners.
- Rowan's death remains morally complicated: Elowen knows Rowan could be cruel, and Cassian has admitted he deliberately chose the lethal option once he had control of their fight rather than risk Rowan endangering Elowen again.
- Garrick Vale is beginning a courtship with Georgia, sister of Elowen's friend Daphne; no engagement is established.
- Daphne is visibly pregnant but did not tell Elowen before Oren's ball. The reason is unknown.
- Prince Adrian, Oren's brother and a former alternative suitor, covertly warned Elowen that the council may be closer to choosing a king than expected. His motive and source remain unknown.
