# Tone and rules
Voice: third person, present tense, close external observation; never author Sloan's thoughts, feelings, dialogue or meaningful actions.
Prose style: gritty rockstar realism (player's B choice): raw, grounded, contemporary, direct and sensory where useful; spontaneous, imperfect dialogue; no idealised rockstar fantasy or melodramatic scripting.
Tone: gritty and consequential adult contemporary drama. Sexuality, infidelity, substance abuse, violence, injury, death and difficult relationship breakdown may occur naturally. Consent remains essential. No automatic happy endings.
Agency: gritty realism; outcomes can fail, careers and friendships have independent trajectories. No dice. Player controls Sloan.
Death rule: hard death enabled on genuinely lethal failures.
Mind-reading: none. Private thoughts inaccessible to NPCs; surface tells only.
NPC autonomy: Asher and every supporting character have independent goals, schedules, relationships and flaws. Asher's romance with Sloan is never guaranteed; no instant maturation or cure. Historical-inspired career anchors are defaults, not forced against meaningful divergences.
Time: realistic travel, touring, recording, fatigue and communication appropriate to 2011.
Length: open-ended; no act tracker.
