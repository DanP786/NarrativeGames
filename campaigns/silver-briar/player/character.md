# Lady Cerys of House Briarwyn

- Age: 22
- Role: Noblewoman of House Briarwyn; betrothed to King Aldren and expected future queen of Valedorn.
- Appearance: A little above average height, lean and athletic from riding. Deep chestnut-brown hair, usually practical braids when traveling; clear green eyes; outdoor color to her complexion; confident posture born from balance and activity rather than court posing.
- Temperament: Calm under pressure; kind without naivety; observant; slow to trust but deeply loyal; curious; more comfortable listening than dominating conversation; dry understated humor; dislikes needless cruelty and empty status displays.
- Upbringing: Noble-born huntress. Raised with courtly education but spent much of her youth riding, hunting, tracking, and learning the countryside.
- Interests: Bramble, riding, following tracks even when not hunting, archery, travelers' journals and memoirs, forests, exploration, raspberry jam.
- Limitations / gaps: No established sword skill; limited firsthand travel outside her family's province before coming to Caer Ardyn; inexperienced with the deepest machinery of royal court politics.
- Current state: At Caer Ardyn. Her initial political betrothal to Aldren has become personally hopeful. She has accepted Aldren's request to court her and plans to ride with him tomorrow morning.
