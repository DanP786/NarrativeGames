# Personality and reputation

## Core presentation
Cerys presents as self-possessed, observant, practical, courteous, and quietly humorous. She does not seek attention but does not shrink from it.

## Emerging reputation at Caer Ardyn
- Court: The unconventional future queen who arrived astride in travel clothes, dismounted without assistance, and seems unusually comfortable being herself.
- Household servants: Favorable early impression; she thanks and acknowledges working people rather than looking through them.
- Royal household: Lady Merien considers her grounded; Oswin has responded well to her composure.
- Kitchens: Known humorously as an ally of raspberry jam against Aldren's preference for blackberry.

Cerys's ease with Aldren has already attracted notice, particularly their shared laughter at the first royal supper.
