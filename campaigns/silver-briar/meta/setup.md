# Campaign setup

Created: 8 August 2026 (repository reconstruction from live play)
Slug: silver-briar

## Established onboarding answers
- Genre / references: Original medieval fantasy closely inspired by the myth and story-shape of King Arthur, but with original names, setting, history, and cast. Courtly romance, chivalry, political intrigue, divided lords, old legends, and possible ancient mystery.
- Voice: Third person, past tense.
- Prose style: Serious, accessible literary/cinematic medieval romance; flowing but clear, with courtly dialogue, sensory detail when useful, and emphasis on relationships and politics.
- Length: Open-ended sandbox.
- Tone / content posture: Courtly, romantic, politically charged, fundamentally grounded. No specific content exclusions were established during initial play; treat sensitive escalation conservatively unless the player specifies otherwise.
- Stakes / scope: Personal story of Cerys and Aldren's marriage against kingdom-scale stakes in a recently reunited realm.
- Agency posture: Choices determine the story; no predetermined Arthurian fate. Consequences are real, but the world should not force the original myth onto Cerys.
- Death rule: Soft-fail by default pending explicit player preference; do not kill Cerys without establishing a different rule.
- Mind-reading rule: None established; thoughts are private. Old magic may exist, but no telepathy is assumed.
- Main thread: Can Cerys and Aldren turn a politically useful marriage into a genuine partnership while helping a fragile kingdom survive the grudges left by the Sundering?
- Character seed: Lady Cerys of House Briarwyn, age 22, noble-born huntress. Calm under pressure, kind without naivety, observant, slow to trust and deeply loyal, with dry humor. She rides astride, favors practical travel clothes, follows tracks for curiosity as often as hunting, shoots a bow accurately more often than not, reads travelers' journals and memoirs, and loves raspberry jam. She has chestnut-brown hair, clear green eyes, an athletic rider's build, and carries herself confidently.
- Opening situation: Cerys arrived on horseback at Caer Ardyn to meet King Aldren, her betrothed, before the formal betrothal/wedding ceremonies.

## Reconstruction note
This campaign state was created after substantial live play when it was discovered that onboarding had not been committed. Established events through Cerys accepting Aldren's invitation to court her are preserved in the session chronicle.
