# Tone and rules

- Voice: Third person.
- Tense: Past.
- Prose style: Serious, accessible literary/cinematic medieval romance. Flowing but clear sentences; moderate sensory detail; courtly dialogue and emotional subtext; avoid faux-archaic overload.
- Tone: Arthurian-inspired courtly romance and political intrigue, with warmth, wit, chivalry, old grudges, and an undercurrent of ancient mystery.
- Content posture: No explicit exclusions established. Escalate darker or sexual content only as fiction and player direction establish it.
- Agency: The story is inspired by Arthurian themes, not bound to Arthurian outcomes. Cerys's choices can alter every expected trajectory.
- Death rule: Soft fail only unless the player explicitly changes this rule.
- Mind-reading: None. Thoughts are private. No NPC may know Cerys's internal narration without visible tells or an in-world information path.
- Magic: Old beliefs and possible ancient powers exist at the edge of common knowledge; no specific magic system is yet established.
