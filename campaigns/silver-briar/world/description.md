# Valedorn

Valedorn is an old human kingdom of broad forests, fertile river valleys, mist-covered moors, eastern woodlands, and rugged western highlands. Its capital is the white-walled city of Caer Ardyn, where sovereigns swear before the ancient Stone of Oaths. Honor, hospitality, and sworn promises carry deep cultural weight.

## Recent history
Twenty-two years before Cerys's arrival, King Edric III died without a universally accepted heir. Rival interpretations of succession and long-suppressed noble feuds erupted into nearly two decades of conflict known as the Sundering. Noble houses fought private wars, trade failed, mercenaries and bandits flourished, and every region suffered.

King Aldren was born during the early Sundering, son of Lord Rowan of House Ashcombe. Rowan died while escorting refugees when Aldren was sixteen. Aldren completed the mission rather than pursuing vengeance, beginning a reputation for honor and restraint. Over subsequent years he settled disputes, honored promises, paid soldiers reliably, refused to sack captured towns, and pardoned defeated rivals who submitted. Enough lords eventually rallied to him that organized resistance became politically untenable. He was crowned three years before Cerys's arrival, though some great houses still question his blood-right and wait for him to fail.

## House Briarwyn and the royal marriage
House Briarwyn is an old eastern house whose lands lie between historically hostile regions. During the Sundering it protected roads, sheltered refugees, kept its granaries comparatively fair, and avoided opportunistic conquest. The marriage binds Briarwyn to the Crown, reassures eastern lords, and symbolizes alliance rather than conquest. Opposition comes from old royal-blood houses, disappointed families who wanted their own daughters chosen, western nobles wary of Briarwyn influence, and traditionalists suspicious of Cerys's unconventional habits.

## Court and custom
Caer Ardyn's court is magnificent, observant, and politically dense. Seating, clothing, dances, compliments, and toasts may carry political meaning. Guest-right is sacred; public insults between houses are frowned upon; witnessed oaths carry immense weight. Servants hear much and gossip travels quickly. Aldren's celebrated knights are known as the Companions of the Silver Hart.

## Faith and old belief
Most people worship the Light Above, whose temples stand throughout the realm. Older customs endure around standing stones, sacred groves, springs, and ancient ruins. Whether old powers truly survive remains uncertain in common knowledge.
