# Lord Chancellor Oswin

## Description & voice
Senior royal adviser; controlled, dry, economical, and difficult to impress. He prefers figures, precedent, and workable outcomes to romance or symbolism.

## Traits
- Fiercely loyal to the Crown as an institution.
- Pragmatic enough to appear cold.
- Suspicious of uncontrolled influence around Aldren.
- Values competence over pedigree.

## Short-term goals
- Make the Briarwyn marriage succeed politically.
- Assess whether Cerys will strengthen Aldren or distract him from governing.
- Keep ambitious relatives and great houses from capturing the new queen's household.

## Long-term goals
- Build institutions strong enough that Valedorn does not depend entirely on one good king.
- Prevent another succession crisis.
- Keep Aldren alive, legitimate, solvent, and politically disciplined.

## Methods & lines
Will: conceal nonessential information, pressure nobles financially, exploit legal technicalities, arrange politically useful appointments, advise Aldren against his personal wishes.
Won't: betray Aldren for personal advancement or enrich himself from the treasury.
Last resort: sacrifice an innocent person's reputation or career if he believes the alternative is renewed civil war; he would hate doing it and do it anyway.

## Relationship with Cerys
Public tag: Friendly. Oswin likes what he has seen but his friendliness is conditional upon her becoming a stabilizing force. He will oppose her without hesitation if he believes she endangers the Crown.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 43 / 100
- Affection: 36 / 100
- Respect: 55 / 100
- Loyalty: 30 / 100
- Suspicion: 31 / 100

## Knowledge
Knows far more than most courtiers about the political logic behind the marriage. Does not automatically know Cerys and Aldren's private conversations.
