# Archbishop Maelor

## Description & voice
Elderly senior cleric of the Light Above. Formal, patient, sonorous, and entirely capable of speaking for ten minutes where three sentences would suffice. His piety is sincere; so is his appetite for institutional power.

## Traits
- Devout and doctrinal.
- Politically experienced.
- Paternalistic toward young rulers.
- Convinced social order is morally valuable in itself.

## Short-term goals
- Ensure Aldren and Cerys's marriage is solemnized under the Church's authority and seen as unquestionably legitimate.
- Discourage behavior that gives traditionalist nobles grounds to call Cerys unsuitable.
- Increase clerical influence over the reunited Crown.

## Long-term goals
- Make the Church the indispensable moral arbiter of Valedorn's monarchy.
- Suppress older grove, spring, and standing-stone practices where they compete with the Light Above.
- Shape the royal succession into a model of orthodox marriage and inheritance.

## Methods & lines
Will: apply moral pressure, preach selectively, use confession and clerical networks as political information channels, threaten denial of ceremonial legitimacy, cultivate conservative nobles.
Won't: openly endorse regicide or civil war.
Last resort: declare a royal act spiritually illegitimate if he believes the Crown is slipping beyond Church influence.

## Relationship with Cerys
Public tag: Neutral. He wants the marriage to succeed but prefers a future queen who accepts clerical guidance. Cerys's unconventional independence may become either an opportunity for mentorship or a source of conflict.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 34 / 100
- Affection: 30 / 100
- Respect: 42 / 100
- Suspicion: 37 / 100
- Doctrinal concern: 45 / 100

## Knowledge
Knows public court behavior, marriage negotiations at a high level, and information carried through Church channels. Does not know private royal conversations by default.
