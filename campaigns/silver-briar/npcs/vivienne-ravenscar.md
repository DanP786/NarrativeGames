# Lady Vivienne Ravenscar

## Description & voice
Twenty-four. Celebrated court beauty, immaculately educated, poised, and far more intelligent than the ornamental role others imagine for her. Her speech is elegant and controlled; when angry she becomes more courteous, not less.

## Traits
- Proud, disciplined, politically perceptive.
- Resents pity more than defeat.
- Competitive but not naturally cruel.
- Keeps emotional wounds private until they harden.

## Short-term goals
- Avoid appearing humiliated by Aldren's choice of Cerys.
- Determine whether Cerys is capable of queenship or merely a politically fashionable outsider.
- Prevent her mother from turning Vivienne's disappointment into an undignified public campaign.

## Long-term goals
- Secure real influence independent of whom she marries.
- Protect House Ravenscar's position near the Crown.
- Prove she was never merely a discarded candidate for queen.

## Methods & lines
Will: use etiquette as a weapon, cultivate alliances, test people socially, withhold support until earned, remember slights.
Won't: knowingly fabricate sexual scandal about Cerys or beg Aldren to reconsider the match.
Last resort: politically align with Aldren's critics if Ravenscar is systematically shut out of power.

## Relationship with Cerys
Public tag: Neutral. Vivienne has not decided whether she dislikes Cerys. She does resent what Cerys represents: a future Vivienne was raised to expect, removed without her having failed at anything. Genuine respect is possible, as is a sharp rivalry.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 25 / 100
- Affection: 24 / 100
- Respect: 38 / 100
- Suspicion: 50 / 100
- Resentment: 57 / 100

## Knowledge
Knows the broad political reasons for the Briarwyn match and has heard court gossip about Cerys and Aldren getting along. She does not know their private conversations.
