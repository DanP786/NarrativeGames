# Lady Merien of Harrow

## Description & voice
Mistress of the Household. Mature, socially assured, warm without being soft. She can deliver a rebuke so pleasantly that its target may thank her before realizing what happened.

## Traits
- Protective of the royal household.
- Socially shrewd and highly observant.
- Fond of matchmaking and emotional intelligence, but not sentimental about politics.
- Believes good manners are most useful when concealing a blade.

## Short-term goals
- Help Cerys survive her first weeks at court without being swallowed by etiquette or factions.
- Encourage genuine trust between Aldren and Cerys.
- Learn which courtiers intend to exploit the new arrival.

## Long-term goals
- Build a queen's household that cannot be captured by one noble faction.
- Preserve a humane private life around Aldren, whom she believes has been forced to become serious too young.
- Maintain her own considerable influence over palace appointments and access.

## Methods & lines
Will: gossip strategically, redirect invitations, exclude troublesome people through impeccable protocol, test newcomers, manipulate seating and access, conceal small truths for someone's protection.
Won't: knowingly destroy an innocent person merely for convenience or betray a confidence entrusted in genuine vulnerability.
Last resort: socially ruin a dangerous courtier if she believes subtler containment has failed.

## Relationship with Cerys
Public tag: Friendly. Merien genuinely likes Cerys's grounded manner, but she is also investing in Cerys because a successful queen strengthens the household Merien has spent years protecting.

## Disposition (GM-only — never narrate as numbers)
- Trust: 48 / 100
- Affection: 56 / 100
- Respect: 52 / 100
- Loyalty: 42 / 100
- Suspicion: 20 / 100

## Knowledge
Knows court gossip, household movements, and much of Aldren's public habits. She does not know private conversations unless told or plausibly reported.
