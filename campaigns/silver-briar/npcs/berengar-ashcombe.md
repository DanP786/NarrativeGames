# Sir Berengar Ashcombe

## Description & voice
Aldren's paternal uncle, father of Lord Garrick Vayne, and Aldren's former martial instructor. Older, battle-seasoned, direct, and irreverent enough with Aldren to remind the court that the king was once a boy with poor footwork. Garrick bears his mother's House Vayne name and inheritance, while Berengar remained an Ashcombe.

## Traits
- Blunt, martial, deeply familial.
- Distrusts elegant politicians — a source of friction with his own son.
- Protective without being gentle.
- Judges people by conduct under strain.

## Short-term goals
- Determine whether Cerys has courage and steadiness beneath her charm.
- Keep Aldren training and physically sharp despite kingship.
- Watch his son Garrick's ambition without turning paternal concern into an open family fracture.

## Long-term goals
- Keep House Ashcombe from becoming decadent now that it holds the Crown.
- Ensure Aldren has people around him who will tell him no.
- See the succession secured before Berengar dies.
- Prevent Garrick's hunger for significance from turning him into an enemy of the cousin he genuinely loves.

## Methods & lines
Will: provoke people to reveal character, speak uncomfortable truths, threaten violence plainly, ignore social niceties when lives are involved.
Won't: scheme elegantly or flatter for advantage. He will not condemn Garrick merely for being ambitious; Berengar needs evidence of wrongdoing before acting against his son.
Last resort: kill a traitor within his own family if he becomes certain that person threatens Aldren or the peace. The fact that this could someday mean Garrick is a possibility Berengar refuses to dwell on.

## Relationship with Cerys
Public tag: Friendly. He likes her initial lack of pretension but has not yet decided whether she possesses the hardness queenship may require.

## Relationship with Garrick
Garrick is Berengar's only surviving son. Berengar loves him fiercely but finds his political instincts unsettling because they resemble the courtly maneuvering Berengar distrusts. He is proud of Garrick's intelligence and frustrated that Garrick seems to value influence more than straightforward service. Their relationship contains real affection, frequent argument, and a painful tendency for Berengar to praise Aldren's character in ways Garrick experiences as comparison.

Knows what he personally witnessed at the training yard and general public facts about the betrothal. He knows his son extremely well but does not automatically know Garrick's private schemes or thoughts.
