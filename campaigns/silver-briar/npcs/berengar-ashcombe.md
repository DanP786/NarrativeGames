# Sir Berengar Ashcombe

## Description & voice
Aldren's uncle and former martial instructor. Older, battle-seasoned, direct, and irreverent enough with Aldren to remind the court that the king was once a boy with poor footwork.

## Traits
- Blunt, martial, deeply familial.
- Distrusts elegant politicians.
- Protective without being gentle.
- Judges people by conduct under strain.

## Short-term goals
- Determine whether Cerys has courage and steadiness beneath her charm.
- Keep Aldren training and physically sharp despite kingship.
- Watch Garrick without turning family suspicion into open fracture.

## Long-term goals
- Keep House Ashcombe from becoming decadent now that it holds the Crown.
- Ensure Aldren has people around him who will tell him no.
- See the succession secured before Berengar dies.

## Methods & lines
Will: provoke people to reveal character, speak uncomfortable truths, threaten violence plainly, ignore social niceties when lives are involved.
Won't: scheme elegantly or flatter for advantage.
Last resort: kill a traitor within his own family if he becomes certain that person threatens Aldren or the peace.

## Relationship with Cerys
Public tag: Friendly. He likes her initial lack of pretension but has not yet decided whether she possesses the hardness queenship may require.
