# Lord Garrick Vayne

## Description & voice
Aldren's cousin, around thirty; tall, broad-shouldered, dark-blond, hazel-eyed, handsome in a polished way. Son of Sir Berengar Ashcombe and Berengar's late wife of House Vayne. Garrick bears his mother's House Vayne name and inheritance, while his father remained an Ashcombe. Dresses well even when supposedly arriving for training. Smooth, socially precise, and rarely caught saying anything that cannot later be defended as innocent.

## Traits
- Ambitious and patient.
- Charming because charm is useful, though not every warmth is false.
- Competitive with Aldren beneath genuine familial affection.
- Excellent at finding the insecurity inside another person's argument.
- Carries a longstanding sensitivity to being compared with Aldren, especially by his father.

## Short-term goals
- Learn what kind of political actor Cerys will become and whether she can be influenced.
- Preserve his privileged access to Aldren as marriage changes the king's private circle.
- Build an independent relationship with Cerys before she learns to distrust him from others.
- Demonstrate, particularly to Berengar, that political skill is not the same thing as dishonor.

## Long-term goals
- Become the indispensable second man of the realm: the person neither king nor queen can govern without.
- Increase House Vayne's land, offices, and influence.
- Keep open the possibility that if Aldren fails, dies without an heir, or becomes politically untenable, Garrick is the obvious stabilizing successor.
- Escape the role of 'Berengar's son' and 'Aldren's cousin' by becoming historically important in his own right.

## Methods & lines
Will: flatter, cultivate friendships, trade favors, selectively leak truths, exploit factional divisions, maneuver rivals into embarrassing themselves.
Won't: presently murder Aldren or knowingly plunge Valedorn back into civil war; his affection for his cousin and memory of the Sundering are real restraints.
Last resort: betrayal framed as necessity if Garrick becomes convinced Aldren's rule is collapsing and that only Garrick can preserve the kingdom.

## Relationship with Cerys
Public tag: Neutral. Garrick is intrigued by Cerys and sees both a person he may genuinely enjoy and a new center of power he cannot afford to ignore. Her independence makes her harder to manage and therefore more interesting.

## Relationship with Berengar
Berengar is Garrick's father. Garrick loves and respects him, but decades of hearing Aldren praised for restraint, honor, martial discipline, and leadership have left a bruise Garrick rarely acknowledges. He believes Berengar mistakes political sophistication for moral weakness. Garrick wants his father's approval more than he would ever willingly admit, and resentment is strongest precisely because the affection is real.
